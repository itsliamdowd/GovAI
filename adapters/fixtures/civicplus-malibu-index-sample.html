Agenda Center

View the current and previous two years’ agendas and minutes for the City Council and all commissions, committees and boards. Prior agendas and minutes can be found in OnBase, the City’s records management program.

Live web-streaming of City Council and Planning Commission meetings in progress, as well as videos from past meetings, are available in the Video Center.

Please check the agenda for instructions on how to participate in the meeting or you can find more information on our Open Meetings Public Participation page.
