## Residential Trash Service (CR&R)

CR&R provides the City of San Juan Capistrano's residents and businesses with solid waste and refuse disposal services. CR&R is the only legal company authorized to provide these services under a franchise agreement with the City. For information on available services, trash pickup days or any related matter, contact the CR&R Customer Service Department. The page also links CR&R rates as of July 1, 2023 and July 1, 2022 (PDFs).

If a premise is unoccupied for more than forty-five days, the customer may contact CR&R to request that service be canceled and that containers be returned to CR&R. The customer must give CR&R notice to cease service due to vacancy. The customer must also give reasonable evidence that the premises were vacant for the period in question. There will be no charges for services that are not needed or used.

## Self-Hauler Permit (Residential)

The City Manager may grant a self-hauler permit to the owner, tenant or occupant of residential premises in the City, upon request. The applicant must first (1) submit a form that includes the address of the premises and (2) show that arrangements have been made to return any containers belonging to the City's solid waste franchisee. Applications are available on the City website. Self-haulers must renew their permit at the start of each fiscal year and comply with Municipal Code Chapter 3.

Each application, initial or renewal, must include:
- A list of all bins, carts, rolloff boxes and other containers to be used.
- A list of all transportation and disposal equipment to be used.
- A written explanation of where all solid waste will be delivered for disposal and diversion.
- A written plan showing how not less than 50% of solid waste collected will be diverted from disposal in compliance with AB 939 through and including January 1, 2020. After that date, the plan must show how diversion of not less than 75% will occur in compliance with AB 341.
- Any other information the City Manager deems necessary to protect public health, safety and sanitary needs.

## Residential Organics Recycling (Green Cart)

The City and CR&R Environmental Services began a Residential Organics Recycling Program on July 1, 2020. Food scraps and yard waste are co-collected in the green cart. The material goes to CR&R's Regional Organics Anaerobic Recovery (ROAR) Facility in Perris, CA. All residents' monthly solid waste services include three carts: refuse, dry recyclables, and organics/green waste. If you do not have an organics green cart, contact CR&R Customer Service at 877-728-0446 to order one.

Put the contents of your kitchen food waste container into the green cart on your regular curbside garbage collection day. Do not place the kitchen food waste container outdoors at curbside. For questions, contact Noreen Swiontek, Environmental Programs Analyst, (949) 487-4307.

## Kitchen Food Waste Containers and What Goes in the Green Cart

Public Works is distributing FREE kitchen food waste containers to residents. Each comes with a promotional envelope containing 10 Bio-Bag compostable bags (plus a $1.00 off coupon) and an instructional flyer. Staff hopes to offer all San Juan Capistrano residents a container by June 2023.

Accepted foods include bread, pasta, rice, beans, dairy, nuts, coffee, tea, fruits, vegetables, processed foods, meat and fish. Frozen, spoiled or moldy foods are also accepted. Not accepted: liquids; glass, plastic, foam, metal or foil; single-use coffee pods; dryer or vacuum lint; ashes; pet food, pet waste or cat litter; dead animals; diseased plants; garbage or trash; diapers or used toilet paper; clothing or textiles; rocks, sand, dirt, lumber or logs; pills or medications; chemical or cleaning wipes; paper or "plastic-like" compostable to-go containers, cups and single-use utensils; chewing gum. When in doubt, put it in the black refuse cart.

Liners are optional. Compostable bags of any brand are allowed if Biodegradable Products Institute (BPI) or ASTM D6400 approved. Never use plastic bags. Compostable bags are sold at:

- Marbella Farmer's Market, 31109 Rancho Viejo Road
- Costco Wholesale, 33961 Doheny Park Road

## Household Hazardous Waste (HHW) Collection

Orange County Household Hazardous Waste Collection Centers are free, drive-through facilities. They accept products such as latex and oil-based paint, antifreeze, batteries, used oil, fertilizers, pesticides, herbicides and hobby supplies. The County has four permanent collection facilities open five days a week, including Saturdays. The Orange County San Juan Capistrano HHW Collection Center is at 32250 La Pata Ave. For information on the HHW program and the free Stop and Swap program, call 714-834-6752.

Transportation rules:
1. A maximum of 15 gallons or 125 pounds may be transported per vehicle, per trip.
2. Materials should be in original containers (except motor oil, fuels and antifreeze).
3. All containers must have lids, be sturdy, non-leaking, and protected from breakage.
4. Do not combine types of waste.
5. Do not mix oil-based paint with latex paint.
6. Dry out empty paint cans and discard them in regular trash.
7. To have a container returned (oil containers and boxes/crates), notify the attendant in advance. Some containers may not be returnable.

## Medication and Sharps Disposal

Take-back option: the San Juan Capistrano Household Hazardous Waste Collection Center is at 32250 Avenida La Pata, phone 714-834-4000, hours Tuesday-Saturday, 9am-3pm. The City asks residents to sign up for its newsblasts for information on medication take-back events. Call 714-834-4000 for additional disposal options.

Do not flush medications down the toilet or drain. To dispose of them in the trash:
1. Take the medications out of their original containers.
2. Mix them with an undesirable substance, such as cat litter or used coffee grounds.
3. Put the mixture into a disposable container with a lid, or into a sealable bag.
4. Conceal or remove personal information, including the Rx number, on the empty containers.
5. Place the sealed container and the empty medication container in the trash.

It is against California law to dispose of sharps in household trash. Sharps must be in a heavy-duty, puncture-resistant container with a tight-fitting lid, such as an empty detergent, fabric softener or bleach bottle. The California Sharps Program offers free container kits. Residents may also drop off home-generated sharps at the County's HHW Collection Center (32250 Avenida La Pata).
