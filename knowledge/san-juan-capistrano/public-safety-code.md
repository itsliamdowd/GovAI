## Police Services — Law Enforcement Provider

The City of San Juan Capistrano contracts its primary law enforcement services from the Orange County Sheriff's Department. It has done so since April 19, 1961. The page gives the city's population as over 36,000 and its area as 14 square miles.

The mission of SJC Police Services is to provide responsive, professional law enforcement services to all people in the city. Deputies are committed to promptly responding to calls for service, to protecting lives and safeguarding property, to starting and maintaining crime prevention programs, and to apprehending criminal offenders.
