## City Council — Elections, Terms, and Selection of Mayor

Elections for City Council seats are held on the first Tuesday in November of even-numbered years, coinciding with state and national elections. Council Members are elected to a four-year term. Terms are "overlapped," so each election fills either two or three open seats.

The Mayor and Mayor Pro Tem are selected at the first meeting of each December to serve a one-year term. Sitting Council Members fill these positions by a vote of the majority of members.

## Public Records — How to Request and Access Records

The City Clerk is custodian of the City's records. Records are stored at City Hall, off-site at a records repository, and in digital form. An online public records portal allows searches of Ordinances, Resolutions, Minutes, Agreements and City Council Policies. If you cannot find a record, email the City Clerk's office or call 949-443-6310.

Records that are disclosed to the public may be viewed at City Hall at no charge. The cost of copying records is charged to the requestor, based on the type of copy requested. If mailed, these costs are also paid by the requestor.

You can submit a request in any of these ways:
- Complete an online records request form.
- In person at City Hall, 30448 Rancho Viejo Road, Suite 110, at a departmental counter. If the request is extensive, you will be referred to the City Clerk.
- US Mail: 32400 Paseo Adelanto, San Juan Capistrano, CA 92675.
- Fax: 949-493-1053.
- Email the City Clerk.

## Public Records — Routine vs. Comprehensive Requests

Routine Requests are for records commonly known to be disclosable, where a single department can respond during the normal process of providing customer service. The department responsible for the record fulfills the request, and records are made available for viewing or copying on request.

Comprehensive Records Requests involve records from multiple departments, are broad in nature, or need extensive research, copying or off-site retrieval. They also include requests that need City Attorney review for possible exemption from disclosure. These requests are made to the City Clerk, who coordinates the response. Disclosable records are made available for viewing at the Office of the City Clerk after they are gathered and reviewed by the City Attorney.

For marriage licenses, passports, birth and death certificates, fictitious business names and recordation of documents, contact the Orange County Clerk-Recorder.

## City Manager's Office — Role and Contacts

The City operates under the Council-Manager system of local government. The City Manager is appointed by the Council and serves as administrative head of city government. The City Manager advises the Council on policy and fiscal matters and prepares the annual budget for City Council approval. All department heads report to the City Manager except the City Attorney, who reports directly to the City Council.

The City Manager's Office includes these divisions: Human Resources and Risk Management, Public Safety and Emergency Management, Open Space and Real Estate Management, Information Technology, and Special Projects.

Benjamin Siegel has been City Manager since February 2016 and can be reached at (949) 234-4563. Matisse Reischl is Assistant City Manager and can be reached at (949) 443-6315.
