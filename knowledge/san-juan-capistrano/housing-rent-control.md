## Mobile Home Park Rent Control — Annual Space Rent Increases

The City's Rent Control does not specifically create affordable housing, but it limits annual space rent increases to the prior year's CPI-U. The CPI-U for 2025 was 3.2%. It applies to space rent increases from April 1, 2026 to March 31, 2027.

## County of Orange Section 8 (Housing Choice Voucher) Program

Orange County Housing Authority (OCHA) is a Division within Orange County Community Services. It administers federally funded programs that provide monthly rental assistance to qualified tenants in privately owned rental housing. The largest program is the Housing Choice Voucher Program, also known as "Section 8". Section 8 assistance lets very low-income families, seniors, disabled persons, or individuals pay approximately 30% of their monthly income toward rent. OCHA pays the remaining rent directly to the property owner or manager. Applicants must earn below 50% of the County's median income. OCHA serves most Orange County cities, including San Juan Capistrano. For details, call OCHA at 714-480-2740.

Source: https://www.sanjuancapistrano.org/212/Housing

## Inclusionary Housing Requirement and In-Lieu Fee (AB 1483)

San Juan Capistrano requires 10% of residential units be reserved for lower-income households when developing two or more units. An in-lieu fee may be paid instead of including the units. The housing in-lieu fee is calculated monthly under Municipal Code Section 9-5.103. The City posts the code section and a Housing In-Lieu Fee Formula Spreadsheet (XLS) on its Assembly Bill 1483 page.

Source: https://www.sanjuancapistrano.org/213/Assembly-Bill-1483

## Permanent Local Housing Allocation (PLHA) Program

The State Department of Housing and Community Development (HCD) administers the SB 2 PLHA program. It is funded by a $75 fee that took effect January 1, 2018. The City is compliant with the program's eligibility requirements: a certified Housing Element and a completed Annual Progress Report. Eligible uses include affordable rental and ownership housing, assistance for persons experiencing or at risk of homelessness (including rapid re-housing and rental assistance), accessibility modifications in lower-income owner-occupied housing, and down payment assistance.

The amended Five-Year Plan allocates:
- 21% to rehabilitation of housing owned and occupied by households earning less than 120% of the area median income.
- 55% to assisting persons experiencing or at risk of homelessness.
- 19% to accessibility modifications.
- 5% to administration.

The City Council adopted the PLHA Five-Year Plan Amendment on June 17, 2025.

## Housing Authority

The San Juan Capistrano Housing Authority was formed by the City Council on January 17, 2012. It took over the housing functions of the Community Redevelopment Agency and is a separate legal entity from the City. It has seven members, who are the City Council members. The City Council also appoints two tenants of the Housing Authority as members if the Authority has tenants. The Housing Authority Secretary is Christy Jakl, City Clerk, at 949-443-6310.

Source: https://www.sanjuancapistrano.org/187/Housing-Authority

## Accessory Dwelling Units and SB 9

City and State law permit all single-family residential properties to be developed with an ADU up to 800 square feet, if it complies with City Municipal Code standards. The City can permit larger ADUs under certain conditions. SB 9 took effect January 1, 2022. It permits 2-unit development and lot splits, under specified circumstances, on parcels zoned for single-dwelling unit development. For SB 9 questions, contact Planning staff at planning@sanjuancapistrano.org.
