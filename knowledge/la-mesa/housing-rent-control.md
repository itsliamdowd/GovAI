## Housing Division — Overview

The Housing Division focuses on the use of programs and funds to develop neighborhood revitalization programs, affordable housing opportunities and economic development programs. It takes primary responsibility for implementing the City's Federal Community Development Block Grant (CDBG) funds. It also provides mid- and long-range planning through specific and neighborhood plans linked to the policies of the General Plan.

Primary responsibilities: administration of the City's Annual Federal CDBG Funds; Downtown Revitalization Programs; Economic Development Activities; Housing and Neighborhood Revitalization Program Development; operation of the Housing and Rehabilitation Loan Program.

## Fair Housing and Tenant/Landlord Services

State and Federal Fair Housing Laws prohibit discrimination in the sale, rental or financing of housing based on race, color, national origin, religion, sex, sexual orientation, ancestry, disability, source of income, any arbitrary status, or familial status. Familial status includes children under the age of 18 living with parents or legal custodians, pregnant women, and people securing custody of children under the age of 18. A housing provider who refuses to rent or sell a home, offers unequal terms, quotes different prices, or applies different policies based on the protected classes is violating Federal and/or State Law.

CSA San Diego County is under contract with the City of La Mesa to provide Fair Housing and tenant/landlord services. CSA mediates tenant/landlord disputes and investigates/monitors complaints of discrimination. It also provides community education and awareness seminars on tenant/landlord and Fair Housing rights and responsibilities. These services are free of charge.

To submit a concern/complaint, visit the CSA San Diego County website, email outreach@c4sa.org, or call 619.444.5700. If you are deaf, hard of hearing, or otherwise require telephone relay services, dial 711. CSA San Diego County is at 327 Van Houten Avenue, El Cajon, CA 92020.

## General Plan Housing Element (2021-2029)

The Housing Element is the City of La Mesa's housing plan. All California cities and counties must plan to meet the housing needs of everyone in the community and update the plan every eight years. La Mesa's portion of the County's Regional Housing Needs Assessment (RHNA) target for the 2021-2029 Housing Element period is 3,797 new units, further broken down by income group.

The La Mesa City Council adopted the 2021-2029 Housing Element (the sixth update, also called the 6th Cycle Housing Element) on July 27, 2021. The Housing Element received full certification from the California Department of Housing and Community Development (HCD) as of July 6, 2023. The adopted Housing Element and the July 6, 2023 HCD Compliance Letter are available through links on the page.

For more information, contact the Community Development Department at 619-667-1177 or planning@cityoflamesa.us.

## Grossmont Specific Plan Housing Clarifications (Housing Element Program 9)

The City is aligning the Grossmont Specific Plan with state housing law and the 6th Cycle Housing Element, Program 9. Proposed changes would clarify that housing is a priority, reference state laws allowing by-right housing development, and add housing as a key Site Development Issue or goal. Language would also be added to Title 24, Section 24.16.030 to align with state law, which allows certain housing development to be approved ministerially (e.g. without a site development plan approved by Planning Commission). The Specific Plan area is generally bounded by Interstate 8 to the south, Jackson Drive to the west, Parkway Drive to the north, and Amaya Drive to the East.
