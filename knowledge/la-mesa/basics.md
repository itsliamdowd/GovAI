## City Council Meeting Schedule

Regular meetings of the City Council are held on the second and fourth Tuesdays of each month at 6:00 p.m., except for both meetings in August and the second meeting in November, which serve as the summer and holiday recesses, respectively, pursuant to La Mesa Municipal Code Section 2.08.010. Meetings are held in the Council Chambers. Hearings are noticed in local publications. Adjourned sessions and special meetings are held as needed.

## City Council — Composition and Government Structure

The La Mesa City Council is composed of four councilmembers and a mayor. Councilmembers are elected at-large to serve four-year terms, with two seats up for election during each November General Municipal Election in even-numbered years. The mayor is also elected at-large to a four-year term during the November General Municipal Election. All council and mayoral elections are non-partisan.

The City of La Mesa was incorporated on February 16, 1912 as a General Law city. It has a council/manager form of government: the council sets policy and gives direction on all matters concerning the city, and the city manager carries out council policy and oversees day-to-day operations.

## Agendas and Minutes — How to Access

City Council, Board and Commission agendas, including accompanying staff reports and minutes, are available on the City's Agenda Center. Upcoming meetings are listed in the "Upcoming Meetings" section under either the City Council Meetings header or the Board, Commission, and Standing Subcommittee Meetings header. To view past meetings, scroll to the "Past Meetings" section under the same headers, select the year, then select the meeting type.

Agenda formats: "Agenda Cover Page PDF" is the agenda only, without links to reports. "Agenda HTML" is the agenda with links to staff reports and related documents. "Agenda PDF" is the full agenda packet as one document, with no links. To receive email notifications when agendas are available, use "Subscribe to Agenda Alerts."

## Public Comment — Speaking Time and General Public Comment

Time allotted (subject to change by the Mayor or presiding officer): General Public Comment (items not on the agenda): 3 minutes. Consent Calendar (any or all items): 3 minutes. Agenda Items (not on Consent Calendar): 3 minutes. Time cannot be combined or yielded to another speaker. Individuals who use a translator receive twice the amount of time.

In accordance with state law, an item not on the agenda may be brought forward by the public for comment, but the City Council, Board, or Commission cannot discuss or take action on it at that meeting. If appropriate, the item will be referred to staff or placed on a future agenda.

## Written Comments (eComments)

eComments are available once an agenda is published and are limited to 3,700 characters (approximately 500 words). To submit one, locate the meeting in the "Upcoming Meetings" section, click the comment bubble icon, select the agenda item, and click the orange "Leave Comment" button.

Deadline: from the time the agenda is published until 24 hours prior to a regularly scheduled meeting or 2 hours prior to a special meeting. If you have difficulty submitting an eComment, you may email it to cityclrk@cityoflamesa.gov; the same deadline still applies for it to be included in the official meeting record. eComments will not be read aloud as part of a regular meeting item; however, any member of the legislative body or the public may read them during the allotted comment time.
