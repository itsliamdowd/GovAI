## Trash and Recycling Collection

The City contracts with EDCO for trash and recycling collection and for bulky waste disposal. Trash on Highway 78 is managed by the California Department of Transportation (Caltrans). Submit a maintenance request along the highway to Caltrans through its website.

Mattress disposal is at Palomar Transfer Station, 760-603-0153. Residents can drop off up to 3 mattresses for free. Anything over 3 incurs a cost.

Source: https://www.cityofvista.com/residents/recycling-information

## Household Hazardous Waste Collection Facility

Residents of Vista, Encinitas, Escondido, Solana Beach, San Marcos, and Carlsbad can drop off household hazardous waste at no cost. Proof of residency is required. The facility is at 1145 E. Taylor Street, across the street from the City Public Works Yard. It is open Saturdays only, 9:00 am to 3:00 pm. No appointment is necessary.

Accepted items include lawn and garden poisons (pesticides, herbicides, fungicides), batteries, fire extinguishers, fingernail polish removers, pool and photo chemicals, home-generated sharps, fluorescent light bulbs, old and unused medicines, and e-waste (computers, televisions, toaster ovens, etc.). Free door-to-door collection is available to residents 65+ or households with a registered handicap placard. To schedule it, call 800.714.1195.

Source: https://www.cityofvista.com/residents/household-hazardous-waste-facility

## Summer Day Camps — Fees and Payment

Mini Explorers, Explorers 1 & 2 and Multi Sports 1 & 2 cost $207 Vista resident/$253 non-resident per week. Counselor-in-Training costs $104 Vista resident/$121 non-resident per week.

Pay in full for the first week of camp and a $10 deposit per week for remaining weeks to reserve. Weekly camps must be paid in full by Thursday the week prior to the start of camp, or the reservation is cancelled. Youth Scholarships are available for this program.

Source: https://www.cityofvista.com/departments/recreation-comm-services/summer-day-camps/camp-fees

## Report a Problem

Use the City's Report a Problem system to report graffiti, potholes, roadway matters, traffic lights out, street lights out, and more. You can use the online form or call 760.639.6141 Monday-Thursday 7:30 am-5:30 pm and alternate Fridays 7:30 am-4:30 pm. Issues may also be reported using the Access Vista app, available from your preferred app store. Park maintenance issues and irrigation runoff are also reported through the Report a Problem system.

Source: https://www.cityofvista.com/home/

## Stormwater and Water Quality Hotline

If you see something being dumped into the storm drain or street, or see water running in the street when it is not raining, report it to the Water Quality Hotline or use the Report a Problem form. Stormwater & Water Quality Protection Program Hotline: 760.643.2804. Vista Municipal Code Chapter 13.18, Stormwater Management and Discharge Control Program, sets out the related prohibitions and authorities.

Source: https://www.cityofvista.com/residents/stormwater-water-quality-protection
