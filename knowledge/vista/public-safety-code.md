# Public Safety & Code Enforcement

## Food Trucks — Inspection and Licensing by Code Enforcement

The City inspects and licenses all food trucks operating in Vista. Food truck vendors can schedule an inspection by contacting Code Enforcement at 760-639-1641. Residents booking a food truck are asked to make sure the vendor is licensed to operate in the City of Vista and compliant before booking.

## Outdoor Dining and Temporary Signs — Compliance After COVID-19 Emergency Order

With the lifting of the COVID-19 emergency order in May 2022, the allowances for outdoor dining and temporary signs were rolled back to pre-COVID requirements. Compliance requirements cover banners, portable signs, window signs, directional signs, outdoor seating, outdoor lighting, use of on-street parking spaces, and temporary shade structures. To continue outdoor dining, contact the Planning Department at 760-639-6105. Depending on zoning and site requirements, changes may be allowed through the Use Permit process.

Source: https://blog.cityofvista.com/food-truck-0

## Live Entertainment Permit

Businesses hosting bands, karaoke, and other live events are required to obtain a Live Entertainment Permit. The City has waived the permit fee for the first two years. To receive the waiver, submit a complete Live Entertainment Permit Package, including a list of all employees and proof of their Responsible Beverage Service training, to Sara Trench at strench@cityofvista.com.
