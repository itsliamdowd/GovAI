## Online Permit Application (City of Vista Online Permitting System)

Go to cityofvista.com/permits and create an account. The first time you log in, you must use the link in the email you receive to activate your account. Once logged in, click the Create Application button at the top of the page, select the permit type from the list, and click Begin Application. The February 2022 guide says Water Heaters was the permit type available online at that time.

Section 1: enter a Project Name and Address. The address must be located within the Vista city limits. Section 2: enter applicant and property owner information. An email address and phone number are required for both people, and they can be the same person. If a contractor will do the work, enter their California Contractor License number on the License Number line. Section 3: select whether you are (1) the property owner planning on doing the work yourself or (2) hiring a licensed contractor. Also enter the valuation of the project and a more detailed description of the intended work.

Section 4: if your permit type requires plans, add them in PDF format and add the "Plans" tag to the file. Click Submit, then Accept on the next screen. You will receive a confirmation email from VistaCityworks@cityofvista.com. For additional information, contact the Development Services counter at (760) 639-6108.

Source: https://cityworks.cityofvista.com/Permits/How%20to%20Apply%20for%20a%20Permit%20Online.pdf

## Business License — Online Services

The City's business license portal offers these online options. Apply: submit a business license application online. Renew: renew a business online. Pay: pay an outstanding balance for a business license. Close: close a business online. Search: business search, where only active licenses are available. Other: create or sign in to an account profile, report a problem, and request an account information update.

The portal lists the City of Vista at 200 Civic Center Drive • Vista, CA 92084.

Source: https://blrenewals.cityofvista.com/
