## Fire Advisory Board

The Board of Supervisors established the Fire Advisory Board on October 21, 2014. It gives input on the operations and maintenance budget of the San Diego County Fire Protection District (formerly County Service Area No. 135). It is a public forum to plan, discuss and implement changes that improve fire protection services in the unincorporated area of San Diego County.

## Drought Disaster — Reporting Agricultural Losses

The USDA has designated San Diego County as a natural disaster area due to severe drought (April 2026). If your operation experiences any loss due to drought, fill out an Agricultural Damage Assessment Form. Return it by email to sdcawm@sdcounty.ca.gov or to any Agriculture, Weights and Measures (AWM) office:
- AWM Kearny Mesa Office: 9325 Hazard Way, Ste. 100, San Diego, CA 92123
- AWM San Marcos Office: 151 E. Carmel St., San Marcos, CA 92078

These reports help determine eligibility for state and federal support. Affected operators may also contact local Farm Service Agency (FSA) offices for eligible emergency loan assistance, with an eight-month application window from April 6, 2026.

Source: https://www.sandiegocounty.gov/disaster-preparedness-for-farms-and-ranches/

## Agriculture and Livestock Evacuation Pass (Ag Pass) Program

Commercial farm and ranch operators may need to evacuate in a wildfire or other emergency. An Ag Pass can potentially let you back into the restricted area to care for crops and animals. Law enforcement or fire officials decide whether it is safe to enter. An Ag Pass does not guarantee you can go in.

Source: https://www.sandiegocounty.gov/disaster-preparedness-for-farms-and-ranches/

## Highly Pathogenic Avian Influenza (HPAI) — Reporting Sick Birds

HPAI has been detected in wild birds in California. The California Department of Food and Agriculture (CDFA) is the lead agency. The greatest risk is to commercial and non-commercial poultry farmers and owners whose flocks could be infected.

Report any unusual or suspicious sick or dead domestic, pet, or collection birds immediately to the CDFA Sick Bird Hotline at (866) 922-2473. For unusual or suspicious wild bird deaths, or questions about wildlife rehabilitation, contact the California Department of Fish and Wildlife (CDFW).

Source: https://www.sandiegocounty.gov/disaster-preparedness-for-farms-and-ranches/
