The Assessor Parcel Number (APN) must be listed on the first page of all deeds, quitclaim deeds, mortgages, and deeds of trust submitted for recording. ## Child and Family Well-Being (CFWB) — About the Department

Child and Family Well-Being integrates the First 5 Commission of San Diego, Child Welfare Services, childcare services, and other resources. Its goal is to prevent children and youth from entering the child welfare system and to keep families together. Programs include Prevention and Early Intervention Services, Child Care System Coordination, First 5 San Diego (children ages 0-5 and pregnant individuals), Emergency Response (Child Abuse Investigations), Foster Care and Adoption Services, Extended Foster Care (ages 18 to 21), and Placement and Supportive Services.

Youth shelter programs are Polinsky Children's Center, a short-term shelter (up to 10 days) for children placed in protective custody, and San Pasqual Academy, a campus for youth ages 12 to 19 in foster care. For more information, call (858) 616-5811.

Source: https://www.sandiegocounty.gov/department/cfwb/about-child-and-family-well-being/

## Polinsky Children's Center — Services

The A.B. and Jessie Polinsky Children's Center (PCC) is a licensed Temporary Shelter Care Facility managed by the County of San Diego. It serves children 0 to 17 years old who cannot stay with their families due to child abuse, neglect, or abandonment. Children stay temporarily until they can be placed with relatives, friends, or another environment, and may also return home.

Each child gets a physical and mental health evaluation, and medical professionals are always onsite. Mental health services include on-site assessments, individual and group therapy sessions, and help finding the best placement. Child developmental specialists support children under six years old. Most children attend their same school, and PCC helps coordinate transportation. San Diego Unified School District is on-site at PCC. Contact: 858-514-4600.

Source: https://www.sandiegocounty.gov/about-the-a-b-and-jessie-polinsky-childrens-center/

## County Fire — Services and Areas Served

County Fire provides fire and emergency medical services to 1.73 million acres of unincorporated San Diego County, with staff from both the County and CAL FIRE. Services include fire protection and prevention for structures and wildland areas, medical emergency response and rescues, building plan review and fire safety inspections of businesses and homes, and medical direction to EMS agencies. The Emergency Medical Services Office has regulatory oversight of the entire San Diego county region prehospital system.

Communities served: Borrego Springs, Boulevard, Campo, Deerhorn Valley, De Luz, Descanso, Dulzura, Harbison Canyon, Intermountain, Jacumba, Jamul, Julian, Lake Morena, Mount Laguna, Ocotillo Wells, Otay Mesa, Palomar Mountain, Pine Valley, Potrero, Ramona, Ranchita, San Pasqual, Shelter Valley, Sunshine Summit, and Warner Springs. County Fire also provides cooperative fire protection to Deer Springs Fire Protection District.

Source: https://www.sandiegocounty.gov/department/county-fire/about-county-fire/

## County Fire Marshal Program

The County Fire Marshal Program is part of the Community Risk Reduction Division in San Diego County Fire. The Fire Marshal is responsible for proposed development project and plan review, fire protection systems plan review, and fire safety inspections for businesses and other facilities. The programs cover properties in the San Diego County Fire Protection District and Deer Springs Fire Protection District. For licensing and event permit review, the page points to the San Diego County Sheriff's Department Licensing and Permits and the County of San Diego Department of Environmental Health and Quality Community Event Permits.

Source: https://www.sandiegocounty.gov/department/county-fire/about-the-fire-marshal/
