## Regional Decarbonization Framework

The Regional Decarbonization Framework (RDF) sets a vision of zero-carbon emissions in the San Diego region by mid-century. It guides the work of the Office of Sustainability and Environmental Justice (OSEJ). Between 2021 and 2024, OSEJ worked with over 2,000 community members, academic researchers, and regional partners to create three RDF Foundational Publications:

- RDF Technical Report: a science-based study showing possible pathways to zero carbon emissions in the largest emitting sectors of buildings, electricity, and transportation.
- Workforce Development Study: recommendations for building a strong, inclusive workforce and just transition opportunities.
- Let's Get There Playbook: a resource guide listing practical steps that governments, organizations, and residents can take in response to the climate emergency. A current draft is available.

You can sign up for RDF updates by email or SMS/text message on the page.

Source: https://www.sandiegocounty.gov/regional-decarbonization-framework-reports/

## SALC 2.0 — Sustainable Agricultural Lands Conservation Project

In December of 2022, the San Diego County Local Agency Formation Commission (LAFCO), in partnership with the County's Planning & Development Services Department (PDS), was awarded a $450,000 Sustainable Agricultural Lands Conservation (SALC) planning grant. The SALC 2.0 Project aimed to support existing and emerging farmers through timely market research and to curb the premature conversion of agricultural lands.

The SALC 2.0 Final Report, "Cultivating Solutions," is available. It was prepared by PDS and LAFCO and includes a Market and Gap Analysis and a Strategic Action Plan. Appendices cover the Community Input Summary, Market Analysis, Gap Analysis, and Strategic Action Plan. PDS shared the report with the Board of Supervisors through a Board Memo on June 17, 2026.

Past outreach: a virtual public workshop, "Project Close-Out: Findings & Next Steps," on Thursday, March 26, 2026 at 6 PM; a public review period for the Draft Strategic Action Plan, November 10th – December 10th, 2025; and an in-person "Forum for Cultivating Solutions" on Tuesday, May 27th, 2025 at 9:00 AM.

Project contact: Michael Blackmun, AgConservation@sdcounty.ca.gov, 619-915-9730.

Source: https://www.sandiegocounty.gov/salc-2-0-final-report-cultivating-solutions/
