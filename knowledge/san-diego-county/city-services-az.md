## Animal Services — Who Is Served, Emergencies, and Shelters

County Animal Services is the open-admission animal services agency for unincorporated San Diego County and serves the unincorporated areas only. County Animal Services provides licensing to residents of the unincorporated areas of the county only. For animal-related emergencies in unincorporated areas only, call 619-236-2341 (24/7 Emergency Line).

The County's new 20,000-square-foot shelter in Santee opened on October 15, 2026. The opening marks the permanent closure of the County's Bonita shelter. All Bonita sheltering operations have moved to the new Santee location. The department also offers low-cost spay and neuter, low-cost vaccinations, and microchips.

Source: https://www.sandiegocounty.gov/department/animal-services/

## Camping at County Parks — Reservations

You can make a reservation online 24 hours a day. Telephone reservations: (858) 565-3600, (877) 565-3600, Monday to Friday, 8 a.m. to 5 p.m. For online reservations, you can book campsites up to 24 hours before the first day of your stay. If you need to make a reservation on the same day, call the reservation center.

Cabin camping is offered year-round at eight of the campgrounds. Cabin beds are generally wood-framed, and the page notes "no mattress or bedding" for several cabins (for example Agua Caliente, Dos Picos, Lake Morena); at William Heise County Park, mattresses and bedding are not included. Tents or RVs are not allowed at the Dos Picos cabins because of limited space. Oakoasis County Park is available only for youth groups.

Showers: All County campgrounds have showers. The showers use quarters or tokens and cost 50 cents for 4 minutes.

Source: https://www.sandiegocounty.gov/service/camping-at-county-parks/

## Report Child Abuse or Neglect

Call 800-344-6000 to make a report. You can call 24 hours a day, every day. Report suspected child abuse or neglect at 858-560-2191. Within California, call toll free 800-344-6000. Call if you are concerned about a child's safety, a parent afraid you may harm your child, or a child or youth who believes you are being abused.

When a report comes in, a social worker evaluates it to determine if the child is at risk. The County could investigate within 24 hours, investigate within 10 days, or refer to a community agency without investigating. Mandated Reporters (as identified by law) must contact authorities if they suspect or are told about child abuse. Non-urgent reports (Mandated Reporters only) can be submitted online, and you can self register.

Source: https://www.sandiegocounty.gov/service/report-child-abuse-or-neglect/

## Urgent Support for Foster Youth and Caregivers (FURS)

The Family Urgent Response System (FURS) is a free hotline for current or former foster youth (up to age 21) and caregivers. Call or text 833-939-FURS (833-939-3877). You can call 24 hours a day, every day, and get help right away for any big or small issues. Email: FURS@dss.ca.gov.

You can talk to a trained counselor or peer. If you want more support, a team can come directly to you and help you create a plan to stabilize your situation and keep you safe. The team will follow up by connecting youth and caregivers to local services and support.

Source: https://www.sandiegocounty.gov/service/urgent-support-for-foster-youth-and-caregivers/

## Extended Foster Care and Independent Living Skills

Extended Foster Care: if you are in foster care, you can get Extended Foster Care from 18 until you turn 21. You become part of the program when you turn 17. You must be doing any of these: completing high school or an equivalent program; enrolling in college, community college or a trade program; employed at least 80 hours a month; or participating in a program or activity to improve job skills. If you cannot do any of those because of a medical condition, you can still qualify. If you have a child, you can qualify if you were in foster care when you turned 18. Your social worker must approve the place where you live. If you choose to leave foster care, you can re-enter Extended Foster Care any time before your 21st birthday. If you are 21 or older you cannot get Extended Foster Care, but you can visit 211SanDiego.org or call 2-1-1. Phone: 619-767-5275. Email: cfwbefcinfo.hhsa@sdcounty.ca.gov.

Independent Living Skills: for current or former foster youth, you become part of the program when you turn 16. It serves foster youth 16 to 18 years old in placement outside their home (help from County staff) and people 18 to 21 years old who are non-minor dependents or former foster youth (help from outside agencies). Call 866-457-4636.

Source: https://www.sandiegocounty.gov/service/help-for-current-and-former-foster-youth-to-live-independently/
