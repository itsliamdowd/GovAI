## Vehicle Abatement — What Code Enforcement Investigates

The Code Enforcement Division assists Chula Vista communities with eliminating public nuisance vehicles from neighborhoods. It investigates dismantled, inoperable or wrecked vehicles, boats, campers, trailers, or parts thereof on private property stored in public view. It also investigates vehicles illegally parked on designated lawn areas and auto repair in residential areas.

Anyone who feels a vehicle (or parts) is in violation can file a complaint. Code Enforcement will provide the property owner with written notification of the violation to be corrected.

## Abandoned Vehicles on Public Streets and Parking Rules

Report all abandoned vehicles on public streets to the Chula Vista Police Department using the ACT Chula Vista app or Submit a Service Request. Provide a complete description of the vehicle including make, model, color, location, and license number if possible.

Do not leave a vehicle parked on a public street in the same place for more than 72 hours. Vehicles parked on a public street must be currently registered. Any vehicle with expired registration over six months may be impounded without notice by the police department. Vehicles must be able to be driven safely on a roadway and kept in operating condition with inflated tires.

## Responsible Beverage Service Training

Chula Vista adopted a Responsible Alcoholic Beverage Service Ordinance in June 2014. It requires Chula Vista on-sale alcohol retail licensees and their employees to complete a certified training course in alcohol service every four years. The due date for completing training is six months after hiring a new employee or opening a new business, whichever date is earliest.

Licensee Education on Alcohol and Drugs (LEAD) training is offered free of charge by the Department of Alcoholic Beverage Control (ABC) at locations throughout San Diego County. The ABC website also lists approved fee-based Responsible Beverage Service (RBS) training programs. The requirements are in Chula Vista Municipal Code Chapter 8.27.

## Police Senior Volunteer Patrol (SVP)

The Senior Volunteer Patrol was established in 1994 and is made up of formally trained, uniformed volunteers age 50 and older. No law enforcement experience is required. To get started, complete a ride-along with an SVP patrol team: call (619) 476-2417 or email svpp@chulavistapdca.gov.

Volunteers must attend a 3-day academy, commit to at least one year, perform a minimum of six hours of service per week (typically one 6-hour shift), and attend monthly meetings (approximately one hour). Applicants must pass a background investigation, be 50 years of age or older (some waivers may be available), possess and maintain a valid driver's license (some waivers possible), and be a U.S. citizen or permanent resident.

SVP members do not carry weapons, conduct traffic stops, issue moving vehicle citations, make arrests, or confront individuals. They observe and report. Their duties include enforcing parking regulations (handicap parking, RV permits, fire lanes, time-limited parking), assisting the Vehicle Abatement Program, and conducting Vacation Checks for residents who are away from home.

Source: https://www.chulavistaca.gov/departments/police-department/volunteering

## Police Cadet Program

The Chula Vista Police Cadet Program began in 1968 and acquaints young people ages 14 to 20 with the objectives of public safety. To be eligible, you must be 14-20 years old and registered as a full-time student with a grade point average of 2.0 or above. You must not have been convicted of a felony or serious misdemeanor, must be of good moral character, and must pass a minimum background check and oral interview. For questions, email cadets@chulavistapdca.gov.

## Fire Extinguisher Size Requirements

The minimum size fire extinguisher for a business or multi-family residence is 2A-10BC for every 6000 square feet of area, and not to exceed 75 feet of travel distance to access the extinguisher. Single-family residences should consider the area they wish to protect and be guided by the vendor's recommendation.

Source: https://www.chulavistaca.gov/departments/fire-department/about-cvfd/faqs

## Disposing of Fire Extinguishers and LPG Cylinders

Fire extinguishers may be disposed of if they have been depressurized, and are then considered 'metallic waste'. An out of compliance LPG cylinder must be purged and empty before it can be recycled. For either item, City residents can contact Pacific Waste Services at 619-421-9400 and schedule a 'Bulky Item Pick-Up'. The call must be placed two days prior to the resident's regular trash service day for pick-up that week.

## Christmas Tree Recycling

Chula Vista residents can recycle Christmas trees by placing them at the curb with their regular trash for pick-up between December 26 and January 5. Trees must not exceed 48-inches in length, and longer trees must be cut to this size. Metal tree stands must be removed, and wooden stands are acceptable. Flocked trees will be picked-up as regular trash. Residents living in apartments should contact their apartment manager about FREE Christmas Tree Pick-Up. Trees are collected one day a week at each apartment complex when scheduled through Republic Services.
