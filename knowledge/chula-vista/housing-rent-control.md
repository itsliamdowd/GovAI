## Mobilehome Rent Review ("Rent Control")

The Housing Division implements a rent stabilization process for eligible mobilehome residents under Chula Vista Municipal Code Chapter 9.50 - Mobilehome Park Space Rent Review. Residents are eligible for rent control if they meet certain criteria and have paid the Administrative Fee for that fiscal year.

Rent control limits rent increases from a park owner to once per year and only by the Current Annual Permissive Increase, detailed in the Annual Permissive Notice. The Annual Permissive Notice (English & Spanish) is at 2.7% as of August 12, 2026. If a park owner proposes an increase over the permissive rate for that year, a resident may petition the increase to the Mobilehome Rent Review Commission. Other notices listed on the page are the Administrative Fee Courtesy Notice (English & Spanish) and the CVMC 9.50 ('Rent Control') Disclosure.

## Mobilehome Administrative Fee

The Mobilehome Administrative Fee is $5 for Fiscal Year July 1, 2026 – June 30, 2027. It is DUE BY SEPTEMBER 1, 2026, and no late payments are accepted. Bills addressed to the resident on file OR current resident should arrive on or around the beginning of June 2026. Payment is due by July 1st each year with a 60-day grace period for full payment without late penalty.

When the payment period is open, payments can be made to the Finance Department or online using the "Pay Fee" link on the page. A CVMC 9.50 Administrative Fee FAQ is available for download. For questions or assistance, contact the Department of Housing and Homeless Services at 619-691-5047.

## Mobilehome Communities and State Resources

The City of Chula Vista has 31 mobilehome and trailer parks, with nearly 3,600 spaces. The City provides services related to mobilehomes including Title 25 Inspections, Rent Review ("Rent Control"), and Mobilehome Park Closures.

Commonly contacted agencies listed on the page include the following:
- **Mobilehome Residency Law:** state regulations on operations of mobilehome parks.
- **State Office of the Mobilehome Ombudsman / Mobilehome Assistance Center:** a complaint processing office that primarily assists the public with questions or problems associated with manufactured home (mobilehome) living, including utility health and safety issues and complaints with manufactured home dealers, sales, and warranties.
- **State Mobilehome Residency Law Protection Program:** created through the Mobilehome Residency Law Protection Act of 2018 (Assembly Bill 3066). Through the program, homeowners may seek assistance from the California Department of Housing and Community Development (HCD) relating to the Mobilehome Residency Law. The program is effective 2020.

For registration and title documents for new or resold manufactured homes, contact the State Housing and Community Development Department.

## Fair Housing — Filing a Discrimination Complaint

The City's Fair Housing Program works to reduce housing discrimination in rental, sales, lending, and insurance markets on the basis of race, sex, color, religion, national origin, familial status (presence of children), or handicap, in compliance with the Fair Housing Act. The City has contracted with Legal Aid Society of San Diego (LASSD) as its Fair Housing Program Administrator. The California Fair Employment and Housing Act of 1959 also protects against discrimination based on ancestry, age, sexual orientation, gender identity, marital status, familial status, and source of income.

If you believe you have been denied housing or the opportunity to apply for housing in Chula Vista, or have been a victim of illegal discrimination or harassment because of a characteristic protected by federal or state law, contact LASSD online or call 1-877-534-2524. You may also file a complaint online directly with the U.S. Department of Housing and Urban Development (HUD). Contact LASSD within one year of the incident, document as much information as possible, and speak to a housing counselor at LASSD. Depending on the facts, the case may be referred to a legal representative for further investigation, and an attempt will be made to assist in resolving the issue. If the issue remains unresolved and there is evidence that a violation has occurred, the complaint may be tried in court.

## Landlord-Tenant Issues and Eviction Resources

The relationship between a renter and the property owner is governed by federal and state laws. The renter and property owner should try to resolve problems by open communication and discussion, and any issues are resolved as a matter between two private parties. The City of Chula Vista does not enforce federal and state laws related to landlord-tenant issues. Resources for tenants and landlords focus on California laws and are available on the Chula Vista Landlord-Tenant Webpage.

Tenants in California have certain protections from eviction under state law, as well as under local laws in some cities and counties; see https://housing.ca.gov/tenant/protection_guidelines.html. If you believe you have been unlawfully evicted or need legal advice, consult an attorney. For low or no cost legal help, visit lawhelpca.org and/or Tenant Resources, or call Legal Aid Society of San Diego at 1-877-534-2524.

## Housing Choice Voucher Rental Assistance (Section 8)

This HUD funded program subsidizes the rents of qualified very low-income tenants to affordable levels. In general, to be eligible for a housing choice voucher, a household's income may not exceed 50% of the area median income (AMI) adjusted for household size. A family that is issued a voucher is responsible for finding a suitable housing unit of its choice where the owner agrees to rent under the program.

For Chula Vista residents, the program is administered by the County of San Diego Housing Authority, and you may apply in person or online. For questions about portability to or from Chula Vista, you must contact the County. Contact: Housing Authority of the County of San Diego, 3989 Ruffin Rd, San Diego CA 92123; TOLL FREE (877) 478-5478 | LOCAL (858) 694-4890.

## Mobilehome Community Housing Improvement Program (CHIP)

CHIP offers loan funds for eligible mobilehome owners to make health and safety related repairs. Currently, the City offers up to $10,000 in a 0% deferred 5-year forgivable loan. No monthly payments are required, and after the fifth year of occupancy, loans are forgiven. Eligible applicants must be living in a designated Mobilehome Home Park (MHP) zone and have income below 50% of the area median income. Contact (619) 691-5047 to be placed on the current waiting list.

Source: https://www.chulavistaca.gov/departments/housing-and-homeless-services/homeowner-homebuyer

## Western Chula Vista Rehabilitation Program (Single-Family Homes)

The Western Rehabilitation Program offers low interest loans to single-family homeowners below 80% of the area median income for home improvements and energy efficiency upgrades. Currently, the City offers a 0% or 3% deferred loan, dependent on income, for up to $25,000. Priority is given to homes located within targeted "low income" census tracts. Condos and Townhouses are not eligible. Contact (619) 691-5047 to be placed on the current waiting list.

## First-Time Homebuyer Program

Eligible families with incomes at or below 80 percent of AMI may apply for a 3 percent interest, deferred-payment loan of up to 22 percent of the purchase price (maximum loan of $120,000) to purchase a single-family home, townhome or condominium. The loan may be used toward down payment and closing costs. No payments are required for 30 years, unless the owner sells or no longer occupies the home as a primary residence, at which time the principal balance, including accrued interest, must be repaid.

To qualify, the buyer must obtain a fixed-rate first trust deed loan; have adequate income and a good credit rating; and provide a minimum down payment of 3 percent. Applicants also must attend a homebuyer education class and pre-purchase counseling. The program is serviced by the San Diego Housing Commission; questions go to fthb@sdhc.org. Existing recipients who wish to refinance should contact affhousing@chulavistaca.gov or call the housing line at (619) 691-5047.

Source: https://www.chulavistaca.gov/departments/housing-and-homeless-services/homeowner-homebuyer
