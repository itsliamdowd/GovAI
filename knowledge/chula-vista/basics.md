## City Overview

The City of Chula Vista is the second-largest city in San Diego County, with a population of 276,000 and more than 52 square miles of coastal landscape, canyons, rolling hills, mountains, parks, and trails. "Chula Vista" means "beautiful view." City Hall is located at 276 Fourth Avenue, Chula Vista, CA 91910.

The city has more than 64 parks and 60 sports fields, and four golf courses. The Chula Vista Elite Athlete Training Center is a 155-acre U.S. Olympic and Para-Olympic training site owned by the City, one of only nine in the United States.

## City Government Structure — City Manager

Chula Vista operates under a "Council-Manager" form of government. The City Council appoints a City Manager, who is responsible for implementing all of the City Council's policy decisions and direction. The Administration Department, headed by the City Manager, includes one Assistant City Manager and two Deputy City Managers responsible for the administration of all affairs of the City, including day-to-day operations.

## City Attorney's Office — Role and Code Enforcement Support

The Office of the City Attorney serves as legal counsel to the City Council, all City Boards and Commissions, the City Manager, City Clerk and all City departments. It is made up of the Litigation and Advisory divisions and the Neighborhood Protection Unit. Marco Verdugo was sworn in as City Attorney on April 9, 2024.

For code enforcement, the City Attorney assists with enforcing City Code provisions related to building standards, zoning requirements, neighborhood preservation, and other conditions or conduct affecting public health, safety or welfare. Violations are addressed through administrative actions, civil actions and/or criminal prosecution. The City Attorney also obtains legal orders for the abatement and/or demolition of substandard properties.

The Neighborhood Protection Unit's City Prosecutor prosecutes infraction and misdemeanor violations of the City's Municipal Code Ordinances and some limited violations of State law. Most criminal prosecutions are handled by the San Diego County District Attorney.

## Elections and Voter Registration

The City Clerk conducts local elections. Chula Vista consolidates with statewide elections in March or June and November of each even numbered year. Councilmembers are elected by district (as of 2016); the Mayor and City Attorney are elected at large. The two candidates who receive the most votes in the March or June election participate in a runoff in November. If there are two or fewer candidates for any seat, the election for that seat is held in November. The term for each position is four years, with a limit of two consecutive terms.

Online voter registration is available. Registration forms are also available in the Office of the City Clerk and at all Chula Vista Public Library branches. Voter information, including polling locations, is on the Registrar of Voters' website.

Source: https://www.chulavistaca.gov/departments/city-clerk/elections

## Boards and Commissions — Eligibility and Applying

To serve as a voting member on most boards and commissions, you must be a resident of the City of Chula Vista and at least 18 years of age. City Council may appoint non-residents (City Charter Section 602(D)). Applicants are encouraged to apply even if no current vacancy exists, and eligible applications are kept for consideration for one year from the date received. Some boards require a resume with the application; the Planning Commission requires a resume and letter of reference. Rules are in the Municipal Code (Title 2, Chapter 2.25) and the City Charter.

Source: https://www.chulavistaca.gov/departments/city-clerk/boards-commissions

## Public Records Requests and Copy Fees

The City Clerk is the custodian of public records for the City. Many records can be viewed online to avoid fees, including City Council agenda packets, meeting minutes, resolutions, ordinances, and building permits 1992-current. Records not found online can be requested using the Records Request Form or the Public Records Request Center.

Direct copying fees: emailed records, no charge; paper copies of records, $1.00 for the 1st page and $0.10 each additional page; paper copies of State-mandated filings (statements of economic interests and campaign statements), $0.10 per page. Requests requiring computer programming are charged the full cost including overhead for the time to create the document or program, and the requester must provide a deposit to cover estimated costs, as calculated by City staff.

Source: https://www.chulavistaca.gov/departments/city-clerk/public-records

## Training Records for Local Officials

The City maintains records of required trainings completed by local officials and those in designated positions, in accordance with California Senate Bill 827 and Government Code section 53235.2. Records are retained for at least five (5) years and are available upon request. To request them, contact the City Clerk's Office at cityclerk@chulavistaca.gov or (619) 691-5041, or submit a request through the City's Public Records Request Center.

Source: https://www.chulavistaca.gov/departments/city-clerk/public-records

## FPPC Disclosure Filings

The City Clerk's Office is the filing officer for Fair Political Practices Commission (FPPC) disclosure forms under the Political Reform Act. Filings available for public review include Form 460 and other campaign disclosure statements, Form 700 (Statement of Economic Interests), Form 802 (Ticket Distribution Disclosure), Form 803 (Behested Payment Disclosures), and Form 806 (Agency Report of Public Official Appointments).

Source: https://www.chulavistaca.gov/departments/city-clerk/public-records/forms-filings

## Statements of Economic Interests (Form 700)

Form 700 filings are public records, and any member of the public may inspect and obtain copies. Filings can be viewed through the Public Access Portal, and electronic Form 700 filing is through the NetFile Filer Access Portal. City employees in designated positions must file Form 700 and comply with applicable ethics training requirements. Elected officials file directly with the California FPPC.

Copies of statements filed by elected officers (un-redacted statements and/or those filed prior to 2015) may be obtained from the FPPC, 428 J Street, Suite 620, Sacramento, CA 95814, or the Office of the City Clerk, 276 Fourth Avenue, Chula Vista, CA 91910.
