## Parks/Trees Division

The Parks/Trees Maintenance Division maintains all City parks, Civic Center grounds, landscaped medians, and all street and park trees. Responsibilities include maintaining turf in parks on a scheduled basis, regularly inspecting playground equipment, landscaping, irrigation, and park buildings and repairing as necessary, and removing graffiti from parks and public right-of-ways citywide. The division also maintains the tree trimming schedule, replaces parkway trees as needed, controls weeds in City right-of-way areas and City-owned properties, and administers city-wide tree trimming and landscape maintenance contracts.

Contact: (714) 895-2876 or publicworks@westminster-ca.gov.

Source: https://www.westminster-ca.gov/departments/public-works/maintenance-division/parks-trees

## Parkway Tree Planting Programs

The City offers two programs to help residents and business owners offset the cost of planting trees. In the first program, a City employee plants the tree the resident/business purchased. Send a check for $330.00 per tree and root barrier to: City of Westminster, Attention Veronica Johnson, 8200 Westminster Boulevard, Westminster, CA 92683. Include the completed and signed form. City staff will plant the tree, which takes approximately four to six weeks.

In the second program, the resident/business plants a designated tree from the provided list themselves. The City offers a Tree Voucher that includes the tree and root barrier. After filling out and signing the voucher, call Public Works Yard at (714) 895-2876 to schedule a time and date to visit the City Corporation Yard (14381 Olive St., Westminster, CA 92683). Bring the signed voucher to obtain paperwork for the free tree from a local nursery.

The City will keep the tree trimmed, but the homeowner or business owner is responsible for watering it. The municipal code related to parkway trees is 12.12.130.

Source: https://www.westminster-ca.gov/departments/public-works/maintenance-division/parks-trees

## Solid Waste, Sewer and Recycling Services

Solid waste and sewer services are provided by Midway City Sanitary District (MCSD). For information on services provided, including recycling, contact MCSD at (714) 893-3553. CR&R is MCSD's approved solid waste service provider for commercial customers; for information on services, contact CR&R at (714) 372-8272.

State mandates listed by the City:
- AB939 (California Integrated Waste Management Act): requires at least 50% of a jurisdiction's solid waste be diverted from landfills through waste management programs.
- AB341 (Mandatory Commercial Recycling): requires commercial properties generating 4CY or more of solid waste per week to have a recycling program.
- AB1826 (Mandatory Commercial Organic Recycling, MORe): requires commercial properties generating 2CY or more of solid waste per week to have an organic recycling program.
- SB1383 (Short-Lived Climate Pollutant Reduction): expands AB341 and AB1826 regulations to all waste generators, including single-family and multi-family residents, self-haulers, and the City, to reduce statewide disposal of organic waste by 75% by 2025.

Source: https://www.westminster-ca.gov/departments/public-works/recycling

## Mandatory Commercial Recycling (AB 341)

A business, including public entities, that generates four cubic yards or more of commercial solid waste per week, or is a multifamily residential dwelling of five units or more, shall arrange for recycling services. Businesses can take one or any combination of the following: self-haul to a recycling facility; subscribe to a recycling service with the City's commercial franchise hauler CR&R; and/or subscribe to a recycling service that may include mixed waste processing that yields diversion results comparable to source separation.

For additional information, contact Midway City Sanitary District at (714) 893-3553.

Source: https://www.westminster-ca.gov/departments/public-works/recycling/mcr-mandatory-commercial-recycling

## Water Billing and Late Fees

Water bills are issued every 8 weeks on Tuesdays, based on your zone. Rates are on a tiered structure. Bills are mailed out 28 days prior to the due date.

A late fee of $10 is added the day after the due date. An additional $15 hang tag fee is added if payment is not made by the date on your late notice. If payment is still not received, a $135 disconnection fee applies.

## Animal Bites — Reporting

If you have been bitten by an animal and there is a skin break, Animal Control may have to quarantine the animal. Dogs and cats are routinely subject to quarantine if they have caused a skin break with their teeth to a human. Animal owners who know their dog or cat has bitten another person are required to report the incident to Animal Control immediately.

Not all animals are subject to quarantine. Animal Control does not routinely examine low risk animals such as rabbits, rodents, hamsters, or guinea pigs.

To report an animal bite, complete the online form or call Animal Control at (714) 548-3201 and email animalcontrol@westminster-ca.gov.

Source: https://www.westminster-ca.gov/departments/police/how-do-i/report/animal-bites
