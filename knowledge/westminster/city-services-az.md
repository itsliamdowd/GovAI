## City Clerk's Office

The City Clerk's Office records and archives the actions of the City Council, Successor Agency to the Westminster Redevelopment Agency, Westminster Housing Authority and Westminster Public Financing Authority. It maintains and updates the Westminster Municipal Code, accepts claims and service of other legal documents, administers the City's contracts and city-wide records management program, and accepts statements of economic interest and campaign statements as the local filing officer. As the local elections official, it administers elections in consolidation with the County of Orange.

Office: (714) 548-3237 | Fax: (714) 379-2895. Email: CityClerkCSR@westminster-ca.gov. Interim City Clerk: Sandra Escudero. Records Coordinator: Daisy Guzman (dguzman@westminster-ca.gov).

## City Council Meetings — Public Comment

In person: complete a green speaker slip (agenda items) or a yellow speaker slip (non-agenda items) available at the entrance of the Council Chamber and submit it to City Clerk staff. When your name is called, step up to the podium and state your name and city of residence (both optional). Each speaker has three minutes.

In writing: email eComments@westminster-ca.gov, or mail or drop off during regular posted business hours to the City of Westminster, City Clerk's Office, 8200 Westminster Blvd., Westminster, CA 92683. Comments must be received at least 2 hours prior to the meeting to become part of the official meeting record and be provided to the City Council. Comments received by the deadline will not be read verbally at the meeting.

To request to speak in advance, email your request and name to eComments@westminster-ca.gov at least 2 hours before the meeting.

By Zoom: click "Raise Hand" and a staff member will prompt you to speak. By phone: dial the number and Meeting ID # on the first page of the Agenda, then dial *9 to "raise your hand" and *6 to unmute when prompted.

## City Council Meetings — Interpreters, Translation and Captions

Members of the public may request an interpreter in advance for the Oral Communications and Public Hearing sections of a meeting. Per Resolution No. 5135, a non-English speaking member of the public may bring their own interpreter or request that the City provide an interpreter in Vietnamese or Spanish. Requests must be made at least 48 hours in advance for a regular meeting, and at least 10 hours in advance for a special meeting. The City will make reasonable efforts to accommodate the request, though it is not legally obligated to provide an interpreter. Speakers who use a City-provided or personal interpreter are allowed twice the standard speaking time; speakers who self-translate into another language are not.

Agendas and the meeting webpage are available in applicable languages. To translate an agenda or meeting document, open it through the City's meeting archive and click Translate in the DocAccess right-hand panel. In Zoom, click the Show Captions icon (CC) for closed captions; to translate captions, click the up arrow next to the CC icon, enable Translation under Captions and translation, select a language, and click Save.
