## Traffic Engineering Division

The Traffic Engineering Division provides design and project management for transportation improvement projects such as Intelligent Transportation Systems, traffic signals, street lights, signing, and striping. It is responsible for traffic signal timing and for maintenance of all traffic control devices and signals, roadway signs, and curb markings (such as red curb and yellow curb). It also conducts engineering studies of traffic conditions and works with the Police Department and other agencies to improve traffic conditions in the City.

For information or inquiries, call the Traffic Engineering Division at 714-548-3457.

## Street Maintenance Division (Potholes, Curbs, Crosswalks, Signs)

The Street Maintenance Division maintains City streets in a safe, drivable condition. Its responsibilities include:
- Repairing potholes and other patching needs.
- Repainting crosswalks, legends and red curb, and replacing or repairing regulatory and warning signs.
- Repairing or replacing damaged concrete curbs, gutters and sidewalks to help alleviate drainage issues and eliminate height differentials.
- Maintaining the City's catch basins and storm drains.
- Conducting scheduled sweeping of the streets, arterial sidewalks, and City parking lots.

Contact: (714) 895-2876 or publicworks@westminster-ca.gov.

Source: https://www.westminster-ca.gov/departments/public-works/maintenance-division/street-maintenance-sweeping

## Street Sweeping Schedule and Holidays

Street sweeping of residential streets can occur anytime between the hours of 8 am and 1 pm. To find your street sweeping day, use the interactive street sweeping/trash pick up map (also available as a Street Sweeping Map PDF) and enter your address in the upper left hand corner.

No street sweeping on these observed City holidays: New Year's Day, President's Day, Memorial Day, Fourth of July, Labor day, Thanksgiving Day, Day after Thanksgiving, and Christmas Day. If your street sweeping day falls on an observed holiday, sweeping is skipped for that cycle and takes place on your next regularly scheduled day.

Source: https://www.westminster-ca.gov/departments/public-works/maintenance-division/street-maintenance-sweeping

## Police Traffic Unit and Parking Enforcement

The Traffic Division manages the investigation and documentation of traffic collisions and enforcement of laws related to safe driving. Parking enforcement officers maintain order by issuing parking citations and impounding vehicles. The Traffic Division also manages special events, which often require advanced planning for orderly control of traffic and parking.

Office Phone: (714) 548-3220. Traffic Division Sergeant: Sergeant J. Kent, JKent@westminster-ca.gov.

Source: https://www.westminster-ca.gov/departments/police/operations-division/traffic-parking-control

## Most Issued Parking Violations

The Police Department's top five parking violations, in descending order:
1. Street sweeping: vehicles not moved on sweeping days.
2. Expired vehicle registrations: vehicles are required by law to maintain current registration.
3. Parking too close to a fire hydrant, which is within 15 feet of either side of a fire hydrant.
4. Parked hazardously or on the wrong side of the road: if a vehicle's right side wheels are not both within 18" from the right curb, it is too far out into the lane of traffic.
5. Blocking driveways.

Parking citations are issued to keep city streets free of traffic hazards for both motor vehicles and pedestrians.

Source: https://www.westminster-ca.gov/departments/police/operations-division/traffic-parking-control
