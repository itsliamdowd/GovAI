## Police Area Command Service Model

On January 1, 2011, the Westminster Police Department implemented an Area Command service delivery model. The City is divided into two geographic areas, the East and West Command Areas. Each has one Police Lieutenant designated as Area Commander and at least one Police Service Officer.

The Area Commander is not a substitute for dialing 9-1-1 in an emergency or for calling the dispatch center for a routine request for police services. Maps and contact information for the Area Commanders are posted on the City website.

## Code Enforcement — How to File a Complaint

Complaints may be filed with the Code Enforcement Division in person, by mail, telephone and via email. You may give your name, address, and telephone number so the officer can contact you. Give the complete address of the problem location and identify the specific complaint or nuisance, including description, type of problem, and vehicle license number if applicable.

An officer will inspect the location as soon as possible. If a violation is observed, the property owner or tenant is given written notification to correct it within a specified period of time. If it is not corrected, further enforcement may follow, including fines and legal action.

To file a complaint, call (714) 548-3246, email codeenforcement@westminster-ca.gov, or use the online form. Many complaints can also be reported through the MyWestminster App.

## Signs on Public Right-of-Way, Front Lawn Paving, and Other FAQ Reports

No sign may be placed or maintained upon any portion of a public right-of-way, including street signs, street trees, or utility poles.

Front lawns may not be paved. Current City codes require that at least 50% of your front yard be landscaped with foliage, and the front lawn may not be used for off-street parking or for permanent storage of vehicles.

To report an illegal addition or garage conversion, a broken/damaged/falling fence, an illegal business run from home (such as Air BnB or auto repair), or an overgrown/bare lawn, contact the Code Enforcement Division at (714) 548-3246 or use the My Westminster mobile app.

## Landlord and Commercial Property Owner Requirements

In Westminster, landlords leasing more than 3 residential units (units do not have to be on the same property) must also obtain a business license. Contact the Business License Division at (714) 548-3254. The California Department of Consumer Affairs published "California Tenants: A Guide to Residential Tenants' and Landlords' Rights and Responsibilities" in 2012.

All commercial property owners are required to maintain their property, including paint, landscaped areas, parking areas, commercial center signage, and other structural improvements. Commercial parking areas are for light passenger vehicles, and no overnight parking or storage is permitted. All commercial property owners that lease to a tenant must obtain a business license; contact a Business License Technician at (714) 548-3258.

## Property Maintenance, Weeds, and Trash Cans

Broken or discarded furniture, appliances, vehicle parts, household items, trash, vegetation and landscape trimmings that constitute a public nuisance may not be stored on residential property. Trash cans may only be placed out for pick-up the evening before or on trash pick-up day and should be minimized from public view on all other days.

Failure to keep property free of rubbish, overgrown vegetation and weeds, or other hazardous material is a violation. If citations are not complied with, the City may take corrective action and place a lien on the property for reimbursement.

Source: https://www.westminster-ca.gov/departments/community-development/code-enforcement/codes-for-a-better-community

## Garage Sales

Garage and yard sales in residential zones are limited to three per residence, per calendar year, for up to three days each. Items sold are limited to household personal property and may not be items purchased for resale. A permit must be obtained from City Hall prior to the sale. Garage sale permits are $10. Garage sale signs may not be posted on public property, utility poles or light poles.

Source: https://www.westminster-ca.gov/departments/community-development/code-enforcement/codes-for-a-better-community

## Garage Conversions, Driveway Extensions, and Accessory Structures

Converting your garage to another use is illegal. Any alteration or installation that interferes with the required parking for your vehicles is prohibited.

Concrete driveway extensions that increase hard surface may require a planning or building permit and are not always allowed, depending on zoning, lot coverage, drainage, and easements. Installing concrete without proper approval may result in enforcement action, fines, and a requirement to remove the concrete and restore the area. Contact the Planning Division before pouring concrete. Driveway Extension Flyers are available in English, Vietnamese and Spanish (PDF).

Living in accessory structures such as workshops, storage sheds, garages, or enclosed patios is prohibited. Single family residential zoning limits each lot to one housing unit, and all structures require building permits. Recreational vehicles and trailers may not be used as living quarters unless they are in an approved mobile home park.

Source: https://www.westminster-ca.gov/departments/community-development/code-enforcement/codes-for-a-better-community

## Walls and Fences

Walls and fences have a maximum height limit of six feet, except in front yards, where the limit is three feet. A variance must be approved by the City prior to construction of overheight fences.

## Home Business Activity in Residential Areas

Commercial or industrial activities in residential neighborhoods, such as auto repair, storage of materials and equipment, deliveries, or parking for visiting clientele, may not take place on your property or in your home.

A Home Occupation Permit may be obtained from the City for a home office for certain types of businesses, such as word processing, that do not involve customers or employees coming to your home. The City's Planning Division can provide additional information.

## Vehicle Rules — Inoperable, Lawn Parking, Oversized

Inoperable, wrecked, junk or dismantled vehicles may be legally stored only in an enclosed garage.

Vehicles may be parked only on paved driveways, streets, or in garages, and may not obstruct the public sidewalk, street or alley.

Oversized vehicles are defined as any commercially registered vehicle weighing over 2,000 pounds. These vehicles may not be parked or stored in residential neighborhoods.

Source: https://www.westminster-ca.gov/departments/community-development/code-enforcement/codes-for-a-better-community

## Street Vending Enforcement

Street vending is legal in California under the Safe Sidewalk Vending Act (SB 946) and SB 972, but food safety regulations still apply. Code Enforcement works monthly with the Orange County Health Care Agency on routine enforcement sweeps. The Orange County Health Care Agency has primary jurisdiction over food vendors.

To report vendors potentially operating in violation of health or safety regulations, contact the Orange County Health Care Agency at 714-433-6000 or the Westminster Code Enforcement Division at 714-548-3478. Provide the date observed, approximate time(s), location, and type of vendor (food cart, merchandise, etc.).

Source: https://www.westminster-ca.gov/departments/community-development/code-enforcement/codes-for-a-better-community

## Animals

Dogs over the age of four months must have a dog license, which can be obtained at City Hall. A household may not possess more than three dogs or three cats at any one time. Animals must be leashed when outside of confined private property, and animal waste must be immediately removed and disposed of in a sanitary manner. For more information, contact the City's Animal Control Officer at (714) 548-3201.

## Leaf Blowers

In residential areas, leaf blower use is limited to 8:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m., Monday through Saturday. Only the residential occupant may use a leaf blower on Sundays and holidays, between 9:00 a.m. and 5:00 p.m. Only one leaf blower per residential address may be used at one time, and use is restricted to a maximum of fifteen minutes per day.

## Business Licenses, Food and Alcohol Sales

Anyone doing business in the City of Westminster must obtain a business license, including home occupations. Some types of businesses also require a Police Permit. The Business License Tax is based on gross receipts. Applications and information are available from the Business License staff.

Businesses involving food preparation and service must meet Health Department requirements. Those serving or selling alcoholic beverages must obtain permits through the Department of Alcoholic Beverage Control (ABC). ABC contact number is (714) 558-4101.

Source: https://www.westminster-ca.gov/departments/community-development/code-enforcement/commercial-violations

## Conditional Use Permits and Home Business Permits

Conditional Use Permits (CUP) are approved by the Planning Commission and can include special conditions such as hours of operation. Examples of businesses requiring a CUP are auto repair, video arcades, motels, and businesses serving alcohol and/or providing entertainment. Speak with the Planning Division about fees and the length of the approval process before signing a lease. A CUP stays with the business location.

A home business permit and business license may be obtained for certain home businesses that do not generate vehicular or pedestrian traffic, deliveries to the residence, employees and/or customers, or manufacturing of a product. Only one room in the home may be used. Applications are available through the Planning Division.

Source: https://www.westminster-ca.gov/departments/community-development/code-enforcement/commercial-violations
