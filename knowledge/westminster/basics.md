## History of Westminster — Key Facts

Westminster was founded by Reverend Lemuel P. Webber as the second Presbyterian temperance colony in Orange County, after Anaheim. Proceedings to form a municipality called Tri City, combining Westminster, Barber City, and Midway City, began in 1957. Midway City withdrew before the vote and remains unincorporated today. The proposal to incorporate Westminster and Barber City was approved by a vote of 1,096 to 1,008, and Westminster became California's 337th city in 1957.

In 1996 Westminster was designated an "All-America City" by the National Civic League. The Westminster Mall is the City's primary sales tax generator. Little Saigon, in the Westminster/Garden Grove area, had a 2010 population of 36,000 Vietnamese Americans.

Source: https://www.westminster-ca.gov/our-city/about-westminster/history

## Rose Center Theater

The Rose Center is located at 14140 All American Way, between the Sid Goldstein Freedom Park and Coastline Community College. It was built in 2006. The Rose Center Theater is a 17,286 square foot theater with 419 seats. Four musicals are produced in-house each year, and the stage is rented to independent theater troops, symphonies, musical groups, high schools, community organizations, and others.

The theater is operated by The Friends of the Rose Center Foundation, a non-profit community volunteer group organized in 1999. To learn more, buy tickets, or hold an event at the theater, go to www.rosecentertheater.com or call (714) 793 - 1150.

## City Council Meetings — Public Comment

In person: complete a green speaker slip (agenda items) or a yellow speaker slip (non-agenda items) available at the entrance of the Council Chamber and submit it to City Clerk staff. When your name is called, step up to the podium and state your name and city of residence (both optional). Each speaker has three minutes.

In writing: email eComments@westminster-ca.gov, or mail or drop off during regular posted business hours to the City of Westminster, City Clerk's Office, 8200 Westminster Blvd., Westminster, CA 92683. Comments must be received at least 2 hours prior to the meeting to become part of the official meeting record and be provided to the City Council. Comments received by the deadline will not be read verbally at the meeting.

To request to speak in advance, email your request and name to eComments@westminster-ca.gov at least 2 hours before the meeting. By Zoom, click "Raise Hand." By phone, dial *9 to raise your hand and *6 to unmute when prompted.

## City Council Meetings — Interpreter and Language Access

Members of the public may request an interpreter in advance for the Oral Communications and Public Hearing sections of a meeting. Under Resolution No. 5135, a non-English speaking member of the public may bring their own interpreter or request that the City provide an interpreter in Vietnamese or Spanish. Requests must be made at least 48 hours in advance for a regular meeting, and at least 10 hours in advance for a special meeting. The City will make reasonable efforts to accommodate the request, though it is not legally obligated to provide an interpreter.

Speakers who use a City-provided or personal interpreter are allowed twice the standard speaking time; speakers who self-translate into another language are not. Closed captioning and translated captions are available through the Zoom Webinar (Show Captions icon (CC)). Agendas and meeting documents opened through the City's meeting archive can be translated using the Translate button in the DocAccess viewer.

## Serving on a City Commission, Board, or Committee

Advisory bodies are the Citizens Oversight Committee (Measure Y), Cultural Arts Commission, Parks & Recreation Commission, Planning Commission, Personnel Board, Traffic Commission, and Youth Committee. Appointments are made for terms that generally expire every two even numbered years, coinciding with the General Municipal Election cycle, and to fill unscheduled vacancies. Applications are accepted year-round. Current vacancy notices are on the Public Notices page.

Unless otherwise noted, applicants must be a resident of Westminster. Applicants must provide a completed and signed application, a signed Code of Ethics, a copy of a driver's license or state-issued identification card, and a utility bill or other bill such as an insurance, credit card, rent, or other statement. Email documents to CityClerkCSR@westminster-ca.gov, or submit in person or by mail to City of Westminster - City Clerk's Office, 8200 Westminster Blvd., Westminster, CA 92683.

Residency is verified through the Orange County Registrar of Voters' voter registration records. If an applicant is not a registered voter, contact the City Clerk's Office at (714) 548-3237 for alternative verification options. Applications and Code of Ethics remain on file for one calendar year and are subject to public disclosure. Copies of driver's licenses/ID cards and supplemental documents such as bills and statements are exempt from public disclosure.

Source: https://www.westminster-ca.gov/departments/city-clerk/boards-commissions-committees

## Filing a Claim Against the City

Download and complete the Claim Against the City Form. Submit it in person or by mail to City of Westminster - City Clerk's Office, 8200 Westminster Blvd., Westminster, CA 92683. No electronic claims will be accepted.

Under Government Code Section 911.2, claims for death, injury to person, or to personal property must be filed no later than six (6) months after the occurrence. Claims for damages to real property must be filed not later than one year after the occurrence. No employee of the city may give legal advice to any claimant relating to private claims. Claimants are advised to consult their private attorney.

## Public Records Requests

Under the California Public Records Act (PRA), the public has the right to inspect, and be provided a copy of, most written information retained by State and local agencies. Public records are open to inspection at all times during the office hours of a local agency. A local agency is not required to create a document or compile a list in response to a request.

To request city records, fill out the online request form. Once it is completed, the City Clerk's Office will begin to process your request. For police records, contact Westminster Police Department - Records Manager, 8200 Westminster Blvd., Building 5, Westminster, CA 92683.

Source: https://www.westminster-ca.gov/departments/city-clerk/public-records-requests
