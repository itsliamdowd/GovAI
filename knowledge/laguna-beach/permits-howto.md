## Alarm Permits

If you have a burglary or alarm system for your home or business in Laguna Beach, you must obtain an alarm permit from the City through its alarm program administrator, Alarm Program Systems (APS). Each permit is valid for two years. The fee is $65 for a new residential or business alarm system, and renewals are $45. To apply and register online, use the Alarm Program Website. For help getting registered, call the help desk at (888) 865-9770.

False alarm penalties:
- Three false burglary alarms in 12 months with permit (per false alarm): $175
- More than six false burglary alarms in 12 months with permit (per false alarm): $350
- Burglary false alarm without a permit: $175
- Robbery false alarm regardless of permit: $265

There is a 10% charge per month for all unpaid balances, up to a maximum of 100%. For questions about alarm permits or fees, contact Alarm Program Systems at (888) 865-9770.

Source: https://www.lagunabeachcity.net/government/departments/police/how-do-i/alarm-permits

## Business License — How to Apply

Doing business in Laguna Beach requires a City business license. All business license applications must have a physical address listed. The City will not issue business licenses to virtual mailbox locations or P.O. Boxes. To apply online, visit https://LagunaBeach.HdLGov.com

Business Licenses contact: (949) 393-4645, LagunaBeach@HdLGov.com. City Hall hours are Monday – Thursday 7:30 a.m. to 5:30 p.m. and Alternate Fridays 7:30 a.m. to 4:30 p.m. Front Desk/Cashier hours are Monday – Friday 7:30 a.m. to 4:00 p.m. and Alternate Fridays 7:30 a.m. to 4:00 p.m. City Hall is closed alternate Fridays.
