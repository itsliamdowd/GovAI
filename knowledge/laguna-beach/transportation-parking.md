## Parking — How to Pay and Enforcement Hours

Laguna Beach's parking meters and pay stations take credit and debit cards in addition to coins (meters) and bills (pay stations). You can also pay with your mobile phone using the Laguna Beach Parking App, or by scanning the QR code on the meter and selecting the one-time payment option. To report a broken parking meter, call the police non-emergency line at (949) 497-0701.

Parking enforcement hours are from 8:00am - 9:00pm.

## Disabled Placards & Plates

Vehicles displaying a valid disabled parking placard or plate may park in any on-street metered parking space, or any space in a City-operated parking lot, without paying. Vehicles must still obey sign and curb markings and can park for no more than 72 hours.

## RV Parking and RV Parking Permits

RVs may be parked on City streets for up to five hours in a 24-hour period. The Laguna Beach Police Department issues RV Parking Permits, which allow parking for up to 72 hours on City streets.

To obtain the permit, visit the Police Department during normal business hours (8am to 5pm, 7 days a week). Provide your Driver's License, a description of the RV, the license plate and registration, and the location where the RV will be parked. Direct other questions to the Police Department non-emergency number: (949) 497-0701.

Source: https://www.lagunabeachcity.net/live-here/parking-and-transportation/parking

## Bus & Charter Parking

Bus and charter parking is only available on sanctioned spots on Laguna Canyon Road. The spots are marked by bagged meters in front of the tennis courts next to Festival of Arts. The closest location to the bagged meters is 650 Laguna Canyon Rd, Laguna Beach, CA 92651.

Parking is NOT available at any open meters not marked by bags on Laguna Canyon Road, along the Pacific Coast Highway, or in any lots throughout the city including Lot 16. Bus and charter parking cannot be reserved in advance and is first come, first serve.

## Rent Meter Bags

Meter bags cover parking meters and authorize the towing of any vehicle parked in front of a bagged meter unless authorized. Authorized uses are construction, moving vans and PODS, tour buses, film shoots, and special events. For film shoots, you must first get a permit from the Transit & Community Services Department's Recreation Division, then you may apply for meter bags.

Cost is $25 per day/per meter, Monday through Friday. The weekend rate is $125 per meter, which includes bagging from approximately 3:00 p.m. on Friday to approximately 6:00 a.m. on Monday.

The Transit & Community Services Department will place your bag between 6:00 a.m. and 7:00 a.m. If the targeted space is occupied, the meter bag will be placed at the nearest available space. Bags are typically removed between 6:00 a.m. and 7:00 a.m. the morning after their scheduled date has expired.

Contacts: Parking Lot Operations (949) 464-6647; Meter Bag Application (949) 464-6645; Parking Tickets and Citations (949) 497-0355. Hours are Monday – Thursday 7:30 a.m. to 5:30 p.m. and alternate Fridays 7:30 a.m. to 4:30 p.m.

## Trolley — Coastal Route

Non-summer season trolley routes run Sept. 8, 2026 - June 25, 2027. The Coastal Route is free trolley service on Coast Highway between North Laguna/Heisler Park, downtown, South Laguna/Mission Hospital and Ritz Carlton in Dana Point.

Hours: Monday-Thursday 7:30 a.m. to 6 p.m.; Friday 7:30 a.m. to 10 p.m.; Saturday 9 a.m. to 10 p.m.; Sunday 9 a.m. to 7 p.m. Frequency is every 20-30 minutes. Track trolley status in real time with the Laguna Beach Trolley app.

## Laguna Local On-Demand Transit

The City offers a free, on-demand, shared-ride transit service called Laguna Local between residential neighborhoods and major activity centers within the City. Non-summer service hours: Monday-Thursday 8 a.m. to 6 p.m.; Friday 8 a.m. to 10 p.m.; Saturday 9 a.m. to 10 p.m.; Sunday 9 a.m. to 7p.m.

Book rides with the Laguna Beach Transit app (iOS or Google Play), at https://lagunabeach.tripshot.com/, or by calling Transit dispatch at (949) 497-0766 during operating hours. Laguna Local must temporarily suspend the ability for passengers to transport bicycles. For questions, email lagunabeachlocal@lagunabeachcity.net.

## Senior Transportation — Sally's Fund and Age Well Senior Services

The Laguna Beach Trolley provides service throughout the city and all trolleys are wheelchair-accessible.

Sally's Fund provides free transportation to and from the Laguna Beach Community and Susi Q Center. Residents 60 years plus can schedule a ride to attend the lunch program or classes Monday – Friday from 8:30 a.m. – 5:00 p.m. Transportation should be scheduled in advance and is first-come, first-serve. Sally's Fund: (949) 499-4100.

Age Well Senior Services provides free, non-emergency medical transportation for Laguna Beach residents at least 60 years old to Mission Hospital in Laguna Beach and Mission Hospital in Mission Viejo. It also provides non-emergency medical transportation to locations outside Laguna Beach in Irvine, Newport Beach, and San Clemente, a maximum distance of 15 miles from residence. Age Well Senior Services: (949) 855-8033.

## Senior Transportation — OCTA and OCTA Access

Seniors receive a discount on fares for the fixed-route bus routes operated by the Orange County Transportation Authority (OCTA). Contact OCTA for rate information and to purchase bus passes online: (949) 560-5660.

A person is eligible for OCTA ACCESS service if they are unable to board or exit a fixed-route bus, get to or from a bus stop due to physical and/or environmental barriers, or do not understand how to ride the bus. Eligibility is based on a person's functional abilities and limitations due to a disability, not a specific diagnosis or disability. OCTA has a formal certification process that follows ADA guidelines. Call the ACCESS Eligibility Office or download the Access Service Application. OCTA Access Service: (949) 560-5956. Transit Division/Dispatch: (949) 497-0766.
