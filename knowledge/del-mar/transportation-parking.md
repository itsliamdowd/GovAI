## Parking Enforcement — Hours and Responsibilities

The City's Parking Division enforces City, County of San Diego, and State of California regulations. Parking officers patrol City streets seven days per week, year-round. They give information to the public, give warnings as warranted, and write citations when necessary. Parking Officers, along with the City's Beach and Community Patrol Officers, also enforce animal regulations and report violations of other code infractions.

Parking and enforcement staff work daily, year-round, with operating hours 8:00 am – 8:00 pm. Staff work extended patrol hours until 10:00 pm on Fridays and Saturdays, with later evening patrol hours between Memorial Day and Labor Day.

## Downtown Del Mar and Civic Center Parking

In Downtown Del Mar, time limits are posted and enforced every day. No-limit free parking can be found on public streets close to the shopping area.

The Del Mar Civic Center parking garage is at 1050 Camino del Mar (enter off of 11th or 10th streets). Cost: free all day, every day. 72-hour limit.

## Voluntary Pre-Paid Parking Permit

Cost: annual only, $827. Not transferrable. The permit doubles posted time in 10 minute, 20 minute, 90 minute and 2 hour zones. It applies to all City meters and pay machines.

Limits and exclusions:
- Cannot park more than 72 hours on any City street or parking lot.
- Not valid in the L'Auberge permit parking area.
- Excludes all green curbs, red curbs, fire hydrants, bike lanes, loading zones, posted No Parking zones and handicapped spaces.
- Not valid at Seagrove Lot and/or Maiden Lane.
- $26 replacement fee.

All permits require a signed application on file with the Finance Department.

## Bike and E-Bike Rules

Cyclists are subject to the same rules of the road as motorists. Riders who don't follow the rules may be ticketed and fined under the California Vehicle Code (CVC) or Del Mar Municipal Code (DMMC). The DMMC allows first-time violators to take a bicycle training safety course in lieu of a ticket and/or fine.

Key guidelines:
- Cyclists under 18 years old must wear a helmet and wear it properly.
- Traffic lights, signs, and signals apply to all road users, including bicycle and e-bike riders.
- Cyclists must ride in the bicycle lane when it is available. You can leave the bike lane to turn left, pass someone, avoid a road hazard or avoid cars turning right.
- Yield to pedestrians. Do not weave in and out of traffic. Ride in the same direction as car traffic.
- It is illegal to carry passengers on your e-bike unless your bike has an extra permanent seat or when using a child safety seat.
- Do not attach a bicycle or e-bike to any moving vehicle with an operator or rider on board.
- In Del Mar, it is illegal to ride bicycles or e-bikes in parks, preserves, sidewalks in commercial areas, and on most trails around the San Dieguito Lagoon (see DMMC 14.54.040 for the complete list of restrictions).

An e-bike is an electric bicycle equipped with fully operable pedals and an electric motor of less than 750 watts. California designates three classes of electric bicycles, and the class tells you where and how you can ride.
