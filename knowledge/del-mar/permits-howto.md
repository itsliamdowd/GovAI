## Tent/Canopy Operational Permits

An operational permit is required to operate an air-supported temporary membrane structure, a temporary special-event structure, or a tent with an area of more than 400 square feet. Multiple tents placed close together may be considered one structure. Approved permits are not transferable to another location. A structure shall not be used for more than 180 days within 12 months on a single premise. Apply at least 10 business days before the event.

The application package consists of:
1. A completed Tent Permit Application.
2. A Certificate of Flame Resistance for the tent, walls, and any decorations used within the tent.
3. A scaled or fully dimensioned site plan showing the nearest street or cross street, the location of the tent(s) with measured setbacks to buildings, vegetation and parked vehicles, fire lanes, fire hydrants and fire protection equipment, generators and all power sources (minimum of 20 feet from tents), and the dimensions of each tent.
4. A detailed floor plan with the intended use, occupant load per tent, sidewalls, exits (tents with more than 10 people require two exits), and exit signs. Illuminated exit signs and fixtures are required when a tent is capable of 50 or more occupants. The plan must also show fire extinguisher(s) (minimum size 2-A:10-B:C with current service tag), "No Smoking" signs, all items in the tent, any cooking or food-warming equipment, and the method of anchoring/weighting/securing the tent. No open flame devices are allowed in tents or canopies without fire department approval. Heaters must be approved before installation.
5. The Tent Permit Fee: $194 ($67.00 application/$127.00 Inspection), paid upon application approval.
6. Email the completed permit to fireprevention@cityofsolanabeach.ca.gov. Questions? Call (858) 720-2415.

An inspection is required when directed by the Fire Prevention Division. The tent must be fully installed and ready for use before the inspection. Cooking and propane require prior approval, and additional separation, fire extinguishers, and safety measures may be required.

## ADU and JrADU Permits — How to Apply

State law requires a streamlined, ministerial review process for proposed ADUs and JrADUs that exempts all applications meeting each of the specified objective review standards from discretionary permit review, including design review. Applicants fill out an ADU Application Packet (Fillable PDF), pay the associated fees (PDF), and submit development plan sets. Plans that meet the requirements in Del Mar Municipal Code Chapter 30.91 will be approved within 60 days of a complete application submittal. If the ADU or JrADU application is processed at the same time as a permit application for a new primary dwelling unit on the lot, the City may delay acting on it until the City acts on the primary dwelling unit application.

Contact Planning & Community Development at City Hall, 1050 Camino del Mar, Del Mar, CA 92014; 858-755-9313; planning@delmar.ca.us.

Source: https://www.delmar.ca.us/814/ADU-Incentive-Program

## ADU Rules — Number and Size of Units

The City's ADU regulations are in DMMC Chapter 30.91 (June 2025 version). The adopted regulations became effective on June 12, 2025, the date the California Coastal Commission granted final certification approval. ADUs are allowed in all of the City's residential zones and the Carmel Valley Precise Plan Specific Plan (CVPP).

A lot with a single dwelling unit may have a maximum of one ADU and one JrADU. The JrADU must be within the walls of the primary dwelling unit and is a maximum of 500 square feet. It needs a separate entrance, an efficiency kitchen, and access to a bathroom. An existing duplex may have a maximum of two ADUs per lot. State-law size maximums cited are 850 square feet, or 1,000 square feet for an ADU with more than one bedroom. An attached ADU is limited to a maximum of 50% of the size of the existing primary dwelling unit. Prospective applicants are encouraged to contact the Planning Department for clarification.

Source: https://www.delmar.ca.us/642/Accessory-Dwelling-Units-ADUs

## ADU Amnesty Program (Legalizing Unpermitted Units)

The ADU Amnesty Program is in effect through December 4, 2025. It provides incentives to property owners to obtain permits to legalize accessory dwelling units built without permits. Owners should contact City staff for information. The legalization process involves obtaining an Administrative Coastal Development Permit and a Building Permit, and recording a deed restriction with the County Recorder requiring the unit to be used as a dwelling unit. Options are available for partial or full reimbursement of permit fees, plan preparation costs, and/or code enforcement fines. Details are in City Council Resolution 2023-48 and the City Council Policy (PDF).

Source: https://www.delmar.ca.us/814/ADU-Incentive-Program

## ADU Incentive Program (Affordable Rental ADUs)

The ADU Incentive Program seeks owners willing to dedicate affordable dwelling units, made available for rent to low income households for at least 30 years, in exchange for incentives. A contract is required for participation (a template contract is available as a PDF). Unless the ADU or JrADU already has an ADU permit, applicants must submit an ADU Application Packet, pay fees, and submit development plan sets. Plans meeting DMMC Chapter 30.91 are approved within 60 days of a complete application submittal.

## Fire Hazard Zones, Building Requirements, and Defensible Space Inspections

The updated 2025 Fire Hazard Severity Zone layer is available on the City's Public Map Viewer to check which zone applies to your property. Any new construction in Very High and High Fire Hazard Severity Zones must comply with Chapter 7A of the California Building Code (wildfire resistant construction standards) and Government Code Sections 51175–51189. The City adopts the California Building Standards by reference in DMMC Chapter 23.12, and fire codes are reflected in DMMC Chapter 10.04.

Annual Wildland-Urban Interface (WUI) Program Inspections (Weed Abatement) are conducted by the Fire Department. Residents can voluntarily schedule an inspection at weedabatement@delmar.ca.us or 858-755-1522.

As of July 1, 2021, when you sell a property in a high or very high fire hazard severity zone, you need documentation of a compliant Defensible Space Inspection under Section 4291 of the Public Resources Code. This is separate from the annual weed abatement inspection and is coordinated through the City's Fire Marshal. To schedule, call (858) 720-2415 or email Del Mar Fire Prevention.
