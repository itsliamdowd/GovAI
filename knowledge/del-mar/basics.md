## About Del Mar

Del Mar was incorporated in 1959. It is a seaside village about 20 miles north of San Diego, with a population of approximately 4,200 people, covering 2.2 square miles. It draws upwards of 2 million visitors annually. The community is primarily single-family residential neighborhoods, with retail and restaurants downtown, a small commercial area, and several hotels.

The City employs approximately 60 full-time employees and 70 part-time, seasonal employees. They work out of the Civic Center, Lifeguard Headquarters, Del Mar Fire Station, and the Public Works yard and administration building. Law enforcement is provided through a contract with the San Diego County Sheriff's Department. Fire administration is provided through a mutual agreement between the cities of Del Mar, Encinitas, and Solana Beach.

On February 27, 2021, at a Special Council meeting, the City Council adopted this vision statement: "The vision for the City of Del Mar is to be a residential community with a vibrant downtown and a tranquil place of natural beauty where residents and visitors feel safe and enjoy opportunities for recreation and quality of life."

## City Council — Members, Role and Meetings

Members of the City Council are Tracy Martinez (Mayor), John W Spelich (Deputy Mayor), Terry Gaasterland (Councilmember), and Dan Quirk (Councilmember). Council members are elected by the voters of Del Mar, and elections are held in November of even-numbered years. To run, a candidate must be a resident and registered voter in the City of Del Mar and be nominated by no less than 20 registered voters of the City of Del Mar. Effective January 1, 2025, Councilmembers receive $950 per month and the Mayor receives $1,000 per month.

The City Council sets City policy, determines budget priorities, allocates City resources, and hears appeals from decisions of the Design Review Board and Planning Commission. It appoints the City Manager to manage day-to-day operations and the City Attorney to provide legal advice.

Council meetings are held on the first and third Tuesdays of the month at 4:30 p.m. in Council Chambers, 1050 Camino Del Mar, Del Mar, CA 92014. Refer to the City Council meeting schedule (PDF) for meeting dates.

## Watching Meetings Live and On-Demand

City Council, Design Review Board, and Planning Commission meetings are streamed live on the web on the scheduled meeting date. The City Council stream starts at 4:30 PM, and the Design Review Board and Planning Commission streams start at 6 PM. The meetings are also available on demand. The live-stream links are for viewing only; to make a public comment, use the City's Public Comment webpage.

On cable, watch live beginning at 4:30 p.m. on Spectrum TV Ch. 24 (AT&T Channel 99). Recorded meetings air on the same channel beginning either at 9 a.m. on the day following the meeting (typically Tuesdays) or at 6 p.m. two days following the meeting (usually Wednesdays). Additional programming is available on Del Mar TV, and Del Mar TV programs are on Vimeo.

## City Advisory Committees and Volunteering

The City has volunteer committees that advise the City Council, as well as a Planning Commission and a Design Review Board. The clerk keeps a current list of citizen advisory committees, their vacancies, committee members, and regular meeting dates. To volunteer, complete the citizen interest form (PDF) and see the Volunteer Opportunities section of the website.

Under the Maddy Act (Government Code Section 54970), the Local Appointments List is posted annually by December 31. It shows all current appointed members of ongoing city boards, commissions, and committees and their terms of office.

The City Council adopted Standard Operating Principles for advisory boards and committees on February 3, 2014, and reviewed and updated them on January 10, 2022. The Conflict of Interest Code requires members of the Finance Committee, Design Review Board, Planning Commission and City Council to file a Form 700 Financial Conflict of Interest filing upon assuming office, annually, and upon leaving office.

## Council Statements of Economic Interests (Form 700)

City Council members must disclose financial interests on FPPC Form 700, "Statement of Economic Interests," and file annually. Del Mar incorporates the FPPC requirements in Del Mar Municipal Code Chapter 2.12. The statements are available for public review upon request by emailing cityclerk@delmar.ca.us. The form can be obtained from the Fair Political Practices Commission website or from the City Clerk's Office at 1050 Camino del Mar, Del Mar, CA.

## City Budget and Finance Department

The City operates on a July 1 to June 30 fiscal year. It adopts a biennial budget in June of odd-numbered calendar years, with a budget update for year two in even-numbered years. The Fiscal Year 2025-2026 and 2026-2027 Operating and Capital Budget was adopted on June 16, 2025. The Budget & Financial Reports page links to the adopted budgets, Annual Comprehensive Financial Reports (ACFR), financial reports, and Quarterly Treasurer's Reports.
