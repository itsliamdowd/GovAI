## Building Permits — When One Is Required

With limited exception, a Building Permit is required anytime you are constructing, altering, moving, adding to, repairing, or demolishing a structure within the City of Poway. It is required for work inside or outside any structure. The City's Building Division provides plan check, permitting, and inspection, and the City contracts with EsGil Corporation to provide these services.

For questions about building permits, call the Building Division at 858-668-4644. Include your name, address and daytime phone number, and give as much information as possible about your question or request. For new construction, expansion of an existing structure, modifications to commercial properties, or moving your business, refer to the Development Services page.

## Building Permit — How to Apply

All permit applications are received, processed and issued through City of Poway Online Services. Effective June 1, 2022, only electronic submittals are accepted. Complete and submit a City of Poway Building Permit Application to City of Poway Online Services.

Include these with the application: Plot Plan, Foundation Plan(s), Floor Plan(s), Electrical, Plumbing, Mechanical, Roof Plan, Building Elevations, Soils Report (if required), Compaction Report (if required), Framing Plan / Cross Sections, Structural plans / details / calculations, Truss Drawings, Energy Compliance, and Schedules. More detail is in the City information sheet "Building Permit Plans & Requirements" and at the Building Division Counter. Samples of plans are in the information sheet "Sample Plans for Typical Single-Story Dwelling."

A qualified individual, such as a drafter, building designer, architect or engineer, needs to prepare plans for a new residence, home addition, or home remodel. Some commercial projects require plans prepared by an architect or structural engineer. For projects designed per city standards, such as retaining walls, patio covers and second-story decks, a simple, legible and scaled plot plan is all that is needed.

## Building Permit — Plan Review Steps and Timing

The actual time to process a permit varies with the nature of the project.
- Step 1: Submit the building permit application online (review the Building Permit Application Checklist first).
- Step 2: Development Services reviews the plans. For a 1st submittal, plan for up to 10-12 business days for building reviews and up to 28 calendar days for planning and engineering reviews.
- Step 3: The applicant is notified once plans are reviewed. If there are no revisions, a permit is issued. If revisions are required, continue to Step 4.
- Step 4: The applicant addresses the requested revisions.
- Step 5: The applicant resubmits online for plan review.
- Step 6: For subsequent submittals, the city completes review in up to 10-12 business days for building reviews and up to 28 calendar days for planning and engineering reviews.
- Step 7: The applicant is notified. If there are no revisions, a permit is issued. If revisions are required, return to Step 4.

## Building Inspections — Scheduling

After a Building Permit is obtained, schedule an inspection by calling 858-668-4646 and leaving a message on the automated line, or schedule online through the portal. For requests received before 4 p.m., the inspection is scheduled for the next business day unless you request a different day in your message. Building inspections are performed Monday through Friday, including the Fridays that City Hall is closed. The number of inspections varies by project but is generally 5 to 6 for residential projects.

## Building Permit Exemptions — Residential Projects

The City lists these residential projects as exempt from a Building Permit:
- One-story, detached accessory structures such as tool and storage sheds and playhouses, with a floor area of 120 square feet or less, unless in the Very High Fire Severity Zone. They cannot be within the front or street side yard setback areas, and the eaves cannot overhang adjacent properties.
- Fences and non-retaining walls not more than 6 feet tall. Solid fences and walls over 4 feet in height cannot be located within the front yard setback area.
- Kitchen counters and cabinets.
- Retaining walls 3 feet in height or less measured from the top of the footing with no surcharge.
- Water tanks supported on grade that do not exceed 5,000 gallons.
- Painting, papering and similar finish work.
- Prefabricated swimming pools less than 24-inches deep.
- Window awnings supported by an exterior wall that do not project more than 54-inches from the exterior walls and do not require extra support.
- Decks not exceeding 200-square-feet that are not more than 30-inches above grade at any point, are not attached to a dwelling and do not serve the exit door required by Section R311.4.

Flatwork (concrete slabs) on private property does not generally need a Building Permit but may need a Right-of-Way Permit from the Engineering Division. Parking and driveways in the front yard area cannot constitute more than 50% of the front yard setback area. Lighted sports courts require a minor conditional use permit and building permit.

## Green Building Standards and Construction Waste Management Plan

All new construction projects that apply for a building permit must comply with the California Green Building Standards Code. The Code also requires recycling / reuse of construction debris for any "new construction." All Building Permit Applicants for new construction must submit and obtain approval of a completed City of Poway Construction Waste Management Plan (CWMP). Submittal, approval, and implementation are coordinated between the applicant, the City of Poway, and the city's franchise hauler, EDCO Waste & Recycling Services. Applicants, including Contractors and Subcontractors, must include this for any new construction subject to the Code.

For commercial construction building permits, the City advises engaging a Certified Access Specialist (CASP) to inspect the proposed business location for accessibility to individuals with disabilities. ## Rooftop Solar PV, Solar Water Heating and Solar Pool Heating Permits

Under AB 2188 and Poway Municipal Code, Chapter 15.26, qualifying rooftop solar photovoltaic (PV), solar water heating (WH) and solar pool heating (PH) installations that use the City's Permit Application, Checklists and Plan Information Bulletins are expedited for plan review and inspections.

To apply, read the handouts to see whether your system is eligible for expedited review. These include the Eligibility Checklist, Submittal Requirements, Standard Plans, Structural Criteria and Inspection Guide. If it qualifies, submit all required documents through City of Poway Online Services. Payment is due at the time of permit issuance. Only electronic submittals are accepted. For questions on the guidelines, call the Building Division at 858-668-4645.

## Grading Permits — When Required

A grading permit is required in advance of grading for all projects including:
- Excavations 2-feet or greater with slopes steeper than 2:1
- Moving 50 cubic yards or more
- Changing drainage patterns
- Work within open space or easement areas

Retaining walls, pools and patio covers require Building Permits. Projects that clear vegetation or are within an archeological sensitive area shown on the City GIS map require an Administrative Clearing Permit.

Source: https://poway.org/1163/Grading-Permits

## Grading Permits — How to Apply, Timing and Requirements

You need to hire a qualified professional engineer to prepare a grading plan. See the Grading Submittal Checklist for requirements. All applications are received through the City's Online Services, and only electronic submittals are accepted. The cost of plan review is based on the Master Fee Schedule.

The first plan review is typically completed in 4 weeks, with subsequent reviews ranging from 2 to 4 weeks. Generally, three review cycles are required. Once grading plans are approved, securities must be posted and inspection fees paid as shown on the Master Fee Schedule. Prior to grading, provide the print copies, then schedule a preconstruction meeting. For questions, call the Land Development Engineering Division at 858-668-4655.

Source: https://poway.org/1163/Grading-Permits

## Grading Permit Exemptions

On a developed parcel, excavation or fill for minor landscaping improvements or recreational purposes may be done without a permit if all of these conditions are met:
- It is 10 feet away from any structure and three feet from any property line, unless entirely contained by a permitted retaining wall.
- It does not exceed 250 cubic yards of total excavation and fill.
- It does not exceed three vertical feet in depth.
- It does not create a cut or fill slope greater than five feet in height or a slope steeper than three horizontal to one vertical (3:1).
- It does not divert, concentrate or otherwise alter surface or subsurface drainage as it leaves or enters adjacent properties.
- It is outside any open space, utility, access or other easement area, and not in an area where any structure is planned, including patios, swimming pools and accessory structures.

## Planning Permits and Zoning Information

All planning applications are received through the City's online portal. Staff evaluate projects to confirm the type of permit required. As of July 1, 2024, Minor Development Review Applications are no longer required for single family homes.

Planning permit types listed include Tree Removal Permit - Private, Administrative Clearing Permit, Landscape Plans, Block Parties, Special Event TUPs, Conditional Use Permits, Minor Conditional Use Permits, Minor Development Review, Development Review Application, Development Review - South Poway Business Park, SB-9 Developments and Urban Lot Splits, and Tentative Maps. Sign permits are governed by Chapter 17.40 of the Poway Municipal Code. A Sign Permit Application and a Temporary Sign Permit Application are available, each with FAQs.

To look up zoning, use Poway's GIS site. Click the "Search" tab, enter your address, then the search icon. Click the "Community" tab and expand "Planning Information." Allowable uses, setbacks and design standards are in PMC 17.08 (Residential) and PMC 17.10 (Commercial).

Planning Division: 13325 Civic Center Drive, 1st Floor, Poway, CA 92064. Phone: 858-668-4610. Hours: Monday - Thursday 7:30 AM - 5:30 PM, Friday 8:00 AM - 5:00 PM, closed every other Friday.

## Master Fee Schedule — Permit and User Fee Updates

City Council adopted the Master Fee Schedule on December 11, 2007. It is available as a PDF download. Resolution 26-016 (May 19, 2026) amended the Master Fee Schedule. Adjustments are limited to the lower of the CPI percentage change or five percent (5%), and updated fees are rounded to the nearest whole dollar.
