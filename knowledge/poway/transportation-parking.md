## Traffic Safety Committee

The City's Traffic Safety Committee (TSC) consists of six members established by the City Manager. Members typically include the Director of Public Works, Fire Chief, City Engineer, City's Traffic Consultant, Sheriff's Poway Station Traffic Sergeant, and a representative of the Poway Unified School District.

The TSC meets to discuss traffic safety issues raised by residents or City staff, and makes recommendations to the City Council. Three meetings are scheduled each year and open to the public. Meetings are subject to cancellation if there is no item on the agenda. The public is welcome to address the committee during Public Comment regarding any traffic-related issues not on the agenda, however the committee will not be able to discuss these items during the meeting. The TSC may direct staff to follow up with the concerned citizen after the meeting. To get notifications, select "Committee – Traffic Safety" in the calendar section. To report a traffic issue, submit a Service Request.

## Lake Poway Parking and Entry Fees

There is a non-resident parking fee at Lake Poway weekends and holidays only. Poway residents are free with valid proof of residency and may obtain a resident sticker at the Lake Poway entry station or office. The parking fee is collected from 6 a.m. to 30 minutes before sunset. Those in possession of a valid fishing permit are not required to pay. Active military personnel, with ID, are free. The overflow lot located at the bottom of Lake Poway Road is temporarily closed.

Entry fees: Poway Resident No Charge; Non-Resident $10; Military with ID No Charge. The entry station is open weekends and holidays year round, 6:00 AM - 30 minutes before sunset.

Lake Poway is at 14644 Lake Poway Road, Poway, CA 92064. Lake Poway Office: 858-668-4772, Fax: 858-668-1231. Park hours are daily 6:00 AM - Sunset.

## Traffic Engineering Division

The primary responsibility of the City's Traffic Engineering Division is to resolve transportation and traffic-related issues in the City. The division is responsible for the operation and maintenance of the City's computerized traffic signal system to ensure optimal timing of the City's 56 traffic signals. It also processes traffic control plans for all construction activities within the right-of-way, provides roadway signing/striping design parameters, and responds to traffic-related public complaints.

To contact Traffic Engineering, call 858-668-4640, or email. Please include your name, address, and daytime phone number, and provide as much information as possible relating to your question, complaint, or concern. You may also submit a service request. Traffic Engineering is at City Hall, 13325 Civic Center Drive, Poway, CA 92064. Hours: Monday - Thursday 7:30 AM - 5:30 PM, Friday 8:00 AM - 5:00 PM, closed every other Friday.

## Speed Feedback Sign Requests

Speed Feedback Signs are a common request for higher volume streets with speeding concerns. The City has established criteria for installation, and not all locations will be appropriate. Residents can submit a Speed Feedback Sign Request Form (PDF) for a new permanent sign. Once the request form and the required petition (Petition for Speed Feedback Sign, PDF) are received, staff will complete an engineering evaluation and present findings to the Traffic Safety Committee. Installations recommended by the Traffic Safety Committee must be approved by the City Council and are subject to funding availability.

The Poway Sheriff's Department has a portable speed feedback sign that is placed in various locations throughout the City as a temporary reminder of vehicular speeds. Requests for placement of the portable sign can be made by emailing Traffic Engineering.

## Stop Sign Requests

Stop sign installations must meet criteria established in the California Manual on Uniform Traffic Control Devices (CA MUTCD). While speed is one of the established criteria, it is not the only one, and stop signs should not be installed as a traffic calming measure, per the CA MUTCD. Residents can submit a Stop Sign Request Form (PDF) for an evaluation. Once the request form and the required petition (Petition for Stop Sign, PDF) are received, staff will complete an engineering evaluation and present findings to the Traffic Safety Committee. Installations recommended by the Traffic Safety Committee must be approved by the City Council.

## Street Maintenance, Potholes and Reporting Issues

The Street Maintenance Division is responsible for maintenance and repair of 165 miles of roads and rights-of-way, including sign testing and replacement, tree and shrubbery trimming, curbs, gutters, sidewalk repair and replacement, and weed/litter abatement along the roadside. The division also performs pothole repairs on public property.

The city strives to repair potholes within 24 hours of notice. To report maintenance issues around Poway, use the Poway CityApp or call 858-668-4700. The Streets Maintenance division annually inspects and replaces sidewalk/curb and gutter.

## Annual Pavement Maintenance Program

The Development Services Department manages the citywide pavement management program, accomplished through a combination of the Streets Maintenance Division and private contractors. The city is divided into eight geographic Pavement Maintenance Zones. Pavement maintenance projects are generally scheduled in each successive Zone on an eight-year cycle. The type of pavement treatment depends on the condition of the streets and funding availability.

City crews begin surveying and marking pavement in the fall. As early as January of each year, city crews perform crack seal, deep patch and skin patch work into the summer. Cape seal, slurry seal, and street striping are performed by contractors as Capital Improvements when the weather warms up beginning in the summer. A Pavement Maintenance Zone Map (PDF) is available on the page.

## Street Sweeping

Street sweeping is performed under a contract administered by the Public Works Department and operates on a fixed schedule (PDF). All paved public, city-maintained residential streets, and major arterials and non-major commercial arterials are swept every other week. Sweeping is delayed 1 day for national holidays and will be made up the following day. To ensure your street receives sweeping in front of your home, have your vehicle(s) removed from the street on the designated day between 7 a.m. to 5 p.m.

Street sweeping scheduled services are not affected by the change in City Hall service hours to the fixed compressed work week. Report maintenance issues using the Poway CityApp or call 858-668-4700.

## LED Street & Safety Lighting and Reporting Outages

The City of Poway has approximately 3,300 street and safety light fixtures that are operated and maintained through funding by the Lighting District. The project to convert all Low Pressure Sodium (LPS) light fixtures to LEDs has been successfully completed. GE was the manufacturer selected. Staff selected an 84-watt unit for arterial and collector streets and 39-watts for residential type streets. Both fixture types have 2700 Kelvin color temperature.

The new light fixtures are warrantied for 10 years however outages can still occur. You can report street light outages and issues by submitting a service request.
