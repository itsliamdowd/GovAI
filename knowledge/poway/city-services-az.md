## Parks & Recreation Advisory Committee

The Parks and Recreation Advisory Committee was established by the City so residents can give input on park, recreation and community service projects. It meets on an "as needed" basis to assist with special projects that do not require regular meetings. It has five members appointed by the Mayor with the concurrence of the City Council. Each Council member offers one nomination. The City Manager or designee also sits on the committee.

Term length is two years. The responsible department is Community Services. Applicants must reside within the City of Poway. Compensation: none. Members must submit Conflict of Interest statements (FPPC Form 700). Ethics Training is required within the first year of appointment and every two years thereafter. Sexual Harassment Prevention Training and Education is required within the first six months of appointment and every two years thereafter. City staff provides an initial orientation.

Current members (term expiration 12/31/26): Nick Carruthers, Dan Hober, Juliet Vasel-Phillips, Gregg Brandalise, Sherry Bravo. To apply, use the Committee Application (PDF). For information, contact the City Clerk's Office at cityclerk@poway.org or (858) 668-4530. Agendas are available prior to meetings, and minutes are available following approval.

## Blue Sky Ecological Reserve — Wildlife and Not Feeding Animals

Blue Sky has mountain lion, deer, bobcats, quail, raptors, roadrunners, coyotes, squirrels, rabbits, bats, and many reptiles and amphibians. Wildflowers bloom in spring.

Do not feed animals, whether intentionally or by not securing trash. Animals that eat human food can stop hunting or foraging and could starve, lose their fear of humans and become aggressive, get sick or miss needed nutrients, and disrupt the balance of their ecosystem. Dispose of trash properly. More information: wildlife.ca.gov/Keep-Me-Wild. Native species guides (PDF) are available for Birds, Habitats, Insects-Arachnids, Invasive Species, Mammals, Plants, and Reptiles-Amphibians.

## Entertaining at City Special Events — Application Process

Promotional materials for musical entertainment groups are only accepted October through December and must be postmarked or emailed by December 31. Groups must submit materials each year; materials are kept on file for 1 calendar year only. Materials may be mailed or emailed (type the band name and musical genre in the subject line). Mailing address: City of Poway, Lake Poway Attn: Event Entertainment, P.O. Box 789, Poway, CA 92074.

Materials must include a biography, picture, music and/or video demo, and pricing options. Include various pricing options; performances can range from 1 to 2 hours. Groups selected for the Summer Concert in the Park Series are contacted by staff; if not contacted by March 1, the group was not chosen that year. Groups selected for other special events are contacted approximately 2 to 3 months prior to the event. Submitting material does not guarantee selection.

Selected groups must sign a service agreement with the City, and be insured for general liability in the minimum amount of $1,000,000 with the City endorsed as an additional insured, or sign a waiver / release of liability. They must provide and set up sound equipment (the Summer Concert in the Park Series requires all bands to provide their own). They must arrive 1 hour before performance time to check in with City staff (may vary by event and venue).

## Swim Center — Recreation Swim Rules

Admission is required for all persons entering the Swim Center. Users 7-years-old and under must have a supervising adult in the water at all times, wearing swim attire and within arm's reach. Users 12-years-old and under must be accompanied by a supervising adult into the facility. A swim test is required for diving board use for all people under 18 years of age. Diving boards are not available during posted long course hours.

Groups of 15 or more people are required to make a reservation and may not be permitted access without advanced authorization (see the Group Use Reservation or Party Package page). Unpermitted use of the pool, such as coaching, teaching organized individuals, and/or providing instruction to individuals or groups, is prohibited. Some open recreational swim times are shared with City programs and rentals, and portions of the pool may not be available.
