## Affordable Rental Housing — How to Apply

Poway's affordable rental housing developments are owned and operated by local nonprofit housing developers. The City of Poway and Poway Housing Authority are not directly involved in the leasing and management of these properties. For information about availability and eligibility requirements, contact each affordable housing development directly. Most of these developments have waiting lists due to high demand for affordable housing.

The city provides a Poway Affordable Housing Contact Info (PDF) and an Interactive Map of Affordable Housing in Poway.

## Landlord / Tenant Support — Issues at an Affordable Development

Poway affordable housing developments are independently owned and operated by non-profit developers, and residents can provide feedback or express concerns. The on-site manager at each development is best able to answer a question or respond to a concern quickly. Find the manager for your development in the Affordable Housing Contact Information (PDF).

If the on-site manager cannot address your concern, bring the matter to the attention of the asset manager responsible for the property on behalf of the owner / operator.

Source: https://poway.org/215/Landlord-Tenant-Support

## Fair Housing Program

The Federal Fair Housing Act and California State law protect your rights. Everyone should be able to rent or purchase a home as long as they qualify financially. No one may be denied housing based on race, gender, nationality, religion, disability, marital or family status. This applies whether you are trying to rent, buy, sell or finance a home, and whether you are a tenant, homeowner or landlord.

Additional information is linked from the page: County of San Diego: Fair Housing, Landlord Information, and State of California: Department of Fair Employment and Housing.

## County of San Diego Home Repair Loan Program

The program is for the repair of owner-occupied homes. Deferred loans are offered at three-percent simple interest, calculated annually on the unpaid principal. Income-qualified homeowners may borrow up to $25,000 for a single-family home or $8,000 for a mobile home. Phone: 858-694-4847.

Source: https://poway.org/213/Housing-Program-Resource-Links

## HomeShare and Community Connections

HomeShare and Community Connections is a home-sharing service. It provides affordable housing costs, companionship, extra income, or help around the house through barter agreements. The service is free, has no age restrictions, and is open to low-income residents. The program also provides local social service agency referrals as needed. Phone: 619-284-9281.
