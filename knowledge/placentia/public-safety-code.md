## Emergency Alerts — Nixle and AlertOC

The City of Placentia's Public Safety Emergency Communications manages two web-based, opt-in emergency alert systems, Nixle and AlertOC. They send community-level and wide-spread emergency messages.

Nixle: The City uses Nixle daily. Opt in by texting "92870" to 888-777. Nixle provides general advisories, police and fire activity, and large-scale community notifications. More than one contact method can be registered to a specific address, cell phone, and email address in a single notification system. Past alerts and sign-up information are at https://local.nixle.com/city-of-placentia.

AlertOC: Participants register contact information to receive time-sensitive communications, such as evacuation orders, from the County in a natural disaster or emergency. Landlines, cell phones, and email addresses can be registered to a specific address in a single notification system.

During an emergency incident, authorized officials record a voice, email, or text message for rapid delivery. Nixle and AlertOC can send three million 60-second voice messages, two million emails, and 360,000 text messages per hour.

Source: https://www.placentia.org/214/Alert-and-Warning-Systems

## Code Enforcement — Notice of Violation and Citations

The City emphasizes compliance with municipal codes before enforcement. Depending on the violation, the City sends a Courtesy Notice to the property owner detailing the violation and allowing time to correct it. If no contact is received from the property owner, staff re-inspect the property.

If the violation is not resolved, an Administrative Citation is issued for $100 for each violation. A second citation is $200 per violation if there is a failure to comply. The process repeats, and if non-compliance still exists, a third citation is issued for $500 per violation. After the third citation, staff forward the matter to the City Attorney's Office to determine whether a civil or criminal case will be filed.

Source: https://www.placentia.org/768/Code-Enforcement

## Knox Box for Emergency Access

The Knox-Box Rapid Entry System gives firefighters access in emergencies and/or false alarms. Options are:
- Knox Box: mounted on buildings to store access keys.
- Knox Switches: installed on electrically operated access gates.
- Knox Padlocks: installed on non-electrically operated access gates.

Commercial properties and apartments shall order commercial Knox Boxes. Products requiring an electronic connection must be installed by an approved contractor.

To order online: (1) go to www.knoxbox.com and click "Buy" in the upper right corner; (2) select your location (state/province); (3) enter "Placentia Fire & Life Safety Dept." and click Search; (4) select/confirm Placentia Fire & Life Safety Dept. to view buying options; (5) select a buying option to view and configure the product, then add to cart; (6) confirm the installation address, then complete your purchase or continue shopping. For online ordering assistance, contact Knox-Box at (800) 552-5669 or info@knoxbox.com.

Knox-Box systems cannot be purchased through an unapproved vendor, website, or store. They must be purchased with an e-approval from PFLSD through the Knox-Box official website. Other brands or lock systems will not be accepted. The product is shipped directly to you, and PFLSD does not install Knox-Boxes. Install as directed by the fire inspector, who will help determine what keys and how many sets are needed.

Once installed, call PFLSD at 714-993-8135 to schedule an appointment to close your Knox box, install your padlock, or test your gate switch. Boxes shall contain the keys determined by the fire inspector, and each key shall be clearly labeled. The building keys will be checked and put in the Knox-Box during the appointment. Contact PFLSD if you need to update the keys to your facility for placement into your current Knox Box.

## Fire Hazard Severity Zones (LRA FHSZ)

On June 17, 2025, the City Council adopted Ordinance No. O-2025-04, designating Fire Hazard Severity Zones. A certified copy of the full text may be read and reviewed at City Hall, City Clerk's Office, 401 E Chapman Avenue, Placentia, CA.

On March 24, 2025, CAL FIRE's Office of the State Fire Marshal (OSFM) issued the 2025 recommended LRA FHSZ maps, and the City of Placentia was added. The public comment period has ended. Under Government Code Section 51179 (b)(3), a local agency may not decrease the level of the LRA FHSZ recommended by the OSFM, but may add locations or increase the level.

The page links the Placentia Fire Hazard Severity Zone Map - 2025, an interactive map, and an LRA Fact Sheet. Submit questions by email to FireCRR@placentia.org.

## Placentia Disaster Council

The Placentia Disaster Council has five (5) regular members, each serving a four (4) year term. The City Council appoints all members, who serve at its discretion. Regular meetings are held as needed at Placentia City Hall.

Title 11 - Emergency Organization of the Placentia Municipal Code creates the Council and designates the Mayor as Chair. The Council assists with updating the City's Emergency Operations Plan and Hazardous Mitigation Plan, and with disaster-related tabletop exercises and other emergency management work plan items. The staff liaison is an Administrative Services staff member.

Source: https://www.placentia.org/1096/Placentia-Disaster-Council

## Overnight Permit Parking at the Crowther Parking Lot

On December 2, 2025, the City Council approved a one-year overnight pay-to-park pilot program at the Crowther public parking lot, which is designated a no-overnight parking area. The entire lot is designated for overnight parking from 2:00 a.m. to 6:00 a.m. by permit only.

The overnight parking permit fee is $5.00 per night plus an additional service fee. Permits are purchased through the parking management company ParkMobile. Signage at the lot directs users to register their vehicles and pay the overnight fee using the mobile application. Enforcement began on May 11, 2026. For questions call (714) 993-8189.
