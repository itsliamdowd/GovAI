## Recreational Vehicle Parking

Recreational vehicles (RVs) are motor homes, campers mounted on truck bodies, and any other self-propelled recreational vehicles. Trailers, boats, camper units, and other apparatus that must be hooked to a vehicle to move are currently prohibited in front-yard setbacks.

RVs may be parked in front yard setbacks if they: are on an approved driveway or on an improved concrete surface between the edge of the driveway and the nearest side property line; are operable and have the current year's registration; do not adversely affect traffic safety; do not have people living in them; do not project into the public right-of-way; and have access to the space via a city-approved driveway approach along the street frontage.

A temporary 72-hour or weekly (7-day) recreational vehicle permit can be requested by completing the form. No more than one permit per address is issued at a time. An approved permit is not transferable to any other vehicle or address. Review takes approximately 48 to 72 hours, and the permit is NOT valid until you receive an email with your permit information. The permit is no longer automatic per Placentia Municipal Code 13.48.190.

To park a recreational vehicle anywhere else, a special use permit is needed. Contact the Planning Commission at (714) 993-8165.

## Construction and Demolition Waste Management Plan — Requirements

CALGreen requires recycling and/or salvaging for reuse of a minimum of 65% of non-hazardous construction and demolition project waste. The City of Placentia requires that at least 65% of all construction/deconstruction waste generated within the City be diverted from landfills. Applicants must comply with CALGreen and Placentia Municipal Code Title 8 Chapter 8.04.370, whichever is more stringent.

A C&D Waste Management Plan (WMP) is required (Placentia Municipal Code Section 8.04.380) for: residential additions of 500 square feet or more; tenant improvements of 500 square feet or more; new structures of 500 square feet or more; all projects with demolition, regardless of square footage; all roofing projects; pool demolitions; and other projects determined by the City Administrator to generate sufficient debris to warrant collection by the City Franchisee or authorized Permittee.

All contractors or owner-builders applying for a permit must complete a project form and attest that only an authorized City agent will be used to haul debris and other solid waste generated by the project. If a WMP is required, a WMP Final Compliance Report must be completed within 90 days after the project's completion. City staff will review the WMP within two (2) weeks. The WMP must be submitted and approved by the Public Works Department as a condition of obtaining a building permit. After approval, any changes must be submitted and pre-approved in writing by City staff before construction begins in order to ensure the return of the security deposit.

If you do not use Republic Services, you can haul your own materials as long as you use your own forces and equipment.

## Construction and Demolition Deposits, Refunds, and Forfeiture

Deposits range from a low of $100 to a high equivalent of 3% of the total project value.

Demolition:
- Less than 200 square feet: $100.00
- 201 to 1,000 square feet: $500.00
- 1,000 to 5,000 square feet: $1,000.00
- Over 5,000 square feet: 3% of the total project value

New construction:
- Less than 1 ton of material hauled off-site: $100.00
- Between 1 and 5 tons hauled: $500.00
- 5 to 50 tons hauled: $1,000.00
- Over 50 tons hauled: 3% of the total project value

If a project has both demolition and construction components, the deposit is the larger of the two amounts.

Refund: within 90 days after construction is completed, submit the WMP Final Compliance Report to the Public Works Department along with the signed-off final permit and receipts for all discarded and recycled materials. Receipts must include facility information, tonnage amounts, project address, and appropriate coding indicating whether the material was recycled or disposed of. Once compliance is determined, the refund takes approximately 4 to 6 weeks from the date all required documents were submitted.

The deposit may be forfeited in whole if you: begin work before submitting a completed WMP; begin work before receiving written WMP approval; change the WMP without Public Works approval; do not use approved facilities or permitted haulers; do not meet the 65% minimum diversion rate; have total actual tonnages materially different from the WMP estimates; or do not submit Final Compliance paperwork and receipts within 90 days of Final Building Inspection approval.

## Street Sweeping

The City contracts with Guardian Street Sweeping. Move all vehicles off the street during the times/days posted for street sweeping. Remove trash containers and recreational items, such as basketball hoops, from the gutter area on your service day. Don't rake or blow leaves or grass into the gutter or streets; place them in your automated green waste container. A street sweeping map (PDF) and a 2026 calendar (PDF) are available on the page.

Services are not performed on: New Year's Day, Martin Luther King Jr. Day, President's Day, Memorial Day, Independence Day - July 4, Labor Day, Thanksgiving Day (including the following Friday), and Christmas Eve & Christmas Day (unless otherwise noted).

Parking along city streets is prohibited on street sweeping days. Residential areas are swept twice each month, and the day and frequency are posted on signs (for example, the first and third Monday or the second and fourth Wednesday). The "No Parking Zone" is enforceable during the block of time on the posted sign, regardless of the location of the sweeper. Vehicles parked on the street during those days and hours will be cited under Placentia Municipal Code, Chapter 13.48.220. To contest a citation, follow the instructions on the back of the citation ticket. For citation questions, call the Placentia Police Department at (714) 993-8164.

Report street sweeping issues to the Public Works Department at (714) 993-8131. City Hall is closed every Friday. Sweeping may not occur during inclement weather (e.g., heavy rain and high winds); call (714) 993-8131 on those days for the most up-to-date information.

## Stormwater Program — Reporting Discharges and Pollution

Stormwater regulations are codified in the City's Municipal Code Title 16 Chapter 16.20. Anyone who violates water quality regulations is subject to enforcement under Chapter 16.20. The storm drain system releases untreated water into channels, rivers, and ultimately the ocean; it is not designed to be a waste disposal system. Urban pollutants that can wind up in the street include cleaners, construction debris, oil and other automotive fluids, paint, pesticides and fertilizers, and yard and pet wastes.

To report a discharge:
- Emergency: dial 911.
- Non-hazardous discharge during normal business hours: contact the Public Works Department at (714) 993-8131.
- Discharges into the storm drains during non-business hours: call (714) 993-8164.
- Alternative: the County of Orange water pollution reporting hotline is available 24 hours a day to report illegal dumping, illegal discharges into storm drains, or pollution spills. Call 877-89-SPILL or report online.
