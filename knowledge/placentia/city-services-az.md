## Construction and Demolition Waste Management — Plan Requirements

CALGreen requires recycling and/or salvaging for reuse of a minimum of 65% of non-hazardous construction and demolition project waste. The City requires that at least 65% of all construction/deconstruction waste generated within the City be diverted from landfills. Applicants must comply with CALGreen and Placentia Municipal Code Title 8 Chapter 8.04.370, whichever is more stringent.

A Construction and Demolition (C&D) Waste Management Plan (WMP) is required under Placentia Municipal Code Section 8.04.380 for: residential additions of 500 square feet or more; tenant improvements of 500 square feet or more; new structures of 500 square feet or more; all projects with demolition, regardless of square footage; all roofing projects; pool demolitions; and other projects determined by the City Administrator to generate sufficient debris to warrant collection by the City Franchisee or authorized Permittee. All contractors or owner-builders applying for a permit must complete a project form and attest that only an authorized City agent will be used to haul debris and other solid waste generated by the project.

City staff will review the WMP within two (2) weeks. The WMP must be submitted and approved by the Public Works Department as a condition of obtaining a building permit. After approval, any changes must be submitted and pre-approved in writing by City staff before construction begins, to ensure the return of the security deposit. If you do not use Republic Services, you can haul your own materials as long as you use your own forces and equipment.

Source: https://www.placentia.org/603/Construction-and-Demolition-Waste-Manage

## Construction and Demolition Waste Management — Deposit, Refund and Forfeiture

All contractors or owner-builders must place a deposit with the City to ensure compliance with the required use of an authorized City agent. The deposit ranges from a low of $100 to a high equivalent of 3% of the total project value. Demolition deposits: less than 200 square feet, $100.00; 201 to 1,000 square feet, $500.00; 1,000 to 5,000 square feet, $1,000.00; over 5,000 square feet, 3% of the total project value. New construction deposits: less than 1 ton of material hauled off-site, $100.00; between 1 and 5 tons, $500.00; 5 to 50 tons, $1,000.00; over 50 tons, 3% of the total project value. If a project has both demolition and construction components, the deposit is the larger of the two amounts.

To get a refund, submit the WMP Final Compliance Report to the Public Works Department within 90 days after construction is completed, along with the signed-off final permit and receipts for all discarded and recycled materials. Receipts must include facility information, tonnage amounts, project address, and appropriate coding to indicate that the material was recycled or disposed of. Once compliance is determined, the refund takes approximately 4 to 6 weeks from the date all required documents were submitted.

The deposit may be forfeited in whole if you: begin work before submitting a completed WMP; begin work before receiving written WMP approval; change the WMP without Public Works review and approval; do not use approved facilities or permitted haulers; do not meet the 65% minimum diversion rate; have total actual tonnages materially different from the WMP estimates; or do not submit Final Compliance paperwork and receipts within 90 days of Final Building Inspection approval.

## Recreational Vehicle Parking

Recreational vehicles (RVs) are motor homes, campers mounted on truck bodies, and other self-propelled recreational vehicles. Trailers, boats, camper units, and other apparatus that must be hooked to a vehicle to move are currently prohibited in front-yard setbacks.

RVs may be parked in front yard setbacks if they: are on an approved driveway or on an improved concrete surface between the edge of the driveway and the nearest side property line; are operable and have the current year's registration; do not adversely affect traffic safety; do not have people living in them; do not project into the public right-of-way; and have access to the space via a city-approved driveway approach along the street frontage. To park an RV anywhere else, a special use permit is needed; contact the Planning Commission at (714) 993-8165.

Source: https://www.placentia.org/222/Recreational-Vehicle-Parking

## Street Sweeping — Parking Enforcement and Citations

Parking along city streets is prohibited on street sweeping days. Signs are posted at the entrances to individual streets and the main entrances to residential neighborhoods. Residential areas are swept twice each month, and the posted signs show the day of the week and frequency, such as the first and third Monday or the second and fourth Wednesday. The "No Parking Zone" is enforceable during the block of time on the posted sign, regardless of the location of the street sweeper on the scheduled route. Vehicles parked on the street during those days and hours will be cited under Placentia Municipal Code, Chapter 13.48.220.

To contest a citation, follow the instructions on the back of the citation ticket. For questions about street sweeping citations, call the Placentia Police Department at (714) 993-8164.
