## ADU/JADU — Types and How to Start

Placentia allows Accessory Dwelling Units (ADUs) and Junior Accessory Dwelling Units (JADUs) in existing residential neighborhoods, in compliance with the Placentia Municipal Code and State laws. Generally, owners of single-family residential property are eligible to build an ADU or JADU. Owners of multifamily residential property are eligible to build ADU(s).

- **Detached ADU:** Newly constructed and separate from the primary home. It needs minimum 4' side and rear setbacks, is 16' to 18' high, and is up to 1,200 square feet.
- **Attached ADU:** Newly constructed and shares at least one wall with the primary home. It needs minimum 4' side and rear setbacks, may be up to two stories with a height of 25 feet (or the zoning height limit, whichever is lower), and the increased floor plan cannot exceed 50 percent of the existing living area.
- **Conversion ADU:** Converts existing space within the primary structure, a garage or an accessory structure. It has its own entrance. Setbacks are not required for an existing garage converted to an ADU. No replacement parking is required when converting or demolishing a garage, carport or covered parking structure.
- **JADU:** Converted existing space within the primary residence, limited to 500 square feet. It has its own entrance and must have a kitchen, but full-size appliances are not required. A dedicated bathroom is not required.

ADUs under 750 square feet (detached and attached) are exempt from certain impact fees. No additional parking can be required within one-half mile walking distance of public transit. ADUs cannot be used for short-term rental of less than 30 days. A newly constructed, non-manufactured, detached ADU is subject to the California Energy Code requirement to install solar systems.

Step 6 of the process is to apply to the Planning Department using the ADU/JADU Submittal Requirements. Applications can be submitted in person or through the SmartGov portal. For more information call the Planning Department at (714) 993-8124.

## Preapproved ADU Plans

The City's pre-reviewed ADU program accepts prototype plans that residents can use to build an ADU on their property. The plans are for new construction only. The applicant must provide a site plan for their property and complete the prototype plans' coversheet. The coversheet needs the APN number, the zoning, the lot size, the required setbacks, the style selected, the window materials, and the colors to be used.

Three sets of 24"x36" plans must still be submitted to the Building Safety Division for Plan Check Review. No construction is permitted without an approved Building Permit. All applicable plan check and development impact fees still apply.

Currently the City has not taken in any submittals for ADU plans for "Preapproval Use". To submit plans to be offered for preapproved use, contact Development Services at (714) 993-8124. Questions can also go to the Planning Department at (714) 993-8124.

## Building and Safety — Hours, Inspections, and Records

Building Division hours are Monday through Thursday 7:30 AM to 6:00 PM. The division is closed Friday through Sunday. Call (714) 993-8124. Plans accepted into review on or after January 1, 2026 must comply with the new 2025 California Building Code.

Inspections can be scheduled Monday through Thursday from 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM by calling (714) 993-8124. Provide the project address, permit number, type of inspection, contact information, and gate code (if applicable). Voicemail requests are not confirmed until the Building Division calls back. The city does not schedule same day inspections. Cancelations require a minimum 24 hours' notice. A re-inspection fee of $123 is charged for same day cancelations, no shows, and not ready for inspection.

The Building Inspection Manager and Inspector are available at the counter Monday through Thursday from 7:30 AM to 8:30 AM and 4:30 PM to 5:00 PM. No Plan Examiner is available at the counter.

To check whether a building permit (including electrical, plumbing, and mechanical) was issued for a property, first check the Placentia Records website online. If you do not find the record, call (714) 993-8124. For Mobile Home Permits, contact the Department of Housing and Community Development, 3737 Main Street, Suite 400 Riverside, CA 92501, (951) 782-4420.

## Building Permit Application and Plan Submittal Requirements

After planning and zoning clearance is obtained, a building permit application and plans are submitted to the City Building Division. A property owner, California licensed contractor, or authorized agent for the owner must complete and sign the application. Call (714) 993-8124 for more information.

To pull a building permit, bring:
- Valid government issued identification.
- A State Contractor's identification card, with verification of worker's compensation insurance (unless working alone).
- A NOTARIZED letter of authorization, if you are an agent of a contractor or homeowner.
- A Placentia city business license number.

A permit for an HOA or apartment complex requires a notarized letter of authorization from the association, management, or landlord. A grant deed may be required in some instances.

Projects requiring plan submittal include, but are not limited to:
- Photovoltaic (solar) installations
- Room additions and extensive remodels
- Commercial and industrial tenant improvements
- Wireless communication facilities
- Permanent identification signs
- Pools and spas (pre-site required on new construction and remodel)

Over-the-counter plan checks are available for walls/fences (standard details available), roofing (with a weight load below 6 lbs.), and remodeling (depending on extent, two sets of plans may be required).

Minimum required plans:
- Three (3) sets of plans drawn to scale with a wet stamp and wet signature by a California licensed Architect or Engineer.
- Two (2) sets of structural calculations wet stamped and wet signed by a California licensed Engineer.
- Two (2) sets of Title 24 Energy calculations.

Solar and patio cover residential plans may be on 11" x 17" paper if clear and legible. All other residential, commercial, and industrial plans must be on at least 24" x 36" paper.

Items not requiring plan check can be obtained over the counter, for example water heater, gas line, heating and air conditioning, electrical, and stucco. Inspection requests can be made by calling (714) 993-8124, 7:30 am to 6:00 pm Monday through Thursday. Roofing material must be on the roof sheathing at the (B1) inspection. A CRRC certified Cool Roof is required in climate zone 8 for reroofs where more than 50% of the roof is being replaced. An ICC ESR Report must be provided at the time of permit application.

## Photovoltaic (Solar) System Permit Submittal

The Development Services Department reviews each photovoltaic system installation. Solar collectors must be tested and approved by the International Association of Plumbing and Mechanical Officials (I.A.P.M.O.), the City of Los Angeles Solar Collecting Testing Laboratory, or another agency approved by the Chief Building Official.

Submit two (2) sets of structural calculations wet stamped and wet signed by a licensed California Engineer. Also submit two (2) sets of plans, wet stamped and wet signed by a licensed California Engineer or Architect, dimensioned and drawn to scale. The plans must show:
- Property lines
- Location of units on the lot, with roof direction and pitch
- North arrow
- Proposed collector location and installation method
- A sample or depiction of the collectors
- Construction details
- Calculations verifying the number and/or area of collectors
- Photographs of the proposed location
- Existing or proposed landscape material that may shade the collectors

Solar collectors for domestic hot water heating must provide a minimum of fifty percent (50%) year-round efficiency. For central water heating systems, a minimum of twenty (20) gallons of hot water per bedroom per day must be provided. Manufacturer's specifications and proof of certification must be submitted.

## Business License — Planning and Zoning Clearance

Any individual, corporation, sole proprietor, partnership, or entity that wishes to conduct business within the City of Placentia must secure a business license. For all new commercial and industrial businesses, including change of ownership and sub-lease, obtain a Building and Zoning Compliance Application (PDF). For all new home based businesses, obtain a Home Occupation Permit (PDF).

Massage establishments, practitioners/therapists, and operators/managers must obtain a City business license. The City accepts the California Massage Therapy Commission certification for therapists and practitioners. A supplemental business license application is required for both establishments and practitioners/therapists. ## Business License — How to Apply, Renew, and Fees

All businesses operating in Placentia must obtain an annual business license for each location. If you are opening a new business, buying an existing business, or transferring a business to your ownership, you must apply for a new license under your name. To apply or renew, visit the business license counter at Placentia City Hall, 401 E. Chapman Avenue, or use the business license renewal website.

First-time businesses within city limits need a Building and Zoning Compliance Application (PDF) or an Application for Home Occupation Permit (PDF). These are only approved in person at the Development Services counter at Placentia City Hall, 401 E. Chapman Avenue. After Planning approval, you can apply for your business license online. Businesses outside city limits apply on the business license website.

For online renewal you need the online PIN (bottom-right corner of your renewal notice) and the business license number. Online renewal is currently allowed only for Contractors, Cost of Doing Business, Exempt, Industrial/Storage, Professionals, Rental Property, Retail Sales, Services, and Wholesale/Manufacturing.

- Retail Sales or Services: In City $94; Outside City $61
