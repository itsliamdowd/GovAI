## Coyote Safety and Reporting

Coyotes are attracted to garbage, bird seed, suet, bread, pet food, compost, rotting dropped fruit, and drippings from outdoor grills. They may also pursue domestic cats and small breeds of dogs. USDA tips include: do not feed coyotes, eliminate sources of water, secure garbage containers, and don't leave small children unattended outside if coyotes have been seen frequently in the area. Keep pets confined, provide secure nighttime housing, and walk dogs on a leash. Cut back brushy areas, close off crawl spaces under porches and sheds, and scare off coyotes with loud noises, bright lights, or water sprayed from a hose.

To report aggressive coyote behaviors, call OC Animal Control at (714) 935-6848 during business hours Monday through Friday from 8:00 am - 5:00 pm. During a holiday or after hours, call (714) 259-1122 and leave a detailed voicemail; they will return the call as soon as possible. The page also links to a map of coyote sightings in your neighborhood and a form to report a sighting.

## City Clerk — Services and Proclamation Requests

The City Clerk is an elected position. The Clerk attends City Council meetings, directs the taking of official minutes, and certifies completed minutes. The Clerk maintains certified minutes, resolutions, ordinances, and other official city documents, as well as copies of all agreements and contracts entered into by the City. The Clerk also administers oaths, certifies documents, is custodian of the City Seal, and conducts election activities for general and special elections.

## Placentia Disaster Council

The Placentia Disaster Council consists of five (5) regular members who serve a term of office for four (4) years. All members are appointed by the City Council and serve at the discretion of the Council. Regular meetings are held as needed at Placentia City Hall. Title 11 - Emergency Organization of the City of Placentia Municipal Code creates the Council and designates the Mayor to serve as Chair. The Council assists with updating the City's Emergency Operations Plan and Hazardous Mitigation Plan, and with disaster-related tabletop exercises and other emergency management work plan items. Staff liaison: Administrative Services staff member.
