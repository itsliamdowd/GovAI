## Recreational Vehicle Parking: Where RVs May Be Parked

Recreational vehicles (RVs) are motor homes, campers mounted on truck bodies, and any other self-propelled recreational vehicles. Recreational apparatus such as trailers, boats, camper units, and other apparatus that must be hooked to a vehicle to move are currently prohibited in front-yard setbacks.

In 2023, the City Council revised the ordinance on where residents may park RVs. RVs may be parked in front yard setbacks if they:
- Are either on an approved driveway or on an improved concrete surface between the edge of the driveway and the nearest side property line
- Are operable and have the current year's registration
- Do not adversely affect traffic safety
- Do not have people living in them
- Do not project into the public right-of-way
- Have access to the space via a city-approved driveway approach along the street frontage

To park a recreational vehicle anywhere else, a special use permit is needed. For more information, contact the Planning Commission at (714) 993-8165.

## Recreational Vehicle Temporary Permit (72-Hour or 7-Day)

Residents can request a temporary 72-hour or weekly (7-day) recreational vehicle permit by completing the form on the city's Recreational Vehicle Parking page. No more than one permit per address shall be issued at a time. If approved, the permit is not transferable to any other vehicle or address.

The permit takes approximately 48 to 72 hours to review and is NOT valid until you receive an email with your permit information. The Recreational Vehicle Permit is no longer automatic per Placentia Municipal Code 13.48.190.

## Street Sweeping — Parking Restrictions and Citations

The City contracts with Guardian Street Sweeping. Vehicles must be moved off the street during the times and days posted for street sweeping. Parking along city streets is prohibited on street sweeping days. Signs are posted at the entrances to individual streets and the main entrances to residential neighborhoods. Residential areas are swept twice each month, and the frequency is posted on the signs with the day of the week and frequency, such as the first and third Monday or the second and fourth Wednesday.

The "No Parking Zone" is enforceable during the block of time on the posted sign, regardless of the location of the street sweeper on the scheduled route. Vehicles parked on the street during those days and hours will be cited under Placentia Municipal Code, Chapter 13.48.220. To contest a citation, follow the instructions on the back of the citation ticket. For questions about street sweeping citations, call the Placentia Police Department at (714) 993-8164.

## Street Sweeping — Schedule, Holidays, and Service Issues

Look up street sweeping information by address using the Street Sweeping Map (PDF) and the Street Sweeping Calendar for 2026 (PDF) on the city's Street Sweeping page. On your service day, remove trash containers and recreational items such as basketball hoops from the gutter area. Do not rake or blow leaves or grass into the gutter or street; place them inside your automated green waste container.

Services are not performed on the following holidays (unless otherwise noted): New Year's Day, Martin Luther King Jr. Day, President's Day, Memorial Day, Independence Day (July 4), Labor Day, Thanksgiving Day (including the following Friday), and Christmas Eve and Christmas Day.

Report issues or concerns with street sweeping to the Public Works Department at (714) 993-8131. City Hall is closed every Friday. Sweeping may not occur during inclement weather (e.g., heavy rain and high winds). Call Public Works at (714) 993-8131 on inclement weather days for the most up-to-date information.

## Police Traffic Bureau

The Traffic Bureau's primary roles are reducing traffic collisions on Placentia's roadways and promoting a safe and efficient flow of traffic. Its programs include DUI enforcement, high visibility in and around schools, parking education and enforcement, pedestrian/bicycle education and enforcement, and seat belt/car seat enforcement.

The Traffic Bureau is a sub-group of the Patrol Division and is managed by the assigned motor sergeant. It consists of two motorcycle officers and two full-time Parking Control Officers. The Traffic page links to information on how to retrieve an impounded vehicle (PDF), updated codes concerning private party impounds (PDF), and California Department of Motor Vehicles information on vehicle codes.

## Streetlight Repairs

There are over 3,600 streetlights along Placentia public streets. Southern California Edison (SCE) owns and maintains approximately 3,459 of them, and the City owns the remaining lights. To report a broken, dark, or cycling SCE streetlight, call Southern California Edison at 1-800-611-1911 or report online. You must have a specific address and a pole number for the streetlight that is out.

Streetlight safety lights mounted above the traffic signal lights are owned and maintained by the City. To report a broken, dark, or cycling City-owned streetlight, contact the Public Works Department at (714) 993-8148 or fill out a service request on the city's website.

If you live in a homeowner's association (HOA) area, the streetlights are probably maintained by Edison. Call Southern California Edison (1-800-611-1911) or your Homeowner's Association for repairs.

Source: https://www.placentia.org/1050/Streetlights

## Streetscape and Transportation Advisory Commission

The Streetscape and Transportation Advisory Commission has five (5) regular members, each serving a four (4) year term. All members are appointed by the City Council. Regular meetings are held at 6:00 p.m. on the third Monday of every other month (odd months) in City Hall.

The Commission advises the City Council on traffic safety issues, active transportation programs and projects, and transportation management, including neighborhood traffic and parking management programs, permit parking districts, and speed hump installation requests. It also advises on the removal, planting, replanting, or disposition of public trees in the public right of way or on public property, and reviews appeals of tree removal requests denied by City Staff. The staff liaison is the City Engineer/Traffic Engineer and/or designated staff member(s).

Source: https://www.placentia.org/293/Streetscape-and-Transportation-Advisory-

## City Council Old Town Placentia Parking Committee

The Committee consists of two (2) regular members of the City Council and meets as needed. It serves in an advisory capacity to the City Council on parking in Old Town Placentia. It initiates parking studies and impacts as needed in Old Town and presents findings and recommendations to the City Council. The staff liaison is the City Administrator, Deputy City Administrator, and/or designated staff member(s).
