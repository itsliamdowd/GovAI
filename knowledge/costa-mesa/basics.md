## About Costa Mesa

Costa Mesa is just one mile from the Pacific Coast in the heart of Orange County. It encompasses 16 square miles and has a population of about 112,780. It was incorporated in 1953. It is a general law city with a council-manager form of government and a staff of approximately 450 full-time employees. Tax revenues are about $118 million annually.

The city offers 28 parks, two municipal golf courses, 20 public schools and three libraries. Orange Coast College, Coastline Community College and Vanguard University have campuses in Costa Mesa. The Orange County Fair and Event Center is within the city limits.

Statistics listed: median age 34.9 years, median household income $91,857, median home value $870,000. Zip codes are 92626, 92627, 92628.

## Mayor and City Council — Roles and Terms

The Mayor is the official head of the City and the presiding officer of all City Council meetings and various ceremonial occasions in the community. The Mayor signs all official documents relating to local government and is a voting member of the City Council. The Mayor's term is 2 years (Ord. 16-05).

The City Council is the legislative body of the City. Members adopt the laws and policies for local government, appoint a city manager, define the powers and duties of appointed officers and employees, adopt a budget, and appropriate funds to operate City government. Council Members' term is 4 years (Ord. 16-05).

## Fire & Rescue Department Overview

The Fire & Rescue Department protects life, property and the environment through mitigation, prevention, response, and recovery services. The Fire/Rescue/Emergency Medical Services Division staffs 6 fire stations, 24 hours a day, 7 days a week.

The Fire Prevention & Community Risk Reduction Division provides public education, emergency management, life safety planning and inspections, and code enforcement services. It also reviews building construction plans, conducts building construction and business inspections, investigates citizen complaints, and manages the city's hazardous materials disclosure program. Both divisions are supported by the Fire Administration division.

The Department is currently budgeted for 90 full-time staff members, 84 sworn positions and six non-sworn positions, plus part-time staffing of 4 full-time equivalents.

Source: https://www.costamesaca.gov/government/departments-and-divisions/fire-rescue/about
