## Housing and Community Development Division — Contact and Programs

The Housing and Community Development Division (HCD) of the Community Development Department plans and administers Council-approved programs and projects in the City's 5-year Consolidated Plan and Annual Action Plans. Contact HCD at (714) 754-5678 or hcd@costamesaca.gov.

The plans are also the City's application to the United States Department of Housing and Urban Development (HUD) for its annual allocation of Community Development Block Grant (CDBG) and HOME Investment Partnerships (HOME) funds. HCD also administers CalHome State funds and Home Investment Partnerships-American Rescue Plan (HOME-ARP) federal funds.

Programs listed: Affordable Housing Projects; CDBG Public Service Grants Program; Code Enforcement; Fair Housing Services; Homeowner Housing Rehabilitation Loans and Grant Program; Infrastructure and Facility Improvement; Program Administration; Tenant Based Rental Assistance for families with minor children.

## Residential Rehabilitation Programs

The Housing and Community Development Department provides grants and programs to very-low income, single-family homeowners for interior and exterior property improvements needed due to code and building violations, lead-based paint hazards, and other hazardous conditions.

To request more information, contact Housing and Community Development at (714) 754-4870 and provide your property address and phone number. The city does not discriminate against persons on the grounds of race, color, sex, or national origin.

Single Family Rehabilitation Programs: HOME Loan (English and Spanish versions), HOME Grant (English and Spanish versions), and CALHOME Loan.

## CDBG Public Service Grants Program

Entitlement communities such as Costa Mesa may spend up to 15% of an annual federal CDBG allocation on public services. A majority of clients assisted must meet the government's definition of a low- or moderate-income household (household income does not exceed 80% of the county median income).

Eligible public services include childcare, youth services, homeless services, substance abuse treatment, health care, senior citizen services, and disability services. Grant funds may pay for labor, supplies, materials, and most operating costs (utilities, insurance).

Current Grant Recipients (FY 2025-2026): Assistance League Costa Mesa, Fair Housing Foundation, Families Forward, Human Options, Project Hope Alliance, Trellis International.

The 2026-2027 CDBG Public Services Grant Application acceptance window is closed. For more information, contact Nancy Sa, Housing and Community Development at (714) 754-5678 or nancy.sa@costamesaca.gov.

## Affordable Housing Development Projects

- **Civic Center Barrio:** Four affordable housing agreements with Civic Center Barrio Housing Corp. produced 30 multi-family rental units. The projects have a 55-year affordability period.
- **Costa Mesa Village:** Orange County's first single-resident occupancy (SRO) project, on the site of a former 96-room hotel. Units are available to tenants earning at or below 50% of the median income in Orange County and are preserved as affordable in perpetuity.
- **Habitat for Humanity:** The City provided Redevelopment Housing set-aside funds and HOME funds for 11 homes sold to low-income, first-time homebuyers. The homes are re-sold in perpetuity as affordable for other first-time homebuyers.
- **St. John's Manor:** A 36-unit, senior-citizen, income-restricted apartment project. City set-aside funds and federal HOME funds preserved its affordability for an additional 55-year period.
- **Costa Mesa Family Village:** A multi-family rental project for low-income families with 72 income-restricted units and an affordability period of 55 years, funded with Community Development Block Grant funds.

## Costa Mesa Housing Authority

The Costa Mesa Housing Authority was formed on Jan. 17, 2012 to serve as the city agency responsible for providing quality, affordable housing in Costa Mesa after the dissolution of the Redevelopment Agency. It accepted the assets and responsibilities of the former Redevelopment Agency. It applies for federal and state funding and uses those resources to provide housing assistance to Costa Mesa residents and to fund housing projects that serve low-income persons.

The Authority may acquire and lease land, acquire and rehabilitate existing units, finance construction of new or rehabilitated units, own and operate units, and operate programs and services that promote home ownership and responsible tenancy for persons of low income. It is also authorized to provide housing counseling, referrals, and advisory services in conjunction with new or rehabilitated housing projects.

The Housing Authority is governed by a board made up of the members of the Costa Mesa City Council. A member's term ends when he or she is no longer on the City Council. The page is listed under Disbanded Commissions and Committees.
