## Parks and Community Services

Parks and Community Services Department: 77 Fair Drive, Costa Mesa, CA 92626; (714) 754-5300. The department page links to Schedule Appointment and Recreation Classes Sign-Up.

The department operates three community centers, the Downtown Aquatics Center and the Costa Mesa Senior Center. It allocates and monitors athletic field use, provides youth, teen, adult recreation classes and arts and culture programming, and manages the operating agreements for the Costa Mesa Golf and Country Club and the Costa Mesa Tennis Center.

Staff administer parks and facility reservations, special event permits, model aircraft fly permits, animal licensing, community gardens, and recreation programs and activities. Staff also support the Parks and Community Services Commission, Arts Commission and the Fairview Park Steering Committee.

## Fire & Rescue — Services

Costa Mesa Fire & Rescue provides rapid response to life, health, fire, rescue, and environmental emergencies, as well as protection of property, fire cause and origin determination, and life safety inspections and education.

The Fire Prevention/Community Risk Reduction function enforces State adopted fire & life safety codes, reviews building construction plans, conducts building construction and business inspections, investigates citizen complaints, and manages the city's hazardous materials disclosure program.
