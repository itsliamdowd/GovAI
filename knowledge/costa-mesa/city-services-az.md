## Waste Collection and Recycling — Overview

The Public Works Department oversees all commercial waste collection and recycling services. Residential waste collection services are administered by the Costa Mesa Sanitary District. The City uses a non-exclusive franchise system for trash and recycling service to multi-family (5 or more units) and business properties, and for any for-hire hauling of temporary waste collection containers from construction and demolition projects.

A permit system allows contractors to self-haul the waste generated by their primary business activity (i.e. roofing, land grading, etc.). ## SB 1383 Organic Waste Requirements

In September 2016, Governor Brown signed SB 1383, establishing methane emissions reduction targets. It sets targets of a 50% reduction in the level of statewide disposal of organic waste from the 2014 level by 2020 and a 75% reduction by 2025. It also requires a 20% recovery of edible food for human consumption. These regulations apply to residential, commercial and multifamily properties. For more information, contact your waste hauler.

## AB 827 — Food Establishment Disposal Containers

Effective July 1, 2020, Assembly Bill 827 requires food establishments to provide customers with disposal containers for three types of materials: food scraps (organics), recyclables, and trash. This applies to materials generated from products purchased and consumed onsite. All commercial food service establishments that provide food for purchase and immediate consumption on site must comply.

Businesses must provide customers with recycling and organics collection bins adjacent to each in-house trash bin (restroom bins are excluded). Bins must be in clear view, easily accessible, and properly labelled. If all materials are consumed off-site and the business does not already provide a trash container for customers, organic recycling collection bins are not needed. Full-service restaurants are exempt from providing customers with organics and recycling collection bins.

## Street Sweeping — Schedule and Contacts

The City's contractor sweeps 850 residential/arterial lane miles of streets weekly. Residential areas are swept weekly between 7:00 a.m. and 2:30 p.m., Monday through Friday. Industrial and arterial streets are swept weekly between 12:00 a.m. and 6:00 a.m., Monday through Friday.

Streets are not swept on these City-observed holidays: New Year's Day, Memorial Day, Independence Day, Labor Day, Thanksgiving Day, and Christmas Day. Parking restrictions relating to street sweeping hours are not enforced on holidays. Sweeping schedules during holidays will resume the following week.

Contacts: General Maintenance (714) 754-5123; Maintenance Supervisor, Henry Granados (714) 327-7475; Street Sweeping Hotline (714) 327-7471.

Source: https://www.costamesaca.gov/government/departments-and-divisions/public-works/maintenance-services/street-sweeping

## Street Sweeping — Parking Enforcement

On July 19, 2022, City Council approved revised regulations to enforce the parking regulations related to street sweeping at any time during the posted hours. All public arterial and residential streets will be swept once a week. Citywide parking prohibitions are in effect on sweeping days, and signs with a dedicated hourly window period are placed at the entrances to each residential tract. The sign at the entrance of any street displays the day of the week of sweeping and the hourly window period.

The police department will perform enforcement anytime the street sweeper is actively sweeping. Any vehicles found impeding the street sweeper will receive a citation.

## Costa Mesa 311 — Report Issues

Costa Mesa 311 is a free smart phone app for reporting an issue and/or accessing city services. It can be downloaded at the Apple app store and Google Play. Residents can submit, track and view requests for services such as graffiti removal and pothole repairs through the app or the City's website.

Requests submitted via smartphone automatically alert City staff to the precise location of the problem, and residents can upload a photo from their smartphone camera. The app also provides access to TESSA, Costa Mesa news, calendar listings, public meeting agendas and more.
