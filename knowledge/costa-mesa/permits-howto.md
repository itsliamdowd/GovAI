## TESSA Online Permit and License Portal

TESSA (Totally Electronic Self-Service Application) is the City's online portal for permitting, licensing, and inspections. TESSA launched in Summer 2023. It is web-based and works on desktops, tablets, and mobile devices. It can be launched from the homepage of the City website or from the CM 311 main menu.

In TESSA you can:
- Submit and track permit or license applications.
- Upload project documents and revisions.
- Schedule inspections and view results.
- Pay invoices online.
- Receive digital copies of your Permits and Licenses.
- Access historical records, plans, and permits.
- Communicate with City staff.
- Submit community concerns through CostaMesa311.

Residents, contractors, business owners, developers, and others can use TESSA. The most common uses are applying for a building permit or a business license.

Source: https://www.costamesaca.gov/government/departments-and-divisions/community-development/building-safety/permit-processing-center-information

## TESSA — Available Applications and Finding the Right One

TESSA can be used to apply for Building Permits, Business Licenses, Land Use and Planning Applications, and Public Works and Fire Department Permits.

If you are unsure which application to submit, click apply in TESSA. You will be asked "Do you need help getting started?" Click yes, and TESSA will ask standard questions about your reason for the visit. It will then direct you to one or a group of applications that may be relevant. Review the brief description of the application and, if it matches, apply. You can also come into City Hall or contact the City at 714-754-5000.

Source: https://www.costamesaca.gov/government/departments-and-divisions/community-development/building-safety/permit-processing-center-information

## TESSA — Creating an Account and Getting Help

You need a valid email address to create a TESSA account. A How to Create an Email Address guide is available if you don't have one.

If your project started before TESSA launched, TESSA has your basic case information. Create your TESSA account with the same email you provided during your application submission, log in, and your project should appear under your dashboard. If your email has changed, contact a building technician at 714-754-5273. For previously submitted or acquired documents, use the City Clerk's Document Archives.

For questions or assistance, call 714-754-5245 and ask for help with TESSA. Staff will connect you with the right team member.

## Insta-Permits

Insta-Permits are fast-tracked permits you can obtain instantly online. The applicant must use the City Standard Plans. Once the plans are completed, the application and issuance of the permit takes 20 minutes or less. The Insta-Permit Program webpage explains which project types have Insta-Permit applications and how they work.

## Specific Plans and Urban Plans (Approved Plans for the City)

Some areas of Costa Mesa have special development and land use standards that provide more flexibility than the Zoning Code allows. These are called Specific Plans and Urban Plans, and they help implement goals and policies of the General Plan. The General Plan is the City's comprehensive, long-range policy document guiding future development and conservation. The City regulates property use through the General Plan together with zoning, subdivision, and building codes, specific plans, and urban plans.

Plans listed on the page: Freedom Homes - Great Home Makeover; East 17th Street Specific Plan; Newport Boulevard Specific Plan; North Costa Mesa Specific Plan; Redevelopment Plan; SoBeca Urban Plan; Theater and Arts District; Westside: 19 West Urban Plan; Westside: Mesa West Bluffs Urban Plan; Westside: Mesa West Residential Ownership Urban Plan; Orange County Fairgrounds; 2015-2035 General Plan; Zoning Map; One Metro West.
