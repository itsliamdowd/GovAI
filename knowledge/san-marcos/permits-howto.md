## Pre-Application Meeting for Planning Projects

A pre-application meeting with the Planning Division is a required step in the planning process before you submit an application. It is held to review your application for completeness, and no applications will be accepted without completing this step. If your application is found to be incomplete, you will be asked to prepare the necessary information and resubmit for a new meeting.

Questions go to the Planning Division at 760-744-1050, ext. 3204, 1 Civic Center Drive, San Marcos, CA 92069. Hours are Monday to Friday, 7:30 a.m. - 5:30 p.m., closed alternating Fridays.

## Before Getting a Building Permit — Requirements

Step 1. Grading and pad elevation certification: completion of grading and certification of pad elevations is required before obtaining permits for new buildings. Submit pad certifications to the Construction Engineering Department at least 24 hours prior to permit issuance.

Step 2. School fees: all school fees must be paid before permit issuance. Ensure school letters include the APNs (Assessor's Parcel Numbers) for each permit.

Step 3. "Will serve" letters: for projects requiring new water and sewer agency services, obtain and submit to the city "will serve" letters from the relevant water and sewer agencies.

Step 4. SDG&E work orders: secure SDG&E work orders for all new services prior to permit issuance.

Source: https://www.sanmarcosca.gov/Business-Services/Building-Division/For-Contractors

## Scheduling Inspections and Inspection Guidelines

Schedule all inspections through the city's permitting system. Guidelines by inspection type:
- Underground plumbing: a 10-foot head of water is required, and all waste pipes must be fully exposed.
- Foundation inspections: provide a setback certification before scheduling the inspection.
- Rough frame inspections: include rough electrical, mechanical, and plumbing in this stage.
- Shear nail and "OK to wrap" inspections: plumbing and electrical installations, straps, and nail protection must be in place before these inspections.

Source: https://www.sanmarcosca.gov/Business-Services/Building-Division/For-Contractors

## Who Does What and Final Inspection Sign-Off

- Planning Division: governs zoning, site use, and land development regulations.
- Fire Department: ensures compliance with fire safety codes and systems.
- Engineering Department: addresses infrastructure, grading, and stormwater management.
- Construction Engineering: oversees site preparation, including grading and pad certification.

All four departments must approve the project before the inspector can sign off on the final inspection. Water and sewer must also sign off on the permit before any final inspection can be performed. The city's tip is to maintain communication with the planner or engineer assigned to your project.

Source: https://www.sanmarcosca.gov/Business-Services/Building-Division/For-Contractors

## Certificate of Occupancy

Certificates of occupancy are issued for:
- New residential buildings, including single-family dwellings and detached ADUs.
- New commercial buildings.
- Tenant improvements that establish or expand a use.

Final inspections must be completed and approved by all relevant departments. The inspector cannot approve the final inspection until all internal and external department requirements have been met.

## Project Workshops and Advisory Commissions

The city requires applicants to hold public workshops for significant development projects. These meetings are not part of the formal project approval process. They let the community meet the project applicant and their team, meet the assigned city staff, review the scope of the project, ask questions, and receive a tentative processing schedule and other project information.

Three appointed advisory commissions give input to the city's planning processes and projects: the Planning Commission, the Climate Action Plan commission, and the Traffic Commission. All provide an opportunity for the public to attend and comment.
