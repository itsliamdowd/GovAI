## July City Hike — Heart of the City

Heart of the City is on Saturday, July 11, 2026 (second Saturday) at 8 a.m., with sign-in at 7:30 a.m. Difficulty is Easy-to-Moderate. It is a 6.5-mile loop hike with 580' of elevation gain towards the northern city limits. The route passes through Civic Center Park on urban trails that follow the rail trail east, then visits Hollandia Park and catches views of Mulberry Park.

Meeting location: San Marcos Community Center (Outdoor Hearth), 3 Civic Center Drive, San Marcos 92069. Hikers are asked to pick up after themselves and their pets, stay on official trails and out of natural and sensitive habitats, and keep voices down. Anyone listening to music or audio should use headphones. Contact Parks & Recreation at 760-744-9000 or parksandrec@sanmarcosca.gov. The Park Ranger Hotline is 760-744-9494, ext. 3707.

## Elections — Schedule and Voter Registration

San Marcos elections are consolidated with statewide general elections held on the first Tuesday after the first Monday in November of even-numbered years. In 2026, the Mayor and City Council Districts 1 and 2 are up for election. In 2028, City Council Districts 3 and 4 are up.

Registration forms are available at the City Clerk Department, 1 Civic Center Drive. You can also register online through the County of San Diego Registrar of Voters or the California Secretary of State. If you have recently moved, changed your name, or wish to update your political party, you need to re-register to vote. The County Registrar of Voters will only recognize your most recent registration. There is no need to cancel your previous registration unless you are moving out of the county.

## Elections — Running for Office

The City Clerk is the election official. The City is a Charter City governed by a five-member City Council consisting of an elected Mayor and four City Council Members. Terms are four years, with a limit of three consecutive terms for each position. A City Council candidate must be a registered voter and a resident of the City of San Marcos and of the district they want to represent. A Mayor candidate must be a registered voter and a resident of the City of San Marcos (the mayor is elected citywide).

The nomination period opens July 13, 2026, at 8:00 a.m. The last day papers can be filed with the City Clerk is August 7, 2026, at 5:00 p.m. A candidate may withdraw Nomination Papers on or prior to August 7, 2026, until 5:00 p.m. If an eligible incumbent has failed to file by 5:00 pm August 7, any qualified person other than the incumbent has until 5:00 pm on August 12, 2026 to file (E.C. § 8022, 8024). The filing deadline was extended to Wednesday, August 12 at 5:00 p.m. for Mayor and District 2 City Councilmember because incumbents did not file by August 7, 2026.
