## Fair Housing — Reporting Discrimination

The City of San Marcos is committed to providing equal housing opportunity for all people regardless of race, color, religion, sex, disability, national origin, familial status or any other protected class under Federal and State Law. If you believe you have been a victim of discrimination, or have any questions about fair housing, contact Legal Aid Society of San Diego at (844) 449-3500. You may also contact the U.S. Department of Housing and Urban Development's Fair Housing hotline (800) 669-9777.

## Housing Compliance Reports (AB 987 and SB 341)

The San Marcos Redevelopment Agency is required to report all rehabilitated units or new housing units developed or assisted with funds from the Low and Moderate Housing Fund and counted towards the Redevelopment Agency's housing production or replacement housing obligations. In January 2012 the City elected to retain the housing assets and affordable housing functions of the former redevelopment agency, as the housing successor, subject to the affordable housing-related provisions of California Redevelopment Law (CRL).

The AB 987 Report lists affordable housing units that were either (a) developed or otherwise assisted with Low and Moderate Income Housing Funds; or (b) otherwise counted toward the Agency's inclusionary and replacement housing obligations imposed by Section 33413 of the CRL. The Agency prepared it before its dissolution on February 1, 2012. The report is available as a PDF (82KB). It does not represent a complete inventory of affordable housing units in San Marcos, nor currently available affordable housing units. For current affordable housing opportunities, see the Housing Resources webpage.

The SB 341 Report: On October 13, 2013, the Governor approved Senate Bill 341 (SB 341), which amended CRL provisions on the functions of the housing successor and required new annual reporting. It went into effect on January 1, 2014. In September 2014, AB 1793 was approved, adding annual reporting requirements for homeownership units. By December 31st each year, housing successors are required to provide an independent financial audit to its governing body within six months after the end of each fiscal year; report specified housing financial and activity information as an addendum to the Annual Housing Element Progress Report submitted to HCD; and post specific information on the housing successor's website. The page also links to the SB 341 Report (PDF), Barriers to Racial and Ethnic Equity (PDF, 431KB) and Economic Displacement Risk Analysis (PDF, 858KB).

## Inclusionary Housing Ordinance and In-Lieu Fees

The inclusionary housing ordinance requires 15% of all residential units in new projects to be affordable for lower- and moderate-income households. It helps meet affordable housing goals outlined in the Housing Element. New In-Lieu Fees were adopted on November 26, 2024. Effective March 3, 2025, the new In-Lieu Fee is applicable to for-sale and rental residential developments. For more information, visit sanmarcosca.gov/Fees. Ongoing projects should review the Implementation Policy to understand how these requirements affect them.

Source: https://www.sanmarcosca.gov/Resident-Services/Housing/Developer-Resources
