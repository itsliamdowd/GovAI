## Graduation Weekend Traffic Advisory

The City of San Marcos advised residents and visitors on May 12, 2026 to plan for increased traffic congestion that weekend because of graduation ceremonies at California State University San Marcos. Motorists can expect heavier traffic volumes along Twin Oaks Valley Road and nearby streets surrounding the campus throughout the weekend.

Drivers are encouraged to allow extra travel time near campus areas, slow down and stay alert for increased pedestrian activity, use caution near crosswalks and intersections, and consider alternate routes when possible. Additional graduation-related traffic is also expected the following week near Palomar College during commencement ceremonies.

## Scripps San Marcos Project — Proposed Parking Structure

The Scripps San Marcos project information describes what has been proposed. City staff are reviewing the project, and changes may be requested to conform with city land use regulations and site conditions. The proposal includes a nine-level parking structure for Scripps Health employees, patients and visitors, with 2,100 stalls. The initial buildout would consist of seven levels with an approximate minimum capacity of 1,600 stalls. If Scripps Health determines in the future that additional parking is necessary, the parking structure expansion would occur.

Vehicle access to the parking structure would consist of two driveways off Scripps Way and one dedicated staff driveway off North City Drive.

Source: https://www.sanmarcosca.gov/Business-Services/Development/Planning/Projects/Scripps-San-Marcos/Scripps-San-Marcos-Project-Details

## Scripps San Marcos Project — Proposed Access, Circulation and Transit

Scripps Way would be a new private internal roadway serving as the primary access to the project site. It would bisect the property diagonally, connecting North City Drive to the north and Street 'A' to the west, with between two and four lanes through the site. The North City Drive entrance would be located between Twin Oaks Valley Road and the roundabout intersection with Street 'A.' The Street 'A' entrance would be located north of the intersection with Discovery Street and south of the roundabout.

The proposal also includes an additional driveway on Street 'A' for ambulance drop-off and a driveway for hospital deliveries off Discovery Street to the south. No driveways or vehicle access would occur along Twin Oaks Valley Road.

Pedestrian access would be provided via sidewalks along Street 'A,' North City Drive, Discovery Street, Twin Oaks Valley Road and Scripps Way, plus an internal pedestrian Paseo through the project site.

The closest transit stops are the North County Transit – San Diego Railroad's SPRINTER station at CSUSM, approximately one mile to the east, and the BREEZE bus stop at the intersection of Twin Oaks Valley Road and Craven Road, approximately half a mile to the south.
