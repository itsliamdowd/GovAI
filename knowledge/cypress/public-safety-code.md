## Police Property Release

Property is released by appointment only. Call the Property Unit at 714-229-6657. The Property Unit is located at 5275 Orange Avenue, Cypress, CA 90630, and its business hours are Monday–Thursday, 8:00 a.m. to 3:00 p.m.

Safekeeping: property held for safekeeping will be destroyed if not claimed within 60 days. Written notification is required to retain items longer. Property will generally be released to a third party with a notarized letter, or with an incarcerated Property Release Form (PD-203) if the owner is incarcerated. There are some exceptions, so contact the Property Unit. Evidence: contact the Property Unit or the Investigating Officer, as release varies case by case. Generally, a court order or case adjudication is required.

Found property is held for 90 days. Finders should contact the Property Unit after 60 days and before 90 days and complete a found Property Claim Form (PD-31). Found weapons cannot be released. Stolen or embezzled property may be turned over to the claimed true owner under Penal Code Section 1413 after a Declaration of Ownership is filed and the owner has had an opportunity to be heard.

Weapons held per 5150/8102 Welfare and Institutions Code require a court order for release. You must file a written request with the Cypress Police Department within 15 days of the date of the notice. If no request is received within 15 days, the weapon(s) will be destroyed without further notice. Weapons seized for safekeeping under Penal Code Section 12028.5 and/or FC 6389(c) (domestic violence or restraining order violations) require a law enforcement gun release from the Department of Justice, which includes a background check. Contact the Property Unit to place a hold while that process is completed. More information: https://oag.ca.gov/

## Police Records Requests (Reports, Citations, Log Items)

Exact cash, Visa or Mastercard are accepted. To request a copy of a record, print and complete the Application for Release of Record form. Mail it to Cypress Police Department, 5275 Orange Avenue, Cypress, CA 90630. Proper identification is required when the record is released. Bring your completed form and picture identification (DMV Drivers license or ID), and be prepared to give a legitimate and legal reason for the request.

Fees: Citation (copy of lost ticket), Crime/Incident Report (copy), Log Item (Computer Call For Service) (copy), and Traffic Accident/Collision Report (copy) are each $1.00 first page and $0.10 per additional page. For a lost citation, contact the police department business lobby for a certified copy and have your identification and the date you were cited.

Crime/incident and traffic collision reports are available if you are directly involved in the case. Each request is reviewed case by case. Turnaround may be from the same day to as long as 10 days, and most requests are generally reviewed within 48 hours. Cases containing juvenile information must be referred to the Orange County Juvenile Court for approval of release. Log items are public information except for information not releasable under the California Public Records Act, and anyone may review the log during business hours. Requests for multiple log items are reviewed case by case. Log items needed for court should be requested well in advance of the court date.

## Police Ride-Along Program

Eligible participants include members of adult or youth groups, college or university law enforcement students, police officers from other jurisdictions, visiting dignitaries or officials, other members of the criminal justice system, and any other person over the age of 16 with approval from the Watch Commander, Patrol Supervisor or Community Relations Unit supervisor. The fee is $80.00 (upon approval and criminal history verification process), and it may be waived at the Chief's discretion. Bring picture identification (DMV Drivers license or ID).

Ride Alongs are scheduled in four (4) hour blocks that coincide with patrol shifts, which change at 6am and 6pm daily. Participants may ride only once every six (6) months and must carry a valid photo ID or drivers license. A requested date and time is not guaranteed. Applicants will be contacted about approval and their scheduled date and time. Participants must wear business casual clothing, wear seat belts, and remain in the patrol car unless told otherwise. Weapons are prohibited except for other law enforcement officers. Cameras and recording devices need prior approval from the Chief of Police or designee.

Applicants aged 16–17 complete the Ride-Along Application and Waiver (Minor), and applicants over 18 complete the Ride-Along Application and Waiver (Adult). For juveniles, the parent(s) or guardian(s) must sign the waiver and have it notarized. Submit the forms to Cypress Police Department, 5275 Orange Ave., Cypress, CA 90630, Attn: Community Services (Ride Along). Questions: Community Services Unit at (714) 229-6626.

## Police Department Career Opportunities

Requirements: a high school diploma or G.E.D. (a Bachelor's degree in Criminal Justice, Public Administration or related field is desirable). Applicants must be at least 21 at time of appointment, be of good moral character with no felony convictions, and be a U.S. citizen or have filed an application for citizenship. They must also hold a valid California Class "C" driver license with an acceptable driving record. Bilingual skills are highly desirable.

Testing phases may include a written exam, writing proficiency exam, physical agility test, chief's oral exam, background investigation, polygraph examination, and psychological and medical examinations. Entry level applicants selected as officers complete the Regular Basic Course, a six month police academy (the POST-mandated minimum is 982 hours of instruction). The department also recruits lateral applicants from other law enforcement agencies. For more information or to be contacted by the Recruiting Team, email cypd@cypressca.org or call the Personnel & Training Unit at (714) 229-6630.
