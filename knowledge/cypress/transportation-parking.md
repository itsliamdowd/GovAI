## Traffic Commission — Meetings and Location

The Traffic Commission meets on the third Tuesday of each month at 3:00 p.m. at City Hall in the Executive Board Room, 5275 Orange Avenue, Cypress, or at another location specified in the posted meeting agenda. The departmental liaison is Public Works/Engineering.

## Street Sweeping — Parking Restrictions

Parking restrictions on sweeping day are from 8:00 a.m. to 12:00 p.m. Area 23 is posted until 1:00 p.m. on Friday. Residents are asked to move cars and keep the street clear for the sweeper. Arterial streets may not follow the schedule shown on the map, so residents should follow all posted no-parking signs.

## Street Sweeping — Holidays

Street sweeping and parking enforcement will not occur on observed holidays. The City of Cypress observes: Christmas Eve through New Year's Day, Martin Luther King, Jr. Day, Presidents Day, Memorial Day, Independence Day, Labor Day, Veterans Day, Thanksgiving Day, and Day After Thanksgiving Day.
