## Business License — Who Needs One and When to Apply

Under Chapter 15 of the Cypress Municipal Code, all businesses and non-profit entities must obtain and maintain a Cypress business license/tax certificate to transact business or occupy space in the City. Businesses located outside of Cypress but conducting business activity within the city limits also need one. A business license application must be submitted within 30 days of the first date of conducting business in the City.

All businesses performing work within Cypress must have an active license regardless of where the business is located. This includes, but is not limited to, contractors, sub-contractors, gardeners, caterers, food trucks, etc. Out of town contractors must renew their license prior to applying for a building permit. Building permits will not be issued to contractors who do not possess a valid business license.

## Business License — Application, Renewal, Fees and Payment

A completed Business License Application is required for all persons conducting business within the City. Applications and annual renewals can be submitted online through the City's 3rd Party Administrator, HdL Companies - Fresno, CA. All licenses expire annually on June 30. Renewals are sent to business owners the first week of July to allow for the reporting of total gross receipts from July 1 of the previous year to June 30.

The current administrative fee for new business licenses and license renewals is $35. A Disability Access and Education Fee of $4 is added to any application for a local business license or similar instrument or permit, or renewal thereof (Assembly Bill 1379, effective January 1, 2018). Effective January 1, 2019, applicants and renewals must also comply with Assembly Bill 3002 (Government Code section 4469.5) and read the Notice to Applicants for Business Licenses and Commercial Building Permits before applying or renewing.

Online payment options are credit card (American Express, Visa, MasterCard, or Discover) and e-check, an electronic debit from your bank account. There is no additional charge for e-check. Credit card payments are subject to a 2.9 percent convenience fee (minimum $2.00), collected by the third-party credit card processor.

For assistance, a business license representative can be reached by email or at (714) 229-6712 Monday through Friday from 8:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m.

## Business License — Supporting Documents Required

Fictitious Business Name Statement: In-town businesses using a fictitious business name that does not include the owner's surname must first file a Statement through the Orange County Clerk/Recorder. A copy of the Statement will be requested during application review. The business address and the ownership on the Statement must exactly match those on the Application. The Orange County Clerk Recorder is at 222 S Harbor Blvd. #100, Anaheim, CA 92805, 714-834-2500, or 12 Civic Center Plaza #101, Santa Ana, CA 92702.

Seller's Permit: Businesses involved in the sale or lease of merchandise, vehicles, or other tangible property must obtain a Seller's Permit from the California Department of Tax and Fee Administration (formerly the State Board of Equalization). The Seller's Permit number must be provided in the Application. CDTFA is at 16715 Von Karman Avenue #200, Irvine, CA 92606, 949-440-3473.

Corporation: If the business is a corporation, a copy of the Articles of Incorporation is required.

## Construction Runoff and Stormwater Requirements for Building/Grading Permits

All construction projects, at a minimum, shall conform with the best management practices outlined in the Construction Runoff Guidance Manual. The manual is intended to help applicants for building or grading permits understand and comply with the regulatory requirements for water protection during construction.

If you plan to construct a project on land greater than 1 acre, you must submit a Notice of Intent (NOI) to the State of California Water Resources Control Board and prepare a Stormwater Pollution Prevention Plan (SWPPP).

## Water Quality Management Plan (WQMP)

All new development/redevelopment projects require a Water Quality Management Plan (WQMP). The WQMP prescribes post-construction Best Management Practices (BMPs) for the property to prevent and abate storm water runoff pollution.

Contact the Public Works Department at (714) 229-6752 before preparing your conceptual or preliminary WQMP to ask about further requirements. You can apply online.

## Building Design Criteria for Cypress

For specific questions, contact the Building Division at (714) 229-6730.
- Cypress is located in Seismic Design Category D.
- The ultimate wind speed is 110 mph, and ASD Basic wind speed is 85 mph. Most areas of Cypress can be classified as wind exposure category C. Some areas may meet the criteria for exposure B (>= 1,500 feet away from open fields), and the design professional is responsible for justifying the exposure selected.
- For additions and remodels, soil bearing pressure is based on 1,500 pounds per square foot unless a higher value is justified by a soils report. A 1/3 increase is allowed for wind/seismic.
- For retaining walls, lateral soil bearing pressure is based on 100 pounds per square foot per foot below natural grade unless a higher value is justified by a soils report. Without a soils report, the coefficient of friction is 130 psf x contact area of footing or 1/2 DL, whichever is less.
- The climate zone is Zone 8.
- Rainfall design is based on two inches of rainfall per hour.

## Electric Vehicle Charging, Heating/Cooling and Reroof Permit Requirements

Electric vehicle charging station permits: complete the Guidelines for Residential and Non-Residential Buildings checklist and apply online.

Heating/cooling alterations: download and complete the CEC-CF1R-ALT-04-E Form and bring it with you when applying for the permit.

Reroof: (1) If the re-roof project requires roof sheathing to be replaced, a Radiant Barrier type sheathing is required. (2) For final inspection, download and complete the Smoke Detector Verification form and leave it for the inspector to pick up. You may also email the form to the Building Division before calling for final inspection.
