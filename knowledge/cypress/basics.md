## Addressing the City Council at a Meeting

Residents are encouraged to address the City Council on matters appearing on the City Council meeting agenda. California's public meeting laws prevent the City Council from discussing or taking action on items that are not on its agenda. If you address the City Council about an item that is not on the agenda, the City Council may provide a brief response and will often assign it to staff for follow-up.

City Council meetings are business meetings and do not include the opportunity for dialogue or question and answer with Council Members or staff. The Brown Act, California's open meeting law, limits discussions at City Council meetings to the items listed on the agenda and requires gatherings of a majority of the City Council to be open to the public and noticed in advance. The Brown Act does not limit residents' ability to communicate with Council Members outside a City Council meeting. The City Council meets on the 2nd and 4th Tuesday of the month.

## Veteran Recognition Program

The program is open to any person who has honorably served in the military. A veteran may be a Cypress resident, have immediate family in Cypress, work in the City, or be recognized posthumously. Veterans are recognized at a Cypress City Council meeting and honored with a framed biography and photograph displayed in the lobby at City Hall.

To nominate a veteran, complete the Veteran Recognition form and submit it with a military photograph, if available, to the City Clerk's Office at 5275 Orange Avenue, Cypress, or by email at cityclerk@cypressca.org. Forms are also available at City Hall, the Cypress Community Center and the Cypress Senior Center. Questions: City Clerk's Office at (714) 229-6685.
