## HBRV Campground — Location, Hours, and Contact

The Huntington Beach RV Campground is at 20903 Pacific Coast Highway, Huntington Beach, CA 92648. Phone: (714) 536-5281 (Option 2). Hours of operation are Monday-Sunday 7 a.m.-10 p.m. The campground is open September 3 - May 31 and closed June 1 - September 2.

It is just south of the Huntington Beach Pier, between 1st Street and Huntington Street, within the Huntington City Beach Parking Lot. Water and electricity are available at each site, and all spaces include 30 and 50 amp connections. A disposal station is centrally located. Fire pits are available on the beach between 5 a.m. and 10 p.m. Public restrooms are available between 7 a.m. and 10 p.m.

## HBRV Campground — Making a Reservation

Reservations can be made up to three months before the arrival date. They become available three months prior to arrival at 8 a.m. For example, reservations for October 4 open on July 4 at 8 a.m. Reservations may be made online until the day before the arrival date. In-person reservations for arrival in less than 24 hours are accepted at the camping gate. The reservation policy states that reservations can only be made online at hbrv.org and that there are no phone-in reservations.

All fees must be paid in full when making a reservation. Only two campsites may be booked per reservation. Once a reservation is made, the arrival date may only be advanced (based on availability) and not reduced. Maximum stay per month is 14 nights.

## HBRV Campground — Site Types and Rates

There are two site types:
- 30' Beachfront RV Camping Spot: accommodates RVs that are no more than 30 feet in length.
- 45' RV Camping Spot: accommodates RVs up to 45 feet in length.

Total length means the total length of the RV, including bike racks, trailer tongues, and anything that may add to the length of the RV.

Standard rates:
- Sunday-Thursday: $75.00 per night.
- Friday-Saturday: $100.00 per night.
- Holidays: $100.00 per night. Holidays include federal holidays, the week leading up to Thanksgiving, and the week between Christmas and New Year's Day.

Discounts: Seniors (62+) get $10.00 off per night. Disabled campers get $10.00 off per night.

Additional fees: Site Lock Fee is $10.00 per night (optional, for securing your desired site). Reservation Change Fee is $10.00.

For RV camping at the Pacific Airshow, the page directs visitors to pacificairshowusa.com/rv-camping.

## HBRV Campground — Rules and Regulations

Making a reservation indicates agreement to these policies:
- Check-in is between 2:00pm and 10:00pm. Check-out is 12:00pm. The gate closes at 10:00pm. Quiet hours are between 10:00pm and 6:00am.
- Failure to check in on the entry date results in cancellation of the entire stay and forfeiture of reservation fees.
- Maximum occupancy per campsite is one (1) recreational vehicle, one (1) extra vehicle under 22ft, and eight (8) persons. Additional vehicles and guests must pay the current day-use fee. Campsites cannot be reserved unless occupied by an RV.
- Only factory-built, self-contained RVs are allowed. They must include running water, an internal flushable toilet with holding tank, shower, and cooking facilities. No conversions, tent camping, or cassette toilets are allowed.
- Front-row spaces accommodate RVs up to 30 feet in length (24 feet in sites 2 and 20). Second-row spaces accommodate RVs up to 45 feet in length. All lengths include overhang.
- RVs in front-row campsites (1 thru 20) must back into their spaces and may not overhang the marked yellow safety lines. This policy is strictly enforced.
- Dogs must be kept on a leash and under a responsible person's control at all times and are not allowed on the sand.
- Fire pits must be a minimum of 8 inches off the ground.
