## Homeless System of Care

The City of Huntington Beach has established a System of Care for people experiencing, or at risk of, homelessness. It focuses on outreach, engagement, case management, and linkage to shelter, housing, and other supportive services. The program is run by the City's Homeless and Behavioral Health Division.

The page has a link for reporting non-emergency complaints related to homelessness. The 2023 and 2024 Annual Reports are in the Document Center archive, and the City says it will soon release its 2025 Annual Report.

Source: https://www.huntingtonbeachca.gov/departments/city_manager/homeless_solutions/index.php

## Navigation Center

The City's future Navigation Center will be at 17631 Cameron Lane/17642 Beach Boulevard. Plans, construction efforts, and the timeline are on www.HBHomelessSolutions.com. For the Homeless Liaison Officer Program and ways to participate in helping the homeless, residents are directed to the City's Homeless Solutions page.

Source: https://www.huntingtonbeachca.gov/government/boards_commissions/homeless_task_force.php

## Affordable Housing — Community Enhancement Division

## Fair Housing Program

Services provided by the Fair Housing Foundation include:
1) Fair housing and discrimination, investigations, and resolution;
2) Tenant and Landlord counseling;
3) Education and outreach activities;
4) Affirmatively further fair housing activities.

Source: https://www.huntingtonbeachca.gov/departments/community_development/affordable_housing/index.php

## Inclusionary Housing — Affordable Homeownership Program

The City provides an Affordable Homeownership Program to help low-, moderate-, and middle-income households own a home in Huntington Beach. The page also lists an Inclusionary Housing In-Lieu Fees Summary (12/19/2025).

Source: https://www.huntingtonbeachca.gov/departments/community_development/affordable_housing/index.php

## Residential Rehabilitation Grant & Loan Program

The City offers financial assistance through the Residential Rehabilitation Program to help eligible low-income homeowners repair and improve their homes.

Source: https://www.huntingtonbeachca.gov/departments/community_development/affordable_housing/index.php

## Tenant Based Rental Assistance (TBRA)

The City uses HOME funds to support three Tenant-Based Rental Assistance (TBRA) programs designed to help eligible residents with housing stability. One of the three is the Tenant Based Rental Assistance Program for Mobile Home Owners (MHTBRA).

Source: https://www.huntingtonbeachca.gov/departments/community_development/affordable_housing/index.php
