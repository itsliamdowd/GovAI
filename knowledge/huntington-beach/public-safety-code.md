## Fire Department Divisions

The Emergency Response Division has eight fire stations. Paramedic engine companies are located in each station and staffed by four personnel. The Division also coordinates the Community Emergency Response Team (CERT) Program and the Radio Amateur Civil Emergency Services (RACES) Program.

The Community Risk Reduction Division, formerly the Fire Prevention Division, enforces local, state, and federal codes through inspection and code enforcement, plan review, public education and fire investigative services. It also operates the Hazardous Materials Business Plan Program (HMBP), which identifies, inspects, and monitors businesses that use and store hazardous materials.

The Marine Safety Division provides year-round lifeguard services on the City's beach, including medical aid and code enforcement services. It also manages the summer Junior Lifeguard Program. Lifeguard services are also provided to Sunset Beach. The Division consists of 14 permanent Marine Safety Officers and management staff, supported by more than 150 recurrent ocean lifeguards.

Source: https://www.huntingtonbeachca.gov/departments/fire/about_us/index.php

## Fire Inspections, Plan Review and Inspection Scheduling

The Fire Department performs fire inspections at a frequency determined by its Community Risk Assessment. Inspections follow the California Fire Code, which the City has adopted. The Community Risk Reduction division conducts plan reviews for all new residential and commercial construction for compliance with fire and life safety codes and standards.

All plan submittals are accepted through the HB Accela Citizen Access (ACA) portal. To schedule an inspection, submit the request through the Huntington Beach Accela Portal or by email. Questions about plan check services or fire construction inspections go to Deputy Fire Marshal Steve Eros.

## Smoke Alarm and Carbon Monoxide Detector Requirements

Smoke detectors: install one in every bedroom and one outside of sleeping areas. If the home has more than one level, a smoke detector is required on all levels.

Carbon monoxide detectors: at least one is required. It can be plug-in or battery operated. It must be approved by the Office of the State Fire Marshal (check the package for the OSFM seal). CO detectors are required on every level of the home and outside of sleeping areas. Combination smoke/CO detectors are acceptable if they carry the OSFM seal.

Source: https://www.huntingtonbeachca.gov/departments/fire/our_community_and_risk_reduction/index.php

## Fire Extinguisher Basics

Fire extinguishers can be obtained from a fire extinguisher servicing company, which can supply and install them and set up annual service programs. They can also be bought at hardware and home center stores. Extinguishers must be serviced or replaced yearly and mounted correctly.

- Minimum size is 2A10BC.
- Install one extinguisher within 75 feet of travel distance for an office or residential application. Other occupancy types or business activities may require less spacing.
- Mount extinguishers no more than 4 feet off the floor.
- Mount a sign indicating the location of the extinguisher if it is not easily visible.
- Check the pressure gauge monthly to verify the extinguisher is still charged.
- Service or replace extinguishers annually.

## Police Recruitment — Joining HBPD

HBPD has open continuous recruitment for Police Recruits, Police Officers (experienced/lateral), and Communications 911 Dispatchers (experienced/lateral).

Police Recruit: applicant testing is held bi-monthly. The Physical Agility Test consists of three events:
- 800-yard run: max time of 3 minutes 30 seconds.
- Obstacle course (165 lb. dummy drag, 99-yard obstacle course, and a 6' chain link fence climb): max time of 1 minute.
- 6' solid wall climb: pass or fail.

Police Officer (Experienced/Lateral): qualified lateral candidates are eligible for the 3% at 50 CalPERS retirement plan.

911 Dispatchers: applicants with a POST Public Safety Dispatcher Certificate and experience as a public safety dispatcher in a California law enforcement agency are highly encouraged to apply.

Police Cadet: a part-time program for people aged 18 to 25 who are currently full-time high school or college students.

Officer Atkins can be reached at 714-536-5936.

## Crime Information — Victim Resources

U.S. Immigration and Customs Enforcement (ICE), Homeland Security Investigations (HSI) administers a Victim Notification Program. It lets eligible victims and witnesses obtain information about a criminal alien's release from custody. Victims and witnesses must register with HSI to receive notification.

Under Proposition 9, the "Victim's Bill of Rights Act of 2008" ("Marsy's Law"), police officers provide victims a pamphlet or card with the Victim's Bill of Rights on it. The Neighborhood Watch newsletter provides information about crime prevention techniques and neighborhood crime prevention opportunities. Domestic Violence information is available to assist victims or friends of victims.
