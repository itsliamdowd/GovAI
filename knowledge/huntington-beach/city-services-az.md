## Beach Information — Headquarters and Rules

Beach Headquarters phone: (714) 536-5281. Location: 103 Pacific Coast Highway Huntington Beach, CA 92648-5183. Emergency: 911. Office hours are Monday - Friday 8 a.m. - 5 p.m., closed Saturday and Sunday. The Huntington Beach City Beach is a 3.5-mile stretch of shoreline.

Beach and pier rules (Municipal Code Chapter 13.08):
- Beach curfew is 10:00 pm, and pier curfew is midnight.
- Board surfing is prohibited when blackball signs are posted.
- Riding bikes or skateboards is prohibited on the pier.
- Possession and/or consumption of drugs or alcohol is prohibited.
- Dogs must be on a leash and are only permitted on the service road and at Dog Beach.
- Smoking is prohibited on the beach and pier.
- Barbeques must be within 6' of a firepit and up off the sand. There are 127 fire pits, located South of First Street to Beach Blvd.
- Bicycle Path speed limit is 5 MPH when pedestrians are present and 10 MPH maximum.

## Trash & Recycling — Service Types and Collection Hours

All homes and businesses in Huntington Beach must have trash service. All single family homes and many condos and townhomes use individual wheeled carts emptied one time per week (residential cart service). Multi-family properties with two to four dwelling units may have either residential cart or commercial bin service. Multi-family properties with 5 or more dwelling units and all other commercial properties must use commercial bin service, which may include a residential style cart for recycling or organics.

To request a change in service type from residential to commercial or vice-versa, contact the Trash and Recycling Office at (714) 375-5010. To start, stop or change commercial service, contact Republic Services at (714) 847-3581. Carts and bins are property of Republic Services; do not take them when you move.

Service can happen anytime from 7:00AM - 6:30PM, on weekly regular schedules depending on the type of service and area of town. The next holiday affecting the collection schedule is Thanksgiving Day, Thursday, November 26.

## Free Compost for Residents

Compost is free to Huntington Beach residents at the City Yard, 17371 Gothard Street, Monday - Friday, 8 AM to 4:30 PM. Bring a scoop or shovel and your own containers to fill. ## Edible Food Donation (Commercial Generators)

California law requires businesses classified as commercial edible food generators to donate all leftover or unsold edible food that would otherwise be thrown away. They must do this by contracting with an edible food recovery organization or service.

Tier 1 includes supermarkets with annual revenue of $2 million or greater, grocery stores with a total facility size of 10,000 sq. ft. or more, and food service providers, food distributors, and wholesale food vendors. Tier 1 generators were required to contract with a recovery organization or service and keep donation records starting January 1, 2022.

Tier 2 includes hotels with on-site food facilities; restaurants with a total facility size of 5,000 or more square feet or 250+ seats; health facilities with an on-site food facility and 100+ beds; state agency cafeterias with 5,000 or more square feet or 250+ seats; local education agencies with an on-site food facility; and large venues and events. Tier 2 generators were required to comply starting January 1, 2024.

Source: https://www.huntingtonbeachca.gov/departments/public_works/trash_recycling.php

## Special Event Permits

A permit is required for any organized activity involving the use of public property including parks, beaches, parking lots and street areas. Contact the Specific Events Office at 2000 Main Street - 5th Floor, office hours Monday - Friday 8:30am - 4:30pm.
