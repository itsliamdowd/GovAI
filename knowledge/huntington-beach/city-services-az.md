## Payment Scam Warning and Card Fees

The City has been made aware of a phishing scam targeting applicants with projects pending before the Planning Division/Commission and Permit Center. Scammers pretend to be City officials requesting payments. Official City emails will only come from an "@surfcity-hb.org" email address. Online payments (including credit/debit card payments) are made only through the City's secure HB ACA Portal. The City will never ask you to wire money to a bank account unsolicited.

Payments by check or electronic bank transfer are accepted only using payment instructions provided by the City through official communications, and only if that payment method is first requested by an applicant. If you receive an unexpected or different payment request, do not respond or submit payment. To verify a request, contact your assigned case planner or permit technician directly, or call the Permit Center at 714-536-5271.

Effective 7/1/2024, all credit and debit card transactions are subject to a service fee of 3.00%.

Source: https://www.huntingtonbeachca.gov/departments/community_development/index.php

## 2025 California Building Standards Code

The new 2025 California Building Standards Code goes into effect on January 1, 2026. The 2022 California Building Standards Code remains in effect and applies to all plans submitted prior to January 1, 2026. Applications that remain incomplete after March 31, 2026, will be voided. To move forward, a new application and plans stating the 2025 California Building Standards Code must be submitted.

## Affordable Housing Programs

The Community Enhancement Division provides housing programs and projects that help low- and moderate-income residents. Phone: (714) 536-5582. Programs include:
- **Affordable Housing Viewer:** helps find affordable rental locations and ownership properties throughout the City.
- **CDBG & HOME Program:** assists nonprofit organizations that provide services and housing to low- and moderate-income residents.
- **Fair Housing Program:** services from the Fair Housing Foundation include fair housing and discrimination investigations and resolution, tenant and landlord counseling, education and outreach, and affirmatively furthering fair housing.
- **Inclusionary Housing Program:** an Affordable Homeownership Program for low-, moderate-, and middle-income households.
- **Residential Rehabilitation Program:** financial assistance to help eligible low-income homeowners repair and improve their homes.
- **Tenant Based Rental Assistance:** HOME funds support three TBRA programs for eligible residents.
- **Mobile Home Information:** useful information for mobile home owners.

Source: https://www.huntingtonbeachca.gov/departments/community_development/affordable_housing/index.php

## Permit Center and Building Permits

Permit Center hours are Monday-Friday 8:00 am-3:00 pm. Building permits are required to erect, enlarge, alter, remove, demolish, or repair a building or structure. Examples include patio covers, fences, block walls, re-roofs, stairway & guardrail replacements, tenant improvements, signs, and paving and restriping a parking lot. For questions about when permits are required, call (714) 536-5241.

Permits are valid for 1 year from the date of issuance. They also extend 180 days from the day of an approved inspection (partial approval does not count).

Source: https://www.huntingtonbeachca.gov/departments/community_development/building_inspection/index.php

## Building Permit Fees

There is no single fee for the various permits. Fees are based on the valuation of the project and the number of plumbing, mechanical and electrical systems. The building, planning, and fire (if applicable) plan check fee is collected when plans and documents are submitted for plan check. Permit fees are collected when permits are issued. Permits may be paid for by cash, check or credit cards between 8:00 a.m.-3:00 p.m. Per the California Building Code (Section 109.4), work commencing before obtaining the necessary permit(s) is subject to an additional fee, as approved by the adopted fee schedule.

## Construction Hours

Under HBMC 8.40.090 (D): Special Provisions - residents with a valid City permit are allowed to commence construction, repair, remodeling, or grading of real property between the hours of 7:00 A.M. to 7:00 P.M. from Monday thru Friday, including Saturday. On Sunday or a Federal holiday, construction, repair, remodeling, or grading of real property is not allowed.

## Code Enforcement — How to Report a Complaint

Code Enforcement matters may be reported using MyHB. The Code Enforcement Request Hotline is 714-375-5155.

Effective March 9, 2023, a complaint about a potential Municipal or Zoning Code violation by a business in a commercial, mixed use or industrial zone must be submitted through the City's MyHB App or the HB ACA Portal. It must include:
- the address (address number and street name)
- a detailed description of the complaint about the business
- your name and contact information (phone number and/or email address)
- a photo of your Driver's License, State-Issued ID, or City of Huntington Beach Employee Badge

Per HBMC 1.18.025, the City is unable to accept, process, or investigate a complaint without the required information.
