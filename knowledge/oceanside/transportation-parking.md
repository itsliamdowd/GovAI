## Parking Lots, Map and Permit Requests

The City provides City parking lots for access to the beaches, the harbor, and the downtown business area. Each parking lot has specific guidelines based upon local activity, and the Parking Map shows locations and guidelines.

To request a permit, first read the rules and regulations for the permit type. Then use the Parking Permit Request Portal. You will be asked to create a user ID and password and upload any required documentation. Once the application is approved, you will receive an email to log back in and pay for the permit. Confirm that the email address on your account is valid.

For physical permits, at payment you can choose to have the permit mailed to you or be ready for pickup in the office. An email will be sent when the permit is ready for pickup at the City's Cashier office. Government issued ID matching the permit holder is required at pickup. Virtual permits are activated after payment has been completed for the time period requested.

## Annual Beach Parking Lot Permit

The Annual Beach Parking Lot Program allows patrons to park in designated beach and harbor pay lots without paying the parking fees. The permit costs $100 for permanent residents and $200 for non-residents. See the Annual Beach Parking Lot Permit Rules and Regulations for additional information and requirements.

## Beach Area Resident Permit Parking (Oceanside Traffic Code Sec. 13.24)

The program gives residents within the boundaries of the metered area and four-hour parking zones an on-street permit parking alternative. Eligible residents may purchase resident and guest permits at a cost of $10 each. See the Beach Area Resident Permit Rules and Regulations for eligibility requirements and additional information.

## Preferential Parking Permit Program (Oceanside Traffic Code Sec. 10.30)

The program allows residents impacted by correctible parking intrusions to obtain a parking permit. Only residents within an approved Preferential Permit Parking Area are eligible. Permits cost $15 each. See the Preferential Permit Rules and Regulations for additional information and requirements.

## Oversized Vehicle Permit Parking (Oceanside Traffic Code Sec. 10.28.3)

The program allows a resident to receive a temporary permit to park an oversize vehicle or non-motorized vehicle on the street overnight due to special circumstances. The 24-hour permit costs $5 each. The annual permit costs $150 and is only issued due to special circumstances. See the Oversized Vehicles Permit Rules and Regulations for additional information and requirements.

An oversized vehicle is any vehicle that exceeds 25 feet in length, or 84 inches in width, or 84 inches in height. A non-motorized vehicle is any trailer, travel trailer, tow dolly, semi-trailer, boat trailer, utility trailer, or any other vehicle with means of self-propulsion.

## Contest a Parking Citation (CVC 40215(a))

The City has contracted with a parking citation service provider. If you cannot access the online link, call 1-800-989-2058. You may also access your citation online at oceanside.citationinfo.com.

To contest a citation, you must submit a Request for Review within the time frame established by the California Vehicle Code. Late submissions will not be accepted. The deadline is:
- within 21 calendar days of the issuance of the Parking Citation, or
- within 14 calendar days of the notice date shown on the Notice of Parking Violation.

A Request for Review may be submitted in one of these ways:
- Online: Contest your Parking Citation. An email will be sent acknowledging receipt.
- In writing: send a written explanation with your name, address, and phone number describing your reason for contesting, all applicable documentation (documents will not be returned), and a copy of the citation to City of Oceanside, C/O Citation Processing Center, PO Box 10479, Newport Beach, CA 92658-0479. Certified mail gives proof of mailing. The City is not responsible for documentation lost in the mail.
- In person: complete a Request for Review form and submit any applicable documents (documents will not be returned) at Oceanside City Hall- East Building, 300 North Coast Highway Oceanside CA 92054.
- By phone: call (760) 435-3890 and request that a Request for Review form be mailed to you.

Upon submission of your Request for Review the citation will be placed on hold. Collection activity will cease and late penalties will not be assessed while the citation is under review. You will receive a written response by U.S. Mail with the adjudicator's decision. The response for an upheld citation will include information and deadlines to request a hearing with a third-party adjudicator. Payment of the citation is required before proceeding to a hearing. If the third-party adjudicator dismisses the citation, a refund will be issued.

## Parking Citation Review — Supporting Documentation

Include a written statement of facts and any documents and/or photographs that support your statement. Common documentation:
- Expired Registration: an image of current complete vehicle registration. Receipt of paid registration or incomplete registration does not qualify as a valid registration.
- Fail to Display Receipt: an image of the permit/receipt identifying lot #, date/time of purchase, and expiration.
- Sold Vehicle: a copy of the completed Release of Liability filed with the DMV. Incomplete Release of Liability documents will not be accepted.
- Disabled Person Meter Violation/Expired Meter (no placard displayed): images of all of the following: 1) current disabled placard, 2) DMV placard receipt, and 3) government issued photo ID. Failure to provide all three images will result in the violation being upheld.
- Handicap Zone/Blocking Handicap Zone (no placard displayed): the same three images. Failure to provide all three will result in the violation being upheld.

## Parking Ticket FAQs — Common Situations

All of these are handled by submitting an administrative review:
- Vehicle on the ticket is not yours: submit a copy of the citation and a copy of your vehicle registration.
- Car was not working and you got a street sweeping ticket: submit a copy of a tow bill, a repair bill, or a parts receipt along with a written statement of facts.
- Paid for parking but got a ticket anyway: submit a copy of the citation and the receipt for parking.
- Forgot to put your handicap/disabled placard up: submit a copy of your disabled placard, your disabled placard registration, and a picture I.D. Upon verification of all three documents, the citation can be reduced to an administrative fee of $25.00 as per CVC 40226. The review will be denied if all three documents are not received.
- Contest a ticket in general: submit an administrative review with a detailed statement of the facts and any documents of proof.

## Parking Review Deadline and Status

Under State law, you have 21 calendar days from the issuance of the Parking Citation or 14 calendar days from the notice date shown on the Notice of Parking Violation to request a review. If you fail to do so within the required timeframe, you lose your right to appeal.

If you have already submitted a review and then receive a notice for payment, all tickets are on hold pending the disposition of the review, so payment does not need to be made while the ticket is being reviewed. For review status or information about the review process, call (800) 989-2058. If your review is upheld, payment must be made to continue the dispute process. Your review determination letter will provide details of the next steps and any deadlines.
