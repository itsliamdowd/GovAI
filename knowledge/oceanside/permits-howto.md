## Solar Permits — SolarAPP+

The Building Division accepts SolarAPP+ permit applications, in compliance with SB 379, for Photovoltaic Only and Photovoltaic with Battery Storage permits. SolarAPP+ provides a code-compliance check for the majority of residential, roof-mounted, retrofit photovoltaic systems. Once the Solar PV Plan is approved by SolarAPP+, upload the pre-approved plans and apply for an instantly issued permit through the City's eTRAKiT Customer Self Service Portal. Access to eTRAKiT requires registration as a licensed contractor. Re-roof projects require a separate permit. Projects with main panel upgrades require a separate permit or a non-SolarAPP+ application.

## Building Permit — How to Apply

Commencing January 2, 2025, all applications with plan submittals must be made through the web-based process. Paper plans are no longer accepted. Building Permit applications with plans must be made via the electronic Building Permit Application or through the eTRAKiT portal. No exceptions will be made. For help with a web-based submittal, email development_services_technicians@oceansideca.org. For comments or concerns about this policy change, email DSCStaff@oceansideca.org.

Contractors and their representatives must use eTRAKiT (by completing the eTRAKiT User Application) for these eligible single family dwelling permits: Residential A/C Replacement, Furnace Replacement, Water Heater Replacement and Tankless Water Heater Replacement (each like for like and same location only); Residential Water Re-Pipe; Roof Replacement; Roof Recover; Solar Photovoltaic (Typical and SolarAPP+); Energy Storage System (Typical and SolarAPP+); and Electric Vehicle Charge Station.

For any other building permit, submit an electronic Building Permit Application, or complete and email the Printable Building Permit Application to buildingpermits@oceansideca.org. City staff verify that the submittal meets guidelines. The applicant is then notified with an invoice, a link to process payment, and an invitation to a Box account (free to create) to upload required documents. All documents must be in pdf format and follow the Construction Coalition PDF Guidelines. A Permit Technician verifies the documents and fees paid. Your plan review time frame does not start until required documents are submitted and fees are paid.

## Building Permit — What You May Need, Payment, and Who Can Apply

To apply you may need: property owner name, phone number, and address; contractor name, phone number, address, state license number, City Business License, and current worker compensation certificate; architect name, phone number and address; engineer name, phone number and address. A project valuation is required. Plans may be required. A Box account is needed if submitting documents electronically.

Plan check fees and/or permit fees may be collected. The cashier accepts cash, check, MasterCard and Visa. Online payments are also accepted with an invoice number.

The applicant must be the property owner, a licensed contractor, or an authorized agent. A letter of authorization is required for authorized agents.

## Building Permit — Who Can Be Issued a Permit

Building permits are only issued to California licensed contractors of the appropriate classification, or to owner/builders. An owner-builder is the person who owns the property and acts as the general contractor on the job, and either does the work themselves, has employees or uses licensed contractors. Owners of residential properties need to have owned the property for the last 12 months. In both cases, the building owner, an employee of the owner, the licensed contractor or an employee of the licensed contractor must be present at all inspections.

Source: https://www.ci.oceanside.ca.us/government/development-services/building/building-permits

## Building Permit — Expiration and Extensions

A Building Permit expires if the work is not completed within three calendar years from the date of issuance. An unexpired permit may be granted a one-time, six-month extension. Submitted applications and plans expire two years after the date of submittal. For either, complete a Permit Extension Request and send it to DSCstaff@oceansideca.org. There is a $100 fee for extensions.

## Plan Check — Submittal Requirements

Paper plans are no longer accepted for applications with plan submittals; use the electronic Building Permit Application or eTRAKiT. For help, email Development_Services_Technicians@oceansideca.org. Minimum paper plan size for additions, renovations, and new construction is 24" x 36". For certain minor alterations, an 11" X 17" sheet may be used. Online submittals are accepted for all building division reviews.

Commercial projects (tenant improvement and new): five complete sets of plans. In general a licensed design professional is required, and plans must be stamped and wet signed. Two copies of soils reports, structural calculations, truss calculations, and energy calculations are required as applicable.

New residential buildings: five complete sets of plans, stamped and wet signed if prepared by a licensed design professional. Unlicensed design professionals may only submit plans for up to 4 dwellings on one lot, of wood framed construction, and of not more than two stories and basement in height. Two copies of soils reports, structural calculations, truss calculations, and energy calculations are required as applicable.

Residential additions and remodeling: three sets of plans. Projects that involve removal of walls must show on a framing plan whether they are bearing walls.

Plans are not required for repairs/replacement of residential plumbing, electrical and mechanical systems and re-roofing permits. Those permits can be obtained on the Web Permits page or by walk-in counter service.

Before submitting plans to the Building Division, the applicant routes the plans to other departments depending on the scope of work. Check with the Building Division on which departments you will be routed to, and with those departments about their submittal requirements.

## Plan Check — Review Process and Target Dates

Accepted plans are placed in line for initial review on a first-come, first-served basis. The plan checker prepares an itemized correction list, and the Building Division notifies the applicant to pick up the corrections. After the corrections are addressed, plans are resubmitted and again placed in line first-come, first-served. At the second review, compliant plans are approved; otherwise a second itemized correction list is generated and the process repeats until all corrections are addressed.

Target dates estimate the initial plan review and may fluctuate with volume. Electronic plan submittals typically require 1-2 days for processing and 1-2 days for the return to customer.
- New Commercial Buildings: 21 calendar days
- Commercial Tenant Improvements: 14 calendar days
- New Residential Buildings: 21 calendar days
- Residential Additions: 14 calendar days
- Residential Interior Remodels: 14 calendar days
- Accessory Dwelling Units: 14 calendar days
- Photovoltaic (Residential) Systems: 1 business day
- Photovoltaic (Commercial) Systems: 14 calendar days
- Re-submittals: 7 calendar days
- Revisions: 7 calendar days

## Plan Review Expiration and Extension

Plan review expires two calendar years from the date of submittal. To request an extension, submit a written request before expiration using the Permit Extension Request form, sent to DSCstaff@oceansideca.org. One six-month extension may be granted. If plans have expired beyond the extension, they may be renewed by written request. Renewal procedures may vary depending on Building Code adoptions, plan check fees may need to be collected, and new plans may need to be submitted.

## Scheduling a Building Inspection

Use eTRAKiT to schedule inspections and to check your inspection time (2-hour window). Requests can be made 24 hours a day: click the Online Permitting/eTRAKiT button on the City website, log in to your contractor account and click "Schedule." Homeowners and/or representatives may also schedule in the eTRAKiT portal with a permit number (under "Permits", then "Search Permit"); creating an account is not required. You can also complete the Building Inspection Request Form for issued permits, call 760-435-3925 and leave a voicemail, or schedule in person at the Building Division counter.

You must request the inspection by 3:30 pm on a business day to receive a next business day inspection. Requests received after that time will be scheduled in 2 business days.

Source: https://www.ci.oceanside.ca.us/government/development-services/building/inspection

## Web Inspection Scheduling and Inspector Arrival Time

Inspections scheduled before 3:30 pm will be performed the following business day. For first-time users: visit eTRAKiT, set up an account, link to your permit, and request an inspection.

On the day of your inspection, use eTRAKiT to see the estimated time of arrival of the building inspector: visit eTRAKiT, select Scheduled in the Inspections box, and search the list for your building permit or address. The time shown is not an exact appointment; it reflects a window of time, so be present for the time shown and up to two hours of additional time.

## Preparing for a Building Inspection

The building owner, an employee of the owner, or the licensed contractor or an employee of the licensed contractor must be present at all inspections. The issued permit job card must be on site for the inspector to sign, and the issued approved plan must be on site. Access to all areas where work is being performed is required, and be prepared to unlock gates and doors. Have an appropriate ladder available for roof and attic inspections; inspection staff do not bring ladders.

The owner or licensed contractor doing the permitted work must notify the Building Division when work is ready for inspection. No required-to-be-inspected portion of any building, structure, wiring, plumbing, ductwork or equipment may be covered or concealed without approval of the Building Official or his Deputy. The inspection process culminates with a final inspection and the issuance of a Certificate of Occupancy.
