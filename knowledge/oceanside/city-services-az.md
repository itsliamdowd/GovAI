## Special Events — Permit Requirements and Fees

Special Event permits are required for any event held on public property with an organized group of 50 or more. Special event permits have a $150 permit fee and all permits require liability insurance. If the applicant does not have access to insurance, the city may be able to provide it for an additional fee. Larger events may have more fees and requirements if additional city services are required to support the event.

The Special Events Office is a section of Parks and Recreation. It processes special event and film permits for approximately 200 events every year.

Source: https://www.ci.oceanside.ca.us/residents/special-events

## Special Events — Park, Pavilion and Gazebo Use, and Bounce Houses

The city does not reserve any parks, beaches, or gazebos for private parties. They are all first-come first-served. A special event permit is still required for groups of more than 50, but the permit is not considered a space reservation. The permit holder is responsible for arriving at the space early and securing the venue.

The city has no current special requirements for bounce houses, and they are allowed at parks without a permit. The city recommends that anyone obtaining a bounce house also purchase liability insurance and add the City of Oceanside as the additionally insured party.

Source: https://www.ci.oceanside.ca.us/residents/special-events

## Free Large Item and E-Waste Curbside Pickup

Oceanside residents are able to schedule a free residential large item landfill pickup of 5 items, 5 times a year. They can also schedule a free residential e-waste curbside collection of 5 items, 5 times a year. For either, contact Waste Management at (760) 439-2824 or cssandiego@wm.com. More information is on the Donation & Landfill Curbside Pickups page and the Electronic Waste page.

Source: https://www.ci.oceanside.ca.us/residents/solid-waste-recycling

## Household Hazardous Waste Drop-Off

To make a residential household hazardous waste drop-off appointment, contact Waste Management at (760) 439-2824 or cssandiego@wm.com. More information is on the Household Hazardous Waste page.

Oceanside residents aged 62 and older, those 55 and older living in senior housing, or individuals with ADA restrictions can schedule at-your-door special collection appointments each month. Service is conducted one time per month on a first-come-first-serve basis with a maximum of 15 slots per month. For more information visit the Residential Services page or call 1 (800) 449-7857.

## Food Scraps and Organics Recycling Requirements (SB 1383)

Businesses, multi-family properties and single-family residents are required to separate organic material from landfill material under California law SB 1383. All Commercial & Multi-family properties are required to subscribe to collection services.

Beginning 2024, residential collection service was expanded to include the collection of food scraps and food-soiled paper along with green waste. These materials are to be placed in the green cart, now referred to as the Organics cart. For more information, visit the Food Scraps Recycling at Home webpage.

Source: https://www.ci.oceanside.ca.us/residents/solid-waste-recycling

## Library Board of Trustees

The Library Board of Trustees meets monthly at 2:00 pm on the 4th Tuesday at the Library. It sets policy and makes all rules and regulations pertaining to Library hours of operation, services provided and the selection of materials placed in the facilities. Contact: lbot@oceansideca.org, (760) 435-5564.

If you have special needs because of a disability which makes it difficult to submit comments via email or telephonically, contact Cheri Noel at (760) 435-5564 by noon the day of the meeting to make arrangements.

## Parks & Recreation Commission

The Parks & Recreation Commission meets bi-monthly at 6:00 pm on the 2nd Tuesday (January, March, May, July, September, November) in Council Chambers, 2nd Floor, City Hall North. It considers and makes recommendations to the City Council on policies pertaining to the acquisition, development, operation and maintenance of City park facilities, beach facilities, community center facilities and golf course facilities, and advises in the planning of City recreation and leisure programs and special events. Contact: Manuel Gonzalez, parc@oceansideca.org, 760-435-5561.

Source: https://www.ci.oceanside.ca.us/residents/city-services/boards-commissions-committees/parks-recreation-commission

## Utilities Commission

The Utilities Commission meets bi-monthly, 3:30-5:00 p.m. on the 3rd Tuesday, in Council Chambers - 2nd Floor, City Hall North. It considers and makes recommendations to the City Council on policies pertaining to municipal water facilities, sewer facilities and drainage facilities. Contact: uc@oceansideca.org, (760) 435-5880.
