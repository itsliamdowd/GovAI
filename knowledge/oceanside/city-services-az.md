## Special Event Permits

The Special Events Office is a section of Parks and Recreation. It processes special event and film permits for approximately 200 events every year. Contact the mainline at (760) 435-5540 or specialevents@oceansideca.org. Events Administrator Patrick Young: (760) 435-5215. Recreation Specialist Nick Maddox: (760) 435-5244.

A Special Event permit is required for any event held on public property with an organized group of 50 or more. The permit fee is $150, and all permits require liability insurance. If the applicant does not have access to insurance, the city may be able to provide it for an additional fee. Larger events may have more fees and requirements if additional city services are required.

The city does not reserve any parks, beaches or gazebos for private parties. All are first-come first-served. A permit is not considered a space reservation, and the permit holder must arrive early to secure the venue. Bounce houses are allowed at parks without a permit. The city recommends liability insurance naming the City of Oceanside as additionally insured.

Source: https://www.ci.oceanside.ca.us/residents/special-events

## Establishing or Terminating Utility Services

To establish or cancel water, wastewater and city waste service, call (760) 435-3900. You can also visit in person at 300-C North Ditmar Street, City Hall East.

Request service to be established at least one business day before the desired service date. A $62.00 set-up fee applies to eligible accounts. If water is turned off at the property, service can be restored the same day if the request is received before 2:00 p.m. Same-day requests received after 2:00 p.m. are subject to a $260.00 after-hours fee.

For landfill, recycling and organics services, contact Waste Management at (760) 439-2824.

## Waste Management — Trash, Recycling and Organics Billing and Service

Waste Management of North County provides residents and commercial properties with solid waste and recycling services. Customer Service: (760) 439-2824. City staff email: oceansiderecycles@oceansideca.org.

The city does not bill any WM-provided trash, food scraps and recycling services. All billing is done by WM. To set up new services, call (760) 439-2824. To update services or schedule a courtesy pick up for missed services, contact Waste Management at (760) 439-2824 or cssandiego@wm.com.

To find your collection day, Waste Management customers can enter their service address or customer ID in the WM pickup schedule search and click verify. WM customers who pay by direct debit (ACH/PAD) or mail a check avoid a convenience fee. Fees apply to payments via credit/debit card, digital wallets (e.g., Venmo, PayPal, Apple Pay), or electronic debit.

## Household Hazardous Waste

Household Hazardous Waste cannot be thrown out with regular garbage. It must be taken to a Household Hazardous Waste Collection Center. For information, call Waste Management at (760) 439-2824.

To make a residential household hazardous waste drop-off appointment, contact Waste Management at (760) 439-2824 or cssandiego@wm.com.

Residents aged 62 and older, those 55 and older living in senior housing, and individuals with ADA restrictions can schedule at-your-door special collection appointments each month. Service is conducted one time per month, first-come-first-served, with a maximum of 15 slots per month. For more information, call 1 (800) 449-7857.

Source: https://www.ci.oceanside.ca.us/residents/solid-waste-recycling

## Food Scraps and Organics Recycling (SB 1383)

Under California law SB 1383, businesses, multi-family properties and single-family residents are required to separate organic material from landfill material. Beginning 2024, residential collection service was expanded to include food scraps and food-soiled paper along with green waste. These go in the green cart, now referred to as the Organics cart. All Commercial & Multi-family properties are required to subscribe to collection services.

The Green Oceanside Waste Wizard tells you how an item can be reused, repaired, donated, recycled or disposed of. The City's Zero Waste Team: (760) 435-4500 or greenoceanside@oceansideca.org.

Source: https://www.ci.oceanside.ca.us/residents/solid-waste-recycling

## Elections and Candidacy

The City Clerk is the City's Election Official. Oceanside is a Charter City governed by a five-member City Council. Elected officials serve four-year terms: the Mayor, four City Council members, the City Clerk and the City Treasurer. The Mayor, Clerk and Treasurer are elected at-large. Councilmembers are elected by District. General Municipal Elections are held in November of even-numbered years. In November 2026, Council seats for Districts 1 and 2 will be up for election.

Candidates must be citizens of California aged 18 or older. They must reside in Oceanside and be a registered voter eligible to vote in Oceanside when nomination papers are issued. Candidates must appear in person at the City Clerk's Office during the Nomination Period to be issued Nomination Papers. City Treasurer candidates must meet minimum qualifications under Ordinance No. 22-OR0719-1. Email CityClerk@oceansideca.org to confirm qualifications.

Voter registration forms are available from the City Clerk's office, or register online with the San Diego County Registrar of Voters. The City Clerk is also the Local Filing Officer for Political Reform Act filings, including Form 700.

Source: https://www.ci.oceanside.ca.us/government/city-clerk/elections-fair-political-practices-commission-reporting-program
