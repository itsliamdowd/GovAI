## Police & Fire Commission

The Police and Fire Commission acts in an advisory capacity to the City Council on policy matters pertaining to safety, police, fire and other areas wherein the matter of public safety may be of concern. It meets bi-monthly at 5:00 pm on the 2nd Tuesday of even months (February, April, June, August, October, December). Location: Council Chambers - 2nd Floor, City Hall North.

Contact: pafc@oceansideca.org, (760) 435-3013. Council Liaison: Jimmy Figueroa.

To apply, you can file an electronic application online. You may also download the application, complete the form, and email it to boardsandcommissions@oceansideca.org, or mail a hard copy to: City Clerk's Office, City of Oceanside, 300 N. Coast Hwy, Oceanside, CA 92054.

## Code Enforcement Division — Purpose

The purpose of the City's Code Enforcement efforts is to assure compliance with the zoning ordinance and other code sections that are related to private property usage and which address promoting health, safety, property preservation and community enhancement. The majority of Code Enforcement concerns involve substandard dwelling units, securing of dangerous or abandoned buildings, illegal encampments, unpermitted construction, and wrecked or inoperable vehicles on private property.

The Code Enforcement Division can be reached at 760-435-3945.

## Code Enforcement Regulations — Buildings, Construction and Property

The City's Code Enforcement Division (760-435-3945) can answer questions about the codes and initiate action to enforce them.

Vacant & dangerous buildings: structures that sit vacant and unsecured must be secured from unauthorized entry and kept free of trash, debris, junk and graffiti. New construction or remodeling: before you draw up plans for additional units, converting a garage to living area, or enlarging existing buildings on your property, speak with the Planning Department (760-435-3520) to see if the changes will be allowed by the Zoning Ordinance. Building permits: before you begin a new construction or remodeling project, or make any repairs or changes to your electrical, plumbing or gas systems, speak with the Building Department (760-435-3950) to see if a permit will be required. Garages are not considered habitable space and may not be used for living, dining, cooking, or sleeping purposes.

Trash, debris and junk must not be allowed to accumulate and must be removed. Tall grass, weeds, or dead vegetation are fire hazards and must be cleared according to the City's standards. Trash containers, green waste bins, and recycle bins must be stored in a side or rear yard area screened from public view, except when properly placed for collection.

Source: https://www.ci.oceanside.ca.us/government/development-services/code-enforcement/code-enforcement-regulations

## Code Enforcement Regulations — Vehicles, Animals and Garage Sales

Motor vehicles: it is unlawful to store vehicle parts, wrecked, dismantled or inoperable vehicles anywhere on your property other than in your garage, behind a six-foot solid fence, or other location out of public view. An operable vehicle may not be parked in your side or rear yard setback areas, nor in your front yard except on your driveway. Vacant, unpaved lots cannot be used for parking.

Campers, RVs, trailers and other recreational vehicles: you may not live in any parked vehicle or trailer on your property, any vacant site or a street, nor connect them to any electrical, gas or water system. Vehicle repair: you may make minor repairs to your own vehicle in your driveway or garage. Any other type of repair is not allowed except in a properly licensed and permitted vehicle repair garage. Vehicle repair of any kind is not allowed on the public streets or alleyways.

Animals such as roosters, ducks, geese, or goats are not allowed on your property. You may have only three dogs at a time living on your property. Garage sales: a resident is allowed only two garage sales during a calendar year. Each sale can only take place over a one-to-two-day period and only at your own residence. New merchandise and food cannot be sold at garage sales.

## Administrative Citations — Penalties

The Code Enforcement Division may issue administrative citations for violations of any applicable code. Criminal and/or other legal processes remain available for violations not suited to the administrative process.

Fee schedule (State of California approved): Warning = no penalty; 1st Citation = $100; 2nd Citation = $200; 3rd Citation = $500; 4th and subsequent Citations = $1000.

Penalties are cumulative and violators may be subject to additional fees if not paid within 30 days from the date the citation was issued. Unpaid citation penalties may also be subject to collection, placed as a lien against the subject property, or recovered through the Franchise Tax Board.

Source: https://www.ci.oceanside.ca.us/government/development-services/code-enforcement/administrative-citations

## Administrative Citations — Appeals

You have the right to appeal an administrative citation within 10 calendar days of the issue date. The appeal must be in writing on the form provided, include a return address, and be accompanied by the penalty amount. It may be submitted in person or via mail. If mailed, it must be postmarked within the 10 day period. An appeal will result in an administrative hearing.

Failure to properly file a written appeal within 10 calendar days constitutes a waiver of the right to an administrative hearing and adjudication of the citation, including the amount of the penalty. Appeals not properly submitted within 10 calendar days of the issue date will not be accepted.

Mail appeals to: City of Oceanside, 300 N Coast Hwy, Oceanside, CA 92054, Attn: Code Enforcement Division.

Source: https://www.ci.oceanside.ca.us/government/development-services/code-enforcement/administrative-citations/administrative-citation-appeals

## Administrative Citations — Payment

Administrative citations may be paid online, in person, or by mail. Payments may be made by credit card or by personal check, cashier's check, or money order made payable to the City of Oceanside. Write your citation number on your check or money order. To pay online, have your invoice number and account number ready (found on your invoice).

Mail payments to: City of Oceanside, Attn: Financial Services Dept., A/R, 300 N Coast Hwy, Oceanside, CA 92054. To pay in person, visit the cashier at City Hall - East Building (300 N Ditmar St).

Payment of any penalty does not excuse the obligation to correct the violation(s) nor bar further enforcement by the City.

## Code Enforcement Programs

The Code Enforcement Division manages or assists with several specialized programs designed to prevent or eliminate public nuisances: Residential Large Item Pick-Up, Vehicle Abatement, and Shopping Cart Retrieval. Code enforcement officers also perform inspections and enforce the City's storm water pollution prevention ordinances as part of the Clean Water Program (Water Quality Protection Program).

Source: https://www.ci.oceanside.ca.us/government/development-services/code-enforcement/programs

## Residential Large Item Pick-Up

As part of the City's contract with Waste Management Inc., residential customers may arrange for the disposal of a limited number of large household items such as appliances, furniture, mattresses, water heaters, rolled carpet, and refrigerators. To schedule a pick-up, contact Waste Management at 760-439-2824 no later than 48 hours before your regularly scheduled trash day. Waste Management will remove the items placed at the curb or alley on your next regularly scheduled trash pick-up day.

Items such as tires, construction debris, fencing, siding, oil, chemicals, vehicle parts, or batteries are absolutely prohibited.

Source: https://www.ci.oceanside.ca.us/government/development-services/code-enforcement/programs/residential-large-item-pick-up

## Vehicle Abatement (AVASA)

The storage of abandoned, wrecked, dismantled, or inoperable vehicles or parts is prohibited except in a garage, behind a solid six-foot fence, or other location out of public view. The AVASA program (Abandoned Vehicle Abatement Service Authority) allows the city to remove these nuisances from private property. For questions about the program or complaints about vehicle storage on private property, contact the Code Enforcement office at 760-435-3945.

Source: https://www.ci.oceanside.ca.us/government/development-services/code-enforcement/programs/vehicle-abatement
