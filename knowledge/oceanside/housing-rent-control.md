## Section 8 Housing Choice Voucher Rental Assistance

The Oceanside Housing Authority's largest program provides federal Section 8 Housing Choice Voucher rental assistance to nearly 1400 households with low incomes in the City of Oceanside. Nearly half of OHA's rental assistance households are seniors or individuals with disabilities. The program is funded by the U.S. Department of Housing and Urban Development (HUD).

Contact for Section 8 Housing Choice Voucher rental assistance: (760) 435-3360 or section8@oceansideca.org.

Source: https://www.ci.oceanside.ca.us/government/housing-neighborhood-services

## Affordable Rental Housing — Finding Housing and Eligibility

The Section 8 Housing Choice Voucher Program provides rental assistance that allows low-income families, seniors, and persons with disabilities to live in safe and affordable private market housing. The page also links to a Tenant Screening Report Reimbursement Program, Affordable Rental Housing in Oceanside (PDF), the County of San Diego Affordable Housing Resources Directory, and AffordableHousing.com rental listings.

Rent amounts and formulas differ at each community. Most apartment complexes have waiting lists. Contact the property managers to find out about waiting time, eligibility requirements and actual rental rates.

People earning a low- to moderate-income, up to 120% of the area's median household income, can qualify to live in affordable housing communities. Applicants must also have a qualified income, meet minimum credit qualifications, pass the background check and meet other eligibility criteria.

## Landlord-Tenant Issues and Tenant Protections

The relationship between a renter and the property owner is governed by federal and state laws. Renters and property owners should try to resolve problems through open communication and discussion. Issues are resolved between two private parties. The City of Oceanside does not enforce federal and state laws related to landlord-tenant issues.

Resources listed include A Guide to Tenants-Landlords Rights and Responsibilities (pdf), Rights under California Tenant Protections: Rent Caps & Evictions (pdf), and CA Attorney Consumer Alerts. Resources are also on the Landlord Tenant Issues webpage. For illegal discrimination or landlord/tenant disputes, see the Fair Housing webpage.

## Inclusionary Housing Requirements for Developers

As of February 2024, Chapter 14C of the Oceanside Municipal Code applies to residential projects of ten (10) or more units. Such projects must rent 15 percent of housing units at restricted rents affordable to low-income households, or sell 15 percent of the for-sale units at restricted prices to low or moderate-income levels. As an alternative, developers may pay an in-lieu fee of $20 a square foot.

The City's published income and rent limits are a convenience and do not override legal standards. If there is a conflict with a project's regulatory agreement, the regulatory agreement standards apply.

## Short-Term Rentals — Rules and Prohibitions

A Short-Term Rental (STR) is the rental of any legally permitted dwelling unit (or portion thereof) for occupancy for dwelling, lodging, or sleeping purposes for no more than 30 consecutive days. It does not include bed and breakfasts, hotels, motels or timeshares. The City Council adopted the STR Ordinance on June 19, 2019, and amended it on January 10, 2024, and May 8, 2024.

STRs are prohibited in mobile home parks and at non-conforming panhandle lots. New non-hosted STRs are prohibited outside of the Coastal Zone as of February 10, 2024. STRs are prohibited at properties with an ADU or JADU permitted on or after September 9, 2017. Tenants are not eligible to offer a property for STR. STR Permits are non-transferable. The City's rules are in Chapter 24 and Chapter 34, Article III of the Oceanside City Code. The City does not enforce private CC&R's.

## Short-Term Rentals — Permit Application and Fees

An owner must first apply for a Transient Occupancy Tax (TOT) certificate. If you do not hold a valid one, email BLinquiry@oceansideca.org with the property address or apply online through MUNIRevs. An STR Permit is required for all STR properties as of June 7, 2024, unless the unit is part of a gated HOA with at least 50 units that meets applicable parking requirements and has 24-hour security or on-site management, or the unit is a hosted unit in the Coastal Zone.

To apply, email BLinquiry@oceansideca.org with the property address. Staff will respond within 48 business hours. Proof of ownership may be required, and property managers must submit the Property Management Agreement. The STR Permit fee is $250, charged when the application or renewal is submitted, and permits must be renewed annually. A property inspection fee of $220 is charged at the initial application and at a minimum of every three years thereafter. Fees are non-refundable. Hosted properties require the owner to sign an affidavit affirming the property is their primary residence and that they remain on-site during rentals.
