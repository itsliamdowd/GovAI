## City Council — Overview and Members

The City of Oceanside's five-member City Council is the legislative body of the City, serves as its corporate board of directors, and is responsible for establishing City policy. The Mayor and Councilmembers are elected for staggered four-year terms. The City transitioned to District Elections beginning with Districts 1 and 2 in November 2018, and Districts 3 and 4 elected candidates in 2020. The Mayor is elected at-large. City Council offices are on the third floor, City Hall North, 300 N. Coast Highway.

## Agenda Item Position Slip (Comment Without Attending)

If you cannot attend a Council meeting, you can submit your position and any comments on an agenda item electronically with the Agenda Item Position Slip. The form is also available in hard copy at the City Clerk's Office, 300 N. Coast Highway, 2nd floor, north building. Submit the completed hard copy to the staff at that location.

All Agenda Item Position Slips received up to 4:00 pm the day of the scheduled Council meeting are provided to the Mayor and Councilmembers and become part of the official record. One person can be designated to speak on behalf of a large group while the individual members still submit their own positions or comments.

## Boards, Commissions & Committees — Membership and How to Apply

Members of most commissions, boards, and committees are Oceanside residents of all ages who represent a variety of interests and backgrounds. Education and expertise in a particular area may be considered but are not mandatory. City Council appoints members from applications submitted to the City Clerk's office. Members serve in an advisory capacity to the City Council and do not make political decisions or act in any capacity reserved for elected officials.

Applications are accepted at all times during the year, and the application is found on each Commission page. Electronic applications can be filed. Scheduled vacancies are advertised in the Union Tribune and posted on the page (Current Vacancies as of 10/14/2026). An application remains on file for one year unless you notify the City Clerk Department to pull it. Each time a vacancy exists on the group you applied for, your application is submitted to the City Council for consideration. You will be notified by letter from the City Clerk on the status of appointments.

Groups: Arts Commission; Citizen Investment Oversight Committee; Community Relations Commission; Downtown Advisory Committee; Economic Development Commission; Harbor and Beaches Advisory Committee; Historic Preservation Advisory Commission; Housing Commission; Library Board of Trustees; Manufactured Home Fair Practices Commission; Measure X Citizens' Oversight Committee; Parks and Recreation Commission; Planning Commission; Police and Fire Commission; Rehabilitation Loan Review Committee; Utilities Commission; Youth Commission. Questions: call the City Clerk Department at (760) 435-3001.

## Elections — General Information, Candidacy, and Voter Registration

The City Clerk is the City's Election Official and is responsible for conducting all municipal elections. Oceanside is a Charter City governed by a five-member City Council. Elected City officials serve four-year terms and include the Mayor, four City Council members, the City Clerk, and the City Treasurer. The Mayor, Clerk, and Treasurer are elected at-large, while Councilmembers are elected by the residents of their individual Districts. General Municipal Elections are held in November of even-numbered years and are consolidated with the statewide election through the San Diego County Registrar of Voters. In November 2026, Council seats for Districts 1 and 2 will be up for election. To find out which Council District you live in, use the City of Oceanside Population Viewer website.

Candidates must be citizens of California aged 18 or older, reside in Oceanside, and be a registered voter eligible to vote in Oceanside at the time their nomination papers are issued. Candidates must appear in person in the City Clerk's Office during the Nomination Period to be issued Nomination Papers, and receive a Candidate Handbook at that time. There is an optional cost for a Candidate's Statement in the Sample Ballot. This cost changes with each election. Candidates for City Treasurer must meet the minimum qualifications established by Ordinance No. 22-OR0719-1, required beginning with the 2024 general election. To confirm your qualifications, email CityClerk@oceansideca.org.

The City Clerk is the Local Filing Officer for filings required by the Political Reform Act, including statements of economic interest (Form 700) and candidate and political action committee disclosures. Voter registration forms can be obtained from the City Clerk's office or by registering online with the San Diego County Registrar of Voters. You can find your polling place on the Registrar of Voters website, or City Clerk staff can assist you.
