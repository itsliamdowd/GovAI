## Building Inspections — Scheduling and Access

Building Inspections can be scheduled starting at 8:00 am on any business day and up to three (3) business days in advance, based on availability. Availability depends on staffing levels and may vary. Only available dates are highlighted on the pop-up calendar. If the highlighted date is full, an alert pop-up appears and you must choose the next available date. If no dates are available, try again the next business day at 8:00 am. All inspections must be scheduled through the Civic Portal, and you must have an account to log in and schedule.

To schedule a Planning Division inspection, contact the project Planner directly to obtain the time and date. Do NOT schedule Planning Division inspections on the Online Civic Portal.

During construction, the approved plans and yellow permit card must be available to the inspector at all times during an inspection. No inspection will be conducted without the city approved set on-site. The inspector must be provided with full access to the construction area. Inspectors will not attempt to enter a house with no one home. If the house is occupied, no inspector will enter if no one of legal age is home. Construction must be built according to the city approved plans. Any construction changes to an engineered plan will require the Engineer of Record to be notified. New submittals for any changes will require plan check review.

## Building Inspections — Minimum Inspections Required

Depending on the type and extent of the work, the following minimum inspections are required: Foundations; Under-Concrete-Slab or Under Floor; Under Floor Insulation or Vapor Barriers for Concrete Slab; Rough Electrical, Plumbing and Heating; Framing; Energy and Insulation; Lath and Drywall; and Final.

The rough electrical, plumbing and heating systems must be installed and approved prior to framing inspection. Lath and drywall inspections are performed after all lath and drywall is in place but before any plaster is applied or before gypsum wallboard joints and fasteners are taped and finished. Final inspection is required when the work is completed. The sign-off is the last step prior to issuance of Certificate of Occupancy (CFO) on brand new construction. Additional department (i.e. Planning, Fire, or Public Works) finals may be required prior to building final.

## Residential Solar Inspection Procedures

For Residential Solar / Photovoltaic with or without Energy Storage System:
- The contractor must be onsite at the time of inspection.
- Have a copy of the plans submitted for the permit, the uploaded plans and express checklist, and the electrical single line diagram available for all inspections.
- If the installation or onsite conditions differ from what was submitted and permitted, revise your drawings and upload them to the portal prior to the next scheduled inspection.
- A ladder is required for inspection of the solar arrays and roof access clearances.
- No job card is needed onsite. All approvals will be available in the portal.
- If batteries are installed within a structure, Fire Department inspection and approval is required. Fire Department contact: (714) 288-2546 or (714) 288-2536.

## Public Hearing Notices and CEQA Review Periods

Many projects require discretionary land use approval before construction can begin or a business can open. Depending on the project, approval may also be required from the Design Review Committee, Planning Commission, or City Council. Smaller projects may be approved by staff. ## Historic Property Submittal Requirements

Projects involving properties in one of the city's four historic districts have new submittal requirements. They apply to building additions, new infill construction, and accessory dwelling units (ADUs). As part of a Land Use Application submittal, applicants must provide an evaluation of existing conditions and project compliance with the Secretary of Interior's Standards for Rehabilitation and the applicable City historic preservation design standards. For non-historic properties (non-contributors), the project is evaluated for potential impacts on the integrity of the historic district. The report must be prepared by a historic preservation professional meeting the Secretary of Interior's Professional Qualifications Standards. Consultants may be selected from the City's list of qualified historic preservation professionals.

For accessory structures proposed to be converted to ADUs or other habitable space, the applicant must also submit an assessment of the existing condition of the siding, framing, windows, doors, and roofing, showing the structure can be rehabilitated and adapted to the new use. If the structure is not feasible to rehabilitate, the applicant is given information about the demolition review process. Demolition of the structure should be the last resort.

Forms: Historic Property Construction Acknowledgement Form; Minor Design Review Application. Contact: Community Development Department, 300 E. Chapman Ave., Orange, CA 92866, (714) 744-7220. City Hall Hours: Monday - Friday 8:00 a.m. - 5:00 p.m.

## Special Event Park Permits

A Special Event Park Permit is required for events open to the public, private events with 200 or more guests, or events featuring other components such as vendors, booths, displays, etc. in a City park. All special events with 200 or more attendees must be submitted through the Civic Portal (select Special Events). In-person submissions are no longer accepted.

The page gives two sets of timing and fee figures. The top of the page says applications must be submitted 2–5 months before your intended event date, and a $240 application fee will be charged after the initial review, in addition to standard park permit fees. The Special Event Park Permit Guidelines say an application can be submitted up to five (5) months in advance, but no later than sixty (60) days prior to the event, and a $60 Special Event Park Permit Application fee must be submitted via Civic Portal after initial application review. Upon receipt, a hold for that day is placed on the requested park facility.

If approved, the applicant pays all applicable park permit fees and deposits within ten (10) days of notice of approval. Events in a City park require an event layout noting set-up specifications and a certificate of insurance with an endorsed letter certifying that the City of Orange is additionally insured on the policy. Some special events may require additional documentation.

## Park Rules for Permit Holders

- Monies may not be exchanged in City parks.
- Alcoholic beverages are not allowed.
- Unauthorized vehicles may not drive onto City parks.
- Vendor equipment and jumpers must be hand-carted into the park. Jumpers may not exceed 15x20 feet and must operate from their own generator.
- Cooking devices (other than park BBQ's) must be at least 20 feet from any City building, structure, or overhang.
- Free distribution of food, beverages, and/or supplies to the general public is not allowed.
- Walkways must remain unobstructed.
- Smoking is prohibited at all park buildings, including entrances, at or near playgrounds, and at or near active sport fields.
- Amplified sound is allowed from 9 a.m. - 10 p.m. but may not exceed a noise level of 55 decibels.

## Park Permit Cancellations, Refunds and Inclement Weather

Permit cancellations must be submitted in writing to csinfo@cityoforange.org by the permit holder prior to the event date. Refer to your permit for applicable fees. Approved permit refunds may take up to 6 weeks to receive.

The City of Orange does not refund permit fees for inclement weather cancellations. Permit holders may reschedule, but the request must be made to the Community Services Department within 24-hours of the cancellation. Rescheduled dates depend on availability and may only be reserved up to three months in advance.

## Business License — Who Needs One and How to Apply

The City of Orange requires a business license for all businesses operating in the City, regardless of trade, profession or occupation and regardless of whether the main business address is located within the City (OMC 5.06.015). Any business coming into the City to work must also apply. The licenses cover only the City of Orange, not the County of Orange or any other cities in Orange County. Licenses are renewed annually on a 12 months anniversary basis.

You can apply online or submit your application via mail, fax, or bring it to City Hall. Some businesses requiring extra information will not be able to apply online. Application forms: Inside Application (businesses based inside the City), Outside Application (businesses based outside the City), Contractors or Subcontractors, Apartment Rental Property Owners, Commercial Rental Property Owners.
