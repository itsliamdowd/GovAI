## Agendas, Minutes, and Public Hearing Notices

Before each meeting, the City posts an agenda. Agendas are posted within 72 hours before a meeting at City Hall, the Main Library, and electronically on the City's website. Upcoming meeting dates and agendas are on the Meeting Calendar page. All agenda materials are provided in formats accessible to the public, including individuals with disabilities.

After each meeting, the City provides public access to minutes and video recordings (when meetings are streamed). Official signed minutes are posted once finalized by staff and, when applicable, approved by the legislative body. Public hearing notices are available through the City's online document portal. They are also published in a newspaper of general circulation and displayed on the official posting board at City Hall.

## Public Records Requests

The City supports the public's right to access public records and provides access in accordance with the California Public Records Act (GC 7920-7931). Many records are available online, including agendas and staff reports, minutes, the Orange Municipal Code, ordinances, resolutions, budget and financial reporting, and permit and property history for properties constructed after 1989. For records of properties constructed prior to 1989, submit a public records request.

Records not available online can be requested through the online Public Records Request form, or in person at the Office of the City Clerk, 300 E. Chapman Ave., Orange, CA 92866. Fill out the entire form to expedite your request, and clearly specify the records you seek. The City determines within 10 days from receipt whether the request seeks copies of disclosable public records [GC 6253(c)].

To inspect records, you must make an appointment to return at a later date and time. For more information, call the Office of the City Clerk at (714) 744-5500.

## Boards, Commissions, and Committees — Applying to Serve

Residents of Orange can apply at any time to serve in an advisory capacity on one of the City's boards, commissions, and committees. Applications are accepted on an on-going basis and are kept active for a two-year period. All boards, commissions, and committees are subject to the Ralph M. Brown Act.

The Local Appointments List (Maddy Act, Government Code Section 54972) is posted annually by December 31. Notice of Vacancies are posted on the City's website, at City Hall and the Main Library. Questions: City Clerk's Office at (714) 744-5500.

## Ethics and Sexual Harassment Prevention Training (AB 1234 and AB 1661)

Under Government Code Section 53235 (AB 1234), if a local agency provides compensation, salary, or a stipend to, or reimburses the expenses of, a member of its legislative body, its officials must complete biennial training in general ethical principles and ethics laws. Training is due no later than one year from the first day of service.

Under Government Code Section 53237.1 (AB 1661), local agency officials must receive sexual harassment prevention training and education within six months of taking office. It must be repeated every two years if any compensation, salary, or stipend is provided.

Copies of Certificates of Completion must be provided to the City Clerk. They are public records and must be kept for at least five years.

## Passports — Appointments, Renewals, and Urgent Travel

The City Clerk's Office is an authorized U.S. Passport Acceptance Facility. Passport services, including passport photos, are by appointment only; schedule through the online Passport Appointment system. Walk-in passport services are available at the County of Orange. Contact: City Clerk's Office, 300 E. Chapman Ave., Orange, CA 92866; (714) 744-5500; fax (714) 744-5515.

The City of Orange does not process adult passport renewals. If your passport was issued less than 15 years ago and you were at least 16 when it was issued, renew by mail using Form DS-82 or online. Otherwise, complete a first-time application (Form DS-11) in person with required documents.

For life-or-death emergencies requiring international travel within 72 hours, contact the U.S. Department of State to schedule a passport agency appointment. For international travel within 2 weeks (non-emergency), call 1-877-487-2778 to arrange an appointment.

## Passport Appointment Requirements

Bring a printed, completed Form DS-11 (black ink) and do not sign it until requested by a passport agent. If your passport was lost, stolen, or damaged, reapply with Form DS-11 and Form DS-64. Proof of U.S. citizenship must be a previous U.S. Passport, a certified copy of the applicant's birth certificate, or a Certificate of Naturalization. Photocopies are not acceptable.

Applicants under 16 must appear with both parents or legal guardians. If one parent is absent, the applying parent must present a notarized consent document (Form DS-3053), a court document proving sole custody, or a death certificate. Applicants 16 to 17 must appear with one parent who can present acceptable proof of identity.

A valid original photo ID is required: for applicants 18 and older, the applicant's; for ages 16 or 17, one parent's; for ages 15 and under, both parents'. Out-of-state identification requires an additional form of identification such as a school ID, social security card, or medical card.

The City provides passport photos at $15 per applicant. Your own photo must be in color, two inches by two inches with a white or off-white background, taken within the last six months, in normal street attire (no uniforms, camouflage clothing, glasses, or head attire).
