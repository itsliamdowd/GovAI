## Density Bonus Program for Housing Developers

The City has a Density Bonus Ordinance (Orange Municipal Code Section 17.15) similar to the State Density Bonus Law. State law takes precedence when the City's ordinance is silent or lags behind changes in State Law. The ordinance applies to proposed housing projects of five or more dwelling units. The density bonus ranges from 5% to 25%, depending on the percentage of units targeted for very low or low income families.

Eligible projects are affordable housing, transitional housing, student housing, condominium conversions, transit-adjacent housing, and senior housing. The City and developer enter into a Density Bonus Agreement that specifies the number of affordable units, income levels, affordability period, and maximum rent or sale price. Developers must submit a Land Use Project Application. Questions go to Chad Ortlieb, Senior Planner.

Source: https://www.cityoforange.org/our-city/departments/community-development/housing/affordable-housing

## Development Incentives, Deferred Impact Fees and Gap Funding

The City offers one to three concessions and/or waivers to housing projects that receive a Density Bonus. These include reductions in development standards such as setbacks, minimum unit square footage, parking standards, or landscape standards. Concessions and incentives must result in decreased development costs.

The City may defer impact fees to coincide with occupancy permits for affordable housing projects. The developer must meet with the City and negotiate the terms through a Development Agreement.

To apply for HOME or redevelopment set-aside gap funding, email cdbg@cityoforange.org with: (1) name of applicant, main contact person, email and phone number; (2) proposed project address and number of units; (3) amount needed.

## Short Term Rentals — Permits, Fees and Waiting List

The City defines a short term rental (STR) as the rental of a residential unit for 30 days or less. The STR ordinance is effective June 10, 2021. The maximum number of STR registrations is 125. That limit has been reached and the application process is closed.

To join the waiting list, email your name, address, and phone number to stradmin@cityoforange.org. The FAQ also asks for your City of Orange property address and telephone number.

Any property owner in a zone that permits residential use, excluding mobile home parks, who rents all or part of their property for 30 days or less must obtain a Short-Term Rental Permit ($491) and a Business License ($283), which includes a non-refundable $83 application fee. STR permits expire one year from the date of issuance, must be renewed annually, and are non-transferable.

## Short Term Rentals — Permit Renewal (Owners Portal)

Permitholders cannot renew their STR permit and business license if there is unreported and/or unpaid Transient Occupancy Tax (TOT) on the account. To renew, submit:
- Short Term Rental Permit Renewal Fee $491
- Business License Renewal Fee $245
- Good Neighbor Policy Proof of Notification
- Short Term Rental location diagram

The Good Neighbor Policy must be provided to all forward-facing and adjacent neighbors. Proof is the Good Neighbor Policy Acknowledgment Receipt form, or an alternative such as a certified mailing. The diagram requirement is under OMC Section 5.94.040.B.

Any noise disturbances or violations can result in fines and possible eviction, as well as fines and revocation of the STR permit and business license. Questions: (714) 744-2264 or STRadmin@cityoforange.org.

## Short Term Rentals — Transient Occupancy Tax (TOT)

TOT is owed to the City by anyone who occupies a room for 30 days or less within the City of Orange. It must be paid to the owner or the listing platform on behalf of the City. Taxable receipts include nightly rents, weekly rents, standard cleaning fees, pet fees, internet charges, late check-out fees, and extra person fees. They exclude refundable deposits, platform fees, and items in a special package rate that are subject to CA sales tax.

TOT is required to be collected commencing October 1, 2021 and remitted monthly, regardless of STR permit status. Bookings made prior to July 1, 2021 with TOT not included in the rental cost will not be assessed, though TOT receipts are subject to audit.

Remittance is due by the last day of the following month (for example, January's TOT is due February 28). Late payments incur a 10% penalty plus 0.5% interest per month. A second 10% penalty applies at the 2nd delinquency date. TOT can be paid online through the "Pay TOT Online" link.
