## Housing Element Update — Overview

The City's Housing Element is required by State Law. As part of the City's General Plan, it provides an overview and guides housing programs development, emphasizing affordable and special needs housing, removal of governmental constraints to housing development, and equal access to housing. It must be updated every 8 years.

For the 6th eight-year cycle (2021-2029), the City Council adopted the 2021-2029 Housing Element on February 7, 2022. The State Department of Housing and Community Development (HCD) did not certify the document. HCD provided comments to further revise it and instructed the City to implement a rezoning program concurrent with the revised Housing Element Update. The Housing Element has since been further revised in response to HCD's comments, and the accompanying rezoning program is being brought forward to City Council.

Questions or comments can be sent to Shaun Temple, Interim Community Development Director, at stemple@sealbeachca.gov.

## Housing Element Rezoning Program

HCD instructed the City to implement a rezoning program to allow more housing capacity. The rezoning includes:
- Creating a new mixed-use (commercial and residential) zone for the City's commercial shopping centers.
- Rezoning the former oil production site at 99 Marina (next to Marina Park and Bridgeport) as high density residential.
- Again allowing new residential on the 2nd floor-only along Main Street.

Related documents posted on the page: zoning map updates for the Old Town & Bridgeport Area, Marina Hill & Hellman Ranch Area, Leisure World Area, and Rossmoor Center Area; amendments to the Zoning Code (Title 11) to include the new Mixed Use Zone (MC/RHD); and an amendment to the Main Street Specific Plan to allow residential on the 2nd floor above commercial uses.
