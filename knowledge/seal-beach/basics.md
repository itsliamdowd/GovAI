## City Council — Meetings and Public Comment

The City Council meets the second and fourth Monday of each month. Unless otherwise announced, meetings are held in the City Council Chambers at 211 Eighth Street and typically begin at 5:00 p.m. with a study session or closed session meeting. The televised portion starts at approximately 7:00 p.m. It can be viewed on cable television on channel 3 or on the City's website.

The Oral Communications/Public Comments portion of the meeting is towards the beginning of the meeting. During this time anyone may address the City Council on any issue for a maximum of five minutes.

Meetings are rebroadcast on cable channel 3 the following Sunday at 4:00 p.m. A DVD copy of the meetings, produced at SBTV-3, can be purchased for $15.

Source: https://sealbeachca.gov/seal-beach-government/city-council/

## Meeting Agendas, Minutes and Videos

The City Council meets the 2nd and 4th Monday of each month (or on Tuesday if Monday is a holiday). The Planning Commission meets the 1st and 3rd Monday of each month (or on Tuesday if Monday is a holiday). Live streaming begins at 7:00 p.m. for both.

State law mandates that all agendas be posted at least 72 hours before a meeting. City Council and Planning Commission agendas are published online and in the City Clerk's Office no later than the Friday before the meeting date. They are also posted in the kiosk adjacent to the Council Chamber doors.

Other Boards, Commissions, and Committees include the City Tree Advisory Board, Recreation and Parks Commission, Environmental Quality Control Board, Ad Hoc General Plan/Local Coastal Plan Citizens Advisory Committee, and Seal Beach Cable Communications Foundation. ## Speaking at Meetings, Closed Sessions and Accessibility

Members of the public may speak under "Public Comments" or on any item listed on the Agenda. By State law, closed session is not open to the public. The public has an opportunity to speak on any closed session item before the Council goes into closed session.

To find out what happened at a City Council meeting, call the City Clerk's office the day after a decision. Council approved minutes are available on the City's website or from the City Clerk's Office.

If you require disability related modifications or accommodations, including auxiliary aids or services, to attend or participate in City Council, Planning Commission or other Boards, Commissions or Committees meetings, call the City Clerk's office at (562) 431-2527 at least 48 hours prior to the meeting. For additional information, call (562) 431-2527 x1304.

## Choose Civility Policy

On October 22, 2018, the Seal Beach City Council adopted Resolution 6870 establishing Council Policy 100-14 "City of Seal Beach Civility Policy." All persons attending public meetings or engaged in business in a City facility should strive to:
- Treat everyone courteously.
- Listen to others respectfully.
- Exercise self-control.
- Give open-minded consideration to all viewpoints.
- Focus on the issues and avoid personalizing debate.
- Embrace respectful disagreement and dissent as democratic rights, inherent components of an inclusive public process, and tools for forging sound decisions.

## November 3, 2026 General Election — Voter Registration and Key Dates

To vote, you must be a U.S. citizen at the time you register, at least 18 years of age on or before the next election, not in prison or on parole for a felony conviction, and not found mentally incompetent to vote by a court. You must register to vote at least 15 days prior to the election date. Voter registrations are filed by county. You must reregister every time you move, change your name, or change your political affiliation.

## City Council Candidates — Eligibility, Term and Nomination Papers

The 2026 City Council election covers District One (Old Town & Surfside Colony), District Three (The Hill, Coves, Bridgeport & Heron Point), and District Five (Leisure World). Council terms are four years with a term limit of two terms (8 years). Council Members are paid $600 per month (MC 3.05.010 – Ord. #1561).

Candidates must be a citizen of the United States, a registered voter, a resident of the City of Seal Beach and Council District, and 18 years of age or older. A person is not eligible to hold a position as City Council Member unless he or she is, at the time of assuming office, an elector of the City/Council District and was a registered voter of the City at the time the nomination papers were issued to the candidate.

The nomination period runs from Monday, July 13, 2026, through Friday, August 7, 2026, at 5:00pm. It is by appointment only. Contact City Clerk Gloria Harper at gharper@sealbeachca.gov or (562) 431-2527 x1305 for an appointment to pull nomination papers.

## Ethics and Fiscal Training Records — Public Requests

Under AB 1234, certain City officials who receive compensation or expense reimbursement must complete ethics training every two years. As of January 1, 2026, SB 827 requires City officials to complete fiscal and financial management training every two years. Officials serving as of January 1, 2026 must complete their first training by January 1, 2028, so some records may not yet be available.

Copies of a Certificate of Completion may be obtained by submitting a Public Records Act request. Include a brief description of the records requested, First and Last Name, Email Address, Phone Number, and Mailing Address. The City will respond in accordance with the California Public Records Act. For additional information, contact the City Clerk's Office at (562) 431-2527.

## Request City Council Recognition (Certificates and Proclamations)

Certificates of Achievement, Commendation and/or Service are given to residents, businesses and groups that have achieved something significant or have given exceptional service to the community. They may be given to Seal Beach residents, Seal Beach businesses, or to an individual, business or organization that has provided service directly to the City. Certificates given at a community event may be presented by the Mayor or another Council Member if the Mayor is not in attendance.

A Proclamation may be prepared for a special occasion or commemorative event and may be presented at a City Council meeting or a specific event. It should always have some connection to the City of Seal Beach, and is always signed by the sitting Mayor. Questions: contact the Deputy City Clerk at x1306.
