## Building Permits — How to Obtain

The Seal Beach Building & Safety Division processes building, plumbing, electrical, and mechanical permits. To obtain permits, applications and fees must be submitted to the Building & Safety Division. Once you receive your building permit, you may begin work on the project. Depending on the complexity of the project, inspections are needed as you complete various stages.

The Building Division offers electronic submittals for building permit applications and plans.

Source: https://sealbeachca.gov/departments/community-development/building-safety/permit-contractors-license/

## Plan Check Process

Plan check ensures a project meets code requirements and is safe before construction starts. It reviews code compliance for structural, architectural, grading, and fire safety as shown on submitted plans. Projects requiring plan check include new structures (residence, commercial, industrial building), room additions, remodeling, and tenant improvements, among others. A site plan may be required for any equipment placed outside.

A plan review fee is collected at the time of plan review submittal. Fees are subject to change after official review of the plans by the Department.

## Requesting a Building Inspection

Inspections ensure that the work conforms to the permit, local codes, and the submitted plans. If you have an account in the Civic Access Portal, request your inspection through the portal. If you have a legacy project initiated before the portal, use the legacy request button on the page. All inspections shall be submitted through the portal. Phone calls are available for general inquiries, but the portal is the required method for scheduling inspections.

Inspection requests are responded to at the end of the day and typically executed within two business days.

## Electric Vehicle Charger Permits

Level 1 chargers plug directly into a standard 120 volt receptacle outlet. They do not require a permit from the Building and Safety Division as long as there are no changes to the electrical system to provide the 120 volt receptacle. Any changes to an electrical system require a permit prior to commencing work. Level 2 systems require a 240-volt electrical circuit and typically require an electrical permit and inspections. You will need to provide basic information demonstrating electrical service capacity for the added load.

Single Family Residential: an Electrical Permit is required to install an Electric Vehicle Charging Station (EVCS) onsite or in an attached or detached garage. Approval by an association may be required for permit approval. Multi-Unit Residential, Commercial, and Larger Scale projects: a combination Building/Electrical Permit is required.

Submittal packages should include, at a minimum:
1. A completed Building Permit Application.
2. A completed Residential and Non-residential Checklist for Permitting Electric Vehicles and Electric Vehicle Service Equipment (EVSE).
3. A site plan and electrical plan with a single-line diagram. The site plan must be drawn to scale with full dimensions and must show the location, size, and use of all structures, the location of the electrical panel for the charging system, and a description of the type of charging system and mounting. An electric load calculation worksheet and any additional information based on the checklist responses are also needed.

Building and Safety Division staff will review your application and submittal information within ten business days. For fees, call 562-431-2527 x 1323. Submit applications in person at 211 Eighth Street, 2nd Floor, Seal Beach, CA, or electronically. Electronic submittal is available for building permit applications only.

Once permits are issued and the station is installed, it must be inspected for final approval. To schedule an inspection, call the inspection request hotline and leave a message before 4:00 p.m. to be placed on the inspection schedule for the next day: (562) 431-2527 x1519. For other inspection questions, call (562) 431-2527 x1323.

Source: https://sealbeachca.gov/building-safety/electric-vehicle-charging-stations/

## Solar Permits — SolarAPP+

Seal Beach has partnered with SolarAPP+ to streamline permits for eligible small solar projects. SolarAPP+ provides a code-compliance check for the majority of residential, roof-mounted, retrofit photovoltaic systems. Users must be a licensed contractor and must be added to the SolarAPP+ program eligibility list before submission to the City. Individual users in Leisure World shall obtain Golden Rain Foundation permits to the City prior to submission on SolarAPP+.

Steps:
1. Register and submit your design through the SolarAPP+ webpage.
2. A processing fee is charged by SolarAPP+.
3. Download the approved plans.

Then apply for a City of Seal Beach Electrical Photovoltaic Building permit. Contractors must have an active Seal Beach Business license. Pay for the permit and return the signed permit card to the City. Complete the inspection request form to schedule an inspection.

Source: https://sealbeachca.gov/departments/community-development/building-safety/solarapp/

## ADU Amnesty Program (AB 2533)

Under AB 2533, a streamlined process is available to legalize an unpermitted accessory dwelling unit (ADU) or junior accessory dwelling unit (JADU) constructed prior to January 1, 2020. ## Residential and Guest Parking Permits

Residential parking permits and guest permits are purchased and managed online. Steps: (1) create a resident account; (2) provide vehicle information for resident permit(s); (3) provide proof of residency through DMV verification or validation of residence. Only one account is allowed per address.

Residential permits cost $20 per permit and are effective annually from November 1st through October 31st. Annual renewal takes place October 1st to 31st. Permits are checked using license plates, so there are no physical permits to display.

Guest permits cost $30 per permit, with a limit of two per household. They are virtual only and valid annually from November 1st through October 31st.

Source: https://sealbeachca.gov/departments/parking/parking-permits/

## Merchant and Contractor Parking Permits

Merchant permits are valid from July 1st through June 30th each year. They are only available to businesses with active licenses, are non-transferable, and require vehicle information for each pass. The cost is $50.

Contractor permits are for contractors doing business in the city and are valid for 12 months from the date of purchase. They are only valid from 7:00 am to 5:00 pm, Monday through Friday, and require an active Contractor Business License. The cost is $60, and they are non-transferable.

For Boat Launch, Pier Platform Permits, or Contractors permits, email parkingpermits@sealbeachca.gov.

Source: https://sealbeachca.gov/departments/parking/parking-permits/

## Business License — General Information and Fees

Under Seal Beach Municipal Code Title 5, a business license is required for all businesses operating within City limits, including temporary activities and contractors with offices outside the City. Apply, renew, pay, and update information online through the Manage Licenses Online link. For questions, email sealbeach@hdlgov.com or call (562) 269-2894.

For most businesses, the current fee is $227 per year. Businesses listed in Municipal Code Section 5.10.025 Part C and Part D pay the $227 general fee plus additional amounts as described in the Code. An exemption or reduced license is available for businesses with an annual gross revenue/receipts of $25,000. To qualify, submit a copy of the most recent federal tax return (Schedule C or Schedule E from the Form 1040 or a Form 1065) with the application, and again annually at renewal. This policy does not apply to home occupation permits. Banks, nonprofit organizations, and qualifying disabled veterans are not required to pay the annual tax but must obtain a business license prior to conducting business in Seal Beach.

Business licenses are valid from July 1st through June 30th. Businesses applying after January 31st are charged a reduced rate, as the license also expires June 30th.

Source: https://sealbeachca.gov/departments/finance/business-license-information/

## New Business License Applications

An application fee and an inspection fee are required with each application. Both must be paid in advance or the application will not be processed. Once processed, you receive an invoice for the business license fee due. Payment of the business license does not constitute zoning, building, or fire code approval. Check with the Department of Community Development to determine whether your business can legally be established at your location.

New food service establishments must also comply with Seal Beach Municipal Code Chapter 9.25 (Fats, Oil and Grease Management and Discharge Control). Upon confirmation of compliance, a FOG Permit is issued annually and requires payment of the annual permit fee.

Source: https://sealbeachca.gov/departments/finance/business-license-information/

## Business License Renewal and Late Penalties

Business licenses expire June 30th each year and must be renewed annually. Renewal forms are mailed to all active licensees by June 1st. Payments are due July 1st for general business licenses and home occupation permits. For licenses based on gross receipts, the due date is August 1st. Penalties are cumulative.

General Business Licenses and Home Occupation Permits:
- August 1st: penalty equal to 25% of the business license amount
- September 1st: 50%
- October 1st: 100%

Gross Receipts Business Licenses:
- September 1st: 25%
- October 1st: 50%
- November 1st: 100%

Source: https://sealbeachca.gov/departments/finance/business-license-information/

## Home Occupation Permits

Municipal Code Section 11.4.05.60 requires a valid Home Occupation Permit, along with a City business license, for individuals conducting business from their home. Not all business types are permitted for home occupation; review the Municipal Code or contact the Community Development Department.

A reduced business license rate is available for disabled persons or seniors (with proof of age over 65) engaged in a home occupation where gross revenue/receipts do not exceed $10,000 (before deductions) in any calendar year. To qualify, submit a copy of the most recent federal tax return (Schedule C or Schedule E from the Form 1040 or a Form 1065). A home occupation with a start date in the initial year of business must pay the appropriate full business tax/permit fee.

## Special Event Permits

A Special Event Permit is required for one-time uses or events that do not commonly take place on a property, events on public property, and events requiring street closures or other private use of public areas. Examples include large gatherings, a party on private property with live music, beach weddings (ceremony only, no receptions), carnivals/fairs, Christmas tree and pumpkin patch lots, haunted houses, high school graduation night events, live music/outdoor dining, outdoor/sidewalk sales and street fairs, public fireworks displays, and large temporary structures like tents or canopies. Failure to secure a permit will result in termination of your event, citation by the City, or both.

Submit the application and all supporting documentation together to the Recreation and Community Services Department. Submittal requirements are on pages 3 and 4 of the application packet. Incomplete applications will not be accepted. Applications can also be submitted via the City's public web portal, where you can track your permit status.

The application must be submitted no later than 4:00 p.m., thirty (30) days prior to the event date. Review takes a minimum of 30 days. Events at Eisenhower Park or on the beach during peak season are not guaranteed. Peak season begins the Friday of Memorial Day weekend and ends on the Tuesday after Labor Day. Any written appeal of Staff's decision must be filed within ten (10) calendar days of receipt of the decision, and a fee must be submitted when the appeal is filed.

Questions: Anthony Nguyen, Community Services Coordinator, anguyen@sealbeachca.gov, (562) 431-2527 x 1344, City Hall: 211 8th St Seal Beach CA 90740.
