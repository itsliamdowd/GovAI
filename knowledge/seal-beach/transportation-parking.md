## Parking Citations

The Traffic Division enforces Seal Beach parking ordinances and manages meters and parking fees. Citations can be paid online or in person at the Seal Beach Police Department.

Common California Vehicle Code violations listed by the City: 22500(B) Parking in a Crosswalk; 5204 Current Registration Tabs; 22507.8 Parking in a Disabled-Designated Space; 22514 Parking near a fire hydrant; 22500.1 Parking in a Fire Lane; 22500(F) Parking on the Sidewalk; 22502(A) Curb Parking.

Common Seal Beach Municipal Code violations listed by the City: 20.015 Head in Parking Only; 15.010 No Parking Areas; 15.105(F)(1) Expired Parking Meter; 15.105(F)(5) Exceeded Posted Time Limit; 20.010 Failure to pay for parking; 15.105(F)(6) Parking outside of parking stall. For help, contact Parking at (562) 799-4100 x1119 or x1106 or parkingpermits@sealbeachca.gov.

## Paid Parking Program Overview and Passport Zones

The City's Paid Parking Program covers residential permits, visitor parking, other permits, Main Street and beach lot parking, and citations. Visitor parking can be requested online or in person at the Police Department during business hours. Payment can be made by phone.

Passport Mobile Zones: 120 Main Street Lot #9079; 300 (E) Main Street Lot #9077; 301 (W) Main Street Lot #9078; 1st Street Beach Lot #9074; 8th Street Beach Lot #9075; 10th Street Beach Lot #9076.

Source: https://sealbeachca.gov/departments/parking/

## Main Street Parking Lots

Paid parking is enforced daily from 8:00 am to 6:00 pm, and parking must be paid by plate. Locations: 120 Main Street Lot (2 pay stations, Passport Mobile Zone #9079); 300 (E) Main Street Lot (1 pay station, Zone #9077); 301 (W) Main Street Lot (1 pay station, Zone #9078).

The hourly rate is $1.00 with a 2-hour time limit. There is no re-parking within 150 feet of the current parking space for a minimum of 4 hours (SBMC 8.15.120). Pay stations accept credit/debit cards, coins, mobile and contactless payments. The Passport mobile payment system is available at www.passportparking.com.

Vehicles over 16 feet are prohibited in these lots, and each vehicle must occupy one space. Overnight parking is prohibited daily from 12:00 am to 6:00 am, but permit exemptions are available. For help, contact Parking at (562) 799-4100 x1119 or x1106 or parkingpermits@sealbeachca.gov.

Source: https://sealbeachca.gov/departments/parking/main-street-parking/

## Beach Lot Parking Permits

Beach permits are annual permits that give access to park in the 1st Street, 8th Street, and 10th Street Lots. They may be purchased by residents and non-residents and are non-transferable.

Cost: $117 for residents; $70 for resident seniors (65+ / income less than $35K/year); $180 for non-residents. The city does not replace lost or stolen beach permits, and they must be purchased at the total cost to replace them. For help, contact Parking at (562) 799-4100 x1119 or x1106 or parkingpermits@sealbeachca.gov.

Source: https://sealbeachca.gov/departments/parking/beach-lot-parking/

## Residential and Guest Parking Permits

Residents can purchase and manage residential permits, including guest permits, online. Step 1: create a resident account. Step 2: provide vehicle information for resident permit(s). Step 3: provide proof of residency through DMV verification or validation of residence. Only one account is allowed per address.

Residential permits are checked by license plate, so no physical permit is displayed. A residential permit costs $20 per permit and is effective annually from November 1st through October 31st. The annual renewal process takes place from October 1st to 31st.

Guest permits cost $30 per permit, with a limit of two per household. They are virtual only and do not require a physical display in the vehicle. They are valid annually from November 1st through October 31st.

Source: https://sealbeachca.gov/departments/parking/parking-permits/

## Merchant and Contractor Parking Permits

Merchant permits are valid from July 1st through June 30th each year and are only available to businesses with active licenses. They are non-transferable and require vehicle information for each pass obtained. The cost is $50.

Contractor permits are for contractors doing business in the city and are valid for 12 months from the date of purchase. They are only valid from 7:00 am to 5:00 pm, Monday through Friday, and require an active Contractor Business License to qualify. A contractor permit costs $60 and is non-transferable.

For Boat Launch, Pier Platform Permits, or Contractors permits, email parkingpermits@sealbeachca.gov.

Source: https://sealbeachca.gov/departments/parking/parking-permits/

## Senior Transportation Services — Eligibility and Registration

The Senior Transportation Services program provides free transportation services for Seal Beach residents ages 60 and over. Only registered users can use the fixed-route shuttle and Dial-a-Ride service.

To register as a new rider, download the Senior Transportation Program Application and provide a copy of a photo ID showing proof of Seal Beach residency and date of birth. Accepted ID includes a CA driver's license, CA ID Card, or a Golden Rain Foundation ID card for Leisure World residents.

For questions, contact Anthony Nguyen, anguyen@sealbeachca.gov, (562) 431-2527 x1344, or Iris Lee, ilee@sealbeachca.gov, (562) 431-2527 x1322.

Source: https://sealbeachca.gov/departments/public-works/traffic-transportation/

## Senior Shuttle Service

The Senior Shuttle is a 16-passenger shuttle equipped with wheelchair access. It runs on a fixed route throughout major City stops, including Seal Beach Pier/Old Town area, Leisure World, and Rossmoor Shopping Center/North Seal Beach Community Center. Operating hours are Monday through Friday 9:00a.m. to 11:00a.m., 12:00p.m. to 4:00p.m.

Source: https://sealbeachca.gov/departments/public-works/traffic-transportation/

## Senior Dial-a-Ride

Dial-a-Ride is provided by California Yellow Cab. It is a reservation-based service operating Monday through Friday, 7:30a.m. to 5:00p.m. There is no cost to use the service. Rides go to any location within City limits, and up to three (3) miles outside City limits and within Orange County for non-emergency medical purposes. Users may also go to the VA Hospital in Long Beach.

To make a reservation: (1) call (877) 224-8294 at least 24-hours in advance; (2) provide your Senior Transportation Program ID (#SBXXXX); (3) request "ROUNDTRIP" if you need a ride back home; (4) provide the pick-up date and time; (5) provide the pick-up and drop-off locations; (6) note any special accommodation needed. Same day reservations to/from any location cannot be guaranteed.

To confirm whether you are already registered, call California Yellow Cab, Melissa Gomez or Cristina Valle, at (714) 427-2555.

Source: https://sealbeachca.gov/departments/public-works/traffic-transportation/

## Safety Action Plan and Local Roadway Safety Plan

The City of Seal Beach Safety Action Plan (SAP) aims to develop a comprehensive strategy to minimize fatalities and severe accidents on local roadways. It was prepared in compliance with Safe Roads and Streets For All (SS4A) requirements and incorporates a Local Roadway Safety Plan (LRSP). The SAP was adopted on January 13, 2025 and amended on June 16, 2025. The Technical Memo shall be ratified by Council in July 2025.

The LRSP identifies a framework to identify, analyze, and develop traffic safety enhancements on the City's roadway network. It analyzes collision data to identify high-crash locations, high-risk locations, and citywide trends.

Source: https://sealbeachca.gov/departments/public-works/traffic-transportation/
