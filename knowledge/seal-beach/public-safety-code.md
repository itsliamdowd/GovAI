## Fire Services — Orange County Fire Authority

Fire services for Seal Beach are provided by the Orange County Fire Authority (OCFA), a regional fire service agency. OCFA serves 23 cities in Orange County, including Seal Beach, and all unincorporated areas. It protects nearly 2 million residents from 77 fire stations throughout Orange County. Seal Beach is part of OCFA Division 1.

The Division 1 Division Chief is Craig Covey, phone (714) 638-6343.

## Ask City Hall — Questions and Service Requests

Residents can email AskCityHall@sealbeachca.gov with questions. Questions can be about COVID-19 or can be a request such as fixing a street light that is out. The City's goal is to respond within 72 hours or sooner, depending on the volume of inquiries and other emerging issues that may require resources to maintain an essential service.
