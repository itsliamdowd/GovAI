## Short-Term Vacation Rentals & Transient Occupancy Tax (TOT)

The City of Solana Beach has a Short Term Vacation Rentals (STVR) & TOT page for property owners who want to rent out a property for 7 to 30 consecutive days. The same page covers the Transient Occupancy Tax (TOT), which is charged to transient guests in hotels/motels and short-term property rentals.

For questions, contact the City of Solana Beach at (858) 720-2400.
