## City Council — Structure and Scheduling a Meeting

The City of Solana Beach is a general law city with a City Manager/Council form of government. The City Council is a legislative body made up of a directly elected Mayor and four Councilmembers, each elected within their District. The Mayor and Councilmembers also serve on Council Standing Committees, some Citizen Commissions, and Regional Governing Boards.

To schedule a meeting with a City Councilmember, contact the Executive Assistant at 858-720-2430 or jguy@cityofsolanabeach.ca.gov.

## Council Standing Committees

Council Standing Committees are long-term or permanent committees made up of two Councilmembers. They must comply with the noticing and posting provisions of the Ralph M. Brown Act (Govt. Code section 54950, et seq.). They are not required to schedule regular meetings. If they set a regular schedule or schedule a special meeting, agenda postings are required for each meeting. These meetings are open to the public.

- Business Liaison (Councilmembers David Zito & Jewel Edson): coordinates with the Chamber of Commerce, Cedros Merchants Associations, and Village walk (Highway 101) Association on City/Business issues. Meets quarterly on the 3rd Monday of the month at 5:00 p.m. (January, April, July, October).
- Fire Department Governance & Organizational Evaluation (Jewel Edson & Jill MacDonald): explores and evaluates potential fire department governance and organizational structures.
- Highway 101 & Cedros Ave Development (Mayor Lesa Heebner & Jewel Edson): coordinates with the Highway 101 Village Walk Association on funding to revitalize the Highway 101 corridor, landscape issues, and business development. Meets as needed.
- Parks & Recreation (David Zito & Jewel Edson): liaison to the Parks & Recreation Citizen Commission. Meets as needed.
- Public Arts (Mayor Heebner & Jewel Edson): liaison to the Public Arts Citizen Commission. Meets as needed.
- School Relations (Kristi Becker & Jill MacDonald): handles relations with San Dieguito Union High School District, Solana Beach School District, and private schools within Solana Beach. Meets quarterly on the 1st Thursday of the month, 7:30 a.m. - 8:30 a.m. (March, June, September, December).
- Solana Beach - Del Mar Relations (Mayor Lesa Heebner & Jewel Edson): discusses items of mutual interest to each city. Meets as needed.

## How to Watch Council Meetings

There are three ways to watch a Council Meeting:
1. In person at City Hall, 635 S. Highway 101, Solana Beach, CA 92075.
2. On television: Cox Channel 19, Spectrum (Time Warner) Channel 24, or ATT U-Verse Channel 99.
3. Stream live online through the "Watch Live Meetings" link on the City website.

The View Assessment Commissions are also broadcast live and archived on the Commission's webpage. Other Citizen Commission and Council Standing Committee meetings are not usually broadcast live or videotaped. Audio recordings of certain Citizen Commissions may be obtained through a Public Records Request.

For more information, contact the City Clerk's Department at (858) 720-2400 or clerkoffice@cosb.org.

## Meeting Agenda Posting and Minutes

City Council Meeting Agendas and related reports are generally published one week before a Regular City Council meeting. California Government Code requires Regular Meeting Agendas to be posted at least 72 hours before the meeting and Special Meeting Agendas at least 24 hours before. The same posting rules apply to Citizen Commission and Council Standing Committee meetings. All meetings are subject to cancellation. Check the City's Meeting Calendar to verify meetings, or contact the City Clerk's Office.

Minutes of City Council meetings, Citizen Commissions, and Council Standing Committees are posted once approved.

Source: https://www.cityofsolanabeach.org/en/government/public-meetings

## Supplemental Materials for Council Meetings

Supplemental materials or comments received after the agenda is posted, up to two hours before the meeting, are published online with the item. Late items received after the cut-off (2 hours before the meeting start time) may not be prepared and distributed in time to be included in the record. If you miss the cut-off, attend the meeting to speak and submit a document if needed. You must speak at a meeting to submit something for the record. Submittals before the meeting should be emailed to the City Clerk's Office. Handouts and presentations submitted during the meeting are not included on the published item.

Source: https://www.cityofsolanabeach.org/en/government/public-meetings

## Public Comment at Council Meetings

Written correspondence on an agenda item should be emailed to the City Clerk's Office at clerkoffice@cosb.org two hours before the meeting start time. Include the meeting date AND the Agenda Item # (as listed on the agenda) in the subject line. Correspondence received later than two hours before the start will not be available online with the agenda posting and may not be distributed to Council ahead of the meeting.

Speaking protocol:
- Each speaker gets 3 minutes per item, unless the item is on Consent.
- Consent: 3 minutes total to address all Consent Calendar items on the agenda.
- In person: submit a speaker slip to the City Clerk before the meeting, or before the announcement of the Section/Item.
- Remote: register to speak before 5:00 pm the Monday preceding a Wednesday meeting. The registration link is on each agenda.

Source: https://www.cityofsolanabeach.org/en/government/public-meetings

## Participating in Council Meetings by Zoom or Phone

To watch only, no registration is needed. Use the City website livestream, YouTube Live, or the local Public, Educational, & Government (PEG) Channel.

To join and comment: register using the Zoom link in the meeting agenda, check your email for a confirmation with your Zoom link, then click the link at the meeting time or dial in by phone. Registration is required to provide public comment via Zoom.

When it is your turn, wait for your name to be called, unmute (*6 on a phone), state your name for the record, give your comment within the allotted time, then mute (*6 on a phone).

Troubleshooting tips: use the correct Zoom link from the confirmation email, join 5–10 minutes early, try another device or browser (Chrome recommended), check your internet connection, restart your device, or use the phone dial-in as a backup.

Source: https://www.cityofsolanabeach.org/en/Meetings/Public-Participation-Guide

## Presenting Materials During Public Comment

If you plan to present materials and have registered to speak, indicate during registration that you will be presenting. Submit your materials in advance (PDF or PowerPoint preferred) to clerkoffice@cityofsolanabeach.ca.gov. Include your name and agenda item number. Submission is preferred in advance; same-day submissions may be accepted but are not guaranteed. Late submissions may not be accommodated. Staff may assist with displaying materials during your speaking time.

## Meeting Accessibility (ADA) and Language Access

Persons with a disability may request an agenda in alternative formats. Anyone who needs a modification or accommodation to participate in a meeting should contact the City Clerk's office at (858) 720-2400 at least 72 hours before the meeting. The Participation Guide lists examples of accommodations: assistive listening devices, closed captioning, screen reader–accessible materials, and language interpretation services.

To request an accommodation, email clerkoffice@cityofsolanabeach.ca.gov or call 858-720-2400. Submit requests at least 72 hours in advance when possible.

Accessible participation options include interactive Zoom participation with AI language support, telephone audio access, and broadcasts on the local PEG television channel.

For language access, residents may use free tools to translate City documents: Google Translate, DeepL Translator, or Microsoft Word's built-in translation feature. For assistance, contact the City Clerk's Office at clerkoffice@cityofsolanabeach.ca.gov. The City will make reasonable efforts to assist with language access requests.

## City Clerk Department

The City Clerk Department coordinates agenda postings for public meetings and is the Custodian of the City's Official Records. The City Clerk is the elections official for the City and coordinates municipal elections. The Department also handles Fair Political Practices Commission filings (Local Form 700 Filings, Local Conflict of Interest Code, Local Campaign Filings) and legislative administration: publishing and posting Council actions and agendas, recording and preserving official Council actions, certifying City documents, and codifying the municipal code.

The City Clerk page also points to San Diego County Clerk / Recorder / Assessor's Office services: birth, marriage, and death certificates; fictitious business names; recording documents; residential owner address change; and property tax assessments.
