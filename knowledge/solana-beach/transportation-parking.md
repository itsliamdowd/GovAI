## Green Bike Lanes — What the Markings Mean

Green paint does not change the rules of the road. It makes drivers and bicyclists more aware of each other as they share the road, and it marks areas where they frequently intersect.

Dashed green paint marks places where bicyclists and drivers are likely to cross paths, such as between a through lane and a right turn lane, or where they share the area at right turns. Bicyclists have the right of way there. Drivers must yield to bicyclists before driving across the dashed paint and make their right turn from as far right as possible. This helps prevent the "right hook" collision, when a car turns right in front of a bicyclist.

Solid green with solid white lines: drivers may not cross a solid white line into a bike lane except to enter a driveway. Bicyclists always have the right of way.

## Neighborhood Traffic Management Program (NTMP)

City Council approved the Neighborhood Traffic Management Program to help city staff and communities manage traffic through neighborhoods, including speeding, cut-through traffic, walking safety and accident locations. The specific neighborhood concern must be identified first. It may then be addressed through Stage 1 or Stage 2 measures.

Stage 1 measures are generally deterrents that rely on the driver to change their behavior. Stage 2 measures tend to make a driver change their behavior through a physical device in the roadway. The program page has tables of Stage 1 and Stage 2 tools, usage guides, a process flowchart, and the full text of the NTMP.

For a complaint, concern or question about traffic in your neighborhood or the city, fill out a Community Action Request Form or contact the City of Solana Beach Engineering Department at (858) 720-2470.

## Electric Bike Classes and Where They Can Ride

An e-bike is an electric bicycle equipped with fully operable pedals and an electric motor of less than 750 watts. E-bikes do not need registration, licensing or insurance like a car or motorcycle. The same rules of the road apply to all types of e-bikes and human-powered bicycles.

- Class 1 (pedal-assisted): allowed wherever regular bicycles are allowed. The motor assists only when the rider is pedaling. Power maxes out at 20 mph.
- Class 2 (throttle-assisted): allowed wherever regular bicycles are allowed. It has a throttle-actuated motor. Power maxes out at 20 mph.
- Class 3 (pedal-assisted): allowed in bike lanes or separated bikeways adjacent to the roadway. Not allowed on trails. Riders must be 16 years old and all ages must wear a helmet. Power maxes out at 28 mph. Any e-bike that can go over 28 mph is not street legal.

Source: https://www.cityofsolanabeach.org/en/community/public-safety/electric-bike-safety

## Electric Bike and Bicycle Rules of the Road

Bicyclists and e-bike riders are subject to all the same rules of the road as motorists. Riders who don't follow the rules are subject to ticketing and fines as defined by the Vehicle or Municipal Code.
- Riders 17 years and under must wear an appropriate helmet.
- Traffic lights, signs and signals apply to all road users, including e-bike riders. Riders must stop at red lights, stop before a right turn at red lights, and stop at stop signs.
- Ride to the right edge of the road as practical. If riding slower than traffic, ride in the bike lane. When a bicycle lane is available, bicyclists are required to use it unless they are traveling as fast as traffic.
- Dismount and walk your e-bike on sidewalks and crosswalks. You must stop at crosswalks for pedestrians.
- Weaving in and out of traffic and riding against the flow of traffic are prohibited.
- Do not attach a bicycle, e-bike or similar motorized device to any moving vehicle or motorized or non-motorized wheeled device with an operator or rider on board.
- Proper reflective equipment must be used during night operation. Use a white front light, red rear light and reflectors.
- It is illegal to carry passengers on your e-bike unless your bike has an extra permanent seat or when using a child safety seat. Passengers under 18 must wear a helmet. Class 3 e-bike riders must not transport passengers.
