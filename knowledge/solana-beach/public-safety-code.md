## Code Enforcement — Overview and Contact

The Code Enforcement Division works with residents and business owners to maintain the appearance and safety of the community through enforcement of City ordinances and land use regulations. These include those related to licensing of specific types of businesses, short-term vacation rentals and Transient Occupancy Tax (TOT) collection.

For questions, contact Code Enforcement at 858-720-2413 or CodeCompliance@cosb.org.

## Code Enforcement — How to Report a Complaint

A signed citizen's complaint form must be submitted before a complaint can be investigated. All complaints filed with the city are confidential. Include your name, address and phone number on the form so Code Compliance Staff can contact you with questions. Copies of the Complaint Form (English and Spanish) are on the Code Enforcement page, or can be obtained at City Hall, 635 South Highway 101, Solana Beach, CA 92075.

Location-specific concerns such as potholes, graffiti, or illegal construction activity can also be reported through the City of Solana Beach App. Reports are assigned to appropriate City staff by complaint type, and you can follow the progress of the corrective action. The app is available in the iTunes Store (iPhone) and Google Play Store (Android) by searching "City of Solana Beach."

## Parking & Traffic Citations — Payment

Call the Citation Processing Center at 1-800-989-2058 for more information about citations. Payment for tickets is not accepted at City Hall.

To pay by mail, send the proper penalty amount in Money Order or Check (payable to the City of Solana Beach). Do not send cash. Enclose the Notice of Parking Violation with your payment and/or proof of correction and mail it to: City of Solana Beach Citation Processing Center, P.O. Box 10479, Newport Beach, CA 92658-0479.

Payment by credit card may be made online at www.citationprocessingcenter.com or by calling 1-800-989-2058. Allow a few days for your citation to be entered into the database. The penalty fee increase begins 21 days after your citation is entered into the database. Your Citation Number starts with SB on the Notice of Parking Violation.

A payment plan for indigent persons is available in accordance with California Vehicle Code 40220.

## Fire Prevention — Plan Checks and Permits

The Fire Prevention Division processes plan checks of new construction and remodel projects, and performs acceptance testing and inspections of fire sprinklers (commercial/residential) and special hazards systems for new and remodeled development. It also oversees business inspection and weed abatement programs and enforces hazardous materials storage/use and disposal laws. For questions about Fire Operational and Prevention Permits, contact Fire Prevention at (858) 720-2410 or fireprevention@cityofsolanabeach.ca.gov.

To start a plan review, email the completed Fire Permit Application, plans and supporting documents to fireprevention@cityofsolanabeach.ca.gov. Each document must be in its own PDF file, clearly named with the property address and type of document. All files must be flattened with no security locks/restrictions. Once review is complete and approved, you will be notified by email for payment and issuance. Plan check payment may be made by cash, check made out to the City of Solana Beach, or credit card (2.9% fee will be added).

## Tent/Canopy Permits

An operational permit is required for an air-supported temporary membrane structure or tent with an area in excess of 400 square feet per California Fire Code (CFC) 105.7.16. A structure or tent less than 400 square feet does not require a permit. An application must be submitted prior to erecting a tent or canopy. Approved permits are not transferable to another location.

Documentation required: a Certificate of Flame Resistance (issued by State Fire Marshal); a Floor Plan showing all tables, chairs and other items under the tent(s), exit signs, extinguisher placement, and occupant load; and a Site Plan identifying address, APN, zoning, property boundaries, adjacent roads including ingress/egress, proposed location of sales inventory, existing and proposed structures/improvements (tents, power, vehicular parking and drive aisles, restroom locations), their dimensions, and whether they are temporary or permanent. Restrooms and parking improvements must be ADA compliant.

Allow a minimum of 3 business days to process this permit.

## Free Smoke Alarm Installation Program

The Solana Beach Fire Department's Smoke Alarm Program provides smoke alarm installation, testing, and home safety assessments. Through a partnership with the American Red Cross, smoke alarms are provided at no cost to residents. To request an installation, contact fireprevention@cityofsolanabeach.ca.gov or 858-720-2415, or fill out the online Smoke Alarm Installation Request form.

Placement tips: install smoke alarms inside every bedroom and outside each sleeping area; place alarms on every level of your home, including the basement; mount per the manufacturer's instructions; keep alarms at least 10 feet away from cooking appliances. Test alarms monthly and change batteries regularly.

## Calling 911

For any situation requiring immediate intervention from emergency services, call 911. A 911 call from a landline is routed to the designated 911 dispatch center. A call from a cell phone is routed to the dispatch center of the California Highway Patrol and then routed again, which may slow the process. You will probably get a quicker response using your home phone when possible.

AMR provides emergency and non-emergency medical transport service for the City of Solana Beach.

## Smoke and Carbon Monoxide Alarm Requirements

Property owners are required by law to install both smoke and carbon monoxide detectors in any building in which people will be living. A list of approved devices can be found on the State Fire Marshal website.

Smoke alarms should be installed outside of each separate sleeping area in the immediate vicinity of bedrooms, in each room used for sleeping, and in each story within a dwelling unit. Carbon monoxide alarms should be installed on every level or story and where any fuel-fired appliances are present, including fireplaces, furnaces, water heaters and boilers. Follow the manufacturer's installation instructions.

## Safely Surrendered Baby Program

Under County of San Diego designation, all Hospital Emergency Rooms and designated Fire Stations are locations where newborns can be safely surrendered to care without prosecution of the parent. The Solana Beach Fire Station is designated as a Safely Surrendered site. It displays the Safe Haven Site sign and a smaller sign with instructions in case the firefighters are not in quarters. Learn more at www.safe4baby.org.

## Community Emergency Response Team (CERT)

CERT educates people about disaster preparedness and trains them in basic disaster response skills: disaster readiness, extinguishing small fires, basic first aid, search and rescue tactics, use of radios, and mass casualty incidents. Anyone in good health with a sense of community can join, and members are not expected to place themselves in dangerous situations. Classes are free and open to all residents or employees of Solana Beach and/or Del Mar who are 18 years or older.
