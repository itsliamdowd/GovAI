## Construction & Development — Where to Start

Four City divisions manage construction and development. Building Services issues a building permit and inspects projects that affect only one component of a structure, such as a new water heater, EV Charger, or window replacement. Planning Services guides larger projects, such as a larger residential remodel, that may need more than one permit as well as review of lot and zoning requirements. The Engineering Department reviews projects that need special permits or plans, to ensure compliance with safety requirements and coordinate private projects with public infrastructure. Fire Prevention & Inspection Services handles fire inspection scheduling and questions about structures and fire safety codes. The building code is SBMC Title 15 BUILDINGS AND CONSTRUCTION.

Useful numbers: Building Permit Counter (858) 720-4450 (closed 12:00 PM - 1:00 PM); Inspection Request Line (858) 720-4455; Fire Prevention Services (858) 863-3116; Planning Services (858) 720-2440; Engineering Services (858) 720-2470, pw-eng@cosb.org.

## Call 811 for DigAlert Before Excavating

Excavators' permits are not valid until they have contacted DigAlert (Underground Service Alert of Southern California) by calling 811, or by creating an online ticket at www.digalert.org, at least two (2) full working days before excavation work begins, to get an inquiry identification number.

## Engineering Permits — Encroachment, Grading, Sewer, Transportation

An encroachment permit is required whenever construction or temporary storage is being conducted in the public right-of-way. Repairs to the public sewer, such as private lateral connections, also require encroachment permits. A grading permit is needed if earth is being moved on-site and the work is not covered under a building permit. A sanitation (sewer) permit is required when connecting to the public sewer. A Transportation Permit is also available.

Engineering Counter: (858) 720-2470, pw-eng@cosb.org. Engineering Services contact: Dan Goldberg, (858) 720-2474, dgoldberg@cosb.org.

## Construction & Demolition Recycling

You must use EDCO, Inc roll-off boxes for all construction projects within the City of Solana Beach. If EDCO, Inc cannot provide roll-off boxes for your project, you must provide verification of the reason(s) and the name of the roll-off provider. EDCO San Marcos Recycling Center: (760) 744-2700. The recycling target is 65% of waste.

A copy of your WMP and receipts (tickets) for all recycling and disposal must be submitted before your project can receive final Building approval. For Demolition Permits, receipts for the demolition waste should be provided before the first footing inspection after the building permit has been issued. Regulations are in Section 6.36 of the Solana Beach Municipal Code. Questions about the C & D Recycling Ordinance or the form: (858) 720-2470.

## Special Event Permits

The Parks and Recreation Department issues permits for special events in the City's public areas, playing fields, beaches, parks, and community centers, including road closure permits and film permits. Any event with 25 or more people on public property requires a Special Event Permit. Events such as block parties may also require one. No private events are allowed at Fletcher Cove or on City beaches. Applications are available for Special Event Permits, Sound Permits and Film Permits.

## Sign Permit — Application Process

A Sign Permit is required for a permanent sign on a commercial property, a temporary sign, or banners for a special event. Standard sign regulations are in SBMC 17.64. Properties within the Highway 101 Specific Plan follow table 5-4 of the specific plan. Questions: planning@cosb.org or (858) 720-2440.

Step 1: Email the completed Sign Permit Application, written landlord authorization for the sign type, size, design, and location, and Sign Plans (minimum of a title sheet, site plan and sign elevations) to planning@cosb.org. Step 2: Planning issues an invoice for the application fee after receiving all documents; it must be paid to move to review. Step 3: Reviews are done electronically and each review is typically 2-3 weeks. Step 4: After Planning approval, submit a completed Building Permit Application and project plans to counter@cosb.org for Building Department review and permit issuance.

Source: https://www.cityofsolanabeach.org/en/sign-permits

## Sign Permit Types & Temporary Banner Rules

A Standard Sign Permit is required before placing, erecting or installing any sign or sign structure unless expressly exempted by SBMC 17.64. A Comprehensive Sign Plan (CSP) is required for all commercial, office, industrial, and civic uses consisting of four or more tenant or occupant spaces on a lot or on two or more lots subject to a common development permit or plan.

Under SBMC 17.64.055, temporary and special event banners may not exceed one square foot for each linear foot of building frontage, and not exceed 32 square feet in area. They may be displayed for a consecutive period not to exceed 14 days, for a maximum of 30 days per year, except grand opening banners, which may be displayed up to 60 days. Only one grand opening banner is permitted for the life of the business, unless ownership transfers. Banners must be wind and water resistant and securely fastened, and the applicant must have an approved sign permit.

## Tent/Canopy Permit

An operational permit is required for an air-supported temporary membrane structure or tent having an area in excess of 400 square feet per California Fire Code (CFC) 105.7.16. A structure or tent less than 400 square feet does not require a permit. An application must be submitted prior to erecting a tent or canopy. Approved permits are not transferable to another location.

Required documentation: Certificate of Flame Resistance (issued by State Fire Marshal); Floor Plan showing tables, chairs and other items under the tent(s), exit signs, extinguisher placement, and occupant load; and Site Plan (address, APN, zoning, property boundaries, adjacent roads including ingress/egress, and locations of structures/improvements). Restrooms and parking improvements must be ADA compliant. Allow a minimum of 3 business days to process.

Source: https://www.cityofsolanabeach.org/en/community/public-safety/fire-department/fire-prevention-plan-checks-permits

## Fire Flow Analysis Request

A Fire Flow Analysis is required in accordance with the California Fire Code to verify that adequate water supply is available for the proposed project. Complete the required form and email it to fireprevention@cityofsolanabeach.ca.gov with: project name and address, permit number, applicant/contact name and contact information, project square footage, and type of construction. Incomplete information may delay the review.

Questions about your fire flow analysis go to Santa Fe Irrigation District: Jason Simmons, 858-227-5770, jsimmons@sfidwater.org.
