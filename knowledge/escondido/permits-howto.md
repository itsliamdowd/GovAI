## Building Division — Hours, Contact and Plan Submission

The Building Division counter is open Monday through Friday from 8 am to 5 pm. It is on the first floor of City Hall, 201 North Broadway, Escondido, CA 92025. Phone: 760-839-4647. Fax: 760-432-6819. The Building Official is Douglas L. Moody.

Permit types not required to use the portal must bring their application to the front counter or email it to buildingpermits@escondido.gov. If the application is complete and accepted, the applicant is given a link to upload and submit plans electronically.

## Plan Check and Permit Expirations & Extensions

Plan check is valid for 1 year after submission. A 90-day extension is available with written approval.

A permit is valid for 1 year after issuance and extended 180 days after inspection. A 180-day extension is available with written approval.

Extensions may be granted after a written request is submitted and approved by staff and/or the building official. The request must include the site address, permit number, reason for the additional time needed, and a signature.

Due to the new California Building Code update effective January 1, 2026, plan check extensions will NOT be provided on any applications submitted prior to that date.

## Scheduling a Building Inspection Online

The City has moved to a fully online system for scheduling and tracking building inspections. Inspections are no longer scheduled through the website or by phone; everything is done through the City Portal (https://cwols.escondido.org/portal). Through the portal you can schedule and reschedule inspections 24/7, view inspection time frames online at 9AM the morning of your inspection, and track progress and results in real time.

You need a City Portal account and a search code from your permit. The search code is printed on your inspection card and permit. To get it, email the permit number and job address to BLDsearchcode@escondido.gov, or call (760)839-4647 EXT 1.

If you are the applicant listed on the permit:
1. Log in to your City Portal account. From My Dashboard, under the My Applications tab, find the Permit Number column and click the magnifying glass next to the permit number.
2. To find the search code, go to Print Info on the Permit/License Info page and select Building Permit or Inspection Record Card. The search code is at the top of the document.
3. Return to the Home Page / My Dashboard and go to the My Inspections tab. Enter the search code in the search bar on the right.
4. Under the Actions column, click the Calendar icon next to the permit number. Select the Inspection Type, then click Next.
5. Choose the date and time, fill in your name and phone number, and click Submit. You should see the message "Inspection Successfully Requested."

If your Contact Email is not listed on the permit, you will be unable to schedule an inspection. Contact administrative staff at (760) 839-4647 EXT 1 or BLDsearchcode@escondido.gov.

Source: https://www.escondido.gov/1280/New---Online-Building-Inspection-Request

## Building Inspection — Eligibility, Limits and Confirmation

A permit must be in one of these statuses to schedule an inspection:
- ISSUED
- REV_WIP (under review but cleared to proceed with inspections)

Permits in REV_STOP are not eligible for inspections. If you believe your project should be "ISSUED," contact Admin Staff at (760) 839-4647 ext. 1. For "REV_WIP" status, contact your Building Inspector at ext. 3.

The limit is a maximum of 4 inspections per permit per day. Developers have a maximum of 4 permits and 4 inspections per permit per day.

Requested inspections change from "Requested" to "Confirmed" in the City Portal the morning of the inspection by 9 AM.
- Requests made before 3 PM are added to the schedule for the next business day, unless a later date is chosen or it is a holiday.
- Requests made after 3 PM or on holidays are scheduled for the second business day following the request.

Confirmed inspections display a time window under the Requested Date column (My Inspections tab, then Scheduled). Morning (AM) is 9:00 AM – 12:30 PM. Afternoon (PM) is 12:30 PM – 4:00 PM.

Source: https://www.escondido.gov/1280/New---Online-Building-Inspection-Request

## Pre-Approved ADU Program (PAADU)

PAADU plans are pre-approved designs for new construction, detached ADUs. They can save time and money on design and engineering costs. The plans are NOT specific to a property or site. Applicants are still required to submit a building permit and go through the standard ADU permitting process.

Four plans are available for download (PDF and ZIP):
- Plan 1: Studio or 1 bedroom / 1 bath (484 sq.ft.)
- Plan 2: 2 bedroom / 1 bath (644 sq. ft.)
- Plan 3: 2 bedroom / 2 bath (851 sq. ft.)
- Plan 4: 2 or 3 bedroom / 2 bath (1,000 sq. ft.)

ProjectDox requires plans to be uploaded individually in accordance with a specified naming convention. The ZIP files are labeled to meet these standards, and applicants only need to rename the file with their specific permit number. Example: if your permit number is B24-2022 and the file is named "B00-0000A102-Site Plan," rename it "B24-2022A102-SitePlan."

Guidebook materials are available in English and Spanish. They include the PAADU Guidebook, Initial Considerations Handout, Program Process Sheet, Create Your Site Plan Worksheet, Option Selections Handout, Old Escondido Neighborhood PAADU Adaptation Guide, and a Frequently Asked Questions Sheet. For help using the plans with ProjectDox, contact the Planning Division at planning@escondido.gov.

Source: https://www.escondido.gov/1207/Pre-Approved-ADU-Program-PAADU

## Zoning Administrator — Hearings and Appeals

The Zoning Administrator is authorized to consider minor conditional use permits, variances, reasonable accommodation requests, some grading exemptions, and proposed modifications to an approved Precise Development Plan. Public Hearings are scheduled as needed, and notice is provided in compliance with local and state law. A staff report is provided at least 72 hours prior to a scheduled meeting.

Actions of the Zoning Administrator may be appealed to the Planning Commission within 10 calendar days in accordance with Section 33-1303 of the Escondido Zoning Code. If no appeal is filed, the approval of a permit or application becomes effective on the 11th calendar day following the date of the decision.

## Business License — How to Apply and Renew

Anyone who conducts business within the City of Escondido must obtain a business license, even if the business headquarters is not in the City boundaries. Examples include construction contractors, booths at antique stores or swap meets, and technology consultants. You are not authorized to conduct business until the license has been approved.

A license is issued to a specific owner, and a change in ownership requires a new application. It is valid for one year and must be renewed annually. Failure to renew results in penalties of 25% per month (not to exceed 100%). Licenses are approved for a specific location, and any change of address requires a new supplemental form and review process with a $20.00 change of address fee. The active license is emailed upon approval.

- **New license:** Visit the online Business License portal and create an account.
- **Renewal:** Visit the portal and log in using the renewal code provided via email.
- **Questions:** Contact HdL at (760) 270-9397 or escondido@hdlgov.com. Assistance is available in multiple languages.

Source: https://www.escondido.gov/1134/Business-License

## Fee Deferral Policy

The fee deferral policy reduces the up-front cost of development for new residential projects involving five or more units and for businesses that foster beneficial economic development. To be eligible, you must be constructing five or more residential units under a single phase, or have a business that is either expanding its floor area or constructing a new building within the Escondido city limits.

To apply, complete the Fee Deferral Application (PDF) and return it to the Building Division. Attach a current preliminary title report (no more than 6 months old). If the property is leased, a copy of the lease is required before a fee deferral is granted. At a minimum, provide floor plans for the project.
