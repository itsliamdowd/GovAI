## Start Utility Service

Request service through the Start Utility Service form on the City website. The form asks for name, the address of the property where service should start, email address, phone number, and the date you'd like service to start (Monday through Friday only). A customer service representative will contact you within 2 business days of receipt of the request. If you do not receive a telephone call within 2 business days, call 760-839-4682 to follow up.

When contacted, you will need to provide your driver's license number and your social security number, confirm the property address and telephone number, and say where you'd like the bill mailed.

Water service can only be started Monday through Friday, and a 3-day notice is required when requesting by the form. If you need it turned on before 3 days, call 760-839-4682. Service cannot be started the same day a request is made. In emergency situations, same-day service is available for a fee: Emergency Start Fee $225. Monday through Thursday, the fee applies after 3:30 p.m. On Friday, it applies after 2:30 p.m.

Source: https://www.escondido.gov/1084/Start-Utility-Service

## Stop Utility Service

Request a stop of service through the Stop Utility Service form on the City website. The form asks for the name on the account, the Utility Billing Account Number, email address, the address of the property where service should stop, the date you'd like service to stop (Monday through Friday only), the address where you'd like to receive your closing bill, and a phone number where you can be contacted within the next 2 business days.

Service can only be stopped Monday through Friday, and a 3-day notice is required when requesting by the form. If you need it turned off before 3 days, call 760-839-4682. A customer service representative will contact you within 2 business days of receipt of the notice to confirm receipt and processing of the request. If you do not receive a call within 2 business days, call 760-839-4682 to follow up.

Pay your closing bill on time. Closing-bill accounts are sent to the collection agency two days after the due date.

Source: https://www.escondido.gov/1085/Stop-Utility-Service

## Library Board of Trustees — Meetings

The Library Board of Trustees meets at 4 pm on the 2nd Thursday of each month in City Council Chambers. Agendas are available prior to the meetings. Minutes are available following approval. The board provides oversight of library services and advises the City Council, City staff, and the Library Director. Its responsible department is the Community Services Department.

Trustees serve three-year terms and must reside or work within the geographic boundaries of the General Plan. The position has no compensation, and trustees must submit conflict of interest statements.

Source: https://www.escondido.gov/848/Library-Board-of-Trustees
