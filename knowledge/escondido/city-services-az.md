## Library Board of Trustees

The Library Board of Trustees meets at 4 pm on the 2nd Thursday of each month in City Council Chambers. Agendas are available prior to the meetings. Minutes are available following approval.

The Board provides oversight of library services and advises the City Council, City staff, and the Library Director on plans, policies, and programs. Its focus areas include reviewing customer concerns and suggestions, determining expenditures of library trust funds, and reviewing and approving policies and fees recommended by staff. Members serve three-year terms with no compensation. Members must reside or work within the geographic boundaries of the General Plan and must submit conflict of interest statements. The responsible department is the Community Services Department.

Source: https://www.escondido.gov/848/Library-Board-of-Trustees

## Parks Maintenance

The Parks Maintenance section of the Public Works Department cares for Escondido's parks, median and parkway landscaping, and open spaces. ## Recreation Division

The Recreation Division offers programs and activities for people of all ages, from infants to older adults. ## Animal Control

The San Diego Humane Society (SDHS) issues dog licenses and provides animal control and sheltering services by contract to the City of Escondido. SDHS responds to animal complaints; call SDHS Dispatch at 619-299-7012. Animal complaint hours are 7 am and 10 pm 7 days a week. At all other times, and on holidays, call the Escondido Police Department's Communications Center at 760-839-4722. SDHS responds to Priority-1 incidents during off times.

Priority-1 incidents include: Dangerous Animal Threatening Human; Possible Rabid/Biter; Animal at Large; Major Injury to Animal; Cruelty to Animal; Animal Inside a vehicle in distress; Fighting Animals; Other significant public safety hazards involving animals.

Animal Control address: 3450 East Valley Parkway, Escondido, CA 92025.

Source: https://www.escondido.gov/554/Animal-Control

## Recycling & Waste Reduction Division

The Recycling and Waste Reduction Division gives educational presentations at schools, multifamily complexes, and public events. It holds workshops on backyard composting and vermicomposting (worm bin), coordinates cleanup events at Dixon Lake, and oversees the Household Hazardous Waste program. Listed programs include Christmas Tree Recycling, Compost Workshops, Dixon Lake Cleanups, Free Mulch Distribution Information, and the Earth Day Recycling Art Contest for Escondido K-8 students. For other programs, call 760-839-6216.

For food recovery concerns, such as missing signage or labeling on waste bins or a lack of educational and outreach materials on proper disposal methods, email Recycling or call 760-839-6216. Office: 201 N Broadway, Escondido, CA 92025. Environmental Programs Manager: Kim Silva.
