## Mobilehome Rent Control (Proposition K)

The Mobilehome Rent Control Administration office monitors the Mobilehome Rent Control Ordinance (also known as "Proposition K"), which was passed by the voters of Escondido in 1988. Under the ordinance, if a mobilehome park owner wants to increase the rent charged to an existing resident, the park owner must first obtain approval from the Mobilehome Park Rental Review Board through a public hearing process. The City Council sits as the Rent Review Board and meets as needed upon the request of a park owner for a rent increase.

The page offers these documents: Average Rent by Park (PDFs for 2020-2021 through 2024-2025), Mobilehome Park Application for Space Rent Increase (Long Form and Short Form), the Mobilehome Rent Control Ordinance, Mobilehome Rent Review Guidelines, and Rent Control History. Park owners can also find Annual Park Update Forms (2025-2026 Survey Cover Letter, Park Contact Info, and Annual Rent Control Survey Form) and the Application for Permit To Operate.

Source: https://www.escondido.gov/231/Mobilehome-Rent-Control-Administration

## Short-Term Rentals — Permits Required for Hosts

A host must obtain the following permits prior to advertising or renting any privately-owned residential dwelling to any transient for a period of 30 consecutive days or less: an Escondido business license, a short-term rental permit, and a transient occupancy tax registration certificate. Applicants can apply for all three permits on the City's Licensing Portal. Applications are taken first-come, first-served.

STRs must be located more than 500 feet from a parcel containing a school. Home-based schools and daycares are not considered schools for this requirement. STRs located in the unincorporated areas of Escondido are subject to San Diego County regulations. The short-term rental ordinance is Chapter 16, Article 8 of the Escondido Municipal Code (EMC 16-8), effective July 1, 2025.

Source: https://www.escondido.gov/1215/Short-Term-Rentals-STR

## Short-Term Rentals — Rules and Enforcement Timeline

Guest limit: a maximum of two guests per bedroom, plus two additional guests. For example, a permitted rental with three bedrooms would have a maximum occupancy of eight guests. Quiet hours are between 10:00 PM and 7:00 AM (EMC 17-229). Guests must use only the on-site parking spaces associated with the approved short-term rental unit, and street parking is not permitted. STRs are solely for overnight lodging purposes, not large gatherings or events.

Program timeline: Phase One (July 1 - August 31, 2025), the licensing portal is open. Phase Two (September 1 - November 14, 2025), STR owners who have not applied receive letters and door hangers. Phase Three (November 15 - December 31, 2025), investigation of unpermitted STRs begins. Phase Four (January 1, 2026), unpermitted STRs are subject to administrative and criminal enforcement.

Source: https://www.escondido.gov/1215/Short-Term-Rentals-STR

## Short-Term Rentals — How to Report Problems

To report an STR complaint, call the 24/7 STR reporting hotline at 760-291-8173. Other options: the "Report It" Mobile App (available in the Apple App Store and Google Play); City Code Compliance Division, 760-839-4650; and Local Police Department (Non-Emergency), 760-839-4722. For emergencies (Fire/Police/Medical), dial 911.

Each permitted STR must provide a responsible contact who is available 24/7 while guests are present and can be on site within the hour, if needed. Approved permits list the rental address, responsible contact information, and permit number on the City website at https://escondido.hdlgov.com/Search/Index/STRPermit.

Source: https://www.escondido.gov/1215/Short-Term-Rentals-STR
