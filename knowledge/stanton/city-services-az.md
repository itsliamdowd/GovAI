## Community Services Commission

The Community Services Commission consists of five members appointed by the City Council. Regular meetings are held on the third Monday of every month at 5:30pm in the City Council Chambers, 7800 Katella Avenue, Stanton.

The Commission serves as City Ambassadors regarding information about City services. It acts in an advisory capacity to the City Council on matters such as development and rehabilitation of Parks and Recreation Facilities, and it reviews and recommends changes to the Rules and Regulations governing the use of City facilities. It also reviews and provides feedback regarding various Community Services projects.

Current commissioners: Chair Amy McBride (appointment expires November 2028); Vice Chairperson Marlon-Ray Velasco (November 2026); Commissioner Hanni Hoang Phan (November 2026); Commissioner Brian A. Barker (November 2026); one Commissioner seat is vacant (November 2028).

## Trash and Recycling

The City has an exclusive partnership with CR&R Incorporated to provide solid waste services to residential and commercial customers. Services include trash, recycle, and organics; bulky item pickup (residential only); holiday tree collection (residential only); construction and demolition; and household sharps collection. To start service, contact a CR&R Customer Service Representative at (714) 372-8272 or email the Public Works Department at PublicWorks@StantonCA.gov.

For all customer service and billing inquiries, contact CR&R at (714) 372-8272. Inquiries may include establishing service, change of address, cart replacement, bulky item pickup, temporary bin services, and missed pickups. For information on self-hauling (Stanton Municipal Code § 6.04.070), contact (714) 890-4205.

CR&R does not collect trash, recyclables, or organics on these holidays: Memorial Day, Independence Day, Labor Day, Thanksgiving Day, Christmas Day, New Year's Day. Collection of containers will occur on the following day. Office hours at 11292 Western Ave, Stanton: Monday to Friday 8am to 4pm; Saturday 8am to 1pm; Sunday closed.

## Residential Permit Parking

Several Stanton neighborhoods have opted in to the City's Residential Permit Parking program. If you live in a Permit Parking area, permits are available at Stanton City Hall at the Public Works counter. One of the following must be provided: valid proof of residency (utility bill, rental agreement, mortgage agreement); proof of vehicle ownership with Stanton home address (car registration); or photo ID with Stanton home address (driver's license, passport, etc.).

Each residential unit, such as a home or an apartment, can be issued up to three permits at a cost of $25 each. Permits are valid for a two-year period and are a hangtag that can be moved from vehicle to vehicle. The current two-year period is from January 1, 2026 until December 31, 2027, and all existing permits expire as of December 31, 2027. Permits for the 2028/2029 cycle will be available to all residents on November 1, 2027. Parking enforcement will begin January 1, 2028.

Up to 100 overnight guest permit parking passes per year can be obtained per residence at no fee. Each overnight guest permit is valid for one full day till noon of the following day. The City is closed on Fridays.

Multi-family properties, such as condos or apartments, can be included in the permit parking program. Applications for new parking permit zones are available at the Public Works counter and the fee is $660 to begin the process. 75% of residents must vote to approve a new parking zone or to remove an existing parking zone. To begin the process in your neighborhood, call the Public Works Department at (714) 890-4205.

## Fraud, Waste & Abuse Hotline

The fraud hotline is for reporting suspected fraud by the City or its employees, officials, contractors or vendors, and/or breaches of City-Wide Policies and Standards of Conduct that have a financial impact on the City, including waste or abuse of City resources. Examples: theft of City resources (e.g., cash, equipment, supplies, or materials); intentional misuse of City equipment or property; accepting a bribe, kickback or illegal gratuity; payroll fraud or time abuse; record falsification. The hotline is not a substitute for employment-related grievances or complaints by City employees.

Any employee, contractor or community member can report through the City's independent third-party anonymous and confidential reporting service. Use the STOPit Solutions secure online reporting form (24 hours a day), or download the STOPit app from the Apple App Store or Google Play. In the app, click "ENTER ACCESS CODE," enter "StantonCommunity," click "Next," accept the terms and conditions, and click "Begin" to submit a tip. The portal and app allow you to report incidents anonymously in real-time, submit photo and video evidence anonymously, and use two-way anonymous communication to follow up.

The City will not retaliate, nor tolerate retaliation, against those who, in good faith, report suspected fraud, waste or abuse or participate in an investigation. Reporters may be protected through the State of California Whistleblower Protection Act or other State or federal laws.
