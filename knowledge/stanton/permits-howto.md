## Residential Permit Parking — Getting a Permit

Several Stanton neighborhoods have opted in to the City's Residential Permit Parking program. If you live in a Permit Parking area, parking permits are available at Stanton City Hall at the Public Works counter. To be issued permits, you must provide one of the following:
- a valid proof of residency (utility bill, rental agreement, mortgage agreement)
- proof of vehicle ownership with Stanton home address (car registration)
- photo ID with Stanton home address (driver's license, passport, etc.)

Each residential unit, such as a home or an apartment, can be issued up to three permits at a cost of $25 each. Permits are valid for a two-year period and are a hangtag that can be moved from vehicle to vehicle.

Source: https://www.stantonca.gov/departments/public_works___engineering/permit_parking.php

## Residential Permit Parking — Permit Cycle and Guest Passes

The current two-year period is from January 1, 2026 until December 31, 2027. All existing permits expire as of December 31, 2027. Permits for the 2028/2029 cycle will be available to all residents on November 1, 2027. Staff recommends obtaining permits early, before the new cycle starts, because parking enforcement will begin January 1, 2028.

Up to 100 overnight guest permit parking passes per year can be obtained per residence at no fee from the City. Each overnight guest permit is valid for one full day till noon of the following day. The City is closed on Fridays.

Source: https://www.stantonca.gov/departments/public_works___engineering/permit_parking.php

## Residential Permit Parking — Starting a New Permit Parking Zone

Multi-family properties, such as condos or apartments, can now be included in the permit parking program, in accordance with the California Attorney General's recommendations. Applications for new parking permit zones are available at the Public Works counter, and the fee is $660 to begin the process. 75% of residents must vote to approve a new parking zone or to remove an existing parking zone. To begin the process in your neighborhood, call the Public Works Department at (714) 890-4205.

Source: https://www.stantonca.gov/departments/public_works___engineering/permit_parking.php

## Plan Check Center — Submitting Building Plans Electronically

Only the Building and Planning divisions accept electronic (PDF) plan submittals through the plan check center. To begin using the Stanton Permit Center:
1. Create an account at https://stanton.cts.city/
2. Verify your email address and then log back in at that website.
3. Fill out the online permit application.
4. Upload your PDF plans and documents.
5. Review of Plan Checks will begin upon receipt of payment.

The Building Division is temporarily not accepting online payments. Contact (714) 890-4286 or AMcQuade@StantonCA.gov for more information.

Source: https://www.stantonca.gov/departments/community_development/building_regulations/plan_check_center.php

## Plan Check Center — PDF Submittal Guidelines and Other Departments

Follow the PDF Submittal Guidelines or your project will be rejected. The uploaded PDF plan document should include:
- Mechanical, Electrical, Plumbing, and Structural plan sheets
- Structural Calculations
- Title-24 Documents
- Geotechnical Report
- Soils Report
- Homeowners Association (HOA) Approval Documents

Public Works will only accept Grading, WQMP's and Sewer Improvement projects via the Plan Check Center. If your project requires any other Public Works or Fire Department review, or Orange County Fire Authority review, submit your paper plans to them separately. For questions, email Building Permit Technician Alyssa McQuade at AMcQuade@StantonCA.gov.

Source: https://www.stantonca.gov/departments/community_development/building_regulations/plan_check_center.php

## Planning Division — Public Counter

The Planning Division reviews and processes project applications and administers the Zoning Code. Staff are available to assist in person, over the phone, or via email Monday through Thursday, 7am-6pm. The counter is closed for lunch from 12pm-1pm, and City Hall is closed every Friday. For more information, contact Ivana Velasco at IVelasco@StantonCA.gov or call the main Planning Division line at (714) 890-4232.
