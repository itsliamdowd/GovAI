# Public Safety & Code Enforcement

## Code Enforcement — Overview

The Code Enforcement Team enforces City codes on commercial signage, land use/zoning, inoperable vehicles, public nuisances and other hazards or violations that would harm residents' health, welfare and safety. The program works through field investigations, notification, citation issuance, public education and coordination with other agencies. Related pages cover Advertising & Signage, the Commercial Code Compliance Checklist, Garage Sale Guidelines, the Oversized Vehicle Ordinance, Residential Code Compliance and Short-Term Rentals.

Source: https://www.stantonca.gov/departments/public_safety/code_enforcement/index.php

## Public Safety Services Department — Police, Fire, Animal Control, Parking Control

The Public Safety Services Department manages the City's public safety contracts. It is the liaison for police, fire and animal control services. It also coordinates citywide code enforcement and emergency management. Code Enforcement inspects residential, industrial and commercial locations for violations. These include building, business license and regulatory permits, graffiti, housing, nuisance and zoning codes.

The Parking Control Program enforces City codes and ordinances. It imposes parking laws and has jurisdiction over the hearing process for contested parking citations. It also administers the permit-parking program and collects all parking fines.

Source: https://www.stantonca.gov/departments/public_safety/index.php

## Residential Code Compliance — Vehicles, Trash Cans, Garage Sales, Property Storage

- Inoperative, unlicensed or dismantled vehicles must be parked in fully enclosed garages in residential zones. Storage in driveways or yards is prohibited.
- Vehicles over 10,000 pounds may not be parked in residential areas.
- Homes may not be used for commercial/industrial activities except when a home occupation permit has been issued by the Planning Division.
- Discarded furniture, appliances, vehicle parts, household items, trash, debris, tree limbs or vegetation that constitute a public nuisance may not be stored on properties.
- Trash cans may only be in public view near the curb on the day of trash collection. At all other times, they must be stored in an area not visible from the public right of way.
- Garage sales in residential zones must be limited to four per year and no longer than three days duration. All items must have been used at your home. Merchandise may not be brought in from elsewhere for sale.
- All fences visible from the public street must be maintained so they do not create a safety hazard.
- Vehicle repair must be performed within an enclosed building.
- You may not park on your lawn or any unpaved surface.

## Oversized Vehicle Parking Restrictions and 72-Hour Permits

Effective October 14, 2021, the following vehicles are not permitted to park on all streets in the City of Stanton:
- Campers/Trailers
- Recreational Vehicles
- Vehicles exceeding 10,000 lbs., 22 ft. in length, or 7 ft. tall and 7 ft. wide

Violations may be subject to administrative citations, towing and/or other enforcement actions. Exceptions include vehicles with a 72-hour City-issued permit for loading or unloading. Oversized vehicles with a City-issued permit must park within 1/10th of a mile from the permit's registered residential address. A utility connection between a residence and an oversized vehicle may not encroach into the public right-of-way, including across or above the street or sidewalk. See Stanton Municipal Code Section 10.08.080.

Temporary 72-hour Parking Permits can be obtained at the front counter of City Hall during regular business hours, Monday through Thursday, or through the online link on the page. For last-minute after-hours requests Friday through Sunday, call the Code Enforcement Hot-Line at (714) 890-4220. Request a late permit by leaving your name, phone number, address, make, model and license plate. A public safety staff member will follow up within 72 hours.

## Short-Term Rentals — Prohibited; How to Report

A short-term rental is a dwelling unit rented for fewer than 31 consecutive days. Short-term rentals are prohibited in all residential zones under SMC § 20.105.020, SMC § 20.210.020 and SMC § 20.400.330.E.4 (Accessory Dwelling Units). The Code Enforcement Division regularly monitors short-term rental listings to identify rentals operating illegally.

To report a suspected short-term rental, contact the Code Enforcement Hotline at (714) 890-4220 or email CodeHotline@StantonCA.gov. Administrative fines starting at $100 with a progression to $500 may be issued each day a violation continues. A Responsible Party who violates or fails to comply may also be guilty of a misdemeanor.

Source: https://www.stantonca.gov/departments/public_safety/code_enforcement/short-term_rentals.php

## Illegal Fireworks — Penalties and How to Report

The City's enforcement strategy includes a Social Host Ordinance. It holds property owners or tenants liable for the sale, storage, or use of illegal fireworks, with fines starting at $1,000 that increase with repeat offenses. Businesses involved in selling or storing illegal fireworks risk suspension or revocation of their license, especially for repeated violations.

For fireworks in progress, call OCSD at (714) 647-7000. To report illegal sales, call the Code Enforcement Hotline at (714) 890-4220. More information is at StantonCA.gov/Fireworks.

Source: https://www.stantonca.gov/news_detail_T9_R276.php

## Graffiti and Illegal Storm Drain Discharge

Public Works crews remove graffiti from walls and buildings by cleaning and painting over it. Public Works only abates graffiti on public property and in areas in the line of sight from major arterial streets. All other graffiti issues are handled through the City's Code Enforcement Department.

The only permitted discharge into a gutter is stormwater. Releasing concrete, paint, plaster or other materials is a violation of the City's water quality ordinance and may be fined. To report illegal discharge, call the Department of Public Works at (714) 890-4205 during normal business hours, or the Stanton Sheriff's Department at (714) 647-7000.

Source: https://www.stantonca.gov/departments/public_works___engineering/faq.php

## Safe Streets Together — Code Enforcement Patrols and Hotspot Signage

Code Enforcement officers have begun after-hours patrols during late-night and weekend hours, and individuals found violating City ordinances are subject to citation. Street signs in neighborhood hotspots include "No Stopping Anytime," time-restricted parking and other restricted traffic movements. People who disregard the posted signs are subject to traffic stops by law enforcement. "Safe Streets" cameras have been deployed in designated hotspot areas to monitor and record activity.
