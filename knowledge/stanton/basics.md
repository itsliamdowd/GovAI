## City Council — Structure, Members & Meetings

Stanton operates under a Council/Manager form of government. The City Council is the policymaking body and the City Manager carries out Council policy. The Council is made up of the mayor, who is elected at large, and four City Council members elected by voters in their districts. Councilmembers serve a four-year term and elections are held in November of even-numbered years. An interactive map is available to search for your Council District by address.

## City Council Actions — Ordinances, Resolutions, Minute Orders

The City Council may take three forms of action: ordinances, resolutions and minute orders.

An ordinance is the most binding form of action, the legislative enactment of City law. It requires a four-fifths vote for adoption. If a four-fifths vote is not received, the ordinance must return to the next regularly scheduled Council Meeting for adoption. Ordinances become effective 30 days after adoption.

A resolution establishes Council policy or directs certain types of administrative action, and is usually effective after a majority vote at the meeting. A minute order is the most informal action and requires majority approval of procedural actions such as filing a report or instructing the administrative staff to take certain actions.

## Meeting Agendas & Public Comment

All City Council and Commission meetings are open to the public. Agendas are posted at Stanton City Hall, 7800 Katella Avenue, Stanton, 90680 and online at www.StantonCA.gov at least 72 hours before any regularly scheduled meeting. Special Meetings are posted in the same manner and locations 24 hours in advance. All items are open to the public except for Closed Session.

To comment, attend in person and submit a Request to Speak card (Planning Commission: a Request to Speak paper) and approach the podium and state your name for the record. Public comments are limited to three minutes. No action may be taken on non-agenda items. Submit the card to:
- City Council: the City Clerk
- Planning Commission: the Community and Economic Development Director
- Community Services Commission: the Community Services Director

You may instead e-mail comments with the subject line "PUBLIC COMMENT ITEM #" (insert the agenda item number) or "PUBLIC COMMENT NON-AGENDA ITEM #". Comments received no later than 5:00pm before the meeting are compiled, provided to the body, and made available to the public before the start of the meeting. Staff will not read e-mailed comments at the meeting, but the official record includes all e-mailed comments received until the close of the meeting. E-mail addresses:
- City Council: PVazquez@StantonCA.gov
- Planning Commission: CommunityDevelopment@StantonCA.gov
- Community Services Commission: CommunityServices@StantonCA.gov

## Public Records Act Requests

Anyone may initiate a request for public records. All requests should be directed to the City Clerk's Office, the City's official Custodian of Records. Requests should be in writing and as specific as possible. A formal written request is not mandatory, but a completed Public Records Request Form lets the City Clerk's Office respond in a timely manner. The form can be submitted online or printed and completed for in-person submission.

Submit the form by:
- Mail: 7800 Katella Ave, Stanton, CA 90680 - Attention: City Clerk
- E-Mail: PVazquez@StantonCA.gov
- Fax: (714) 890-1443
- City Hall: 7800 Katella Ave, Stanton, CA 90680 - Attention: City Clerk (drop off at the front counter)

Requests sent by e-mail: you will receive an e-mail confirmation within three days of submission. If you do not receive a confirmation, call (714) 890-4245. Requests sent by e-mail are not valid without confirmation.

A city agency has ten days in which to respond to a request under the Public Records Act. Fees for copies are $0.10 per page, unless the requested document has an established statutory fee. The City also has an Online Records Library with public records such as agendas, staff reports, minutes, ordinances and resolutions.
