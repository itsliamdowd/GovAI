## Housing Programs — Community & Economic Development Department

The Community & Economic Development Department coordinates activities that preserve and enhance the character of Stanton's neighborhoods. Housing staff implement the programs identified in the City's Housing Element and the federally funded Community Development Block Grant (CDBG). They work with the Public Safety Team to ensure that residential properties are safe and sanitary. The page says to check back for available loans or grants to assist homeowners or renters.

Source: https://www.stantonca.gov/departments/community_development/housing_program.php

## Stanton Housing Authority Annual Report

Under California Health and Safety Code (HSC) Section 34328, the Stanton Housing Authority must file an annual report of its activities for the preceding fiscal year. The report is filed with the City Clerk of the City of Stanton and with the Department of Housing and Community Development (HCD). The Stanton Housing Authority Annual Report is linked on the Housing Stanton page.

Source: https://www.stantonca.gov/departments/community_development/housing_program.php
