## Fire Department — Heartland Fire & Rescue

The Lemon Grove Fire Department is a member of Heartland Fire & Rescue. On January 1, 2010, the cities of El Cajon, La Mesa and Lemon Grove agreed to co-manage fire and emergency medical services. Heartland Fire & Rescue has eight fire stations, nine engine companies, two truck companies, three paramedic transport units and one BLS transport unit (Peak Hour Unit), serving 180,000 residents. Lemon Grove staff continue to offer local fire prevention and public education services.

Equipment housed at Station 10: Medic Engine 10, Medic Engine 210 and American Medical Response Unit 257. Each engine is staffed with a Captain, Engineer and Paramedic/Firefighter. Paramedic Medical Transport services are provided by a private contractor, American Medical Response. Volunteer opportunities include the CERT, Reserve Firefighter and Fire Explorer programs. Lemon Grove City Hall is at 3232 Main Street, Lemon Grove, CA 91945, (619) 825-3800.

## Fire Hazard Severity Zone Maps

On Monday, March 24, 2025, CAL FIRE released updated Fire Hazard Severity Zone maps. A Fire Hazard Severity Zone (FHSZ) is an area the State classifies as having a "Moderate," "High," or "Very High" wildfire risk. The maps are available for review and public comment on the City website at https://forms.lemongrove.ca.gov/Fire-Hazard-Severity-Zone-Map. Public comments will be submitted with the staff report to the City Council at the time of ordinance adoption.

Timeline:
- April 8 – May 8, 2025: receive public comment.
- May 6, 2025, at 6:00 p.m.: public hearing on the ordinance at the City Council Regular Meeting.
- May 20, 2025, at 6:00 p.m.: second reading and adoption of the ordinance (date subject to change to June 3, 2025).
- After adoption, the ordinance is submitted to the Board of Forestry and Fire Protection.

California Government Code Section 51179 requires the City to designate "Moderate," "High," and "Very High" zones by ordinance within 120 days. It does not allow local agencies to decrease the level of a zone or remove areas from the recommended map, but it does allow them to include additional areas. ## Private Property Trespassing Guidance for Businesses

Section 602 of the California Penal Code defines trespass as entering the property of another without permission or right to do so. A trespass violation exists if a Peace Officer or the Owner or Owner's Agent asks an individual to leave and the individual refuses, or returns to the property after being asked to leave. If an individual leaves voluntarily after being asked, a trespass arrest typically is not possible unless other crimes have been committed.

The City suggests these steps to deter trespassing:
- Post "No Trespassing" signs citing section 602 of the Penal Code at entrances and gates.
- Lock and secure dumpsters and recyclable receptacles at all times.
- Avoid offering food or money.
- Lock and secure power outlets, water spigots (remove handles), gates and restroom doors.
- Avoid allowing trash, shopping carts or personal belongings to accumulate on the property.
- Cut back shrubbery and foliage to prevent hiding places.
- Consider a private security service to conduct patrols.

Filing a "No Trespass Letter" with the Sheriff's Department does not give permission for the Sheriff's Department to immediately respond and arrest the suspect. If your business is in a public place that is reasonably open to the public, any individual can be present and that is not a crime. For questions, contact the City of Lemon Grove Code Enforcement Division at 619-825-3820 or the San Diego Sheriff's Department Non-Emergency line at 858-868-3200. For an emergency, call 911.

## LEAD Training for Off-Sale Alcohol Retailers

Effective January 2, 2025, all employees of Lemon Grove off-sale alcohol retailers must become LEAD (Licensee Education on Alcohol & Drugs) trained. LEAD is a prevention and education program from the Department of Alcohol Beverage Control (ABC). The training is free, online or in person, and runs approximately 3 hours. The City suggests that licensees, managers and assistant managers, and all employees who sell or deliver alcoholic beverages attend.

Online training and FAQs are at https://www.abc.ca.gov/education/lead-training/. In-person training is offered through the Marian Novak Responsible Hospitality Coalition, (858) 354-2217. The City encourages retailers to review Chapter 18.27 of Title 18 of the Municipal Code. For questions, contact Shaun Dersham at (619) 825-3827 or sdersham@lemongrove.ca.gov. The training does not meet the RBS requirements under AB-1221.
