## Just Cause Eviction Urgency Ordinance

On February 3, 2026, the Lemon Grove City Council adopted an urgency ordinance requiring just cause for the termination of residential tenancies within the City of Lemon Grove. The vote was four-fifths. The ordinance takes effect immediately and adopts State law under California Civil Code section 1946.2, with additional provisions that expand tenant protections locally. Tenants subject to no-fault just-cause terminations are entitled to enhanced notice and relocation assistance.

The ordinance remains in effect for 180 days from its effective date of February 3, 2026, unless extended by a four-fifths vote of the City Council or replaced by a regular ordinance, whichever occurs first. The City will conduct community outreach over the coming months to gather input as it evaluates longer-term policy options.

Tenants whose tenancies are terminated without just cause are encouraged to seek assistance from the Legal Aid Society of San Diego at (877) 534-2524 or https://www.lassd.org.

## Relocation Payments for No-Fault Just Cause Evictions

All tenants subject to no-fault just cause terminations are entitled to a direct payment equal to:
- One month of the U.S. Department of Housing and Urban Development (HUD) Small Area Fair Market Rent for the zip code in which the rental unit is located at the time the termination notice is issued, and
- One month of the tenant's actual contract rent in effect under the lease.

The payment increases if the tenant's household income is below 80 percent of the Area Median Income for San Diego County, or if the tenant is 60 years of age or older, disabled, or has minor children in the household. In those cases the required direct payment is two months of HUD Small Area Fair Market Rent for the applicable zip code and one month of the tenant's actual contract rent in effect under the lease.

## Homeless Services — Who to Contact

The City of Lemon Grove doesn't have a local homeless shelter. For resources, contact 2-1-1 San Diego to get connected with County Services. Call 9-1-1 immediately to report a threat to public safety. Call (858) 868-3200 for non-emergency Sheriff Department response. Call (619) 825-3810 to report a site. Code Enforcement handles private party trespassing.

When the City's Public Works Department is contacted about removal of a homeless encampment, the City will post a notice 72 hours before clean up.

To support or donate funds for hygiene kit items and other needed items, contact Christian Olivas, Management Analyst, at 619-825-3813.

## Lemon Grove Homeless Action Plan

The City Council approved the Homeless Action Plan (approved document dated 9.02.2025). The 2025 Point-in-Time Count identified 110 unsheltered individuals in Lemon Grove. The Lemon Grove School District reported 264 students experiencing housing insecurity. 297 residents reached out to 2-1-1 San Diego for homeless services in 2024, and 350 households in Lemon Grove receive Housing Choice Vouchers.

The plan's goals are to:
- Move people out of encampments and into homes.
- Increase public awareness and knowledge about housing and homelessness.
- Collaborate with other East County cities, the County Office of Homeless Solutions, the District 4 Supervisor's office, school districts, and other organizations.
- Obtain additional funding to expand homeless outreach and housing programs.
- Prevent additional residents from becoming unhoused through diversion programs.

Residents can report encampments through the City's "Report an Issue" tool. Listed resources are 2-1-1 San Diego, County of San Diego Housing Choice Vouchers (a rental assistance program), and Lemon Grove School District McKinney-Vento Services (education support for housing-insecure students). Lemon Grove does not operate shelters within city limits but coordinates with neighboring cities and the County for services. Contact: Portland Bates, Senior Management Analyst, (619) 825-3827, pbates@lemongrove.ca.gov.

## San Diego County Troy Street Project

On March 4, 2025, San Diego County officials joined the Lemon Grove City Council in a Special City Council Meeting at Roberto Alvarez Auditorium to discuss the County of San Diego Temporary Housing Project. ## Home-Sharing Permits and Vacation Rental Ban

Ordinance No. 452, adopted May 7, 2019, allows home-sharing with regulations and prohibits vacation rentals in Lemon Grove. Home-sharing is a hosted short-term rental: the resident host rents his or her primary residence to transient occupants for 30 consecutive days or less while the host resides on-site. Vacation rentals (unhosted short-term rentals) are prohibited.

Process:
- Read the ordinance. Questions go to the Community Development Department at (619) 825-3800.
- Apply for a Home-Sharing Permit. The application is $79. The permit number must be used on all advertising.
- If approved, apply for a Business License, which is $49.
- Collect and remit transient occupancy tax (TOT) as specified in the TOT Form.
- Renew the Home-Sharing Permit and Business License annually.

Rules:
- A primary residence requires living there a minimum of 275 days per year. Proof of residency requires at least two of: motor vehicle registration, driver's license, voter registration, tax documents, or a utility or phone bill dated within the last 30 days.
- The host must be present in the dwelling at least five hours within every 24-hour period of home-sharing use.
- Each rental must be a minimum of three (3) days, two (2) nights.
- Occupancy is limited to 2 guests per bedroom, excluding the host's bedroom, plus one additional guest.
- A long-term tenant may be a host only with the property owner's approval, and the owner must sign the application.
- Condominium hosts need a letter of authorization from the condominium association.
- Guests must park on-site, not on the street.

## Accessory Dwelling Units (ADU) and Junior ADUs

An ADU is an attached or detached residential dwelling unit on the same lot as an existing single-family or multi-family dwelling, providing complete independent living facilities. A junior accessory dwelling unit (JADU) is no more than 500 square feet, contained within a single-family residence, and contains only an efficiency kitchen. A JADU is not permitted within multi-family dwelling units.

Under AB 1332, the City has a preapproved ADU plans program with streamlined 30-day approval for preapproved plans. No changes to preapproved plans are allowed, including room reconfiguration or window relocation. Preapproved plans and a site-specific plan must be submitted with an ADU permit application.

Under AB2533, the City may not deny a permit for an unpermitted ADU or JADU constructed before January 1, 2020 unless correcting the violation is necessary to comply with conditions that would otherwise deem a building substandard. The City may not penalize the applicant. Homeowners may obtain a confidential third-party code inspection from a licensed contractor before applying for a permit. A Lemon Grove Substandard Housing Checklist is available.

Planning Division: (619) 825-3805.
