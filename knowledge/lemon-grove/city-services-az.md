## Alarm Permits

Under Lemon Grove Municipal Code Chapter 8.32, an alarm permit is required to operate an alarm system within the City of Lemon Grove. It is required for alarm systems in both businesses and residences. The permit is valid until there is a change in the alarm user or a change in address location of the alarm system. It is non-transferable to a new location or alarm user.

To apply, email the completed application and submit payment online at the Lemon Grove payment processing site (the City is not accepting online payments at this time). By mail, send the completed application and a check payable to the City of Lemon Grove to: City of Lemon Grove, Attn: License Clerk, 3232 Main St, Lemon Grove, CA 92119. You can also apply in person at City Hall.

## False Alarm Penalty Fees

The Lemon Grove Municipal Code allows a maximum of two (2) false alarms in any six month period without penalty. For each false alarm in excess of two in any six-month period, the penalties are:
- Third false alarm: $50
- Fourth false alarm: $100
- Fifth false alarm: $150
- Each additional false alarm: $200

If you receive notice of a false alarm penalty, correct the cause of the false alarms and submit timely payment of your fees to the City.

## Construction and Demolition (C&D) Debris Recycling

C&D Debris means discarded materials removed from the project site during construction or demolition operations. It includes asphalt, concrete, brick, lumber, sheet rock, tile, cardboard, and metals. Land Clearing Debris includes dirt, rocks, shrubs, trees, or other woody landscape materials.

All projects are required to recycle a minimum of 75% of the C&D Debris (by weight) and 100% of the Land Clearing Debris. The C&D ordinance (Title 13, Chapter 13.32) went into effect on December 1, 2005, and was updated in May 2011. The page also links a Conversion Rate Table, Recycling Centers, and a Waste Management Plan (WMP) Form.

## Fire Department — Heartland Fire & Rescue

The Lemon Grove Fire Department is a member of Heartland Fire & Rescue. On January 1, 2010, the cities of El Cajon, La Mesa, and Lemon Grove agreed to co-manage fire and emergency medical services. Heartland Fire & Rescue has eight fire stations, nine engine companies, two truck companies, three paramedic transport units, and one BLS transport unit (Peak Hour Unit) serving 180,000 residents. Lemon Grove staff continue to offer local fire prevention and public education services.

Equipment housed at Station 10: Medic Engine 10, Medic Engine 210, and American Medical Response Unit 257. Paramedic Medical Transport services are provided by a private contractor, American Medical Response. Volunteer opportunities include the CERT, Reserve Firefighter, and Fire Explorer programs.

## Fire Hazard Severity Zone Maps

On Monday, March 24, 2025, CAL FIRE released updated Fire Hazard Severity Zone (FHSZ) maps. A FHSZ is an area classified by the State as having a "Moderate," "High," or "Very High" wildfire risk, based on vegetation, terrain, weather patterns, and fire history. The maps are available for review and public comment at https://forms.lemongrove.ca.gov/Fire-Hazard-Severity-Zone-Map. Public comments will be submitted with the staff report to the City Council at the time of ordinance adoption.

Timeline:
- April 8 – May 8, 2025: receive public comment.
- May 6, 2025, at 6:00 p.m.: public hearing on the ordinance at the City Council Regular Meeting.
- May 20, 2025, at 6:00 p.m.: second reading and adoption of the ordinance (date subject to change to June 3, 2025).
- After adoption, the ordinance is submitted to the Board of Forestry and Fire Protection.

California Government Code Section 51179 requires the City to designate "Moderate," "High," and "Very High" zones by ordinance within 120 days. It does not allow local agencies to decrease the level of a zone or remove areas from the recommended map, but does allow adding areas not identified by the State Fire Marshal.

## Parking Citations — How to Pay

You may pay a parking citation in person, online or by mail. Your citation will not show in the system until 7 days after the date written on your citation. Payment plans are available upon request. For alternate payment options, visit www.citationprocessingcenter.com.

To pay online, you need one of these combinations: Citation Number & Time of Violation; Notice Number & 5 Digit Zip Code; License Plate Number & State Issuing; or Citation Number & Date of Violation. Citation inquiries: (800) 989-2058.

Source: https://www.lemongrove.ca.gov/living-in-lemon-grove/public-safety/sheriff-s-department/parking-citations/

## Parking Citations — How to Appeal

Appeals must be submitted within 21 days from the date the citation was issued. Wait 3 business days from the time of issue before appealing. If your appeal is denied, you have 21 days from the date of notification to pay the citation at the original amount.

You may appeal by mail, in person or online. To appeal online, you must first find your citation using one of the search options above. To appeal by mail, send your parking citation with all supporting backup to: City of Lemon Grove- Citation Processing Center, PO BOX 10479, Newport Beach, CA 92658. Citation inquiries: (800) 989-2058.

Source: https://www.lemongrove.ca.gov/living-in-lemon-grove/public-safety/sheriff-s-department/parking-citations/
