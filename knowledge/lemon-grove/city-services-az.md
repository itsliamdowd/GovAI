## Animal Licenses

All dogs living within the City of Lemon Grove must be licensed, in line with Lemon Grove Municipal Code Section 18.16.030. Renewals may be purchased in 1 and 3-year increments. Specify the license term you want on your application. The term may not exceed the expiration of your rabies vaccination date.

For questions, contact Cynthia Garcia at (619) 825-3823, or cgarcia@lemongrove.ca.gov.

## Animal Services — Chula Vista Animal Care Facility

The City of Lemon Grove uses the City of Chula Vista for animal services. Phone: (619) 476-2476. Hours: Monday–Friday 8:30 a.m.–5:00 p.m.; Saturday–Sunday 8:30 a.m.–4:30 p.m.

If you are bitten by an animal, or your animal bit someone else, call Chula Vista Animal Services. Be ready to give a description of the animal, its name, the address where it lives, the owner's name, the time of day you were bitten, and the location on your body. If you see an animal alone and injured, or creating a traffic hazard or a danger to human safety, call Chula Vista Animal Services immediately. If an injured animal's owner is present, the owner is responsible for getting it medical care.

Call Chula Vista Animal Services during daytime business hours to report animal disturbances, noise complaints, sanitation problems, mistreatment or abuse, neglect, or other animal-related problems. For a dead animal, call the City's contractor, Dead Animal Removal (D.A.R.) Services, at (619) 390-8204.

## Lemon Grove Library and Cool Zones

The Lemon Grove Library is a branch of the San Diego County Library system at 3001 School Lane, Lemon Grove CA 91945, phone 619-463-9819. It offers books, digital resources, public computers, 3-D printing, and events for all age groups. Branch Manager: Amrit Gill.

The County designates air-conditioned Cool Zone sites in summer where older adults and others can escape the heat. The current program season runs from June 1 - October 31. The County of San Diego, in partnership with SDG&E, provides free electric fans to those who are 60 years of age and older, or disabled, living on limited incomes. Relying on electric fans for cooling is not recommended if temperatures exceed 90 degrees. Find a Cool Zone near you on the County of San Diego's web page.

## Organic Waste and Edible Food Recovery (SB 1383)

SB 1383 regulations went into effect on January 1, 2022 and require every California city and county to have an enforceable SB 1383 ordinance. The law sets statewide targets to reduce disposal of organic waste by 50% below 2014 levels by 2020 and 75% by 2025. It also requires the State to increase edible food recovery by at least 20% by 2025. The City's Trash & Recycling page lists resources including the List of Edible Food Recovery Organizations (FROs) in Lemon Grove and the Edible Food Recovery Guide.

## Construction & Demolition (C&D) Debris Recycling

All projects must recycle a minimum of 75% of C&D Debris (by weight) and 100% of Land Clearing Debris. C&D Debris includes asphalt, concrete, brick, lumber, sheet rock, tile, cardboard, and metals. The C&D ordinance (Title 13, Chapter 13.32) went into effect on December 1, 2005 and was updated in May 2011. ## Street Sweeping

The City contracts with Sweeping Corp of America. Residential streets are swept once a month on different days throughout the City. Commercial streets are swept twice a month. Keep the street in front of your home free of vehicles and other obstacles by 6:00 a.m. Trees that are too low or overgrown vegetation, if privately owned, should be trimmed. Street sweepers will not collect piles of leaves or green waste intentionally left in the gutter. A map of the sweeping schedule is available on the page. Contact City Hall at (619) 825-3800.

## Sewer Spills, Maintenance and Laterals

Report any sewer spill or manhole overflow immediately to the Lemon Grove Sanitation District at 619-825-3810. After 5:30 pm and on weekends, call Heartland Fire & Rescue Dispatch at 619-441-1621.

The Sanitation District maintains and repairs the sewer main line. If a licensed plumber, acting as the property owner's agent, determines that a blockage is in the district's main line, as verified by video, the district or its agent will respond to confirm and clear it. The property owner is responsible for the lateral, from the structure to the sewer main. Digging in the public right of way requires an Encroachment Permit from the City. Lateral work must be performed by a contractor with a California C-34 Plumbing Contractor's License or higher. The Sanitation District and the City must inspect the installation after the work is complete but before backfilling.

Any property or building needs a permit from the Sanitation District before connecting to the district's main lines. Permits are available at Lemon Grove City Hall, 3232 Main Street, and costs vary by property type.

Source: https://www.lemongrove.ca.gov/our-government/public-works/sanitation/wastewater/

## Stormwater Pollution — Reporting and Prevention

Report illegal dumping (paint, chemicals, etc.) and irrigation runoff, which are prohibited from flowing into the City's storm drain system. Stormwater Hotline: 619-825-3820 or email Promero@lemongrove.ca.gov. The Stormwater Division is by appointment only at 3232 Main Street, (619) 825-3820.

To prevent pollution: avoid over-watering, clean up after pets, sweep yard waste into a green waste can instead of hosing off paved areas, use non-toxic household cleaning products, and use soap sparingly when washing your car at home. Pour soapy bucket water down the sink, not in the street. The JRMP Annual Report is available upon request at City Hall. Helix Water District customers may be eligible for rebates (hwd.com/rebates) and free residential water efficiency evaluations upon request.

Source: https://www.lemongrove.ca.gov/our-government/public-works/sanitation/wastewater/stormwater/

Residents can use Report-A-Problem to submit issues such as graffiti, potholes, dead animals, malfunctioning traffic signals, and abandoned vehicles, with location, description, and photographs. Due to limited staffing, response times are around 12 business days unless the issue impacts the public's safety.
