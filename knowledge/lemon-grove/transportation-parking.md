## Parking Citations — How to Pay

You may pay your parking citation in person, online or by mail. Your citation will not show in the system until 7 days after the date written on your citation. Payment plans are available upon request. For alternate payment options, visit www.citationprocessingcenter.com.

To pay online you need one of the following: Citation Number & Time of Violation; Notice Number & 5 Digit Zip Code; License Plate Number & State Issuing; or Citation Number & Date of Violation. Citation inquiries can be made by calling (800) 989-2058.

Source: https://www.lemongrove.ca.gov/living-in-lemon-grove/public-safety/sheriff-s-department/parking-citations/

## Parking Citations — How to Appeal

Appeals must be submitted within 21 days from the date the citation was issued. Wait 3 business days from the time of issue before appealing. If your appeal is denied, you have 21 days from the date of notification to pay the citation at the original amount.

You may appeal by mail, in person or online. To appeal online, you must first find your citation using one of the search options listed for online payment. To appeal by mail, send your parking citation along with all supporting backup to: City of Lemon Grove- Citation Processing Center, PO BOX 10479, Newport Beach, CA 92658. Citation inquiries can be made by calling (800) 989-2058.

Source: https://www.lemongrove.ca.gov/living-in-lemon-grove/public-safety/sheriff-s-department/parking-citations/

## Connect Main Street — Main Street Traffic Control (March 2024)

Effective March 11, 2024, one-way traffic control was set up in the southerly direction on Main Street from Central Ave to Burnell Ave. Main Street northbound is closed from Burnell Ave to Central Ave.

Work began on February 22, 2024. It includes removal of 33 trees between Central and Burnell Ave (replacement trees to be planted), clearing and grubbing, and surveying. Crews will also mill asphalt to prepare the subgrade for public improvements and trench for utility installation.

Source: https://www.lemongrove.ca.gov/news/posts/connect-main-street-project-kickoff/

## Road Closure — Central Ave at Lemon Grove Ave (Starting September 3, 2024)

Central Ave at Lemon Grove Ave closed to westbound and eastbound vehicular traffic starting September 3, 2024, for Phase 1-2 of the Connect Main Street Project. The affected area remains open to pedestrians. The closure was projected to last approximately 4 weeks, with detour routes indicated.

The closure was for pavement and striping improvements around the MTS rail crossing, a sidewalk and an ADA ramp on the southwest side of Central Ave/Lemon Grove Ave, landscaping, and minor traffic signal modifications. For questions, call the Department of Public Works at 619-825-3810 between 7:30 a.m. – 5:30 p.m.

## SDG&E Gas Line Repair Along Main Street (January–April 2026)

SDG&E repairs along Main Street (between Davidson Ave. and Buena Vista St.) begin January 14, 2026 and are expected to continue through April 17, 2026. Construction is scheduled on weekdays from 7:00 a.m. to 7:00 p.m., with night or weekend work only if necessary. Temporary traffic control measures will be in place. Bike paths along Main Street are expected to remain partially open.

The work temporarily affects Phase 3 of the Connect Main Street project. SDG&E has told the City that Phase 3 improvements will be fully restored once all safety work is complete. If you smell gas and believe it may be an emergency, evacuate the area immediately and call 1-800-611-7343 from a safe location. Media and project inquiries: Leonardo Flores, Project Manager, (619) 676-5708.

## Madera Street Improvement Project

The Madera Street Improvement Project covers Madera Street between Massachusetts Avenue and 69th Street. It will study how people get around, how fast cars are going, and where collisions have happened, in order to implement safety features. The project is in its early design stages, with more opportunities to get involved later.

Improvements currently being studied: raised crosswalk at Ramon Street, roundabout at Ramon Street, 4-way stop at Ramon Street, travel lane narrowing, bike lanes, bike lanes separated by a raised curb, improved street lighting, street trees, accessible curb ramps, and curb extensions.

An open house is scheduled for October 5 from 4 to 7 p.m. at San Altos Elementary (1750 Madera St, Lemon Grove, CA 91945). You can drop by anytime during the meeting to learn more, ask questions, and take a brief project survey. A brief 10-question survey on travel habits and experiences is also available at bit.ly/4hhwV0N. Project website: bit.ly/4ieIvLa. Project contact: Atilano Moran at (619) 825-3811 or amoran@lemongrove.ca.gov.
