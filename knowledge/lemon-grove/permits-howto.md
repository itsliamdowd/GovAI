## Alarm Permit — Requirement and How to Apply

Under Lemon Grove Municipal Code Chapter 8.32, an alarm permit is required to operate an alarm system in the City of Lemon Grove, for both businesses and residences. The permit is valid until there is a change in the alarm user or a change in address location of the alarm system. It is non-transferable to a new location or alarm user.

To apply, complete the Alarm Permit Application and pay the $75 fee. Submission options:
- Online: Email the completed application. Payment through the Lemon Grove payment processing site is listed as "coming soon".
- Mail: Send the completed application and a check payable to the City of Lemon Grove to City of Lemon Grove, Attn: License Clerk, 3232 Main St, Lemon Grove, CA 91945.
- In person: Visit City Hall during its business hours.

## Alarm Permit — False Alarm Penalties

The Lemon Grove Municipal Code allows a maximum of two (2) false alarms in any six-month period without penalty. For each false alarm in excess of two in any six-month period, the penalty is:
- Third false alarm: $50
- Fourth false alarm: $100
- Fifth false alarm: $150
- Each additional false alarm: $200

If you receive notice of a false alarm penalty, correct the cause of the false alarms and pay your fees to the City on time.

## Building Permit — How to Apply

Building permit applications are accepted only in person at the Building Division counter, and all documents, including the application and plans, must be hard copies. The Building Division does not accept online applications or digital submittals. Print and complete the Building Permit Application. Applications typically require four sets of full-sized D sheets (24" x 36"), with plan sets folded by hand to 9" x 24".

Process:
1. Check with the Planning Department at (619) 825-3805 to confirm the project is allowed.
2. Prepare the documentation.
3. Schedule an appointment to submit the package by calling (619) 825-3800.
4. If the application and payment are accepted, the project is routed for review. Most projects are reviewed by Building, Engineering, Fire, and Planning.

Each review typically takes 30 days minimum. Please do not check status earlier than 30 days from the submittal date. Applicants are emailed an approval or comments once all departments finish. If comments are received, resubmittal is necessary and the 30-day review repeats. After an emailed approval, schedule an appointment at (619) 825-3800 for permit issuance, which often requires an issuance fee that includes inspection costs.

Plan check and permit fees may be collected, and the cashier accepts cash, check, MasterCard, and Visa. The applicant must be a licensed contractor, property owner, or authorized agent. Authorized agents need a letter of authorization from the property owner.

## Building Permit — Inspections and Counter Hours

Keep the building permit and approved plans at the site whenever work is being performed. Inspection costs are included in the permit issuance fee. No additional inspection fees are typically necessary unless the inspector determines the inspection was unnecessary.

Schedule inspections by calling (619) 825-3808, which is an unanswered phone line, so leave a message. Requests received before 3pm typically occur the next business day. No confirmation call is given. Inspections occur between 11am - 3pm, Monday - Friday, except major holidays, with no specific time given. For more information, call (619) 825-3847 or email Building2@lemongrove.ca.gov.

The inspection card is the certificate of occupancy (C of O). Lemon Grove does not issue a separate C of O.

Building Division counter hours are 7:30am - 12:00pm, Monday - Thursday, and the office is closed on Fridays. Appointments between 8:00am - 11:30am receive priority service at (619) 825-3800. Appointments after 1pm may be available on request. The Building Division is at 3232 Main Street, Lemon Grove, CA 91945.

## Encroachment Permit

Encroachment permits are required for any work in the city's right-of-way or a city easement. Examples include:
- Minor new improvements such as sidewalks, pedestrian ramps, and curb cuts for driveways
- Removal and replacement of existing curb, gutter, and sidewalk
- Street light work
- Trenching
- Utility services such as water and sewer laterals
- Roll off trash containers in the right-of-way

Applications and fees are submitted to the Engineering Division for review. Once the application is approved, the permit is issued. For questions call 619-825-3810. Initial fees and/or deposits are due in person when the engineering permit submission is made at City Hall. Additional fees and/or deposits may be required if the initial deposit is exhausted.

Wireless facilities in the public right-of-way are covered by an Encroachment Policy for Wireless Facilities that the City Council adopted on October 15, 2019. Design standards and the application are linked on the page.

## Transportation Permit

Transportation permits are required to operate or move a vehicle or special equipment considered an unusually large, wide, or heavy load through the City of Lemon Grove. Submit the application to the Engineering Division for approval. A Certificate of Insurance naming the City of Lemon Grove as additional insured for a minimum of $1 millions is required, along with a $16 permit fee. For questions call 619-825-3810.

## Business License — How to Obtain

Any business or individual conducting business in the City of Lemon Grove must have a business license. This includes businesses licensed in another jurisdiction, rental properties with three or more units, and businesses run out of a home or apartment. Applications received without the required fee will not be processed. A business license does not supersede zoning or building regulations. Before signing a lease or contract, contact the Planning Department to confirm zoning and the Building Department to confirm building use. For zoning, building, and signage questions, contact City staff at 619-825-3805.

Submit the application to City Hall in person, Monday through Thursday, between 7:00 a.m. and 5:00 p.m., by mail, or by email to Serena Amavisca at samavisca@lemongrove.ca.gov. Planning staff must review and approve all applications. License types:
- In-City: for businesses located within city limits. This includes Home Occupation (home-based businesses) and Home Sharing (short-term rentals of 30 consecutive days or less in a primary residence). In-City and Home Occupation applications must be submitted along with the Home Sharing application.
- Out-of-City: for businesses not based in Lemon Grove that conduct business within the city limits.

All business signs must be approved by the City, and new signage requires a Sign Permit. Temporary signs must also be approved and permitted by the City Planning Department.

Source: https://www.lemongrove.ca.gov/business-development/business-licenses/

## Business License — Annual Renewal

Business licenses are valid for one calendar year and expire on December 31st, regardless of when the application was submitted and approved. During the last quarter of the year, a courtesy renewal notice is mailed to the mailing address on file. The license must be renewed by January 31st to avoid late penalty fees.

Pay renewals by mailing a check, by credit card over the phone at (619) 825-3800, or at City Hall, Monday through Thursday, 7:00 a.m to 5:30 p.m.

## Home-Sharing Permit — Process and Fees

Home-sharing is a "hosted" short-term rental of 30 consecutive days or less in the host's primary residence, with the host on-site throughout the stay. Vacation rentals (unhosted short-term rentals) are prohibited in Lemon Grove.

Steps:
1. Read the home-sharing ordinance (Ordinance No. 452). Questions go to the Community Development Department at (619) 825-3800.
2. Apply for a Home-Sharing Permit. The application is $79. The permit number must be used on all advertising.
3. If approved, apply for a Business License, which is $49.
4. The permit and license are issued and the host is added to the City's Home-Sharing Registry.
5. Collect and remit transient occupancy tax (TOT) as specified in the TOT Form.
6. Renew the Home-Sharing Permit and Business License annually. If you stop home-sharing, notify the City.

Source: https://www.lemongrove.ca.gov/our-government/community-development-department/planning-division/current-planning-information/home-sharing/

## Home-Sharing Permit — Eligibility and Rules

- Host: the property owner, or with the owner's approval, a long-term tenant or lessee. Tenants need the owner's signature on the application. Only one host is allowed per dwelling unit.
- Primary residence: you must live there a minimum of 275 days per year. Proof of residency requires at least two of the following: motor vehicle registration, driver's license, voter registration, tax documents showing the unit as your primary residence, or a utility or phone bill with the unit's address dated within the last 30 days.
- Presence: the host must be in the dwelling at least five hours within every 24-hour period it is used for home-sharing.
- Apartments need the property owner's authorization. Condominiums need a letter of authorization from the condominium association, plus the owner's signature if renting.
- Each rental must be a minimum of three (3) days, two (2) nights. There is no limit on how often you can home-share.
- Occupancy is limited to 2 guests per bedroom, excluding the host's bedroom, plus one additional guest.
- Guests must park on-site, not on the street. Those spaces are in addition to the dwelling's required parking.

## ADU Permits — Preapproved Plans

An ADU permit application using a preapproved design must include the preapproved ADU plans and a site-specific plan. The project description should state that it is a preapproved ADU design and identify the plan preparer. No changes to preapproved plans are allowed, including reconfiguring rooms, relocating windows, or attaching to structures. Other departments must still review the submitted plans and are likely to have comments, because their comments depend on site characteristics such as topography. AB 1332 provides a streamlined 30-day approval for preapproved plans.

Anyone providing preapproved ADU plans must submit plans that are not site-specific, including architectural plans and structural drawings and calculations, to the Lemon Grove Building Department for review and approval.

Under AB2533, homeowners may obtain a confidential third-party code inspection from a licensed contractor before applying for a permit. This applies to unpermitted ADUs or JADUs built before January 1, 2020. The City provides a Lemon Grove Substandard Housing Checklist.
