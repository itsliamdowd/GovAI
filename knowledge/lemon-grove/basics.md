## About Lemon Grove and City Hall

The City of Lemon Grove was incorporated in 1977 and officially became California's 414th municipality on July 1, 1977. It has more than 27,000 residents. It is nine miles from downtown San Diego and 12 miles from the airport.

Lemon Grove City Hall is at 3232 Main Street, Lemon Grove, CA 91945, phone (619) 825-3800. Hours of operation are Monday - Friday 8:00 A.M. to 5:30 P.M.

The City is governed by a five-member City Council elected at large with four-year alternating terms. The Mayor is elected directly by the voters. The City Clerk, City Manager, and City Attorney positions are filled by City Council appointments. The City contracts law enforcement services with the San Diego County Sheriff's Office. Fire services are provided by a Joint Powers Authority with Heartland Fire and Rescue, which also serves the cities of La Mesa and El Cajon.

## Parks, Recreation and Community Programs

The City maintains 8 smoke and alcohol-free public parks. Signature events include the annual summer Concerts and Movies in the Park and the Annual Community Bonfire. The Day Camp program offers several sessions for youth throughout the year. Adult softball is played year-round and is designed for adults of all ages and skill levels. The City holds quarterly community clean-up events and has four social media platforms.

## City Manager's Office

The City Manager is the City's chief administrative officer and implements the policies and programs established by the City Council. The office coordinates and prepares City Council agendas, prepares the annual budget for City Council consideration, and supervises and coordinates City departments. The current City Manager is Tony Winney.

The City Manager's Office oversees the City Clerk (Brown Act compliance, City Council and Planning Commission agendas and minutes, public records, elections, and FPPC filings). It also oversees Human Resources / Personnel (recruitment, employee benefits and compensation, labor relations, training, and employment opportunities).

## City Council Vacancy — Appointment Eligibility and Process (August 2025)

After Councilmember Sitivi "Steve" Faiai passed away on July 8, 2025, the City Council opened applications for appointment to the City Council. The appointee serves through December 2026. The position is part-time with monthly compensation of $803, complete with benefits.

Applicants must be a United States citizen and at least 18 years of age on or before appointment. They must be a resident and registered voter in the City of Lemon Grove at the time of application. They must not have been convicted of certain felony crimes involving bribery, embezzlement, extortion, theft of public funds, perjury, or conspiracy to commit any of those crimes (as outlined in California Elections Code Section 20).

The application period was Thursday, August 7 – Thursday, September 4, 2025 at 5:00 p.m. The deadline was firm and late applications would not be accepted. Applications were available at the City Clerk's Office, City Hall, 3232 Main Street, Lemon Grove, CA 91945, or online at www.lemongrove.ca.gov. The Council Applicant Information Forum was Wednesday, August 13, 2025 at 5:30 p.m. at the Lemon Grove Community Center, 3146 School Lane. Interviews and presentations were set for Tuesday, September 16, 2025 at 6:00 p.m. Each qualified applicant had three minutes to present, and presentation order was set by a random drawing conducted by the City Clerk.

## Troy Street Sleeping Cabins (County of San Diego Project)

The Troy Street Sleeping Cabins project was approved by the San Diego County Board of Supervisors in July 2024. It is on State-owned property leased to the County, so the City of Lemon Grove has no land use authority over the approval and will not contribute financially. The County reduced the number of cabins from 70 to 60. They will serve as interim housing for veterans, seniors, youth, and adults.

The facility will not be first-come first-served. Clients will be assessed and given background checks, and sex offenders, arsonists, and felons will not be eligible. The County states there will be zero tolerance for alcohol and drugs, and no guests will be allowed. The site will have perimeter fencing and be secured 24 hours a day/7 days a week with a hired security guard and on-site management. The County or the site operator will handle complaints. The County maintains a project website (Troy Street Sleeping Cabins | Engage San Diego County) with a direct way to contact the County. Construction was projected to begin July 2025, with completion estimated by Summer 2026.
