## Residential Trash Collection — Schedule and Cart Placement

CR&R collects residential refuse weekly. Collection occurs between 7 a.m. and 7 p.m., Monday through Friday. Have containers out by 6:30 a.m. on collection day. Containers can be placed out no earlier than 7 p.m. the evening before collection. All containers must be returned to the storage area by 7 p.m. the night of collection.

Place carts curbside or in the alley by 6:30 a.m. Carts should be at least one foot apart and three feet away from obstructions such as fire hydrants, trees, or cars. Wheels and handles must face your property. Carts placed on the sidewalk, in the driveway, or in the parkway will not be collected. Carts with handles facing the street will not be collected either. Uncollected carts typically get a yellow notification tag explaining why and how to contact CR&R to schedule a new collection.

## Holiday Trash Collection

Residential refuse is not collected on New Year's Day, Memorial Day, July 4th, Labor Day, Thanksgiving, and Christmas. If the observed holiday falls on a weekday, collection is delayed by one day for the remainder of the week. If the observed holiday falls on a weekend, collection follows the regular schedule. Check the City Calendar for upcoming holiday schedules.

## Summer Saturday Trash Collection

During the summer months, specific locations in West Newport and on Balboa Island may be eligible for Saturday collection in addition to the regular weekday collection. Saturday service collects only Landfill/Municipal Solid Waste (black-top carts). Recyclables (blue-top carts) and Organics (green-top carts) are collected on the normal weekday schedule. Service starts two Saturdays before the 4th of July and ends the Saturday before Labor Day.

West Newport: houses on the streets from the River Jetty to A St, and between Balboa Blvd and Ocean Front, receive Saturday collection. Balboa Island: property owners who pay the Transient Occupancy Tax (TOT) and are approved by the City's Revenue Division are eligible. To verify eligibility for your property, call 949-644-3055.

## Residential Carts and Recycling Cart Sizes

Each Newport Beach household receives a black-top cart for trash, a blue-top cart for recyclables, and a green-top cart for organics waste. All trash and recycling must be placed in the proper carts. Residents can choose a 32, 64, or 96-gallon blue-top recycling cart. They can also get up to one free additional recycling container, and may request an additional cart for a nominal fee. Recycling service day is the same day as trash collection.

Source: https://www.newportbeachca.gov/government/departments/municipal-operations/trash-recycling

## Expanded Residential Recycling Program (SB 1383)

Under SB 1383, recycling is mandatory for all households. Residents must place clean recyclables in the blue-top carts. Organic waste includes grass clippings, yard waste, food and kitchen scraps, and food-soiled paper. Residents may opt into a two-gallon pail for collecting organic materials in the kitchen.

All households may have one trash cart, up to two recycling carts, and one organics cart at no additional charge. Trash collection fees are paid entirely by the City, and residents pay a recycling fee that offsets the City's costs. Beginning in July 2022, any additional carts are available for an extra charge, billed quarterly. Cart rollout service, where CR&R workers move carts to the street and return them empty, is also available.

## Organics Recycling — Compostable Bags and Business Compliance

Compostable bags with the "Biodegradable Products Institute" (BPI) logo, newspaper bags, or brown paper bags may line the food scrap pail and go into the residential organics cart. Plastic bags such as grocery bags are not allowed in the organics cart. Lack of compliance with organic recycling laws may trigger escalating fines.

Businesses and multi-family complexes needing help with organics recycling or employee training can contact the Public Works Department, Municipal Operations Division at 949-644-3055.

## Bulky Item Pickup

Bulky items are household items that cannot fit into your automated carts and must be manageable by a two-person crew. Examples include couches, desks, chairs, tables, mattresses, refrigerators, washers/dryers, dishwashers, monitors, microwaves, televisions, printers, scanners, computers, and keyboards.

Each household covered under the City's residential service contract with CR&R may order up to five bulky pickups per year, with a maximum of five items per collection, for free. Additional collections beyond the first five may be arranged for an additional cost. To arrange a pickup, contact CR&R at 949-667-4158.

## Sharps Disposal

Sharps (syringes, needles, autoinjectors) are hazardous waste and are illegal to dispose of in the trash. They may be collected door side in an approved puncture-proof container. All Newport Beach residents can receive one free sharps container with pre-paid postage and return box every three months through the City's residential refuse contract with CR&R. A fresh container is delivered upon collection of a filled one.

Sharps by mail kits are also available at no charge at City Hall and the OASIS Senior Center. Complete the Sharps Proof of Residency Form at those counters to receive a kit.

Source: https://www.newportbeachca.gov/government/departments/public-works/municipal-operations/trash-recycling/additional-waste-services

## Pharmaceutical Waste Disposal

In Orange County, pharmaceuticals can be taken to one of four Orange County Household Hazardous Waste Collection Centers, or to a pharmacy participating in the State of California Medicine Takeback Program. They can also be disposed of in a trash can after "treating" them. To locate a participating pharmacy, use https://medtakebackcalifornia.org/. The page advises calling ahead to confirm services are available.

To treat medicine for trash disposal, add water and mix capsules, pills, or liquids with salt, dirt, or ashes from the fireplace/barbecue. For pills in a pack, wrap the entire package with several layers of duct tape. Then seal the medicine in its original container with the safety cap and tape the lid with duct tape. For liquids, place the bottle inside a plastic container. Hide the medication in an outer bag or box wrapped in several layers of newspaper.

## Household Hazardous Waste — Door-Side Collection

CR&R collects household hazardous waste (HHW) such as motor oil, oil filters, paint, batteries, household cleaners, pool chemicals, electronic waste, and pesticides. Contact CR&R at 949-667-4158 to arrange door-side collection. A CR&R representative will ask for a specific count and list of the items to be collected, which is required by law, so be prepared to provide it.

## Street & Alley Sweeping

Weekly street sweeping removes debris from the streets and keeps pollutants from entering the ocean, waterways, and Newport Harbor. The City recognizes that street parking during sweeping is a challenge, especially in the summer months. To report issues or concerns about street sweeping, submit a request through the City's Quest work order request system at https://apps.newportbeachca.gov/quest/.

## Library Children's Services

The Newport Beach Public Library is at 1000 Avocado Avenue, Newport Beach, CA 92660, phone 949-717-3800. Children's programs include storytimes, coding classes, art projects, STEAM programs, and reading with therapy dogs. The 1000 Books program is a year-round reading program for children who have not yet entered kindergarten. Sign up for a free Beanstack account to register.

The Summer Reading Program runs seven weeks each summer for adults, teens, and kids. Student subscription databases are free to use with your library card. Schools can use the Schools and Community Services forms to plan field trips, request a librarian speaker, or get assignment support.
