# City Services A–Z

## Residential Trash & Recycling Carts

Each Newport Beach household receives a black-top cart for trash, a blue-top cart for recyclables and a green-top cart for organics waste. All trash and recycling must be placed in the proper carts. The City's waste hauler is CR&R Environmental Services.

Residents can choose a 32, 64 or 96-gallon blue-top recycling cart and up to one free additional recycling container. An additional cart can be requested for a nominal fee. Recycling is collected on the same day as trash collection.

Source: https://www.newportbeachca.gov/government/departments/municipal-operations/trash-recycling

## Expanded Residential Recycling (SB 1383) — Organics and Extra Carts

State Senate Bill 1383 makes recycling mandatory for all households and requires recycling of organic waste. Organic waste includes grass clippings, yard waste, food and kitchen scraps, and food-soiled paper. Every household receives a green-top cart for organic waste. Residents may also opt into a two-gallon pail for collecting organics in the kitchen.

All households may have one trash cart, up to two recycling carts, and one organics cart at no additional charge. The City pays trash collection fees entirely, and residents pay a recycling fee that offsets the City's costs. Since July 2022, any additional carts cost extra and are billed quarterly.

CR&R uses split-body trucks for alley collection on the Peninsula, on Balboa Island and in Corona del Mar. These trucks collect recyclables and green waste at the same time.

Source: https://www.newportbeachca.gov/government/departments/public-works/municipal-operations/trash-recycling/expanded-residential-recycling

## Bulky Item Pickup

Bulky items are household items that don't fit in your automated carts. They must be manageable by a two-person crew. Examples include couches, desks, chairs, tables, mattresses, refrigerators, washers/dryers, dishwashers, monitors, microwaves, televisions, printers, scanners, computers and keyboards.

Each household covered by the City's residential service contract with CR&R may order up to five bulky pickups per year, with a maximum of five items per collection, for free. Additional collections cost extra. To arrange a pickup, contact CR&R at 949-667-4158.

Source: https://www.newportbeachca.gov/government/departments/public-works/municipal-operations/trash-recycling/additional-waste-services

## Medication and Sharps Disposal

In Orange County, pharmaceuticals can be taken to one of four Orange County Household Hazardous Waste Collection Centers. They can also go to a pharmacy participating in the Medicine Takeback Program with the State of California, or be disposed of in a trash can using the page's "treat and seal" instructions. The page advises calling a pharmacy ahead to confirm its drop-off or mail-back service.

Sharps (syringes, needles, autoinjectors) are hazardous waste and are illegal to dispose of in the trash. All Newport Beach residents can receive one free sharps container with pre-paid postage and return box every three months through the City's residential refuse contract with CR&R. Sharps by mail kits are also available at no charge at City Hall and the OASIS Senior Center. To get a kit, complete the Sharps Proof of Residency Form at those counters.

Source: https://www.newportbeachca.gov/government/departments/public-works/municipal-operations/trash-recycling/additional-waste-services

## Household Hazardous Waste Collection

CR&R collects household hazardous waste (HHW) at the door. Examples are motor oil, oil filters, paint, batteries, household cleaners, pool chemicals, electronic waste and pesticides. Contact CR&R at 949-667-4158 to arrange collection. CR&R's representative will ask for a specific count and list of the items, which is required by law, so have this information ready.

Source: https://www.newportbeachca.gov/government/departments/public-works/municipal-operations/trash-recycling/additional-waste-services

## Street & Alley Sweeping

Weekly street sweeping removes debris from the streets and keeps pollutants from entering the ocean, waterways and Newport Harbor. Residents and businesses with issues or concerns about street sweeping can report them through the City's Quest work order request system at https://apps.newportbeachca.gov/quest/. For street sweeping questions, you can also call the Utilities Department Administrative offices at (949) 644-3011.

Source: https://www.newportbeachca.gov/government/departments/utilities/street-sweeping

## Utilities Department — Contacts

For billing inquiries, account information or customer service, call the Revenue Division at (949) 644-3141. For water and sewer emergencies, call (949) 644-3011 or the Police Department after-hours at (949) 644-3717.

For questions about water, wastewater (sewer), storm drain, street sweeping, streetlights or graffiti abatement, call the Utilities Department Administrative offices at (949) 644-3011. The department oversees water, wastewater, storm drain and tidal valve systems, street sweeping, streetlights, and oil and gas operations.

## Water Service Requests (Quest)

The City's Quest system handles water service requests online at https://apps.newportbeachca.gov/quest/. The Water Services category includes these request types: Start Water Service, Close Water Account, Update Water Billing Info, Inefficient Water Use and Water General Questions.

Other Quest categories include Graffiti, Leaf Blowers, Municipal Code Violations, Parks, Refuse, Sewers & Drains, Street Lights, Streets, Trash, Trees and Water Conservation.

Source: https://www.newportbeachca.gov/government/departments/utilities/water-services/start-or-stop-water-service

## Newport Beach Public Library

The Library is at 1000 Avocado Avenue, Newport Beach, CA 92660. Phone: 949-717-3800. Locations are Central Library, Crean Mariners Branch Library, Balboa Branch Library and Corona del Mar Branch Library. Hours are posted on the Library's Hours & Locations page.

Library services listed include computers and Wi-Fi, printing, media lab, passports, interlibrary loans, homebound service, study/meeting rooms, test proctoring and literacy services. The eBranch offers downloadable books, audio, magazines and databases all day, every day. The Library also runs children's programs and storytimes, plus a teen section.

Source: https://www.newportbeachca.gov/government/departments/library-services

## Recreation & Senior Services — Parks, Classes and Registration

The Recreation & Senior Services Department has three divisions: Administration, Recreation Services and Senior Services. Together they oversee the City's 66 parks, beaches, marine habitats and 10 recreation facilities. Programs serve people from infants to older adults.

Class, camp and afterschool program registration is online at https://apm.activecommunities.com/cnbreg/Home. A registration form is also available on the page. The Fall 2026 Newport Navigator, a digital guide to afterschool programs, recreation classes, camps and special events, is available, and Fall class and camp registration is open.

Source: https://www.newportbeachca.gov/government/departments/recreation-senior-services

## Animal Shelter — Location, Appointments and Lost Pets

The Newport Beach Animal Shelter is at 20282 Riverside Drive. Services such as owner redemption of animals and viewing animals for adoption are by appointment only. Call 949-718-3454 to make an appointment.

To report a lost pet, call the shelter at 949-718-3454. Animals typically become available for adoption after 5-7 days in custody. Donated items may be left outside the shelter's gate for collection.

Source: https://www.newportbeachca.gov/how-do-i-/find/animal-shelter

## Animal Shelter — Adoption Fees and Redemption Fees

Adoption fees:
- Adult Dogs: $150
- Puppies: $225
- Adult Cats: $90
- Kittens $110

Fees to redeem impounded animals or surrender a pet:
- Impound fee: $70
- Daily board & care: $25 per day
- State-mandated unaltered fine for dog or cat (first impound): $35
- State-mandated unaltered fine for dog or cat (second impound): $50
- State-mandated unaltered fine for dog or cat (third or subsequent impound): $100
- Owner surrender: $100 altered / $200 unaltered
- Veterinary medical expenses: Actual cost

Adoption starts with a completed Animal Adoption Application (cat or dog form). No application is approved until the animal has been held for the entire stray holding period.

Source: https://www.newportbeachca.gov/how-do-i-/find/animal-shelter

## Animal Shelter — Donations and Volunteering

Donated supplies can be dropped off at the Animal Shelter from 8:00 am. to 4:00 p.m. Monetary donations go through the Friends of the Newport Beach Animal Shelter (FONBAS) at http://fonbas.org/make-a-donation.html.

Volunteer applicants must be over the age of 18, have valid health insurance and maintain an active tetanus vaccination. They are fingerprinted by the Police Department as part of the application process.

Source: https://www.newportbeachca.gov/how-do-i-/find/animal-shelter
