# Transportation & Parking

## Parking Permits — How to Purchase and Display

Parking permits can be bought online (recommended for fastest service) or in person at Newport Beach Civic Center, 100 Civic Center Dr., Bay 1A: Monday–Thursday 7:30 a.m.–5:30 p.m., Friday 7:30 a.m.–4:30 p.m. Permits bought online may be mailed or picked up at the Civic Center. Permits are vehicle-based and non-transferable.

Permits must be permanently affixed to the lower right (passenger side) inside windshield and be clearly visible at all times; improper display may result in citation. Senior Discount (65+): one permit at 50% off. Military Discount: one permit at 25% off. Required documentation: valid California Driver's License, vehicle registration, license plate number (military discount requires proof of service). Replacement permits cost a $12 fee with proof of original purchase, proof of vehicle sale/transfer or windshield replacement, and return of the original permit (if available).

Permits are not valid for vehicles over 22 feet in length or over 84 inches in width or height, or vehicles without a windshield. Some spaces have additional size restrictions (e.g., 18-foot limits). Permits do not guarantee parking availability. Two or more violations within a 12-month period may result in permit revocation. Entry is prohibited when lots are posted as "FULL."

## Master Parking Permit

The Master Parking Permit allows a vehicle to park in any City-operated metered space or municipal parking lot without paying meter or parking fees, subject to availability. 2026 fees (prorated based on purchase date): purchased January 1 – September 30: $645; purchased October 1 – December 31: $161. Permits are valid from January 1 to December 31.

Source: https://newportbeachca.gov/government/departments/finance/revenue-division/parking-permits

## Annual Parking Permit (Blue Pole)

The Annual Parking Permit allows a vehicle to park in designated City parking spaces marked with a painted "A" symbol without paying a parking fee, subject to availability. It is valid only in marked "A" spaces and select municipal lots, and is not valid in spaces without an "A" designation or in lots posted "No Annual Permit." 2026 fees (prorated): purchased January 1 – September 30: $223; purchased October 1 – December 31: $56.

## Balboa Municipal Lot Overnight Permit

The Balboa Municipal Lot Overnight Parking Permit allows a vehicle 20 feet or less in length to park in the Balboa Municipal Parking Lot day and/or overnight without paying a parking fee, subject to availability. Overnight parking is defined as 3:00 a.m. to 6:00 a.m. Permits are issued to a specific vehicle, are non-transferable, and are valid through December 31 of the purchase year. The page lists 2025 fees (prorated): purchased January 1 – September 30: $309; purchased October 1 – December 31: $77.

## Balboa Village Patron/Resident Parking Permit

This permit allows a qualified vehicle to park in designated public parking spaces within the Balboa Village Area for up to two hours without payment, subject to availability and posted time limits. It is valid only during the off-peak season (October 1 – April 30) and is not offered during peak season (May 1 – September 30). 2026 fee (non-prorated): October 1 – April 30: $18.

Available to Newport Beach residents (includes Corona del Mar and Newport Coast). Requires proof of residency, valid California Driver's License, and vehicle registration. The permit is stickerless and assigned to the vehicle's license plate. The vehicle must be moved after 2 hours or applicable fees must be paid. Valid for one vehicle; may be transferred once per season.

## Residential Zone 1 (Newport Island) Parking Permit

Parking on public streets in Newport Island (Residential Zone 1) is restricted May 15 – September 15 each year; only vehicles displaying a valid permit may park during this period. Permanent Permit (bumper sticker, non-transferable): $24. Guest Permit (hang tag, transferable): $24. Replacement fee for lost or stolen permits: $24 each. Residents may purchase up to three permits per residence, in any combination, with proof of residence.

Guest permit: hang tag on inside rearview mirror. Permanent permit: bumper sticker on left (driver's side) of rear bumper; license plate, make, color and year are required. Permit holders must still comply with the 72-hour parking ordinance. Permits can be bought at nb.getapermit.net or at Newport Beach Civic Center, 100 Civic Center Dr.

## Residential Zone 2 (Newport Heights) and Zone 3 (Eastbluff) Parking Permits

Zone 2 – Newport Heights: the permit allows parking in excess of posted time limits if space is available. Signed streets are normally restricted to 2 hours on school days (September–June) and to 1 hour on Margaret Drive (8:00 a.m.–6:00 p.m. daily, except federal holidays) unless the vehicle displays this permit. Permanent Permit (bumper sticker, non-transferable): $24; Guest Permit (hang tag, transferable): $24; Replacement Fee (lost/stolen): $24.

Zone 3 – Eastbluff: the permit allows parking beyond the posted 1-hour limit on signed streets on school days (September–June). Permanent Permit: $24; Guest Permit: $24; Replacement Fee: $24. A maximum of three permits per residence may be purchased in any combination of permanent or guest permits. Residents are not required to buy a permit. Proof of residence is required for both zones.

Most areas require payment 8:00 a.m. – 6:00 p.m., daily (including weekends and holidays). Some locations, such as the Balboa Pier Lot, require payment 24 hours a day. ## Parking Citations — Pay or Contest

Payment or appeal must be completed within 21 days of issuance. Late payments are subject to additional fees and may forfeit appeal rights. A citation may be contested by requesting an Administrative Review within 21 days of the citation issue date; for the quickest response, use the online appeal system. For citation or parking questions, contact the Revenue Division at revenuehelp@newportbeachca.gov or 949-644-3141.

The Balboa Peninsula Trolley is operated by the City as an alternative to cruising for a parking space on the Balboa Peninsula. OCTA grant funding allows the City to operate the service for free to its users. ## OASIS Senior Transportation Service

OASIS Senior Center provides affordable, shared transportation for Newport Beach residents age 60 and older who are no longer driving or are temporarily unable to drive and live in a private residence (not in congregate living communities). Rides are available for medical appointments, shopping, essential errands, and OASIS Senior Center classes and events. Hours: Monday–Thursday 7:45 a.m.–4:30 p.m., Friday 7:45 a.m.–4:00 p.m.

First-time users must complete pre-registration; to register, call 949-644-3244. Reservations must be made at least 3 business days in advance, and all rides are subject to availability. Cost: $3 each way to locations within Newport Beach; $1.50 each way to the OASIS Senior Center; wheelchair attendants ride free. Riders must purchase a Ride Card ($12, $30, or $60), available at the OASIS Administration office or from a driver.

Additional services listed: OCTA Access Program (shared ride service covering all of Orange County), 714-560-5956; Age Well Senior Transportation (non-emergency medical transportation in the South Orange County area), 949-855-8033.

Source: https://www.newportbeachca.gov/government/departments/recreation-senior-services/senior-services-oasis-senior-center/transportation-service

## E-Bike Safety Rules

Effective January 2026, Newport Beach Municipal Code 12.56.060 requires all riders of e-bikes and other motorized wheeled conveyances to operate safely on public streets, sidewalks, and in public parks. Riders must travel at reasonable speeds, obey traffic signs and signals, yield to pedestrians and vehicles when required, use bicycle lanes when posted, and wear a properly fitted helmet if under 18. The Police Department provides enforcement alongside education, targeting children in particular, and posts bike safety presentations in its Announcements section.

Source: https://www.newportbeachca.gov/trending/community-issues/e-bike-safety

## Bicycle Safety — Rules of the Road

Under California law, drivers are required to provide at least three feet of space when passing bike riders. Bicyclists need to use lights from dusk to dawn, with a front white light and rear red flashing light or reflectors, and must wear a helmet if under 18. Bicyclists must travel in the same direction of traffic.

The City's "Same Road, Same Rules" page offers bike education resources including Bicycle Rider's Safety Contract, Bicycle Safety: A Parent's Guide, Sharing the Road: Same Roads, Same Rules, Same Rights, High-Risk Bicycling Situations for Children, the Newport Beach Bicycle Trails Map, and Safety Guidelines for Bicyclist & Motorists.

## Street and Sidewalk Maintenance — Reporting

Maintenance crews inspect, schedule and repair City streets, alleys and bike trails, as well as deteriorated or damaged sidewalks, curbs and gutters within the City's right-of-way. The program is intended to prevent trip and fall incidents on sidewalks and ensure proper water flow in the gutters to the storm drain system. Contact: 949-644-3055.

Street & Sidewalk Issues and Traffic Signs & Signal Issues can be reported through the City's Report page. The City's mobile app, myNB, lets users snap a photo and send a report when maintenance is needed.

Source: https://www.newportbeachca.gov/how-do-i-/report/street-sidewalk-issues

## Encroachment Permits — Work in the Public Right-of-Way

Encroachment Permits are required for any construction in the public right-of-way, including City easements, City owned property, parks, streets, alleys or parkways. They are issued and inspected by the Public Works Department. A licensed state contractor must be retained to perform all work in the public right-of-way. To verify a property line before construction, call the Public Works Department at 949-644-3311.
