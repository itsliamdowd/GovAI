## City Government Structure — Council-Manager Form

The City of Newport Beach was incorporated September 1, 1906 and the current City Charter was adopted in 1954. The City operates under a Council-Manager form of government. Its seven City Council Members are elected by district, but are voted on by the population as a whole. They serve four-year terms.

The Council consists of the Mayor and six other members. It is responsible for policy-making, passing local ordinances, adopting the budget, appointing committees and hiring the City Manager, City Attorney and City Clerk. Every even-numbered year, either three or four members residing in separate council districts are elected by the entire community.

## Mayor and Mayor Pro Tem

Annually, the Council chooses one of its members as Mayor and one as Mayor Pro Tem. The Mayor is the presiding officer of the City Council.

## City Council Meetings — Location, Time and Schedule

Regular City Council meetings are held twice a month in the City Council Chambers, 100 Civic Center Drive, Newport Beach, CA 92660. Meetings typically begin at 4:00 p.m., unless otherwise adjusted to accommodate the Council's business needs. During August and December, the Council holds at least one regular meeting in each month. If a scheduled meeting falls on a holiday, it will convene on the next business day.

Council Meeting Dates for 2026: January 13 and 27; February 10 and 24; March 10 and 24; April 14 and 28; May 12 and 26; June 9 and 23; July 14 and 28; August 25; September 8 and 22; October 13 and 27; November 10 and 17; December 8.

Study Sessions are generally held on the same day as regular meetings and typically begin at 4:00 p.m. These sessions provide an opportunity for staff to present projects, initiatives, and policy matters for Council discussion. Study Sessions are informational in nature—no formal action or voting takes place.

## Submitting Written Comments to the City Council

Questions and comments for City Council consideration may be submitted in writing to the City Clerk. To allow adequate time for review, written comments should be received by Monday at 4:00 p.m. prior to the Tuesday City Council meeting for inclusion in the agenda packet.

Materials received after this deadline, but before 2:00 p.m. on the day of the meeting, will be distributed to the City Council in hard copy and made available to the public at the meeting.

## Public Records Requests

You can make a request for a public record by submitting a Public Request Form to the City Clerk Department in person or by mail. Requests are generally processed in 10 days. The City will determine whether it has disclosable records within 10 days after receiving your request. In some instances, depending on the complexity of the request, the City may take a 14-day extension in which to conduct a search to determine whether responsive records exist.

Many public records are available online. If you are requesting election information, ordinances, resolutions, agendas, meeting minutes, or contracts, try searching in Document Center, which provides immediate access to search and view City documents. Certain records may not be disclosed because they are exempt from disclosure by law (California Public Records Act, Government Code section 7920.000 et seq.). For help, contact the City Clerk's Office at 949-644-3005.

## City Manager's Office

The City Manager's Office carries out all City policies by supervising and coordinating City departments. It also prepares and presents the annual budget to the City Council, coordinates the preparation of City Council agendas, and addresses the strategic and policy needs of the Council, City and community. The City Manager is hired directly by the City Council. All department heads report to the City Manager except the City Clerk and the City Attorney, both of whom report directly to the City Council.

Seimone Jurjis was appointed as Newport Beach City Manager in December 2025. He can be reached by telephone at 949-644-3001.
