## Permit Center — What It Does and Where It Is

The Permit Center is a one-stop shop for processing all construction permits in one location, Bay C at 100 Civic Center Dr. The team aims to handle quick permits at the counter, one-hour reviews at a workstation, or 2-hour reviews by appointment. The team includes Building, Fire, Planning, Public Works and Health.

The Permit Center processes and issues contractor business licenses and all types of construction permits. These include building, combination, mechanical, electrical, plumbing, swimming pool & spa, solar, street closure and encroachment. Payments for all of these are accepted at the counter.

## Building Permits — Why They Are Required

Permits are how the City of Newport Beach regulates construction and reduces the hazards of unsafe construction. They are designed to ensure that all construction in the city is safe and meets construction codes. The model construction codes are adopted by reference into Title 15 of the Newport Beach Municipal Code. Federal, state and local laws, such as those covering energy conservation, also govern construction.

The page warns that a project that does not comply with the City's building codes could reduce the value of your investment. It also says property insurers may not cover work done without a permit. The page's Schedule Inspection link is on the Plan Checks, Permits & Inspections page.

Source: https://www.newportbeachca.gov/government/departments/community-development/building-division/plan-checks-permits-inspections

## Public Right-of-Way Permits — Encroachment, Street Closure, Oceanfront

Public Works issues permits for construction activity within the City's public rights-of-way. Specialty Permits are issued for dredging, harbor docks and pier transfers.
- Encroachment Permits are issued for all types of construction in the public right-of-way. They are different from, and separate from, Building Permits.
- Temporary Street and Sidewalk Closure permits are issued for placing dumpsters, cranes and/or reserving parking for construction activities, or for other temporary street closures.
- Oceanfront Encroachment Permits allow property owners along certain sections of the oceanfront to encroach onto the public right-of-way for specific projects.

Construction within City property must have a state licensed contractor and an Encroachment Permit before any work is performed. Fees are on the Fee Schedule and are charged based on your scope of work.

Source: https://www.newportbeachca.gov/government/departments/public-works/permits

## Temporary Street Use Permit — Fees and How to Apply

A Temporary Street Closure Permit allows private temporary use of public streets or sidewalks per Municipal Code 12.62. Common fees:
- Temporary Street Use Permit: $102.00 with Engineer review, or $43.00 without Engineer review.
- Temporary No Parking Sign: $0.75 each.
- Crane Refundable Deposit: $1,000.00 per location.

Fees are subject to change, and all other fees are determined by scope of work. Call the Public Works Department at 949-644-3311. All Temporary Street and Sidewalk Use Permit applications can only be submitted in person, at the City Hall Civic Center, Bay C.

Source: https://www.newportbeachca.gov/government/departments/public-works/development-services/permits/temporary-street-use-permit

## Temporary Street Use Permit — Dumpsters, Cranes and Material Storage

- **Dumpsters:** Any dumpster placed on public property requires a Temporary Street Use Permit. Dumpsters cannot be placed in alleys, red zones or within 15 feet from any street corner. Commercial solid waste haulers must be franchised by the City per Municipal Code 12.63.030.
- **Cranes:** If a construction project requires a crane on a City street, a $1,000 refundable deposit is required with your permit fee and application. Request an inspection after the work is completed so a Public Works Inspector can check the location. The deposit refund is processed if no damage occurred.
- **Material storage:** Construction materials may be stored on City property for a limited time. A permit is required for storage overnight or multiple days. Conditions include reflective delineators for night-time visibility, and heavy plastic under loose dirt or gravel with sandbags to keep materials out of the storm drain system.
- **Not allowed on public property, streets or alleys:** construction office trailers, porta potties, storage containers and unhitched trailers.

Source: https://www.newportbeachca.gov/government/departments/public-works/development-services/permits/temporary-street-use-permit

## Temporary Street Use Permit — Moving Trucks, PODS and No Parking Signs

Reserving street parking for a moving truck, or placing PODS, requires posting No Parking Signs. Moving trucks or PODS in parking-sensitive areas are permitted only for a short time. The City limits storage pods on City streets to a total of 5 days. Some tight residential areas (Balboa Island) cannot accommodate larger moving vans.

If posting temporary "No Parking" signs is a permit condition, City staff prepare them with the permitted date and time. For the signs to be enforced, the applicant must post them and notify Police Department Dispatch at least 72 hours in advance. Otherwise, towing will not be enforced.

Source: https://www.newportbeachca.gov/government/departments/public-works/development-services/permits/temporary-street-use-permit

## Special Event Permit — When Required

A Special Event Permit is required for any organized activity on public property, or on private property impacting public spaces, in Newport Beach. This includes parks, beaches, streets and facilities. The City's Special Event Permit Application page covers the application forms, required documentation, submittal deadlines and approval procedures. It is under Recreation & Senior Services and has an "Apply Now" button for the online application.

Source: https://www.newportbeachca.gov/government/departments/recreation-senior-services/special-event-permit-application
