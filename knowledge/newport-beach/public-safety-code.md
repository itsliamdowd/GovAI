# Public Safety & Code Enforcement

## Report a Problem — Public Works Contact and After-Hours Line

For immediate assistance to report a problem or concern, or with questions, contact the Public Works Department during normal business hours: Monday–Thursday 7:30 a.m. to 5:30 p.m. and Friday 7:30 a.m. to 4:30 p.m. Call 949-644-3311.

After hours, call 949-644-3717 (PD Dispatch non-emergency). Residents may also use Quest, the City's online request system, to get a problem or concern to the correct staff member.

Source: https://www.newportbeachca.gov/government/departments/public-works/resources/report-a-problem

## Fire Department — Divisions, Programs and Resident Resources

The Newport Beach Fire Department has 152 full-time employees and over 200 part-time/seasonal employees. They provide 24-hour protection and response to residents, businesses and visitors. Its divisions are Emergency Medical Services, Fire Operations, Fire Prevention and Lifeguard Operations. The department also lists a Fire-ISO Class 1 Rating.

Resident quick links on the department page include the Newport Ready Guide, the Pulse Point App, Ready, Set, Go!, and the "What's Happening in My Neighborhood" map, which shows calls for service on an interactive map. Programs listed are the Community Emergency Response Team (CERT), the Fire Medics Program and the Fire Cadet Program (formerly Explorer Post 309).

Source: https://www.newportbeachca.gov/government/departments/fire-department
