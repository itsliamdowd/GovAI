## City Council Meetings — Schedule and Location

The Anaheim City Council adopts a meeting calendar each year setting at least two regular meetings per month, subject to change. Meetings are held on Tuesdays. Open session starts at 5:00 p.m., closed session or workshops at 3:00 p.m., and noticed public hearings at 5:30 p.m. All meetings are held at Anaheim City Hall, City Council Chamber, 200 S. Anaheim Blvd. Any changes to a meeting's location or start time are noted on the agenda.

For more information, contact the Office of the City Clerk at (714) 765-5166 or visit the office at 200 S. Anaheim Blvd, 2nd Floor, Anaheim, CA.

## City Council Meetings — Speaking and Public Comment

There are three ways to address the City Council:
- **In person:** Speak at the podium in the Council Chamber during the Public Comment period. The speaker must submit a speaker card to the City Clerk. Name and contact information on the card are optional.
- **Remote via webinar:** Complete and submit a Remote Speaker Card at www.anaheim.net/councilwebinar by 5 p.m. on the date of the Council meeting. To address Closed Session, Workshop, and/or Public Hearing(s), submit the card before the start time of that item as listed on the agenda. Remote participants are called on after in-person participants. Webinar dial-in: 877-853-5247.
- **By e-mail:** Comments are sent to the City Council by e-mail. The City encourages submitting them at least 2 hours before the scheduled start time of the meeting. Otherwise they are included as late correspondence on the following day. Comments are posted on the City's website and made part of the official public record.

The time limit for public comments is three (3) minutes per speaker unless a different time limit is announced. For public hearings, the project applicant or issue initiator has twenty (20) minutes for the initial presentation and ten (10) minutes for rebuttal. Residents within the noticed area of the subject property have ten (10) minutes. All other members of the public have three (3) minutes. Public hearings regarding legislative matters: three (3) minutes. Speakers who use profane language or disrupt the meeting risk being removed (AMC 1.12.017).

## City Council Meetings — Language Interpretation and ADA Accommodation

Spanish interpreting services are provided at City Council meetings. Simultaneous Spanish interpretation is provided through headsets. Consecutive interpretation (Spanish-to-English) is also available to anyone addressing the Council by stating the request at the podium. For translation services in other languages, contact the City Clerk's office no later than 48 hours prior to the scheduled meeting.

For disability-related accommodation, contact the Office of the City Clerk in person at 200 S. Anaheim Blvd., 2nd Floor, or by telephone at (714) 765-5166. Contact the Clerk's office no later than 72 hours prior to the scheduled meeting. Requests made fewer than 72 hours before the meeting will be addressed to the greatest extent possible.

## Office of the City Clerk — Services, Contact and Hours

The Office of the City Clerk has three core functions: official records, elections and passport services. Responsibilities include maintaining the city's legislative history, City Charter and Municipal Code, and administering special and general municipal elections. The office also coordinates Council meeting activities in compliance with the Brown Act, administers the city-wide Records Management Program, and manages Council-appointed boards and commissions. The City Clerk is Theresa Bass, CMC. The City Clerk is appointed by and reports to the City Council.

Office of the City Clerk, 200 S. Anaheim Boulevard, 2nd Floor, Room 217, Anaheim, CA 92805. Phone: (714) 765-5166. Fax: (714) 765-4105. Hours: Monday - Friday, 8 a.m. - 5 p.m. Passport Services is by appointment only, Monday - Friday, 9 a.m. - 3 p.m., phone (714) 765-4540.

## Elections — November 3, 2026 General Municipal Election

The next General Municipal Election will be held on Tuesday, November 3, 2026. It will fill the Mayor (at-large) seat and three Council Member district seats (Districts 2, 3, and 6), each for a term of four (4) years. The incumbents, all eligible for re-election, are Mayor Ashleigh Aitken, District 2 Carlos A. Leon, District 3 Natalie Rubalcava, and District 6 Natalie Meeks.

The City Clerk serves as the elections official. The Clerk handles local candidate filings, nomination papers, City measures, publication of legal notices, and campaign and conflict of interest filings required by the Fair Political Practices Commission. For election questions, call the Office of the City Clerk at (714) 765-5166 (hours Monday - Friday, 8 a.m. - 5 p.m.). For County or Statewide elections, visit the Orange County Registrar of Voters (ocvote.gov) or the California Secretary of State (sos.ca.gov). The Registrar's "OC Ballot Express" lets voters track a ballot from start to finish.
