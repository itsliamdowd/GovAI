# Housing, Rent & Tenant Resources

## California Tenant Rights (Anaheim Housing Authority)

The Anaheim Housing Authority page says California tenants are protected by a wide range of housing rights. For example, you may be protected from overly high rent increases and certain types of evictions. Other laws protect you from discrimination, retaliation, and lockouts where the landlord has not gotten a court order to evict you.

To learn more, visit the CA Attorney General's Office Landlord-Tenant page (https://oag.ca.gov/consumers/general/landlord-tenant-issues). The Housing Authority page also links "Know Your Rights as a California Tenant" flyers in English, Spanish and Vietnamese.

Source: https://www.anaheim.net/668/Housing-Authority

## Anaheim Housing Authority — Programs and Contact

The Anaheim Housing Authority administers federally-funded programs and grants allocated by the Department of Housing and Urban Development (HUD) in the City of Anaheim. The Housing Choice Voucher (HCV) program has two options. The tenant-based option lets assisted households use a rental assistance voucher in open rental market units. The project-based option ties a voucher to a specific unit.

Housing Authority: 201 S Anaheim Boulevard, Suite 201, 2nd Floor, P.O. Box 3222, Anaheim, CA 92803-9987. Section 8 Rental Assistance Program: Phone 714-765-4320 (TTY - Dial 711), Fax 714-765-4331. Hearing impaired: 711 – (714-765-4320). Hours: Monday - Friday 8 a.m. - 5 p.m.

## HUD Programs Administered by the City

The Housing Authority page lists these HUD programs:
- Community Development Block Grant (CDBG): provides funding to agencies and businesses that provide a benefit to low and moderate income persons, prevent or eliminate slums and blight, or meet needs having a particular urgency.
- Emergency Solutions Grant (ESG): provides funding to nonprofit organizations and public entities sponsoring projects that benefit low-and moderate-income homeless persons, provide shelters for the homeless, and aid in the transition of this population to permanent housing.
- HOME Investments Partnerships (HOME): provides funds to local governments to implement local housing strategies designed to increase the supply of affordable housing.
- Housing Opportunity for Persons with AIDS (HOPWA): provides funding for housing and related support services of low–income persons living with HIV and AIDS.

## Project-Based Voucher (PBV) Waiting Lists — Applicants

The PBV waiting list is open to individuals who live or work in the city of Anaheim, CA. People living in unincorporated areas of Anaheim do not qualify for the AHA live/work preference. The applicants page links to a list of PBV buildings and a direct online application.

Source: https://www.anaheim.net/675/Applicants

## Waiting List — Application Updates and Status Check

Changes to a current application must be made in writing using the Update Form. Mail it or drop it off at Anaheim Housing Authority, 201 S. Anaheim Blvd. Suite 203, Anaheim, CA 92805. Allow 6-8 weeks for reported changes to be reflected on your application. You must report changes of address and/or phone numbers, changes in family composition, and changes of employment.

If your mail is returned by the post office, you will be removed from the waiting list. AHA cannot estimate how long your wait will be, as this depends on funding availability. Check your status at https://housingportal.anaheim.net/AHAStatusCheck.

Source: https://www.anaheim.net/675/Applicants

## Section 8 Participants — Reporting Changes, Moves and Inspections

Program participants must report any changes in income within ten (10) days of the change occurring. They must also report before adding or removing someone from the household. ## Family Self-Sufficiency (FSS) Program

The FSS Program is a voluntary program available to all participating Section 8 families. AHA provides participants with referrals for supportive services such as job training/education, child care, transportation assistance, and budget/debt counseling. A savings bonus is provided to those who graduate the program.

Source: https://www.anaheim.net/733/Participants
