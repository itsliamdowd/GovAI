# Public Safety & Code Enforcement

## Anaheim 311 — Non-Emergency Line

311 is the City of Anaheim non-emergency phone number that connects callers to City Hall. Community members can call 311 within city limits or dial 714-765-4311 outside of city limits to reach a customer service representative. The TDD/TTY number is 714-765-5125. Representatives are available in both English and Spanish, and translation services are available for other languages.

311 Call Center hours are Monday through Friday, 7:00 a.m. to 5:30 p.m. After hours, self-service options are available, or callers may leave a message. Residents can also use the city's online system to ask a question or report a concern. City Hall is at 200 S. Anaheim Blvd., Anaheim, CA 92805.

Source: https://www.anaheim.net/189/Anaheim-311

## Anaheim Anytime — Report Neighborhood Issues 24/7

Anaheim Anytime lets residents, businesses and visitors share problems, issues or other concerns they see in their neighborhood or around town with the city 24 hours a day, seven days a week. Use it to report graffiti, abandoned items, streetlight outages and other issues. Every Anaheim Anytime inquiry is assigned and followed up on.

Requests are monitored during business hours. If you have an emergency, call 911. If you have a non-emergency police issue, question or request, call (714) 765-1900.

Source: https://www.anaheim.net/2128/Anaheim-Anytime

## Short-Term Rental (STR) Violations — Code Enforcement Hotline

The City has established evening short-term rental enforcement from 8 p.m. to 2 a.m. If a violation is reported in the evening, the night-time officer will respond. If it is reported during the day, the day-time officer will respond and enforce accordingly.

Short-Term Rental Hotline: (714) 745-0190. Residents may also email pictures or video of violations to the STR enforcement address listed on the page.

The STR Code Enforcement program is administered by the Community Preservation Division. The division can investigate complaints and issue notices of violation. It can also issue Civil Citations according to the approved penalty schedule and conduct compliance inspections when deemed necessary.

Source: https://www.anaheim.net/3278/Report-STR-Violations

## Fire/Life Safety Section — Inspections, Permits, Weed Abatement

The Fire/Life Safety Section of the Community Risk Reduction Division provides fire safety inspections and fire-code permits. It coordinates the Knox-Box Program and a private hydrant-testing program, and it responds to resident complaints. It regulates trade shows, carnivals, fairs and outdoor assemblies. It issues permits for pyrotechnic displays, tents, canopies and temporary membrane structures, and other one-time permits required by the Fire Code.

The section also coordinates the city's Weed Abatement Program and brush clearance inspections. It reviews fuel modification plans for the Wildland Urban Interface Area of the city, which is designated as the Special Protection Area. The page also links to Wildfire Home Assessments and to the fee schedule for this section. Contact: Anaheim Fire & Rescue, 201 S. Anaheim Blvd., (714) 765-4000.

Source: https://www.anaheim.net/701/Life-Safety-Section

## Fire Investigation

The Fire Investigation Section investigates all fires involving large dollar loss, fatalities or injuries to firefighters and/or civilians. It also investigates undetermined fires referred by company officers and any fires deemed suspicious in nature. The unit determines origin and cause and provides investigative services, including criminal prosecution of arson-related crimes.

Office: Anaheim West Tower, 201 S Anaheim Boulevard, Suite 300, Anaheim, CA 92805. Phone: 714-765-4000. Fire Investigation and US&R: 714-765-4902. Emergency: 911.

Source: https://www.anaheim.net/679/Fire-Investigation
