## Solid Waste & Recycling — Services and Contacts

The city of Anaheim contracts with Republic Services to provide solid waste services. Services include Bulky Item Collection (large household items that cannot fit into containers, for residential and commercial), Container Exchange (containers that have graffiti, are damaged or worn out can be exchanged), Container Rentals (3 to 30-yard rental containers for residential cleanups and construction debris removal), and Extra Trash Service (extra weekly pickups for a nominal fee). For these services, contact Republic Services Customer Service Department at 714-238-2444.

Other numbers listed: Utility Billing 714-765-3300; Anaheim Service - Trash Disposal 714-238-3300; Trash Billing Questions 714-765-6883; Compost Bins 714-765-6883; Hazardous Waste Disposal 714-834-4000. The City of Anaheim does not provide curbside pick-up for tires; for tire disposal, contact Public Works Operations at 714-765-6860.

## Solid Waste — Self-Hauling Recyclables and Organics (SB 1383)

Self-hauling means transporting waste generated on-site instead of using the City's collection service with Republic Services. Self-haulers must source separate all recyclable materials and organic waste generated on-site and transport them to a permitted solid waste facility, operation, activity, or property that processes or recovers recyclable materials and organic waste. Full requirements are in Anaheim Municipal Code Section 10.10.040.

Self-haulers must maintain records for a minimum of five years for all recyclable materials and organic waste transported to each facility, and provide them to the City upon request. Records include delivery receipts and weight tickets from the receiving facility; the type and quantity of material transported (in tons or cubic yards); and the name and location of each facility receiving the material. If a facility does not have scales capable of weighing the material, the self-hauler is not required to record the weight but must keep a record of the facility receiving the material.

Source: https://www.anaheim.net/474/Solid-Waste-Recycling

## Bulky Item Collection

Anaheim offers free bulky item pickup provided by Republic Services. Each household is entitled to three (3) bulky item pickups per calendar year at no cost, with collections limited to twenty (20) items per pick up. Move items to the curb on the scheduled pick-up date. Republic Services does not enter homes to retrieve items.

To schedule an appointment, contact Republic Services Customer Service at (714) 238-2444 or visit www.anaheim.net/republicservices. Schedule pickups prior to your move-out date, as this service is not intended for emergency removals. Once your three annual pick-ups are used, they renew at the start of the following calendar year.

For abandoned bulky items on public property, such as alleys, streets or sidewalks, contact 3-1-1 or file an Anaheim Anytime Request, and the Public Works Department will coordinate pick-up with Republic Services. This does not affect your household's designated annual pick-ups.

Source: https://www.anaheim.net/6276/Bulky-Item-Collection

## Neighborhood Clean-Up Program

The City, in partnership with its waste services provider, offers up to five (5) large 30-yard roll-off dumpster bins, free of charge, to neighborhoods, Homeowner Associations (HOAs), and mobile home parks for Friday-Monday-hosted cleanup events. Any neighborhood associations, HOAs, or mobile home parks within Anaheim city limits are eligible. Events must be coordinated and led by official neighborhood representatives, HOA board members, or authorized mobile home park leaders. The program is not intended for individual homeowners disposing of materials from personal home renovations, remodeling, or private projects.

To schedule, fill out the online request form at least three (3) weeks in advance of your preferred cleanup date. A maximum of five (5) containers are available per event, scheduled first-come, first-served. A Neighborhood Services Coordinator will contact you to confirm details. For questions, be prepared to provide your council district, call (714) 765-4456 and ask for a Neighborhood Services Representative.

Source: https://www.anaheim.net/1289/Neighborhood-Clean-Up

## Street Sweeping — Schedule

Street sweeping takes place in neighborhoods Mondays through Thursdays. Fridays see street sweeping in alleyways and major streets, with ticketing only if signs are posted. Morning routes Monday through Thursday are from 8 a.m. to noon. Afternoon routes are from noon to 4 p.m. The sweeper can come anytime during the morning or afternoon route hours. If the sweeper comes after noon on a morning route or after 4 p.m. on an afternoon route, there is no ticketing.

For street sweeping questions, call (714) 765-6860. To request an exemption for a disabled vehicle, call (714) 765-4444.

## Street Sweeping — Citations

Citations are issued where signs are posted indicating "No Parking for Street Sweeping." Signs are posted every 250 feet on main streets and at the entrances to neighborhoods. Citations are issued under California Vehicle Code section 22507.6. Street sweeping citations must be addressed within 21 calendar days of the issue date. For citation inquiries, call (888) 255-9711. To request a street sweeping exemption for special circumstances, call (714) 765-4444.

## Anaheim 311

311 is the City of Anaheim non-emergency phone number. Call 311 within city limits, or dial 714-765-4311 outside of city limits, to reach a customer service representative. TDD/TTY: 714-765-5125. Representatives are available in English and Spanish, and translation services are available for other languages. Call Center hours are Monday through Friday, 7:00 a.m. to 5:30 p.m. After hours, self-service options are available or callers may leave a message. You can also use the online system to ask a question or report a concern.

## Anaheim Anytime — Report a Problem Online

Anaheim Anytime lets residents, businesses and visitors report problems or concerns to the city 24 hours a day, seven days a week. Use it to report graffiti, abandoned items, streetlight outages and other issues. Every inquiry is assigned and followed up on. Requests are monitored during business hours. For an emergency, call 911. For a non-emergency police issue, question or request, call (714) 765-1900.

Request categories include: Electric and Water (utility account questions, reporting water waste, streetlight problem, water outages/leaks/flooding, power outage); Graffiti; Homeless Concerns; Housing; Neighborhoods (parking enforcement, potholes or street maintenance, coyote sighting, yard or property maintenance, shopping cart, traffic signal issue, trash left in a public area, tree trimming question); Parks and Recreation (class or community center question, park question, rent a room at a community center); Planning & Building; Police; and Fire & Rescue (fireworks regulations).

Source: https://www.anaheim.net/2128/Anaheim-Anytime
