# City Services A–Z

## Bulky Item Collection

Anaheim offers free bulky item pickup through Republic Services. Each household gets three (3) bulky item pickups per calendar year at no cost, with collections limited to twenty (20) items per pick up. Move the items to the curb on the scheduled pick-up date. Republic Services does not enter homes to retrieve items. Once the three annual pick-ups are used, they renew at the start of the following calendar year.

To schedule, call Republic Services Customer Service at (714) 238-2444 or visit www.anaheim.net/republicservices. Schedule pickups prior to your move-out date, because the service is not intended for emergency removals. For abandoned bulky items on public property, such as alleys, streets or sidewalks, contact 3-1-1 or file an Anaheim Anytime Request. This does not affect your household's designated annual pick-ups.

## Solid Waste & Recycling Services

The city of Anaheim contracts with Republic Services for solid waste services. Services include bulky item collection, container exchange (containers that have graffiti, are damaged or worn out can be exchanged), container rentals (3 to 30-yard rental containers for residential cleanups and construction debris removal), and extra weekly trash pickups for a nominal fee. For these services, call the Republic Services Customer Service Department at 714-238-2444.

Other numbers listed on the page: Utility Billing 714-765-3300; Anaheim Service - Trash Disposal 714-238-3300; Trash Billing Questions 714-765-6883; Compost Bins 714-765-6883; Hazardous Waste Disposal 714-834-4000. The City of Anaheim does not provide curbside pick-up for tires. For tire disposal, call Public Works Operations at 714-765-6860.

## Self-Hauling Recyclable and Organic Materials (SB 1383)

Self-hauling means transporting waste generated on-site instead of using the City's collection service with Republic Services. Self-haulers must source separate all recyclable materials and organic waste generated on-site. They must transport those materials to a permitted solid waste facility, operation, activity, or property that processes or recovers recyclable materials and organic waste. The full requirements are in Anaheim Municipal Code Section 10.10.040.

Self-haulers must keep records for a minimum of five years and provide them to the City upon request. Records must include delivery receipts and weight tickets from the receiving facility, the type and quantity of material transported (in tons or cubic yards), and the name and location of each facility receiving the material.

## Neighborhood Clean-Up Program

The Annual Neighborhood Cleanup Program offers up to five (5) large 30-yard roll-off dumpster bins, free of charge, to neighborhoods, Homeowner Associations (HOAs), and mobile home parks for Friday-Monday-hosted cleanup events. Neighborhood associations, HOAs, or mobile home parks within Anaheim city limits are eligible. Events must be coordinated and led by official neighborhood representatives, HOA board members, or authorized leaders from mobile home parks. The program is not intended for individual homeowners disposing of materials from personal renovations, remodeling, or private projects.

To schedule, fill out the online request form at least three (3) weeks in advance of your preferred cleanup date. A maximum of five (5) containers are available per cleanup event, on a first-come, first-served basis. A Neighborhood Services Coordinator will contact you to confirm details. For questions, have your council district ready, call (714) 765-4456 and ask for a Neighborhood Services Representative.

Source: https://www.anaheim.net/1289/Neighborhood-Clean-Up

## Christmas Tree Pickup

Christmas trees are picked up starting Dec. 29, 2025, through Jan. 16, 2026, on your regular collection day. Place your tree next to — not in front of — your containers. If your tree is more than 6-feet tall, cut it in half as you're able and remove all tinsel, ornaments and tree stands. Cut the tree into 4-foot lengths. If you live in an association community, check with your management company.

After Jan. 16, 2026, you can cut your tree into small pieces to fit in your brown yard waste container. You can also schedule a bulky item collection by calling Republic Services Customer Service at (714) 238-2444.

## Street Sweeping — Schedule

Street sweeping takes place in neighborhoods Mondays through Thursdays. Fridays see street sweeping in alleyways and major streets with ticketing only if signs are posted. Morning routes Monday through Thursday are from 8 a.m. to noon. Afternoon routes are from noon to 4 p.m. The sweeper can come anytime during the morning or afternoon route hours. There is no ticketing if the sweeper comes after noon on a morning route or after 4 p.m. on an afternoon route.

For street sweeping questions, call (714) 765-6860. To request an exemption for a disabled vehicle, call (714) 765-4444.

2026 holidays with no street sweeping and no citations: New Years Day (Thursday, Jan. 1, 2026), Martin Luther King Jr. Day (Monday, Jan. 19), Presidents Day (Monday, Feb. 16), Memorial Day (Monday, May 25), Fourth of July (Friday, July 3 — observed as July 4 falls on Saturday), Labor Day (Monday, Sept. 7), Veterans Day (Wednesday, Nov. 11), Thanksgiving (Thursday, Nov. 26), Day after Thanksgiving (Friday, Nov. 27), Christmas (Friday, Dec. 25), and New Years Day (Friday, Jan. 1, 2027). Sweeping occurs with no citations on Farmworkers Day (Tuesday, March 31, 2026), Juneteenth (Friday, June 19, 2026) and Indigenous Peoples Day (Monday, Oct. 12, 2026).

## Street Sweeping — Citations

Citations are issued where signs are posted indicating "No Parking for Street Sweeping." Signs are posted every 250 feet on main streets and are also at the entrances to neighborhoods. Citations are issued under California Vehicle Code section 22507.6. Street sweeping citations must be addressed within 21 calendar days of the issue date.

For citation inquiries, call (888) 255-9711. To request a street sweeping exemption for special circumstances, call (714) 765-4444.

Source: https://www.anaheim.net/480/Street-Sweeping

## Parks Division — Parks, Regulations, Contact & Hours

The Community Services Department's Parks Division is responsible for the scheduling, maintenance and ongoing improvement of over 800 acres that make up the 68 parks in Anaheim. Facilities include sports courts, dog parks, exercise equipment, splash pads, and nature trails.
