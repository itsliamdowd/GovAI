## Building Permits — Applying and Permit History

Apply for a permit through the City's online Customer Self-Service (CSS) Portal. The same portal is used to search permit history and permit reports. Inspection records are public information. To check them, search the CSS Portal by permit number or project address, and see the project's Workflow for status.

Source: https://avcity.org/154/Building-Safety

## Building Inspections — How to Schedule

Inspections are requested online through the CSS Portal, and you must have an account on the portal. Search for the permit and click the highlighted Permit Number. Then click Available Actions to schedule the inspection. Inspections must be scheduled for each permit type (Building, Mechanical, Electrical and/or Plumbing). Inspections scheduled before 4pm will be scheduled for the next business day unless otherwise specified.

Scheduled inspections cannot be canceled online. To cancel, call the Building Department at 949-425-2540 between 7:30am and 8:30am.

Source: https://avcity.org/154/Building-Safety

## Building Inspections — Hours, Inspector Assignment and Daily Schedule

Inspections are held Monday through Friday (including closed Fridays). The AM window is 8am–noon and the PM window is noon–3:30pm. Daily inspections are posted by 8am on the CSS Portal. An inspector is assigned on the morning of the scheduled inspection day, and you can view the assignment on the CSS Portal. Inspections Team contact information is in the City directory for the Building & Safety department.

Source: https://avcity.org/154/Building-Safety

## Building Inspections — Reasons You Can't Schedule and Final-Inspection Clearances

Common reasons an inspection can't be scheduled:
- The building permit has not been issued yet.
- You need to create a CSS Portal account and search for the permit.
- The inspection category/type requested has already been approved for this project.
- A required inspection has been skipped.
- The building permit has expired.

Before a final inspection, you may need clearance from these City departments:
- Planning: 949-425-2525

You may also need final approval from these outside agencies:

## Work Without a Permit — Penalty

Failure to pay fees and obtain a permit before commencing work is a violation of the Aliso Viejo Municipal Code. A violation may result in an assessment that includes double the required permit fees. This applies to work on a building, structure, mechanical, gas, electrical or plumbing system done before the necessary permits are obtained.

Source: https://avcity.org/DocumentCenter/View/2423/Building-Permit-Fees

## Building Permit Fee Waiver — 25% for Residential Homeowners

A waiving of 25 percent of the building permit fees applies to these residential homeowner permit types, at the stated thresholds:
- Solar photovoltaic for one-half watt/square foot of the total square footage of the home.
- Small residential rooftop solar energy systems.
- Solar thermal that meets 50 percent of the domestic hot water needs.
- A tankless domestic water heater that eliminates the traditional hot water heater and is the appropriately sized natural gas tankless water heater for the entire house.
- Windows and doors with glass replacing 90 percent of total windows and glass door area within the livable area of the home. The glass must be ultralow-e with a low solar heat gain coefficient, meeting U value = 0.34 and SHGC = 0.33 or lower.

See chapter 15.68 of the Aliso Viejo Municipal Code for more information.

Source: https://avcity.org/DocumentCenter/View/2423/Building-Permit-Fees

## Accessory Dwelling Units (ADUs) and JADUs

An ADU is a residential unit that can be added to a lot with a single-family or multi-family residence. It can be a conversion of existing space or new construction, attached or detached. ADUs are independent units with their own kitchen, bathroom, living, and entry areas. A Junior ADU (JADU) is limited to no more than 500 square feet in size.

ADUs and JADUs are regulated by state law, the City's Municipal Code, and the California Building Code. Zoning requirements are in AVMC 15.14.080 Accessory Dwelling Units. The City accepts pre-approved building plans from the County of San Diego and the City of Newport Beach for plan check and permitting. Additional project-specific information will still be required.

Source: https://avcity.org/410/Accessory-Dwelling-Units-ADUs

## Business Registration — Who Must Register and How

Aliso Viejo does not have a Business License program. Businesses must obtain approval from the Community Development Department before starting operations. A Business Registration form is required for:
- New businesses operating within a commercial tenant space
- Change of business ownership
- Rebranding or change to an existing business name
- Relocation of an existing business

It is not required for established businesses located outside Aliso Viejo that do work in the City (for example, a general contractor performing permitted construction), or for home-based businesses.

Email completed forms and attachments to planning@avcity.org. A floor plan of the tenant space is required. Label the purpose of each room and include the total square footage. Tenant improvements and/or signage require building permits. All permits must be approved and finaled before business operations begin. Existing businesses that already have a Certificate of Occupancy do not need to re-register.

Source: https://avcity.org/442/Business-Registration

## Business Registration — Zoning and Use Permit Check Before Leasing

Before signing a lease, contact the Planning Division at 949-425-2525 or planning@avcity.org to verify the business is permitted at the desired location. Some businesses may require a use permit. A required use permit must be approved before business operations begin or building permit applications are submitted to Building & Safety. The Building & Safety Division issues a Certificate of Occupancy only for new construction or when there is a change in use.

Source: https://avcity.org/442/Business-Registration

## Home-Based Businesses

Registration is not required for home-based businesses. They must comply with Aliso Viejo Municipal Code Section 15.14.150 Home Occupations. If a third-party agency requires documentation from the City, complete the Notification of Home Occupation Application and email it to planning@avcity.org.

Home-based businesses that involve customers visiting the residence in person are prohibited, such as medical, dental, massage, or other service-based occupations. Home-based food preparation in a home kitchen (Cottage Food Operations) is regulated by the Orange County Health Care Agency and subject to their approval.

Source: https://avcity.org/442/Business-Registration

## Encroachment Permit — Fees and Contact

The Encroachment Permit Application is handled by the Public Works Department, 12 Journey, Suite 100, Aliso Viejo, CA 92656. Phone: 949/425-2530. Fax: 949/425-3899. Email: public-works@AVCity.org.

Single Day or Non-Construction Permit: minimum fee is $183.69. Multi-Day Permit: inspection fees are based on actual costs billed at the current rate. Initial deposits are determined by estimating 1 hour/day for the project duration, or $3,650, whichever is greater. The application asks for the address of encroachment, purpose/description, start date, duration in days, completion date, and applicant and field contact details.
