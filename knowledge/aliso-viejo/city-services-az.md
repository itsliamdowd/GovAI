## Senior Mobility Program (Transportation)

The City and the Orange County Transportation Authority (OCTA) provide a Senior Mobility Program for Aliso Viejo residents age 60 and older. Orange County Yellow Cooperative provides scheduled and on-demand curb-to-curb transportation. Destinations are:
- Sea Country Senior and Community Center in Laguna Niguel
- Florence Sylvester Senior Center in Laguna Hills
- Aliso Viejo Ranch
- Aliso Viejo City Hall
- Iglesia Park Community Center
- Aliso Viejo Library

Service is available from 9 a.m. to 4 p.m, Monday through Friday, excluding holidays. Vehicles are wheelchair accessible. There is no cost for Aliso Viejo residents. The service is only for trips to and from designated senior centers, and it is not for medical appointments, shopping, errands or social engagements. For more information, call 949-425-2589.
