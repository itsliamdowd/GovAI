## About Fountain Valley

Fountain Valley is in the heart of Orange County. It lies just north of Huntington Beach, Costa Mesa, and Newport Beach, and just south of Santa Ana and Anaheim. The city is close to freeways, John Wayne Airport, and Los Angeles International Airport.

The city was incorporated in 1957 and is known as "A Nice Place to Live." The page credits well maintained streets and parkways, 20 parks, a wide range of recreation programs for all ages, and great weather throughout the year. Businesses also consider it a "Nice Place to do Business."

## Mile Square Park and Recreation Center & Sports Park

Mile Square Regional Park is an urban park in Fountain Valley totaling 640 acres. It contains 3 regulation golf courses, 2 regulation soccer fields, baseball and softball diamonds, an archery range, a wilderness area, and 2 fishing lakes. It also has concession operated bike and paddle boat operations, a wide expanse of picnic areas, and numerous picnic shelters.

On the westside of Mile Square Park, the city owns and operates a 78-acre Recreation Center and Sports Park, newly expanded and renovated in 2008. It has a Recreation Center (indoor gym, racquetball courts, and community rooms), 15 ball fields, 3 dedicated softball fields, a walking trail, a playground, and a great lawn. The great lawn hosts community events such as the Annual Egg Hunt, Concerts in the Park, Classic Car Show, and Tree Lighting Ceremony.

## City Hall — Hours and Location

City Hall business hours are 7 a.m. - 5 p.m., Monday through Thursday. City Hall is closed every Friday beginning August 16, 2024, and closed on holidays. Check the Event Calendar for specific closure dates, or call (714) 593-4400 for more information. Emergency services remain available 24 hours per day, 7 days per week, by calling 9-1-1.

City Hall is at 10200 Slater Avenue Fountain Valley, CA 92653. The City Yard (714-593-4600) is open 6:30 a.m. to 5 p.m. Monday through Thursday. Other City facilities listed are the Police Department, Senior Center, and Recreation Center.

## City Hall — After-Hours Services

When City Hall is closed, these services work as follows:
- Street sweeping continues as normally scheduled.
- Refuse collection continues as normally scheduled. Direct inquiries to Rainbow Disposal at 714-847-3581.
- The Fountain Valley branch of the Orange County Library continues as normally scheduled. For Library hours, call 714-962-1324.
- Building inspection requests can be made online, using the email associated with your permit.
- Building permits and business licenses are only available during City Hall's normal business hours.
- Code Enforcement staff is only available for contact during City Hall's normal business hours.

## Water Payments and Water Turn On/Off

Water payments (714-593-4420) are accepted during City Hall's normal business hours. Payments may also be placed in the secure water payment depository box at the front entrance to City Hall, or made through the city's Online Portal.

Requests for water turn on/off may be made during City Hall's normal business hours at the regular rate. They may also be made on Friday, Saturday, and Sunday at the after-hour rate. Residents who request turn on/off on Friday, Saturday, or Sunday must first pay all fees in full at the Police Department. The Police Department only accepts cash in the exact amount for such payments. Call 714-593-4420 during City Hall's normal business hours, or 714-593-4485 after City Hall's normal business hours.

## City Clerk's Office

The City Clerk's Office is part of the Administration Department. It updates and maintains the Fountain Valley Municipal Code and administers the City's contract and records management programs. It receives and processes public records requests, tort claims, subpoenas and summons. As local filing officer, it receives and archives statements of economic interest and campaign statements. As local elections official, it administers elections in consolidation with the County. It also runs the City's Risk Management Program.

Contact: Rick Miller, City Clerk Administrator, City Clerk Office, 10200 Slater Avenue, Fountain Valley, CA 92708. Phone: 714-593-4445. Fax: 714-593-4494. Quick links on the page include the California Fair Political Practices Commission (FPPC), Council Member FPPC Reporting and Disclosures, and the Orange County Registrar of Voters.

## Inviting the City Council to an Event

Organizations can invite the Mayor and City Council members to an event using the City Council Invitation Form. Staff will contact the requester with the councilmembers' availability as soon as possible. Submitting the form does not guarantee that council members will attend. The request will be considered and a member of staff will contact the requester.

The form asks for the councilmember(s) invited, organization name, event name/description, and type of event (Award Ceremony, Dinner / Reception, Grand Opening / Ribbon Cutting, Workshop, Meeting, or Other). It also asks for:
- event date
- arrival time
- length of stay (30 minutes to 3 hours)
- whether there is a speaking opportunity
- whether the event is public or by invitation only
- attire
- location details
- a day-of-event contact name and phone
- whether reserved parking will be provided
- the requester's email and phone

Source: https://www.fountainvalley.gov/1509/City-Council-Invitation-Form

## Moment with the Mayor Podcast

"Moment with the Mayor" is a City of Fountain Valley podcast hosted by Mayor Jim Cunneen. It is Season 3 of "A Nice Place to Podcast." It keeps the community updated on City projects, programs, initiatives, and community partnerships. Each month features new topics and guests. It can be heard on PodBean and watched on YouTube.

Season 3 Episode 1 features Fire Chief Chris Nigg. Season 3 Episode 2 features Police Chief Craig Heredia.

## Mile with the Mayor Walks

Mayor Jim Cunneen invites residents to the Mile with the Mayor series, an informal opportunity to stay active, connect with neighbors, and talk about the community. The walks reflect the City's commitment to Fitness Valley. Residents of all ages and fitness levels are welcome to walk a mile alongside the Mayor through local parks and neighborhoods.
