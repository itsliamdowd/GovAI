## City Parks & Facilities

Fountain Valley has over 150 acres of neighborhood parks, activity buildings, and athletic facilities. The FV Sports Park has a fitness trail. Bounce houses are not permitted in any city-operated parks, but they are allowed on the county side of Mile Square Park. For bounce house questions, call the Ranger Station at 714-973-6600 and select Option 5. The city does not permit public neighborhood parks to be used by businesses for events and activities. For questions about that policy, call 714-593-4448.

## Recreational Vehicle (RV) and Trailer Parking Permits

An RV parking permit is required before parking an RV on any City street. A permit is valid for 36 hours from time of issuance, and no more than 8 permits may be obtained each month. To register, first come to the Police Department (Mon-Sat, 7am-5:30pm) to complete a registration form, and bring a valid driver's license and RV registration. After registering, call the Police Department to obtain a permit. No decal, sticker or other physical form of a permit need be displayed on the RV. You may obtain two 36 hour permits per call, with a maximum of 8 per month. An RV may not be parked in excess of 72 hours (10.44.080 FVMC). An RV with a current permit on file is not exempt from street sweeping (10.44.076 FVMC).

Under FVMC 10.44.265, no person may stop, stand or park a recreational vehicle in excess of twenty-two feet in length, a width of eighty-four inches (excluding mirrors) or ten thousand pounds in weight on any street 40 feet wide or less, curb to curb, or cul-de-sac thereof. Exceptions require a permit from the police department in advance: (1) a 36-hour loading/unloading or short-term use permit, no more than eight periods per calendar month, limited to the area in front of the residence or in reasonable proximity; (2) a visitor parking permit for 24 hours, no more than three periods per month per residence, only for persons visiting a city resident, with proof that the driver resides outside the city; (3) a permit if a person proves that a medical necessity requires street parking. Police Department: 714-593-4485.

## Residential Trash and Recycling Collection

Republic Services provides automated collection and recycling for residential customers with trash can service. Each residence gets three 95-gallon carts: blue for recycling, brown for trash, and green for green waste and food waste. Each cart holds roughly three 32-gallon cans. Smaller carts are available on request, and additional green waste and recycling containers are available at no additional charge. Additional trash containers are available for a one-time delivery fee of $75.

Residential collection is weekly. Carts should be placed along the curb by 7:00 a.m. and stored out of public view by 9:00 p.m. on the scheduled pick up day. ## Trash Service by Property Type

All homes and businesses in Fountain Valley must have trash service. Single-family homes and many condos and townhomes use individual wheeled carts emptied one time per week. Multi-family properties with two to four dwelling units may have either residential cart or commercial bin service. To request a change in service type, contact (714) 847-3581. Multi-family properties with 5 or more dwelling units and all other commercial properties must use commercial bin service, which may include a residential style cart for recycling or organics. Contact Republic Services at (714) 847-3581 to start, stop or change commercial service.

Source: https://www.fountainvalley.gov/477/Trash-Disposal-Recycling

## Missed Trash Collection

If trash, recycling, or yard waste was not emptied on your normal collection day, contact Republic Services within 24 hours of your collection day (including Saturday morning) at (714) 847-3581 to arrange a make-up collection. Missed pickups are typically collected the next business day, depending on when Republic Services was notified. Leave the cart out at the curb so it can be emptied. A missed pickup can also be reported through the "With My Resource" app.

## Bulky Item Pickup (10/4 Program)

Residential customers can schedule a bulky item pickup up to 4 times a year, with up to 10 items per pickup, at no charge. Commercial and multi-family customers can have bulk items collected for a per item fee. Schedule through Republic at (714) 847-3581.

Qualifying items: area rugs (rolled and secured, not over 6 feet long); bagged trash in excess of cart capacity (not over 40 pounds per bag, sealed with string, tape, plastic or wire tie); furniture, mattresses, box springs, bed frames, headboards, and footboards; household appliances (ovens, built-in stove tops, range hoods, refrigerators, dishwashers, water heaters, water softeners, clothes washers or dryers, trash compactors, microwave ovens); moving boxes and shipping cartons in excess of recycling cart capacity; pool equipment; securely bundled palm fronds or tree trimmings/branches in excess of green waste cart capacity (not over 4 feet long and 40 pounds per bundle).

Not accepted: remodeling items such as intact vanity cabinets, sinks, toilets, bathtubs, intact windows and doors (cast iron tubs are not accepted); universal waste and hazardous waste; construction and demolition waste.

Source: https://www.fountainvalley.gov/478/Bulky-Items

## Beverage Container Recycling (CRV)

California Redemption Value (CRV) is a deposit paid at purchase on certain beverage containers: 5 cents on containers less than 24 ounces and 10 cents on containers 24 ounces or larger. CRV centers in Fountain Valley:
