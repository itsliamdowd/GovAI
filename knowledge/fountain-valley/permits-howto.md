## Building Permits — When Required and Plan Size

Permits are required when any owner, contractor, or authorized agent intends to construct, enlarge, alter, repair, move, demolish, or change the occupancy of a building or structure. They are also required to erect, install, enlarge, alter, repair, remove, convert or replace any electrical, gas, mechanical, or plumbing system regulated by the code. The applicant must first make application to the building official and obtain the required permits.

Plan size minimum is 18 inches by 24 inches and the maximum plan size is 24 inches by 36 inches.

Source: https://www.fountainvalley.gov/397/Building-Permits

## Building Permits — Work Exempt From Permits

Exempt work: one story detached accessory buildings used as tool and storage sheds, playhouses, and similar uses, provided the floor area does not exceed 120 square feet and the ceiling height is not over 7 feet (check with the Planning Department for setback requirements); fences not over 6 feet high and block walls not over 3 feet; oil derricks; retaining walls not over 4 feet in height measured from the bottom of the footing to the top of wall, unless supporting a surcharge or impounding Class I, II or III-A liquids; water tanks supported directly upon grade if the capacity does not exceed 5,000 gallons and the ratio of height to diameter or width does not exceed 2:1; sidewalks and driveways not more than 30 inches above adjacent grade, not over any basement or story below, and not part of an accessible route; painting, papering, tiling, carpeting, cabinets, counter tops, and similar finish work; temporary motion picture, television, and theater stage sets and scenery; shade cloth structures for nursery or agricultural purposes, not including service systems; swings and other playground equipment accessory to detached 1- and 2-family dwellings; window awnings supported by an exterior wall that do not project more than 54 inches from the exterior wall and do not require additional support of Group R-3 and U occupancies; nonfixed and movable fixtures, cases, racks, counters, and partitions not over 5 feet 9 inches high.

Unless otherwise exempted, separate electrical, mechanical, and plumbing permits will be required for the above-exempted items. Exemption from the permit requirements does not grant authorization for any work to be done in violation of the code or any other laws or ordinances of this jurisdiction.

Source: https://www.fountainvalley.gov/397/Building-Permits

## Solar Photovoltaic (PV) Permit Process — 10 kW or Less

This streamlined process applies to solar PV installations of 10 kW or smaller in one- and two-family dwellings. A building permit is required. Submittal requirements are:

- A completed permit application form.
- Compliance with the eligibility checklists for expedited permitting.
- A completed Standard Electrical Plan, or an electrical plan with the required details if the standard plan is not used.
- A roof plan showing roof layout, PV panels, and fire safety items.
- Completed expedited Structural Criteria with required documentation. For non-qualifying systems, provide structural drawings and calculations stamped and signed by a California-licensed Civil or Structural Engineer.

Permit applications can be submitted to the Building Department in person at City Hall or electronically through the Plan Check Center. Applications using a standard plan may be approved "over the counter." Permits not approved over the counter should be reviewed in one to three days. Residential solar plan review and permit fee not to exceed: $500.00.

Once the system is installed, it must be inspected before final approval. Schedule on-site inspections by calling the Building Department at (714) 593-4429. Inspection requests received prior to 3:00 p.m. are typically scheduled for the next business day. If the next business day is not available, inspection should happen within a five-day window.

Source: https://www.fountainvalley.gov/988/Solar-Permit-Process

## After-the-Fact Permits for Unpermitted Work

If work that requires a permit has been completed without permits, the After-the-Fact Permit Process may be used to gain acceptance of the "as-built" work. The responsibility for compliance lies with the property owner. The City enforces code requirements in effect at the time of permit application to protect the general public welfare and to protect current and future owners and/or occupants of the property.

## Planning Division — Planning Applications and Permits

Applications listed by the Planning Division:

- **Administrative Wireless Communication Facilities Permit:** required for any new or colocated wireless facility.
- **Area Variance Application:** minor variances are an adjustment of a code requirement by up to 10% permitted by Fountain Valley Municipal Code 21.50.030.
- **Banner Permit Application:** a temporary banner permit is required to hang a banner on a building for a maximum time of 60 days. The banner must be removed for a minimum of 30 days before another temporary banner permit can be issued.
- **Development Review Application:** required for all non-residential architectural modifications (e.g., building materials, colors, doors, facades, landscaping, roof materials, windows, etc.) and requires Planning Commission approval.
- **Foreclosure Property Registration Form:** Fountain Valley Municipal Code Section 8.53 requires registration of properties in the foreclosure process.
- **Planning and Development Application:** a general application for all planning entitlements. Public noticing requirements are not typically required for tentative parcel maps or development reviews; verify with a Planner at 714-593-4425.
- **Preliminary Housing Application:** SB 330 provides eligible housing development projects enhanced streamlining and an optional vesting opportunity through the filing of a Preliminary Application.
- **Preliminary Review Filing Form:** an informal screening process to discuss major project issues before submitting a formal application.
- **Resolution Review Application:** required for the transfer of a conditional use permit.
- **Sign Permit Application:** an administrative sign permit is required for properties that do not have an approved sign program, in addition to a building permit.
- **Small Format Restaurant Permit Application.**
- **Special Event Permit Application:** also known as Temporary Use Permit.
- **Temporary Use Permit Application:** required for Parking Lot Sales and Special Events.
- **Variance Findings Form:** must be submitted along with the Planning and Development Application if submitting for a variance.

Fee documents listed: Foreclosure Property Registration Fees and Fines, and the Planning Department Fee Schedule.

Source: https://www.fountainvalley.gov/981/Applications-Fees
