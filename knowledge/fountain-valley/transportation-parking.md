## Electric Vehicle Charging Station (EVCS) Permits

Fountain Valley has adopted a streamlining ordinance (Title 18, Chapter 18.30) that facilitates approval of qualifying EVCS, in line with State requirements of AB 1236 (2015). Applicants installing a charging station at an existing building must accurately complete the application checklist to be issued all necessary permits.

Submittal requirements: a completed application form, plus a site plan and electrical plan with a single line diagram, and an electric load calculation worksheet. The site plan must be drawn to scale with full dimensions. It must show the location, size and use of all structures, the location of the electrical panel for the charging system, and the type of charging system and mounting. Applicants must also complete and submit the appropriate Eligibility Checklist for expedited review: Residential, Multi-Family Residential, or Non-Residential EVCS Expedited Review Eligibility Checklist.

Applications can be submitted online or at the Permit Center Counter. City staff review the application within one to ten days. Permit fees are based on the current adopted fee schedule. Once all permits are issued and the EVCS is installed, it must be inspected for final approval.

## Traffic Bureau

The Police Department's Traffic Bureau consists of one sergeant and two motor officers. Over 100,000 vehicles travel the City's 145 miles of streets every day. The Bureau conducts data driven enforcement, traffic enforcement and education at schools, and collaborates with school officials on safe pick-up zones and traffic flow patterns. It also runs a bicycle safety class for youthful offenders and their parents, conducts DUI and safety checkpoints and DUI saturation patrols throughout the year, and investigates and documents traffic collisions.

For traffic related questions or concerns, call the Traffic Bureau at (714) 593-4481.

## Commercial and Large Vehicle Parking on City Streets

FVMC 10.44.250 prohibits stopping, standing or parking any vehicle in excess of 84 inches width (excluding mirrors), in excess of 22 feet in length, or in excess of 10,000 pounds in weight on any city street.

Exceptions: a vehicle making a delivery or pick up of goods, moving items to or from a location, or when construction activities are occurring; public transportation and passenger buses; emergency services vehicles; and police permit parking as authorized in the FVMC.

Fountain Valley Police Department: 714-593-4485.

## Recreational Vehicle (RV) and Trailer Street Parking Permits

A Recreational Vehicle (RV) parking permit is required before parking an RV on any City street. A permit is valid for 36 hours from time of issuance, and no more than 8 permits may be obtained each month. In no event may an RV be parked in excess of 72 hours (10.44.080 FVMC). An RV with a current permit on file is not exempt from street sweeping (10.44.076 FVMC).

To obtain a permit, first come to the Police Department (Mon-Sat, 7am-5:30pm) to complete a registration form. Bring your valid driver's license and RV registration. This information is stored in the Parking Database. After registering, you can obtain a permit by calling the Police Department. No decal, sticker or other physical form of permit needs to be displayed. You may obtain two 36 hour permits per call, with a maximum of 8 per month.

FVMC 10.44.265 prohibits stopping, standing or parking any recreational vehicle in excess of twenty-two feet in length, a width of eighty-four inches (excluding mirrors) or ten thousand pounds in weight on any street in the city having a width of forty feet or less, curb to curb, or cul-de-sac thereof. The registered owner or other person in control of the vehicle is also guilty of the offense if aware the vehicle was so parked and the person parking had express or implied permission to operate it. Parking is allowed with a permit issued in advance by the police department:
- A 36-hour loading, unloading, or other appropriate short term use permit, with no more than eight such periods in a calendar month. Parking is limited to the area in front of the residence from which the loading or unloading will occur, or in reasonable proximity if the front is not available.
- A 24-hour visitor parking permit, with no more than three such periods per month per residence. It may only be issued to persons visiting a resident within the city, and the visitor must provide proof that the driver of the RV resides outside the city. Parking is limited to the area in front of the host residence, or in reasonable proximity if the front is not available.
- A permit may be granted if a person proves that a medical necessity requires street parking.

Questions: Fountain Valley Police Department, 714-593-4485.

## Street Sweeping Schedule and Parking Enforcement

Street sweeping parking tickets are enforced on all regular scheduled street sweeping days; check street signage when parking. All vehicles and trash carts must be off the street on street sweeping day, and vehicles parked on the street during regularly scheduled street sweeping will be cited. The map on the page shows when sweeping is scheduled for your street. The schedule for your neighborhood is also posted on the first street light pole at the entrance to your neighborhood.

The street sweeping contractor observes these holidays: New Year's Day, Memorial Day, Independence Day, Labor Day, Thanksgiving Day, and Christmas Day. Due to the Labor Day Holiday on Monday, September 7, 2026, sweeping is adjusted: Wednesday, September 9 moved to Thursday, September 10; Thursday, September 10 moved to Friday, September 11; Friday, September 11 moved to Tuesday, September 15.

For additional information, call the Public Works Department at 714-593-4600 or (714) 593-4433. For customer service concerns about quality or scheduling of street sweeping, call Field Services at 714-593-4600 Monday-Thursday, or 714-593-4608 on Friday.

## Obtaining a Police or Traffic Collision Report

Police report: call the Police Department at (714) 593-4485 to complete the request by phone, or come to the front counter and fill out a document request. Fees depend on the type and size of the reports requested. The Records Bureau has up to 10 days to respond, and you will be advised at the time of your request whether you are approved.

Traffic collision report: copies are provided online through a LexisNexis portal. Allow a minimum of 21 days from the date of the collision before searching, so the report can be reviewed and approved for release. For traffic collisions resulting in a death or arrest, contact the Records Bureau at 714-593-4450. For problems with the LexisNexis site, contact their Support Staff at 866-215-2771.

## Orange Coast Medical Center (OCMC) Parking Structure Project

The project is at the southwest corner of Talbert Avenue and Foster Street. It would replace an existing surface parking lot of 231 parking spaces (2.23 acres) with a freestanding parking structure totaling 784 parking spaces for hospital employees only. The structure has one subterranean level, three covered parking levels, and a rooftop parking level, for five parking levels in total. A pedestrian bridge on the third level would connect it to a freestanding elevator and staircase tower on the OCMMC campus, on the east side of Foster Street. The structure would be 36 feet high at the rooftop parapet, with an elevator tower at the southeast corner under 42 feet.

Status: on June 16, 2026, the City Council introduced for first reading an Ordinance approving Code Amendment (CA 24-01) to amend the OCMC Specific Plan to allow a standalone parking facility at this site. It was approved 4 – 1 at first reading. On July 17, 2026, the City Council adopted Ordinance No. 1637 for the project.

The Mitigated Negative Declaration had a 30-day public review period from March 3, 2026 to April 2, 2026. The Planning Commission considered the item on April 22, 2026 at 6:00 p.m., and the City Council on June 16, 2026 at 6:00 p.m. Both hearings were held at the City Council Chambers, 10200 Slater Avenue, Fountain Valley, CA 92708.
