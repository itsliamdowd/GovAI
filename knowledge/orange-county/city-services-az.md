## Household Chemical Disposal

You can dispose of motor oil, car batteries, leftover paint or unused pesticides at a Household Hazardous Waste Collection Center. There are four in Orange County. They operate Tuesday through Saturday 9 a.m. to 3 p.m., with the exception of certain holidays. For locations and other information, call (714) 834-6752.

Source: https://www.ocgov.com/about-county/emergency/handling-hazardous-wastes-materials

## Treatment of Hazardous Waste (Businesses)

Treatment means any method, technique or process which changes the physical, chemical or biological character or composition of a hazardous waste, or removes or reduces its harmful properties or characteristics. Businesses planning to treat hazardous waste on-site must notify the Certified Unified Program Agency (CUPA) by completing a notification form and obtaining authorization from the CUPA. Specialists will conduct inspections of treatment facilities. More information is on the OC Health Care Agency's CUPA website.

Source: https://www.ocgov.com/about-county/emergency/handling-hazardous-wastes-materials

## Website Accessibility — Reporting an Issue or Requesting Accommodation

If you have a disability and the format of any material on the County's web pages interferes with your ability to access the information, contact the Department ADA Coordinator. Please indicate the nature of the accessibility problem, the web address of the requested material, your preferred format, and your contact information. You may also use the ADA Title II Grievance and Complaint Form.

You may alternatively send feedback to the County ADA Title II Manager. Mailing address: CEO Risk Management, 400 W. Civic Center Drive, 5th Floor, Santa Ana, CA 92701. Voice: (714) 285-5540 (7-1-1 CA Relay Service). Fax: (714) 285-5599. Email: ada@ceo.oc.gov.

The County provides a free web enhancement tool (AudioEye Visual Toolkit), activated by clicking the accessibility icon at the bottom left corner of the web page.

Source: https://www.ocgov.com/about-county/contact-county/accessibility
