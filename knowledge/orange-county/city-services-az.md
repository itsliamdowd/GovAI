## Aliso Viejo Library

The Aliso Viejo Library (OC Public Libraries) is at 1 Journey, Aliso Viejo, CA 92656. Phone: (949) 360-1730. Email: ocpl.alisoviejo@occr.oc.gov. Hours: Mon - Thu 10:00 am - 7:00 pm; Fri - Sat 9:00 am - 5:00 pm.

Services include an AWE Station (a computer for children ages 2-8 with educational games in English and Spanish), free public Wi-Fi, public computers, hotspots for checkout by library card holders (depending on availability), and laptops and chargers for checkout and use in the library. Cloud Printing is limited to 50 pages at a cost of .15 per page. The library also has a Library of Things (musical instruments, tech, toys, kitchen items and more), outdoor patio seating with Wi-Fi, a first-come, first-served Quiet Room, and one private study room that can be reserved online. World language materials are available in Arabic, Chinese, French, German, Italian, Japanese, Korean, Persian, Russian, and Spanish.

Book clubs for adults: the Morning Book Club meets the 4th Tuesday of each month at 10:30am, and the Happily Ever After Book Club (romance) meets the 2nd Saturday of each month at 2:30pm. Check the branch calendar or email ocpl.alisoviejo@occr.oc.gov for details. The Friends of the Library Bookstore can be reached at 949-360-1730, select 4.

## Treatment of Hazardous Waste (Businesses)

Businesses planning to treat hazardous waste on-site must notify the Certified Unified Program Agency (CUPA) by completing a notification form and obtaining authorization from the CUPA. Specialists will conduct inspections of treatment facilities. Treatment means any method, technique or process which changes the physical, chemical or biological character or composition of a hazardous waste, or removes or reduces its harmful properties or characteristics.

## Ocean and Bay Water Quality and Closures

The Ocean Water Protection Program ensures that all public recreational waters meet applicable water quality standards for swimming and shellfish harvesting. It routinely monitors ocean waters for microbes, responds to sewage spills and other unauthorized discharges of waste, closes ocean and bay waters, posts warning signs, responds to illness complaints, and issues health advisories. Call the Ocean & Bay Water Closure and Posting Hotline at (714) 433-6400.

Source: https://www.ocgov.com/gov/health/eh/water/default.asp

## Well Permits

The Well Permitting Program regulates the construction and destruction of all types of wells to prevent groundwater contamination and to abate safety hazards. The Orange County Well Ordinance is enforced in the unincorporated areas of the County and in all cities except Anaheim, Buena Park, Fountain Valley, Orange, and San Clemente. If you plan to construct, reconstruct or destroy a well, including soil borings, monitoring wells, and cathodic protection wells, you must obtain a Well Permit prior to initiation of work. If the work is within one of the excepted cities, contact the appropriate city officials.

Source: https://www.ocgov.com/gov/health/eh/water/default.asp

## Cross Connection Control, Backflow Testers, and Liquid Waste Hauling

Cross-connection control inspections are conducted within water users' premises and at recycled water use areas to assure that the drinking water supply is not connected to potential sources of contamination. Backflow prevention device testers are also registered.

The Liquid Waste Hauling Vehicles Program registers vehicles and vessels used to haul non-hazardous liquid wastes from sources such as septic systems, portable toilets, boats, and grease traps. These vehicles and vessels are inspected annually and are provided with the necessary registration so that the wastes may be legally collected and/or disposed of within Orange County.

Source: https://www.ocgov.com/gov/health/eh/water/default.asp

## OC Fraud Hotline

Orange County Fraud Hotline: (714) 834-3608, available twenty-four hours a day, seven days a week. It is used to report suspected fraud, misuse of County resources by vendors, contractors, or County employees, and significant violations of County policy.
