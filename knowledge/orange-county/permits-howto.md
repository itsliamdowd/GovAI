## Pesticide Restricted Material Permits

Commercial requests for restricted material permits are evaluated by licensed County Inspectors. These requests are reviewed for compliance with environmental, health, and worker safety regulations. Operators in possession of restricted material permits are then monitored for strict compliance with State laws and regulations.

Source: https://www.ocgov.com/gov/pw/agricultural/pesticide/inspection

## Pest Control Businesses — Licensing and Registration

All pest control businesses are required to be licensed by the State and registered in each County where they perform work prior to performing pest control. The California Department of Pesticide Regulation website has more information. Commercial pesticide applicators who conduct business in Orange County submit pesticide use reports monthly to the Agricultural Commissioner.

Source: https://www.ocgov.com/gov/pw/agricultural/pesticide/inspection

## Reporting a Pesticide Illness, Injury or Complaint

Agricultural Commissioner Staff investigate illnesses, injuries, or complaints related to or suspected to be related to pesticide use. The Pesticide Regulation page has a link to report a pesticide illness, injury or complaint.

Source: https://www.ocgov.com/gov/pw/agricultural/pesticide/inspection
