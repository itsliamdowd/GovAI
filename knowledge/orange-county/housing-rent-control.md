## Pay Property Taxes Online

Property taxes can be reviewed and paid online with a credit card or electronic check. Search for your property by entering your parcel number, property address, tax lien number or watercraft/aircraft registration number. More information is on the Treasurer-Tax Collector's website.

## Property Tax Savings Programs

The County provides information on property tax savings programs that may prevent you from paying more taxes than necessary. More information is available from the Assessor.

## Orange County Housing Authority (OCHA) — Rental Assistance

OCHA administers federally funded programs that provide rental assistance to qualified tenants in privately owned rental housing. The largest program is the Housing Choice Voucher Program. Participants who receive a Housing Voucher can use the rental assistance in a variety of rental dwellings and locations with almost any property owner who is willing to participate in the program.

Source: https://www.ocgov.com/homeless-services

## Homeless Services — Outreach Referrals and Help Lines

Orange County Outreach and Engagement gives referrals to housing, physical health, behavioral health and other supportive services. The 24/7 Outreach Referral Hotline is (800) 364-2221. Email: OCOE@ceo.oc.gov.

2-1-1 Orange County: dial 211, or 888-600-4357 if outside of OC. OC Navigator helps people find health, wellness and other supportive resources.

Source: https://www.ocgov.com/homeless-services

## Shelter Programs

The County of Orange provides several shelter programs for individuals and families experiencing homelessness. They are operated through partnerships with nonprofit organizations and are located across the county to serve different regions. The Yale Navigation Center is by referral only. To make a referral, contact OC Outreach & Engagement.

Source: https://www.ocgov.com/homeless-services

## Shelter List via 2-1-1 Orange County

A list of available shelters is provided by dialing 2-1-1. The line is toll-free and open 24 hours a day. Callers are connected to trained, multilingual information and referral specialists. 2-1-1 also refers callers to organizations that provide food, transportation, prenatal care, substance abuse services, government assistance programs, workforce development, health insurance access programs, prevention and safety services, child development care, elder care, and medical, dental and vision care.

Source: https://www.ocgov.com/services/homeless-services/shelter-resources
