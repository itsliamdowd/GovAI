## Building Permits — How to Apply

Building & safety permitting and inspections are done online on OpenGov (the City's OpenGov Permit Portal). Permits are issued digitally through the portal. If assistance with the application is needed, call 714-690-3340. City staff is available to assist applicants at the Building Permit Counter inside City Hall, 7822 Walker St., La Palma, 90623.

The Building & Safety Division Fee Schedule adopted by the La Palma City Council lists all current Building & Safety fees for the current fiscal year.

Source: https://www.cityoflapalma.org/128/Permits-Inspections

## Building Plan Check

The City of La Palma contracts for Building and Safety Services with Bureau Veritas North America, Inc. Almost all improvement plans will require plan check prior to permit issuance. Three sets of plans and a plan check fee are required to initiate the plan check process.

Some improvement plans may require Fire and/or Health review and approval. In this case, additional plans must be submitted directly to the Orange County Fire Authority (OCFA) and/or the Orange County Health Care Agency (OCHCA) with their respective plan check fees.

Source: https://www.cityoflapalma.org/127/Building-Safety

## Building Inspections — Scheduling

Building inspections are available Monday–Thursday (closed on Fridays) from 8:30 a.m. to noon. Depending on the type of inspection, you may need to be present.

Generally, 24-hour notice is required to schedule an inspection on the City's OpenGov Portal. If you call before 8:30 a.m., the City may be able to accommodate a same-day inspection request.

Source: https://www.cityoflapalma.org/128/Permits-Inspections

## Building Code and Handouts

The City of La Palma has adopted the 2025 California Building Standards Codes, including the 2025 California Fire Code, all as amended locally. The local amendments are in Ordinance 2025-02 (Building Code) and Ordinance 2025-03 (Fire Code), which are codified in the Municipal Code.

The Building & Safety page offers handouts for all construction projects, residential projects, and commercial projects. Examples include the Construction Waste Management Plan (CWMP), Residential Plan Submittal Requirements, Pool Barrier Form, Owner-Builders Form, and Tenant Improvement (TI) Checklist. Building & Safety is at City Hall, 7822 Walker St., La Palma, CA 90623, phone 714-690-3340.

## Front Yard Landscaping and Artificial Turf Permits

No permit is required from the City for planting drought-tolerant plant materials and associated irrigation within the front yard of residential properties. A fee permit is required for the installation of artificial turf in front yards of residential properties. A completed application, manufacturer brochure and a small sample of the synthetic grass material are all that is necessary for the City Planner to help process the no-fee permit over the counter.

The La Palma Zoning Code states that landscaping should encourage the use of drought-tolerant plant materials, drip irrigation systems, and minimal use of turf and artificial turf, where feasible.

## Dumpster Permits

A Dumpster Permit is required before placing any large trash bin within the street or public right-of-way. Placing a dumpster in a residential drive or on private property does not require a permit. Permits are processed through the City's online Permitting and Licensing Portal. For more information, call the Public Works Department at 714-690-3352.

Source: https://www.cityoflapalma.org/190/Public-Works-Permits

## Encroachment / Excavation Permits

An Encroachment/Excavation Permit is required when any person/contractor wishes to perform any type of work in the public right of way within the City of La Palma. Proof of liability insurance must be submitted at the time of permit application. A permit is required before any work begins. Fees vary based on the scope of the project. Permits are processed through the City's online Permitting and Licensing Portal. For more information, contact the Public Works Department at 714-690-3325.

Source: https://www.cityoflapalma.org/190/Public-Works-Permits

## Sidewalk Vending Permits

A Sidewalk Vending Permit is a form of an encroachment permit needed whenever a vendor wishes to sell goods or services in the public right of way within the City of La Palma. A copy of a Health Permit and/or California Dept. of Tax and Fee Admin Seller's Permit is required at the time of application, in addition to other requirements including a City Business License. A permit is required before any vending begins. For more information, call the Business License Division at 714-690-3342.

Source: https://www.cityoflapalma.org/190/Public-Works-Permits

## Self-Hauling Permits

A Self-Haul Permit is required when requesting to transport your waste (in this case, recyclable materials and/or organic waste) off your property yourself instead of using EDCO's (the City's waste hauler) collection services. Self-hauling also includes business back-hauling, when a business transports organic waste and/or recyclable materials to a destination owned and operated by the business using their own employees and equipment.

Per State law and La Palma Municipal Code Sec. 18-7, customers that wish to self-haul their organic waste must ensure that organic waste is kept out of the landfill. Customers can either sort the materials onsite and take them to a facility that recovers recyclable and/or organic waste materials, or take the mixed materials to a high diversion processing facility, as required by applicable laws including 14 CCR, Div 7, Chapter 12, Section 18988.3. For more information, contact the Public Works Department at 714-690-3325.

Source: https://www.cityoflapalma.org/190/Public-Works-Permits

## Oversize Load Permits

An Oversize Load Permit is required for any vehicle whose loaded height, weight, or length is larger than what is normally allowed on City streets. The City must approve the proposed route of travel and the mover must show proof of liability insurance. The original permit shall be carried in the vehicle to which it refers at all times while the vehicle is being moved within the city limits. For more information and permit fees, contact the Public Works Department at 714-690-3325.

Source: https://www.cityoflapalma.org/190/Public-Works-Permits

## Business License — Requirements and Categories

A business license is required for all businesses and in-home businesses operating within City limits, including temporary activities and contractors with offices located outside of the City but conducting work in the City. An Application for Building Permit and Certificate of Compliance and Occupancy is also required for all non-residential businesses to verify Zoning Compliance and Building Compliance. A Fats, Oils and Grease (FOG) Permit is required for qualifying food service establishments. The Certificate of Compliance and Occupancy and FOG Permit (if applicable) must be completed prior to issuance of the Business License.

There are five license categories, all applied for on OpenGov: (1) Contractors, meaning general contractors holding an A or B State Contractor's License and specialty contractors holding a C2-C61, ASB, or HAZ State Contractor's License with offices outside of La Palma; (2) Commercial Industrial, for businesses with an office inside La Palma city limits; (3) Professional Services Outside of La Palma, for businesses with offices outside city limits; (4) Home Occupation; and (5) Sidewalk Vending.

Source: https://www.cityoflapalma.org/288/Business-Licenses

## Business License — Application Details and Contacts

Commercial Industrial applicants must upload a commercial or office layout, a business plan (e.g., hours of operation, type of service or product), and copies of any required licenses (e.g., Seller's permit, Fictitious Business Name statement of filing, State Licenses, FEIN, Articles of Incorporation). Restaurant or food establishment applications must obtain a health permit from the County, which requires completion of the combined Air Quality Management District (AQMD) and Orange County Fire Authority (OCFA) form. Professional Services Outside of La Palma applicants do not need a business plan or layout. A sidewalk vending business license requires a right-of-way encroachment permit; see LPMC Chapter 36, Article VI.

For questions about business licenses and the application for Building Permit and Certificate of Compliance and Occupancy, call the Business License Division at 714-690-3342. New businesses and new ownership of existing businesses are encouraged to contact the Planning Division at 714-690-3336 to discuss their land use before submitting an application. For FOG Permit questions, contact the FOG / NPDES Inspector at WaterQualityInsp@lapalmaca.gov. The office is at City Hall, 7822 Walker St., La Palma, CA 90623.

Source: https://www.cityoflapalma.org/288/Business-Licenses
