## City Council — Composition, Mayor, and Responsibilities

The City Council has five members who serve four-year staggered terms. Elections are held on the first Tuesday of November of even-numbered years. Each year the Council appoints a Mayor and a Mayor Pro Tem from its own members to serve one-year terms. The Mayor presides over City Council meetings, represents the Council at business and ceremonial events, and executes all City ordinances, resolutions and contracts. The Mayor Pro Tem performs these duties in the Mayor's absence.

As the legislative body, the Council enacts local laws (ordinances), adopts the annual City Budget and Capital Improvement Plan, and reviews and adopts proposed policies, agreements, contracts and other City business items. The Council also sits as the governing board of the La Palma Planning Commission and the Successor Agency to the La Palma Community Development Commission. It appoints the City Manager, the City Attorney, and the members of Council-appointed committees and ad hoc committees.

## City Council Meetings and Public Comment

City Council Meetings are held once a month on the first Tuesday at 6:30 p.m., unless otherwise specified or changed by the City Council. The Council may also schedule Special Meetings as needed. Meetings are held in person at the La Palma Civic Center, 7822 Walker Street, La Palma, CA 90623. Live meeting audio and recordings are available on the Agendas and Minutes page.

Public comment is limited to 5 minutes per speaker and is for items NOT listed on the Agenda. By law the Council cannot take action on such comments. The matter is referred to staff for response or action, or placed on the Agenda of a future meeting.

- In person: fill out a "Speaker Card" at the entrance to the Council Chamber and give it to the City Clerk.
- By phone (Zoom conference call): dial 213-338-8477 or 206-337-9723, Meeting ID 848 8444 8176, Passcode 302171. After the Mayor opens public comment, press *9 to request to speak. When your name is called and you are prompted, press *6 to unmute and begin speaking. Closed captioning will be turned on, and each user must enable it on their own device.

On June 2, 2026, the City Council approved a video streaming system for the Council Chambers. Because of equipment delays, the City anticipates video streaming to begin at the October 6, 2026 Council Meeting.

## Elections — Local Election Information

The City Clerk is the local elections administrator and coordinates with the County of Orange on Municipal Elections. This includes the candidate nomination process and the receipt and processing of initiatives, referendum and recall petitions. La Palma City Council elections are held every two years, in even-numbered years. Seat terms are staggered, so not all five seats are up in the same year. For questions about nominations or elections, call the City Clerk at (714) 690-3334.

The City has used district elections since 2024. In 2026, Districts 2 and 4 have open City Council seats. The candidate filing period opens in July and generally ends the first week in August. The California General Election is Tuesday, November 3, 2026. The Orange County Registrar of Voters will announce the vote-by-mail beginning date.

## City Council Districts

La Palma moved to a district-based election system for the City Council in 2024. The Council finalized the district map and election cycles at the November 1, 2022 meeting, and enacted the district map on December 13, 2022. Election years by district:
- District 1: 2024
- District 2: 2026
- District 3: 2024
- District 4: 2026
- District 5: 2024

The change followed a certified letter alleging that the at-large election system violated the California Voting Rights Act. On April 19, 2022, the Council adopted a Resolution declaring its intention to move to by-district elections. The City appropriated $155,000 for the transition. None of the districts could be drawn as majority-Latino. The City can redraw the districts after the 2030 United States Census. For questions about district elections, call the City Clerk at (714) 690-3334.
