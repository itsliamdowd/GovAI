## Trash Collection

Republic Services, Inc. provides weekly residential and commercial refuse services under contract with the City of Villa Park. To establish residential or commercial service or to report problems with service, contact Republic Services at (714) 238-3300. To report complaints that could not be resolved with Republic Services, contact the City at (714) 998-1500.

For bulky item pick up, recycling, electronic collection, or containers, the City directs residents to the Villa Park Republic Services, Inc. website (https://www.republicservices.com/locations/california/villa-park/92861). Residential and commercial rates effective through June 30, 2026 are posted on the page. Rates effective July 1, 2026 – June 30, 2027 are also posted.

## Holiday Trash Collection

If your service day falls on or after a listed holiday, your service is delayed by one day. The listed holidays are New Year's Day, Memorial Day, Independence Day, Labor Day, Thanksgiving, and Christmas. When one of these holidays falls on a weekday, collection for the remainder of the week is delayed by one day. For all other holidays, service is on schedule.

## Recycling Containers and Organic Waste (SB 1383)

Republic Services is the City's trash and recycling hauler. Each single-family residence receives three 96-gallon containers: a tan container for trash, a green container for recyclable items, and a brown container for yard waste. All containers are collected the same day each week.

Beginning January 1, 2023, residents can place organic waste, such as food scraps and yard trimmings, in the existing brown yard waste cart. Acceptable items are yard waste, food waste, and food-soiled paper & paper. Not acceptable are recyclables, plastic bags & wrap, trash & pet waste, construction waste, fats, oils & grease, and hazardous waste. Only paper bags are allowed in the brown cart. Plastic bags of any type are not acceptable, even if labeled compostable or biodegradable. Compostable cups, plates, and utensils are also not acceptable. Cooled fats, oils, or grease in a sealable container go in the trash cart.

For questions about the organic recycling program or how rates may be affected, contact Republic Services at (800) 700-8610. To report SB 1383 compliance issues, use the City's online Feedback page (https://villapark.org/About-Us/Contact-Us/Feedback). Anonymous complaints may be placed by calling City Hall at (714) 998-1500.

Source: https://villapark.org/Villa-Park/Residents/Recycling

## Pets and Equine Animals in Residential Zones

In all residential zones, keeping pets customarily incidental to a permitted principal residential use is permitted when no commercial activity is involved. Equine animals are permitted for recreational purposes only, and no equine is permitted on a building site with less than 10,000 square feet of land area. The maximum number of equines by land area is:
- 10,000 – 14,000 (14,999) sq ft: 2
- 15,000 – 24,000 (24,999) sq ft: 3
- 25,000 – 34,999 sq ft: 4
- 35,000 – 44,999 sq ft: 5
- 45,000 sq ft and over: 6

The maximum number of adult equines for any one building site is 6. Offspring are considered adults when 8 months old. Keeping wild, exotic, or non-domestic animals is prohibited on all residentially-zoned properties.

Source: https://villapark.org/Villa-Park/Residents/Animal-Services

## Senior Mobility Program

The Senior Mobility Program provides door-to-door taxi services for eligible Villa Park residents to limited destinations. Participants must be at least 60 years of age. Qualified residents must apply, and upon approval a Senior Mobility ID number is issued to authorize participation. The program operates twenty-four (24) hours, seven (7) days a week, including holidays. Residents can apply online (https://villapark.org/Villa-Park/Residents/Senior-Mobility-Program-Form) or at City Hall.

Source: https://villapark.org
