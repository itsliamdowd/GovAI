## Holiday Trash Collection

If your service day falls on, or after, a listed holiday, your service will be delayed by one day. For all other holidays, service is on schedule. When one of these holidays falls on a weekday, collection for the remainder of the week is delayed by one day. The listed holidays are New Year's Day, Memorial Day, Independence Day, Labor Day, Thanksgiving and Christmas.

## Keeping Pets, Equines and Exotic Animals in Residential Zones

In all residential zones, keeping pets customarily incidental to a permitted principal residential use is permitted when no commercial activity is involved. Equine animals may be kept for recreational purposes only, and no equine is permitted on a building site containing less than 10,000 square feet of land area.

Maximum equines by land area:
- 10,000 – 14,000 (14,999) sq ft: 2
- 15,000 – 24,000 (24,999) sq ft: 3
- 25,000 – 34,999 sq ft: 4
- 35,000 – 44,999 sq ft: 5
- 45,000 sq ft and over: 6

The maximum number of adult equines for any 1 building site is 6. Offspring are considered adults when 8 months old. Keeping wild, exotic, or non-domestic animals is prohibited on all residentially-zoned properties.

## Senior Mobility Program (Door-to-Door Taxi)

The Senior Mobility Program is funded by the Orange County Transit Authority (OCTA) and provides door-to-door taxi service to eligible Villa Park residents. The current cab operator is California Yellow Cab. Both standard sedans and vans with lift equipment are available. Participants must be 60 years of age or older, have an approved application on file, and be issued a Senior Mobility ID Number. The program operates seven (7) days a week, twenty four (24) hours a day, including holidays.

Guidelines: registered participants have a usage limit of 8 one-way trips per month (or 4 round-way trips) per registered residential address, and trips must start or end at the registered Villa Park residence. Travel is limited to no more than 6 miles per one-way trip. Rides must be scheduled 24 hours in advance, and a round trip requires two one-way trips scheduled in advance. The page says eligible trips as of July 1, 2026 are limited to Medical, Nutrition, and Senior Centers. Its guideline list gives the trip categories as Medical, Nutrition, Senior Center, Government.

Sign up with the online application on the City's page. For questions, call Villa Park City Hall at (714) 998-1500. The page also lists other options: Abrazar, OCTA Fixed Bus Routes, OC Access, and the Senior Non-Emergency Medical Transportation (SNEMT) Program.
