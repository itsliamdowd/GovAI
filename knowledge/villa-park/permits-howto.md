## Building Permit — How to Apply

The Building Department calculates and collects permit and development fees, issues permits, and inspects projects under construction for compliance with the Building, Plumbing, and Electrical Codes. It also issues Stop Work notices for any construction project being worked on without the appropriate City permits. All applications submitted on or after January 1, 2026, are subject to the updated 2025 Building Code.

Apply through the Building Department Portal at https://villapark.portal.iworq.net/portalhome/villapark. The portal has a Building Permit Application and a Solar Permit Application. Use it to fill out a Building permit or Plan Check application, find field inspection information on an existing permit, or view property profile information. If an application is incomplete, staff follow up to help complete it. All Plan Check submittals must be in "PDF" format, or you can give the Building Department a flash drive. Staff can also help you fill out an application at City Hall during normal Building Department hours.

## Building Plan Check — Engineering Review Risk and Hold Harmless Agreement

The City can proceed with the Building Plan Check while the Engineering Plan Check is still pending. That review is conducted at the applicant's own risk. Comments, revisions, or requirements from the Engineering review may require changes to the submitted plans, which could cause delays or additional costs. By electing to submit an application, the applicant accepts this risk. A Hold Harmless Agreement is available on the Building and Safety page.

## Building Inspections — Scheduling

Building Department field inspections are performed only on Monday, Wednesday and Friday, 9:00 AM - 12:00 PM. Call at least 24 hours in advance to schedule an inspection, and request an inspection on one of these days.

## Planning Department — Approvals and Applications

The Zoning Code regulates the size and height of buildings, setbacks from property lines, and specific types of uses. It also sets the procedures for development approvals such as Use Permits and Variances. Depending on the project, approval may be issued administratively or by the City Council. The City Council serves as the planning commission and reviews and approves conditional use permits, variances, and minor partitions. The City Planner reviews all site plans for residential and commercial development.

Submit applications for a Conditional Use Permit (CUP), Site Plan Review, Variance, etc. through the online portal at https://villapark.portal.iworq.net/VILLAPARK/new-permit/600/1219. All plans must be submitted online in "PDF" format. The Planning Department is at 17855 Santiago Boulevard, Villa Park, California 92861. Phone: (714) 998 – 1500. Fax: (714) 998 – 1508. The City currently contracts with Sagecrest Planning+Environmental for Planning Services.

Source: https://villapark.org/Departments/Planning

## Zoning Code — Residential Development Standards

Villa Park regulates the size and location of residential development to keep buildings in proportion to the land area. The minimum net lot area is 20,000 square feet throughout most of Villa Park. Property owners, architects and draftsmen should review the zoning code before planning a new development or home renovation, because a proposed plan may not meet the City's zoning requirements. City staff will help property owners review the development standards. The Villa Park Municipal Code for zoning inquiries is at https://library.municode.com/ca/villa_park/codes/code_of_ordinances. The Contractor's State Licensing Board provides free information on particular contractors at www.cslb.ca.gov.

## Accessory Dwelling Units (ADU/JADU) — Items Required Before Plan Check and Building Permit

For an ADU or JADU, these items must be submitted for plan check review and before building permits are issued:

1. A recorded Restrictive Covenant. The owner or applicant submits the property's legal description, and City staff prepare the covenant. The owner picks it up from City Hall, signs and notarizes it, and returns it to the City. City staff sign and notarize it and return it to the owner. The owner then records it at the Orange County Clerk-Recorder's office and returns it to the City, attention City Planning Department.
2. An address assignment for the ADU/JADU, plus signage, following the Orange County Fire Authority's recommended guidelines. Contact the City Planning Department for more information.
3. A "Will Serve" letter from Serrano Water District, 18021 Lincoln Street, Villa Park, CA 92861-6446. Phone: 714-538-0079. Email: info@serranowater.org.
4. A lot survey and grading plan for new buildings, so on-site drainage flows are properly conveyed to the public rights-of-way. The grading plan must be signed and stamped by a Civil Engineer.

The City also has a pre-approved detached ADU plan program, linked from the Building and Safety and Planning pages.

## Grading Permit

A Grading Permit is required for any grading work specified in the Orange County Grading Manual and Chapter X of the City's Municipal Code. Apply at https://portal.iworq.net/VILLAPARK/new-permit/600/9737. In general, a permit is required for:
- All new construction on vacant land.
- Reconstruction of existing developments (after demolition of building and foundations).
- Fill of 50 cubic yards or more.
- Soil removal and re-compaction (over-excavation) for proposed structures of 50 cubic yards or more.
- Stockpiles.
- Site drainage modification, including construction of an ADU and addition of impervious areas.
- Fill of 3 feet or more on a natural slope steeper than 5:1.
- Grading that impacts sensitive resources and environmentally sensitive land.
- Removal of underground storage tanks.
- Grading within privately owned open space easements or City-owned open space.
- Slope restoration/repair projects.

Most grading has planning and zoning implications, so you must get Planning Department approval for the proposed land use before grading begins. The owner/permittee must prevent polluted discharges from leaving the site and entering the City's storm drain system. Submittal requirements include the Grading Plan Preparation Guideline, Rough Grading Plan Preparation Guideline, Submittal Requirement Checklist, and Grading Notes. Engineering Hours: Monday, Wednesday and Friday from 8:00 AM - 12:00 PM. The City contracts its Engineering services through NV5.

## Business License — Who Needs One and How to Apply

Any individual, partnership, corporation or non-profit organization that wishes to conduct business within the City of Villa Park must obtain a Business License (Villa Park Municipal Code Section 8-1.3). Submit a new application online at https://villapark.portal.iworq.net/portalhome/villapark. Once the application is approved, a payment link is sent to the email address provided, and the license is issued after payment is received. To apply in person, office hours are on the Contact Us page (https://villapark.org/About-Us/Contact-Us/City-Hall).

The application must be completed in its entirety to be accepted. If the business is located in the City of Villa Park, also complete the supplemental NPDES/SB 205 form. Other forms listed are a Peddlers & Solicitors Application, an Affidavit of Gross Receipts, and Application Requirements for Home Occupations. Contact the Business License Division if you terminate your business.

Source: https://villapark.org/Villa-Park/Businesses/Business-License

## Business License — Fees, Renewal and Late Penalties

New Business License fees: each application for a new Business License must include an Administrative Fee of $49.00 + a State Fee of $4.00 + a License Tax of $50.00 + a 4% Technology Fee totaling $107.

Renewal notices are mailed in the month prior to the expiration month, with a 30-day grace period to renew. License tax for Retail Sales and Professional Businesses located within the City is based on gross receipts in arrears, so the renewal must report gross receipts for the last 12 months prior to the expiration date. Each renewal has a $49.00 Administrative Fee, which must be added to the total tax and any other applicable fees due. Renew online at https://villapark.portal.iworq.net/VILLAPARK/entities/1101. Late penalties are assessed at 10% per month. Failure to receive a renewal notice does not relieve the business owner of the responsibility for renewing.

Source: https://villapark.org/Villa-Park/Businesses/Business-License

## Film Permit — Application, Hours and Fees

Submit a completed application a minimum of one week prior to the proposed date of filming. Filming may only take place between 7:00am-8:00pm Monday-Friday and 8:00am-8:00pm on Saturday and Sunday. No filming is permitted on holidays. Commercial filming permits for filming within the City limits are $100.00 per day, and non-commercial and student filming permits are $50.00 per week. For fee information, contact Villa Park City Hall at (714) 998-1500.

Pay in cash, check or credit card. The completed form can be mailed (check must be included), hand delivered with payment to Villa Park City Hall during regular office hours, or emailed to info@villapark.org (payments can be processed over the phone). The applicant must give written notification to adjacent property owners at least 5 days before filming, only if there will be impact to on-street parking, filming will occur on public right-of-way, or excessive noise is expected.

Source: https://villapark.org/How-Do-I/Apply-For/Film-Permit

## Film Permit — Insurance Requirements

Before any filmmaking, motion picture or television production, the applicant must give the City certificates of insurance showing comprehensive general liability and property damage insurance. The minimum amounts are $1 million for death or injury to one person, $1 million for death or injury to more than one person in any one accident, and $100,000 property damage, or any additional amount the City Manager deems necessary. The permittee must also maintain a Worker's Compensation policy. The policies must name the City as an additional insured.
