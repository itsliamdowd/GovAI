## Street Sweeping

Villa Park streets are swept by Sweeping Corp of America (SCA). The service helps keep the City a pleasant place to live, keeps pollutants from entering storm drains, and complies with government regulations. To find out when your road will be cleaned, use Villa Park's Street Sweeping Map and the 2026 Calendar, both linked on the page.

To report problems with service, contact SCA at (216) 777-2750. To report complaints that could not be resolved with SCA, contact the City at (714) 998-1500.

## Senior Mobility Program — Eligibility and Service

The City's Senior Mobility Program is funded by the Orange County Transit Authority (OCTA). It provides door-to-door taxi service to eligible Villa Park residents. The current cab operator is California Yellow Cab. Both standard sedans and vans with lift equipment are available. Participants must be Villa Park residents age 60 or older. They must have an approved application on file and be issued a Senior Mobility ID Number before participating. The program operates seven (7) days a week, twenty four (24) hours a day, including holidays.

Eligible trips as of July 1, 2026 are limited to Medical, Nutrition, and Senior Centers. The program guidelines list the trip categories as Medical, Nutrition, Senior Center, Government.

Source: https://villapark.org/Villa-Park/Residents/Senior-Mobility-Program-Form

## Senior Mobility Program — Trip Limits and Scheduling

- Registered participants have a usage limit of 8 one-way trips per month (or 4 round-way trips) per registered residential address.
- Trips must start or end at the registered Villa Park residence.
- Travel is limited to no more than 6 miles per one-way trip.
- Rides must be scheduled 24 hours in advance. For a round trip, two one-way trips must be scheduled in advance.

To sign up, fill out the online application on the Senior Mobility Program page. For questions, call Villa Park City Hall at (714) 998-1500.

Source: https://villapark.org/Villa-Park/Residents/Senior-Mobility-Program-Form

## Public Works — Street System Maintenance

The Public Works Department maintains the City street system, including signs, markings, and traffic signals. It also maintains water distribution on City medians, streets, and right-of-ways, and the City's landscape, storm drains, and Civic Center.

Contact: 17855 Santiago Boulevard, Villa Park, California 92861. Phone (714) 998 – 1500. Fax (714) 998 – 1508. The City Engineer's office hours are Monday, Wednesday, Friday, 8:00am - 12:00pm.

Source: https://villapark.org/Departments/Engineering-and-Public-Works

## Right of Way and Transportation Permits

Public Works has an online portal for permit applications. Residents can apply for a Right of Way Permit and a Transportation Permit through it. All plans must be submitted online in "PDF" format.
