## Code Enforcement — What It Covers and How to Report

Code Enforcement activity is complaint-based. It covers inoperable vehicles, poor property maintenance, debris accumulation, inappropriate storage of vehicles or materials, correction of the City's Municipal Code violations, and land use requirements. Anonymous complaints may be reported by calling (714) 998-1500, or by using the Code Violation Complaint Form on the Code Enforcement page.

The form's complaint types are: Graffiti, Abandoned Vehicle, Poor Property Maintenance, Debris Accumulation, Inappropriate Storage of Vehicles or Materials, Street Light Outage, Pothole or Road Hazard, Landscaping or Maintenance Issue, Short-Term Rental, and Other. The name, full address, phone and email fields on the form are optional.

## Construction and Gardening Hours (Noise Control)

Under Villa Park Municipal Code § 6-6.7 on noise control, construction is permitted on weekdays from 7:00 am to 8:00 pm and on Saturdays from 8:00 am to 8:00 pm. Yard work is also permitted during those hours. It is additionally allowed on Sundays from 8:00am to 8:00 pm.

## Short-Term Rentals Prohibited

Short-term rentals are NOT allowed in the City of Villa Park. To report one, contact City Hall at (714) 998-1500 or complete the Code Violation Complaint Form.

Municipal Code Sec. 23-3.5 makes it unlawful to offer or rent a residential dwelling, a dwelling unit or a room in a dwelling for compensation for less than thirty (30) consecutive days. It is also unlawful to occupy one for less than thirty (30) consecutive days under such an arrangement. Maintaining an advertisement of a prohibited short-term rental is also prohibited.

Source: https://villapark.org/Departments/Code-Enforcement

## Community Emergency Response Team (CERT)

In a large-scale disaster such as a damaging earthquake, flood or fire, emergency resources may not be immediately available. Gas, water, electricity and telephone service may also be unavailable for some period of time. Individuals, neighborhoods, businesses and schools may have to subsist on their own for 72 hours or more. CERT awareness and training is meant to help people prepare their home and business and give them practical skills to help themselves, their family and their neighborhood until professional emergency personnel arrive and public utilities can be restored.

The CERT page links to a Family Survival Checklist, a Family Emergency Plan, and the Red Cross "21 Weeks to Prepare."
