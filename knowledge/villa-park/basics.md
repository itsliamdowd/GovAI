## City Council — Structure and Members

Villa Park is governed by a five-member City Council. Every even-numbered year, either two or three members are elected by the voters at large for a four-year term. Council Members can only serve two full terms. The City Council selects the Mayor and Mayor Pro Tem each year.

Current members listed on the page: Jordan Wu, Mayor (2022-2026); Robert Frackelton, Mayor Pro Tem (2024-2028); Nicol Jones, Council Member (2022-2026); Kelly McBride, Council Member (2024-2028); Crystal Miles, Council Member (2022-2026). The City Council deliberates and takes action in open session in compliance with the Ralph M. Brown Act. The City Council appoints the City Manager/City Clerk, who works with a contracted City Attorney to carry out Council policies.

## City Council Meetings — Agendas, Viewing and Accessibility

Council follows an Agenda prepared the preceding Thursday. Agenda packets are made available to the public at the City Hall counter and online on the City's website. Archived Agenda packets are also available in PDF format online. City Council meetings are televised live on Spectrum Cable Channel 3 and AT&T U-verse Channel 99 and streamed live on the City's website.

To request special assistance at a meeting, contact the City Clerk's office at least 48 hours prior to the meeting. The agenda contains a "Consent Calendar" that lets routine or non-controversial items be handled by one motion. Items are discussed separately only if a Council Member, staff or the public requests that the item be removed.

## How the Council Acts — Ordinances, Resolutions and Votes

An ordinance or amendment to an ordinance requires two readings at two separate Council meetings, with few exceptions, after which it is published. Ordinances adopted this way take effect 30 days after adoption. A resolution requires only one reading and becomes effective upon adoption. Unless a higher vote is required, the affirmative votes of at least three members of the City Council are required to enact any ordinance or resolution, or to make or approve any order for the payment of money.

## City Manager

The City Manager is appointed by the City Council and is the Chief Administrative Officer of the City. The City Manager advises the City Council on the financial condition of the City and on all legislative policy matters. The City Manager supervises all City Staff and is responsible for the operation of all City Departments and Services.

## City Clerk

The City Manager is also the City Clerk for the City of Villa Park. The City Clerk is the City's Custodian of Records and Election Official, responsible for all General Municipal and Special Elections. The Clerk records the actions and proceedings of City Council meetings, administers the City's Records Management Program, maintains the Municipal Code, and administers regulations relating to the Fair Political Practices Commission. The Clerk also provides research and information services to the public.

The City Clerk coordinates and prepares City Council agendas. A complete City Council agenda packet is available for review in the City Clerk's Office prior to City Council meetings. It is also available in digital format.

Villa Park is a General Law City governed by a five-member City Council elected at-large, each holding a four-year term. A person is ineligible to hold office as a member of the City Council if that person has served in the office for two full terms. The City consolidates its General Municipal Election with the County of Orange in conjunction with the Statewide General Election on the first Tuesday of November of even-numbered years. The City Clerk oversees municipal elections, the issuance and acceptance of nomination papers, City Measures, and the publication of all legal notices.

## Voter Registration and Eligibility

To be eligible to vote in California, an individual must be a United States citizen, a resident of California, and 18 years of age or older on Election Day. For registering to vote, verifying voter information, or finding a polling location, visit the Orange County Registrar of Voters (ocvote.org).
