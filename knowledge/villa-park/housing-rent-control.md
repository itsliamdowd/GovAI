# Housing, Rent & Tenant Resources

## Housing Element (6th Cycle, 2021-2029)

The Housing Element is one of the seven mandated elements of the City's general plan. Housing element law, enacted in 1969, requires local governments to plan for the existing and projected housing needs of all economic segments of the community. The state Department of Housing and Community Development (HCD) reviews local housing elements for compliance with State law.

The City's Planning page lists these documents for the 2021-2029 Housing Element Update: the FINAL City of Villa Park 6th Cycle Housing Element, the HCD Substantial Approval Letter (December 11, 2025), the HCD Statutory Compliance Letter (July 12, 2023), earlier drafts, the Housing Element adopted on June 28, 2022, HCD comments and responses, and an FAQ on the Housing Element Update.

## Short-Term Rentals — Prohibited

Short-term rentals are NOT allowed in the City of Villa Park. Under Sec. 23-3.5(a), it is unlawful to offer, make available for rent, or rent a residential dwelling, a dwelling unit or a room in a dwelling for compensation for less than thirty (30) consecutive days. It is also unlawful to occupy one for less than thirty (30) consecutive days under a rental agreement, lease, license or other means, for compensation or consideration.

Under Sec. 23-3.5(b), no person or entity shall maintain any advertisement of a prohibited short-term rental. The ordinance is Ord. #2018-612, § 2.

## Pre-Approved Detached ADU Plan Program

The City's Pre-Approved Detached Accessory Dwelling Unit (ADU) Plan Program follows Assembly Bill (AB) 1332 and applies only to detached ADUs. Its goal is to expedite ADU permitting and support the City's fair share regional housing goals. The pre-approved plans reflect approval only from the City Building Division and always require site-specific plans. These may need approvals from City Planning, Public Works/Engineering, Orange County Fire Authority, Serrano Water District and other agencies.

Design professionals keep the copyright to their plans. A property owner who wants to use a pre-approved plan must contract with the design professional directly. The City says posting a plan is not an endorsement of the design professional.

The City said it was in the first phase of soliciting professional designs and would post accepted plans on the webpage. For more information, contact the City Building Division or City Planning Division at (714) 998-1500.

## Pre-Approved Detached ADU — Property Owner Steps

A property owner using a pre-approved detached ADU plan takes these steps:
1. Obtain pre-approval from Planning, Building and Engineering. Bring a site plan to the Planning Division, then get preliminary or pre-submittal review from the Building Division and the Engineering Division.
2. Select an ADU from the list of pre-approved plans on the City's website.
3. Contact the design professional of your choosing to incorporate site-specific requirements.
4. Submit a Permit Application and pay the site-specific plan review fees for each City department or division. Plan review starts once fees are paid.
5. If fire sprinklers are required, they can be a deferred submittal. All deferred submittals must be submitted, approved and permitted before framing inspection.
6. The Building Division approves the plans and notifies the applicant once all departments approve.

Separate permits are required for grading, encroachments onto public right-of-way, photovoltaic systems, retaining structures, and fire sprinklers.

## ADU and JADU — Items Required for Plan Check and Building Permits

The following items must be submitted for plan check review and before building permits are issued for an ADU or Junior ADU (JADU):
1. A recorded Restrictive Covenant. The owner submits the property's legal description and City staff prepare the covenant. The owner picks it up from City Hall, signs and notarizes it, and returns it to the City. City staff sign and notarize it and return it. The owner then records it at the Orange County Clerk-Recorder's office and returns it to the City Planning Department.
2. An address assignment for the ADU/JADU and signage, following Orange County Fire Authority's recommended guidelines. Contact the City Planning Department.
3. A "Will Serve" letter from Serrano Water District, 18021 Lincoln Street, Villa Park, CA 92861-6446. Phone: 714-538-0079. Email: info@serranowater.org.
4. A lot survey and grading plan for new buildings, so on-site drainage flows to the public rights-of-way. The grading plan must be signed and stamped by a Civil Engineer.
