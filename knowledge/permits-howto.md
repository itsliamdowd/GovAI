# Permits & How-To Guides

## Building Permits — Overview & Process

Santa Monica requires building permits for most residential and commercial work. The city's system is fully electronic: applications go through Citizen Access (for trade permits and plan review) and the Electronic Plan Review system. Basic steps are: prepare docs, submit online, pay review fees, await plan review with possible corrections, receive approval, pull permit in person or virtually, schedule inspections.

Source: https://www.santamonica.gov/process-explainers/how-to-get-a-building-permit

## Building Permits — What Needs a Permit

Most residential remodels, additions, electrical upgrades, plumbing work, mechanical installations, ADUs, solar panels, EV chargers, water heaters, roofing, fences, decks, and demolition need a permit. Trade permits (electrical, plumbing, mechanical) can often go through "Express" (same-day counter review) or online. Owner-Builders may pull permits for their own single-family homes but must sign an Acknowledgment form and carry liability insurance. Contractors must hold a current Santa Monica business license, California Contractors License with the applicable classification, and proof of workers' compensation insurance.

Source: https://www.santamonica.gov/process-explainers/how-to-get-a-building-permit

## Building Permits — Pulling the Permit

After plan review approval, pull the permit in person at the Permit Services Center (City Hall, 1685 Main St, 8 a.m.–3 p.m. Mon–Thu, closed Fridays) or virtually via Microsoft Teams by scheduling through the Virtual Permit Issuance website. Bring proof of payment, valid photo ID, proof of property ownership (deed or escrow statement), business license and California Contractors License (if contractor), and Owner-Builder Acknowledgment form (if owner-builder). The city verifies docs and fees, then issues the permit the same day.

Source: https://www.santamonica.gov/process-explainers/how-to-get-a-building-permit

## Building Permits — Solar & Express Permits

Solar installations on residential roofs qualify for automated approval through SolarAPP+ (no formal plan review needed). Single-family residential owners may apply for an online Express Permit for certain work, which the city issues entirely online within a couple of days. Minor work such as re-roofs, mechanical installations, and patio covers often qualify for same-day counter review at the Permit Services Center counter.

Source: https://www.santamonica.gov/process-explainers/how-to-get-a-building-permit

## ADU (Accessory Dwelling Unit) Permits

ADUs are ministerial projects: only a building permit is required, with no design review or public hearings. Coastal Zone properties also need a Coastal Development Permit or Waiver from the California Coastal Commission. Detached ADUs must be at least 4 feet from the primary dwelling, max 850 sq ft for studio/1-bed or 1,200 sq ft for 2+ beds, with independent exterior access. No development fees are charged, though units over 750 sq ft may incur Public Works impact fees. Submit plans online through Citizen Portal or Electronic Plan Review System.

Source: https://www.santamonica.gov/preapproved-adu-program

## ADU — Preapproved Plans

The city's Preapproved ADU Program (AB 1332) offers expedited review using standard plans. Preapproved detached ADUs only. Include the plan's standard number in the application description and plan title block. All ADUs must have their own address. Property owner(s) must sign and return a Hold Harmless Agreement with the plan-check submittal. Solar panels will be required for new detached ADUs. Expedited review versus standard plan check.

Source: https://www.santamonica.gov/preapproved-adu-program

## Business License — General Process

All businesses operating in Santa Monica must register for a city business license. Steps: (1) check zoning conformance with Planning Division via 311; (2) get state Seller's Permit if retail/wholesale; (3) complete Business License Application (and supplemental forms for industrial wastewater, police permits for bars/restaurants with entertainment, or firearms/secondhand dealing); (4) submit online, email, mail, or by appointment; (5) receive payment instructions and wait for review; (6) once approved, receive license certificate. You may not open until the certificate is issued.

Source: https://www.santamonica.gov/process-explainers/starting-a-business-in-santa-monica

## Business License — Renewals & Tax Registration

Business licenses expire July 1 each year, with a 60-day grace period (last renewal day without penalty is August 31). Renewal is done annually online. The city charges an annual business license tax based on gross receipts for most categories, plus possible business improvement district assessments and property-based fees depending on location. A 10% Utility Users Tax also applies. Contact the Business License Office at (310) 458-8745 or Business.License@santamonica.gov.

Source: https://www.santamonica.gov/process-explainers/starting-a-business-in-santa-monica

## Business License — Specific Categories

Special business types (contractors, residential/commercial landlords, event planners, vehicle-for-hire, non-city-based cannabis delivery) have separate forms and additional requirements. Short-term rental (home-share) hosts need a business license and a separate home-sharing license; only hosted rentals under 31 days are permitted (whole-unit vacation rentals remain prohibited). Sidewalk vendors need both a city vending permit and a county health permit for food.

Source: https://www.santamonica.gov/process-explainers/starting-a-business-in-santa-monica

## Community Event Permit — Who Needs One

Community event permits are required for gatherings of 150+ people at eligible sites, events that obstruct traffic, or events with temporary structures. Apply through the Open Counter portal (permits.santamonica.gov/community-events) or the city's Community Event Permit portal. Check eligibility first: the portal shows estimated costs, additional permits needed, and whether your event qualifies. Holding an unpermitted event is a misdemeanor.

Source: https://www.santamonica.gov/process-explainers/how-to-obtain-a-community-event-permit

## Community Event Permit — Timeline & Categories

Category 1 events (150–500 people, no street closures) require 30 calendar days' notice. Street closures and "Open Street" events need 60 days' notice. All applications must include uploaded approvals (police security review, fire permits, site plans) no later than 10 calendar days before the event. Fire special event permits need at least 7 business days' notice. Events with 150+ people need an approved zero-waste plan; email the recycling division at least 3 weeks ahead. Food vendors may need a county temporary food facility permit.

Source: https://www.santamonica.gov/process-explainers/how-to-obtain-a-community-event-permit

## Community Event Permit — Requirements & Insurance

Common approvals include police security review and fire department site-plan approval. Cooking, generators, tents, and temporary structures need fire permits. Alcohol requires a state ABC license. Drone light shows need a complete packet 45 days in advance. All required approvals must be uploaded by the deadline. Once uploaded and fees are paid, the Community Event Permit becomes available to download digitally. Bring the permit to the event and present it upon request.

Source: https://www.santamonica.gov/process-explainers/how-to-obtain-a-community-event-permit

## Block Party Permit

Block parties have a separate, simpler process: apply online only, provide at least 14 calendar days' notice (30 days for holidays), gather neighbor petition signatures, and pay a $72.99 fee. Events on private property only (no street use) may need a Temporary Use Permit instead, filed at least 2 weeks ahead.

Source: https://www.santamonica.gov/process-explainers/how-to-get-a-block-party-permit

## Film Permit — Who Needs One & Process

Commercial, student, educational, and non-profit filming on public property require a permit, as does still photography on public property. Submit the application through Film Santa Monica's online portal at filmsantamonica.com at least 5 business days before the first day of filming. City departments (Public Works, Fire, Parking) review through the portal. The Film Santa Monica Concierge Team is your main contact. Once approved, the permit is issued through the portal.

Source: https://www.santamonica.gov/process-explainers/how-to-film-in-santa-monica

## Film Permit — Fees & Costs

Standard film application fee is $995 for a 7-day permit. Non-profit shoots: $75 simple or $150 complex (with documentation). Student productions: $50 simple or $125 complex. Additional charges include rider fees ($175 standard, $225 after hours), drone admin fee ($99), and notification fees ($250 base). Location fees vary by site (downtown and Pier are $3,854.25 full-day; small shoots 1–20 people are $145.75). Many locations waive the application fee. Police and fire officers billed separately when required. Fees are subject to change; verify on the official Film Santa Monica Processing Fees PDF.

Source: https://filmsantamonica.com/resources

## Film Permit — Additional Requirements

Encroachment on a "Street of Significance" requires a Temporary Traffic Control Plan reviewed by the Traffic Planning Division. Other streets need a Traffic Control Plan Supplement approved by Transportation Planning. Alley clearance below 11 feet requires a Roadway Closure Permit from the Mobility Division. Temporary no-parking signs must be requested at PW.Permits@santamonica.gov at least 5 business days before filming. The city may also require a filming survey or a community meeting. Contact Film Santa Monica Concierge at (310) 319-6263 or filmsantamonica.com.

Source: https://www.santamonica.gov/process-explainers/how-to-film-in-santa-monica

## Encroachment & Use of Public Property Permit

Any work or temporary use in the public right-of-way—sidewalk, street, alley, dumpsters, scaffolding, cranes, sidewalk dining, construction staging—requires an encroachment permit. Apply through the One-Stop Permits Center or directly with Public Works. Sidewalk dining requires a separate encroachment permit and a business license. Street encroachments must comply with traffic and pedestrian flow requirements.

Source: https://www.santamonica.gov/process-explainers/how-to-obtain-a-community-event-permit

## Short-Term Rental / Home-Sharing License

Only hosted home-sharing is allowed (entire-unit vacation rentals are prohibited). Hosts must live in the unit during guest stays of fewer than 31 days. A business license and home-sharing license are required. Application fee is $44 for estimated annual revenue under $100,000 and $119 above. Licenses expire June 30 each year and must be renewed annually. The city publishes a list of licensed properties and enforces the home-sharing and vacation rental ordinances. Report violations to code.enforcement@santamonica.gov or www.smgov.net/homeshare.

Source: https://www.santamonica.gov/press/2019/12/10/settlement-with-airbnb-guarantees-compliance-with-home-sharing-ordinance

## Sidewalk Vending Permit

Sidewalk vendors must have a city vending permit and a county health permit (for food). Obtain the vending permit through the city's One-Stop Permits Center. County health permits come from the Los Angeles County Department of Public Health.

Source: https://www.santamonica.gov/process-explainers/how-to-obtain-a-community-event-permit

## Temporary Use & Conditional Use Permits (TUP/CUP)

Projects needing design review, zoning variances, or special use approvals go through TUP (Temporary Use Permit) or CUP (Conditional Use Permit). Apply through the One-Stop Permits Center via santamonica.gov/topic-explainers/onestop. Timelines vary; private-property events typically need 2 weeks' notice.

Source: https://www.santamonica.gov/process-explainers/how-to-obtain-a-community-event-permit

## Dog License

Dog licenses are administered by SMPD's Animal Control unit. Proof of current rabies vaccination is required. Apply at the police non-emergency line (310) 458-8491 or through City Hall.

Source: https://www.santamonica.gov/permits-licenses

## Alarm System Permit

Residential and commercial alarm systems require a permit. Apply through the city or directly to SMPD at (310) 458-8491.

Source: https://www.santamonica.gov/permits-licenses

## Taxi, Pedicab, Newsrack, Banner, Tree Removal, & Sign Permits

Each of these permit types has its own application and requirements. Routes to the applicable department or the One-Stop Permits Center at santamonica.gov/topic-explainers/onestop.

Source: https://www.santamonica.gov/permits-licenses
