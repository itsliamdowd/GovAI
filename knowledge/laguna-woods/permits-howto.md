# Permits How-To

## Building Permits — Before You Start

Before starting any construction or demolition, contact the City of Laguna Woods to ask about building permit requirements. The permit counter is open 7:30 a.m. to 2:30 p.m. (closed 12 to 1 p.m.), Monday through Friday (closed holidays), and by appointment. Call City Hall at (949) 639-0500 to confirm hours or schedule an appointment.

State law requires the City to enforce the California Building Standards Code. Homeowners association permit approvals, plan reviews and inspections are in addition to the City's obligations under state law, not a substitute for them. Buildings, structures and certain equipment within a homeowners association must still comply with the California Building Standards Code.

## Hiring a Contractor — License Requirement

State law requires anyone who contracts to perform work on a project valued at $500 or more (combined labor and materials) to hold a current, valid license from the California Contractors State License Board. To check a contractor's license, use the Contractors State License Board's online License Check page or call (800) 321-CSLB. The City's building permitting page also links to Contractors State License Board information for seniors on hiring a contractor.

Source: https://www.cityoflagunawoods.org/building-permitting/

## Building Plan Review — Maximum Turnaround Times

Maximum turnaround times for plans taken in for review are listed below. "Initial" is the first review and "Subsequent" is each review after that.
- Residential New Construction: Initial 7 days; Subsequent 5 days.
- Residential Additions and/or Accessory Building: Initial 5 days; Subsequent 3 days.
- Residential Remodels: Initial 5 days; Subsequent 3 days.
- Commercial/Non-Residential New Construction: Initial 10 days; Subsequent 5 days.
- Commercial/Non-Residential Additions: Initial 7 days; Subsequent 5 days.
- Small Commercial/Non-Residential Remodels and/or Tenant Improvements: Initial 7 days; Subsequent 5 days.
- Large/Complex Commercial and/or Non-Residential Projects: Initial 10 days; Subsequent 5 days.

A "day" means Monday through Friday, excluding only federal holidays and holidays observed by the City. Counting generally begins on the first weekday after the City takes in the plans.

## Scheduling a Building Inspection

Building inspections are generally available Monday through Friday (closed holidays). Schedule an inspection up to seven calendar days in advance, using the online calendar on the City's "Schedule Building Inspections" page or by calling City Hall at (949) 639-0500. Inspections are offered in two windows: 8 a.m. to Noon (mornings) and Noon to 5 p.m. (afternoons).

Source: https://www.cityoflagunawoods.org/schedule-building-inspections/

## Planning Division — Development Applications and Permit Requirements

The Planning Division ensures development is consistent with the California Environmental Quality Act (CEQA), the Laguna Woods General Plan, the Laguna Woods Zoning Code, and other laws, plans, rules and regulations. It is part of the Planning & Environmental Services Department.

Property owners, businesses, developers and others seeking information on a prospective development application or permit requirement should email planning@lagunawoods.gov for the fastest response. You may also leave a voicemail at (949) 639-0561. In-person and virtual appointments are available by request.

Planning resources listed on the page: CEQA Guidelines, Forms, General Plan, GIS Maps, Impact Fees (Development Impact Fees 2026-27), Objective Design Standards, and a Zoning Overview, Zoning Map and Zoning Code. Application pages listed: Cannabis Businesses, Sidewalk Vendors and Other Permits.

## Cannabis Storefront Retailer — Permitted Use and Application

Cannabis storefront retailers with a valid Type 10 (storefront retailer) license from the California Department of Cannabis Control may operate in Laguna Woods. They must comply with City Ordinance No. 22-01 (Cannabis Business Tax), City Ordinance No. 23-01 (Cannabis Storefront Retailers Regulatory Framework), and applicable state law. Cannabis cultivation, distribution, testing laboratories and microbusinesses requiring state licensure may not operate anywhere in the city. Cannabis consumption lounges and cannabis events requiring state licensure are also prohibited.

The application documents include an Application Checklist and Submittal Instructions (updated February 16, 2024) and a Frequently Asked Questions document (updated July 20, 2024). Required forms include the PES-A Land Use Application, PES-AA Supplemental Information Form and PES-02 Cost Recovery Agreement for Deposit-based Planning Applications. Required business tax forms are the AS-CSR-01 Cannabis Business Tax Registration Form and the AS-CSR-02 Cannabis Business Tax Statement Form.

Source: https://www.cityoflagunawoods.org/cannabis/
