## Law Enforcement — Orange County Sheriff's Department

Law enforcement services are provided by the Orange County Sheriff's Department, under contract with the City. As the City's Police Department, the Sheriff's Department is responsible for patrol, 9-1-1 emergency response, investigation, and related support services (e.g., crime scene analysis, custody/jail operations, coroner functions, and missing person searches). For more information, call (949) 770-6011. In case of an emergency, call 911 immediately.

The Orange County Sheriff's Department's Southwest Station is located at 11 Journey, Aliso Viejo, CA 92656 and can be reached at (949) 770-6011. The station houses the sworn and professional staff who provide law enforcement services for Laguna Woods, as well as certain crime reporting and fingerprinting services.

## Reporting a Crime That Is Not a Life-Threatening Emergency

To report a crime that is not a life-threatening emergency or that has already occurred, call the Orange County Sheriff's Department at (949) 770-6011 and a deputy will respond to your location. If you are no longer at the scene of the crime, call from your home or another safe and comfortable location, as high call volumes may result in longer wait times.

Visiting City Hall or an Orange County Sheriff's Department facility will result in similar wait times, as deputies are not always immediately available on-site.

## When to Call 9-1-1 vs. Non-Emergency Numbers

Dial 9-1-1 when there is a medical, fire or police emergency, such as: life or lives in danger, a fire, serious injury, a serious medical condition, or a serious crime in progress. When in doubt, dial 9-1-1. Assistance is always dispatched to the most critical incidents first.

Do not dial 9-1-1 to ask for directory assistance, cellular phone technical assistance, or directions, or to report any non-emergency situation. Non-emergency numbers for the Orange County Sheriff's Department are South Orange County: (949) 770-6011 and North Orange County: (714) 647-7000.

A 9-1-1 call from a cellular phone will be connected to either the California Highway Patrol (CHP) dispatch center or the Orange County Sheriff's Department (OCSD) dispatch center.

## Public Safety Services Department

The Public Safety Services Department includes law enforcement and animal control and shelter services, for which the City contracts with the Orange County Sheriff's Department and the City of Laguna Beach, respectively. It also includes other public safety agreements and emergency management functions. It handles liaison activities with the Orange County Fire Authority (a joint powers authority of which the City is a member) and County of Orange-contracted 9-1-1 emergency ambulance services.

The department's page lists these record requests: Ambulance Performance, Crime and Incident Reports, Fire Incident Reports, and Traffic Collision Reports. Its agency partners are the City of Laguna Beach, Orange County Fire Authority, and Orange County Sheriff's Department. In the event of an emergency, call 9-1-1.

## AlertOC — Emergency Mass Notification

AlertOC is a mass notification system designed to keep Orange County residents and businesses informed of emergencies that may require immediate life saving actions. Residents may also choose to subscribe to non-emergency messages from the City of Laguna Woods and other participating cities or agencies. AlertOC is operated by the County of Orange in collaboration with local cities.

By registering, time-sensitive voice messages from the County of Orange (including the Orange County Sheriff's Department) or cities in which you live or work may be sent to your home, cellular, and/or business telephone. Text messages and/or email may also be sent to certain devices.

Laguna Woods residents and businesses are strongly encouraged to register additional telephone numbers and email addresses, and to "opt-in" to receive non-emergency messages. Information provided to private mass notification systems (e.g., Laguna Woods Village's Everbridge emergency notification system) is not automatically registered with AlertOC or vice versa. To register, visit the AlertOC website or call (949) 639-0500.

Source: https://www.cityoflagunawoods.org/alertoc/

## Fire Hazard Severity Zones

The City is required to adopt fire hazard severity zones that are at least equal to those recommended by the State Fire Marshal. The three zones are moderate, high, and very high. They are used for various purposes set forth in state law, including designating areas where the State of California's defensible space standards and wildland urban interface building codes apply. Certain disclosures are also required during real estate transactions involving the sale of real property located in high and very high fire hazard severity zones.

The City's current fire hazard severity zones reflect the State Fire Marshal's mandatory minimum recommendations dated March 24, 2025. They became effective on July 18, 2025. For building-level visualization in the GIS version of the map, toggle on the "Fire Hazard Severity Zones (2025)" layer under "Hazard Maps" and the "Buildings" layer under "Other Maps."

Source: https://www.cityoflagunawoods.org/fire-hazard-severity-zones/

## Safety Bulletin — Distraction Theft Awareness

In an April 12, 2024 bulletin, the Orange County Sheriff's Department said distraction thefts are an increasing concern. They occur when criminals use a variety of techniques to catch victims off guard, pull their focus and then steal personal items such as jewelry, wallets or other objects of value. Thieves most often work in pairs or groups in retail center stores and parking lots.

The Sheriff's Department advises residents to be alert and be assertive. If someone gets too close, loudly and clearly say "no" to draw the attention of others. If you are a victim of distraction theft, report it to the Sheriff's Department at 949-770-6011. If you are witnessing a crime in progress, always call 9-1-1.

Source: https://www.cityoflagunawoods.org/safety-bulletin-distraction-theft-awareness/
