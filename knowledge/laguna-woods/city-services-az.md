## Residential Trash & Recycling — Laguna Woods Village

Residential solid waste handling services are provided by CR&R Incorporated under a franchise agreement with the City. Contact CR&R at (949) 625-6735 or LagunaWoods-Recycles@CRRMail.com. The City's Laguna Woods Village page links to a "Laguna Woods Village Waste Collection Days Flyer" and a "What Goes Where" flyer. Both flyers are available in English, Chinese, Korean and Spanish.

No collection occurs on New Year's Day, Memorial Day, Independence Day, Labor Day, Thanksgiving or Christmas Day. When a holiday falls on a weekday, collections are delayed by one day each for the rest of the week. If a holiday falls on a Saturday or Sunday, collection days stay unchanged.

## Residential Trash & Recycling — Apartment Complexes/Senior Living Facilities

Residential solid waste handling services are provided by CR&R Incorporated under a franchise agreement with the City. Contact CR&R at (949) 625-6735 or LagunaWoods-Recycles@CRRMail.com. The holiday schedule is the same as for Laguna Woods Village. No collection occurs on New Year's Day, Memorial Day, Independence Day, Labor Day, Thanksgiving or Christmas Day. A weekday holiday delays collection by one day for the rest of that week.

Multi-family residential dwellings of five units or more must arrange for recycling services under the State's Mandatory Commercial Recycling Law (AB 341). To make arrangements, contact CR&R at (949) 625-6735 or LagunaWoods-Recycles@CRRMail.com. Multi-family dwellings must also arrange organic waste recycling for green waste, landscape and pruning waste, non-hazardous wood waste, and similar materials. This excludes food waste and food-soiled paper.

Source: https://www.cityoflagunawoods.org/reswasteservices/

## Residential Organics Recycling — Laguna Woods Village

Residents are encouraged to put organic waste in any of the green lid carts ("organic carts") located throughout Laguna Woods Village. Organic carts are collected on Wednesdays. To find your nearest cart, use the interactive or printable map on the City page. You can also contact CR&R Incorporated at (949) 625-6735 or LagunaWoods-Recycles@CRRMail.com. Due to high call volumes, the page recommends email.

Accepted items are food, food-soiled paper (pizza box bottoms only) and pet food such as birdseed, cat food and dog food. Pet waste, litter and bedding go in trash (landfill) containers, not organic carts. Organic waste may be bagged in compostable or paper bags, or wrapped in newspaper. If an organic cart is full or unavailable, waste may be placed in a nearby trash container. Lids on trash, recycling and organics containers should be kept closed when not in use.

Source: https://www.cityoflagunawoods.org/lwvorganics/

## Home-Generated Sharps Waste Disposal

Laguna Woods residents can drop off needles, lancets and other home-generated sharps waste at City Hall during regular business hours, at no charge. Residents must place sharps in a rigid, needle puncture-resistant container (e.g., laundry detergent bottles, soda bottles, or medical sharps containers) before leaving home. Loose materials cannot be accepted. For more information, call City Hall at (949) 639-0500.

State law makes it illegal to dispose of home-generated sharps waste in trash or recycling containers. The term includes needles, lancets and syringes used to deliver insulin or subcutaneous ("subQ") fluids to pets.

Source: https://www.cityoflagunawoods.org/lwvwasteservices/

## Household Document Shredding Drop-off

Laguna Woods residents can schedule appointments to drop off personal documents at City Hall for shredding, at no charge. Documents are not shredded on-site. They are stored in locked containers until a National Association for Information Destruction® AAA certified company shreds them at a separate facility.

Proof of Laguna Woods residency is required. Bring no more than the equivalent of three 13-gallon kitchen trash bags of documents per appointment. Remove binders, hanging file folders and large binder clips beforehand. Manila folders with metal prongs, paper clips, rubber bands and staples are okay. Do not bring newspapers or magazines unless they contain personal information. X-rays, CDs, cassettes, flash drives, discs and batteries are not accepted. For more information, call City Hall at (949) 639-0500.

Source: https://www.cityoflagunawoods.org/shredding/

## Dog Licensing, Pet Limits and Spay/Neuter

Every dog four months of age or older must be licensed by Laguna Beach/Laguna Woods Animal Services. For dog licensing, call (949) 497-3552.

A maximum combination of three cats and dogs, no more than two of which may be dogs, may be kept in any residence. Keeping more requires a private kennel license from Laguna Beach/Laguna Woods Animal Services, (949) 497-3552. Private property owners and homeowners' associations may have additional rules on the number or type of pets.

Every dog six months of age or older must be spayed or neutered, certified sterile, or licensed as an unaltered dog. Every cat six months of age or older must be spayed or neutered, certified sterile, or meet unaltered cat requirements. For more information, call (949) 497-3552.

Source: https://www.cityoflagunawoods.org/animal-services/

## Senior Mobility Program

The Senior Mobility Program subsidizes taxi travel for Laguna Woods residents who are at least 60 years of age. All transportation is provided by Yellow Cab. For more information, contact City Hall at (949) 639-0500.

Enrolled residents get an ID card that reduces Yellow Cab fares to these co-pays for trips starting or ending in Laguna Woods:
- $0 to or from the Laguna Hills Transportation Center, Irvine Station, or the Santa Ana Regional Transportation Center.
- $8 for trips up to 10 miles within Orange County (each way).
- $18 for trips between 10.1 and 15 miles within Orange County (each way).
- $28 for trips over 15 miles within Orange County (each way).
- $28 for trips to or from VA Long Beach.
- $40 for trips to or from John Wayne Airport.

Residents can enroll on a walk-in basis at City Hall from 8 a.m. to 5 p.m., Monday through Friday (closed holidays). Call (949) 639-0500 to confirm business hours. Enrollment is free. Bring proof of age (at least 60) and proof of Laguna Woods residency, complete the enrollment form and waiver, and have a photograph taken for your ID card. Enrollments are valid through June 30, 2027.

Source: https://www.cityoflagunawoods.org/smp/

## Public Facilities, Library & Parks

- **City Hall**: 24264 El Toro Road, Laguna Woods, CA 92637.
- **Public Library Building**: 24266 El Toro Road, Laguna Woods, CA 92637. It opened on January 27, 2024. The County of Orange leases it for the Laguna Woods branch of OC Public Libraries. It has 1,161 square feet of interior space and an adjoining 573 square foot outdoor activity room.
- **"A Place for Paws" Dog Park**: 23190 Ridge Route Drive, Laguna Woods, CA 92637. It offers off-leash dog exercise, about 6,400 square feet of dog-friendly artificial turf, a dog water station and seating. The property is owned by the Golden Rain Foundation (GRF). The City's use is provided for through June 30, 2027, or as may be extended by mutual agreement.
- **City Centre Park**: 24121 Moulton Parkway, Laguna Woods, CA 92637. It is a 0.54-acre passive recreation space with a walking trail, picnic tables and a small gathering area.
- **Woods End Wilderness Preserve**: 24995 El Toro Road, Laguna Woods, CA 92637. It has 10.6 acres of natural open space and is leased to the County of Orange as part of the Laguna Coast Wilderness Park. Its trail gives close access to the James Dilley Greenbelt Preserve and Barbara's Lake.
