## Water Quality (Stormwater) Program

The Water Quality (Stormwater) Program addresses water pollution affecting the municipal separate storm sewer system and waters of the United States, including compliance with NPDES permits. For more information, contact the Planning & Environmental Services Department at (949) 639-0500 or use the online contact form at lagunawoods.gov/contact-us/.

Common "point source" pollutants include construction waste and wastewater (such as paints, stains and sealants). They also include household waste (such as trash, cleaning chemicals, yard waste and animal fecal materials). Landscape irrigation, irrigation water and lawn watering runoff ("over-irrigation") are also prohibited. The City's primary water quality regulations are in Chapter 4.14 (Water Quality) of the Laguna Woods Municipal Code. Violations may result in administrative, civil and/or criminal penalties.

Source: https://www.lagunawoods.gov/waterquality/

## Animal Services and Dog Licensing

Animal control and shelter services are provided by the City of Laguna Beach's Animal Services Division under contract with the City. Call (949) 497-0701, or call 911 immediately in an emergency. Every dog four months of age or older must be licensed by Laguna Beach/Laguna Woods Animal Services. Dog licensing information is at petdata.com/for-pet-owners/lgn, or call (949) 497-3552.

Pet limit: a maximum combination of three cats and dogs, no more than two of which may be dogs, may be kept in any residence. Keeping more requires a private kennel license from Laguna Beach/Laguna Woods Animal Services, (949) 497-3552.

Spay/neuter: every dog six months of age or older must be spayed or neutered, certified sterile, or licensed as an unaltered dog. Every cat six months of age or older must be spayed or neutered, certified sterile, or meet unaltered cat requirements.

Source: https://www.lagunawoods.gov/animal-services/

## Coyotes and Wildlife

The City asks residents not to feed wildlife. Flyers are available in English, Chinese, Korean and Spanish. Coyotes can exhibit aggressive behavior and attack people and pets. "Attractants" that may increase coyote activity in residential areas include off-leash dogs, free-roaming cats, pet food and water bowls left outside, and fallen fruit. Coyote Awareness Flyers are posted in English, Chinese, Korean and Spanish.

Source: https://www.lagunawoods.gov/animal-services/

## Senior Mobility Program (Subsidized Taxi Travel)

The Senior Mobility Program subsidizes taxi travel for Laguna Woods residents who are at least 60 years of age. All transportation is provided by Yellow Cab. For more information, call City Hall at (949) 639-0500.

Co-pays for trips starting or ending in Laguna Woods:
- $0 to or from the Laguna Hills Transportation Center, Irvine Station, or the Santa Ana Regional Transportation Center.
- $8 for trips up to 10 miles within Orange County (each way).
- $18 for trips between 10.1 and 15 miles within Orange County (each way).
- $28 for trips over 15 miles within Orange County (each way).
- $28 for trips to or from VA Long Beach.
- $40 for trips to or from John Wayne Airport.

Source: https://www.lagunawoods.gov/smp/

## Senior Mobility Program — Enrollment

Residents can enroll on a walk-in basis at City Hall from 8 a.m. to 5 p.m., Monday through Friday (closed holidays). Call (949) 639-0500 to confirm business hours. Enrollment is free and takes only a few minutes. It generally includes:
- showing proof of age (at least 60 years)
- showing proof of Laguna Woods residency
- completing the enrollment form and waiver
- taking a photograph for your identification card

Enrollments are valid through June 30, 2027.

Source: https://www.lagunawoods.gov/smp/

## Public Library Building (OC Public Libraries, Laguna Woods Branch)

The Public Library Building is at 24266 El Toro Road, Laguna Woods, CA 92637. It opened on January 27, 2024. The 1,734 square foot building has 1,161 square feet of interior space and an adjoining 573 square foot outdoor activity room. It is leased to the County of Orange for the Laguna Woods branch of OC Public Libraries. The library page is at ocpl.org/libraries/laguna-woods.

## Parks and Public Facilities

- City Hall: 24264 El Toro Road, Laguna Woods, CA 92637.
- "A Place for Paws" Dog Park: 23190 Ridge Route Drive. It has approximately 6,400 square feet of dog-friendly artificial turf, a dog water station and seating. The property is owned by the Golden Rain Foundation of Laguna Woods (GRF). The City's use of it runs through June 30, 2027, or as may be extended by mutual agreement.
