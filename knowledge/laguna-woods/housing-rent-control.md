## General Plan — Housing Element Requirement

State law requires general plans to address a minimum of seven topics: circulation, conservation, housing, land use, noise, open space, and safety (California Government Code Section 65302). The City's General Plan page links to the Housing Element at https://www.lagunawoods.gov/housingelement. It also links to the Conservation, Land Use, Mobility, Noise, Open Space, and Safety Elements and to the CEQA documents.

## Zoning — Residential Zoning Districts

All public and private property in Laguna Woods is in one of several zoning districts. An overlay zoning district may also apply to some property. Residential districts listed on the Zoning page are Residential Community (RC), Residential Multifamily (RMF), and Residential Towers (RT). The Residential Community-Maintenance (RC-MT) Overlay Zoning District is also listed. Site-specific zoning is shown on the City's Zoning Map (GIS), under the "Planning Maps" tab in the Layers List. The Municipal Code contains tables of permitted uses for each district. To discuss a use not listed, contact the Planning Division at planning@cityoflagunawoods.org.

Source: https://cityoflagunawoods.org/zoning

## Planning Division — Contact for Property Owners and Development Questions

Property owners, businesses, developers, and other parties seeking information on a prospective development application or permit requirement should email the Planning Division at planning@lagunawoods.gov for the fastest response. You may also leave a voicemail at (949) 639-0561. In-person and virtual appointments are available by request. The Planning Division is part of the Planning & Environmental Services Department. It also prepares updates of the Laguna Woods General Plan and Zoning Code.

Source: https://www.cityoflagunawoods.org/planning/
