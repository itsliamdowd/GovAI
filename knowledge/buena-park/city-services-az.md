## Animal Control — SEAACA

The City of Buena Park contracts animal control services with the South East Area Animal Control Agency (SEAACA). SEAACA provides animal control, pet licensing, adopt-a-pet programs, veterinary services, low-cost vaccinations, deworming, flea treatment, and microchip IDs.

SEAACA is at 9777 Seaaca Street, Downey, CA 90241. Phone: (562) 803-3301. Fax: (562) 803-3676. Except for pet licensing, call (562) 803-3301 ext. 239. Website: https://www.seaaca.org/

## Pet Rules — Number of Pets, Leash, Waste, Barking

Each residential property may have no more than a total of three dogs and three cats over the age of six months.

Whenever a dog leaves its licensed premises, it must be on a leash and under the control of a person competent to restrain it. Animal waste must be disposed of immediately, both when walking a dog and when maintaining the animal's licensed premises. Residents are asked to attend to their dogs' needs to minimize excessive barking, howling or yelping.

## Wildlife Calls — SEAACA

SEAACA can respond to calls about wildlife that are sick, injured, deceased, or have had physical contact with a human or domestic animal. After consultation with the State of California Department of Fish and Game, SEAACA is not authorized to relocate healthy wildlife.

A SEAACA Wildlife Information Sheet is available for download from the page. It has tips to discourage wildlife activity and what to do about nuisance wildlife.

## Coyote Management

Residents can report coyote sightings to the City and view a Map of Coyote Sightings on the page. The City Council approved a Coyote Management Plan, which is linked on the page. Coyote activity typically increases in the spring and summer.

Recommended steps from the City:
- Keep cats and small dogs indoors or in close presence of an adult.
- Feed pets indoors.
- Remove fallen fruit and standing water from your yard.
- Avoid bird feeders.
- Install motion-sensor lighting.
- Keep yards free of thick brush and weeds.
- Enclose the bottoms of porches and decks.
- Fully enclose outdoor animal enclosures.
- Teach children to avoid areas with dense vegetation or brush.
- Never feed wildlife.

## Cable Television — BPTV and Cable Providers

## Graffiti Removal and Reporting

Report graffiti by email at BuenaParkGraffiti@buenapark.com or by phone at (714) 821-8658 (Graffiti Hotline). The hotline is 24-hour. Give a specific address or location and a description, plus your name and a daytime phone number. Graffiti can also be reported online.

The City removes graffiti from the first floor of any public structure facing the street, as quickly as possible except on rainy days and holidays. It encourages private property owners to remove graffiti promptly and provides donated or recycled paint to residents as available.

## Sewer Laterals — Property Owner vs. City Responsibility

The City is responsible for the sewer mainline only. The property owner maintains the sewer lateral serving private property, up to where it connects to the "saddle" or "wye" connection of the sewer main. This does not always end at the sidewalk, curb, or parkway. Contact Public Works at (714) 562-3655 to clarify where your connection is. See Municipal Code 13.04.010 and 13.04.210.

If a plumber says the line is broken, do not sign any contract first. Confirm with a Closed Circuit TV camera inspection and get several price quotes. For help, call Public Works at (714) 562-3655.

Source: https://www.buenapark.com/city_departments/public_works/faqs.php

## Sewer Lateral Repair — Licenses, Permits, and Pipe

A plumber with a C-36 contractor's license and a City business license can work entirely on your property. Work within the public right of way (under the sidewalk, in the parkway, or in the street) requires one of these licenses, a valid City license, and a $1,000,000/$2,000,000 insurance policy on file:
- General Engineering Contractor Class A
- Sewer Contractor Class C-42
- Pipeline Class C-34

Work in the public right of way also needs a public works permit. For more information, contact a Public Works Permit Technician at (714) 562-3670.

Permits for work on your property are issued by the City's Building Department. Permits for work within the public right of way are issued by the Public Works Engineering Division. If there are no root problems, vitrified clay pipe (VCP) is recommended. Sch 40 PVC or Sch 40 ABS can also be used.

Source: https://www.buenapark.com/city_departments/public_works/faqs.php

## Street Sweeping

Street sweeping is done 5 days a week, Monday through Friday. In some areas the time is 8:00 a.m. to 12:00 p.m. and in others 12:00 p.m. to 3:00 p.m. There is no parking on the street during the posted street sweeping time for your neighborhood. "No Parking - Street Sweeping" signs are posted at the entrances and exits of each neighborhood. A city-wide schedule map is linked on the page.

If your sweeping day falls on New Year's Day, Memorial Day, July 4th, Labor Day, Thanksgiving, or Christmas Day, your street is skipped that week and resumes on the regular day the following week.

Source: https://www.buenapark.com/city_departments/public_works/faqs.php

## Painting Address Numbers on Curbs

Under Buena Park Municipal Code Section 10.16.030 and City Council Resolution No. 9903, a property owner may paint the address number on the curb. Two requirements apply:
- The number must be on the curb directly in front of the owner's own property.
- It must be black numbers on a white background per Buena Park Std. Plan No. 311.

A property owner may authorize an independent contractor to do the work. The owner must authorize the contractor in writing. The contractor must have the authorization in hand before and during the work.

## Trash and Recycling — Collection Day and Holidays

On collection day, place refuse, recycling, and green waste containers at the curb with the lid arrows pointing toward the street. Avoid obstructions such as parked cars and utility poles, and avoid traffic areas such as bike lanes. Set green waste containers at least two (2) feet away from your trash cart(s). A city-wide Trash Collection schedule map is linked on the page.

Collection is delayed one day for the rest of the week in which one of these holidays falls: New Year's Day, Memorial Day, July 4th - Independence Day, Labor Day, Thanksgiving Day, Christmas Day. Holidays that fall on a Saturday do not delay service. Holidays that fall on a Sunday do delay service for the rest of the week. On all other holidays, set out containers on your regular service day.

If trash was not picked up, call EDCO at (714) 522-3577.

Source: https://www.buenapark.com/city_departments/public_works/utilities/trash_services/index.php

## Free Curbside Pickup of Large and Bulky Items

Residential refuse customers can place up to six large and bulky items at the curb for free pickup, up to three times each calendar year. Call EDCO at (714) 522-3577 at least two days before your normal weekly trash pickup. Place the items at the curb alongside your trash cans. A separate truck collects them on your pickup day.

Acceptable items include furniture, scrap metal, stoves, refrigerators, air conditioners, microwave ovens, water heaters, and one television or computer monitor. Unacceptable items are tires, dirt, concrete, and asphalt. Hazardous chemicals such as paints, thinners, solvents, pesticides, and liquid wastes are not accepted.

Commercial and multi-family customers with drop-off bins should contact EDCO about options.

Source: https://www.buenapark.com/city_departments/public_works/utilities/trash_services/index.php
