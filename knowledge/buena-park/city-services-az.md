## Trash & Recycling — Collection Day Instructions

On your scheduled collection day, place refuse/waste, recycling, and green waste recycling containers at the curb with the arrows on the lid pointing toward the street. Avoid placing cans next to obstructions such as parked cars or utility poles, and do not place them in traffic areas such as bike lanes. Set green waste recycling containers out a minimum of two (2) feet away from your trash cart(s).

A city-wide map with the entire Trash Collection schedule is available on the City's trash services page. If your trash was not picked up, call EDCO at (714) 522-3577.

## Trash & Recycling — Holiday Service Schedule

Observed holidays: New Year's Day, Memorial Day, July 4th - Independence Day, Labor Day, Thanksgiving Day, and Christmas Day. Trash collection is delayed one day for the remainder of the week in which the holiday falls. For example, if the holiday is on Monday, Monday pickup occurs Tuesday, Tuesday pickup occurs Wednesday, and so on.

Holidays that fall on a Saturday do not delay service. Holidays that fall on a Sunday do delay service for the remainder of the week. For all other holidays, including Martin Luther King Jr.'s Birthday, Lincoln's Birthday, President's Day, Washington's Birthday, Cesar Chavez Day, Juneteenth, and Veterans Day, set out containers on your regular service day.

## Free Curbside Pickup of Large and Bulky Items

Residents can place up to six large and bulky items at the curb for pickup. The service is FREE to all Buena Park residential refuse customers. Call EDCO at least two days prior to your normal weekly trash pickup at (714) 522-3577, then place the items at the curb alongside your trash cans. A separate truck picks them up on your pickup day. Residents may use this program up to three times each calendar year.

Acceptable items: furniture, scrap metal, stoves, refrigerators, air conditioners, microwave ovens, water heaters, and one television or computer monitor. Unacceptable items: tires, dirt, concrete, asphalt, and hazardous chemicals such as paints, thinners, solvents, pesticides, and liquid wastes.

The service is only available to residential refuse customers. Commercial and multi-family customers with drop-off bins should contact EDCO about options.

Source: https://www.buenapark.com/city_departments/public_works/utilities/trash_services/index.php

## Water Service — Starting Service, Billing, and Disputes

For billing inquiries in English, Spanish or Korean, or to establish or discontinue water service, call (714) 562-3721. The water application requires: name, address, home telephone number, two current forms of identification (presented in person), and proof of legal possession or repossession of the premise (e.g., escrow closing statement, rental agreement). There is a service deposit of $35.00 on your first bill.

Your first water and trash bill may arrive anywhere from two to 62 days from the start of service. Your meter is then read on approximately a 60-day cycle, and you receive your bill within ten business days after the meter reading. If you believe the City has made an error in your bill, or if you question your obligation to pay the bill, contact the Water Customer Service Office at (714) 562-3721 within 15 days of the billing date. For water emergencies AFTER HOURS ONLY, call (714) 562-3902.

## Water Conservation and Watering Schedule

No automated irrigation between 9 AM and 6 PM, and limit to a maximum of 15 minutes per station. Recommended watering schedule:

Winter (November – March): water ONCE per week. Street addresses ending in 1, 3, 5, 7, and 9 water MONDAYS. Addresses ending in 0, 2, 4, 6, and 8 water THURSDAYS. No watering other days.

Summer (April – October): water a maximum of THREE days per week. Addresses ending in 1, 3, 5, 7, and 9 water Mondays, Wednesdays, and Fridays. Addresses ending in 0, 2, 4, 6, and 8 water Tuesdays, Thursdays, and Saturdays. No watering on Sundays.

To report water waste, call (714) 562-3655 and state the location, the time, and the day of the observed water waste. The Buena Park 2025 Water Shortage Contingency Plan (PDF) is available on the page.
