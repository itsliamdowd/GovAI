## Disaster Preparedness — Local Hazards

The City's disaster preparedness page lists the most likely emergencies in Buena Park: earthquake, fire, floods, hazardous materials accidents, heat waves, blackouts, and pandemics and disease. It also links to information on landslides and mudslides, tsunamis, and radiation emergencies. Some parts of Buena Park can flood and have flooded. Hazardous materials incidents can come from truck wrecks, train derailments, or nearby warehouses.

## Fire Hazard Severity Zone Maps

Fire Hazard Severity Zones (FHSZs) are designated areas identified based on wildfire risk and are required under California Government Code Section 51179. On March 24, Cal Fire released updated FHSZ Maps for an area that includes a small section in the northern part of Buena Park. The updated map is to replace the existing FHSZ map adopted by ordinance in 2012. Areas within State Responsibility Areas are labeled as Moderate, High, or Very High hazard zones. FHSZ maps assess hazard, not risk.

Building requirements:
- Very High Fire Hazard Severity Zones: New buildings will require a buffer of 100 feet of defensible space. They will need to meet construction requirements for exterior wildfire exposure, which includes ignition-resistant construction (Chapter 7A of the California Building Code). Natural Hazard Real Estate Disclosures will be required at the time of sale of property.
- High Fire Hazard Severity Zones: New buildings will need to meet the same exterior wildfire exposure construction requirements (Chapter 7A of the California Building Code). Natural Hazard Real Estate Disclosures will be required at time of sale of property. Defensible space requirements do not currently apply in the high hazard severity zone.
- Moderate Fire Hazard Severity Zones: Currently, there are no requirements.

Under Assembly Bill 211, local governments must adopt these maps by ordinance. Senate Bill 63 mandates that local agencies make the maps publicly accessible within 30 days of their transmittal. Under California Government Code Section 51179 (b)(3), cities and counties may not decrease the level of hazard designated by the Office of the State Fire Marshal. For questions about the maps or adoption process, or to submit public comment, email city@buenapark.com or call (714)562-3500.

## Fireworks — Reporting Illegal Activity

Illegal firework activity that has been seen can be reported through the online survey on the City's Fireworks page. The survey is not monitored regularly and should not be used for emergency situations. For non-emergency situations requiring immediate attention, contact the Buena Park Police Department Dispatch at (714) 562-3902 or email dispatch@buenapark.com. For emergencies or fireworks causing immediate danger, call 9-1-1.

The Buena Park Police Department has a dedicated hotline for reports and tips on the possession and sale of illegal fireworks: (714) 562-3847. This hotline should not be used for reporting illegal fireworks being set off, and should not be used in emergency situations. A map on the Fireworks page shows the location of every illegal firework activity reported through the survey.

## Fireworks — Rules and Fines

Possession or use of illegal fireworks, and allowing illegal fireworks on your property, is prohibited in Buena Park.

Fines for possession or use of illegal fireworks: 1st Violation - $1,000; 2nd Violation - $2,000; 3rd+ Violation - $3,000.
Fines for social hosts (property owners, tenants, organizers): 1st Violation - $2,000; 2nd Violation - $4,000; 3rd+ Violation - $6,000.

The City allows the use and possession of safe and sane fireworks, which may be discharged within the city only on July 4th of each year, between the hours of 10 a.m. and 10 p.m. The discharge of fireworks is prohibited on any public property with the exception of all public streets & rights-of-way other than La Palma Ave., between Western Ave. and El Monte Dr. The Orange County Fire Authority firework safety information sheet is available in English, Spanish, and Korean.

## Fireworks Citations — Payment and Appeal

A citation amount may be paid online, by mail, or in person. Payment must be received by AUGUST 1, 2026. The citation received is the only notice; no further notice will be sent by mail. Accepted forms of payment are personal check, cashier's check, or money order payable to the City of Buena Park, with the citation number written on it. Check, money order, credit card, and cash payments are accepted in person at the Finance counter on the first floor of Buena Park City Hall. Buena Park City Hall is open Monday through Thursday, 7:00 am to 6:00 pm. Mail to: City of Buena Park Attn: Finance PO Box 5009 Buena Park, CA 90621-5009. The Finance Department can be reached at (714) 562-3722.

To contest an administrative citation, complete a Request for Administrative Hearing Form and include the full payment amount with it. Both the form and the full payment must be returned within TWENTY (20) days from the date of the citation. Anyone who wants an administrative hearing but is financially unable to deposit the administrative fine may submit an Application for Advance Deposit Wavier along with the Request for Administrative Hearing Form. Eligibility is determined based on monthly household income. The completed forms must be returned within TWENTY (20) days from the date of the citation. Questions: email city@buenapark.com.

## Police Officer Citizen Referral Bonus Program

Residents who refer a qualified Police Officer candidate can earn a $2,000 referral bonus once the candidate is successfully hired by the Buena Park Police Department. The bonus is paid in two installments: $1,000 after the referred officer starts their employment with the City, and $1,000 after the referred officer successfully completes their probationary period.

The referral must be made by a Buena Park resident. The referred candidate must meet all Police Officer qualifications and successfully complete the hiring process. Current or previously hired Buena Park officers are not eligible. Referrals are submitted through the form on the program page.

## Building Plan PreCheck (Crossbeam)

Buena Park Plan PreCheck by Crossbeam is a free, voluntary, AI-powered pre-check of construction plans, available to anyone submitting a building project in Buena Park. No account is required. Applicants upload plans as PDFs, a folder or a ZIP file at BuenaPark.com/PlanPreCheck, and enter an email address and the Buena Park project address. It can be used for new homes, accessory dwelling units (ADUs), home remodels, commercial tenant improvements, and other building projects.

Results are typically available within an hour as a list of potential corrections. A project can be re-checked up to three times. Applicants who resolve more than half of the items identified during the pre-check, as confirmed through a re-check, receive a FastPass certificate to include with their building permit submittal. FastPass submittals receive priority review. The City's goal for projects using Plan PreCheck is one round of corrections.

Plan PreCheck is an advisory pre-check only. It does not issue permits, does not approve or reject plans, and does not replace a City plan check. The Building Official remains the final authority on all permit decisions. Plans and reports remain the property of the user and are not kept or stored after the pre-check is complete.
