## Parking Citations: How to Pay

A parking citation amount may be paid by check or money order made payable to the City of Buena Park. Write the citation number on the check or money order, and do not send cash. Citations may also be paid online by credit card or debit card.

To pay, visit the 24-hour Parking Services Center website. You will need the citation number, or the vehicle license plate number associated with the citation if the citation number is not available.

## Parking Citations: How to Contest

If you received a parking citation issued by the Buena Park Police Department and believe it was issued in error, you may request an Initial Administrative Review of the citation. You must contest the citation within the first twenty-one (21) calendar days following its issuance, by requesting the review online or in writing. The online request is made through the 24-hour Parking Services Center website, where you will need the citation number or the vehicle license plate number.

Mail written requests to: Request for Parking Citation Review, Parking Citation Service Center, P.O. Box 11923, Santa Ana, CA 92711-1923. Include your citation number, vehicle license number and all relevant information that supports your case, including copies of supporting documents and any photographs (not returned).

For questions, call the Parking Services Center Monday through Friday from 8 a.m. to 5 p.m. at (888) 300-9915, or the 24-hour information line at (888) 207-5715.

## Traffic and Transportation Department — Services

The Traffic and Transportation Department falls under the Director of Public Works. Its major function is the operation and maintenance of more than 80 signalized intersections within the City of Buena Park. The department also works jointly with several other cities to improve traffic signal coordination between cities.

Other major functions include placing and maintaining traffic signs (stop, yield, speed limit, etc.) and street markings, handling citizen reports about transportation issues in the city, implementing traffic signal synchronization projects, and performing engineering and traffic surveys. The department is headed by Principal Engineer Deepthi Arabolu. Contact the Traffic and Transportation Division at (714) 562-3670.

## Traffic Maps and Documents

The Traffic and Transportation Department offers these commonly requested documents for download:

- **Buena Park Truck Route Map:** shows the streets in the city where commercial trucks are permitted by ordinance.
- **Buena Park Speed Limit Map:** shows all city streets with a speed limit greater than 25 miles per hour. It is taken from Buena Park Municipal Code (BPMC) section 10.24.010, which contains the official speed limits for any street with a limit greater than 25 MPH.
- **Buena Park ADT Interactive Map:** shows the results of the most recent city Average Daily Traffic (ADT) counts. ADT is the average number of vehicles, in both directions of travel, that use a road segment on a given day. A hard copy is available as a PDF.
- **Buena Park Truck Traffic Map:** shows the ADT percentages for commercial vehicles on all city truck routes.

## Reporting a Street Light Outage

In Buena Park, the majority of street lighting is operated by Southern California Edison (SCE). Report street light problems directly to SCE through their "Report an Outage" website, using the "Report a Street Light Outage" page. To speak to an SCE Customer Service Specialist, call 1-800-655-4555.

Source: https://www.buenapark.com/city_departments/public_works/utilities/street_light_issues.php

## Reporting an Intersection Safety Light Outage

Most lighting owned by the City is limited to intersection safety lights. These are the lights on top of the traffic signal poles at signalized intersections. To report one that is out, email the Public Works Department or call the Public Works front counter at 714-562-3670.
