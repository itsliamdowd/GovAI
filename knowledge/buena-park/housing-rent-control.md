## Affordable Housing — Rental Listings and Developments

Orange County Community Services maintains a list of affordable rental properties throughout Orange County, which includes Buena Park (the "Affordable Rental Housing List"). You must contact the apartment complex directly to inquire about vacancies and eligibility requirements.

## Home Improvement Loan Program — Overview and Terms

The City of Buena Park provides low interest loans to qualified homeowners to improve their homes. The level of assistance is determined by household income eligibility and available equity in the property. If your household income is within the income limits, submit the Home Improvement Program Pre-application (PDF). A Home Improvement Program brochure (PDF) is also available. For questions, call Economic Development at (714)562-3586.

Deferred Loan Program: income eligibility is 80% or below of County Median Income. Loan amount is $60,000. Interest rate is one percent (1%) interest for a maximum of 30 years; payments are deferred. Term is 30 years or upon sale, transfer of ownership or equity is taken out of property.

Eligible improvements may include but are not limited to: plumbing/electrical upgrades, handicap modifications, kitchen/bath upgrades, interior painting, and new flooring.
