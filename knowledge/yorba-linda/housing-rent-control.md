## Affordable Housing Programs

Since the inception of its housing programs, the City has assisted in the provision of hundreds of affordable housing units, extending both homeownership and rental housing opportunities to low and moderate-income households. The City's Affordable Housing List (PDF) lists affordable rental options in Yorba Linda. Interested persons must contact the property manager of each location for information on rents, qualifications, and availability. This is not a rental assistance program.

The Community Development Block Grant (CDBG) program is a federal program administered by the U.S. Department of Housing and Urban Development (HUD). It provides communities with financial resources to address a range of community development needs for income-qualified areas and/or households. The Mortgage Assistance Program is currently closed, and no applications are being accepted at this time.

Helpful documents on the page: Affordable Housing FAQs (PDF), Affordable Housing Myths & Facts (PDF), and Median Household Income Chart for Orange County (PDF).

## Housing Element (2021-2029)

On February 9, 2022, the City of Yorba Linda adopted the 2021-2029 Housing Element and Appendix. The Housing Element is part of the City's General Plan and includes goals, policies, programs, and quantified objectives to further the development, improvement, and preservation of housing. For jurisdictions in the Southern California Association of Governments (SCAG) region, the 6th cycle planning period extends from October 2021 through October 2029.

The City has launched a website, YLLocalControl.com, to keep the public informed and to allow the public to participate in the process of updating the Housing Element. Residents are encouraged to check back frequently for updates. Questions can be emailed to the Housing Element team.

Source: https://www.yorbalindaca.gov/344/Housing

## Rental Assistance — Orange County Housing Authority (Section 8)

The City of Yorba Linda cooperates with the Orange County Housing Authority (OCHA), which administers the Section 8 Voucher Program. The Housing Assistance Payments Program assists low-income, elderly, and disabled households by paying the difference between 30% of an eligible household's income and the actual rent.

The OCHA releases Section 8 lottery applications on a periodic basis. When the application period is open, the City will post the notice on its OCHA page and will have applications available at City Hall. There is a great demand for Section 8 vouchers and rarely is there sufficient available to meet the needs in Orange County.

Source: https://www.yorbalindaca.gov/348/Orange-County-Housing-Authority

## Other County Rental Assistance Programs

The County of Orange also administers rental assistance programs for special needs groups. These programs serve extremely-low-income persons:
- Family Unification Program: Section 8 assistance to families whose children are at risk of being placed in out-of-home care or delayed in returning from care because of the families' inadequate housing.
- Aftercare Program for Disabled: Section 8 assistance specifically targeted to disabled applicants.
- Shelter Plus Care Program: rent subsidies to homeless persons with disabilities.
- Hope for Elderly Independence: Section 8 assistance to frail elderly persons.

Contact the County of Orange or OCHA for more information on these programs.

Source: https://www.yorbalindaca.gov/348/Orange-County-Housing-Authority

## Fair Housing and Equal Opportunity

The City of Yorba Linda works in partnership with the Fair Housing Council of Orange County to ensure unimpeded access to fair housing opportunities. The Fair Housing Council is a private nonprofit organization formed in 1965. Its mission is protecting the quality of life in Orange County by ensuring equal access to housing opportunities, fostering diversity and preserving dignity and human rights.

The Council contracts to serve all of Orange County's local governments for the provision of fair housing services for their residents. It handles more than 250 cases of alleged housing discrimination in the county each year.

## Short-Term Rentals — Prohibited

A short-term vacation rental (also called a vacation rental or STR) is a rental of a residential dwelling unit or accessory building for periods of less than 30 consecutive days. Per Title 5, Chapter 5.50.030 of the Yorba Linda Municipal Code, short-term rentals are prohibited in Yorba Linda.

Source: https://www.yorbalindaca.gov/690/Short-Term-Rentals

## Short-Term Rentals — How to Report a Violation

To report a short-term rental within Yorba Linda, complete the online Report a Code Violation Form. Residents may also call the Code Enforcement hotline at 714-961-7138 to leave a voicemail. Provide the address of the property and any other relevant information.

## Homelessness — Regional Approach and Navigation Centers

The City of Yorba Linda participates in the North Orange County Service Planning Area ("North SPA"), which collaborates on a regional approach to homelessness, including identifying suitable locations for multiple shelters. The North SPA also includes the Cities of Anaheim, Brea, Buena Park, Cypress, Fullerton, La Habra, La Palma, Los Alamitos, Orange, Placentia, Stanton, and Villa Park.

The North SPA has opened two Navigation Centers, one in Buena Park and one in Placentia. They provide housing for those experiencing homelessness, health resources, job and social skills training, and opportunities to reunite with family and friends.

Source: https://www.yorbalindaca.gov/773/Homelessness-Information-Resources

## Homelessness — City Initiatives

The Bridges at Kraemer Place is a year-round emergency shelter located in Anaheim. A member of the Yorba Linda City Council represents the City on the Orange County Bridges at Kraemer Place Community Advisory Board.

Through the Orange County Sheriff's Department's Behavioral Health Liaisons (BHL) program, the City has its own BHL assigned to Yorba Linda. BHLs are Deputies who receive additional training on mental illness and how to connect the homeless population with available services and resources.

Source: https://www.yorbalindaca.gov/773/Homelessness-Information-Resources

## Homelessness — Other Resources and Guidance for Residents

2-1-1 OC is a central point for public information. Individuals needing assistance can dial 2-1-1 or text their zip code to 898211 for resources. The County of Orange provides a variety of behavioral and mental health programs to assist homeless individuals. The Orange County Health Care Agency administers several shelters. A North Orange County Pocket Resource Directory (PDF) is available on the City's page.

When interacting with individuals experiencing homelessness, the City's guidance is to treat them with respect, make donations or volunteer with organizations helping people experiencing homelessness, and not encourage panhandling by giving money. The City also says not to allow anyone to camp or loiter on private property, including HOA property. The City encourages donating to a charity that serves those experiencing homelessness rather than directly to the individual.
