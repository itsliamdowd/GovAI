## Building Division — Over-the-Counter (OTC) Appointment Program

Effective April 1, 2026, the Building Division will implement a new Over-the-Counter (OTC) Plan Check and Inquiry Appointment Program. It replaces unscheduled walk-in service with scheduled appointments for OTC plan reviews, code consultations, and large project submittals. Appointments can be online or in person. Walk-in service remains available for simple transactions such as permit pickups, fee payments, and status checks.

## Building Permit Process — Walk-In, Appointments, and When a Permit Is Required

The Building Department serves walk-in customers on a first-come, first-served basis during normal business hours. For a new construction project, you may schedule an appointment to meet with a Building Division representative Monday through Thursday between 8 a.m. and 3 p.m. Questions about the permit process: 714-961-7120.

Permits are required for: new buildings; additions (bedrooms, bathrooms, family rooms, etc.); residential work (decks, garages, fences, fireplaces, pools, water heaters, etc.); renovations (garage conversions, kitchen expansions, etc.); reroofing or roof repairs; electrical and solar systems; plumbing systems; and heating, ventilating, and air-conditioning ("HVAC") systems.

The Building Department issues Building, Electrical, Mechanical, Plumbing, and Demolition permits.

## Online Permit Submittals — Eligible Projects and How to Apply

The Building Department accepts online permit submittals for these projects only: Air Conditioning and Heating Units; *Electrical Service Panel Upgrades; *Electrical Vehicle Charging Stations; Re-Piping (all gas, water, drain, waste, and vent piping); Re-Roofs with like-for-like roofing and no engineering calculations for increased roof loading; *Roof Mounted Photovoltaic Systems; and Water Heaters. ## Online Permit Submittals — Processing Times

Initial electronic applications may take up to ten City observed working days to process. Additional wait times may be added for incomplete applications, the accuracy of information on plans, outer departmental approvals, and incorrect payment processing.

Once an application is approved for permit processing, the Building Permit Technician emails the applicant the permit cost and the necessary forms. The applicant must be able to print, sign and scan the documents. No phone photos are accepted. Once the forms are returned and the correct payment is processed, the permit is issued.

If a plan review is required, the plans are routed to a Plan Check Engineer. There is a 15-working-day review period, which also applies to resubmittals. Incomplete plans are sent back for revisions. Once a plan check is approved, the Building Permit Technician emails the permit cost and forms. After the correct payment is processed and the forms are returned, the approved plans are emailed back with an issued permit.

Source: https://www.yorbalindaca.gov/790/Online-Permit-Process

## Building Inspections — Hours and Scheduling

Building inspections are conducted Monday through Thursday from 8 a.m. to 3 p.m. and alternate Fridays from 8 a.m. to 2 p.m., excluding Federal holidays. The contractor or property owner is responsible for calling for inspections at specific times during construction.

Schedule an inspection through the online Citizen Portal, or scan the QR code with a smartphone. Provide: project address, permit number, contact name, telephone number, type of inspection, and preference for a morning or afternoon inspection. Inspection requests received after 3:30 p.m. will be inspected on the next available business day. Inspectors cannot guarantee specific inspection times.

After a permit is issued, you can also call the Building Inspection Hotline at 714-854-7411. If you cannot schedule by hotline or web tool, contact the Building Division at 714-961-7120.

Source: https://www.yorbalindaca.gov/479/Inspections

## Building Inspections — Requirements on Site

An adult over the age of 18 must give the inspector access to the job site for each inspection. The job card and set of approved plans must be present. If the site cannot be accessed or you do not have the plans and/or job card, contact the Building Division at 714 -961-7120 to cancel your inspection. If an inspection requires a ladder (such as a roof or attic), the owner or contractor must provide, set up and secure it; City inspectors do not carry ladders on their vehicle. Smoke and carbon monoxide detectors will be checked upon final inspection.

## Building Permit Exemptions — Specific Projects

No permit is required for:
- 1-story detached accessory structures with floor area not exceeding 120 square feet. Contact the Planning Department Counter for setback requirements at 714- 961-7130.
- Masonry or concrete fences not over 3 feet high, and wrought iron, wood, vinyl, or chain link fences not over 6 feet high, measured from the lowest adjacent finish grade and not part of an existing swimming pool barrier fence. Zoning Code height limits still apply.
- Movable cases, counters and partitions not over 5 feet, 9 inches high.
- Retaining walls not over 4 feet in height (bottom of footing to top of wall), unless supporting a surcharge, supporting a sloped backfill with a slope greater than 2:1, or impounding flammable liquids.
- Water tanks supported directly upon grade if capacity does not exceed 5,000 gallons and the height-to-diameter or width ratio does not exceed 2 to 1.
- Platforms, sidewalks, and driveways not more than 30 inches above grade, not over any basement or story below, and not part of an accessible route.
- Painting, papering, tiling, carpeting, cabinets, countertops, and similar finish work. A permit is required if the project involves removal or replacement of sinks, electrical-related items, or drywall. Contact the Building Department Counter at (714) 961-7120.
- Temporary motion picture, television, and theater stage sets and scenery.
- Window awnings supported by an exterior wall of Group R, Division 3, and Group U Occupancies projecting not more than 54 inches and needing no additional support.
- Prefabricated swimming pools accessory to a Group R, Division 3 Occupancy that are less than 18 inches deep, do not exceed 5,000 gallons, and are installed entirely above ground. Contact the Planning Department Counter for setbacks at (714) 961-7130.
- Decks not exceeding 120 square feet, not more than 30 inches above grade at any point, not attached to a dwelling, and not serving the existing door required by Section R311.4.

Unless otherwise exempted, separate plumbing, electrical and mechanical permits will be required for the exempted items above.

Source: https://www.yorbalindaca.gov/482/Building-Projects

## Solar Permits — SolarAPP+ Automated Review

SolarAPP+ provides a code-compliance check for the majority of residential, roof-mounted, retrofit photovoltaic systems. Only projects that conform to the Eligibility Checklist (PDF) are eligible for instant permitting through the SolarAPP+ portal.

1. Register and submit your design through the SolarAPP+ web page. SolarAPP+ charges a processing fee. Download the approved plans.
2. Complete a Solar Self-Certification form (PDF) and upload it to SolarAPP+. An active City of Yorba Linda Business License is required. The contractor must also be added to the SolarAPP+ program eligibility list; email team@solar-app.org to request this. Apply for a City of Yorba Linda SolarPV permit via the Accela Citizen Access Solar App, upload all documents and pay for the permit. Return the signed permit card to the City.
3. Once the permit is issued, schedule inspection by calling the Building Inspection Hotline at 714-854-7411 or using the Online Building Inspection Request tool. If unable, contact the Building Division at 714-961-7120.

## Preapproved ADU Program

The City is soliciting professionally designed plans for a detached pre-approved Accessory Dwelling Unit (ADU) program, in accordance with Assembly Bill 1332, to expedite ADU permitting. The City is currently in the first phase of soliciting professional designs. Once plans are accepted, they will be posted on the program page.

Standard pre-approved plans reflect approvals from the Building Division only and will always require site-specific customizations. These may include requirements and approvals from Planning, Public Works/Engineering, Orange County Fire Authority, and the Yorba Linda Water District. A homeowner who wants to use a design must contract directly with the designer, who keeps the copyright. Submittal documents for designers: Preapproved ADU Plan Checklist, Standard Plan Minimum Submittal Requirements, and Hold Harmless Agreement. Questions: Building Division, 714-961-7120.

## Appeals to the Planning Commission

Under Section 18.36.810 of the Yorba Linda Municipal Code, a determination, interpretation, decision, or similar action by the Community Development Director may be appealed to the Planning Commission within 15 days of the date the written action is delivered or mailed to the person who requested it. A timely appeal is heard at the next available Planning Commission meeting. The Commission may affirm, reverse (wholly or partially), or modify the action. Its written decision is final unless timely appealed to the City Council under Section 18.36.820.

Per Section 18.36.830, file the appeal with the Community Development Department on forms provided by the Community Development Director, together with an appeal fee established by resolution of the City Council. Form: Application to Appeal Decision of the Zoning Administrator to the Planning Commission (PDF). Community Development: 4845 Casa Loma Avenue, Yorba Linda, CA 92886; Phone: 714-961-7130.

Source: https://www.yorbalindaca.gov/206/Planning-Commission

## Appeals to the City Council (Planning Commission Decisions)

Any decision of the Planning Commission may be appealed to the City Council within 15 days of the Commission's decision by any person aggrieved or by any member of the City Council. A timely appeal is considered at the next available City Council meeting as a public hearing item, with notice given pursuant to Section 18.36.700(C). The City Council may affirm, reverse (wholly or partially), or modify the decision. Its written decision is final.

File the appeal with the City Clerk on forms provided by the City Clerk, together with an appeal fee established by resolution of the City Council. Form: Application to Appeal Decision of the Planning Commission to the City Council (PDF).

Source: https://www.yorbalindaca.gov/206/Planning-Commission
