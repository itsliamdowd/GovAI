## Animal Care Services (Mission Viejo Animal Services Center)

Since January 1, 2017, the City of Rancho Santa Margarita has partnered with the City of Mission Viejo for animal care and sheltering services. Mission Viejo Animal Services provides a pro-humane animal shelter, 24-hour response to emergency calls, pet adoption and fostering, low-cost vaccination clinics, animal licensing, volunteer opportunities, and community outreach and events.

Contact the center via animalinfo@cityofmissionviejo.org or by phone at (949) 470-3045 to report stray and/or aggressive animals, animal bites, stray or loose animals, animal cruelty complaints, injured wildlife, deceased animals, and nuisance complaints. For service requests and complaints, including barking and wildlife, call the Animal Services Center directly at (949) 470-3045. Direct questions, comments, or suggestions to ask-shelter@cityofmissionviejo.org.

Shelter address: 28095 Hillcrest, Mission Viejo, CA 92692. Phone: (949) 470-3045. Fax: (949) 365-0181. Shelter hours: 12:00 p.m. - 4:00 p.m. seven days a week. Dog kennels and cattery close 15 minutes prior to the shelter closure. The shelter serves Mission Viejo, Laguna Niguel, Aliso Viejo, Rancho Santa Margarita, and Laguna Hills.

Pet licensing is available online at https://licensepet.com/wl3/Reg/msvj. Licensing questions can be directed to petlicensing@cityofmissionviejo.org.

## Animal Control — Rules & Contacts

The City of Rancho Santa Margarita contracts with the City of Mission Viejo for Animal Control Services. Enforcement requests must be made through Mission Viejo Animal Care Services.

Rules come from the Rancho Santa Margarita Municipal Code, Adopted Urgency Ordinance No. 16-08 Animal Control. 5 adult dogs or cats are allowed per household. Every person owning or having custody of five (5) or more dogs or cats over the age of four (4) months must apply for an additional animal license from the City of Mission Viejo. Dogs over the age of 4 months who have been in the City more than 30 days are required to have a dog license and shall wear the license at all times. Property owners or occupants are responsible for any animal cry or sound that may disturb the peace and comfort of the neighbors. Animal waste must be removed and disposed of by the person having custody of the animal, in a sanitary manner.

For animal bites, barking dogs, and dog licensing, call (949) 470-3045.

## Street Sweeping and No-Parking Restrictions

To keep City streets clean and increase the effectiveness of street sweeping operations, the City restricts parking in fourteen (14) areas throughout the City. No parking during street sweeping restrictions applies only on streets with fixed signage indicating the days and times parking is restricted.

The page provides a Street Sweeping Parking Restriction Map, a 2026 Citywide Street Sweeping Calendar, and No Parking Street Sweeping Calendars for October 2026, November 2026, and December 2026. A monthly schedule by week and route is also posted. ## Solid Waste & Recycling

The City's solid waste contract is with CR&R Waste Services. For specific questions about recycling, call CR&R Waste Services at 949-728-0446. The City's Solid Waste Services page explains how to discard various types of materials. In California, certain materials cannot legally be disposed of in the trash; see the City's Illegal Dumping Materials page.

Senate Bill 1383 (SB 1383) went into effect January 2022 and requires all residents, multi-family properties, and commercial businesses to implement an organic waste program. See the Organics Recycling page for details. The Recyclable Materials page lists the materials that can be placed into residential/commercial recycling containers.

## Stormwater Program and Pollution Reporting

The Stormwater Management section of the Public Works Department manages stormwater quality in the City. If you live in Southern Orange County, runoff from landscape irrigation that enters streets and catch basins is prohibited. See Orange County's Tips to Prevent Overwatering brochure and h2oc.org for more information.

For additional information on the City's Stormwater Program, contact the Public Works Department at 949-635-1800, extension 6501.

To report a chemical and hazardous materials spill, call 911. To report water pollution, contact the County of Orange hotline at 877-897-7455. For mosquito and rodent issues, contact the Orange County Vector Control at 714-971-2421.

## Community Parks

The community parks in Rancho Santa Margarita are privately owned and maintained by a Homeowners Association (HOA), which includes the Rancho Santa Margarita Landscape and Recreation Corporation (SAMLARC). While most of the parks are open to the general public, SAMLARC and the other HOAs (where applicable) may have specific restrictions on use or require keyed access of some amenities. For questions about park use and availability, call your local HOA or SAMLARC at 949-709-0010.

Current parks: Altisima Park, Arroyo Vista Park, Canada Vista Park, Central Park, Cielo Vista Park, Estrella Vista Park, Lago Santa Margarita Beach Club, Mesa Linda Park, Monte Vista Park, Solana Park, Tijeras Creek Park, Trabuco Mesa Park, Vista Verde Park.

## Senior Mobility Program

The Senior Mobility Program is funded by the OCTA and provides a taxi service to eligible Rancho Santa Margarita residents. The current cab operator is OCY Management, LLC.

Service is provided to Rancho Santa Margarita residents who are 60 years of age or older. Qualified residents must have an approved application and be issued a Senior Mobility photo identification card before being authorized to participate. To determine eligibility and register, visit RSMconnect.org and select the Senior Mobility Program tab, or call 949-216-9700.
