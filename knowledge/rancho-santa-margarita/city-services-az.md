## Lago Santa Margarita and O'Neill Regional Park

All parks in the City are privately owned, operated and maintained by homeowner associations, except O'Neill Regional Park and County trails. Public access to most parks is available.

O'Neill Regional Park (approximately 3,358 acres) offers overnight camping and day/picnic use. About 3.5 acres are available for RV camping. It also has an equestrian campground, an arena, and 18 miles of riding trails.

The Lago Santa Margarita Beach Club is owned and operated by SAMLARC. Its grill and picnic area, boating, swim lagoon, 2 volleyball courts, and Fiesta Room are provided for local SAMLARC residents.

Lago Santa Margarita welcomes boaters and anglers, and the walk around the lake is 1.1 miles. California law prohibits swimming in manmade recreational lakes where the water is not recirculated. A Fishing Derby is held regularly the 3rd Saturday morning in May. A Star-Spangled Spectacular is held for the Fourth of July.

Source: https://www.cityofrsm.org/406/Recreation

## Solid Waste & Recycling

The City's contract is with CR&R Waste Services. For specific questions about recycling, call CR&R Waste Services at 949-728-0446. The City's Solid Waste Services page explains how to discard various types of materials.

## Yard Waste

Senate Bill 1383 requires all residents and businesses to separate "green" waste from other trash. It can only be placed in the green organic waste containers.

Accepted: Dry Leaves, Garden Trimmings, Grass Clippings, Green Plants, Weeds, Untreated Wood Waste/Chips, Organic/Food Waste.

Not accepted: Animal Waste, Stone & Rubble, Palm Branches, Plastic Liners, Treated Timber, Plant Pots.

## Animal Control

The City contracts with the City of Mission Viejo for Animal Control Services. Enforcement requests must be made through Mission Viejo Animal Care Services. For animal bites, barking dogs, and dog licensing, call (949) 470-3045.

Rules under the Rancho Santa Margarita Municipal Code, Adopted Urgency Ordinance No. 16-08:
- 5 adult dogs or cats are allowed per household. Anyone owning or having custody of five (5) or more dogs or cats over the age of four (4) months must apply for an additional animal license from the City of Mission Viejo.
- Dogs over 4 months old who have been in the City more than 30 days must have a dog license and wear it at all times.
- Property owners or occupants are responsible for any animal cry or sound that may disturb the peace and comfort of the neighbors.
- Animal waste must be removed and disposed of in a sanitary manner by the person having custody of the animal.

## Rental and Utility Assistance

The City cooperates with the Orange County Housing Authority (OCHA), which administers the Section 8 Voucher Program. Its Housing Assistance Payments Program assists low income, elderly, and disabled households by paying the difference between 30% of an eligible household's income and the actual rent. To check eligibility for OCHA programs, call OCHA at 714-480-2700 or visit the OCHA website.

County programs for extremely-low-income persons:
- Family Unification Program.
- Aftercare Program for Disabled.
- Shelter Plus Care Program (rent subsidies to homeless persons with disabilities).
- Hope for Elderly Independence.

## Mello-Roos Districts — Trabuco Canyon Water District

The Mello-Roos districts were formed under the Mello-Roos Community Facilities Act of 1982 following public hearings and landowner elections. All were approved by more than a 2/3 majority. The City of Rancho Santa Margarita does not levy, administer, collect or use the Community Facilities District (CFD) special taxes, and did not form any of the CFDs affecting the community. All taxes are incurred annually and are levied/collected at the same time as property taxes.

Source: https://www.cityofrsm.org/355/Trabuco-Canyon-Water-District
