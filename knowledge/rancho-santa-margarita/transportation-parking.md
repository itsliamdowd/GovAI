## Vehicle Parking Rules

Vehicles may be parked on a public street for 72 hours, unless otherwise prohibited. The Orange County Sheriff's Department enforces this. Vehicles on public streets must be in operable condition and have current tags and registration. If not, they may be cited and/or towed away.

A vehicle cannot block the public sidewalk, street, or alley. Large commercial trucks and trailers cannot be parked in any residential neighborhood except when actively loading or unloading. Only emergency roadside vehicle repairs may be made to a vehicle parked on the public street, sidewalk, or alley. Motor homes, campers or trailers may not be used for living or sleeping quarters. Automobiles, motor homes, trailers, campers, and boats may not be stored on public roadways.

Source: https://www.cityofrsm.org/221/Vehicle-Parking

## Vehicle Parking — Reporting Complaints

Vehicle parking issues can be called in 24 hours a day to the Orange County Sheriff's Department at (949) 770-6011. You may also submit a complaint online using the City's Community Connection Citizen Request Tracker (CRT). The CRT is monitored during regular business hours only.

## Project 529 Bike Registration

Project 529 is a community-based bike registration program designed to help reduce bike theft. Cyclists register their bikes online, which makes it easier for law enforcement and the community to identify and recover stolen bikes. The program provides a unique identification sticker for each bike, which acts as a deterrent to thieves and aids in the return of lost or stolen bicycles.

Register through the Rancho Santa Margarita Police Services page on Project 529: https://project529.com/garage/organizations/ranchosantamargaritapoliceservices/landing?code=X5A4MA

## E-Bike and Bicycle Safety Initiative

RSM Police Services developed an E-bike Safety Initiative in response to concerns about e-bike rider safety, e-bike injury traffic collisions, and e-bike theft, particularly unsafe and inexperienced e-bike riding by juveniles. The initiative has three phases: education, engagement, and enforcement.

Education: Child Safety Deputies and Motor Deputies give lectures at middle schools, both public and private. Topics include e-bike categories, speeds, capabilities, traffic laws, helmet use, and safety equipment such as reflective clothing, lights, bells, and horns. RSM Police Services has partnered with Mission Hospital to provide free helmet fittings for children in need. After each presentation, underage riders are recommended to take a competency test.

Engagement: In early summer, RSM Police Services works with local businesses, nonprofits, and Mission Hospital. It participated in Bike Rodeo and E-Bike Safety events in 2022 and 2024 at the Bell Tower Regional Community Center, and plans to host an E-Bike and Bicycle Safety Event annually.

Enforcement: The enforcement phase targets individuals who persist in unsafe practices. Motor units have towed several electric motorcycles. Enforcement actions will be taken against unsafe riding, with parents notified.

Source: https://www.cityofrsm.org/707/E-Bike-and-Bicycle-Safety-Initiative
