# Public Safety & Code Enforcement

## Construction Noise — Permitted Hours

Construction noise is regulated under Santa Monica Municipal Code 4.12.110. Normal construction is allowed Monday–Friday 8 a.m.–6 p.m. and Saturday 9 a.m.–5 p.m. Construction is prohibited all day Sunday and on listed holidays. Jackhammers, pile drivers, pavement breakers, and other high-noise work (exceeding noise limits in SMMC 4.12.110(b)) are limited to Monday–Saturday, 10 a.m.–3 p.m. Work outside these hours requires an After-Hours Construction Permit.

Source: https://www.santamonica.gov/construction-noise-complaint

## Code Enforcement — How to Report Violations

Report code violations through Santa Monica's 311 system: dial 311 (or (866) 311-7266 from outside the city), online at santamonica.gov/311, email 311@santamonica.gov, or the City of Santa Monica mobile app. Available 7:30 a.m.–5:30 p.m. Mon–Thu, 8 a.m.–5 p.m. Fri. For urgent code issues outside regular hours (evenings/weekends), call the police non-emergency line (310) 458-8491.

Source: https://www.santamonica.gov/topic-explainers/code-enforcement

## Code Enforcement — What's Covered

The Code Enforcement Division handles complaints involving unpermitted construction, substandard housing, noise violations, property maintenance issues, and zoning violations. Staff respond to violations during regular business hours (Mon–Fri 7 a.m.–5:30 p.m.). Weekend staff handle unpermitted or early-morning construction, and after-hours emergencies go through police dispatch. Contact the Division at (310) 458-4984 or code.enforcement@santamonica.gov.

Source: https://www.santamonica.gov/topic-explainers/code-enforcement

## Noise Ordinance — General Rules

Santa Monica's noise ordinance limits sound levels in public and private spaces. Permissible hours vary by activity: construction 8 a.m.–6 p.m. weekdays (9 a.m.–5 p.m. Sat), loud work 10 a.m.–3 p.m. weekdays/Sat. Residential quiet hours (late night/early morning noise prohibition) apply. Violations are handled by Code Enforcement. Report via 311.

Source: https://www.santamonica.gov/programs/noise

## Leaf Blower Ban

Leaf blowers are banned citywide in Santa Monica. Violators can be cited. Use rakes, brooms, or hand tools instead. Report illegal leaf blower use to 311 or Code Enforcement at (310) 458-4984.

Source: https://www.santamonica.gov/topic-explainers/code-enforcement

## Smoking Ban — Beaches & Public Spaces

Smoking and vaping are prohibited on Santa Monica beaches, parks, the Santa Monica Pier, Third Street Promenade, and outdoor dining areas. First offense is a criminal infraction with a $100 base fine plus penalties, rising to $200 for a second offense within a year and $500 for later violations within a year. Sidewalks and public places where not expressly prohibited allow smoking if it's not within 20 feet of doors or open windows.

Source: https://www.santamonica.gov/blog/public-smoking-in-santa-monica

## Smoking Ban — Multi-Unit Housing

Smoking is prohibited in shared spaces (hallways, common areas) of multi-unit housing, and in new rental units. Existing units may have designated smoking/non-smoking units based on local rules. Disputes are handled by landlords and tenants under the lease and city ordinance.

Source: https://www.santamonica.gov/blog/public-smoking-in-santa-monica

## Fireworks — Prohibited

Fireworks, sparklers, and special effects are prohibited in Santa Monica, including at special events. Violators are subject to criminal penalties. Legal fireworks displays require a permit from the Fire Department.

Source: https://www.santamonica.gov/topic-explainers/public-safety-codes

## Dogs on Beach — Leash Rules

Dogs are permitted on Santa Monica beaches with restrictions. Dogs must be on-leash or under voice control in designated areas. Off-leash areas exist at certain times (check beach signage or call 311). Violators can be cited. Report unleashed or aggressive dogs to police non-emergency (310) 458-8491 or 311.

Source: https://www.santamonica.gov/park-rules

## Anti-Camping Ordinance

Sleeping or camping on public property is prohibited in Santa Monica. Violations are enforced by police and Code Enforcement. Homeless services and emergency assistance are available through the city's Housing Office and community partners.

Source: https://www.santamonica.gov/topic-explainers/public-safety-codes

## Santa Monica Police Department (SMPD)

Address: 333 Olympic Dr, Santa Monica, CA 90401. Non-emergency dispatch: (310) 458-8491. Emergency: 911. Services include patrol, investigations, community policing, and crime prevention. Special units: Traffic, Gang Task Force, Homeless Outreach Team, and others. Records Unit handles police report requests.

Source: https://www.santamonica.gov/police

## Santa Monica Fire Department (SMFD)

Address: 333 Olympic Dr, Santa Monica, CA 90401. Non-emergency: (310) 458-8669. Emergency: 911. Services include fire suppression, emergency medical response, fire prevention inspections, and code enforcement. Fire Records Unit handles public record requests for fire reports and permits.

Source: https://www.santamonica.gov/fire

## SM Alerts Emergency Notification System

Santa Monica Alerts is a free emergency notification system that sends warnings about threats (floods, hazmat, fires, earthquakes, etc.) to residents and workers via phone, email, and text. Sign up at santamonica.gov or through the City of Santa Monica mobile app. Alerts are multilingual and reach vulnerable populations.

Source: https://www.santamonica.gov/santa-monica-alerts

## Community Policing & Programs

SMPD offers community policing programs including Neighborhood Watch, Police Cadet programs, and victim services. Fire Department programs include fire safety education and CPR training. Contact 311 or department non-emergency lines for details.

Source: https://www.santamonica.gov/police
