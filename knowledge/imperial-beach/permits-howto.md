## Building Division — Responsibilities and Counter Hours

The Building Division enforces state and municipal codes/ordinances. It reviews and approves plans for all proposed buildings or building additions. It issues all required building, plumbing, mechanical and electrical permits. It performs all the inspections associated with those permits within the City. It also conducts inspections related to the Code Compliance Division to gain compliance on municipal code and building code violations.

City Hall is open Monday through Thursday from 7:30 am to 5:30 pm. Staff are available during non-inspection hours to assist with permit help from 7:30 am to 9 am and 3:30 pm to 5 pm. Kiosks are available for applicant use. Building Official: Fred Huddleston. Building Housing Inspector I: Pete Viney.

## Building Permits — When Required and How to Apply

Building permits and inspections are required for new construction, additions, and remodeling work to confirm that all structures meet applicable City, State and Federal building code requirements. Most repairs or alterations require a building permit.

Imperial Beach uses an entirely online building permit, plan check, and payment processing system. To apply for all building permits, applicants must first create a Citizen Self-Service (CSS) Account in the CSS portal. The page links to instructions for creating a CSS account and for the online building permit submittal process.

On September 14, 2024, the City introduced a new login process for its "Permitting and Licensing System". Existing users who log in for the first time after September 14, 2024 must re-register using the same email they previously used for the system. The new account automatically links to existing permits and plans.

## Building Inspections — Requesting an Inspection

No inspections are conducted on Fridays. Inspections must be requested by 5:00 pm the day before you wish to have your inspection. Inspections are conducted between 9 am to 3:30 pm, Monday through Thursday.

Request an inspection by calling the inspection hotline at 619-628-1360 or through the Citizen Self-Service (CSS) portal. Staff will reach out on the morning of inspection with a detailed time frame. You can also confirm that your inspection has been scheduled, the estimated timeframe, and the name and contact number of your inspector on the "Today's Inspection" page. For further days/hours of operation, see the Holiday Schedule and Friday Closures (PDF).

## FEMA LOMR-F / CLOMR-F Processing Suspension

Starting August 14, 2020, FEMA suspended processing Applications of Letters of Map Revisions based on fill (LOMR-Fs) and Conditional Letters of Map Revision based on fill (CLOMR-Fs) in the City of Imperial Beach. The suspension applies to the entire County of San Diego and to Los Angeles, Orange, San Luis Obispo, Santa Barbara and Ventura counties. It is for an unspecified period while FEMA prepares Biological Evaluations (BEs) of the effects of National Flood Insurance Program (NFIP) implementation on Federally-listed species and their critical habitat in California.

Applications for new LOMR-Fs or CLOMR-Fs received prior to August 14, 2020 will be processed and completed, assuming all necessary information has been provided. Requests to reopen CLOMR-Fs and LOMR-Fs closed due to inactivity must also have been received prior to August 14, 2020. Requests received after that date are suspended with no determination, and a notice of suspension is sent to the applicant. For further information, email FEMA.

Source: https://www.imperialbeachca.gov/225/Building-Division

## Planning Division — Responsibilities and Submittals

The Planning Division is responsible for the General Plan, the Local Coastal Program, the Zoning Ordinance (Title 19 of the Imperial Beach Municipal Code), implementation of the California Environmental Quality Act (Title 16), and the Subdivision Regulations (Title 18). It processes land use entitlements, studies, and permits within the City. It also supports the City Council, the Tidelands Advisory Committee, and the Design Review Board.

Planning permits are applied for through an entirely online system. Applicants must first create a Citizen Self-Service Account in the Citizen Self-Service (CSS) portal. Instructions on creating a CSS Account are available as a PDF. Contacts: Arturo Ortuño, City Planner; Ryan Pua, Associate Planner.

Source: https://www.imperialbeachca.gov/260/Planning-Division

## Current Planning/Development

Current Planning implements the City's General Plan mainly by reviewing specific requests for the use or development of property. Planning staff assist the public with questions on the Land Use Code, zoning verification, business licenses, permitting, and development. The Planning Division reviews and recommends on all administrative and discretionary applications, conducts Public Hearings on Discretionary Applications, and administers the Land Use Code (Title 19). Development plans are reviewed against the General Plan, Local Coastal Program, Design Guidelines and the Land Use (Zoning) Code.

To apply for planning development permits, applicants must first create a Citizen Self-Service Account in the Citizen Self-Service (CSS) portal.

## Zoning

The City of Imperial Beach Zoning Code (IBMC Title 19) dictates what uses are allowed and how land can be developed (e.g. setbacks, height, parking) within the City's jurisdiction. Zones specify a category of uses (such as single-family residential, multifamily residential, commercial) and are adopted by ordinance. The City's zoning is shown geographically on the City's official land use map. The page links to the Planning and Zoning Code (IBMC Title 19) and the City Zoning Map.

## Business Licenses — Applying, Renewing, and Terms

Apply for a new business license through the IB Application Assistant, after reviewing the Business License Instructions (PDF). Existing licenses can be paid or renewed online with an invoice number, or by logging into your account. Changes in ownership, business location, or change of use/business type require a new application. Business licenses are nontransferable. All rental properties are required to be licensed.

Business license taxes vary by type of business, location, and number of employees. All taxes must be paid for a license to be issued. Fees can be paid by mailed check or online via credit card or e-check (processing fees apply). A license is not legal until all applicable city departments have approved the application and the license is posted at the business location.

Existing businesses are emailed renewal notices 30 days before expiry. To avoid late penalties, renewals must be paid by the 30th day after they expire. Contact: 619-628-1423.

Source: https://www.imperialbeachca.gov/179/Business-Licenses

## Special Events — Tent and Canopy Fire Permits

All canopies 400 square feet or larger and tents 200 square feet or larger require a Fire Department permit and site inspection. To obtain a permit, provide:
- the proposed location
- the proposed time of install, use and teardown
- the exact dimensions
- distances to other structures
- the proposed use
- the proposed number of occupants
- whether fire (candles) will be used inside
- whether electricity will be used inside
- whether heat is being provided
- whether cooking or warming will take place inside

Cooking using open flame cannot take place within any tent or canopy unless the material is specifically approved for use near heat or open flame. For inquiries about tent or canopy material, contact the Fire Prevention Division at 619-628-1392.

Source: https://www.imperialbeachca.gov/280/Fire-Safety-For-Special-Events
