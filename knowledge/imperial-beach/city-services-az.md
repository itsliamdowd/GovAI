## Animal Control: Contacts and Field Services

Effective October 1, 2025, the City of Imperial Beach contracts with the San Diego Humane Society for animal care services (shelter and licensing) and animal control. Animal Control: 619.299-7012 ext. 1. Emergency After-Hours Dispatch: 619.299-7012. Animal Shelter: 619.299-7012. Email: info@sdhumane.org. For any human life-threatening situation, call 9-1-1.

Call 619.299-7012 for animal-related concerns such as bites, suspected cruelty, neglect, injured animals or sick or injured wildlife and stray animals running at large. San Diego Humane Society responds to animal-related calls for service from the public, including picking up injured domestic animals, monitoring and responding to bites and enforcing rabies-related quarantines, addressing dangerous domestic animal situations, and investigating cruelty, abuse and neglect.

## Leash Laws

It is illegal to walk your dog off leash unless you are in a designated off-leash area. Dogs are required to be restrained at all times. Away from home, a dog must be on leash unless he is in a designated off-leash area, like a beach or park. At home, a dog must be restrained by a fence or other containment system. Violators may be cited under Imperial Beach Municipal Code Section 6.04.060.

Source: https://www.imperialbeachca.gov/461/Animal-Control

## Report an Issue (Non-Emergency)

The Report an Issue system is for non-emergency issues and is not monitored 24/7. Report any emergency to 911. If a concern needs immediate attention by the Police Department, call the non-emergency Dispatch Center (858) 868-3200. The system does not generate Police Reports.

When reporting potential code violations, it is extremely helpful if the picture is current (less than a week old) and clearly shows the issue. Allow City staff 1-3 business days to respond and/or address reported issues. All complainant information is confidential and will not be disclosed by the City unless required by a court order or subpoena. Start by clicking a category on the page or by downloading the MyImperialBeach app.

Source: https://www.imperialbeachca.gov/647/Report-an-Issue

## Code Compliance — Report a Code Violation

Code violations are reported through the online Code Compliance Request Form (Community Development, Code Compliance Division). The City of Imperial Beach does not respond to anonymous complaints. City Officials will not take action on online complaints until the complainant's identity is verified, so provide current and correct contact information.

Required fields: first and last name, address, city, state, zip code, phone number, violator address (with city, state, zip code), and complaint description. Email address is optional. All complainant information is confidential and will not be disclosed by the City unless required by a court order or subpoena.

Source: https://www.imperialbeachca.gov/242/Report-a-Code-Violation

## Mandatory Commercial Recycling (AB 341)

California Assembly Bill AB 341, enacted July 1, 2012, requires all California cities to divert a minimum of 75% of waste from landfills by 2020. The law also requires businesses that generate four cubic yards or more of solid waste weekly and multifamily complexes with 5 units or more to participate in recycling programs.

Questions: contact the Environmental Department at (619) 424-4095 or Warellano@imperialbeachca.gov.

Source: https://www.imperialbeachca.gov/227/AB-341-Mandatory-Commercial-recycling

## Construction & Demolition (C&D) Recycling

The City requires a Construction and Demolition (C&D) Waste Management Plan for some construction projects. In many cases, state and local law requires recycling of 65% of all construction waste. Those required to divert at least 65% include projects where: project cost is greater than $25,000; project area is 2,500 square feet or more; the project is sponsored by the City or Redevelopment Agency; or the project is governed by the California Green Building Code. The 2016 CALGreen new requirements apply to projects locally permitted starting January 1, 2017.

Construction waste generated in Imperial Beach can be handled through EDCO or by self-hauling to an approved C&D processing facility. The page links to documents including the Construction and Demolition Waste Management Plan (PDF), Construction and Demolition Containers (PDF), EDCO Materials Accepted (PDF), and the 2023 Certified Recycling Facilities List.
