## Banner Hanging Policy

City banner poles at Veterans Park and Triangle Park are for local organizations to hang banners advertising upcoming community-wide events (City Policy 613). Banners must be 3 feet high by 8 feet wide, with grommets at each corner. Waterproof material such as vinyl, plastic, or canvas is recommended. Each set of poles holds 2 banners, and a banner may take only one spot. Placement is first come, first serve; no reservations are required or allowed. Banners must be hung to the eye rings, and no tape or adhesive may be used. Only one banner per event or activity, per pole, is allowed.

A banner may be hung 2 weeks prior to the event date and must be taken down one day after the event date. Banners removed by City staff are kept at City Hall and must be picked up within one week of notification of removal. Include your phone number on the banner. Without it you will not receive a courtesy call, and the banner will be disposed of after one week if not claimed.

Not allowed: any banner advocating a political or discriminatory point-of-view; announcements of regular meetings / classes / lessons / clinics; and any banner advertising religious events or containing religious speech.

Source: https://www.imperialbeachca.gov/311/Banner-Hanging-Policy

## Street Sweeping

The City provides street sweeping through its franchise waste hauler (EDCO). The schedule resumed on May 1, 2021. The City sweeps a total of 130 curb miles per month:
- Weekly sweeping of commercial areas that include open striped and raised curb medians (Palm Avenue, Highway 75, 13th Street, Imperial Beach Boulevard, Seacoast Drive, and Ocean Lane).
- Twice per month sweeping of beachfront areas, including enforcement of posted parking restrictions along the sweeping route.
- Monthly sweeping of non-beachfront residential areas, paved alleys, and parking facilities.

A Street Sweeping Schedule (PDF) is linked on the page. The County's Sheriff's Department enforces the street sweeper public parking regulations.

Source: https://www.imperialbeachca.gov/233/Street-Sweeping

## Report an Issue

The Report an Issue system is for non-emergency issues and is not monitored 24/7. Report any emergency to 911. If a concern needs immediate attention by the Police Department, call the non-emergency Dispatch Center (858) 868-3200. The system does not generate Police Reports. For potential Code Violations, a current picture (less than a week old) that clearly shows the issue is extremely helpful. Allow City staff 1-3 business days to respond and/or address issues reported. All complainant information is confidential and will not be disclosed by the City unless required by a court order or subpoena. Start by choosing a category on the page or by downloading the MyImperialBeach app.

Source: https://www.imperialbeachca.gov/647/Report-an-Issue

## Leash Laws

It is illegal to walk your dog off leash unless you are in a designated off-leash area. Dogs are required to be restrained at all times. Away from home, a dog must be on leash unless in a designated off-leash area, like a beach or park. At home, a dog must be restrained by a fence or other containment system. Violators may be cited under Imperial Beach Municipal Code Section 6.04.060.

Source: https://www.imperialbeachca.gov/461/Animal-Control
