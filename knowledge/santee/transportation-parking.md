## Parking Citations — How to Pay

Parking citations issued in Santee by the San Diego County Sheriff's Office can be paid online, by mail, or in person at the City's Finance Department, located in Building 3. Online payments are made at PayMyCite and are subject to a $5.95 convenience fee.

To pay by mail, send the original citation to City of Santee c/o Parking Citation Services, P.O. Box 11923, Santa Ana, CA 92711. You can also mail the original citation with a check or money order to City of Santee, Attn: Finance Department, 10601 Magnolia Ave, Santee, CA 92071. To pay in person, go to the Finance Department at 10601 Magnolia Avenue, Santee, CA 92071.

Make checks or money orders payable to the "City of Santee" and write the citation number on them. The citation number is in the upper left hand corner on the front of the citation. DO NOT MAIL CASH!

Finance Department: 619-258-4100 ext. 146. Hours: M–Th 8:00am–5:00pm, F 8:00am-1:00pm. Santee City Hall, Finance Department, 10601 Magnolia Avenue, Building 3, Santee, CA 92071.

## Parking Citations — Payment Plan

CVC 40220 allows installment payments for qualifying low-income individuals who apply within 120 days from the issuance of a notice of parking violation, or within 10 days after an administrative hearing determination, whichever is later. A payment plan application form is required. For more information, including how to apply and whether you qualify, call 1-866-353-5867 or visit PayMyCite.

## Parking Citations — How to Contest

Contest the violation by completing a Request for Administrative Review of Notice of Parking Violation within 21 days of the citation issue date or 14 days from delinquent parking notice. Provide any supporting documentation, such as pictures. Original pictures that are mailed will not be returned, so keep copies for your records.

Submit documents in person, by e-mail or by mail to City of Santee, Attn: Finance Department, 10601 Magnolia Avenue, Santee, CA 92071.

## Parking Citations — Handicap Placard Not Displayed

If you received a parking citation for not displaying your handicap placard, request dismissal of the citation by completing the Request for Dismissal Form within 21 days of the citation. Provide the original parking citation, a copy of the disabled placard, and a copy of the disabled placard registration (not vehicle registration).

Submit documents in person, by email, or by mail to City of Santee, 10601 Magnolia Avenue, Santee, CA 92071, Attention: Finance Department.

## Residential Street Sweeping

All residential areas are swept twice a month. Sweeping occurs between 7:00 am and 6:00 pm. Do not park your car on the street between 7:00 am and 6:00 pm on scheduled sweeping days. Do not place any lumber, bricks, basketball hoops or other foreign objects in the gutter or the street. Move any parked vehicle at least every 72 hours (per the vehicle code).

Trash collection is contracted with Waste Management, which has different routes and schedules than street sweeping. Occasionally, delays in trash pick up may mean trash cans need to be removed to allow the street sweeper to pass.

## Report Traffic, Streetlight or Traffic Signal Problems

Use the Santee Portal to report a problem involving streetlights, traffic signals, traffic signs, pavement markings, curb painting, speeding or other traffic safety concerns. When reporting, provide these details:
- Define the problem (e.g. traffic signal malfunction or timing issues, need for traffic signs, pavement marking requests). For street lights, say if a light is out, cycling on and off, or staying on all day.
- Note the time and day when the problem was observed.
- Identify the location, including street name and nearest cross street or address.
- Provide your name (including spelling) and daytime phone number.

City staff will conduct a field investigation and make adjustments and/or issue work orders as appropriate. You will be notified about the results of the investigation.

## Traffic Engineering Division — Services and Contact

The Traffic Engineering Division oversees traffic signal management (design, operations, and timing), streetlights, traffic signs, establishment of speed limits, roadway striping/markings and curb painting, traffic data collection (volumes, collision analysis, vehicle speeds), processing traffic control plans for construction activities within the right-of-way, review of private development projects, truck routes and moving/haul permits, and Capital Improvement Projects. It also responds to traffic-related public complaints and requests.

## Public Transit and Getting Around Santee

Santee has four local bus routes, including commuter express bus service to the Kearny Mesa area. MTS ACCESS provides disabled transportation services. Santee Transit Center provides multi-modal transit operations, with Trolley service to SDSU, Mission Valley & Old Town (Copper Line). Santee's trolley station is on MTS's Copper Line, which replaced the Green Line Trolley on September 29. Metropolitan Transit System (MTS) also provides bus services for the San Diego County region. Uber, Lyft, and other ridesharing services are available in Santee.

Santee has direct freeway access to SR-67, SR-52, and SR-125. Regional access to I-8 is via SR-67 and SR-125. Access to I-15, I-163, I-805, and the La Jolla area is via SR-52.

## Active Santee Plan (Bicycle & Pedestrian)

The Active Santee Plan (ASP) is the City of Santee's Active Transportation Plan. It focuses on enhancing the safety and comfort of existing pedestrian and bicycle facilities, identifying needed improvements to the system, and increasing connectivity to key attracting land uses such as schools, employment centers, retail districts, and recreational areas.
