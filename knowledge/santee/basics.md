## City Council Meetings — Schedule and Location

City Council Regular Meetings are held on the 2nd and 4th Wednesdays in January through October and on the second Wednesday in November and December, beginning at 6:30 p.m. Meetings are held in the City Council Chambers at Santee City Hall, 10601 Magnolia Avenue. Meetings are open for in-person attendance or viewing online on SanteeTV. Copies of the agenda are available at each meeting and are posted on the City Council Meetings webpage and at the City Clerk's Office, 10601 Magnolia Avenue. To get notifications when agendas are published, sign up for e-notifications and select either "All Meetings" or "City Council Meetings."

## City Council Meetings — Public Comment

In-person: submit a speaker slip to the City Clerk before the item is called. Your name will be called when it is time to speak.

Written: email publiccomment@cityofsanteeca.gov with "Public Comment" and the Agenda item number(s) in the subject line. Written comments received before 3:00 p.m. the day of the meeting are distributed to the City Council, posted on the City website, printed for the physical Agenda packet and become part of the administrative record. Written comments will not be read out loud during the meeting. Comments received after 3:00 p.m. but before the start of the meeting will be processed the same way if staff time allows.

Telephonic: telephonic public comment begins July 1, 2026. Email publiccomment@cityofsanteeca.gov before the item is called, with "Telephonic Comment" in the subject line. In the body, give the last four (4) digits of your call-in number, the Agenda item number(s) you wish to speak on, and, if desired, a name to be identified as. The call-in phone number and instructions are listed on each meeting's Agenda.

Speakers are generally permitted three minutes. Under City Council Policy 2026-01, the Mayor may reduce the three-minute limit when numerous comments are expected, and any reduction will be announced before public comment begins. Public/applicant exhibits become City property and are not returnable.

## City Government Structure

Santee operates under a Council-Manager form of government. The City Council appoints a professional City Manager, who coordinates the day-to-day operations and administration of the City. Elections are held in November of even numbered years; terms are staggered. The Council also appoints members to Santee citizen advisory boards, committees, and commissions.

## Boards, Commissions and Committees — List

- Community Oriented Policing Committee (COMPOC): established 8/14/96; makes recommendations regarding the City's Community Oriented Policing Program.
- Salary Setting Advisory Committee: established 1/28/09; makes recommendations to the Council on salary and benefits for the Mayor and City Council.
- Santee Manufactured Home Fair Practices Commission: staff and the Commission seek to prevent excess increases of manufactured home space rents to park residents while providing a fair rate of return to park owners.
- Santee Park and Recreation Committee (SPARC): established by Resolution 75-81 on 9/28/81; advises the City Council on municipal parks and recreation programs.
- City Council & Santee Elementary School District Conference Committee: meets with School Board Members to discuss selected items.
- CDC Successor Agency: ensures the timely and orderly wind down of the former CDC, including payment of outstanding debts.
- Highway 52 Coalition.

## Elections — Council Districts and Voting

Santee is a Charter City governed by a five-member City Council. Since 2018, the four Councilmembers are elected by district and must live in the district they represent. The Mayor is elected at-large by all registered voters in Santee. To find your district, use the interactive map or the adopted map on the City Clerk's page. To locate your polling place, visit the Registrar of Voters. Election results can be viewed on the Registrar of Voters website.

Source: https://www.cityofsanteeca.gov/departments/city-clerk
