## Building Permits: Types and Submittal

All building permit submittals must be done through the Santee Portal (Permitting & Licensing Portal). Paper plans are no longer accepted. The City of Santee is not currently offering expedited review programs.

Permit types listed by the City:
- **Accessory Dwelling Unit (ADU):** an additional dwelling unit on a property with an existing main residence, including attached, detached, and junior ADUs.
- **Commercial Alteration:** a tenant improvement and/or an addition to an existing commercial space. Includes demolition, accessory structures (sheds/garages), electrical vehicle charging stations, pool/spa, reroofs, signs, TI's, and modifications to wireless communication sites.
- **Commercial New:** a new commercial use building. Includes industrial buildings, restaurants, care facilities, shell buildings, storage facilities, and new wireless communication sites.
- **Electrical:** any electrical work outside of the replacement of fixtures in already approved receptacles. Includes any new circuits, wiring, and modifications to existing or new electrical panels.
- **Mechanical:** any mechanical system that provides heating or cooling and the associated duct work, including A/C, FAU's, wall heaters, and mini-splits.
- **Plumbing:** any new piping, modification to existing piping, or repair of piping. Includes gas/sewer/water systems such as water heaters (tank or tankless), purification systems, water softeners, and sewer cleanouts.
- **Residential Alteration:** an alteration and/or addition to an existing residential space. Includes interior remodels, additions, accessory structures (shed, garages), patio covers, decks, pool/spa, electrical vehicle chargers, foundation repairs, reroofs, siding, and windows.
- **Residential New:** new residential dwellings. Includes attached/detached single family dwellings, master planned communities, and multifamily high/low rise apartment buildings.
- **Solar/Photovoltaic Systems and Batteries:** residential and commercial photovoltaic systems and batteries.
- **SolarAPP Solar/Photovoltaic Systems:** State of California permitting program for simple residential roof mounted photovoltaic systems. May include batteries and a main panel upgrade (if included, a SDG&E work order is required).

## Encroachment Permits (Work in the Right-of-Way)

An Encroachment Permit from the Engineering Department is required for any work taking place within the City of Santee's right-of-way. This includes: work within the street, sidewalk or right-of-way; replacement, repair, or installation of a new driveway; sidewalk under-drains and drain lines within the curb (curb core); street or sidewalk closures or any work requiring lane closures; utilities; and temporary dumpsters provided by Waste Management to be located along curbs. The contractor working in the public right-of-way shall hold a State of California issued General Engineering Contractor (Class A) license and meet the City of Santee insurance requirements.

Encroachment Permits shall be submitted digitally on Santee's Permitting & Licensing Portal. Upon receipt of the application, staff will follow up with the amounts of the required fees/deposits and available payment methods. Please allow 10 to 15 working days for processing. The fee can be calculated using the current Fee Schedule. Payment is accepted by cash, check or credit card.

## Moving & Oversized Load Permits

Moving permits are required on all loads exceeding 12 feet in height, 8 feet 6 inches in width, or 65 feet in length. Permits are vehicle specific, and the original shall be carried in the vehicle to which it refers at all times while the vehicle is being moved within the City limits. No movement shall be made between the hours of 7:00-8:30 am and 3:30-6:00 pm. A copy of the City of Santee Moving Permit Regulations can be viewed at the Engineering Department front counter or downloaded.

Pilot cars: one car is required for loads greater than 12' 6" in width. Two cars are required for loads greater than 14' 0" in width, or loads greater than 90' 0" in length.

Route evaluation by the Traffic Engineer (additional charges for staff time apply) is required for loads greater than 15' 0" in width, loads greater than 15' 0" in height, loads greater than 110' 0" in length, or loads greater than 60,000 lbs/axle (purple weight).

Insurance: no fax copies of the Certificate of Insurance will be accepted. It must include the clause: "Should any of the above described policies be cancelled before the expiration date thereof, the issuing company will mail 30 days written notice to the certificate holder named to the left." Minimum commercial general liability insurance is $2,000,000/occurrence and $4,000,000/aggregate, and the City of Santee must be specifically named on a separate insurance endorsement page. Minimum automotive liability insurance is $1,000,000.

Applications may be submitted electronically at SanteePortal.org, and invoices are also paid on the Portal. Allow 10 working days for processing. For fees, refer to the posted fee schedule for the current Fiscal Year. Payment is accepted by cash, check or credit card.

## Business License: How to Apply

Anyone who conducts business within the city is required to have a city business license. This includes businesses that are based or operate within the city and businesses based in other cities that enter Santee to sell goods or services. Anyone who uses a residential location as a place of business needs a home occupation license.

A City business license application is required for a new business. The application fee is $108, which includes a $4 State fee for disability access (AB-1379). Apply online on Santee's Permitting & Licensing Portal. Allow 2 weeks for processing if your business is based in the City. Businesses based outside of the City can be approved upon receipt of a complete application and fee. Business should not be conducted without an approved license. Before purchasing or leasing a business location, confirm that the business will comply with City Zoning and Land Use Regulations.

## Business License: Renewals, Late Fees and Changes

Business licenses expire one (1) year from issuance. Renewal notices are emailed on an on-going basis. Renew online on Santee's Permitting & Licensing Portal. The annual renewal fee is currently $42, which includes a $4 State fee for disability access (AB-1379). Renewals are due on or before the expiration date. There is a 30 day grace period. Penalties are assessed each month the license is not renewed, currently at $17.50 per month. Businesses located out of the city are exempt from late fees.

A business license application is required for a change of address or a change of business name. There is currently a fee of $48, only for a change of location for business licenses located in the City, which follows the same approval process as a new business license. Licenses are not transferable. Changes to ownership require a new business license application and the old account will be closed out.

To get a copy of a misplaced license certificate, log into your account on Santee's Permitting & Licensing Portal.

For questions or to search existing businesses in the City: email Tanya Espinola, Account Clerk at tespinola@cityofsanteeca.gov or call 619-258-4100 ext.146.
