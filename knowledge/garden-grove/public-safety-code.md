## CALEA Accreditation and Public Comment

The Garden Grove Police Department is nationally accredited through the Commission on Accreditation for Law Enforcement Agencies (CALEA®). In 1988 it was the first California agency to be accredited by CALEA®. It received its 11th accreditation in July 2024.

Employees, community members and the public may offer commendations or concerns through the "CALEA Public Comment" portal at https://cimrs2.calea.org/982. CALEA® is not an investigatory body, so the portal should not be used to submit information for investigations. Submissions receive only an acknowledgement.

For questions about the agency, standards or process, contact Lieutenant Kory Ferrin, Accreditation Manager, at (714) 741-5427.

## Police Communications — 9-1-1 and Business Calls

The Communications Division is the primary answer point for all 9-1-1 emergency and business calls to the police department. It operates 24 hours a day, 7 days a week.

Situations that require an emergency response include crimes in progress, injury traffic collisions, life-threatening medical situations and other circumstances where police are needed immediately. Non-emergency calls are prioritized based on the dispatcher's conversation with the caller.

## Civilian Report Writing Unit

The Civilian Report Writing Unit takes reports on incidents that do not necessarily require a police officer to be present. It is staffed primarily by Community Service Officers and Cadets.

Field Report Writers are Community Service Officers who work in the field. They mostly take misdemeanor crime reports but can also handle certain felony reports. Expediters are Police Cadets who take reports by telephone. These are generally misdemeanor offenses and supplemental reports, though some stolen vehicle, burglary and grand theft reports are also taken.

## Drones — Registration and Rules

Federal law requires drones weighing more than 0.55 pounds that are flown outdoors to be registered with the FAA and marked with an FAA-issued registration number. Register at https://registermyuas.faa.gov/. The operator must have the FAA registration certificate in their possession. Drones flown indoors do not need to be registered. Drones weighing more than 0.55 pounds must be registered when flown in public, in your own yard or over your property.

The City regulates drones under Garden Grove Municipal Code, Chapter 8.90, Drones and Unmanned Aircraft Systems, Ordinance No. 2879.

For questions, contact Corporal Pete Arellano at 714-741-5857 or pedroa@ggcity.org. To report an emergency related to drone operation, contact Garden Grove Police Dispatch at 911 or 714-741-5704.

## Code Enforcement — Enforcement Methods and Fines

Code Enforcement aims for voluntary compliance through education. If that is not achieved, officers proceed as follows:

1. **Notices.** A confirmed violation results in a Notice of Violation to the responsible party, with a time frame to address it. A second notice may be issued if there is no compliance.
2. **Administrative Citation.** After the notification process is exhausted, administrative fines may be issued: $100 for the first offense, $200 for the second offense and $500 for any subsequent offense. Fines may be issued for EACH code violation. Re-inspection invoices for cost recovery may add $70 for the first inspection and $100 for all subsequent inspections.
3. **Judicial Remedies.** The City may request these through the City Attorney's Office: criminal prosecution, civil injunction or receivership.
4. **Abatement.** The City may hire a private contractor to remove violations, clean up a property and/or demolish a property deemed to be a public nuisance.

## Permit for Banners, Balloons and Outside Merchandise Display

Permit fees per calendar year: Special Event Sales/Grand Openings $35.00, Non-profit Organizations $10.00, Center wide Sales $60.00. Banners, flags, pennants, valances and balloons, and outside display of merchandise, require a special event or grand opening permit.

- Grand opening sales for new businesses are permitted for thirty (30) days, taken consecutively, within the first year of business. New ownership or management change does not constitute a new business for a grand opening permit.
- Existing businesses may have a special event permit good for a maximum of sixty (60) days per calendar year for temporary advertising devices, and eight (8) days per calendar year for sidewalk sales and other outdoor events.
- Display days do not have to be consecutive. Businesses must contact Code Enforcement at (714) 741-5358 to report the remaining days.
- Sales must be an extension of an established business and conducted on its site. The sales area must not obstruct pedestrian traffic, vehicle traffic or emergency vehicles.
- No off-site signs are permitted. A-frame or portable signs are not allowed.
- The sales area must be cleared of all banners, balloons, merchandise, equipment and trash by 10:00 AM on the day immediately following the end of the sale.

Get an application at the permit counter at Garden Grove City Hall, 11222 Acacia Parkway, Garden Grove, CA 92840.

## Code Enforcement Volunteer Program

Code Volunteers support Code Enforcement Officers with in-field observations and documentation. Volunteers are not asked to inspect their own neighborhood, and no interior inspections or public contacts are required. Areas covered:
- excessive garage sales
- vehicles parked on unpaved areas
- commercial and oversize vehicles parked in residential neighborhoods
- auto repair and unpermitted business activities in neighborhoods
- improperly stored trash cans
- removal of illegal signs as directed
- special projects, as assigned

Volunteers donate four to eight hours per month, primarily on Saturday or Sunday. Requirements are a high school diploma or equivalency and a California driver's license. Applicants undergo a reference check and screening through the Department of Motor Vehicles. The City provides a marked City vehicle, uniform shirt and supplies at no cost to the volunteer, plus training. Selected applicants take a medical evaluation at City expense.

Contact Garden Grove Code Enforcement at (714) 741-5358 or codeenforcement@ggcity.org.
