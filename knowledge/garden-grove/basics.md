## City Manager — Lisa Kim

Lisa Kim is the Garden Grove City Manager. She was appointed on March 1, 2023, and is the first female, Asian-American to lead City operations. She joined the City in April 2016 as the Community and Economic Development Director (date hired: April 4, 2016). From 2019 she was also assistant city manager, overseeing the Office of Economic Development, Planning Services, and the Building and Safety Division.

Her work includes the 2021 Comprehensive Plan to Address Homelessness, which included the launch of the Be Well OC in Garden Grove Mobile Response Team, a street medicine initiative in the works with CalOptima, and the future Central Cities Navigation Center. She also implemented the Garden Grove Business Resource and Resiliency Plan during COVID-19. She initiated community engagement on the proposed relocation of the Garden Grove Police Department headquarters in the Garden Grove Civic Center.

## Assistant City Manager — Matt West

Matt West began as Garden Grove Assistant City Manager on April 1, 2024. He had served as Interim Assistant City Manager since January 2024. His responsibilities include overseeing the development of Orange County's first police canine regional training facility at Pioneer Park.

Before Garden Grove, he spent 22 years with the City of Tustin, where he rose to deputy city manager, assistant city manager, and city manager. As Tustin city manager he served as Executive Director for the development of Tustin Legacy on the former Marine Corps Air Station Tustin. His earlier public service included the City of Upland and Mono County.

Source: https://ggcity.org/city-manager/matt-west
