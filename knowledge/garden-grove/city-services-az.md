## Trash Rate Increase — Effective July 1, 2026

Garden Grove's trash rates increase above the normal annual CPI adjustment starting July 1, 2026. The increase is driven primarily by a new long-term landfill disposal agreement with the County of Orange (the WISE Agreement), not by a change in Republic Services' service fees. The County landfill disposal rate rises from $43.76/ton to $67.00/ton on July 1, 2026 (+53%), then to $74.00 in 2027-28 and $81.00 in 2028-29. The collection service component can adjust up to 5% per year under the agreement with Republic Services.

Monthly rates before and after July 1, 2026:
- Standard residential (35, 64, or 96-gal cart): $29.74 to $33.75 (+$4.01)
- Disabled / low-income senior 65+ (64 or 96-gal): $23.08 to $26.76 (+$3.68)
- Commercial 3-CY bin, 3× per week: $519.49 to $590.59 (+$71.10)

Commercial rates vary by bin size and pickup frequency. Recycling cart and organics/green waste cart rates are not subject to the landfill surcharge. For questions about the disposal portion of the rates, contact OC Waste & Recycling at (714) 834-4000 or info@ocwr.ocgov.com.

## Recycle Garden Grove — Residential Containers

Recycle Garden Grove is a joint program of the Garden Grove Sanitary District and Republic Services. It uses three 110-gallon containers: a green container for recyclable items, a brown container for yard waste, and a black container for trash. The black, green, and brown containers are collected on the same day each week. Waste placed outside the special containers, or in non-automated trash cans, cannot be collected with the automated service.

Source: https://ggcity.org/pw/recycle

## What Goes in Each Container

- **Green (recyclables):** keep recyclables Empty-Clean-Dry. Accepted items include aerosol cans, aluminum and tin cans, cardboard, glass bottles and jars (lids off), newspapers, magazines, and plastic bottles and containers. Food and liquid waste, trash, yard waste, and hazardous waste are not accepted.
- **Brown (yard waste):** accepted items are grass and leaves (not bagged), prunings and weeds, twigs and small branches (4 inches or less in diameter and 2 feet or less in length), and vegetation from the yard. Palm fronds, plastic bags, dirt and rocks, and food waste are not accepted. Do not use plastic trash bags for yard waste.
- **Black (trash):** accepted items include animal and food waste, cat litter, disposable diapers, and old clothes and shoes. Concrete, construction debris, dirt and rocks, furniture, large appliances, recyclables, yard waste, and hazardous waste are not accepted.

Source: https://ggcity.org/pw/recycle

## Used Motor Oil and Filter Pickup

Dumping used motor oil and filters is illegal in California. Residents can take used oil to a State Certified Collection Center or use the City's free Residential Collection Program. To schedule a pickup, call (800) 449-7587. The collection and the phone call are free. For more information, call the City Used Motor Oil Recycled Administrator at (714) 741-5554.

To participate:
- Drain oil into a clean, sealed plastic container not larger than 5 gallons, and not more than 20 gallons in total.
- Place oil filters in a sealed plastic bag.
- Call the 800 number to schedule a collection.
- On the collection day, place the items by your front door or on your driveway as directed.
- The crew arrives between 8 a.m. and 5 p.m.

The program accepts only used motor oil, oil filters, and plastic motor oil bottles. For more than 20 gallons, call (800) 449-7587; minimum handling and testing fees may apply.

Source: https://ggcity.org/pw/recycle

## Household Hazardous Waste

Household hazardous waste should be disposed of at an Orange County Collection Center. There are four collection centers for Garden Grove Sanitary District customers. For information, call the O.C. Household Hazardous Waste Collection Hotline at (714) 834-6752 or visit www.oclandfills.com.

Accepted materials include:
- Oil/latex paint and paint products
- Automotive products (batteries, motor oil, antifreeze, etc.)
- Auto/furniture polish
- Household cleaners
- Wood preservatives
- Pesticides and herbicides
- Hobby and pool supplies
- Empty compressed gas cylinders
- Unused road flares
- Smoke detectors
- Household batteries
- Fluorescent light ballast

Materials not accepted: ammunition, asbestos, biological, business-generated, explosives, radioactive, and compressed gas cylinders.

Battery Beware: don't recycle or trash lithium batteries at home. They are found in cell phones, power tools, laptops, tablets, children's toys, scooters, electric toothbrushes and more.

## Mandatory Commercial Recycling (AB 341)

State law requires the following to arrange for recycling services:
- Any business that generates 4 cubic yards or more of trash per week
- Multi-family communities with five or more units

Contact Republic's customer service at (714) 238-3300 to schedule a free audit and site check to determine which recycling containers will work best. Republic offers 64-gallon carts or 3 cubic yard bins for source-separated recycling. Businesses and multi-family communities may also donate or sell recyclable materials, including by self-hauling them to recycling facilities. More stringent requirements apply under SB 1383; see ggcity.org/organics.

Source: https://ggcity.org/environmental-compliance/mandatory-commercial-recycling

## Mandatory Commercial Organics Recycling (AB 1826)

AB 1826 requires businesses and multi-family premises to divert organic waste from landfills. Multi-family premises with 5 units or more are included if they generate the threshold amount of waste, but they are required to recycle only landscaping/green waste, not food waste. Deadlines:
- April 1, 2016: businesses with 8 cubic yards of organic waste per week
- January 1, 2017: businesses with 4 cubic yards of organic waste per week
- January 1, 2019: businesses with 4 cubic yards of commercial solid waste per week
- January 1, 2020: businesses with 2 cubic yards of commercial solid waste per week (applicable only if CalRecycle determines by January 1, 2020 that statewide organic waste disposal has not been reduced to 50% of the 2014 level)

To find out if your business qualifies for the City and Republic Services source-separated organics program, call Republic Services at (714) 238-3300. Container sizes are a 64-gallon cart or a 2 cubic yard bin. Multi-family complexes that already divert green waste through a landscape service should report this to Republic Services at (714) 238-3300 and be prepared to supply the landscaper's information and, if possible, the receiving facility.

Source: https://ggcity.org/environmental-compliance/mandatory-commercial-recycling

## Street Sweeping

The Parking Enforcement and Street Sweeping Program is managed by the Garden Grove Police Department. Follow all posted street signs for sweeping days and times. Sweeping and enforcement take place RAIN OR SHINE; weather cancellations are posted on the page. Questions: Parking Enforcement and Street Sweeping mainline at (714) 741-5382.

On the posted sweeping day, keep leaves, grass cuttings, trash containers, vehicles, and excess water out of the street from 8 a.m. to 4 p.m. in residential areas and from 5 a.m. to 9 a.m. in other areas.

Streets are not swept on these City-observed holidays: New Years Day, Martin Luther King Jr. Day, Presidents Day, Memorial Day, Fourth of July, Labor Day, Veterans Day, Thanksgiving Day, and Christmas Day. Per the 12/23/2025 update, sweeping was suspended December 24 and 25 and resumed December 26, and was not conducted January 1 and resumed January 2.

Residents are asked to:
- Reset lawn sprinklers for a day other than the sweeping day.
- Avoid raking and power blowing waste into the street.
- Remove trash barrels from the street and/or alleyway on the night of trash collection day.
- Remove basketball hoops from the street or public right-of-way.
- Trim shrubs and trees back to the curb.

Source: https://ggcity.org/street-sweeping
