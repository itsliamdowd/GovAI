## Parking Enforcement and Street Sweeping Program

The Parking Enforcement and Street Sweeping Program is now managed by the Garden Grove Police Department. Program operations and schedules remain unchanged. Residents should continue to follow all posted street signs regarding street sweeping days and times. The Parking Enforcement and Street Sweeping mainline is (714) 741-5382.

The City of Garden Grove sweeps approximately 1500 miles of curb and gutter, preventing an average of 220 tons of debris from entering storm drains monthly. The page also provides a Street Sweeping Map and is available in Spanish and Vietnamese.

## Street Sweeping — What Residents Should Keep Out of the Street

To help the program, keep leaves, grass cuttings, trash containers, vehicles, and excess water out of the street from 8 a.m. to 4 p.m. in residential areas and from 5 a.m. to 9 a.m. in other areas on posted sweeping day.

The City also asks residents to:
- Reset all lawn sprinklers for a day other than the scheduled sweeping day.
- Avoid raking and power blowing waste into the street.
- Remove trash barrels from the street and/or alleyway on the night of trash collection day.
- Remove basketball hoops from the street or public right-of-way.
- Trim shrub(s) and/or tree(s) to back of curb.

Questions: (714) 741-5382.

## Street Sweeping — Holidays and Service Suspensions

Streets will not be swept on these City-observed holidays: New Years Day, Martin Luther King Jr. Day, Presidents Day, Memorial Day, Fourth of July, Labor Day, Veterans Day, Thanksgiving Day, and Christmas Day.

Holiday update posted 12/23/2025: street sweeping services are suspended on December 24 and December 25, and regular operations resume on December 26. Street sweeping will not be conducted on January 1, and services resume on January 2.

## Streets and Street Trees Division

The Streets and Street Trees Division is primarily responsible for maintaining the City's streets, sidewalks, storm drains/flood control channels and 20,000 street trees. It is also responsible for street sweeping, flood control inspection and repair, graffiti removal, hazardous spills response, shopping cart abatement, stormwater pollution prevention, and solid waste management.

The Division maintains 305 miles of streets, alleys, and parking lots. This includes replacement of asphalt, preparation of streets for overlay and slurry seal projects, patching and paving for winterization, and crack sealing. It also maintains 418 miles of sidewalk, 647 miles of curb/gutter, and five miles of block wall. This includes removal and repair of damaged concrete, pouring new concrete, root pruning to prevent damage to concrete, block wall building and repair, and sprinkler repair.

Because of recent reductions in State revenue to the City, the Public Works Department has reduced its services by 10 percent. This resulted in the elimination of staffing in the Street, Trees, and Concrete Sections of the Division, which affects how and to what extent the City's urban forest and concrete network of curbs, gutters and sidewalks are managed. For more information, call (714) 741-5375.

## OC Streetcar

In 2008, the cities of Garden Grove and Santa Ana began a partnership with the Orange County Transportation Authority (OCTA) to develop a last-mile connection from Garden Grove to the Santa Ana Regional Transportation Center. On September 22, 2015, City Council entered into a Memorandum of Understanding (MOU) with OCTA, the lead agency for the project, for design, construction, operations and maintenance.

OCTA is the lead agency for outreach, design, implementation, operations and maintenance of the project. For current project status and updates, visit OCTA's OC Streetcar Website.
