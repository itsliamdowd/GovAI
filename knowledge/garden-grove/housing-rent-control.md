## Housing Authority — Housing Choice Voucher (Section 8) Program and Applications

The Garden Grove Housing Authority (GGHA) operates under federal grants from the Department of Housing and Urban Development to provide rental subsidies to low-income families. The program assists over 2,300 families with their monthly rent. Families qualify based on their income. Once eligibility is determined, families are authorized to seek privately owned rental units. Because of a long waiting list, the Housing Authority accepts new applications only periodically, and a public announcement is made when the program is open. The Housing Authority is currently NOT accepting new applications.

The Project-Based Voucher (PBV) Program will accept registrations beginning October 5, 2026, at 8:00 a.m. through October 19, 2026, at 5:00 p.m., or until a sufficient number of registrations have been received, whichever occurs first. Mainstream vouchers are tenant-based vouchers that assist non-elderly persons with disabilities and their families. They follow the same regulations and policies as the regular HCV program.

Effective September 25, 2023, the Housing Authority address is 11277 Garden Grove Blvd., Suite 100, Garden Grove, CA 92843. Phone: (714) 741-5150.

## Housing Authority — Responsibilities of Current Section 8 Tenants

HUD requires certain things of Section 8 rental assistance recipients. Participation and housing assistance may be terminated if the requirements are not met. Changes in family income (increases or decreases), sources of income, family composition (addition or deletion of family members) or student status for dependents over eighteen must be reported within 3 weeks using the Change Report Form with supporting documents. Failure to report changes constitutes fraud and is grounds for termination of assistance as well as criminal prosecution.

Tenants must give a Notice to Vacate to their owner before moving if the lease allows. They must notify the Housing Authority before vacating the rental unit, and give the Housing Authority a copy of any move notice from the owner.

## Housing Authority — Tenant Rights During Foreclosure (Section 8 Voucher Holders)

Under the federal Protecting Tenants at Foreclosure Act (Pub. L. No. 111-22, § 702 (2009)), Section 8 voucher tenants in foreclosed units have these rights:
- A tenant with an unexpired fixed-term lease (such as one year) may remain until the end of the lease term and cannot be evicted, except for actions by the tenant, family members or guests that constitute good cause.
- If the lease ends in less than 90 days, the new owner may not evict without at least 90 days' notice.
- The new owner wanting the property vacant before selling it is not good cause for termination or eviction.
- Exception: if the new owner wants to occupy the unit as a primary residence, the owner may give a 90 day notice to vacate even if the lease runs longer than 90 days.

If the new owner tells you to leave, offers you money to leave or gives you an eviction notice, contact your worker at the Garden Grove Housing Authority and give a copy of any notice. You may also contact the Fair Housing Foundation, toll free, at (800) 446-3247.

## Housing Authority — Request for Tenancy Approval (RTA) for Owners

The complete RTA packet includes eight forms. Five are posted online: W-9 Form; Lead-based Paint Disclosure Form (for units built before 1978 only, and only if the unit lacks a lead-based paint inspector's certification); Declaration of Ownership and Authorization Form; Tenant Provided Refrigerator Certification; and Unit Amenity Information (2024). Before executing the HAP contract, the owner must inform GGHA and the family of any known lead-based paint or lead-based paint hazards on the premises.

The other three documents are: the Request for Tenancy Approval (HUD form 52517), issued by the Garden Grove Housing Authority (contact the tenant or housing specialist); Proof of Ownership (a copy of a recorded Grant Deed, provided by the landlord); and a copy of a 12 months term lease that conforms to state/federal laws (provided by the landlord).

## Housing Authority — Payment Standards, VAWA Protections and Portability

The payment standard sets the maximum monthly subsidy a family can receive from the Public Housing Agency. Payment standards are based on fair market rents (FMRs) published annually by HUD, and the schedule is used to calculate housing assistance payments for Housing Choice Voucher families.

The Violence Against Women Act (VAWA) provides housing protections for people applying for or living in federally subsidized units who have experienced domestic violence, dating violence, sexual assault, or stalking. The page provides the Notice of Occupancy Rights Under the Violence Against Women Act (Form HUD-5380) and the Certification of Domestic Violence, Dating Violence, Sexual Assault, or Stalking (Form HUD-5382).

If an applicant is qualified for housing assistance as a resident, the family will be permitted to relocate outside of GGHA's jurisdiction.

## Substandard Housing — Reporting and Enforcement

Code Enforcement in the Building and Safety Division works to bring substandard housing into compliance with State and City codes. Officers enforce the 2021 International Property Maintenance Code and the 2022 California codes. When a complaint is filed, an officer investigates, prepares a "Notice of Violations" and directs repairs or demolition. The time this takes varies with the complexity of the case. If a structure is in imminent danger of causing bodily harm, a "Notice and Order" may be issued. Remedies may include posting the structure "Vacate, Unsafe to Occupy" and/or recording a "Notice of substandard building" (Lien) on the property title.

To report construction without permits, garage conversions, and substandard housing conditions, call (714) 741-5350 to leave a voicemail, email codeenforcement@ggcity.org, use the Garden Grove Public Records Request, or report an issue online.

Source: https://ggcity.org/building-and-safety/substandard-housing

## Short-Term Vacation Rentals — Penalties and Affidavit

Short-term vacation rentals are the same as hotels or motels, defined as places rented to transients for a term of less than 30 consecutive days. They are allowed only in commercial zones with a conditional use permit. Renting out a residence in a residential zone for less than 30 days is punishable as a misdemeanor, with fines up to $1,000 per day per violation, six months in jail, or both. Each day a violation exists is a separate offense. Code sections: 9.32.020(E)(1) for single-family and multi-family residential, with 9.08.020.040(A)(4) and 9.08.020.030 (single-family) and 9.12.020.040(A)(4) and 9.12.020.030 (multi-family).

To report a location, complete the AFFIDAVIT FOR VACATION OR SHORT-TERM RENTAL CITATION and hand-deliver or mail it with supporting evidence to: City of Garden Grove, Attn: Code Enforcement, 11222 Acacia Parkway, Garden Grove, CA 92840. For more information, call Code Enforcement at (714) 741-5351.
