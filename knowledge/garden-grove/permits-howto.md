## Building and Safety — Permit Counter and Submitting Applications

The Building & Safety Division applies and administers State and local building regulations for the design and construction of all buildings and structures in Garden Grove. Permit Counter: (714) 741-5307. Email: building@ggcity.org. All plan checks and permit issuance will be accepted until 4:00 pm Monday through Friday.

New applications for commercial, residential, engineering and other permits are submitted through the Development Access Website. The Online Building Permit Application is used to apply for a building permit, find permits and schedule inspections. Online tools on the page also include Project Status (search by plan check #), Records Request (archived permits for a Garden Grove property) and Mapping (the City's GIS map for Permits, Inspections, and Violations).

Effective July 1, 2025, the Community Development Department's updated planning and building fees apply to all permit applications submitted on or after that date.

## Construction Hours

Construction is permitted between 7:00 a.m. and 7:00 p.m. Monday through Friday, and between 9:00 a.m. and 6:00 p.m. on Saturdays. No construction or repair work is permitted on Sundays or federal holidays.

## Plan Check Extension

An application for a permit for any proposed work is deemed abandoned 180 days after the date of filing, unless the application has been pursued in good faith or a permit has been issued. The Building Official may grant one or more extensions of time for additional periods not exceeding 90 days each. The extension must be requested in writing and justifiable cause must be demonstrated.

Source: https://ggcity.org/building-and-safety/plan-check-extension

## Accessory Dwelling Units (ADU and JADU)

The Community Development Department's ADU Program helps residents, contractors, architects and developers understand how to build an accessory dwelling unit (ADU) or junior accessory dwelling unit (JADU) and walks through the steps of processing an application. The City's Pre-Approved ADU Program is called ADU Go.

ADU handouts are available in English, Vietnamese, Spanish and Korean, for Single-Family Residential (New ADU, ADU Conversion, JADU) and Multi-Family Residential (New ADU, ADU Conversion). ADU FAQs are available in the same four languages.

Source: https://ggcity.org/adu

## Reporting Construction Without Permits and Substandard Housing

Code Enforcement officers within the Building and Safety Division address substandard buildings and structures on the property, using the 2021 International Property Maintenance Code and the 2022 California codes. To report construction without permits, garage conversions, and substandard housing conditions, use the Garden Grove Public Records Request, call (714) 741-5350 to leave a voicemail, email codeenforcement@ggcity.org, or report an issue online.

When a complaint is filed, the case is assigned to a code enforcement officer who investigates, prepares a "Notice of Violations" and directs repairs or demolition. The time this takes varies with the complexity of the case. If a structure is a life/safety condition in imminent danger of causing bodily harm, a "Notice and Order" may be issued and immediate remedies sought, which may include posting the structure "Vacate, Unsafe to Occupy" and/or a "Notice of substandard building" (Lien) on the property title. Enforcement tools include verbal and written warnings, letter notifications, office conferences, administrative citations, and civil abatement.

Source: https://ggcity.org/building-and-safety/substandard-housing
