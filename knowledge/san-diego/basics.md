# City of San Diego — basics

Generated 2026-10-17 from https://www.sandiego.gov pages listed in each section's Source line.

## Official City Documents

The Office of the City Clerk provides quick access to a variety of official City policies, laws, actions, agendas and records. These include the City Charter, the Municipal Code, City Council docket, minutes and results, Council Committee agendas and actions, resolutions and ordinances, and more.

Source: https://www.sandiego.gov/city-clerk/official-city-documents

## Municipal Code

The City of San Diego Municipal Code contains the laws of the City of San Diego as passed by ordinances. It is updated as new ordinances are adopted by the City Council. The Office of the City Clerk's Municipal Code page links to the code for browsing.

Source: https://www.sandiego.gov/city-clerk/municipal-code

## City Council Meeting Documents

The docket is the official agenda for the San Diego City Council meetings. The City Clerk's page covers City Council meeting documents: agendas/supporting materials, results summaries, and minutes. City Council dockets can be viewed at https://sandiego.hylandcloud.com/211agendaonlinecouncil

Source: https://www.sandiego.gov/city-clerk/city-council-docket-agenda

## City Charter

The City Charter serves as the constitution for the City. The edition on the City Clerk's page includes articles of amendment through the municipal election of November 2016.

Source: https://www.sandiego.gov/city-clerk/city-charter

The City Clerk's Boards and Commissions page has information regarding the City's boards, commissions, and task forces. It includes their responsibilities, membership lists and application form.

## Mayoral Artifacts Database

The Digital Archives' Mayoral Artifacts database has online artifacts given to the City of San Diego by foreign or domestic governments, Sister Cities, business leaders, private citizens and other organizations. It catalogues items from 1955 to present. The Artifacts Gallery shows historical and interesting artifacts given to the City from 1955 to present. The database is at /digitalarchives/preservation/mayoral-artifacts

Source: https://www.sandiego.gov/digitalarchives/mayoral-artifacts
