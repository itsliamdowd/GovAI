## Affordable Housing Permitting Dashboard

The Development Services Department (DSD) dashboard gives a snapshot of the Affordable Housing permits it processes. It reflects applications at various stages of the permitting process, including in review, permitted/entitled or constructed. The data is an approximation based on current source data since 2020 and is subject to change and validation at a later date. It includes housing units produced by private developers through the Inclusionary Affordable Housing Program.

To use it, filter by clicking on the charts and use the 'Ctrl' key to select additional data. Hover over data elements to display tool-tips. The parameters at the bottom of the dashboard filter data by category. The data is a system-generated estimate of all affordable housing permits processed by DSD and may differ from previous dashboard results or other City reports. Source data is from the Development Services Department, retrieved from https://data.sandiego.gov.

Data filters (project types):
- Add/Alt 3+ Fam, Increase DU: addition or alteration of a three or more unit residential building, with increased dwelling units.
- Add/Alt Companion Unit/Acc Apt: addition or alteration of an Accessory Dwelling Unit.
- Five or More Family Apt: addition or alteration of an apartment building of five or more units.
- Five or More Family Condo: addition or alteration of a five or more unit residential building of condominiums.
- Single-Room Occupant Hotel: addition or alteration of a single room occupant hotel unit.
- Three or Four Family Apt: addition or alteration of a three- or four-unit residential apartment building.
- Two-Family Apartment: addition or alteration of a two-family apartment.

Approval Status:
- Created: the approval has been created and has not received issuance.
- Pending Invoice Payment: the approval is pending payment of the invoice.
- Issued: the approval was issued.
- Completed: the approval and inspections are complete.

Project Processing Code:
- Standard: approvals processed through the standard DSD processes.
- Express: approvals designated under Express Plan Check criteria.
- Expedite: approvals designated under the Expedite Program.

AMI Levels: Federal and State housing assistance programs set maximum incomes for eligibility to live in assisted housing. They also set the maximum rents and housing costs that may be charged to eligible residents, based on their incomes and surveys of the local area median income (AMI).
- Extremely Low Income: 0-30% of AMI
- Very Low Income: >30% to 50% of AMI
- Low Income: >50% to 80% of AMI
- Moderate Income: >80% to 120% of AMI

## Housing Action Packages (Homes for All of Us)

Homes for All of Us is Mayor Gloria's initiative to produce more homes across the city that San Diegans at all income levels can afford. The Housing Action Package includes programs and initiatives to increase the production of homes. The City Council passed the first Housing Action Package in February 2022. The Planning Department expects to bring forward a second Housing Action Package focused on allowing more homes for more people in all communities. The page lists the year completed as 2023.

The initiative focuses on three Strategic Plan priority areas:
- Create Homes for All of Us: allows additional opportunities for homes for people of all incomes, abilities and family compositions in all communities, especially in higher-resource communities currently lacking affordable homes.
- Champion Sustainability: allows for more homes where residents can walk, roll, bike and take transit.
- Foster Regional Prosperity: promotes homes for residents of all incomes in all communities so San Diego continues to have a strong economy.
