## Civic Dance Arts Classes

The Civic Dance Arts program teaches beginner through advanced level classes in tap, jazz, ballet, hip hop and musical theatre dance to more than 3,000 children and adults of all ages. Classes are $41 for 10 week sessions. They are offered at 19 Recreation Centers throughout the City and Balboa Park Facilities.

## Special Events — Permitting Assistance

The Office of Special Events provides permitting assistance for events that occur on public property. Through its one stop permitting process, an event organizer can work with the City to plan all aspects of their event that relate to the use of public property. The City hosts hundreds of events every year, including runs, walks, triathlons, festivals and parades.

Source: https://www.sandiego.gov/specialevents/celebrating-the-citys-special-events
