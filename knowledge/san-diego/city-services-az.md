## Missed Trash and Recycling Collection

If your collection is missed, first check the Environmental Services Department's collection status website to see whether your collection is delayed. If your address is identified as delayed, the City will return the following day to provide service. If your address was not identified as delayed, submit a report through Get It Done under the "Missed Collection" category.

To help ensure collection, residents must provide a serviceable container. If a container needs replacing, start the process through Get It Done. Place containers out no later than 6:00 a.m. on the morning of your normally scheduled collection, because Sanitation Drivers are in neighborhoods as early as 6:15 a.m. Place containers at least three (3) feet from other containers, cars, and objects, and not under tree branches, overhead wires, or basketball hoops. Do not overflow containers, because objects sticking out can become projectiles during automated collection and may prevent the driver from collecting the contents.

Source: https://www.sandiego.gov/get-it-done/talking-trash-what-do-about-missed-collections

## Development Services — Locations and Hours

Mission Valley is the primary location for in-person customer assistance, and most services require an appointment. Appointments are not needed to attend Public Hearings or Planning Commission meetings. Customers should make appointments to make payments in person and can schedule an appointment to view building plans or records. All in-person services at this location are closed on business days between 11:30 a.m. and 12:30 p.m. For assistance before coming, call 619-446-5000. Address: 7650 Mission Valley Road MS# DSD-1A, San Diego, CA 92108 (City Operations Building).

Mission Valley hours (closed 11:30 a.m. to 12:30 p.m.):

## Retired Senior Volunteer Patrol (R.S.V.P.)

The R.S.V.P. program promotes community awareness and helps San Diego residents feel safe. Volunteers perform vacation house checks, You Are Not Alone checks, and other community crime prevention programs. Residents can join as volunteers.

## Special Events Permitting

The Office of Special Events provides permitting assistance for events that occur on public property. Through its one stop permitting process, an event organizer can work with the City to plan all aspects of the event that relate to the use of public property. The City hosts hundreds of events every year, including runs, walks, triathlons, festivals and parades.

Source: https://www.sandiego.gov/specialevents/celebrating-the-citys-special-events

## Commission on Gang Prevention and Intervention — Youth Committee

The Commission's Youth Subcommittee, formed in September 2016, represents people ages 15 to 25 who have been impacted by gangs. It meets the second Saturday of each month, and agendas with locations and times are posted on the Commission's website. The Subcommittee continues to recruit members.

Source: https://www.sandiego.gov/blog/youth-committee
