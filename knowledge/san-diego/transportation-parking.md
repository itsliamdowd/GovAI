## City Street Assessment

The City is formulating strategies and technologies to improve and repair its network of approximately 2,800 miles of streets. The Street Division publishes Pavement Condition Assessment Reports for 2024, 2016, and 2011. It also publishes Street Pavement Condition Maps for 2024, 2016, and 2011.
