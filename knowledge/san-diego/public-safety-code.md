## PulsePoint App — CPR Alerts

Source: https://www.sandiego.gov/fire/save-a-life-today

## Community Emergency Response Team (CERT)

The Community Emergency Response Team (CERT) program trains residents to stay safe in the wake of disasters. San Diego Fire-Rescue personnel and CERT San Diego volunteers teach the community disaster preparedness skills, so everyone can survive the first few hours or days after an emergency. Residents can join the program to help neighbors and friends stay safe in a disaster.

Source: https://www.sandiego.gov/volunteer-program/community-emergency-response-team

## Emergency Preparedness — Make a Plan and Build a Kit

The Office of Emergency Services (OES) advises residents to create emergency plans that support their particular needs. Its "Make a Plan" resource covers building an emergency supply kit and planning for your shelter, vehicle and work space. Its "Build a Kit" resource covers what to include in a household kit, with supplies that will last at least 72 hours.

Source: https://www.sandiego.gov/oes/emergencypreparedness

## Emergency Preparedness — Stay Informed, Get Involved, and Hazard Lookup

OES's "Stay Informed" resources cover Current Incidents, Mitigation, Preparedness, Partners, and how the City of San Diego disseminates emergency incident updates. OES says residents should not self-deploy to a major disaster. It encourages involvement through neighborhoods, churches, schools and volunteer organizations to build resilient communities.

To identify hazards relevant to you (earthquakes, fires, floods, etc.), type your home address into the California Office of Emergency Services website linked on the page. The page also links Ready America, which offers basic emergency readiness information and supply lists for pet owners, families, seniors, businesses and those with disabilities or special needs. It also links Ready San Diego, a County of San Diego resource on how to get alerts and prepare before an emergency.

Source: https://www.sandiego.gov/oes/emergencypreparedness

## Business Emergency Operations Registry

Vendors and contractors of all sizes are invited to register their interest in potentially performing emergency work for the City. When an emergency strikes, the City may use emergency procedures to immediately select a vendor or contractor to reopen damaged facilities. The registry provides a list of vendors and contractors potentially available for emergency goods, services and work if needed.

Source: https://www.sandiego.gov/oes/emergencypreparedness
