## Special Events — Permitting Assistance

The City of San Diego hosts hundreds of events every year, including runs, walks, triathlons, festivals and parades. The Office of Special Events provides permitting assistance for events that occur on public property.

The Office uses a one stop permitting process. Through it, an event organizer can work with the City to plan all aspects of their event that relate to the use of public property.
