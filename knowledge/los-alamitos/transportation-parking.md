## Oak Street Project

Starting March 23, 2026, as part of the City's ongoing pavement management program, improvements will be made to Oak Street from Katella Avenue to Sausalito Street. Work includes grinding and overlay, replacement of damaged curbs and gutters, sidewalk repairs to improve drainage, and modifications to ADA curb ramps to meet current accessibility standards. The contractor is Hardy and Harper. Work will start March 31, 2026 and will take approximately up to six weeks from commencement. An Oak Street Improvements Schedule is available for download on the page.

The driveways at the entrance and exit of the Oak Middle School parking lot will be replaced to comply with ADA slope requirements. The street asphalt work on Oak Street, along with the concrete repairs to these driveways, will be completed during spring break, from April 6, 2026, to April 10, 2026. The City is coordinating with Oak Middle School about the impacts.

Trash will be picked up before 8 a.m. on Friday April 10, 2026 to allow the paving of the street. All other trash pick up days will have regular times. Parking signs will be put up as needed for all upcoming work, showing dates and times.

## Parking Citations — How to Pay

Parking citations can be paid in person at the Police Department, by mail, or online. In person at the Police Department, you must pay with exact cash or a credit card (VISA or Mastercard only). To pay online, visit https://www.citationprocessingcenter.com/CitationSearch.aspx.

Source: https://cityoflosalamitos.org/380/Parking

## Parking Citations — How to Contest

You can contest a parking citation by following the instructions on the back of the ticket or online through the Citation Processing Center at https://www.citationprocessingcenter.com/CitationSearch.aspx. To contest in person, you will be asked to fill out the Parking Citation Review Form. Once the department receives your form, your citation will be placed on hold until it is reviewed and a decision is made. You will receive notice of the decision by mail within 10 business days.

Source: https://cityoflosalamitos.org/380/Parking

## Parking Citations — Payment Deadline and Penalties

All parking citations must be paid within 21 days of issuance. Parking citations that are unpaid or remain uncontested after 21 days will be subject to increased penalties.

## Daylighting Law (AB 413)

Starting January 1, 2025, California's Daylighting Law (AB 413) went into effect to enhance pedestrian safety. Parking is now prohibited within 20 feet of the approach side of any crosswalk (marked or unmarked) and within 15 feet of any crosswalk with curb extensions. These restrictions apply even if curbs are not painted red.

Source: https://cityoflosalamitos.org/380/Parking

## E-bike Ordinance

The Los Alamitos Police Department announced new municipal codes on the safety and use of E-Bikes in the City (Ordinance No. 2025-01). Examples include enforcement actions related to operating in an unsafe manner, obeying traffic laws, using a helmet for riders under the age of 18, and an explanation of each classification of E-Bike.

Students who operate E-bikes must attend an E-Bike safety course hosted by the Los Alamitos Police Department before the Los Alamitos School District allows them to park their E-Bikes on the school campus. After successful completion, the Police Department provides a sticker, valid for two years.
