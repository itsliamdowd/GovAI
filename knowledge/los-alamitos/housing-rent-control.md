## Housing Element of the General Plan

The City's latest Housing Element of the General Plan is for the 2021-2029 planning period. It addresses State Housing Element law, the Regional Housing Needs Assessment (RHNA) allocation for the City, and a site inventory to meet the RHNA. The Element also lays out goals, policies, and actions for the preservation, improvement, and development of housing throughout the City.

## Homeless Outreach — Homeless Liaison Officer Program

The Los Alamitos Police Department has a Homeless Liaison Officer (HLO) program to coordinate resources and actions to address challenges impacting the local community. HLOs are specially trained patrol officers who are the initial point of contact with homeless individuals and who address community concerns involving homelessness. They receive additional training on mental illness, homeless issues and available services and resources. They assess each person's needs and identify how to help from a range of solutions, with the goal of connecting homeless individuals with wrap around services to move them off the streets and into self-sustaining situations.

HLOs collaborate with non-profit service providers and governmental agencies including City Net, Orange County Mental Health, and Veterans Services. These organizations help provide short term and long term housing, employment opportunities, medical care, and substance abuse treatment and mental health services. HLOs seek out and engage chronically homeless persons and, for those who are willing, attempt to provide them with resources and help find housing linked with appropriate services.
