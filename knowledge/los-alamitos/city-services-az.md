## Youth Sports — Programs and Registration

The Recreation and Community Services Department offers youth sport programs for ages 2 - 14 years old. ## Youth Sports — Class Descriptions

Parent & Me Basketball, Parent & Me Soccer and Mini Superstars are led by Recreation Staff but must include parent participation; an award is included in the fee. ## Dog Leash, Cleanup and Noise Rules

Municipal Code § 6.16.100 requires dogs to be on a leash on any public street, park, or other public place. The leash cannot be more than eight-feet long and must be held continuously in the hands of a person capable of controlling the dog. Dogs must be on leash in City Parks. Arbor Dog Park is located off Lampson Avenue, east of Seal Beach Blvd.

Municipal Code § 6.16.200 holds dog owners responsible for picking up after their dogs. Municipal Code § 6.16.110 prohibits permitting a dog to bark, howl, whine and make other loud and unusual noises so as to annoy and become offensive to the neighborhood or persons residing immediately surrounding the habitation of the same.

## Trash Collection — Schedule, Hours and Holidays

Since January 2022, Universal Waste Systems (UWS) has provided trash, recycling, and organics collection. Depending on your address, residential trash pickup is either on a Thursday or Friday. Hours of operation in residential areas are limited to 7am to 6pm with no Sunday collection. Carts should be set out for service by 6am on your collection day.

Observed holidays: New Year's Day, Independence Day, Labor Day, Veteran's Day, Thanksgiving Day and Christmas Day. There is no trash service on observed holidays; trash is collected the day AFTER the normal collection day for the remainder of the week, and the regular schedule resumes the following Monday. Holiday trees are collected annually, for a period of three weeks following Christmas Day.
