## Los Alamitos-Rossmoor Library

Los Alamitos-Rossmoor Library is a branch of Orange County Public Libraries that works with the City of Los Alamitos to provide programs and classes for a wide range of ages, a teen space, and a worldwide language collection. Resources include the AWE Station (computers for children ages 2-8 with educational games in English and Spanish), hotspots for checkout to library card holders (depending on availability), a Laptop Kiosk, reservable private study rooms, and public computers. Free Wi-Fi and wireless printing (up to 50 pages, .15 per page) are provided. Monthly book clubs are available.

Location: 12700 Montecito, Seal Beach, CA 90740. Phone: (562) 430-1048. Hours: Monday - Thursday 10:00 a.m. - 7:00 p.m.; Saturday 9:00 a.m. - 5:00 p.m.; Friday & Sunday closed. For the Friends of the Library bookstore, call 562-430-1048, option 4. The library held a GRAND RE-OPENING ceremony on Monday, November 18th, at 9:30 a.m. after its renovation (update dated November 12, 2024).

## Parks, Recreation & Cultural Arts Commission

The Parks, Recreation, and Cultural Arts Commission is a seven-member Commission, each serving a three year term. It advises the City Council on public recreation and parks, including development of recreational areas, facilities, programs and services, policies on recreational services, and yearly community events that promote the fine arts.

Meetings are at 6:00 pm every other month (even months) in the City Hall Council Chamber, 3191 Katella Avenue, Los Alamitos, CA 90720. Agendas are available 72 hours before each Regular Meeting and 24 hours before each Special Meeting. ## Recreation Bucks Program

When you shop at participating businesses, you earn rewards that can be redeemed for discounts on select Los Alamitos Recreation & Community Services classes/programs. Recreation Bucks can be applied to both Winter 2025 and Spring 2026 class schedules. The program runs from December 1, 2025, to May 29, 2026. Businesses must provide a line item or detailed receipt for purchases so the discount can be redeemed when registering for a class or program. Final discretion for program eligibility is determined by the City of Los Alamitos. For program details, call (562) 431-3538.

Business eligibility: Recreation Bucks are not redeemable for cannabis (cannabis-related), gas stations, home-based businesses, and online food delivery services. Businesses must have a current Los Alamitos business license with no outstanding violations or balance, submit the Acknowledgement Form, display the City-provided vinyl sticker at the entryway, and be in good standing with the City (no outstanding code enforcement cases or outstanding business license fees). To participate, submit the form by email to Jessica Torres at Jtorres@cityoflosalamitos.org or in person at City Hall.

## Trash Collection — Provider, Schedule and Set-Out

Since January 2022, Universal Waste Systems (UWS) has provided trash, recycling, and organics collection for the City of Los Alamitos. Residential trash pickup is on either a Thursday or Friday, depending on address. Hours of operation in residential areas are 7am to 6pm with no Sunday collection. Carts should be set out for service by 6am on your collection day.

Observed holidays are New Year's Day, Independence Day, Labor Day, Veteran's Day, Thanksgiving Day and Christmas Day. There is no trash service on observed holidays; trash is collected the day AFTER the normal collection day for the remainder of the week (for example, Thursday's trash is collected on Friday), and the regular schedule resumes the following Monday. Holiday trees are collected annually for three weeks following Christmas Day.

## Trash Bins — What Goes Where

Green waste bin accepts: branches, brush, flower cuttings, flowers, garden trimmings, grass, hay, house plants, lawn clippings, leaves, pruning, shrubbery, straws, weeds and yard waste. It does not accept: aluminum, animal carcasses/feces, cigarette butts, clothing, diapers, glass, hazardous waste, kitty litter, landscape timbers, plastic bags, plastic containers, plastic straws, rocks, soil, styrofoam, trash, tree stumps and treated lumber.

Gray (organics) bin does not accept: lightbulbs, batteries, paint and solvents, clothing, electronics, syringes, chemicals, television, computers and monitors, microwaves, unused or expired prescription drugs.

Blue (recycling) bin does not accept: lightbulbs, batteries, electronics, food waste, fruit/vegetable scraps, liquids, plants, leaves, dirt, clothes hangers, aerosol cans and mirrors. Empty containers (shake or wipe liquid/food out, no need to rinse) and let them dry right side up before placing them in the blue bin.

## State Solid Waste & Recycling Mandates (SB 1383, AB 1826, AB 341)

SB 1383 targets: reduce organic waste disposal 75% by 2024 and rescue enough organics for people to eat at least 20% of currently disposed surplus food by 2025. Target dates: January 1, 2022 - jurisdictions provide organics recycling services to businesses and residents; January 1, 2024 - jurisdictions take progressive enforcement actions against non-compliant entities; January 1, 2025 - State to achieve 75% reduction of organics disposal and recover a minimum of 20% of disposed edible food. Containers provided by residents themselves would not comply; the jurisdiction or its designee (like a hauler) must provide containers.

Food recovery: certain food businesses must donate the maximum amount of edible food they would otherwise dispose of. The first tier was required to donate starting in 2022, the second tier starting in 2024. Food Finders can help: Chris Wong at (562) 283-1400 extension 105 or cwong@foodfinders.org.

Waivers: certain businesses and multi-family complexes of five units or more may qualify for waivers, but only those who request and receive a waiver from the City are exempt. Submit the SB 1383 Solid Waste & Recycling Regulation - Waiver Request Form to the Development Services Department.

AB 341: all businesses that produce 4 cubic yards or more of trash per week and multi-family properties of five units or more are required to recycle. In 2019 the threshold was lowered: any commercial property with 2-cubic yards or more of waste per-week must have a recycling program. Property or Business Managers without recycling service should contact UWS at (800) 631-7016. AB 1826 requires businesses to recycle organic waste on and after April 1, 2016, depending on the amount generated per week, and includes multifamily dwellings of five or more units (which are not required to have a food waste diversion program).

## Household Hazardous Waste (HHW) Collection

Through the franchise agreement with UWS, residents receive free quarterly household hazardous waste collection (up to four items for collection). To schedule, contact UWS customer service at (800) 631-7016 or email customerservices@uwscompany.com. HHW includes products such as paint, cleaners, oils, batteries and pesticides. E-waste such as televisions, tablets, cell phones and computers is also included in this service.

County of Orange HHW Collection Centers accept waste only from Orange County residents. Rules: a maximum of 15 gallons or 125 pounds may be transported per vehicle, per trip; containers larger than five gallons will not be accepted; containers will not be returned, except oil containers upon request; materials must be in original containers (except motor oil, fuels and antifreeze); all containers must have lids, be sturdy, non-leaking, labeled and protected from breakage; do not combine different types of waste.

Material Exchange Program: Orange County residents can choose up to five items per week from a selection of partially used containers of household, yard, and car-care products. Visit oclandfills.com or call (714) 834-4000.
