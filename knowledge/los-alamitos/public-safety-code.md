## Code Enforcement — Services and Process

The Code Enforcement Division responds to complaints of violations of the Los Alamitos Municipal Code, including substandard buildings, property maintenance, inoperative vehicles, weed abatement and land use violations. When violations are identified, the responsible parties are contacted and requested to abate the conditions. Failure to correct violations could result in a citation or a referral to the City Prosecutor's office.

## Police Department — Requesting Police Assistance

Emergency: call 911. Non-emergency 24-Hour Police service: 562-594-7232. Police Department administrative and business matters: 562-431-2255.

The Police Department lobby is open for administrative business Monday through Thursday from 7:30 am until 5:30 pm. After hours, an officer can be summoned to the lobby by calling 562-594-7232. The lobby is closed Fridays, weekends and holidays. Watch Commander: 562-431-2255, ext. 426.

When reporting an emergency, give the dispatcher the exact location and nature of the emergency, and say whether you need police, fire or paramedic service. Be ready to describe the situation, when it occurred, and whether anyone is injured. Suspect descriptions can include gender, race, age, height, weight, hair color, eyeglasses, and clothing/hat. Vehicle descriptions can include color, type, make, model, license plate number and direction of travel. For weapons, give the type (gun or knife), color and size. Dispatch services are provided by West Cities Communications (West-Comm).

Police Department address: 3201 Katella Avenue, Los Alamitos, CA 90720.

## Requesting a Police Report

To obtain a copy of a police report, complete a Request for Police Records Form (PDF), available on the page and at the Police Department front counter. Photo identification is required: California Driver's License, California Identification Card, Immigration Card, Military Identification, or Passport. The fee for any report or log item is $1.50. Email record request forms and a copy of your identification to the Records Bureau. Records Bureau phone: 562-431-2255, ext. 402.

Traffic Collision Reports (California Vehicle Code Section 20012) are released to involved persons, drivers, injured passengers, guardians, parents of minors, owners of vehicles or property damaged, persons who may incur civil liability, and authorized representatives of a driver (such as an attorney or insurance company). Attorneys and insurance representatives must provide written authorization signed by their client. Traffic collision reports can be requested online.

Crime & Incident Reports are governed by California Government Code 6254 and 6254(f). Information is provided to victims; authorized representatives of the victim (signed waiver or authorization required); the insurance carrier against which a claim has been or might be made; and any person suffering bodily injury, property damage, or loss. No copy is provided if disclosure would endanger the safety of a person involved in the investigation or the successful completion of the investigation or a related investigation. Reports are not released if they contain the name, address and phone number of a witness or confidential informant, or of a victim of certain crimes defined by listed California Penal Code sections.

Arrest Reports (California Government Code Section 6254(f)(1)): contact the Orange County District Attorney's Office at 714-896-7261.

Juvenile Reports: release of juvenile information requires the permission of the Orange County Juvenile Court Judge.

## Emergency Preparedness, CERT, and Citizens Academy

The City's Emergency Operations Center (EOC) is the central hub for managing major incidents, supported by Volunteers in Policing (VIP), the Community Emergency Response Team (CERT), and the Radio Amateur Civil Emergency Service (RACES). Public safety alerts are distributed through the AlertOC mass notification system.

The West Orange County CERT Program serves those who live and/or work in Buena Park, Cypress, La Palma, Los Alamitos, Rossmoor, Seal Beach, Westminster and their attached communities. Initial CERT training covers basic disaster response skills, such as fire safety, light search and rescue, team organization, and disaster medical operations.

The Police Department's Citizens Academy gives community members an inside look at daily operations of local law enforcement through presentations, demonstrations, and scenario-based activities covering patrol operations, investigations, traffic enforcement, emergency response, and more. The Police Department news page states the second Citizens' Academy begins in February.

Contact: Robert Acosta, Support Services Manager, Phone: 562-431-2255 Ext. 445.
