## Solar Photovoltaic (PV) Permit — Expedited Process

Residential solar permits for one- and two-family dwellings that are 10 kW or less in size can use an expedited plan check process. The solar PV project can only be located on one- and two-family dwellings and must not be over 10 kW in size.

Submit the following in person or via the Los Alamitos Permit Center:
- A completed permit application.
- Compliance with the Eligibility Checklist.
- A completed Standard Electrical Plan (Simplified Central/String Inverter Systems or Simplified Microinverter Systems for One- and Two-Family Dwellings). If standard plans are not used, submit an electrical plan with the items listed on the page.
- A site diagram.
- A roof plan showing roof layout, PV panels, and fire safety items.
- Completed expedited Structural Criteria with required documentation. For non-qualifying systems, provide structural drawings and calculations stamped and signed by a California licensed Civil or Structural Engineer.

Applications using the standard plan may be approved "over the counter" at the Building Department if time permits. Permits not approved over the counter should be reviewed in one weeks time. Residential solar plan review and permit fees are not to exceed $450.

## Parking Permits — How to Apply

Residential, guest, oversized vehicle, and temporary solid waste container parking permits are purchased online at losalamitos.getapermit.net. If a resident, landlord, or business owner cannot access the Internet, call 562-431-3538 for additional options.

Residents can also come to City Hall, 3191 Katella Ave, Los Alamitos, CA 90720, during regular business hours (Monday-Thursday, 7:30 AM to 5:30 PM) and should bring all required documents. If you do not have a printer, call the front desk at 562-431-3538 and request to pick up a printed copy of your approved permits.

Requirements: proof of residency is required at account activation. Valid vehicle registration must be provided for each permit requested, and it must match the applicant's qualifying address. Each residential dwelling is eligible for a maximum of three residential permits. To check whether your residence is in a designated permit parking zone, visit https://losalamitos.geoviewer.io/.

## Parking Permits — Fees and Types

- Residential Parking Permit (up to 3 per address): valid 1 year (July - June); free up to 3; hangtag.
- Additional Residential Permits (up to 2): 1 year (July-June); $25 each; hangtag.
- Annual Visitor Permit (up to 2 per address): 1 year (July - June); free up to 2; hangtag.
- Daily Visitor/Event Guest Permits: 1-day only permit, not to exceed 15 permits in total for 1 day; $1.00; printable PDF.
- Replacement Permit: remainder of current cycle; $25; hangtag.
- Oversized Vehicle Permit (applies to all residents, e.g. RV/Boats/Trailers): 3-day permit, not to exceed 9 days total per month; $10 (per 3-day permit); printable PDF.
- Temporary Solid Waste Container (applies to all residents/businesses, 2-40 yard dumpsters): 14-day permit; $10; printout not required.

Source: https://cityoflosalamitos.org/676/Get-a-Permit

## Parking Permits — Display Rules

Resident and Annual Visitor permits must be displayed facing outward on the rearview mirror of the vehicle. Daily Visitor, Event Guest, and Oversized Vehicle permits must be located within a five-inch square in the lower-left corner of the windshield nearest the driver. Permits must be displayed properly to avoid citation.

## Parking Permits — Landlords and Building Managers

Only landlords or building managers who own or work for a residential dwelling on a street block, within the posted street signs, are eligible to receive ONE visitor permit for noncommercial vehicle use. They may not purchase permits on behalf of their tenants. You cannot obtain permits as a landlord/building manager and as a resident for the same building or parcel.

Landlords must provide proof of ownership: a grant deed, recent mortgage statement, or recent property tax bill. Building managers must provide proof of employment in addition to proof of ownership from the owner.

## Parking Permits — Exception Permits, Visitors, and Oversized Vehicles

Certain residential properties without an address in a residential permit parking zone, but with a physical part of the property lying within an adjacent zone, may qualify for a special Exception Permit. Call 562-431-3538 if you believe you may qualify.

Residents at a qualified address can receive up to 2 Annual Visitor permits (transferable hang tags, valid for one permit cycle). For more than two additional visitors, buy Daily Visitor/Event Guest permits for $1.00 each (15 permits max per day). These must be purchased online.

You may purchase up to 3 (QTY) total 3-day Oversized Vehicle Parking permits per month, not to exceed 9 consecutive days. No overnight sleeping is allowed in an RV parked on the street. Temporary Solid Waste Container permits are for project sizes from 2-40 yards and do NOT apply to residential waste bins.

## Alarm Permits

Los Alamitos Municipal Code 8.04.080 requires alarm owners to obtain an alarm permit before using an alarm system that triggers a response from the police, fire, or other emergency agency. Operating an alarm without complying with the ordinance is a misdemeanor. Alarm permits must be renewed on or before January 1st each year.

To apply, renew, or pay an invoice, visit fams.wincitex.com/City/LosAlamitos. Phoenix Group Information Systems processes alarm payments for the City. Payments can be remitted to City of Los Alamitos False Alarm Reduction Program, PO Box 11370, Santa Ana, CA 92711. Call 866-450-2524, Monday - Friday, 8:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m. (PST). For appeals or questions, email FAMS@phxgis.com.

Los Alamitos Municipal Code 8.04.190-8.04.210 allows for the suspension, revocation, and/or discontinuance of police response to alarm calls at your location, at the discretion of the Chief of Police. Records Bureau: 562-431-2255, ext. 402.

## Special Event Vendor Applications

Vendors can review the 2026 Vendor Packet and submit an application online. Complete and return the packet with FULL payment for all events selected and any required insurance/health permits (no partial payments accepted).
