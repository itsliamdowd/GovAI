## Tustin Street Fair & Chili Cook-Off — Parking Locations

Parking options for the Street Fair are:
- Public Parking Structure, 242 W. Main St. It is behind Rutabegorz at Stevens Square, with access from the C St. & 6th St. entrance.
- Water Facility Parking Lot, 275 Main Street, at the corner of Prospect Ave. and 3rd St.
- Civic Center / Library, 300 Centennial Way. This is the largest parking lot. Accessible parking and a path of travel are available from the Civic Center.
- Tustin High School, 1171 El Camino Real. It charges a $5.00 parking fee and is a new addition for parking and shuttle.
- Columbus Tustin Recreation Center, 17522 Beneta Way.

## Tustin Tiller Days — Directions, Parking and Drop-Off

The 69th Annual Tustin Tiller Days Festival will take place October 2-4 at Columbus Tustin Park, 17522 Beneta Way, Tustin 92780. To get there, take the 55 Freeway to Irvine Boulevard, head east, and turn left on Prospect Avenue. Or take the 5 Freeway to Red Hill Avenue or Newport Avenue, head northeast, make a left on Irvine Boulevard, and make a right on Prospect Avenue. Carpooling or the use of rideshare is recommended.

Free street parking is available where allowed. Paid parking will also be available for $15 at the event, and the fees collected will be donated to the Columbus Tustin Middle School fundraiser. Drop-off and pick-up areas, including for Uber/Lyft, will be located on Beneta Way between Prospect Avenue and Livingston Street.

Parking for those with disabilities will be available in the Columbus Tustin Recreation Center and Columbus Tustin Middle School parking lots, accessible via Beneta Way and Prospect Avenue.

## Columbus Tustin Recreation Center — Parking

The Columbus Tustin Recreation Center is at 17522 Beneta Way, Tustin 92780. It offers free parking for 182 vehicles, including 6 ADA accessible spots. The center is on the northwest corner of Columbus Tustin Park, near the intersection of Beneta Way and Prospect Avenue. Contact: 714.573.3009.

Source: https://www.tustinca.org/850/Columbus-Tustin-Recreation-Center

## Traffic Collision Report — Requesting a Copy

To request a copy of a traffic collision report, use the LexisNexis link on the Tustin Police Department page. The online service is available 24 hours a day, seven days a week. A convenience fee of $10 per report is assessed to retrieve and print the report. The page states there is no cost for a copy of a crime or traffic report from Tustin Police Department, and that LexisNexis assesses the $10 convenience fee per report. For questions, call Tustin Police Department at 714-573-3200.
