## City Council — Structure, Members and Duties

Tustin has a Council-Manager form of government. It consists of an elected City Council responsible for policy making and a professional City Manager appointed by the Council. The City Council is a five-member body made up of the Mayor, Mayor Pro Tem, and three Council Members. Current members: Austin Lumbard (Mayor), Ray Schnell (Mayor Pro Tem), and Council Members Ryan Gallagher, Lee K. Fink and John Nielsen.

As a General Law city, the Council's duties include approval of the Budget and oversight of the financial affairs of the City. The Council has the power to act on program and administrative recommendations of City staff, adopt ordinances and resolutions, appropriate funds and approve contracts, establish policy and approve programs, make appointments to advisory boards and commissions, and respond to concerns and needs of residents. The Council also appoints the City Attorney and serves as the Board Members of the Successor Agency (former Redevelopment Agency). Agendas are available prior to meetings, and minutes are available following approval.

## City Council Meetings

The City Council meets on the first and third Tuesdays of each month. Closed Session begins at 4:30 p.m., followed by the Regular Meeting at 6:00 p.m. Meetings are held in the Council Chambers at 300 Centennial Way, Tustin, California 92780. All meetings are open to the public and may be attended in person. The HTML version of City Council agendas may be translated into multiple languages by clicking the HTML version and then choosing a language from Google Translate. ## City Clerk — Services and Contact

The City Clerk's Office coordinates official City Council agendas, legal and legislative documents, and City Council minutes, which are available at City Hall and online. The Clerk is custodian of public records including agreements, campaign disclosure filings, election-related documents, minutes of the City Council, ordinances, resolutions, and Statements of Economic Interests. To request a copy of these records, complete a Public Records Request Form.

The City Clerk conducts Municipal Elections; general municipal elections are held in November of the even number years. The Clerk is the filing officer for Campaign Disclosure and conflict of interest statements required by the Fair Political Practices Commission. The office coordinates updates to the Tustin City Code, which is accessible at City Hall and online. The Clerk also administers oaths of office, attests and seals official documents, and receives and files petitions, appeals, claims, and lawsuits against the City. The Clerk oversees duplicating services for the Civic Center, mail, main phone line reception, and the Shredding Program.

Contact: 300 Centennial Way, Tustin, CA 92780. Phone: 714-573-3027. Fax: 714-832-6382.

## City Budget

The City of Tustin has a bi-annual budget process, with funds appropriated annually for the fiscal year which begins in July and ends in June of the following year. The approach combines the strategic plan and annual financial plan for operations and capital improvements.

## Sports Field Allocation — Long-Term Use

Sports leagues and private organizations needing continuous sports field reservations for practice and games are invited to participate in the facility allocation process, which regulates the use of both lighted and unlighted sports fields during recognized sports seasons. All organizations are considered non-resident until a roster of participants can be produced showing that the majority of participants are Tustin residents.

For long-term use, complete the Facility Allocation Packet, then print it and return it to: Attention: Karl Halili, Tustin Parks and Recreation, 300 Centennial Way, Tustin, CA 92780. Prior to the start of the activity, submit a copy of liability insurance: a Certificate of Liability Insurance in the amount of 1 million dollars naming the City of Tustin as Additional Insured. The "Certificate Holder" must precisely read: The City of Tustin Its Elective and Appointive Boards, Officers, Agents, and Employees, 300 Centennial Way, Tustin, CA 92780. Three endorsements are also required, and each must reference the policy number: Additional Insured Endorsement, 30-Day Cancellation Endorsement, and Primary and Non-Contributory Endorsement.
