## Plan Check by Appointment

Plan check by appointment is offered for minor projects such as patio covers, fences and free-standing block walls, pools and spas, and residential remodels such as kitchen and bathroom remodels. The maximum plan check time allotted for any project is one hour.

To qualify, email the plans to planpermitsubmittals@tustinca.org at least 24 hours before the requested date. The email subject should clearly state "Plan Check by Appointment". You will receive a confirmation of your appointment by email. Planning and Public Works staff will also be available during your appointment time. All appointment requests must be made by email to receive a confirmation.

## Permits Process — Plans, Permit, Inspection, Turnaround

Before any grading or building-related construction or installation can occur, plans must be prepared and submitted to the City. They are reviewed for compliance with Zoning regulations, the Grading Ordinance, and the Building Code. Many proposed construction activities require plans prepared by a licensed design professional. Call a Building Technician at 714-573-3130 during business hours with questions. Submit plans, PV solar plans, and completed permit applications through the Citizen Self Service portal. Send submittal questions to PlanPermitSubmittals@tustinca.org or solar questions to PVSubmittals@tustinca.org.

Unless a special zoning or other approval is required (Planning Commission approval, etc.), first submittal plan check should take approximately 10 working days, with subsequent submittals returned within five working days. Plan checks not completed at the counter are generally returned within 10 working days for first submittals and 5 working days for resubmittals. Applicants can request expedited plan check (with the payment of a slightly higher fee).

Once plan review is complete and the project meets code requirements, a permit may be issued after required fees are paid. A permit is valid for 180 days, unless work is active and progressing, in which case the permit remains valid until the project is complete.

To request an inspection, call the inspection request line at 714-573-3141 and leave your permit number, project address, and the name of a contact person, one day in advance. You can also submit an online request through the Inspection Request Page.

## Permit Fee Payment — Fraud Warning

Fraud related to collection of permit fees is on the rise. Tustin will only collect payment for permits or related fees through the Citizen Self Service (CSS) Portal, or in person at the City Hall Finance counter. The City will never ask you for a bank routing number or wire number. If you think you have received a fraudulent invoice, contact planpermitsubmittals@tustinca.org immediately.

## Business License — Requirement and Application

Every business operating in the city must obtain a Business License before starting business and pay the annual tax each year thereafter. This applies to businesses at a commercial or residential location in the city, any business using a Tustin address or a Post Office box for receiving mail, and businesses based outside Tustin that conduct activity in the city (for example, contractors, consultants, and landscape maintenance companies).

A Business Tax Certificate is typically issued within 2 weeks of submittal of a completed application. Apply online at any time, and refer to the Business License Application Checklist first. All businesses in Tustin must complete a Zoning Questionnaire. Home-based businesses must also complete the Home Occupation Questionnaire. Both must be uploaded during the online application. Contact the Planning Department at (714) 573-3140 before signing any lease or purchase agreement regarding zoning, tenant improvements, and signage.

Business License Technician: 714-573-3144. Hours: Monday-Thursday 8 am - 5 pm; Friday 8:30 am - 4:30 pm. City offices will be closed Wednesday, November 11, 2026 for Veterans Day, and regular hours resume Thursday, November 12, 2026.

Source: https://www.tustinca.org/310/Business-Licenses

## Business License Fees, Renewal, and Penalties

Business license fees start at $25, based on gross receipts and the type of business. Fees are due annually and valid from January 1 through December 31. They are prorated if you open your business after April 1. A state fee of $4 under AB 1379 applies to any applicant for a local business license or similar instrument or permit, or renewal thereof.

A renewal is automatically mailed approximately six weeks before the expiration date. Failure to receive a renewal notice does not relieve the owner of the responsibility to renew. Failure to pay the license tax when due results in a penalty of 25% of the license tax on the last day of each month after the due date. The penalty shall not exceed 100% of the license tax due per Ordinance Number 328, Section 27.

## Special Permits for Certain Businesses

Special permits are required before a Business License is approved for these businesses: Auto broker, Body Art/Tattoos, Live Entertainment, Massage Establishments, Mobile Auto Service, Mobile Food Vendors, Pawn Broker, Secondhand Dealers, Weapons, and other land uses that do not fit exactly into the description of residential or commercial uses.

To determine if you need a special permit, call the Business License Division at (714) 573-3144. Special permits must be submitted in person to 300 Centennial Way Tustin CA, 92780. A seller's permit applies to individuals, corporations, partnerships, and limited liability companies, and both wholesalers and retailers must apply. See the California Department of Tax and Fee Administration (CDTFA) for more information.

## Building Board of Appeals

The Building Board of Appeals hears appeals of determinations made by the City's Building Official that arise during plan check or construction inspection. Meetings are scheduled as needed, on average 1 to 3 per year. The Board has 5 members, who serve 4-year terms. Compensation is $150 per meeting, subject to City Council approval. For more information, contact the Community Development Department at 714-573-3106.

Source: https://www.tustinca.org/561/Building-Board-of-Appeals

## Planning Commission

The Planning Commission meets at 6:00 p.m. on the 2nd and 4th Tuesday of every month. Meetings are held in person in the Council Chamber at 300 Centennial Way, Tustin, CA 92780, and via Zoom. Agendas are available before meetings, and minutes are available following approval.

The Commission has 5 members with 4-year terms. Its responsibilities include making recommendations to the City Council on zoning map and ordinance changes, subdivisions, and appeals on variances and use permits. To contact the Commission, write to City of Tustin, Planning Commission, 300 Centennial Way, Tustin, CA 92780, or call 714-573-3106.
