## Fair Housing Foundation — Tenant and Landlord Services

The Fair Housing Foundation (FHF) provides fair housing services for the City of Tustin. FHF promotes equal access to housing opportunities and provides services regarding general housing-related assistance, housing discrimination, fair housing outreach/education, and tenant and landlord rights and responsibilities. Through CDBG funding, FHF also offers counseling services and investigation of housing discrimination complaints through its housing assistance hotline at 1-800-446-FAIR (3247).

Contact: Fair Housing Foundation, 3605 Long Beach Boulevard, Suite 302, Long Beach, CA 90807. Phone 800-446-3247 or 562-989-1206. Website: www.FHFCA.org.

## Affordable For-Sale Housing — Stafford Glen

Stafford Glen is a for-sale 42-unit community with 35 residential condominium units and seven (7) live/work units, and it is open. It includes two (2) very-low income affordable condominiums: one three-bedroom for $138,594 and one four-bedroom for $149,035. Qualified very-low income households must agree to live in the condominium as their primary residence and understand they are not allowed to purchase additional residential property while owning this affordable unit. Apply through HomeOwnershipOC.org.

## CDBG Program

The City of Tustin participates in the Federal Community Development Block Grant (CDBG) Program. The program provides annual grants on a formula basis principally for low and moderate-income persons (generally defined as members of a family earning no more than 80% of the area median income). Tustin became an entitlement community in 1994 and receives approximately $700,000 annually. Funds go to activities such as assistance to businesses for economic development and job creation/retention, construction, improvements to public facilities, public services activities, fair housing services, and rehabilitation.

Source: https://www.tustinca.org/171/CDBG-Housing

## 2021-2029 Housing Element

The City Council adopted the 2021-2029 Housing Element, and it has been certified by HCD. The Housing Element is one of the state-mandated elements of the City's General Plan and must be updated every eight years. Tustin's final RHNA allocation for 2021-2029 is 6,782 units. State law requires the City to set aside 40% of the total allocation for Very-Low- and Low-income units.

Source: https://www.tustinca.org/1211/Housing-Element-Update

## Housing Element Rezone Project

On January 7, 2025, the Tustin City Council approved the Housing Element Rezone Project. It rezones three sites to accommodate the City's RHNA obligation: Tustin Legacy, Enderle Center, and Tustin Market Place. Questions can be emailed to HousingElementRezone@tustinca.org or directed to the Planning Division at 714-573-3140.
