## Copies of Past Permits and Approved Plans

Building permits issued in 2020 or later can be viewed online. For records not available online, such as building permits and building plans, submit a request through the City's Public Records Request Online Portal. Alternatively, complete the Public Records Request Form and deliver it to the City Clerk's Office by email or in person. The City will respond within ten (10) days of receiving the request. Questions about public records go to the City's Records Coordinator at (949) 461-3421.

Under California Health and Safety Code section 19851(a), building plans are open for inspection as a public record only on the premises of the building department. The City may provide copies of building plans if:
- The City receives written permission to duplicate the plans from both the certified, licensed or registered professional who signed the original documents and the original or current owner of the building. If the building is part of a common interest development, the City must instead receive written permission from the board of directors or governing body of the association (such as a Homeowners Association board).
- The required Affidavit form(s), available in the City Clerk's Office, are completed and the appropriate fees are paid.

Contact the City Clerk's Office during normal business hours at (949) 461-3400 for more information.

## Building Permits — When Required and Review Times

A Building Permit is required for all projects involving the alteration of a structure, in whole or in part. This includes removal and replacement of wall or ceiling coverings, relocation of non-bearing partition walls, property line walls/fences, and erecting of a structure. Building Permits include Plumbing, Electrical, Mechanical, and Demolition.

Before a permit is issued, plans must be prepared by an experienced design professional and reviewed for compliance with zoning regulations and California Building Codes. Complete sets of plans, applicable reports, and permit applications can be submitted over the counter. Separate permit applications and submittal documents are required for Grading, Demolition, Building (Architectural and structural), Landscape, Site Lighting, Site Amenities, and Photovoltaic System.

Planning and Building Division review times for Building Permits are 10 Business Days for the 1st review and 5 Business Days for resubmittals. This applies to ADU, Duplex, Mixed-Use, Multi-Family, Single-Family, and Townhome projects. Mixed-Use and Multi-Family are shown as "10 Business Days*" on the page.

## Residential Permit Review Timelines (AB 2234) and Reviewing Departments

Under AB 2234, the City must determine whether a post-entitlement phase permit application is complete within 15 days of submission. Project reviews for developments of 25 units or fewer must be finalized within 30 days. Developments of 26 units or more must be finalized within 60 days.

The following City departments/divisions review plans: Building, Planning, Public Works, Traffic, Engineering, NPDES – Environmental Compliance, and Waste Management.

## Grading Permits

A Grading Permit is required for developments subject to LFMC Chapter 8.30 (Lake Forest Grading and Excavation Code). Examples of work that may require one:
- Grading, clearing, brushing, grubbing, excavating, or any other activity that results in a land disturbance of one (1) acre or more.
- Any other activity that disturbs less than one (1) acre but may cause or contribute to an unauthorized discharge of pollutants or a violation of any water quality requirement.
- Stormwater treatment facilities.
- Earthwork activities exceeding 50 cubic yards which also exceed two (2) feet in vertical depth, or create a cut slope greater than five (5) feet in vertical height and steeper than two horizontal to one vertical (2:1).

The Public Works Department issues Rough and Precise Grading Permits. Applications can be submitted to publicworks@lakeforestca.gov. Public Works & Engineering review times are 10 Business Days for the 1st review and 10 Business Days for resubmittals, for ADU, Duplex, Mixed-Use, Multi-Family, Single-Family, and Townhome projects.

Source: https://www.lakeforestca.gov/departments/community_development/building/residential_post_entitlement_processing.php

## Encroachment Permits — Application and Review Times

An encroachment permit is required for developments that need work within the public right-of-way or City lands. Examples include:
- Trenching for work on utility lines within the right-of-way
- Utility relocation
- Driveway expansion or curb, gutter, or sidewalk construction
- Placement of storage containers in the street
- Temporary lane closures and/or diversion of traffic to accommodate construction

The Public Works Department issues encroachment permits. Applications can be submitted to PWPermits@lakeforestca.gov. Public Works & Engineering review times are 10 Business Days for the 1st review and 10 Business Days for resubmittals, for ADU, Duplex, Mixed-Use, Multi-Family, Single-Family, and Townhome projects.

## Encroachment Permit — Who Must Obtain One

Encroachment permits are required from property owners and utility companies for any work conducted in the public right-of-way. The right-of-way mainly includes streets and sidewalks, meaning any public area adjacent to your home. A permit is required in advance for any improvements or any situation where you or your contractor plans to encroach upon the right-of-way. Examples are concrete work, brickwork, landscaping, and storing or placing anything in the street/sidewalk/public area during construction.

For more information, contact Randy L. Fiore, Senior Permit Technician, at (949) 461-3487 or submit a ticket at Ask Lake Forest. The Applicants Guide to Encroachment Permit is available on the permits page.

## Transportation Permits — Who Must Obtain One

Transportation Permits are required when a vehicle and/or vehicle load is larger than 8'6" in width, 14' in height, 40' in length (single unit), or 65' in length (combo unit). They are issued on a single trip or annual basis and are enforced by County Sheriffs and the California Highway Patrol. For more information, contact Randy Fiore, Senior Permit Technician, at (949) 461-3487 or submit a ticket at Ask Lake Forest. The Applicant Guide to Transportation Permit is available on the permits page.

## SolarAPP+ Automated Solar Permits

SolarAPP+ provides automated, instant solar plan review and permits. It is only available to appropriately-licensed contractors who have previously registered with SolarAPP+. It covers eligible residential, roof-mounted, retrofit photovoltaic systems, and eligible PV projects can be submitted through it effective September 25, 2023.

Eligibility:
- Main dwelling rooftop, permitted, residential structures only.
- No ballasted systems.
- Located in City of Lake Forest jurisdiction.
- Any change to the Main Service Panel requires a separate application, permit and fees.
- Projects that are not eligible must be submitted for in-house plan check by the City.

Process:
1. Register through the SolarAPP+ Registration Webpage, submit the project through SolarAPP+, and pay the SolarAPP+ processing fee, currently set at $25. Download the approval documents.
2. Create an account and log in to eLakeForest, click "Apply," and choose "SolarAPP+ Residential Solar Permit." Enter the address, permit details (description, square feet, valuation), three required contacts (applicant, contractor, and property owner), and general solar details including the required SolarAPP+ Approval Number. Attach the required documents. After clicking "Submit," wait about 30 seconds for the system to create a permit record.
3. For instant permit issuance, click "Add to Cart" and "Check Out" to pay the project invoice via MyGovPay (3.29% processing fee). A confirmation email receipt with the permit is sent.

Inaccurate or incorrect attachments will result in delays or the revocation of issued permits. No refunds will be issued for revoked permits.

Main panel upgrades need a separate Electrical Permit. A main panel upgrade (MPU) permit may be submitted in person, or by emailing the filled-out electrical permit application with the SCE spot sticker to the Building email.

Inspection: schedule through eLakeForest or call the automated phone line at (888) 890-6298. The required inspection type is SolarAPP+ (IVR Code 188).
