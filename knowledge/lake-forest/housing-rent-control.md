## Short-Term Rentals — Prohibition and Penalties

A Short-Term Rental (STR) provides short-term lodging for compensation under an oral or written agreement. STRs are not permitted in Lake Forest and are a prohibited use in all residential areas (a single exception is for Limited Home Rental). The City Council adopted the Short-Term Rental Ordinance in August 2017. It prohibits non-owner occupied properties from being leased for profit for less than 30 days. The City adopted it in response to resident complaints regarding noise, traffic, and parking.

A Short-Term Rental is a misdemeanor violation of the Lake Forest Municipal Code, with a fine of up to $1,000 per violation. If you are found in violation, the City may file a lien against your property to collect taxes and penalties. For more information on the Short-Term Rental Ordinance, call 949-461-3460. The department phone is (949) 461-3430.

## Short-Term Rentals — Transient Occupancy Tax (Hotel Tax) and HdL Registration

STRs are not legal, but remain subject to the collection of Transient Occupancy Taxes (TOT) for every night the property is used as an STR, plus penalties. The Hotel Tax is 10%. Paying Hotel Taxes is required but does not permit the use of a property as a short-term vacation rental. Failure to register and remit taxes results in penalties and interest, and for an STR each violation is also a misdemeanor offense.

The City contracts with Hinderliter De Llamas and Associates (HdL) for tax collection services. Both STRs and Limited Home Rentals must be registered with HdL. TOT payments through HdL may be made online at the TOT Payments Website. For more information about paying Hotel Taxes, call 949-528-3797.

## Limited Home Rentals

A Limited Home Rental is the rental of your entire primary residence for up to 3 periods fewer than 30 consecutive days (you cannot lease a single bedroom). These are owner-occupied residences that may be leased under limited circumstances but primarily serve as the resident's home. The resident cannot remain on the property when it is leased as a Limited Home Rental. A Limited Home Rental may be rented for a period of less than 30 consecutive days, but no more than 90 days in a calendar year.

A Limited Home Rental must be registered with the City and is subject to Transient Occupancy Taxes (10% Hotel Tax, for every night the property was used as a vacation rental). The City's fact sheet states: "Yes, but not more than 3 times per year and not more than 30 consecutive days." A Limited Home Rental owner must own the property and it must be their primary home. There is a Limited Home Rentals FAQ on the City's page.

Source: https://www.lakeforestca.gov/departments/finance/short-term_rentals.php

## Long-Term Rentals

A Long Term Rental is the rental of a property for more than 30 consecutive days, such as a month-to-month lease or year-round lease. Long Term Rentals are permitted as long as there is not more than one verbal or written agreement for the property. A property with more than one agreement is considered a Boarding House and is also prohibited by the City.

A Long-Term Rental is not subject to TOT and does not require approval by the City. You can lease a room or your entire property as a long-term rental on a month-to-month or longer lease. You must continue to pay property taxes.

## Fair Housing

The City is committed to furthering fair housing practices in the community and cooperates with the Fair Housing Council of Orange County to eliminate discriminatory practices countywide. An impediment to fair housing opportunity is any policy or practice that denies or negatively affects the provision of housing to a person in any protected class. Examples of a protected class include minorities, elderly, and disabled persons.

Documents available on the City's Housing page: "Fair Housing is Your Right Under the Law Q&A Flyer" (English and Spanish PDFs) and the Orange County Analysis of Impediments to Fair Housing Choice (08-06-20)(Final). Housing contact: Adrian Grijalva, Economic Development Manager, (949) 461-3567.

## Housing Rehabilitation Loan Program

The program offers a zero percent deferred interest loan for homes, condominiums and mobile homes. Applicants must meet income qualifications. Current household income and income for the past two years are evaluated. The 0% deferred loan has no monthly payments. Total payment is due if you are no longer residing in the home, or at the time of sale, refinance or transfer of title. Mobile homeowners are eligible to receive up to $25,000 and homeowners up to $40,000.

Other requirements include: applicants must live on the property, may not have received assistance previously, may not exceed the asset limitation, and must have sufficient equity in the home. Any code violations or health and safety items in the home MUST be corrected as a condition of funding. Homes built prior to January 1, 1978 must meet the new federal regulations regarding lead-based paint.

Eligible repairs include stucco, windows, painting, roofing, plumbing, electrical, energy and weatherization items, garage doors, fumigation, flooring and other items necessary for the health and safety of occupants. Examples of ineligible items are room additions, barbecue pits, ceiling fans, swimming pools, and fireplaces.

For questions, email economicdevelopment@lakeforestca.gov or contact Theresa Dobbs, (949) 461-3473, tdobbs@lakeforestca.gov. Submit all applications to Lake Forest City Hall, 100 Civic Center Drive, Lake Forest, CA 92630. A loan application PDF is available on the page.

## Neighborhood Paint Grant Program

The Paint Grant Program is funded with the City's Community Development Block Grant. It provides qualified homeowners exterior home painting and termite treatments as necessary. Grants for single family homes cannot exceed $12,000.00 and mobile homeowners cannot exceed $7,500.00.

Eligibility: (1) properties must be owner-occupied, (2) the home or mobile home must be in need of painting as determined by the City, (3) owners may not have assets in excess of $75,000, excluding the value of the home, (4) the existing exterior paint must be lead-free, and (5) households must not exceed maximum moderate-income limits, by household size (per HUD), for the previous two years.

To apply, email EconomicDevelopment@lakeforestca.gov to request an application or pick one up at the City Hall reception desk. The program is first come first served, as funding permits. Return completed applications to the City of Lake Forest, 100 Civic Center Drive, Lake Forest, California 92630, Attention: Economic Development. Contact: Theresa Dobbs, (949) 461-3473, tdobbs@lakeforestca.gov.

## Community Development Block Grant (CDBG)

CDBG is a federal grant program by the U.S. Department of Housing and Urban Development (HUD), allocated to local governments to provide decent housing, a suitable living environment, and expanded economic opportunities principally for persons of low and moderate income. Eligible programs and projects must satisfy one or more of the following objectives: provide a benefit to low- and moderate-income persons, prevent or eliminate slums or blight, and/or assist with a natural disaster.

Non-profit organizations interested in CDBG funding, or in learning whether their services are CDBG-eligible, should email Adrian Grijalva, Economic Development/Housing Manager. They can also be added to the interest list for the next round of funding. The Notice of Funding Availability (NOFA) is published in the fall of each calendar year.

For Fiscal Year 2026-27, the City awarded CDBG funds to: Age Well Senior Services, Boys & Girls Club of Laguna Beach, Camino Health Center, Fair Housing Council of OC, Family Assistance Ministries, Families Forward, and WISEPlace. Family Assistance Ministries provides assistance to homeless Lake Forest residents, including financial counseling, rental assistance, transitional and long-term housing.

Source: https://www.lakeforestca.gov/departments/economic_development/housing/community_development_block_grant.php

## Permanent Local Housing Allocation (PLHA)

The Building Homes and Jobs Act (SB 2, 2017) established a $75 recording fee on real estate documents to increase the supply of affordable homes in California. The State Department of Housing and Community Development (HCD) created the Permanent Local Housing Allocation (PLHA) program to allocate the funds to local governments. Ninety percent (90%) of PLHA is distributed to Entitlement Jurisdictions based on the 2017 CDBG Formula Allocation, and 10% through a competitive process for non-Entitlement Jurisdictions. Lake Forest is eligible to apply for the Formula Allocation as an entitlement jurisdiction.

The City Council held a public hearing at the City Council Chambers, 100 Civic Center Drive, at 6:30 p.m. on Tuesday, December 20, 2022 to consider the Lake Forest PLHA Plan, which summarizes proposed eligible activities over a five-year period. For questions, contact Adrian Grijalva at agrijalva@lakeforestca.gov or (949) 461-3567.

Source: https://www.lakeforestca.gov/departments/economic_development/housing/permanent_local_housing_allocation_plha.php
