## City Council — Members and Role

The City Council is made up of five Lake Forest residents elected to serve a four-year term. The Council selects a Mayor and Mayor Pro Tem annually. As a general law City, the Council makes policy decisions and gives guidance on issues related to the overall quality of life. The Council also serves as the Housing Authority Board and the Board of Directors for the Rancho Canada Financing Authority.

## City Council Meeting Schedule and Agendas

Under Lake Forest Municipal Code Section 2.04.010, Regular Meetings of the City Council are held the First and Third Tuesday of each month at 5:30 p.m. for Closed Session matters, if required. The Public Session begins at 6:30 p.m. or as soon thereafter as possible. Special Meeting times may be held at a different time and date as listed on the Agenda.

Agendas and related materials are available online at least 72 hours before the scheduled meeting. They are also available for public review at the Office of the City Clerk, 100 Civic Center Drive, Lake Forest, CA 92630. Residents can sign up for the Agenda notification mailing list for City Council, Community Services Commission, Planning Commission, and Traffic Commission meetings. Archived agendas for past City Council, Board, and Commission meetings and recorded meetings are available on the page.

## Public Comment at City Council Meetings

In person: Complete a Request to Speak form and submit it to the City Clerk before or during the meeting. When your name is called, step to the podium and begin with your name and city of residence. Comments are limited to three (3) minutes per person, and the public comment portion of the agenda has an overall limit of 30 minutes. Per the Brown Act, the City Council cannot take formal action on items raised during public comments.

Remotely: Remote public comment on agendized items or during non-agenda public comment can be given by smartphone, cellular phone, or land line. Register at publiccomment.lakeforestca.gov.

In writing: Email comments to council@lakeforestca.gov. Email comments are uploaded to the agenda packet and included in the official public record. Send all email comments no later than 5:00 p.m. on the day of the meeting. Written comments are not read aloud during the meeting.

## City Council Compensation

Members of the Lake Forest City Council receive a monthly stipend of $704 ($8,448 annually). Members also serve as the Lake Forest Housing Authority Board of Directors and Rancho Cañada Financing Authority Board of Directors, but do not receive any compensation for these offices.

City Council members are required by Federal law to participate in either Social Security or an alternative retirement type plan. They do not receive health benefits or any other related benefits.

Compensation is governed by California Government Code section 36516 and Lake Forest Municipal Code section 2.04.050. State law allows a yearly increase of up to 5% per year. Since incorporation in 1991, the Council has elected to receive two increases. The stipend was $600 per month from December 1991 to November 2006, $659 per month from December 2006 to November 2008, and $704 per month from December 2008 to present.

## Council Districts and District Elections

Since the November 2018 election, Lake Forest has elected members of the City Council from individual districts instead of by citywide vote. Voters in each district select one Council representative who must live in that district. The change was made because of the California Voting Rights Act of 2001 (CVRA).

To find your district, use the City Wide Map with Election Districts - 2022 online. Agree to the terms and conditions of the use agreement, click "ok", enter your address into the text box, and press enter. The City Council adopted a new district boundary map in 2022. The law requires the boundaries to be reviewed every 10 years following receipt of the US Census data.

Orange County has transitioned from polling locations to Vote Centers. Voters can vote at any of the designated centers in the County.

## Campaign Disclosure Statements

Under the Political Reform Act (California Government Code Sections 81000-91015), all candidates and committees supporting or opposing candidates or ballot measures must file campaign statements. Campaign Disclosure Statements are public documents, and any member of the public may inspect and copy any statement. Forms must be available as soon as possible during the agency's regular business hours, but not later than the second business day after the statement is received.

The Public Portal shows statements filed beginning in July 2015. For statements filed before July 2015, contact the City Clerk Office. Contributor addresses are redacted on the internet version. Full unredacted copies are available for review in the City Clerk's Office, or copies can be provided for $0.10 per page.

Filers can use the Filer Access Portal (NetFile), a web-based system, to submit Campaign Disclosure reports to the Electronic Filing System free of charge.

Source: https://www.lakeforestca.gov/city_government/city_council/campaign_disclosures.php

## Statements of Economic Interests (Form 700)

The Political Reform Act of 1974 requires persons holding specified public offices to file disclosures of investments, real property interests, and income. They must file within specified periods of assuming or leaving office and annually while holding the office. Elected officials file FPPC Form 700 with the City Clerk, who keeps a copy and forwards the original to the FPPC.

The following officials file Form 700: City Council Members, Commissioners, City Manager, City Attorney, and Finance Director. Public officials specified in Government Code Section 87200 file directly with the FPPC, as do candidates for elected positions. Employees designated in the City's Conflict of Interest Code and certain consultants file with the City, preferably through the Netfile system.

## Elections and Running for City Council

General municipal elections are held on the first Tuesday in November of even-numbered years. The City consolidates with the County of Orange Registrar of Voters Office for all municipal elections. The City Clerk is the filing officer and handles candidates' filings, nomination papers, City measures, legal notices, and campaign and conflict of interest filings. Council members are non-partisan. The Mayor and Mayor Pro Tem are selected by a vote of their peers annually at a City Council meeting in December, to serve the following calendar year.

In the General Election on November 3, 2026, Council seats in Districts 2, 3, and 4 are up for election. Candidates for those districts may pull nomination papers beginning Monday, July 13, 2026. Candidates must be registered to vote in their district before the City Clerk will issue nomination forms. All nomination forms and payment must be filed with the City Clerk by 5:00 p.m. on Friday, August 7, 2026. If an eligible incumbent does not file nomination papers by the deadline, the nomination period will extend four or five calendar days. Candidates must make appointments to pull and file nomination papers by calling the City Clerk's Office at (949) 461-3420.

For specific questions about the election process, contact the City Clerk during regular business hours at (949) 461-3420.

## Campaign Signs (Temporary Noncommercial Signs)

Signs on public property are prohibited. Illegally posted temporary noncommercial signs in the public right of way or on public property are a public nuisance and will be removed immediately (Lake Forest Municipal Code Section 9.164.100). Removed signs are tagged and stored at the southwest dumpster enclosure of the Civic Center Administration Building, 100 Civic Center Drive, Lake Forest, outside the police secured parking lot. The violator is contacted and allowed three (3) business days to pick up the sign(s). After that, unclaimed signs are discarded.

On private property, no permit is required. The maximum is 1 temporary noncommercial sign per street frontage per candidate or proponent/opponent of a ballot measure. The maximum size is 10 sq. ft. A stake sign may be at most 4 feet high, and a wall sign may be no higher than the top of eave or parapet wall. Signs must NOT be displayed earlier than 60 DAYS PRIOR to an election and must be REMOVED no later than 10 DAYS AFTER an election. Posting signs on private property without the owner's or property manager's consent is illegal and a public nuisance.

Removing signs without the sign owner's consent is against the law. California Penal Code Section 488 makes it a petty theft misdemeanor.

Contacts: for signs on private property, Code Enforcement at (949) 461-3465. For campaign signs on public right-of-way, Public Works Maintenance Division at (949) 461-3575.

Source: https://www.lakeforestca.gov/city_government/elections/campaign_signs.php
