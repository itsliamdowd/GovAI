## Traffic Division — Services

The Traffic Division's primary focus is to enhance traffic operations and traffic safety for the community. It works with the Engineering Division on Capital Improvement Projects related to traffic infrastructure and analysis. It also works on citywide and neighborhood traffic and parking improvements, including:
- Implementing neighborhood traffic calming measures.
- Maintaining and replacing traffic signal components.
- Maintaining City pavement and curb markings and street signs.
- Responding to citizen concerns by investigating traffic and parking concerns.
- Conducting traffic studies and maintaining records on traffic counts and speed surveys.
- Working with Police Services and other agencies to improve traffic conditions in the City.

Source: https://www.lakeforestca.gov/departments/public_works/traffic/index.php
