## Wildfire Preparedness — Fire Risk Reduction Community

Dana Point is a Fire Risk Reduction Community, a statewide designation adopted by Resolution No. 2026-01. Dana Point earned it by adopting updated wildfire safety regulations, strengthening its Public Safety Element, implementing local fire safety standards, and partnering with the Orange County Fire Authority to educate residents about defensible space, home hardening, and wildfire preparedness. Property owners may be eligible for insurance discounts through California's Safer from Wildfire program. The City will be prioritized for wildfire prevention and mitigation grants.

## Fire Hazard Severity Zones — What State Law May Require

California has updated its Fire Hazard Severity Zone (FHSZ) maps. Residents can use the interactive map to enter an address and see whether a property is in a designated zone. Depending on the designation, State law may require the following:
- **Defensible space:** Properties in Very High Fire Hazard Severity Zones must maintain 100 feet of defensible space around structures, with the most intensive vegetation management within the first 30 feet.
- **Home sales:** Sellers must disclose a property's FHSZ designation and provide documentation showing compliance with defensible space requirements.
- **Building standards:** New construction and qualifying remodels must comply with California's Wildland-Urban Interface (WUI) building standards, which include fire-resistant roofing, exterior walls, windows, vents, and other wildfire-resistant features.
- **Zone 0 (coming soon):** The State is developing new regulations that will require an ember-resistant area within five feet of structures by limiting combustible vegetation and materials.

Questions: State Fire Marshal, FHSZinformation@fire.ca.gov | (916) 633-7655. City of Dana Point, jmccloskey@danapoint.org. California Department of Insurance, (800) 927-4357.

## PulsePoint Cardiac Emergency App

PulsePoint is a mobile application that notifies nearby potential rescuers of a cardiac emergency while emergency responders are still en route. It alerts CPR-trained citizens of cardiac arrests occurring nearby in public places. When a participating fire/EMS agency receives an emergency call, PulsePoint notifies users in the vicinity so they can give early CPR before EMS arrives. The app shows the exact location of the emergency on a map, provides walking directions to the scene, and shows the locations of Automated External Defibrillators. More information: www.pulsepoint.org.

## Code Enforcement — Overview, Complaints, and Office Hours

The Code Enforcement Division enforces ordinances and laws to preserve and enhance quality of life in Dana Point. The Community Development Department aims to gain voluntary compliance and will use enforcement measures only when voluntary compliance efforts have been exhausted. To submit a complaint, use the Report to Dana Point Mobile Application. Complaints are always confidential and never shared with the violator.

A Code Enforcement Officer is available at the Development Services Counter Monday through Friday 8:00am to 10:00am to answer questions about code compliance. Officers have field duty during the day and are usually away from the office after 10:00am.

## Graffiti Removal and Reporting

To report graffiti, submit a Request for Service online via Dana Point Connect, or call the Graffiti Hotline at (949) 248-3573 or, during working hours, (949) 248-3500.

On private property, the property owner is notified immediately and typically has 48 hours to remove it. If the owner does not remove it, the City will have it removed, usually within 5 working days. On City property, the removal contractor is contacted immediately and removal is within 48 hours.

## Signs, Garage Sales, and Fences

- **Portable business signs:** These must be approved and permitted through the Planning Division. Code Enforcement Officers may instruct businesses to remove the signs until a permit is obtained.
- **Signs in the public right-of-way:** The sign code prohibits signs on or extending over any public property, public easement, or public right-of-way, including real estate and for-sale signs. Code Enforcement will remove such signs and retain them for five (5) calendar days. There is no fine to reclaim confiscated signs. Unclaimed signs are disposed of.
- **Garage sales:** Residential properties are limited to four (4) garage/yard sales per year. Garage sale signs are not permitted in the public right-of-way, including on street signs, medians, or in parks.
- **Fences and walls:** DPMC Section 9.05.120 exempts lightweight fences less than 6 feet and masonry walls less than 36 inches from requiring permits, unless permits are required as a condition of project approval.

## Short-Term Rental Rules for Guests

Rules for vacation rentals in Dana Point:
- **Noise:** No outside noise from the short-term rental shall be heard during the quiet hours of 10:00 P.M to 7:00 A.M.
- **Parking:** A maximum of two parking spaces are allowed per unit unless additional parking is approved by the City. Use the driveway and/or garage before street spots.
- **Occupancy:** Maximum overnight occupancy is two persons per bedroom plus two additional persons within the unit. Daytime maximum occupancy is 2.5 times the overnight occupancy but not to exceed 20 people. The property owner may impose more restrictive limits.
- **Trash:** Trash shall not be stored where it is visible from the street and sidewalk. The owner provides sufficient trash containers and takes the trash and recycling to the curb.

Guests may be cited or fined by the City for creating a disturbance. Surrounding neighbors have a 24-hour hotline to report complaints. A Good Neighbor Brochure (PDF, 688KB) is available.

## Weed Abatement Program

The City administers an annual Weed Abatement Program, authorized by state law, to abate noxious weeds on private property. Many of the areas considered are vacant lots, empty slopes, and open space corridors. The City Council must declare weeds a public nuisance on known properties, give notice to property owners, and give owners the opportunity to abate the weeds or be heard on why they should not be abated. Native brush or chaparral outside of the fuel break zone shall not be removed, but the property owner may have deadwood or litter removed subject to a plan reviewed and approved by the Department of Community Development. For questions, contact Antoinette Kulick at (949) 248-3565. A Weed Abatement and Vegetation Hazard Reduction Information Sheet (PDF, 33KB) is available.

Source: https://www.danapoint.org/City-Government/Community-Development/Code-Enforcement/Weed-Abatement-and-Landscaping

## Water Quality — Storm Water and Runoff Rules

Dana Point Municipal Code Chapter 15.10, Storm Water/Surface Runoff Water Quality, effectively prohibits discharges into the storm water drainage systems and reduces pollutant loads to the maximum extent possible. Code Enforcement Officers investigate all complaints about water quality issues within their district.

Construction sites must use Best Management Practices (BMPs). No solid waste, petroleum byproducts, soil particulate, construction waste materials, or wastewater from construction sites or activities may be placed, conveyed or discharged into the street, gutter or storm drain system. Improper irrigation system timing (over-watering) is also addressed on the page.
