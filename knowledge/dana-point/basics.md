## City Council Meetings — Public Comment

Complete a Speaker Slip by 4:00PM on the meeting day at danapoint.org/comment. Public comment can be provided in these ways:
- Zoom: https://zoom.us/j/96165825101
- Phone: (669) 900-6833, Meeting ID 96165825101
- Email: comment@danapoint.org
- Mail/drop off to City of Dana Point, City Clerk: 33282 Golden Lantern, Dana Point, CA 92629

To be provided to the City Council before the meeting, written comments must be received by 4:00 P.M. on the day of the meeting. Comments should include the meeting date and the Agenda Item number, if applicable. Written comments will not be read aloud during the meeting, but they are a public record and are included in the meeting record.

To comment virtually or by telephone, submit a Speaker Slip by 4:00 P.M. on the day of the meeting so staff can identify you during the public comment period. On Zoom, use any web browser or the Zoom app. By telephone, dial (669) 900-6833 and enter Meeting ID 96165825101#. Participants are muted until called on for their comment time. For technical assistance before the meeting, call (949) 248-3500.

## Public Bids

A list of current bid opportunities is posted on PlanetBids. The Contract Plans and Specifications for applicable projects can be downloaded from the website for free. To bid on a project, every Bidder is required to purchase a hard copy set of the Contract Documents from the City of Dana Point. Upon purchase, the Bidder is added to the City's official plan holder's list. Bidders who have not purchased a hard copy set and are not on the official plan holder's list are deemed non-responsive, may not bid on the project, and the bid will be returned unopened.

For questions about a specific project, contact the City department listed in the description for that project. Plan Holder Lists for projects currently open may be obtained by contacting the assigned City department listed on each bid.

Source: https://www.danapoint.org/City-Government/City-Clerk/Public-Notices-Bids

## Vendor Registration

Vendors interested in doing business with the City can register online through the City of Dana Point Vendor Portal. Registered vendors receive email notifications when a bid or RFP is posted that matches the categories selected during registration. It is the registered vendor's responsibility to maintain current information on the Vendor Portal. All technical questions about the online bid system or vendor portal should be directed to PlanetBids.

Source: https://www.danapoint.org/City-Government/City-Clerk/Public-Notices-Bids
