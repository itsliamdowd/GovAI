# Public Safety & Code Enforcement

## Fire Department — Services and Contacts

The Brea Fire Department's primary mission is life safety services. It provides 24-hour emergency response to fires, explosions, hazardous materials incidents, medical emergencies, accidents, and miscellaneous public assistance requests. It also runs a Fire Prevention and Emergency Preparedness Program covering fire inspections, hazardous process permitting, fire code enforcement, public education, and business emergency planning.

Emergency: 9-1-1. Fire Non-Emergency: (714) 738-6122. Fire Administration: (714) 990-7644, Breafireinfo@cityofbrea.gov. Location: Brea Civic & Cultural Center - Level 2, 1 Civic Center Circle, Brea, CA 92821. Fire Administration hours: Mon-Thur 7:30 a.m.-5:30 p.m.; Alternate Fridays: 8 a.m.-5 p.m.; Closed for lunch: 12-1 p.m.

## Emergency Preparedness and CERT

The City of Brea encourages those who live and/or work in Brea to learn how to be prepared for a disaster. Brea has trained hundreds of residents through the national Community Emergency Response Team (CERT) program. CERT volunteers attend many local events to answer questions and help neighbors prepare.

## PulsePoint App (Cardiac Arrest Alerts)

PulsePoint is a free app that alerts nearby citizens when someone needs critical lifesaving cardiopulmonary resuscitation (CPR). It also shows the location of nearby public access automated external defibrillators (AED). Metro Cities Fire Authority, working with the Metro Cities' dispatch center (Metro Net), is the first agency in Orange County to implement the app.

Source: http://www.ci.brea.ca.us/291/Fire

## Ambulance Program

The Brea Fire Department is developing an in-house ambulance program. The fire department will own and operate the ambulances, and the EMTs on them will be City employees, rather than contracting with a private ambulance company. The program was anticipated to be fully implemented by January 2025.

Source: http://www.ci.brea.ca.us/291/Fire
