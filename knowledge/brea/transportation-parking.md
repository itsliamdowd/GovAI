## Overnight Street Parking and Annual Parking Permits

Parking on all city streets is restricted to 30 minutes between the hours of 2-6 a.m. Residents without adequate on-site parking (garage, carport, or driveway) can apply for an annual parking permit at the Community Development Department on the third floor of the Brea Civic and Cultural Center. Qualification information and the application are in the Community Development section of the city website under "Street Parking Permits." An online temporary, self-issued permit is also available there.

Source: https://www.ci.brea.ca.us/Faq.aspx?QID=63

## Ash Street Cottages — Resident and Guest Parking Permits

Guests can use the Self-Issued Parking Permit to park on the street when visiting. Print the form, complete it, and have guests place it on their vehicle's dashboard. The Ash Street Cottages parking permit stickers are for residents only. The permit only allows residents to park on the restricted streets, and street sweeping restrictions are still in effect. The permit does not exempt vehicles from the 1-hour parking restriction on the east side of Walnut Ave.

Source: https://www.ci.brea.ca.us/Faq.aspx?QID=146
