## Library (Brea Branch, OC Public Libraries)

The Brea Library is one of 33 branches of the OC Public Libraries system and is on the Plaza level of the Brea Civic & Cultural Center, 1 Civic Center Circle, Brea, CA 92821. Phone: (714) 671-1722. Hours: Monday through Thursday 10 a.m. to 7 p.m.; Friday and Saturday 9 a.m. to 5 p.m.; Sunday closed. ## Utility Services — Starting Service, Deposits, Billing

Utility Billing handles water and trash service requests, customer inquiries and payment processing. Call 714-990-7687 to establish or discontinue service or to ask about your bill. For leak detection or water service troubleshooting, call the Public Works Department at (714) 990-7691.

A refundable deposit is due within 10 days of signing up for service. It is refunded after 12 consecutive on-time payments or when service is discontinued. Bills include charges for water, sewer, trash, street sweeping, and urban runoff. Bills are due on the 20th of the month. Accounts delinquent for at least 60 days may be subject to turn-off and a Hang Tag will be issued.

Utility Billing hours: Monday - Thursday 7:30 a.m. - 5:30 p.m.; every other Friday 8 a.m. - 5 p.m. Front Counter Hours are 8 a.m. - 5 p.m., closed on alternate Fridays. After-Hours / Emergency: (714) 990-7911.

## Utility Bill Payment Options

AutoPay deducts the water bill monthly from a checking account. To sign up, call the Water Division at 714-990-7687 or mail the application to City of Brea P.O. Box 2237 Brea, CA 92822-2237. E-Pay, with Paymentus Corporation, allows payment by phone or online 24/7 by electronic check, debit card, or credit card.

A payment drop box is at the Brea Civic & Cultural Center (corner of Birch St. and Randolph Ave) in the circular driveway along Birch Street. If you have a shut-off notice or your service is locked off, call 714-990-7687. Online payments made at that point will NOT prevent a lock-off.

## Reporting Coyote Sightings

Report coyote sightings and encounters to Orange County Animal Care. Daytime: (714) 935-6848. Evening: (714) 259-1122, to be used for an aggressive or injured coyote. If someone experiences a coyote attack or threat, call 911 immediately. The City also runs a Wildlife Watch Program with the California Department of Fish & Wildlife.
