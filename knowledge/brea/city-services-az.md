## Trash Collection and Bulky Item Pickup

Trash collection is provided by the city through Republic Services, a private contractor, also known as Brea Disposal. The charge is part of the City's monthly billing, which also includes water, sewer and storm drains. To request a bulky item pick-up or to order a bin for construction projects, call (714) 238-2444. Brea Disposal at 714-238-2444 also handles customer service, rental bins and hazardous waste disposal questions.

There is no trash collection on these major holidays: Memorial Day, July 4th, Labor Day, Thanksgiving, Christmas Day and New Year's Day. Residential trash collection is then delayed by one day through the rest of that week.

## Household Hazardous Waste Collection Center

It is illegal to dump hazardous waste materials into your regular trash. Dispose of hazardous waste at the Household Hazardous Waste Collection Center, 1071 N. Blue Gum St., Anaheim, CA 92825. The center is open Tuesday - Saturday, 9:00 a.m. - 1:00 p.m.

Accepted items include auto and furniture polish, batteries, herbicides and pesticides, household cleaners, motor oil and auto products, paint and paint-related products, and pool / hobby supplies. For more information, call (714) 834-6752.

## Water Supply and Starting Utility Service

Brea's water is a blended supply purchased from the Metropolitan Water District and the California Domestic Water Company. It is distributed through the city-owned system. City utility bills include water, sewer, street sweeping and trash collection, and they are distributed to customers monthly. Call (714) 990-7687 to set up your service.

## Utility Billing — Deposit, Due Date and Shut-Off

The City of Brea requires a refundable deposit. The deposit is due within 10 days of signing up for service. It is refunded after 12 consecutive on-time payments or when service is discontinued. To waive the deposit, the City accepts a letter of credit from another utility. The letter must show on-time payments, no returned checks, and no shut-offs for non-payment for a period of 12 months or longer.

The utility bill includes charges for water, sewer, trash, street sweeping and urban runoff. Bills are due on the 20th of the month, and unpaid bills are subject to delinquent charges. Accounts delinquent for at least 60 days may be subject to turn-off and a Hang Tag will be issued. If you cannot pay, call 714-990-7687 to discuss payment arrangements. If you have received a shut-off notice or your water service has been locked off, contact the Utility Billing Service office at 714-990-7687.

## Brea Library (Orange County Library, Brea Branch)

The Brea Library is one of 33 branches of the OC Public Libraries system. It is located on the Plaza level of the Brea Civic & Cultural Center, 1 Civic Center Circle, Brea, CA 92821. Phone: (714) 671-1722.

Library hours are Monday through Thursday 10 a.m. to 7 p.m., Friday and Saturday 9 a.m. to 5 p.m., and Sunday closed. Programming includes storytimes, craft programs, book clubs, individualized computer instruction, a writers group and the annual Summer Reading Program. See ocpl.org for the calendar of upcoming events. To reach the Friends of the Brea Library, call Pat at (714) 529-4782.
