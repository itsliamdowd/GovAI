# Housing & Rent

## Homebuyer Program — Affordable Housing Ordinance Units

The City has in effect an Affordable Housing Ordinance, which requires most new residential developments to provide at least 5% to 30% of their units for sale at affordable below-market prices to qualified buyers. To be placed on the interest list for all future affordable housing opportunities, you may complete an application. Applications are also available for pickup at the Housing Information Kiosk located on the 3rd floor of the Brea Civic and Cultural Center.

If you are interested in the down payment assistance program offered by the Orange County Housing and Community Services Department, you may visit their website to learn more.

Source: https://ci.brea.ca.us/169/Homebuyer-Assistance-Program

## Senior Rental Programs — Rent-Restricted Senior Apartments

The following senior apartment complexes offer rent-restricted programs, with rent lower than market rate. BREAL Senior Apartments (age 65 and older) is at 111 N. Orange Ave. Orange Villa Senior Apartments (age 55 and older) is at 137 N. Orange Ave.

Vintage Canyon Senior Apartments (age 62 and older) is at 855 N. Brea Blvd. It has 105 low income units, allows small pets only, and can be reached at 714-529-4261.

Source: https://www.ci.brea.ca.us/172/Senior-Rental-Programs

## Affordable Housing Ordinance — Requirements for New Development

The City of Brea updated its Affordable Housing Ordinance in 2023. It requires new residential development projects of 10 or more units to designate at least 5% to 30% of the units as affordable.

The City also created an Affordable Housing Guide for the development community. It is a directory for developers interested in creating affordable housing. It outlines the benefits of each housing incentive and opportunity, from Accessory Dwelling Units to Density Bonuses, and includes the City's development flow chart.

Source: https://www.ci.brea.ca.us/1712/Affordable-Housing-Ordinance

## Housing Element (2021–2029)

The Housing Element is a required chapter of the City's General Plan. It identifies policies and programs to meet existing and projected future housing needs for all economic segments in Brea. On September 8, 2022, HCD certified the City's Housing Element.

The 6th Cycle planning period runs from October 2021 to October 2029. Brea's Final RHNA for 2021-2029 is 2,365 units. State law requires the Housing Element to be updated every eight years.

Source: https://ci.brea.ca.us/1701/Housing-Element

## Regional Approach to Homelessness (North Orange County, archived 2019 notice)

Brea is part of a plan with 10 other North Orange County cities to address homelessness in the North Service Planning Area (North SPA). The plan includes two shelters, called "Navigation Centers," in Buena Park and Placentia that provide up to 250 additional beds. The Centers were anticipated to open in early 2020.

The Settlement Agreement was approved on June 28, 2019. It allows cities to enforce their local ordinances regarding overnight camping and other related matters. The County of Orange designated three Service Planning Areas (North, Central and South) to direct homeless individuals and families to the county's Coordinated Entry System.

Source: https://www.ci.brea.ca.us/CivicAlerts.aspx?AID=1627&ARC=3568

## Brea Resource Center — Affordable Counseling

The Brea Resource Center (BRC) offers affordable counseling for individuals of all ages, as well as for family groups. Counseling hours are by appointment only Monday through Friday. Session fees range from $30 to $50 for Brea residents, with a one-time intake fee. Fees are $40 to $60 for non-residents, with a one-time intake fee.

To get started, call the Brea Resource Center at 714-990-7150 to set up an intake appointment. The Resource Center is at 695 Madison Way, Brea, CA 92821. Hours are Monday – Thursday 9:00 a.m. – 5:30 p.m. and Friday 9:00 a.m. – 5:00 p.m.

Source: https://www.ci.brea.ca.us/254/Counseling-Support-Groups
