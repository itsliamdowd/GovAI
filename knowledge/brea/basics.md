# City Government Basics

## City Government Overview

Brea is governed by a five-member City Council, elected by residents to serve four-year terms. Each year, the council elects one of its members to serve a one-year term as mayor. Brea is a general law city, and the council appoints a city manager to implement policies and oversee city departments. Three standing citizen commissions directly advise the City Council. Other task-related committees are formed as needed.

City contact: City of Brea, 1 Civic Center Circle, Brea, CA 92821. Phone: 714-990-7600.

## City Council Top Priorities

The City Council's listed top priorities are:
- Provide high quality safety services in order to ensure a safe and secure environment.
- Maintain conservative fiscal policies in order to protect and enhance our financial resources and maintain our investment in the city itself.
- Foster Brea's "family centered" community by supporting Brea's youth and families.
- Protect open space and determine the future of Brea's sphere of influence.
- Promote regional transportation improvements that will benefit future generations of Breans.

## Council-Manager Format, Mayor and City Manager

Brea was incorporated in 1917. The City Council is the official and legally constituted legislative body for the City of Brea. It sets all policy necessary for the provision of public services and adopts ordinances and resolutions as expressions of those policies. The City Council hires the city manager, who implements council policy through the city operating departments. The council also appoints the city attorney to provide counsel on city matters.

The five council members are elected at large to serve one four-year term. Elections are held every two years (in the even numbered years) to fill alternately two and three seats. Each year, the council elects one of its members to serve a one-year term as mayor.

Members of the Planning Commission, Cultural Arts Commission, Traffic Committee, and Parks, Recreation, and Human Services Commission are appointed by members of the council.

## Meetings and Agendas — Finding Agendas and Minutes

Public meetings in the City of Brea are conducted in accordance with the California Ralph M. Brown Act. The City uses the AgendaLink system to publish current meeting agendas and minutes. Meetings are arranged by date, with the most recent at the top of the list. Click "Watch" to view the City Council Meeting, or click "Agenda" or "Minutes" to see just the documents. Keyword search of past agendas only applies to agendas from October 19, 2023, to the present.

Agendas dated prior to October 23, 2023 are in the Archived Agendas and Minutes. Archived agendas and minutes are available for the City Council, Art in Public Places Committee, Cultural Arts Commission, Development Committee, Finance Committee, Investment Advisory Committee, Oversight Board, Parks, Recreation & Human Services Commission, Planning Commission and Traffic Committee. To receive notification of agenda publications and materials, use the agenda subscription sign-up on the Meetings and Agendas page.

For help with the system, contact the City Clerk's Office at (714) 990 7756 or cityclerksgroup@cityofbrea.gov. If you cannot find the meeting records you are looking for, you may submit a records request through the City's Public Records Act Request portal.

## Public Comment at Meetings — In Person and Zoom

In person: arrive at Council Chambers or the meeting location listed on the agenda. If you wish to speak, you may fill out a speaker card at the digital kiosk. Speakers are called in the order they are registered. Each speaker is generally limited to five (5) minutes, unless the Mayor/Chair announces otherwise.

By Zoom (City Council Meetings only): in accordance with SB 707, effective July 1, 2026, members of the public may participate in City Council Meetings remotely via Zoom teleconference. Use the Zoom link or call-in number provided on the agenda and join with your full name. Use the "Raise Hand" button in Zoom, or if calling in, dial *9 to raise or lower your hand. If calling in, press *6 to unmute to speak. Remote public comments are taken once all in-person public comments have been heard under each public comment period.

## Elections — General Municipal Election

Brea consolidates its regular General Municipal Elections with statewide elections held on the first Tuesday after the first Monday in November of each even-numbered year to fill expired City Council and City Treasurer terms. The City Clerk serves as the local elections official and coordinates with the County of Orange in conducting municipal elections.

The next General Municipal Election will be held on Tuesday, November 3, 2026, for three (3) at-large City Council Members, each for a term of four (4) years. The official candidate list: Andrew Herrera, Cecilia Hupp, Blake Perez, Blair Stewart, Steven C. Vargas. For registering to vote, verifying voter information or finding a polling location, the city refers residents to the Orange County Registrar of Voters (https://ocvote.gov/).
