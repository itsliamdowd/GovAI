## Temporary Banner Permits — Fee Waiver

Businesses must obtain a temporary banner permit from City Hall. The City Council approved a 2-year fee waiver for all temporary banner permits, so the permit, which normally carries a $60 City permit fee, is free. The waiver is effective until June 30, 2019. All other rules still apply, including the 24-square-foot size requirement and the 42-day maximum period.

## MV Shuttle

The City launched the MV Shuttle in October 2016 to provide transportation services in parts of the City that used to be served by the Orange County Transportation Authority. As of June 2017, the route connected the Norman P. Murray Community Center, 24932 Veterans Way, with the Laguna Niguel/Mission Viejo Metrolink Station. A local circulator study was launched in 2017 to assess the shuttle's performance and explore demand for more services or destinations. Project information is at https://MissionViejoTransitPlan.com. You can also call Director of Public Works Mark Chagnon at 949-470-3091.

## Council Elections — District-Based Election Hearings (2017)

Mission Viejo's at-large election system means electors from the entire City choose each member of the City Council. In November 2017 the City Council began a series of five public hearings on the process for transitioning from at-large to district-based council member elections. The hearings responded to a demand letter from a Malibu-based law firm claiming the at-large system violates the California Voting Rights Act of 2001 by diluting the vote of the City's Latino residents.

The first hearing was Tuesday, November 14, 2017, and the second was Tuesday, November 28, 2017. Each meeting began at 6 p.m., with the hearing starting sometime thereafter, in the City Hall Council Chamber at 200 Civic Center. Residents could weigh in on the composition of the districts, the content of draft maps and the proposed sequence of elections. The agenda for the November 14 meeting was posted by 5 p.m. Friday, November 10 at https://missionviejo.gov/stream.
