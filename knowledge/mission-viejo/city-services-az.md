## Animal Services — Contact Information and Hours

Mission Viejo Animal Services serves the cities of Mission Viejo, Aliso Viejo, Laguna Niguel, Laguna Hills and Rancho Santa Margarita, including some parts of Trabuco Canyon and Dove Canyon. Animal Services Center (8:00 am - 5:00 pm): 949-470-3045. Customer Service (8:00 am - 5:00 pm): 949-452-8394. Animal Licensing (8:00 am - 5:00 pm): 949-470-8431. Volunteers (8:00 am - 5:00 pm): 949-470-3037. Email: animalinfo@cityofmissionviejo.org.

Visit the Shelter from 12:00 pm - 4:00 pm seven days a week. The Shelter is closed on major holidays.

## Animal Services — Field Officer Calls

Animal Services Officers are on duty every day from 7 am to 10 pm to respond to calls for stray dogs running loose, found animals for pick-up, and deceased animals. Call the main phone line (949-470-3045) and press 2 to leave a message, and an Officer will respond as soon as possible.

## La Paws Dog Park

La Paws Park is at 25100 Felipe Road, just south of Gilleran Park. The dog park is 6 acres. Three acres are landscaped slopes with a walking trail that loops around the park and sitting areas overlooking Arroyo Trabuco Canyon. The other 3 acres are divided into dog play areas, with sections for large and small dogs and a separate area for one owner to use for up to 30 minutes at a time.

Source: https://www.missionviejo.gov/news/resolve-embrace-mission-viejo-parks-trails-and-open-space-year

## Pavion Park

Pavion Park spreads across 5 acres and was the first universally accessible playground in Orange County when it opened in 1988. It has play equipment designated by age, a giant interactive maze, park benches, walking trails and large grassy areas. Mission Viejo has more than 50 parks, trails and open spaces.
