## Temporary Banner Permits — Fee Waiver

Businesses that want to hang temporary banners advertising their services and events must obtain a permit from City Hall. The permit fee is $60. The City Council approved a 2-year fee waiver for all temporary banner permits, so the permit is free. The fee waiver is effective until June 30, 2019.

All other rules still apply, including the 24-square-foot size requirement and the 42-day maximum period.
