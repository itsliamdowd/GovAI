## Point-in-Time Homeless Count — Volunteering

The City of Mission Viejo sought volunteers for Orange County's Point-in-Time Homeless Count and Survey on Saturday, January 28 (page dated January 19, 2017). The Point-in-Time Count and Survey is a biennial count to gauge homelessness in the region and what it will take to help connect people to secure housing. For more information, visit www.pointintimeoc.org or email pointintime@211oc.org.

## Homeless Support — Donation Drop-Off

The City offered two drop-off points for donations of items to be distributed to the homeless in the Mission Viejo area: the Norman P. Murray Community and Senior Center, and Mission Viejo City Hall at 200 Civic Center. Items were accepted through January 25. Requested items: beanies/knit caps, rain ponchos, toothbrushes/toothpaste, AA & AAA batteries, socks, gallon-sized Ziploc bags, deodorant, toilet paper, shampoo/conditioner, hand sanitizer, and hair comb/brush.
