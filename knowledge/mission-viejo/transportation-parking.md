## MV Shuttle: Free Local Shuttle Service

The MV Shuttle is free to ride. It runs from the Norman P. Murray Community Center to the Metrolink train station and many places in between. Riders use it to get to Saddleback College, Mission Hospital, Capistrano Valley High School, The Shops at Mission Viejo and other locations. The City rolled out the service in fall 2016.

The schedule is at cityofmissionviejo.org/MVShuttle. Riders are invited to tag the City using #MVShuttle.

## MV Shuttle Ridership Contest (May 2017)

The City held a ridership contest throughout May 2017 for people who ride the MV Shuttle. From May 1-31, riders collected a card from the driver each time they rode. Riders then dropped off their cards between June 1-9 at the Norman P. Murray Community and Senior Center, 24932 Veterans Way. The top three riders with the most cards each received two round-trip Metrolink tickets. For more information about the ridership contest, call 949-470-3061.
