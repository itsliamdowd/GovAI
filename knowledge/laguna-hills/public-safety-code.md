## Code Enforcement — What the Division Handles

The Code Enforcement Division responds to potential violations of the City of Laguna Hills Municipal Code. It handles concerns about property maintenance, zoning regulations, construction, public nuisances, signs, and land use. When a violation is identified, the responsible parties are contacted and asked to abate it, giving them every opportunity to comply voluntarily. If voluntary compliance cannot be achieved, administrative citations are used.

The division's page also lists information on graffiti removal, short term rentals, and weed abatement.

## Fire Hazard Severity Zones — Updated State Maps

CAL FIRE's Office of the State Fire Marshal recently updated the state's Fire Hazard Severity Zone (FHSZ) Maps for Local Responsibility Areas, which include Laguna Hills. This is the first update since 2007. Property owners can use CAL FIRE's interactive map to check their property's designation (Moderate, High, or Very High) by entering an address. An updated map for Laguna Hills is available as a PDF on the city page.

Under California Government Code 51178.5, the City must make the updated information available for public review and comment within 30 days. Under Government Code 51179, the City must designate moderate, high, and very high fire hazard severity zones by ordinance within 120 days.

The City may include additional areas as very high fire hazard severity zones, following a finding supported by substantial evidence. It may also include additional areas as moderate and high zones. It may not decrease the level of any zone identified by the State Fire Marshal.

## Fire Hazard Severity Zones — Requirements for Property Owners

Very high zone:
- New buildings require a 100-foot buffer of defensible space.
- New buildings must meet ignition-resistant construction requirements (Chapter 7A of the California Building Code).
- Natural Hazard Real Estate Disclosures are required at the time of sale.

High zone:
- New buildings must meet ignition-resistant construction requirements (Chapter 7A of the California Building Code).
- Natural Hazard Real Estate Disclosures are required at time of sale.
- Defensible space requirements do not currently apply.

Moderate zone: currently, there are no requirements.

For insurance questions, call the California Department of Insurance hotline at 1-800-927-4357 or visit http://www.insurance.ca.gov/. FAQs are available in English and Spanish (PDF).

## Wildfire Evacuation Zones and Ready, Set, Go!

Laguna Hills wildfire evacuation zones are LH Zone 1, 2, 5, 10, 12, 13, 14, 18, and 19. A Wildfire Evacuation Zone flyer (PDF) shows which zone you are in.

OCFA's Ready, Set, Go! campaign advises:
- **Ready:** Survey your home for wildfire vulnerabilities and create a defensible space.
- **Set:** Pack a Disaster Kit and a Go! Bag with items to sustain life for 5 to 7 days after a potential evacuation. Key items are prescription medications, important documents, food, clothing, and water.
- **Go:** Plan when to leave, where to go, what to take, and how to get there. Map several routes out of your neighborhood in case roads are blocked.

In a disaster, evacuation routes, shelter locations and other emergency information will be relayed through AlertOC, the City's Public Information Officer, and first responders on-scene.

Source: https://www.lagunahillsca.gov/351/Emergency-Management

## Laguna Hills Alerts (Everbridge Nixel)

To sign up for Laguna Hills city-wide emergency alerts and other notifications, text 92653 to 888-777. The subscription is through Everbridge Nixel, which supports communication by text, email, voice messages, and the Nixle mobile app. Messages can be sent within a radius from .25-miles to 10 miles.

To manage your subscription, visit the Nixel resident login page at nixle.com and select "Resident" in the top right section. If you subscribed by text and do not have a password, text "PASSWORD" to 888-777.

Alert terminology:
- **Evacuation Order:** Mandatory evacuation order with hard road closures. Evacuated individuals will not be allowed to enter the area until the order is lifted.
- **Evacuation Warning:** Voluntary evacuation to give advance warning in a slow moving event.
- **Shelter-in-Place:** You are directed to stay in your residence, school, workplace, or other building because staying where you are is safer than evacuating.

## Safe Homes for O.C. Seniors Grant Program

The program may provide grants of up to $20,000 to eligible senior homeowners for health, safety, accessibility, and home repairs that support independent living. It is funded by Orange County Housing & Community Development and administered by CivicStone, Inc.

Eligibility: age 62 or older; own and occupy your home; meet household income requirements; live in Laguna Hills or another participating Orange County community.

Eligible improvements: roof, plumbing & electrical repairs; accessibility improvements (grab bars, ramps, bathroom modifications); heating & energy-efficiency improvements; code corrections and other health & safety repairs.

Questions: Monique Miner, CivicStone, Inc., (909) 364-9000, monique.safe.homes@civicstone.com. A Safe Homes Program Brochure and a Safe Homes Grant Application are available for download.
