## Building Permits and Inspections

The Building & Safety Division provides plan checking, permitting, and inspection services for new construction, additions, renovations, alterations, and remodels. Permit and inspection services have moved to a 100% online system. Through the City's online portal you can apply for a permit, request an inspection, view scheduled inspections, make a payment, or look up records. The Division is provided under contract with Charles Abbott Associates, Inc. (CAA).

All permit applications submitted on or after 01/01/2026 must comply with the 2025 California Building Standards Code (Title 24). Applications submitted before this date may remain under the prior code unless a plan check expires.

## Pre-Approved ADU Plans

The Pre-Approved ADU Plans Program encourages the construction of new, detached ADUs by offering property owners a selection of Pre-Approved ADU building plans. Some site-specific design work is required. Applicants using a Pre-Approved ADU plan set must submit a complete package through the City's online portal for review and approval.

A complete package must include:
- The selected pre-approved ADU plan, with completed title sheet.
- A scaled site plan showing property lines, parcel dimensions, dimensions of the home and all structures on site (e.g., detached garage, storage, pool), and the location of the proposed pre-approved ADU footprint.
- An updated Title 24 energy calculation report with correct name, address, and exact orientation for site-specific conditions. The owner may contact the entity that prepared the original report (shown on T24.1) to obtain updates.
- A Hold Harmless Agreement.
- The OCFA Residential Plan Submittal Criteria Form.
- The City of Laguna Hills ADU Questionnaire.
- A Soils Report.

Three Pre-Approved ADU plan sets are available for download: Studio - 389 S.F.; 1-Bedroom - 499 S.F.; 2-Bedroom - 789 S.F.

Source: https://www.lagunahillsca.gov/599/Pre-Approved-Accessory-Dwelling-Unit-ADU

## Public Works Permits — Encroachment, Transportation, Grading

The Encroachment Permit Application has moved to an online system. Transportation permits use the Transportation Permit Form (PDF) and Transportation Permit Policies (PDF). Grading permit documents include the Grading Permit Application (PDF), Grading Permit Exceptions (PDF), Erosion Control Notes (PDF), Erosion Control Plan Checklist (PDF), and Standard Grading Notes.

To submit a grading permit application, email pwpermits@lagunahillsca.gov with: a Grading Plan Set (must be prepared by a Registered Civil Engineer), a Geotechnical/Soils Report, a Hydrology Study, a WQMP (if applicable), and a completed Grading Permit Application Form. To submit revisions, email pwpermits@lagunahillsca.gov with only the revised sheets (consolidated in 1 pdf file) and all changes clouded.

Engineering: 24035 El Toro Road, Laguna Hills, CA 92653. Phone: 949-707-2651. Fax: 949-707-2633. Public counter services are by appointment only. Hours: Monday - Friday (and alternating Fridays) 8 a.m. - 5 p.m.

## Residential Parking Permits
