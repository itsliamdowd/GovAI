## Short-Term Rentals — Prohibition, Penalties and Reporting

Short-term rentals (STRs) are prohibited in Laguna Hills under Zoning and Development Code Section 9-10.050 (Uses within zones). STRs are not an enumerated use in the master list of that section, and uses not specified in the list are prohibited. On April 25, 2023, the City Council adopted Ordinance No. 2023-2, establishing Laguna Hills Municipal Code (LHMC) Chapter 6-40 (Short-term rentals). It explicitly prohibits the advertisement and rental of property for short-term rental in all residential zones.

An STR is the rental of any residential dwelling, dwelling unit, or room in a dwelling (in whole or in part), accessory structure, accessory dwelling unit, and/or recreational vehicle for occupancy, lodging, or sleeping purposes for a period of less than 30 consecutive calendar days. Rooms or units within city-approved hotels, motels, and bed and breakfasts are not considered STRs. There are no exemptions to the STR ordinance. Once an STR violation is verified, the City may issue fines of $1,000 for each offense.

To report a suspected STR, use the City's Request Tracker (available 24 hours a day) at https://www.lagunahillsca.gov/requesttracker.aspx, or contact Brad Carrington, Code Enforcement Officer, at 949-707-2663 or bcarrington@lagunahillsCA.gov. Provide the address of the suspected property and any details describing how it may be operating as an STR.

## Housing Choice Voucher Program (Renters and Landlords)

The Orange County Housing Authority (OCHA) administers the Housing Choice Voucher Program (previously known as Section 8) in Laguna Hills. It helps cover the difference between the rent a lower-income household can afford and the rent a participating landlord is charging. Renters can call OCHA at 714-480-2700 to inquire about waiting list status, learn how to transport a voucher into or out of Irvine/Orange County, and find out which property owners accept vouchers. Landlords interested in participating can visit the Orange County Housing Authority's Landlord/Owner webpage.

OCHA also administers Special Purpose Voucher programs for clients who meet specific eligibility criteria and are referred by partner agencies: Family Self-Sufficiency (FSS), Family Unification Program (FUP), Veterans Affairs Supportive Housing (VASH), Non-Elderly Disabled (NED), Mainstream Voucher Program, and Emergency Housing Voucher.

## Welcome Home OC (Landlord Incentives)

Welcome Home OC is a housing assistance program tailored to Orange County property owners as well as qualified families, veterans, foster youth, and seniors in need of permanent housing. Every property owner is eligible to receive a double security deposit, daily holding fees, protection from damage and losses, and a signing bonus with their first 12-month lease. More information is on the Orange County United Way website.

## Affordable Rental Housing and Home Ownership Programs

The County of Orange maintains an affordable housing rental list with contact information for affordable rentals throughout the County. To view it, visit the Orange County Housing Authority and click the Affordable Housing List link under Resources at the bottom of the page.

The County of Orange also offers a homeownership program whereby participants may be able to use housing assistance subsidies for mortgage payments. The Orange County Mortgage Assistance Program (MAP) is a first-time homebuyer assistance program for residents in participating cities, including Laguna Hills: https://affordable-housing.org/orange-county-mortgage-assistance-program/. Resources for buyers listed by the City: California Housing Finance Agency, U.S. Department of Housing and Urban Development, Affordable Housing Clearinghouse, NeighborWorks of Orange County, and Making Home Affordable.

## Housing Rehabilitation — CalHome and Safe Homes for O.C. Seniors

The CalHome Program is a deferred-payment loan program available through a grant to Habitat OC for first-time homebuyer and housing rehabilitation assistance, homebuyer counseling, and technical assistance. It enables low- and very low-income households to become or remain homeowners.

The Safe Homes for O.C. Seniors Program may provide grants of up to $20,000 to help eligible senior homeowners make health, safety, accessibility, and home repairs. It is funded by Orange County Housing & Community Development and administered by CivicStone, Inc. Eligibility: age 62 or older; own and occupy your home; meet household income requirements; live in Laguna Hills or another participating Orange County community. Eligible improvements include roof, plumbing and electrical repairs; accessibility improvements (grab bars, ramps, bathroom modifications); heating and energy-efficiency improvements; and code corrections and other health and safety repairs. Questions: Monique Miner, CivicStone, Inc., (909) 364-9000, monique.safe.homes@civicstone.com.

Source: https://www.lagunahillsca.gov/590/Housing-Information-Resources

## Fair Housing and Landlord/Tenant Counseling

California fair housing laws prohibit discrimination because of race, color, national origin, religion, sex, familial status, disability, source of income, sexual orientation, marital status, age, arbitrary characteristics, gender identity, and gender expression.

Fair Housing Services in Laguna Hills are provided by the Fair Housing Council of Orange County (FHCOC). Services include fair housing enforcement and education, landlord/tenant counseling, mediation, and homebuyer HUD counseling, which includes first-time homebuyer education and mortgage default counseling. FHCOC can be reached at (714) 569-0823 or info@fairhousingoc.org. FHCOC brochures "What is Fair Housing" are available in English, Spanish, and Vietnamese.

Source: https://www.lagunahillsca.gov/590/Housing-Information-Resources

## Accessory Dwelling Units (ADUs) and ADU Financing

Accessory Dwelling Units (ADUs) and Junior Accessory Dwelling Units (JADUs) are independent living facilities located on the same parcel as the primary unit, which may be occupied by extended family members or tenants. For questions, contact (949) 707-2672 or planning@lagunahillsca.gov.

Property owners apply directly with the funding organization, not with the City, for ADU grants and loans. Options listed by the City: OC Housing Finance Trust, CalHFA ADU Financing Program, CalHome Program, California Community Economic Development Association (CCEDA) ADU Program, HPP Cares, One Trust Home Loans, and the CalHFA-Approved Lender List.

## Homelessness Services and Contacts

The City of Laguna Hills contracts with the City of Mission Viejo to provide homeless outreach services. Outreach workers can provide ID vouchers, transportation assistance, other material goods, and resource referrals. An Orange County Sheriff's Deputy is assigned as a Homeless Liaison Officer within Laguna Hills. The 2019 Point in Time Count counted 24 unsheltered individuals within Laguna Hills.

Contacts: call 9-1-1 immediately to report a threat to public safety. Call OCSD at 717-770-6011 for non-emergency Sheriff Department response. Community Outreach Supervisor Carol Sullivan: csullivan@cityofmissionviejo.org, 949-470-3027.

Other resources: dial 2-1-1 or (888) 600-4357 for food, shelter, and human services referrals. The Orange County Health Care Agency Outreach and Engagement team can be reached at (800) 364-2221. The Homeless Multi-Service Center, a drop-in center for homeless individuals with mental illness or co-occurring disorders, is open from 6 a.m. to 3 p.m. daily at 2416 S. Main St., Santa Ana; call (714) 668-1530. The Comprehensive Health Assessment Team - Homeless can be reached at (714) 834-8498. For homeless families or individuals living on the streets in South Orange County, call 714-836-7188 ext. 121 or email outreach@mercyhouse.net.

## Housing Element and Regional Housing Needs (RHNA)

The State Department of Housing and Community Development (HCD) certified Laguna Hills' adopted 2021-2029 Housing Element in a letter dated May 23, 2024. The City Council adopted the Amended 2021-2029 Housing Element Update on March 26, 2024. The Council also adopted Ordinance No. 2024-1 (Housing (H) Overlay Zone and changes to Mixed-Use (MXU) zoning standards) and Ordinance No. 2024-2 (citywide objective design standards for multi-family residential and mixed-use development).

Laguna Hills' RHNA allocation for the 6th Housing Cycle (2021-2029) is 1,985 new housing units: Very Low 568, Low 353, Moderate 354, Above Moderate (market) 710. The Orange County average median income (AMI) for 2021 is $106,700 for a family of four. Very Low Income is typically 30% to 50% of AMI, Lower Income 50% to 80%, and Moderate Income 80% to 120%.

Questions or comments: planning@lagunahillsca.gov (all comments are public record). Jennifer Lowe, Community Development Director, jlowe@lagunahillsca.gov, 949-707-2675. Jay Wuu, AICP, Senior Planner, jwuu@lagunahillsca.gov, 949-707-2662.

Source: https://www.lagunahillsca.gov/516/Housing-Element-Update

## Inclusionary Housing Ordinance — Status

The City is considering a proposed Inclusionary Housing Program (Draft Chapter 9-73 Inclusionary Housing) to address affordable housing obligations for certain new residential development projects. The program has not been adopted. The proposed ordinance is under consideration, and no Inclusionary Housing requirements are currently in effect. Questions may be directed to the Community Development Department.
