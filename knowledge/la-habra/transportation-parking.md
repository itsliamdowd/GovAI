## Traffic Bureau

The La Habra Police Department's Traffic Bureau aims to reduce collisions and the injuries and deaths that result from them. It bases enforcement on collision data, enforcement activity records, traffic volume, and traffic conditions. It works with the California Office of Traffic Safety (OTS) to keep La Habra streets safe from intoxicated and unsafe drivers. It regularly runs check points throughout the City as well as directed traffic safety enforcement.

The Traffic Bureau has a full-time commercial enforcement officer who specializes in vehicle code violations involving commercial vehicles and their drivers.

## Parking Enforcement Unit

The Parking Enforcement Unit is part of the Traffic Bureau. Its Parking Enforcement Officers enforce city parking codes, including the overnight parking ordinance, street sweeping violations, extended parking, and abandoned vehicles.

Municipal code 10.20.180 LHMC prohibits parking a vehicle on a public roadway for more than 30 minutes between 2:00 a.m. and 6:00 a.m. unless the vehicle has a parking permit issued by the city. The law is posted at all major entrances to the City of La Habra.

## Overnight Parking Permit — Requirement and Enforcement

Under La Habra Municipal Code Section 10.20.180, it is unlawful for the owner or driver of any vehicle to let it remain standing in any street in the City between 2 a.m. and 6 a.m. without a permit. The overnight parking ordinance has been in effect since 1969.

It is enforced Monday–Friday, 2 a.m. to 6 a.m., and is not enforced on recognized holidays. The city is worked in grids/areas to ensure equal enforcement throughout the city. Some areas may be cited more frequently because they are high-complaint areas.

The City lists these benefits of restricting street parking: reduced opportunity for vehicle-related crimes, enhanced neighborhood aesthetics, efficient street sweeping, and improved access for emergency, utility, or road repair vehicles.

## Overnight Parking Permit — How to Apply

All Overnight Parking Permit applications, including Temporary Overnight Parking Permit requests, must be submitted online through the City of La Habra Overnight Parking Permit Portal. The City does not accept parking applications by email or in person at the Police Department. Send questions by email to LHPDParkingPermit@lahabraca.gov.

You need to submit:
1. A copy of a valid California driver's license showing the address of the residence listed on the application. It is used to create an account in the permit portal.
2. A copy of all vehicle registration(s) listed on the application.

Permits are virtual. Once a request is approved and paid for, the permit is issued automatically using the license plate(s) you provided, so your license plate is your permit. The City will not mail physical permits. You will be notified by email if your application is approved.

Source: https://www.lahabraca.gov/861/Apply-for-an-Overnight-Parking-Permit

## Overnight Parking Permit — Eligibility, Denial, and Temporary Permits

Parking permits are not issued to motor-homes, boats, trailers, company vehicles, or commercial vehicles in excess of 6,000 lbs. gross weight.

An application may be denied for either of these reasons:
1. There is adequate room to park all vehicles on the premises. Permits are considered on a basis of need and not solely for convenience.
2. A vehicle you are requesting a permit for is not currently registered at the address listed.

If an application is denied, you will receive an email explaining why and how to correct any issues. You must fix any problems and resubmit.

To prevent citations while your application is being processed, request a Temporary Overnight Parking Permit through the same online portal.

If you have a disabled person placard or license plate, complete an application and a permit will be issued at no charge.

## Towing Authority and Parking on Lawns

An overnight parking permit does not exempt a vehicle from these California Vehicle Code (CVC) storage authorities:
- 22651(i): a vehicle with 5 or more unpaid parking violations on file with the DMV may be towed.
- 22651(j): a vehicle parked with no license plates or other evidence of registration may be towed.
- 22651(k): a vehicle parked or left standing upon a highway for 72 or more consecutive hours may be towed.
- 22651(o): a vehicle whose registration has been expired 6 months or more may be towed.

Parking on a lawn is not allowed. La Habra Municipal Code Section 18.10.050 prohibits any vehicle being parked on the lawn, and the vehicle will be cited.

## Parking Citations — Contesting, Paying, and Payment Plans

The Citation Processing Center handles all processes once a citation has been issued, including processing data, accepting payment, establishing payment plans, and contesting citations. Contact them at 800-989-2058 and have your citation number ready. To contest a citation, contact the Citation Processing Center by phone, in writing, or online at 800-989-2058, Citation Processing Center, P.O. Box 10479, Newport Beach, CA 92658.

Payment plan options are available under California State Legislature AB 503 and AB 2544 ("indigent"). To submit an indigent payment plan request, visit the Citation Processing Center. To qualify, you must demonstrate that you are indigent by providing proof of indigence in one of the ways described in the Proof of Indigence (PDF) on the city page.
