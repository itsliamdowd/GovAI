## City Council — Members, Terms and Goals

The La Habra City Council has five members, led by Mayor Jose Medrano and Mayor Pro Tem James Gomez. The other Councilmembers are Rose Espinoza, Daren Nigsarian and Delwin Lampkin. All Councilmembers serve at-large and serve four-year terms. There are no term limits. Each December, the City Council chooses one member to serve as Mayor and one to serve as Mayor Pro Tem. The Mayor Pro Tem acts in the Mayor's place when absent.

Regular City Council elections occur in even years and coincide with the Statewide General Election.

Fiscal Year 2025-2026 City Council Goals and Objectives: Protection of Public Safety; Management of Public Revenues and Fiscal Assets; Maintenance and Improvement of City Infrastructure; Recruitment, Selection, and Training of Employees; Development Activity and Business Assistance; Community Outreach and Civic Engagement; Integration of Excellence in City Operations; Enhancement of the Quality of Life Through Arts, Parks, Recreation, Social Services, and Open Space.

## City Council Meetings — Schedule and Public Participation

The La Habra City Council meets at 6:30 p.m. on the first and third Monday of each month in the Council Chamber located at 100 E. La Habra Blvd. Special meetings are scheduled as needed.

Residents are encouraged to attend. Residents who wish to address the Council may do so in accordance with the guidelines noted in the City Council agendas. City Council agendas are available online or at the City Council meetings.

Online agendas are available on the Thursday preceding Council meetings on the City website at https://destinyhosted.com/agenda_publish.cfm?id=52422. The City Clerk prepares the minutes of the City Council meetings. Minutes can be viewed online at https://www.lahabraca.gov/356/Archived-Council-Videos.

## City Manager's Office

The City of La Habra is a California General Law city with a Council-Manager Form of Government. The City Council establishes the goals and policy for the City and appoints a City Manager to oversee day-to-day operations. The City Manager is the Chief Executive Officer of the City and also serves as the Executive Director of the Redevelopment Agency, Housing Authority and Civic Improvement Authority. The City Manager's Office is responsible for contract administration and monitoring, City Council services, economic development, intergovernmental relations, public information, and special projects.

## City Clerk's Office — Services and Contact

The City Clerk's Office cares for the City's official records and documents, such as agendas, minutes, resolutions, ordinances, and the municipal code. It coordinates all municipal and special elections held by the City, coordinates the annual boards, commissions, and committees recruitment process, and administers the records retention and destruction policy. It serves as the filing officer for the Fair Political Practices Commission (FPPC), such as Campaign Contribution Reports and Statements of Economic Interest. It also administers oaths of office, attests and seals official documents, and accepts petitions, appeals, claims, subpoenas, summons, bids, and contracts.

Copies of agendas, minutes of prior meetings, videos, and other public documents may be requested via the Public Records Request process. To request a recognition at a City Council meeting, complete the Recognition Request Form and submit it to the City Clerk's Office.

## Elections — Campaign Filings and Contribution Limits

Campaign statements are filed by elected officials and candidates of local office, including the Mayor and City Councilmembers. Forms include:
- Candidate Intention (Form 501)
- Committee Information for campaigns receiving over $2,000 (Form 410)
- Committee Information for campaigns receiving less than $2,000 (Form 470)
- Semi-Annual and Pre-Election Campaign Statements (Form 460)
- 24-Hour Independent Expenditure Report (Form 496)
- 24-Hour Contribution Report (Form 497)

Campaign filings can be searched online through the Public Portal for Campaign Finance Disclosure.

The campaign contribution limit for the November 3, 2026 General Municipal Election is $5,900. The city does not have established campaign contribution limits. Under Ordinance No. 1824, adopted by City Council on December 7, 2020, limits default to FPPC limits.

## Elections — General Rules for City Council Candidacy

La Habra General Municipal Elections are held on the first Tuesday following the first Monday in November of even-numbered years and coincide with the Statewide General Election. Candidates must be residents and registered voters of the City of La Habra at the time nomination papers are issued. Residents of unincorporated county territories are not eligible to run for municipal office.

For elections consolidated with the regularly scheduled election, the filing period for nomination documents commences on the 113th day prior to the election. Nomination documents must be filed no later than 5:00 p.m. on the 88th day prior to the election in the office of the City Clerk, during regular office hours. If nomination documents for an incumbent are not filed by 5:00 p.m. on the 88th day before the election, any person other than the incumbent has until 5:30 p.m. on the 83rd day before the election to file.
