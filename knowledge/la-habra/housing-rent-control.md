## Housing Authority — Background and Section 8

The La Habra Housing Authority was formed by the City Council on September 22, 1992, by Resolution Number 4146 under provisions of State Law. At the February 17, 2015 Housing Authority meeting, Resolution No. 2015-1 was approved authorizing the Orange County Housing Authority to continue providing Section 8 Housing Assistance programs for a 10 year period.

## Housing Division and CDBG Funding

The Community Development Department oversees and monitors the Community Development Block Grant (CDBG) funding it receives annually from the Department of Housing and Urban Development. The City of La Habra annually receives over $600,000 in CDBG funding. The primary objective of the program is the development of viable communities by providing decent housing, a suitable living environment, and expanded economic opportunities primarily focused on low- and moderate-income persons and neighborhoods.

The City currently funds these organizations and programs with CDBG funding: Fair Housing Foundation; Graffiti Removal Program (City Program, Community Services Department); Mercy House; Community Services - Community Employment Program.

Housing Division contacts: Susan Louie, Project Manager; Gary Carbajal, Management Analyst II. 110 East La Habra Boulevard, La Habra, CA 90631. Phone: 562-383-4108. FAX: 562-383-4476.

## Fair Housing Program

Through CDBG, the City of La Habra supports the Fair Housing Education, Counseling and Enforcement Program. Services are delivered by the Fair Housing Foundation, a nonpartisan organization that supports both landlords and tenants. The purpose of the program is to counteract and eliminate discriminatory housing practices. For more information about the program or services, contact the Fair Housing Foundation directly at 800-446-3247. The city's Fair Housing page also provides Fair Housing Foundation brochures in English and Spanish (PDF).

The City joined a group of 16 Orange County cities to prepare a Draft Regional Analysis of Impediments to Fair Housing Choice (AI), which HUD requires of direct entitlement cities. The AI examines policies, procedures, and practices that may limit a person's ability to choose their residence free from discrimination.

Source: https://www.lahabraca.gov/1091/Fair-Housing

## Affordable Housing — Acquisition and Rehabilitation

The La Habra Redevelopment Agency has provided financial assistance to affordable housing developers for acquisition and rehabilitation projects. The primary funding sources are the agency's housing set-aside funds and the City's federal CDBG fund allocation. In exchange for the assistance, the agency is able to secure long-term deed restrictions to ensure the units remain available to low- and moderate-income households.

Source: https://www.lahabraca.gov/1094/Affordable-Housing
