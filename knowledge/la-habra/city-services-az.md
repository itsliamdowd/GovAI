## Animal Control

Animal control officers provide field services for La Habra 7 days a week, during day time hours. 24 hour service is available for emergencies, such as stray injured animals or aggressive animals running at large that have caused or attempted to cause injury. Officers respond to calls about strays, animal bites, dog licensing issues, barking complaints, and leash law violations. They also investigate reports of abuse, neglect, or abandonment. All domesticated animals are checked for a license and/or an identifying implanted chip before being taken to the shelter. Animal sheltering is provided through a contract with Inland Valley Humane Society.

Contacts: Animal Services Division, 150 N. Euclid Street, La Habra, CA 90631, phone 562-383-4352, licensing 562-383-4355. Inland Valley Humane Society, 500 Humane Way, Pomona, CA 91766, phone 909-623-9777.

## Dog Licensing

California state law requires all dogs, four months of age or older, to be vaccinated against the rabies virus and licensed within the jurisdiction where the dog resides. Discounts are provided for altered animals and for pet owners who are senior citizens (62 years of age or older). An altered-pet discount requires proof of sterility (a spay or neuter certificate). A senior discount requires a photocopy of a valid current government issued photo identification card showing proof of age and current address.

Complete the license application and attach a copy of your pet's current rabies vaccination certificate. Submit the application and payment at the front counter of the police department (150 N. Euclid St.) during regular business hours, or mail it to: La Habra Police Department - Animal Control, 150 N. Euclid St., La Habra, CA 90631. Dog license renewals may be paid online with a valid rabies certificate on file through the City of La Habra Payment Portal. The invoice number is the dog license number. For licensing fee questions, call 562-383-4355.

## Code Enforcement — Trash Container Removal Policy

Trash cans may be placed on the curb in front of the residence where the trash was generated beginning at 4 p.m. on the day before trash collection. Trash containers must be removed from public view by 10 p.m. on trash collection day. At all other times, trash containers, garbage cans, boxes and other like storage containers may not be placed on the curb or on the property where they are visible from the street. The rule is in La Habra Municipal Code, Section 18.77.040 C 7.

Dumpsters and larger storage bins may be located in front yard areas if the resident has an active building permit that covers excavation, construction or demolition operation, or if it is for a period of time that does not exceed 72 hours for general cleanup of the property.

Contacts: Public Works 562-383-4170; Code Enforcement 562-383-4134; CR&R (general refuse removal service) 562-694-3730. Code Enforcement is at 110 East La Habra Boulevard, La Habra, CA 90631, phone 562-383-4134, hotline 562-383-4143, fax 562-383-4476.

## Residential Waste Collection — Carts and Sorting

CR&R Incorporated provides 3 carts for automated weekly collection of trash, recyclables and green waste. Roll carts into the street and place them against the curb with handles facing your home. Place carts at least 2 feet apart from other carts and objects. It is recommended that you park cars at least 6 feet from the carts on collection day. Do not overfill the cart, and close the lid. Carts come in 35, 64 and 96 gallons. Additional carts are available and cart size can be changed. Green waste service may not be available in all areas and participation is elective.

Organics (green) cart: food waste (bread, fruit, fish, meat, vegetables, fats/oils/grease, dairy) and yard waste (flowers, grass/lawn clippings, leaves and weeds, garden trimmings and prunings, sawdust, wood chips and small wood scraps, tree branches and shrubs). Do not use plastic bags; only compostable bags labeled "Compostable" may be used. Put these in the trash cart instead: plastic bags, painted wood, treated wood, palm fronds, pet waste, dirt/soil, tree stumps.

Mixed recyclables (blue) cart: rinse food residue from containers and flatten cardboard boxes. Set out the recycling cart only when it is full, with the lid able to close completely. Accepted items include aluminum/tin cans, empty aerosol cans, pie tins, cardboard egg cartons, computer/white paper, milk jugs, plastic containers labeled Number 1 through 7, juice bottles, newspapers/inserts/magazines, cardboard, cereal boxes (plastic lining removed), tissue boxes, glass bottles/jars, liquor bottles, laundry detergent boxes/bottles, and junk mail/phone books.

Trash (gray) cart items include aluminum foil, auto glass, carpeting, ceramics, clothing, disposable diapers, drinking glasses, mirrors, palm fronds, pet waste, plastic bags, Styrofoam, toys, waxed and coated paper, and window glass.

CR&R Customer Service: 562-694-3730 or 866-742-8850. Hours: Monday through Friday 8 a.m. to 5 p.m.; Saturday 8 a.m. to Noon.

## Bulky Item Collection

Single-family unit residents are entitled to four (4) free bulky item pickups per year. Multi-family unit residents get four (4) free pickups per year per unit. Each pickup takes up to 4 large items or 20 plastic bags of refuse or bundles of yard waste (less than 50 pounds each). Additional pickups are available for a nominal fee. Commercial properties may request pickups on a per item basis, with a nominal fee for the first two items and a smaller fee for each additional item.

Bulky items are large, hard-to-handle items such as furniture, major appliances, and other household items that can only be moved by 2 or more individuals. Pickups are by appointment only. To schedule, contact the CR&R Customer Service Center at 562-694-3730 or 866-742-8850 (Monday through Friday 8 a.m. to 5 p.m.; Saturday 8 a.m. to Noon).

Source: https://www.lahabraca.gov/188/Bulky-Item-Collection

## Street Sweeping Enforcement

The City Council approved a citywide street sweeping parking enforcement program for residential areas on January 3, 2005. Central Parking System, Inc. began issuing parking tickets in September 2005 to vehicles blocking the path of the sweeper. Enforcement will not occur for vehicles parked after the street sweeper has passed. An overnight parking permit does not exempt you from the street sweeping or other parking regulations. A citation will be issued for each occurrence. Notify your guests about parking restrictions.

Contact: Street Maintenance Division, 621 W Lambert Road, La Habra, CA 90631. Phone 562-383-4170, fax 562-383-4497.

## Holding a Party at a City Park

Private parties are permitted at Community parks. The City does not take reservations; park usage is first come, first served. If you plan to have a jumper, visit the Community Center and complete an "application to use public facility" form. The business providing the jumper must email Andrea Lira a copy of their proof of insurance naming the City of La Habra as additionally insured for $1,000,000.

The City does NOT permit: bounce house/jumper/inflatables that include a water feature, petting zoos, pony rides, trains, laser tag set-ups, or bubble soccer. For more information, contact the Community Center at 562-383-4200.
