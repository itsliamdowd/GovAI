## Fire Department Plan Review

Certain construction projects require Fire Department plan review and inspections. These include: improvements to buildings equipped with fire sprinklers; new or altered kitchen hood fire protection systems; photovoltaic systems that cover 50% or more of the roof; removal and installation of underground flammable or combustible liquid storage tanks; and spray and powder coating booths.

The City contracts with the Los Angeles County Fire Department for fire prevention and suppression services. Contact Evette Almaraz, Fire Inspector, 850 W. La Habra Boulevard, La Habra, CA 90631, 562-691-9369. For new construction or major tenant improvements, contact the Los Angeles County Fire Prevention Headquarters at 323-890-4125.

## Code Enforcement — What It Handles

Code Enforcement staff serve residents and local businesses on the maintenance and use of residential, commercial, and industrial properties within the City's seven square mile limits. Most issues investigated come from citizen complaints, along with proactive work by inspectors. Common concerns include lack of landscaping maintenance, deteriorated property conditions, inoperable vehicles stored in public view, structures built without permits, signage, zoning and land use matters, graffiti on private property, and trash container storage.

Staff work to educate residents to achieve voluntary compliance. The Division works with the City Attorney's Office on more serious property maintenance matters not resolved at the administrative level.

## Citizens' Online Police Reporting System

The La Habra Police Department offers online reporting from home or, during business hours, at the kiosk in the police department's front lobby. It is available for reporting these crimes where no suspect information is known and the offense occurred within La Habra city boundaries: annoying phone calls, identity theft, lost property, thefts, unlawful use of a credit card, vandalism, and vehicle tampering. The system can also be used to request a vacation check of your home while you are out of town, and to document child custody violations.

Reports made through the system are issued a temporary number immediately and the user can print a temporary copy. Once the report is approved, a permanent case number is assigned and the submitter is notified of approval by email (if an email address was provided).

Source: https://www.lahabraca.gov/237/Citizens-Online-Police-Reporting-System

## AlertOC Emergency Notifications

The City uses AlertOC to inform people who live or work in La Habra during emergency events. Authorized City or County officials record a voice, email or text message delivered to known landline telephones and registered participants within minutes by phone, cell phone, email and text. Messages cover health or safety situations such as major fires, flooding, missing persons and road closures. Anyone interested in emergencies at a specific address in La Habra can register.

Residential and business landline numbers are automatically stored and updated through the 911 database. To add cell phone numbers and email addresses, register on the AlertOC website.

## Community Emergency Response Team (CERT)

The La Habra Police Department, with the Los Angeles County Fire Department, offers CERT training in basic safety and life saving skills for a major disaster. The program is approximately 20 hours, covering disaster preparedness, terrorism, fire safety, light search and rescue, team organization, medical triage and disaster medical operations. It is offered in a series of three Saturday classes and is free of charge. Teens accompanied by parents are also eligible.

Follow La Habra PD on Facebook and Instagram for upcoming training dates. To sign up or for more information, contact Jennifer Duong, Admin Bureau Manager, at 562-383-4310.

## City Fire Inspection & Permit Program

As part of the business license process, the Fire Department conducts an annual inspection of businesses and issues permits for various activities, including certain equipment, practices and materials, to ensure compliance with the Uniform Fire Code. The LA County Fire Department performs the inspections on behalf of the City. Fees for inspections and permits are listed on each business's annual business license renewal. The City legally may not charge a fee higher than the cost of providing the service, and all fire inspection and permit fees are set below that threshold, in some cases 50% below actual cost.

Questions can be directed to the Community Development Department at 562-383-4100.

Source: https://www.lahabraca.gov/219/City-Fire-Inspection-Permit-Program

## La Habra FireMed Program

FireMed is a voluntary subscription program that supplements insurance coverage and helps control pre-hospital medical costs, for $48 annually. Any resident within the City of La Habra may join. Membership protects insured members from out-of-pocket expenses related to paramedic treatment and emergency 911 ambulance transportation for situations occurring in La Habra. If you have insurance, the City bills for services and accepts whatever is paid as "payment in full." Membership covers all family members with permanent residence at your address. Aside from the $48 annual fee, no payment is required of members; insurance, Medicare, or MediCAL will be billed. Non-members are billed the usual fees for services to the person receiving them.

To sign up or get more information, call 562-383-4060 or email firemed@lahabraca.gov.

Source: https://www.lahabraca.gov/220/La-Habra-FireMed-Program
