## RV and Trailer Parking Permits

El Cajon offers a web-based RV Parking Permit System for residents who wish to park their RVs on city streets. Residents must obtain a permit to avoid being cited. The permits are free of charge and available online. Applicants must first register for an account with the city's Permitting Site, PACO.

The permit system allows RV owners who live within the city limits of El Cajon to receive a one day parking permit for their motor homes and trailers. Residents can have three active permits in the system at one time and are allowed only 52 parking permits per year. RVs parked on city streets without permits are subject to parking enforcement (El Cajon Municipal Code Title 10, Section 10.28.070).

## Commercial Vehicle Parking on Streets

No person shall park any commercial vehicle of a rated capacity of more than ten thousand pounds on any street or alley for more than one hour of total elapsed time, between the hours of nine p.m. and six a.m. of the next succeeding day, whether the place of parking is the same or is changed, and whether the time of parking is continuous or interrupted. This applies to semi-trucks and cabs (El Cajon Municipal Code Title 10, Section 10.28.080).

## Police Traffic Division

The El Cajon Police Traffic Division works to reduce vehicle and pedestrian accidents, educate the public through enforcement, and investigate collisions. Additional duties include DUI Checkpoints, Car Seat Inspections, and assistance with public events such as the El Cajon Grand Prix Bike Race and the Mother Goose Parade.

Source: https://www.elcajon.gov/your-government/departments/police/your-police-department/traffic

## Street Maintenance, Street Sweeping and Traffic Engineering Requests

The Public Works Maintenance Operations group maintains the City's infrastructure in the public right-of-way and oversees street sweeping of City streets to keep debris and contaminants from entering the storm drains and channels. The easiest way to create and track a work order for any maintenance need is through the City's My El Cajon app, available on Google Play and the Apple Store, or by submitting a request online through Requests and Issues.

For issues concerning signal timing, street light bulb replacements, additional signage, and other traffic matters, submit a My El Cajon app work order. For issues concerning City construction projects, submit a My El Cajon app work order as well.

Source: https://www.elcajon.gov/your-government/departments/public-works/city-streets

## Reporting Non-Emergency Issues

The Requests and Issues system is intended for non-emergency issues and is not monitored 24/7. If a concern needs immediate attention by the Police Department, call the Dispatch Center at 619-579-3311. The system does not generate Police Reports. When reporting potential code violations, it is extremely helpful if the picture is current (less than a week old) and clearly shows the issue being reported.
