## City Council — Structure and Meetings

El Cajon is a charter city with a popularly elected mayor and a council-manager system of government. The Mayor is elected at-large. The four Council members are elected by district for overlapping four-year terms, with elections held in November of even-numbered years.

Except on City holidays, election days, and other days the City Council specifies, meetings are held on the 2nd and 4th Tuesday of each month in two sessions, starting at 3:00 p.m. and 7:00 p.m. Items requiring advance public notice and formal public hearings are normally scheduled for the 7:00 p.m. session.

Source: https://www.elcajon.gov/your-government/city-council

## Mayor and Mayor Pro-Tempore

The Mayor presides over the Council and represents the City on ceremonial occasions. The Mayor may debate, introduce motions from the chair, and vote as a member of the Council. The other Council members can overrule the Mayor on specific points.

Each year on the 2nd Tuesday in December, the Council selects one of its members as Mayor Pro-Tempore for the next twelve months. When the Mayor is absent from an official function, the Mayor Pro-Tempore exercises the full powers and duties of the office.

Source: https://www.elcajon.gov/your-government/city-council

## City Meetings, Agendas, and Minutes

The City Clerk posts City Council meeting agendas at the end of the week prior to a meeting. You can sign up for e-notifications to be emailed when they are posted. Members of the public may contact any member of the City Council about any topic on upcoming agendas.

Archives are available for City Council agendas and minutes (prior to 2019), City Council meeting videos (2017 to present), Planning Commission archives (prior to 2019), Planning Commission videos (2020 to present), and the Successor Agency to the Redevelopment Agency Oversight Board.

Source: https://www.elcajon.gov/i-want-to/learn-about/upcoming-meetings

## City Council Meetings — Remote Participation

To participate remotely you must preregister at ElCajon.gov/RemoteParticipation. After registering you will receive instructions on how to join the meeting.

Your microphone is muted until it is your turn to speak on an item. When the City Clerk calls your name, the moderator unmutes you and your time begins once you start speaking. During your allotted time you can mute or unmute by pressing *6. If you register to speak on multiple agenda items, the process repeats for each item.

Source: https://www.elcajon.gov/i-want-to/learn-about/upcoming-meetings

## Addressing the Council and Appealing Commission Decisions

The City Clerk can furnish a copy of the agenda for any Council meeting you plan to attend. The Clerk also has a copy of the procedures for addressing the Council on any relevant subject. Public announcements required by law are published in The Daily Californian.

Most City boards and commissions act in an advisory capacity to the Council. In every case a petitioner has the right to appeal a commission or board decision to the Council.

Source: https://www.elcajon.gov/discover-el-cajon/about/forms-of-government

## Parks and Recreation Department — Overview

The Parks and Recreation Department's mission is to develop youth, support families, and provide safe places. It offers recreation programs for ages one through senior adult.

The Department supervises five community recreation centers, one banquet/meeting facility, nine sports fields, a skate park, a dog park, outdoor pickleball courts, and a spring/summer aquatics program. It also provides staff support to the City's Veterans Coalition, Teen Coalition, and Recreation Council.

Source: https://www.elcajon.gov/your-government/departments/recreation/about-us

## Gillespie Field Development Council

The Council has five members. Under the County–City Agreement (Resolution No. 104-74), three are appointed by the Board of Supervisors and ratified by the City Council. Two are appointed by the City Council and ratified by the Board of Supervisors. Members need expertise in fields such as real estate, finance, industrial development, or aviation. Members file Statements of Economic Interest (Form 700) with the Clerk of the Board of Supervisors.

Meetings are held on the 3rd Wednesday of the month at 6:00 p.m. in the City of El Cajon Council Chamber, 200 Civic Center Way, El Cajon, CA 92020. Fillable and printable PDF applications are posted on the page.

## City Facts — Location, Incorporation, and Statistics

El Cajon was incorporated on November 12, 1912, as a General Law City. By a vote of the people on June 5, 2012, it became a Charter City. It is 15 miles due east of San Diego. City Hall is at 200 Civic Center Way, El Cajon, CA 92020.
