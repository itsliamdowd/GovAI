## Housing Division — Programs and Contact

The City's Housing Division works to increase, improve and preserve the supply of decent affordable housing, revitalize neighborhoods, expand economic opportunities, and provide improved community facilities and services principally for low income persons. For information on housing programs available through the City, call 619-441-1710 or email housing@elcajon.gov.

The Housing page links to an AB 987 Affordable Housing Database (listed as ending June 2026), an SB-341 Report, and a list of Below Market Rate Housing. ## Housing Element — Planning for Future Housing

The City estimates that approximately 3,200 new houses, condos or apartments will be necessary in the next eight years to keep up with El Cajon's growing population and housing needs. The updated Housing Element will identify potential sites where new housing development should occur and programs for how El Cajon can encourage new housing. For more information or to provide feedback, contact planning@elcajon.gov or 619-441-1742, option 3.

## Shelter and Housing Referrals

Dial 211 or (858) 300-1211 for Community Connect, 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, for housing and shelter information and referrals. 211 Shelter Referral is a confidential shelter referral line (dial 2-1-1, https://211sandiego.org/). Other shelter and housing providers listed by the City:

- Crisis House: crisis intervention, case management, and domestic violence shelter assistance. By appointment, (619) 444-1194.
- East County Transitional Living: emergency shelter, transitional living, drug addiction, and faith-based learning. Call for availability, (619) 442-0456.
- Home Start, Inc.: prevention, outreach, case management, rental assistance, housing assistance, motel vouchers and pregnant shelter programs. By appointment, (619) 430-0032.
- Interfaith Shelters: shelter in local churches. Call for availability, (619) 440-4591.

Source: https://www.elcajon.gov/resident-services/public-safety/addressing-homelessness/need-help

## Homeless Outreach — Requesting an ECHOT Visit

If you know the location of a homeless person who needs assistance, you can request that an El Cajon Homeless Outreach Team (ECHOT) visit the person to encourage them to accept services. To reach the City's homeless outreach team, call the El Cajon Police non-emergency number at 619-579-3311. Accepting outreach services is voluntary. For a medical emergency, or if you feel threatened in any way, call 9-1-1.

For concerns such as trash or alley issues, submit a service request with a detailed description at www.cityofelcajon.gov/service or call 619-441-1658.

Source: https://www.elcajon.gov/resident-services/public-safety/addressing-homelessness/faq
