## Building Codes in Effect (2025 California Building Standards Code)

Beginning on January 1, 2026, the El Cajon Community Development Department will enforce the 2025 Edition of California Building Standards Codes (also known as Title 24 of the California Codes of Regulations). Under State law, the City cannot approve projects that do not comply with the codes in effect at the time of plan submittal.

## Building Permits — How to Submit for a Plan Check

The Project Assistance Center Online (PACO) is the method to submit for plan check: www.elcajon.gov/PACO. To submit an online application you must first register an account with PACO (new PACO log-in procedure as of 4/15/2024). Submittals must be in electronic format via the PACO website. Complete and upload the permit application and any additional forms required for the complexity and scope of your project. Typical forms include the Certification of Easements, energy efficiency documentation, and a hazardous materials questionnaire.

A typical plan check submittal takes about three to four weeks, though time periods vary with workload. You will be contacted by email when the plan check is complete. You can then log in to your PACO account and view corrections to your plans. A recheck typically takes about one to two weeks.

Source: https://www.elcajon.gov/doing-business/building-and-fire-permits

## Contractor Permits

Building Safety can issue a permit to a licensed contractor or the contractor's agent. You need:
- A permit application with project description
- A workers' compensation certificate
- Contractors State License information
- A current City of El Cajon business license
- For reroofs, a reroof application
- For new meters or meter upgrades, documentation of approved location from SDG&E

## Mobile Home Installation Permits and Inspections

Building Safety inspects new mobile home installations, accessory structures for mobile homes, and state mandated mobile home park inspections.

To apply for a mobile home installation permit, submit to the Building Safety Division a permit application, a mobile home installation application, and two copies of the mobile home installation instructions (specific to your mobile home). For accessory structures, include a permit application, the mobile home accessory structures form, and two copies of your plans or standard plan approval. The park manager's signature is required on the mobile home installation application and the mobile home accessories form. Changes to the interior of the mobile home are permitted through the State Department of Housing and Community Development.

A mobile home installation permit application can typically be approved within three weeks. Your mobile home installation contractor will typically call for an inspection of the setup. If your park is scheduled for an inspection, the park manager will notify you of the time and date.

Source: https://www.elcajon.gov/doing-business/building-and-fire-permits

## Business License — Who Needs One and How to Apply

Title 5 of the City of El Cajon Municipal Code requires all businesses conducting business within the City limits to obtain a business license and pay the business license tax. This includes those who work out of their home, and businesses from outside the City that perform services and/or sales at the customer's place of business or residence in El Cajon.

Online filing is the most convenient and fastest method. Online you can apply for a new license, renew, pay a balance, update, close a license, or search for a licensed El Cajon business. The paper application can be filled out online, printed for signature, and submitted to the Finance Department at El Cajon City Hall, 200 Civic Center Way, El Cajon, CA, 92020.

For businesses with a location inside the City limits, the Community Development Department reviews the application to ensure the location is in an approved zone for the business use. Businesses conducted within a residential dwelling (Home Occupation) must comply with the Home Occupation Requirements in the Zoning Ordinance.

Source: https://www.elcajon.gov/your-government/departments/finance/business-licenses

## Business License — Renewals, Special Licenses, and Contact

Business licenses expire on December 31, and courtesy renewal notices are mailed mid-November of each year. The renewal process usually takes four to six weeks in December, January and February, and may take longer if the business location and/or description of business has changed.

In addition to a business license, a special operation license is required for the businesses and activities listed in City of El Cajon Municipal Code Section 5.16.030. Additional forms and fees are required for special operations and other licenses; contact the office for instructions. Monthly business license listings are available through the Online Business License - Business Search.

Contact: Business License Division, 200 Civic Center Way, El Cajon, CA 92020, (619) 441-1668.

Source: https://www.elcajon.gov/your-government/departments/finance/business-licenses

## Special Event Permit

For a small neighborhood event in the public right-of-way, fill out the Block Party Special Event Application and submit it through your PACO account. For larger events requiring street closures and/or taking place in the El Cajon Superblock including Prescott Promenade and surrounding areas, contact Heather Carter (hcarter@elcajon.gov) for a special event application. If unsure which application to complete, call 619-441-1754.

Applications may be submitted up to 365 days in advance. Submitting a minimum of sixty (60) days prior to the event is strongly recommended, especially for large or complex events. Acceptance of your application is not final approval. All approvals, requirements and contract fees are due a minimum of two (2) weeks prior to your event date, and no permit is valid until all provisions are met. Future changes to event plans must be requested in writing and are subject to approval.

Source: https://www.elcajon.gov/i-want-to/apply-for/special-event-permit
