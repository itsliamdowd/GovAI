# City Services A–Z

## Library — Branches & Hours

Santa Monica Public Library operates five locations: Main Library (601 Santa Monica Blvd), Pico Branch (2201 Pico Blvd), Fairview Branch (2101 Ocean Park Blvd), Montana Branch (1704 Montana Ave), and Ocean Park Branch (2601 Main St). Main Library hours: Mon–Thu 10 a.m.–8 p.m., Sat 10 a.m.–5 p.m. Pico hours remain standard (check smpl.org). Fairview, Montana, and Ocean Park have rotating full-service and self-service hours; call (310) 458-8600 or check santamonica.gov/library-locations-and-hours for current schedule.

Source: https://www.santamonica.gov/library-locations-and-hours

## Library — Library Card & Services

Library cards are free and open to all. Services include book/media borrowing, digital collections, public computers, Wi-Fi, study spaces, programming (classes, storytimes, book clubs), and interlibrary loans. No residency requirement to get a card. Visit any branch in person or apply online. The library also hosts the Friends bookstore (hours volunteer-dependent).

Source: https://www.santamonica.gov/library-locations-and-hours

## Trash Collection & Rates

Santa Monica's Resource Recovery & Recycling (RRR) collects trash, recycling, and organics three streams weekly (black/landfill, blue/recycling, green/organics). Residential rates are bi-monthly (six billing cycles per year). A 65-gallon once-weekly cart costs $114.26/month (July 2026–June 2027). Rates increase July 1 annually through 2027 (Schedule adopted June 2023: $114.26 in 2025-26, $126.83 in 2026, $140.78 in 2027). Multi-family and commercial rates vary by size; example: 2 cubic yard 1x weekly commercial bin is $387.84/month (FY 2025-26). Rates cover curbside collection, street sweeping, monthly recycling events, bulky item pickups (3 free per year), and on-demand hazardous waste pickup.

Source: https://www.santamonica.gov/solid-waste-rates

## Trash — Bulky Item Pickup

Three free bulky-item pickups per year are included in your rate. Request through 311; allow 7–10 days. Mattresses, furniture, appliances, e-waste accepted. Additional pickups beyond three/year are billed hourly at approximately $253.37/hour (as of July 2025; verify current rate). Extra trash container is $153.10/month (July 2026–June 2027).

Source: https://www.santamonica.gov/topic-explainers/residential-collection-services

## Water Billing & Conservation Rules

Santa Monica Water Resources (city-owned utility) bills water bi-monthly. Water allowance and conservation thresholds apply: residential single-family units have 22 HCF (hundred cubic feet) per billing period; multi-family have 11 HCF per unit. Conservation tiers encourage reduced use with higher rates for heavier consumption. A drought-responsive three-tier rate structure took effect July 1, 2025 (or soon after, pending Council directive). Customers also pay a bi-monthly fixed service charge and may be subject to penalties for exceeding thresholds during water shortage declarations. Check your bill for "Water Allowance" line to see assigned conservation amount.

Source: https://www.santamonica.gov/media/Public%20Works/Water%20Resources/FINAL218Notice2025_InformationFactSheet_Web.pdf

## Water & Utilities — Billing & Payment

Billing questions: Finance Department Utility Billing, (310) 458-8224. Pay online, by phone, or by mail through the city's utility billing portal. Delinquent accounts may face service interruption. Low-income residents may qualify for assistance; contact Finance or 311.

Source: https://www.santamonica.gov/topic-explainers/water-and-wastewater

## Utility Users Tax

Santa Monica applies a 10% Utility Users Tax on electricity, gas, water, and other utilities. This tax is added to your utility bill monthly.

Source: https://www.santamonica.gov/utilities-taxes

## Public Records Request — Process

Submit requests through the city's online Public Records Request Center at santamonica.gov (you can request anonymously by keeping your reference number). You can also mail or email your request to the City Clerk's Office. The city responds within 10 calendar days and must state whether responsive records exist and whether they can be released. In some cases, a 14-day extension applies. Electronic copies are delivered free through the online system.

Source: https://www.santamonica.gov/process-explainers/how-to-request-public-records

## Public Records Request — Fees & Timelines

Copies cost $0.20 per page. Electronic copies on a flash drive cost $6.50. Certified copies are $24.47 per document. No records will be copied until payment is received. Response is due within 10 calendar days of receipt; extensions of up to 14 days may apply.

Source: https://www.santamonica.gov/process-explainers/how-to-request-public-records

## Public Records — Council Documents & Portals

Council documents are searchable in the public portal at publicdocs.smgov.net. The Rent Control Document Portal holds records back to 2005; older records can be viewed at City Hall (with a workstation for viewing) or requested through the Public Records process.

Source: https://www.santamonica.gov/process-explainers/how-to-request-public-records

## Police Reports — Obtaining Copies

Police reports are requested through the SMPD Records Unit. Contact (310) 458-8431 with questions. After the request is received, the Records Unit contacts you with the fee. Payment is required before copying. Reports include incident details, arrest records, and traffic accidents.

Source: https://www.santamonica.gov/process-explainers/how-to-obtain-a-copy-of-a-police-report

## Fire Records — Obtaining Copies

Fire department records (incident reports, permits, inspections) are requested through the Fire Department's Records Bureau or via the city's Public Records portal. Contact SMFD non-emergency (310) 458-8669 for specifics. Fees apply.

Source: https://www.santamonica.gov/process-explainers/how-to-request-a-fire-record

## Birth, Death, Marriage Records

These are not held by the City of Santa Monica. They are kept by the Los Angeles County Registrar-Recorder/County Clerk. Contact the LA County Registrar-Recorder at (323) 462-2137 or laregistrar.lacounty.gov.

Source: https://www.santamonica.gov/process-explainers/how-to-request-public-records

## Property Records & Tax Information

Property ownership records and property tax information are held by the Los Angeles County Assessor. Contact the County Assessor at (213) 974-3211 or assessor.lacounty.gov.

Source: https://www.santamonica.gov/process-explainers/how-to-request-public-records

## Voting & Elections

Santa Monica holds city elections every four years (council members), with state/federal elections in odd and even years respectively. Register to vote with LA County Registrar-Recorder/County Clerk. Santa Monica residents can check polling locations, voter status, and sample ballots at the County website or through the city's Elections page. City Clerk's Office: (310) 458-8211.

Source: https://www.santamonica.gov/city-clerks-office

## Community Classes & CREST

Santa Monica Parks & Recreation offers community classes in fitness, arts, recreation, sports, and youth programs through the CREST after-school program. Classes are offered at recreation centers and parks. Register online through the Parks & Recreation registration system or call (310) 458-8701. Scholarships available for low-income residents.

Source: https://www.santamonica.gov/parks-and-recreation

## Senior Services & Ken Edwards Center

The Ken Edwards Center (1685 Main St, Room 101) is Santa Monica's senior hub, offering recreation, classes, social programs, fitness, and meals. Eligibility starts at age 55. Call (310) 458-8630 or visit in person. Programs include senior fitness, technology classes, social events, and nutrition support.

Source: https://www.santamonica.gov/departments/community-services

## Sales Tax Rate

Santa Monica's sales tax rate is 10.25% (as of Oct 2026). Rate may vary slightly by LA County. Confirm current rate with the state's Department of Tax and Fee Administration.

Source: https://www.santamonica.gov/topic-explainers/sales-tax

## Transient Occupancy Tax (TOT)

Santa Monica applies a Transient Occupancy Tax on short-term lodging (hotels, motels, vacation rentals). The rate is 15% of the nightly room rate (as of Oct 2026). Hosts of short-term rentals and hotels are responsible for collecting and remitting TOT to the city. Check santamonica.gov for current rate and reporting requirements.

Source: https://www.santamonica.gov/business-taxes

## Business License Tax

All businesses operating in Santa Monica must pay an annual business license tax based on gross receipts. Rates vary by business category; minimum tax applies. License renewal is due July 1 annually. Contact Business License Office at (310) 458-8745 or Business.License@santamonica.gov.

Source: https://www.santamonica.gov/topic-explainers/business-taxes

## 311 Customer Service

Santa Monica 311 is the hub for non-emergency service requests and information. Dial 311 (or (866) 311-7266 from outside the city) 7:30 a.m.–5:30 p.m. Mon–Thu, 8 a.m.–5 p.m. Fri. Online: santamonica.gov/311, email 311@santamonica.gov, or the City of Santa Monica mobile app (24/7). Use 311 for potholes, graffiti, streetlights, missed trash, noise complaints, code violations, parking issues, and general city info.

Source: https://www.santamonica.gov/311
