## Downtown Fullerton Events

First Night in Fullerton is the city's annual New Year's Eve party. It offers family-oriented, alcohol-free activities at numerous locations in the streets, sidewalks and buildings of Fullerton's historic downtown.

The Fullerton Night Market runs April through October. It offers farm-fresh produce, flowers, fresh-baked breads, live entertainment, an outdoor beer garden, arts and crafts, and activities for children. The Winter Market is held in December and features crafters, vendors, entertainment, and an early visit from Santa.

## Fire Department — Stations and Staffing

Fullerton's six fire stations house a ladder truck and six front-line engines. They are staffed 24/7 by Firefighter/Paramedics. The current Fire Headquarters (Station 1) is at 312 E. Commonwealth. Fullerton and Brea re-established their independent fire departments in 2022. The department has served the community since 1908, and the current Fire Chief is Adam Loeser (2018 - Present).

## Library Local History Room — Research Resources

The Local History Room is an archive dedicated to preserving knowledge and artifacts about the history of the Fullerton community. It can be used to research the history of your home, your family, or the town of Fullerton.
- Research Fullerton: digital photos, city directories, and local newspapers.
- Research Your Family: digital yearbooks, birth and death records, and city directories.
- Research Your Home: a digital building database and city directories, for the history of your home or business.

Digital archive collections are Building Stories (Historic and Cultural Resources), Headline History (local newspapers, magazines, and pictorials), and Mapping Fullerton (historic maps and aerials).

Source: https://www.cityoffullerton.com/about-us/arts-culture/history-of-fullerton/library-s-local-history-room
