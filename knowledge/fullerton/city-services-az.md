## Recreational Trails — General Information and Contacts

Fullerton has approximately 28 miles of recreational trails for hikers, equestrians, and mountain bike riders. Trail maps are available for a small fee at the Parks and Recreation Department, or use the Interactive Map of City Parks and Recreational Trails. West Coyote Hills is open to the public, with initial trails in the Robert Ward Nature Preserve. Stay within designated trails, follow all trail use guidelines, and only ride bikes and take pets where specifically approved.

For information, comments, or suggestions about trails, call the Parks and Recreation Department at (714) 738-6575. For trail maintenance issues, call the Public Works Maintenance Department at (714) 738-6897.

Source: https://www.cityoffullerton.com/about-us/how-do-i/find/recreational-trails

## Trail Rules — Dogs and Trail Etiquette

All dogs must be on a leash 10' or shorter at all times in all public parks and trails. Dogs may run off-leash only at the Fullerton Pooch Park, located near the Hunt Branch Library. Owners are asked to pick up after their dogs and dispose of all waste in trash cans.

Hikers must yield to equestrians, and bike riders must yield to both hikers and equestrians. Pass on the left side when safe, and announce your intentions when overtaking. Take special precautions when passing equestrians, because sudden movements may startle horses. Stay on marked paths, respect adjacent private properties, and hold on to your trash until it can be properly disposed of.

## Sharps Disposal

In California, it is illegal to place containers of used sharps (needles, syringes, lancets, auto injectors, infusion sets, connection needles) in household trash. Place them in a strong plastic container like an empty laundry detergent or bleach bottle, seal it with duct tape, label it "DO NOT RECYCLE," and bring it to the Anaheim HHW Center. For more information, visit CalRecycle.ca or SafeNeedleDisposal.org.

## Organic Waste Recycling — SB 1383

SB 1383 requires California to reduce organic waste (food waste, green waste, paper products, etc.) landfill disposal by 75% by 2025, and requires that 20% of currently disposed edible food be recovered for human consumption by 2025. Multi-family, residential, and commercial units must meet these goals by putting organic waste into an organics bin, which is picked up by the waste hauler on the typical collection day. The goal is to get organic waste recycling to all residents and businesses by January 1, 2024.

Source: https://www.cityoffullerton.com/about-us/how-do-i/get/recycling-info

## Commercial and Multifamily Recycling Requirements (AB 1826 and AB 341)

AB 1826 (Mandatory Organics Recycling Law) requires businesses and other generators of 4 or more cubic yards of solid waste per week to arrange for recycling services for food waste, green waste, landscape and pruning waste, nonhazardous wood waste, and food-soiled paper waste mixed with food waste. Multifamily complexes must arrange recycling for the same materials except food waste and food-soiled paper.

AB 341 (Mandatory Recycling Law) requires businesses, including public entities, generating four (4) cubic yards or more of commercial solid waste per week, or a multifamily residential dwelling of five units or more, to arrange for recycling services starting July 1, 2012. Contact Republic Services (MG Disposal) to sign up or learn about recycling options.

Source: https://www.cityoffullerton.com/about-us/how-do-i/get/recycling-info

## Residential Containers, Walk-Out Service and Special Item Pick-Up (FAQs)

Republic Services delivers three 96-gallon containers to each customer: green for recyclables, brown for green waste, and black for trash. Smaller 64-gallon containers can be requested, and 35-gallon containers can be provided in certain situations; contact Republic Services for details. Carts are the property of Republic Services. Damaged carts are replaced at no fee. Lost or stolen carts are replaced at no cost, with a two-time limit during the term of the agreement. Lids are designed to remain closed, but a container will be serviced if a small amount of trash prevents the lid from closing.

Walk-out service, in which Republic Services employees take carts to the curb and return them for elderly and disabled residents, has no extra fee. Residents must meet eligibility requirements and complete an authorization form.

Republic Services' "Special Item Pick-up Program" is offered at no cost to residents year-round, with a limit of 10 items per collection. Air conditioning units, freezers and refrigerators are charged an environmental charge. Christmas tree recycling continues at no extra charge, plus a two-week period after the holiday in which residents may place all the trash generated during the holiday season at the curb.

## Street Sweeping — Schedule and Missed Streets

Public Works' Street Division sweeps all City streets each week, excluding private streets and streets without a curb and gutter. Each area is swept on the same day each week, usually the day after trash pickup. Streets are not swept on holidays and days of heavy rain. If you suspect your street was missed, call (714) 738-6897. Do not dump grass clippings or heavy objects in the gutter, and do not park vehicles in the street on sweeping days.

Thursday Zone: signs have been updated, and the new odd/even schedule begins Thursday, July 2, 2026. Odd-numbered addresses are swept on the 1st & 3rd weeks of the month; even-numbered addresses on the 2nd & 4th weeks. Cul-de-sac residents should refer to posted signs. Warning citations will be issued on July 2, July 9, July 16, July 23, 2026. Regular citations resume on August 6, 2026. The Monday, Tuesday, and Wednesday Zones already operate on the new schedule. In the Friday Zone, beginning July 3, 2026, citations are postponed until all Friday-route signage has been updated, and weekly sweeping continues as normal.

Questions: Public Works Streets Supervisor Rigo Aceves, (714) 738-5344. For street sweeping sign inquiries, call (714) 738-6897.

Source: https://www.cityoffullerton.com/government/departments/public-works/streets-sidewalks/street-sweeping

## Street Sweeping — Citations and Holidays

The Fullerton Police Department Traffic Bureau manages parking enforcement. Citations are issued where signs are posted indicating "No Parking for Street Sweeping." For parking enforcement inquiries, call (714) 738-6810 or email streetsweeping@fullertonpd.org. To contest a citation, follow the procedures on the back of the citation. If you lost your citation, call (800) 376-2794 and have your vehicle license plate number available.

No street sweeping or parking enforcement is performed on these holidays (unless otherwise noted): New Year's Day, Presidents' Day, Memorial Day, Independence Day, Labor Day, Veterans Day, Thanksgiving (including the following Friday), Christmas Eve & Christmas Day, and New Year's Eve. If your sweeping day falls on an observed holiday or during the fifth week of the month, your street is swept on its next regularly scheduled sweeping day.
