## Resident Only Parking Permit — How to Order and Fees

Residents must register for an account on the parking permit portal at https://fullerton.getapermit.net. You must upload a current and valid utility bill (gas, electricity or water) in your name to create your permit account. Once the document is validated, you can log back in to pay for your permit. For help with the portal, email support@getapermit.net; responses are sent Monday through Friday from 8am to 5pm.

A single property is limited to five (5) permits/placards per household. Lost or stolen permits will not be replaced. The annual issuance fee is $10.00 per participating household, and each permit/placard costs $2.00 (limit 5). The maximum assessed to any participating property is $20.00. These fees are not charged to residents who do not request permits.

Participation is optional, but vehicles without a permit are prohibited from parking on the street during the hours of the restriction. Resident Only Permit Parking does not exempt a vehicle from Section 8.44.080 of the Fullerton Municipal Code ("Early Morning Parking") between 2:00 a.m. and 5:00 a.m.

Source: https://www.cityoffullerton.com/about-us/how-do-i/get/resident-only-parking-permits

## Resident Only Parking Permit — Picking Up Permits at City Hall

Your address must be in a designated Resident Only "Permit Parking" area. You must bring an original current utility or telephone bill to City Hall to purchase and pick up your permit(s).

Property owners may purchase a permit online and pick it up at City Hall, bringing the online payment receipt and an original current utility or telephone bill. Owners may also purchase permits directly at City Hall, 303 W Commonwealth Ave., with an original current utility or telephone bill.

Non-property-owning residents must purchase permits onsite at City Hall, 303 W Commonwealth Ave., bringing a landlord authorization letter from the property owner granting permission to obtain permits, plus an original current utility or telephone bill.

## Resident Only Parking — Request Permit Parking for Your Street

Permit parking is available to regulate residential on-street parking problems associated with student parking. Requests must follow these steps:

- Petition: All requests must be made by petition using the City's petition form.
- Identify Problem: The request must identify the source of the parking problem (e.g., high school or college student parking) that is interfering with resident parking availability. Any desired variation from the standard enforcement must be included on the petition.
- Support: At least sixty-five percent (65%) of property owners must indicate support for the request to go to the City Council. Each property counts as one vote, and multiple signatures for the same property count as one vote.
- Submit: Deliver completed petitions to City of Fullerton, 303 W. Commonwealth Avenue, Fullerton, CA 92832, Attention: Traffic Engineering Division.

For additional information call (714) 738-6858.

## Building Inspections — Scheduling by Phone (Voice Permits)

Call the automated Voice Permits™ IVR System at (714) 738-6543, 24 hours a day, for all Building/Fire Permit inspections. You can schedule inspections, cancel inspections, and retrieve inspection results. You need a touch-tone telephone (or the dialing keypad on your mobile phone), a site-specific permit number, and an inspection code.

Menu options: Press 1 to schedule an inspection, 2 to cancel an inspection, 3 to obtain inspection results, 0 to transfer to staff, # to disconnect and hang up, and * for additional information anytime during the call. Enter the permit number followed by the # key, then the inspection code. For scheduling, also enter the inspection date and a gate code (if applicable). Calls placed before midnight will be placed on the following working day's schedule. At the end of the call you are issued a confirmation number, and the City recommends keeping a record of confirmation numbers.

On a smartphone or tablet, use the dialing pad, not the texting or typing keyboard, to enter letters and numbers. Example: BLD12-00001 should be entered by dialing 2531200001. If your cellular connection is weak, try a land-line phone. To view today's inspections live for an estimated time of inspection, visit the EASYDEV Platform.

Example inspection codes: Roof Sheathing 130; Insulation 145; Drywall-1st Layer 150; Plumbing Final 996; Mechanical Final 997; Electrical Final 998. The full code list is in the Inspections Guide (PDF) on the page.

## Alarm System Permit — Who Needs One and Fees

The Fullerton Police Department has contracted with Alarm Program Systems LLC / CitySupport to administer the Alarm Program. Any individual or business with a functioning alarm system at a residence or place of business in Fullerton must obtain an Alarm System Permit. Permits are good until cancelled by the alarm user and must be renewed annually. Permits expire on the anniversary date of their issuance.

Annual Alarm System Permit Fees (New/Renewal): Residential $44.00; Small Commercial (less than 10,000 sq. ft.) $50.00; Large Commercial (10,000 sq. ft. and greater) $100.00.

Contact: Alarm Program Systems / CitySupport, https://fullertonca.citysupport.org, (888) 865-9770, Monday-Friday 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM ET, fullertonca@citysupport.org. Mailing address: Fullerton Alarm Program, Dept LA 25484, Pasadena, CA 91185-5484. If your emergency contact information changes during the year, contact the Alarm Permit Representative to update your records.

## False Alarm Fees

False alarm fines per permit year: 1st-2nd false alarms, no charge; 3rd false alarm, $55.00 each; 4th false alarm, $155.00 each; 5th and every one thereafter, $231.00 each. The first two false alarms are not billed if a current permit is on file; otherwise they are billed at $55.00 each. The "Notice of False Alarm" left by the responding officer is considered your notification of police response.

Under Municipal Code Chapter 3.51, users with more than ten false alarms in a permit year may have their permit reviewed by the Chief of Police, who may revoke it or impose restrictions and conditions. Notice of any action is sent by certified mail. To protest false alarm fees or Chief of Police action, submit a written request for a hearing to the Alarm Coordinator; the request must be received within ten days of receipt of the notification. The hearing is before the City Manager. A user who fails to obtain a permit within sixty days after the effective date of the ordinance, or is more than sixty days delinquent in renewing, is assessed an additional charge. Any violation of the chapter is a misdemeanor.

## Film Permits

A film permit is required for commercial filming or commercial still photography on any public space in Fullerton, including all parks, public right-of-way, public property, parking lots, and City facilities. Commercial filming and photography includes motion picture and television productions, videos, web broadcasts, commercials, and off-news productions. Film permits are also required for student, educational and non-profit filming, and for filming on private property depending on the scope of work and nature of the production.

FilmL.A. is the City's film permit coordinator. Submit applications through the FilmL.A. Online Permit System at filmla.com at least 5 business days before the first day of filming. Fees include an application processing fee and a site use fee. Any other City services required (e.g., Police or Fire staff) are invoiced separately.

Contact: FilmL.A., Inc., 6255 W. Sunset Blvd. 12th Floor, Hollywood, CA 90028, (213) 977-8600, info@filmla.com. FilmL.A.'s Production Planning Department can also be reached at 213-977-8600.
