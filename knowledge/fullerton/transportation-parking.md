## Early Morning Parking Prohibition (2:00 a.m.–5:00 a.m.)

No person shall park a vehicle subject to Section 22500 of the California Vehicle Code on any street or highway, or any City controlled parking facility in the City of Fullerton, between the hours of two a.m. and five a.m. of any day, except as may be provided by lawfully posted notice (F.M.C. 8.44.080 & F.M.C. 8.44.085). Certain areas may be exempted by resolution of the City Council through a Select Street Exemption, an Early Morning Parking Permit, or a Temporary Exemption. An Early Morning Parking Exemptions Map (PDF) is available on the page.

## Early Morning Parking — Select Street Exemption

Property owners may apply for a "Select Street Exemption" for an entire block, either one side or both. Property owners must submit a petition signed by a majority of the property owners on both sides of the street. The smallest area which may be exempted is one city block. Petitions and procedures may be obtained at the front desk of the Public Works Department Engineering Division in City Hall.

There is a $729 non-refundable fee required to forward the petition to the Transportation and Circulation Commission for an exemption (see Engineering Fees). Exemptions are only granted for areas having an off-street parking deficiency under existing City ordinances. Under current standards, anything less than a two-car garage for a typical single-family home is considered parking deficient. Other standards apply to multi-family units/developments.

An exempted street will be posted at the entrance to the street and/or tract with signs reading "Parking Permitted 2:00 a.m. to 5:00 a.m., this side here to corner". Contact the Public Works Department Traffic Division at 714-738-6899 for further information.

## Early Morning Parking Permits

Property owners or tenants of a single-family or multi-family residence may apply for Early Morning Parking Permits, which exempt the permit holder from Fullerton Municipal Code 8.44.080. Permits allow vehicles to park adjacent to the address for which the permit was issued.

The applicant must satisfy all requirements in the Program Policy and apply through the Get-a-Permit website (fullerton.getapermit.net). Once approved, permits may be purchased for a flat fee of $50 per permit plus applicable fees and will be mailed to the account address. There is no pro-rating of permit costs. To verify whether an address qualifies, use the "Check My Address" feature on the Get-a-Permit website.

Permits will not be issued to residents on a street that is posted with signs allowing early morning parking. The permit does not guarantee on-street parking. For help registering for an account or applying, email Permit Support at support@getapermit.net.

## Removing an Early Morning Parking Exemption and Rules on Exempted Streets

Overnight parking exemptions on a street can be removed in the same manner as they were approved, with a majority vote of the block's property owners. On streets with approved early morning parking, street parking is open to the public, including the portion in front of your house.

Restrictions on streets with approved early morning parking:
- Continuous parking in excess of 72 hours is prohibited, and vehicles can be cited and/or towed. Contact the Police Department's Traffic Division at (714)738-5313.
- Vehicles with expired registration are prohibited from parking on the street and are subject to citation and/or towing. Contact the Police Department's Traffic Division at (714)738-5313.
- Vehicles on the street may not be repaired or sold as part of a business at a property designated as residential. Contact Community Preservation at (714)738-5799.
- Commercial vehicles are restricted to 3 hours of continuous parking on public streets. Contact the Police Department's Traffic Division at (714)738-5313.

## Resident Only Parking Permit — Online Account, Fees and Limits

The existing Resident Only Parking permits expire at midnight on December 31, 2022. The City began using a new parking permit portal, and everyone must register for a new account online at https://fullerton.getapermit.net. You must upload a current and valid utility bill (gas, electricity or water) in your name to create your permit account. Once the uploaded document is validated, you can log back in to pay for your permit.

A single property is limited to five (5) permits per household. Lost or stolen permits will not be replaced. The annual issuance fee is $10.00 per participating household, and each permit/placard costs $2.00 (limit 5). The maximum assessed to any participating property is $20.00. These fees are not charged to residents who do not request permits.

Participation is optional, but vehicles without a permit are prohibited from parking on the street during the hours of the restriction. Resident Only Permit Parking does not exempt a vehicle from Section 8.44.080 of the Fullerton Municipal Code ("Early Morning Parking," 2:00 a.m. to 5:00 a.m.). For help with the portal, email support@getapermit.net; responses are sent Monday through Friday from 8am to 5pm. Report violations to the PD Traffic Bureau at (714) 738-5313. For additional information on the process, call (714) 738-6899.

## Request Resident Only Permit Parking for Your Street

Permit parking in Fullerton regulates and manages residential on-street parking problems associated with student parking. Requests must follow these rules:
- Petition: all requests must be made by petition using the City's petition form.
- Identify the problem: the request must identify the source of the parking problem (e.g. high school or college student parking) that interferes with the availability of parking for residents. Any desired variation from the standard enforcement must be included on the petition.
- Support: a minimum of sixty-five percent (65%) of the property owners must indicate support for the request to proceed to the City Council. Each property is one vote (multiple signatures for the same property count as one vote).
- Submit: deliver completed petitions to City of Fullerton, 303 W. Commonwealth Avenue, Fullerton, CA 92832, Attention: Traffic Engineering Division.

For additional information, call (714) 738-6858.

## Getting Resident Parking Permits for Approved Streets

Your address must be in a designated Resident Only "Permit Parking" area. You must bring an original current utility or telephone bill to City Hall to purchase and pick up your permit(s).

Property owners may purchase permits online and pick them up at City Hall, bringing the online payment receipt and an original current utility or telephone bill. They may also purchase permits directly at City Hall, 303 W Commonwealth Ave., with an original current utility or telephone bill.

Non-property-owning residents must purchase permits onsite at City Hall, 303 W Commonwealth Ave., bringing a landlord authorization letter from the property owner granting permission to obtain permits, and an original current utility or telephone bill.

## Reporting a Streetlight Outage

Public Works' Building & Facilities Division is responsible for repairing street lights on public streets. If a street light on a public street has burned out or is malfunctioning, report it online, call Public Works at (714) 738-6897, or email maintenance@cityoffullerton.com.

Fullerton is divided into four street lighting districts, one for each day of the week, Monday through Thursday. The City tries to repair most street lights according to the weekly schedule, but repairs can take longer. There are more than 6,000 City-owned streetlights and only two electricians available for repair work. Special repair parts can take two or more weeks to order. If the problem is with the power system, repairs depend on Southern California Edison's crew availability, and rebuilding an outdated Edison transformer can take two to three months. If a repair request has not been completed within 10 working days, call Public Works at (714) 738-6897 for a status update.

If you live in a homeowner's association (HOA) area, the street lights are probably maintained by Edison. Streetlights mounted on power poles are usually owned and maintained by Edison as well. For those, call Southern California Edison (1-800-611-1911) or your Homeowner's Association.
