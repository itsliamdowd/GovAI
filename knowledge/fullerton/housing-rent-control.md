## Homelessness — City Overview and Recuperative Care/Navigation Center

The City of Fullerton addresses homelessness through cooperation among city staff, volunteers, and nonprofit and private organizations. To support local homeless efforts in the wake of COVID-19, visit lovefullerton.org. The Illumination Foundation's proposed Recuperative Care and Navigation Center is at 3535 West Commonwealth Avenue. The page links a Frequently Asked Questions / Fact Sheet and a Site Plan for the proposed facility.

Because of Martin v. Boise and the North Orange County Settlement Agreement, the City had been legally prohibited from enforcing restrictions on camping on public property. As a result of the City's safe parking program and its support of the Illumination Foundation facility, the Federal Judge has authorized the City to begin limited enforcement of the encampment at Gilbert and Valencia Street.

## Homelessness — Regional Efforts and Navigation Centers

The North Service Planning Area (North SPA) comprises the cities of Placentia, Anaheim, Brea, Buena Park, Cypress, Fullerton, La Habra, La Palma, Los Alamitos, Orange, Villa Park, Stanton, and Yorba Linda. The cities are collaborating on a regional approach to homelessness in response to a Federal Mandate and Martin v. Boise (2018). Under a local mandate by U.S. District Federal Judge David Carter, North SPA cities must work on a regional homeless housing plan that includes Navigation Centers.

The 13 North SPA cities signed a Memorandum of Understanding that resulted in two homeless navigation centers providing a total of 250 beds. The Placentia facility is open, and the Buena Park facility is scheduled to open in July. The North SPA also reached a settlement agreement in the Federal Court case. It specifies the percentage of shelter beds that must be available in order to reinstate enforcement of the City's anti-camping regulations on public property.

The Navigation Centers are referral-only facilities with managed and controlled access in and out via transportation only. They are to provide health resources, job and social skills, and opportunities to reunite with family and friends.

## Homeless Liaison Officers (Fullerton Police Department)

The Fullerton Police Department's Homeless Liaison Officer (HLO) Unit is made up of multiple officers dedicated to assisting the homeless population in Fullerton. The Unit works with partners including City Net and Orange County Mental Health. HLOs conduct Homeless Outreach each week with these organizations. The HLO Unit has direct contact with Orange County Social Services. A Clinician from Orange County Mental Health rides in the HLO police car 4 days a week. The page links to the Fullerton PD website for program details.

## Homeless Outreach — OC Health Care Agency

The OC Health Care Agency serves individuals who are homeless or on the verge of becoming homeless. It offers services to individuals of all ages with behavioral health conditions ranging from mild to severe and chronic. Staff visit known gathering places, including food banks, shelters, and public areas such as parks and libraries, to identify individuals and screen for program eligibility. The team responds to community referrals and works with community partners to support an individual's recovery.

## Homeless Solutions for Business and Property Owners

Business and commercial property owners with concerns about unwanted persons or criminal activity on their property can use the City's Quick Resources for Business. Suggested steps include:
- Post "No Trespassing" signs.
- Sign and send a Trespass Arrest Authorization to the Fullerton Police Department.
- Secure trash enclosures.
- Do not leave empty cardboard boxes publicly accessible.
- Reduce foliage that can be used as hiding spots.
- Ensure adequate lighting.
- Increase presence or implement private security measures.
- Do not feed or give money to non-patrons.

For life-threatening emergencies, call 9-1-1. Call the Code Enforcement Hotline at (714) 738-6553 for:
- Inhabiting of your or your neighbor's commercial property.
- Abandoned shopping carts or other unclaimed property.
- Excessive trash, debris, and outdoor storage.

Call the Police Department at (714) 738-6800 for:
- Drug activity or the presence of drugs or paraphernalia.
- Loitering outside of establishments that sell alcohol.
- Trespassing.
- Impeding access to businesses.
- Aggressive panhandling.
