## About Fullerton

Fullerton is in North Orange County, approximately 25 miles southeast of downtown Los Angeles and less than 20 minutes from Disneyland. The page states a population of approximately 140,000 and over 280+ days of sunshine. The city is home to five universities and colleges, including California State University Fullerton (CSUF) and Fullerton College.

Attractions listed include the Fullerton Museum Center (home of the Leo Fender Gallery), the Muckenthaler Cultural Center, the Fullerton Arboretum (on the CSUF campus, home to Heritage House), two championship golf courses and a driving range, an Olympic-sized swim complex, and more than 40 parks plus recreation and equestrian trails.

Source: https://www.cityoffullerton.com/about-us

## Downtown 3D Model

The City was awarded grant funding in late 2009 to build a 3D model of the downtown core. It includes buildings, selected site features and curb faces, and was completed in June 2010. It is available for download in Google SketchUp© format, and you need the free Google SketchUp© software to view it. For further information, contact Diana McCarthy, (714)738-6561.

Source: https://www.cityoffullerton.com/about-us/maps-gis/3d-models

## Downtown 3D Download Center

SketchUp and Google Earth KMZ files are available. The files must be downloaded in sections because of their size. The model extends approximately one mile in all directions from the intersection of Harbor Blvd. and Commonwealth Ave. and is split into 19 geo-referenced sections. Some SketchUp files are 10M or more, so a high-speed connection is suggested.

Developers and project applicants who are asked to provide a proposed project in the City's 3D model format should download the appropriate SketchUp file(s), insert their proposed structures, and give the modified data to the planner assigned to the project. The City's files must be used so the data is compatible with its geographic information software.

For help, call (714)738-6561 or email DianaM@cityoffullerton.com.

Source: https://www.cityoffullerton.com/about-us/maps-gis/3d-models/3d-download-center

## Volunteer Opportunities

City organizations seeking volunteers:
- **Community Emergency Response Team (CERT):** Trains people in disaster preparedness and basic disaster response skills, such as fire safety, light search and rescue, team organization, and disaster medical operations.
- **Neighborhood Watch:** A crime prevention program operated by Fullerton residents with the support of the police department.
- **Parks and Recreation:** Looks for volunteers for programs for toddlers, school-aged children, and teens.
- **Secret Pals:** Provides scholarships for underprivileged Fullerton children to join City-sponsored recreational programs such as swimming lessons, day camp, and sports camps.

## Downtown Fullerton Overview

Downtown Fullerton has a cluster of more than 70 historic buildings and more than 2,500 "free" public parking spaces. It has over 350,000 square feet of retail and more than 275,000 square feet of office space. Cultural facilities include the Museum Center, the Downtown Plaza, and Plummer Auditorium. The Transportation Center serves more than 350,000 passengers every year.

Source: https://www.cityoffullerton.com/about-us/downtown-fullerton

## Downtown Events

- **First Night in Fullerton:** The City's annual New Year's Eve party, with family-oriented, alcohol-free activities at numerous locations in the historic downtown.
- **Fullerton Night Market:** April through October. It offers farm-fresh produce, flowers, fresh-baked breads, live entertainment, an outdoor beer garden, arts and crafts, and activities for children.
- **Winter Market:** Held in December, with crafters, vendors, entertainment, and an early visit from Santa.

## SOCO District (South of Commonwealth)

The SOCO (South of Commonwealth) District is the area of businesses, eateries and lounges on Santa Fe Avenue, both east and west of Harbor Boulevard. It is anchored by the Fullerton Transportation Center on the east and Heroes restaurant on the west. The plans also include businesses on Commonwealth that back up to the alley separating them from the businesses on Santa Fe.

The page describes an alley and plaza improvement project expected to cost $1.7 million and scheduled to begin construction in 2004. It lists the Fullerton Redevelopment Agency at (714) 738-6877 for further information.

Source: https://www.cityoffullerton.com/about-us/downtown-fullerton/soco-district

## Fullerton Transportation Center

The Fullerton Transportation Center serves Amtrak and Metrolink. On an average workday, 3,000 commuters travel through it, making it the busiest stop along Metrolink's Orange County route. The page links to information on parking, the New Parking Structure Update, the Project Area, the Fullerton Transportation Center Framework Plan, and the Central Harbor Boulevard Corridor Transit Study (OCTA Transit Committee 12-14-17).
