## Police Services

Police services in Laguna Niguel are provided by contract with the Orange County Sheriff's Department (OCSD). Services include patrol, traffic enforcement, accident analysis and investigation, parking enforcement, and general and special investigations. ## Fire Services (Orange County Fire Authority)

The City partners with the Orange County Fire Authority (OCFA) for fire and emergency medical services. OCFA is an "all risk" provider. Besides medical and fire emergencies, it responds to hazardous materials incidents, floods, water rescues, earthquakes, bomb threats, terrorism and more. When OCFA is taxed beyond the norm, automatic and mutual aid resources are available from outside fire agencies.

## Fire Prevention Tips from OCFA

The City asks residents to "watch the water," because drowning happens silently and without warning. Never swim alone, ensure you have barriers around your pool, and learn CPR. Residents living near a wildland urban interface area should have a defensible space, use non-combustible materials, and have a plan should a fire occur.

## Fire Hazard Severity Zones (FHSZ)

State law requires CAL FIRE to map Fire Hazard Severity Zones statewide. On March 24, 2025, CAL FIRE released an updated FHSZ map for Laguna Niguel, classifying areas as Very High, High, or Moderate Fire Hazard Zones. The City Council adopted the updated state-mandated FHSZ ordinance on June 3, 2025. The City cannot decrease a fire hazard severity designation set by CAL FIRE. It may only increase one. Residents can enter their address in the interactive map to see their designated zone.

Properties within the zones must provide defensible space clearance around buildings. OCFA provides defensible space disclosure inspections to homeowners. New buildings must use fire-resistive features such as those in Chapter 7A of the California Building Code (see Building Handout 131-VHFHSZ). Property owners must disclose to prospective buyers that the property is in a FHSZ.

Public Counter Hours: Monday - Friday 8:00 a.m. to 4:00 p.m. CAL FIRE: (916) 633-7655.

## FHSZ Maps and Insurance

According to the California Department of Insurance, CAL FIRE maps are intended to drive local planning decisions, not insurance decisions. Under the Department's regulation finalized in October 2022, insurance companies must provide discounts for wildfire safety actions such as community mitigation and home-hardening, which CAL FIRE's maps do not assess.

## Safer from Wildfires (Insurance Discounts)

Safer from Wildfires is a three-layer approach covering the structure, the immediate surroundings, and the community. Every action under the program qualifies for an insurance discount. The actions listed are:
- Class-A fire-rated roof
- 5 foot ember resistant zone, including fencing
- Ember- and fire-resistant vents
- Non-combustible 6 inches at the bottom of exterior walls
- Enclosed eaves
- Upgraded windows
- Cleared vegetation, weeds and debris from under decks
- Removal of combustible sheds and other outbuildings to at least a distance of 30 feet
- Defensible space compliance
- Becoming a Firewise USA community

## Firewise USA

The Firewise USA recognition program is administered by the National Fire Protection Association (NFPA). It helps neighbors in a geographic area get organized and act to increase the ignition resistance of their homes and community. CAL FIRE Office of the State Fire Marshal's Community Wildfire Preparedness & Mitigation Division assists local communities in receiving the designation.

## Emergency Preparedness

The City is a member of the Orange County Operational Area and the Orange County Emergency Management Organization. In a major citywide emergency, residents should be prepared to sustain themselves and their families for at least 72 hours. The City's Simplified Action for Emergencies (S.A.F.E.) ## Evacuation Zones and Routes

Law enforcement and fire agencies issue evacuation warnings or orders for impacted areas, which may be tied to a particular Zone. The City's evacuation zone map covers all-hazard evacuation zones throughout the entire City, broken down by neighborhood. Residents are encouraged to learn their zone before an emergency. If zone boundaries must change during an emergency, the City would not evacuate an entire zone but would require evacuation of part of a zone or zones.

## Evacuation Terminology

Laguna Niguel uses the New California Standard Statewide Evacuation Terminology, developed by the California Office of Emergency Services.
- Evacuation Order: Mandatory evacuation order with hard road closures. Evacuated individuals will not be allowed to enter the area until the order is lifted.
- Evacuation Warning: Voluntary evacuation to give you advance warning in a slow moving event, allowing individuals time to evacuate an area.
- Shelter-in-Place: You are directed to stay in your residence, school, workplace or other building because it has been determined that staying where you are is safer than evacuating.
