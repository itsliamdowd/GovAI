## Traffic & Transportation Commission

The Traffic & Transportation Commission acts in an advisory capacity to the City Council on all traffic matters, such as reviewing traffic complaints, surveying, making recommendations relative to traffic conditions, and administering and enforcing traffic regulations. Its duties also include the study of public transportation, traffic regulations and traffic control devices, parking, and vehicle and pedestrian safety within the public right-of-way.

The Commission meets on the 4th Wednesday of each month at 7:00 p.m. in the City Council Chambers. It consists of five members and two City Council Liaisons. All Commission members serve two-year terms. For questions, contact Kathy Kelley, Engineering Services Manager, at (949) 362-4337.

## Report a Traffic Signal Problem and Traffic Division Services

To report a problem with a traffic signal light or pedestrian push signal, contact the Public Works Department at (949) 362-4337 or email traffic@cityoflagunaniguel.org.

Traffic Field Operations include designs, construction, traffic facilities and equipment, streetlights, roadway striping, and traffic control signs and pavement markers. The Traffic Division provides technical support and recommendations to the Traffic and Transportation Commission, which meets on the 4th Wednesday of each month at 7 p.m. in the Council Chambers.

## Traffic Signal Synchronization and Signal Upgrades

Public Works maintains 78 traffic signals that use traffic engineering and signal synchronization to allow for minimal stopping, which helps reduce travel times, idling at red lights, and greenhouse gas emissions. In Laguna Niguel, coordinated corridors include Alicia Parkway, Crown Valley Parkway, Niguel Road, La Paz Road, Moulton Parkway/Golden Lantern and Pacific Park Drive.

The Regional Traffic Signal Synchronization and Equipment Upgrades Project, done with OCTA and other regional agencies, covered 30 signal intersection upgrades along the entire length of Crown Valley Parkway. The City has budgeted for continued upgrades along Alicia Parkway, Cabot Road, and La Paz Road. The project is funded with Measure M2 funds as part of the Project P Program. A Traffic Management Center, listed as "coming soon," will capture real-time (not recorded) video feed to capture traffic volume counts, improve signal timing, and assist emergency response vehicles.

## Accessible Pedestrian Signal Push Buttons

Each year, the Public Works Department upgrades pedestrian signal push buttons to ADA compliant accessible pedestrian signal push buttons. The new buttons are vibro-tactile and have customizable audio broadcast of message and sounds for sight-impaired pedestrians. The project is supported with Community Development Block Grant (CDBG) funding.

## City-Owned EV Charging Stations — Fees, Time Limits, and Payment

The charging time limit and fees became effective January 1, 2025, under Resolution 2024-1493.
- Charging fee: Level 2 is $0.25 per kilowatt hour; Level 3 is $0.40 per kilowatt hour. Both are assessed upon plug-in.
- Charging time limit: 3 hours with a 15-minute grace period to move your vehicle.
- Overstay fee: $15.00 per hour ($0.25 per minute), following the expiration of the 3-hour limit and 15-minute grace period.
- Dwell time fee: $10.00 per hour ($0.167 per minute), at the time an EV battery is fully charged and following the expiration of a 15-minute grace period.
- There is no charging time limit, overtime fee, or dwell time fee at the Laguna Niguel/Mission Viejo Metrolink Station.

The City uses ChargePoint. Users may register for a FREE ChargePoint account, or tap-to-pay with a contactless credit or debit card or mobile wallet as a "guest." ChargePoint charges a service fee per charging session in addition to City fees: $0.25 per session for account holders and $0.49 per session for guest users (tap-to-pay).

For feedback, questions, or concerns, contact the Public Works Department at (949) 362-4337 or works@cityoflagunaniguel.org. For ChargePoint Driver Support, call (888) 758-4389.

## Residential Parking Permit Decals

The City administers residential permit parking districts for approved communities. Permit Parking Decals do not require a fee. Not every street or neighborhood requires permitted parking, and you may not obtain a decal if your street is not in a City-approved district.

Steps to obtain a decal:
1. Complete the Permit Parking Request Form.
2. Email the completed form to parkingpermits@cityoflagunaniguel.org with a copy of a recent utility bill with the applicant's name and property address, and a copy of the current California vehicle registration with the property address for each vehicle receiving a decal. If leasing, include a copy of a signed lease agreement with the applicant's name, the property address, and the date of the lease. You may also bring the documents in person.
3. Visit the Public Works Department on the Second floor of City Hall, 30111 Crown Valley Parkway, Laguna Niguel, CA 92677, Monday-Friday 8:00am-5:00pm, and bring a valid and current form of identification. An appointment is suggested; email parkingpermits@cityoflagunaniguel.org.

## Residential Parking Permits — FAQs (Tickets, Replacements, Guest Passes)

If you park in a City-approved Permit Parking District during the permit parking only hours shown on the posted signs without a City-issued decal displayed, you may be ticketed by the Orange County Sheriff's Department. There is no parking permit or decal fee.

If you are new to the area, sold your car, or need a replacement decal, complete a permit parking request form, provide proof of residency, and provide proof that your vehicle(s) is registered to a property within the permit parking district. If a replacement decal is issued, or if you sold your car, the old decal is voided from the City's permit parking database and will no longer be useable.

Guest passes are available on a case-by-case basis, but certain districts do not allow any guest passes. Check the City-approved Permit Parking District list. For questions, call the Public Works Department at (949) 362-4337.

Source: https://www.cityoflagunaniguel.org/1626/Residential-Parking-Permit-Districts

## Requesting a New Residential Permit Parking District

Per Municipal Code Sec. 7-4-607, residents may request permit parking for their community by petitioning the City. First submit a letter requesting permit parking to traffic@cityoflagunaniguel.org. The City will then provide a petition form, and only a City-provided petition form will be accepted.

After a petition is submitted, the City conducts several overnight parking studies over 3-4 weeks, typically between 12:00 AM and 3:00 AM. The process typically takes a minimum of 3-4 months from receipt of the petition to installing signs. Permit parking signs are installed at 300-foot intervals, at locations pre-determined through the petition process, and the City makes all efforts to install them on existing posts or streetlights. For more information, contact parkingpermits@cityoflagunaniguel.org.

## Bike Locker Rental at the Metrolink Station

The City maintains twenty (20) bicycle storage lockers at the Laguna Niguel/Mission Viejo Metrolink train station. Twelve (12) are on the west side of the tracks by the parking lot on Forbes Road, and eight (8) are on the east side along Camino Capistrano. Locker dimensions are triangular: 40 inches high x 30 inches wide x 85 inches long.

Cost is $5.00/month, due the 1st of every month, plus a one-time $50 refundable locker key deposit. Payment of $65 is collected when the City receives a completed Bike Locker Rental Agreement form; it covers the $50 deposit and the first three (3) months of rent. The $5 fee is collected starting in month four. The $50 deposit is refunded within 60 days after you return the locker key.

To rent, complete the Bike Locker Rental Agreement Form and email it to pwencroachment@cityoflagunaniguel.org, or apply in person at the Public Works Department on the Second floor of City Hall during regular business hours. After payment, you receive a locker map and may choose from the available lockers on either side of the tracks. Questions: (949) 362-4337. Appointments: pwencroachmentappt@cityoflagunaniguel.org.

## Aliso Creek Road Pilot Bikeway

In August 2024, Public Works installed a one-way Class IV Separated Bikeway in both the eastbound and westbound directions along Aliso Creek Road, between La Paz Road and Alicia Parkway. The pilot showcased three different types of vertical elements, striped buffer zones, and green conflict zone paint. The City gathered community feedback through surveys, including over 250 hard copy survey responses at the Sea Country Festival and an online survey through the Laguna Niguel Active Transportation Plan website, to determine the type of bikeway to expand citywide. In December 2024, the American Public Works Association of Southern California awarded Public Works a B.E.S.T. Award for Project of the Year in the category of Traffic, Mobility & Beautification for the project.

## Residential Street Sweeping Schedule and Parking Restrictions

The City contracts with Sweeping Corporation of America (SCA) for residential and arterial street sweeping. Residential street zones are swept the day after trash collection, every other week unless otherwise noted. Example: if trash is collected on Tuesday, the street will be swept on a Wednesday (Green zone). Zones: Monday (Blue), Tuesday (Yellow), Wednesday (Green), Thursday (Purple), Friday (Orange). Find your street on the Street Maintenance Schedule GIS layer on the City website.

Parking restrictions every week:
- No Parking Tuesdays & Wednesdays from 10:00am-2:00pm on Hidden Hills Rd and Niguel Ranch Rd.
- No Parking Thursdays & Fridays from 10:00am-2:00pm on Aliso Niguel Rd.

Parking restrictions the 2nd & 4th weeks of the month:
- No Parking 2nd & 4th Tuesday and Wednesday from 10:00am-2:00pm on Parc Vista, Seaglen, and Tessier.
- No Parking 2nd & 4th Thursday and Friday from 10:00am-2:00pm on East Nine Dr and La Gracia.

For street sweeping complaints and return for service calls, contact Sweeping Corp. of America at (310) 436-6510 or the Street Maintenance Superintendent, Jeff Metz, at (949) 362-4344.
