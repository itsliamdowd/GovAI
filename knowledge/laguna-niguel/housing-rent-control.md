## FY 2025-2029 Orange County Regional Assessment of Fair Housing

As a recipient of CDBG funds, the City of Laguna Niguel is required to comply with the Fair Housing Act of 1968 and to affirmatively further fair housing. The Orange County Regional Assessment of Fair Housing (AFH) is the primary policy document the City uses for this purpose. The City participates in preparing the FY 2025-2029 Orange County Regional AFH.

The draft was available for public review and comment from March 14 to April 14, 2025. A notice of comment period and public hearing was set for the April 15, 2025 City Council meeting. The City Council was to consider adopting the AFH at its May 6, 2025 City Council meeting. The AFH public meetings let Orange County residents give feedback on fair housing issues, and any resident may attend.

Source: https://www.cityoflagunaniguel.org/1679/Fair-Housing-Information

## Housing Element and RHNA

The Housing Element is a local plan adopted by the City that indicates the need for housing in terms of affordability, availability, adequacy, and accessibility. State law requires Housing Elements to be updated in 8-year cycles. The City is in the 6th cycle, the 2021-2029 Housing Element planning period. On February 22, 2024, the Department of Housing and Community Development (HCD) certified the revised 6th Cycle Housing Element. The City Council adopted it under Resolution No. 2023-1454.

Under the Regional Housing Needs Assessment (RHNA), the State estimates each region's housing needs for all income groups. On March 22, 2021, SCAG released its final allocation of housing units for each jurisdiction. The City received a final allocation of 1,207 new units for this planning period. In its Housing Element, the City must demonstrate to the State that there are no unnecessary barriers to the housing approval process in order to meet its RHNA allocation.

## Non-Profit Affordable Housing Resources

The City lists these non-profit resources for finding and building affordable housing, rehabilitating and preserving homes, and improving living conditions:
- Jamboree: nonprofit affordable housing developer; Heroes Landing is Orange County's largest veterans housing community, with 75 Veterans Affairs Supportive Housing (VASH) project-based vouchers.
- Mary Erickson Community Housing (MECH): affordable housing for working families in Southern California, including homeownership opportunities for income-qualified households.
- Affordable Housing Clearinghouse: supports moderate to very-low-income populations with financial education and home ownership opportunities. It has funded over $60 million in mortgage loans and $3.5 million in down payment assistance.
- South County Outreach: serves Orange County's homeless, underemployed, seniors, veterans, and at-risk residents, with housing, rental and utility assistance, and food programs.
- Family Assistance Ministries (FAM): housing support for different client needs, plus food, shelter, and personalized coaching.
- The Kennedy Commission: advocacy, research, and education on affordable housing policy in Orange County.
- Dayle McIntosh Center for the Disabled (DMC): connects people with disabilities and older adults to services, resources, and programs.

## Homeless Services and Housing Navigation

The City Manager's Office leads on homelessness-related issues. On July 1, 2024, the City moved its homeless outreach to an in-house Human Services model. Contacts are Management Analyst, Human Services, Stacy Lumley, slumley@cityoflagunaniguel.org, and Assistant City Manager Justin Martin, jjmartin@cityoflagunaniguel.org.

Behavioral Health Liaisons (BHLs), formerly Homeless Liaison Officers, are Orange County Sheriff's Department Deputies with additional training on mental illness and on connecting people with services. To report non-emergency homelessness issues, call Laguna Niguel Police Services Non-Emergency at (949) 770-6011. Call 911 in an emergency. Email: HLO@cityoflagunaniguel.org.
