## Construction and Demolition Waste Reduction and Recycling

The City requires contractors and other construction personnel to submit and obtain approval of the Waste Reduction and Recycling Plan (WRRP). The plan must show they will recycle, reuse, and/or salvage for reuse a minimum of 65% of their nonhazardous construction and demolition waste. Applicable projects are all newly constructed building and demolition projects which require a permit, all permitted non-residential additions and alterations, all permitted residential additions and alterations that increase the structure's conditioned area, volume, or size, and all demolition only projects.

Submit a completed Construction and Demolition Waste Reduction and Recycling Packet and a refundable project-specific deposit to the Building Division. Deposits: $250 - re-roof of non-residential structures; $500 - qualifying non-residential alterations; $750 - qualifying addition to existing residential or non-residential buildings; $1,000 - all newly constructed building and demolition projects which require a permit.

Compliance options are the City's Franchise Waste Hauler, CR&R, self-haul to a City approved recycling facility, or the Waste Stream Reduction Alternative (new construction only). Universal waste from non-residential additions of 1,000 ft or greater and/or building alterations with a permit valuation of $200,000 or above require verification of proper disposal (CALGreen Section 301.3). To comply, identify the universal waste materials and where they will be disposed on one of the first three pages of the construction drawings submitted for a building permit. Contact the Building Division at (949) 362-4360. Public Counter Hours: Monday - Friday 8:00 a.m. to 4:00 p.m.

The Sea Country Senior and Community Center is a 25,000 square foot multi-use facility with two ballrooms, two kitchens, two meeting rooms, and a courtyard. ## Senior Transportation — Senior Wheels

Senior Wheels is funded through OCTA Measure M2 and provides curb-to-curb service on demand 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, including some holidays. Trips are available from Laguna Niguel residents' homes to the Sea Country Senior and Community Center and to non-emergency medical appointments anywhere in Laguna Niguel. Multiple medical destinations outside the city are also served: Saddleback Memorial Hospital, Mission Hospital in Mission Viejo, Mission Hospital in Laguna Beach, Kaiser Permanente in Irvine, Hoag Health Center in Irvine, and VA in Long Beach. Select shopping and airport trips outside of the city are offered. Rides to/from any Laguna Niguel address to the Senior Center are FREE. Rides to eligible locations start at $2.50 each way.

To qualify, you must be a Laguna Niguel resident, at least 60 years of age or older, and unable to drive a vehicle for any reason. Get an application online, by calling the front desk at (949) 425-5151 to mail you one, or in person at the front desk of the center (24602 Aliso Creek Rd.). Fill out the Senior Wheels Application and waiver form, and provide proof of residency with a copy of your Driver's License or a utility bill. Submit it by mail to 24602 Aliso Creek Rd. Laguna Niguel, CA 92677, by e-mail to scscc@cityoflagunaniguel.org, or in person at the front desk. If approved, you will be mailed and e-mailed directions for scheduling trips. If denied, you will be mailed and e-mailed an explanation.

Other local services listed are OCTA Fixed Bus Routes, OC Access, Age Well Non-Emergency Transportation, and the Senior Non-Emergency Medical Transportation (SNEMT) Program. For questions, contact the Sea Country Senior and Community Center at (949) 425-5151.
