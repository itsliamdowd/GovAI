## Trash Collection Day Rules and Holidays

Carts should be placed at the curbside no later than 6:30 a.m. on collection day and removed within 12 hours after service. Place carts with handles and wheels facing toward your home, side-by-side approximately one foot apart and at least three feet from any obstruction (e.g. vehicles, fire hydrants, trees). Items placed outside of carts will not be collected. Cart lids should be closed, and carts should not be overloaded.

If a holiday falls on a Saturday or Sunday, trash is collected on your normal day with no delays. If a holiday falls during the work week, collection for the remainder of the week is delayed by one day. Non-collection days: New Year's Day, Memorial Day, Fourth of July, Labor Day, Thanksgiving Day, Christmas Day.

## Green Waste, Christmas Trees, and Bulky Items

Residents are encouraged to place green waste in the residential green carts. Single-family residential customers with occasional excess green waste may schedule a bulky pick-up.

CR&R collects Christmas trees for a two-week period after the holiday season. Palm fronds are not recyclable and must go in the trash cart. Remove all ornaments, tinsel, lights, and stands. Place the tree in the green waste cart or beside your carts, not in plastic bags. Trees six feet or taller must be cut in half.

Items that cannot fit in your automated carts are bulky items. Each residential customer gets two free bulky item pick-ups per calendar year, and additional pick-ups may be arranged at a nominal charge. New residents can contact CR&R to collect all paper, cardboard, and packing materials free of charge. This counts as both free bulky item pickups.

## Household Hazardous Waste and E-Waste

Household Hazardous Waste (flammable, corrosive, reactive, or toxic products) cannot go in your automated carts and must be taken to a certified collection center. ## Construction and Demolition Waste — Waste Reduction and Recycling Plan (WRRP)

Under CalGreen Sections 4.408, 5.408, and 5.713.8, the City requires contractors and other construction personnel to submit and obtain approval of a Waste Reduction and Recycling Plan (WRRP). The plan must show that at least 65% of nonhazardous construction and demolition waste will be recycled, reused, and/or salvaged for reuse. For questions, call the Building Division at (949) 362-4360.

Applicable projects: all newly constructed building and demolition projects which require a permit; all permitted non-residential additions and alterations; residential permitted additions and alterations that increase the structure's conditioned area, volume, or size; and all demolition-only projects. Universal waste (batteries, e-waste, lamps, cathode ray tubes/glass, aerosol cans) from non-residential additions of 1,000 ft or greater and/or building alterations with a permit valuation of $200,000 or above must be identified on one of the first three pages of the construction drawings submitted for the building permit. The drawings must list the materials and where they will be disposed.

Submit to the Building Division a completed Construction and Demolition Waste Reduction and Recycling Packet and a refundable project-specific deposit: $250 - re-roof of non-residential structures; $500 - qualifying non-residential alterations; $750 - qualifying addition to existing residential or non-residential buildings; $1,000 - all newly constructed building and demolition projects which require a permit.

Compliance options: use the City's Franchise Waste Hauler, CR&R; self-haul to a City approved recycling facility; or meet the Waste Stream Reduction Alternative (new construction only). Public Counter Hours: Monday - Friday 8:00 a.m. to 4:00 p.m. Building & Safety: (949) 362-4360.

## C&D Waste — CALGreen Diversion Requirements

Effective January 1, 2011, CALGreen required diversion of at least 50 percent of construction waste on most new construction projects. Effective January 1, 2017, the required diversion rate was raised to a minimum of 65%. The City includes C&D management requirements in all of its construction project contracts. It requires contractors to report on the project's waste management status, including meeting the diversion rate, before final approval. City staff review permit applications to determine whether a project is subject to the 65% requirement.

CR&R Environmental Services is the City's only authorized franchised hauler of C&D waste. For projects with non-residential additions of 1,000 ft2 or greater and/or building alterations with a permit valuation of $200,000 or above, the construction drawings must list all universal waste materials to be diverted from a landfill and the universal waste processing facility where they will be disposed. This list goes on one of the first three pages.

## Streets and Street Sweeping

The City maintains over 140 miles of streets and approximately 37 miles of Master Plan of Arterial Highways. Routine maintenance includes pot hole repair and asphalt patching as needed, crack sealing, and inspection of water runoff onto sidewalks, gutters, roads, and City stormdrains. The City contracts with Sweeping Corporation of America (SCA) for residential and arterial street sweeping.

Residential streets are swept the day after trash collection, every other week unless noted. Example: if trash is collected on Tuesday, the street is swept on a Wednesday (Green zone). Zones: Monday (Blue), Tuesday (Yellow), Wednesday (Green), Thursday (Purple), Friday (Orange). Find your street on the Street Maintenance Schedule GIS layer on the City website. 6-month calendars are posted for each zone.

Parking restrictions every week, 10:00am-2:00pm: No Parking Tuesdays & Wednesdays on Hidden Hills Rd and Niguel Ranch Rd; No Parking Thursdays & Fridays on Aliso Niguel Rd. Restrictions on the 2nd & 4th weeks of the month, 10:00am-2:00pm: No Parking 2nd & 4th Tuesday and Wednesday on Parc Vista, Seaglen, and Tessier; No Parking 2nd & 4th Thursday and Friday on East Nine Dr and La Gracia.

For sweeping complaints and return for service calls, contact Sweeping Corp. of America at (310) 436-6510 or Street Maintenance Superintendent Jeff Metz at (949) 362-4344. Public Works Department: 30111 Crown Valley Parkway, Laguna Niguel, CA 92677; Phone: (949) 362-4337; Fax: (949) 362-4385.
