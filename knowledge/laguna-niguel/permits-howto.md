## Why a Permit Is Needed

Permits protect property values, and the page says homeowner's insurance policies may not pay for damages caused by work done without permits and inspections. Listing associations require owners to disclose home improvements or repairs made and whether permits were obtained. Many financial institutions will not finance a purchase without proof of a final City inspection.

Permits are required by the State, and work done without a permit is subject to penalty fees. Contractors working on jobs valued over $500 must be licensed by the State. ## Building & Safety — Plans, Permits and Online Applications

The Building & Safety Division provides plan checking, permitting, and inspection services for new construction, additions, renovations, alterations, and remodels. ## Residential Solar Permits with SolarAPP+

SolarAPP+ is an automated online permitting platform for licensed contractors and installers to apply for permits for standard residential rooftop solar and storage systems. It can process solar systems up to 38.4 kW. Eligibility: applicant must be a licensed contractor in the State of California with a SolarAPP+ ID; property is zoned residential; system size < 38.4 KW-dc; roof flush mount only (no ground mount or reverse tilt systems); panel upgrade optional; battery storage optional (systems with wall-mounted batteries >400 pounds are ineligible); no deferred submittals.

Ineligible systems and owner builders must follow the Submittal Checklist and submit through the Online Permit Center.

Steps: (1) Register for and log into SolarAPP+. (2) Complete the SolarAPP+ application, provide the required documents and processing fees, receive an approval ID number, and print the SolarAPP+ inspection checklist. (3) Register/log into the Online Permit Center (Customer Self Service portal). (4) Enter the SolarAPP+ approval ID number and pay fees. (5) The permit is issued the same day once the online application is complete and fees are paid, with an email attaching the inspection card and permit. City watermark stamps are not provided on the plans. (6) Schedule an inspection with the Building Division. SolarAPP+ permits include only one inspection. A set of plans and the SolarAPP+ approval document must be available to inspectors. The city may also offer inspection services remotely using any electronic device.

Cost: a $25 administration fee is paid directly to SolarAPP+ during the application process, in addition to the city's regular permitting fees in the Master Fee Schedule. Revisions: changes to the site, equipment, or size must be approved by revising the plans through SolarAPP+. SolarAPP+ allows three free revisions; each additional revision costs $25. Upload the updated inspection checklist via the online portal using the city permit number. Changes not approved through SolarAPP+ can be processed by uploading updated plans to the city permit on the Customer Self Service portal, with regular plan review processing time and fees.

## Sign Permits — Filing Instructions

Sign Permit applications can be accepted in one of two ways: (1) by emailing one (1) PDF of all required submittal documents to Planning@cityoflagunaniguel.org, or (2) by bringing three (3) copies of all required submittal documents to the Planning Counter during regular counter hours, Monday-Friday 8 a.m. to 4 p.m.

## Planning Commission — Meetings and Appeals

The Planning Commission is the decision-making body for new development and conditional use permits and acts as an advisory board to the City Council on planning and development issues. Its actions are final but can be appealed to the City Council, which can uphold the decision, overturn it, modify it, or send it back to the Commission for further study.

The Commission meets on the 2nd and 4th Tuesday of each month at 7:00 p.m. in the City Council Chambers. It has 5 members, each serving a two-year term.

## Accessory Dwelling Units (ADUs) — Building an ADU

ADUs and Junior ADUs (JADUs) are independent living facilities on the same parcel as the primary unit. Single-family residences are permitted to have one ADU and one JADU per lot. Short-term lodging is prohibited; units are not to be rented for less than 30 days. A Laguna Niguel ADU Submittal Checklist details the City's ADU review requirements. Questions: (949) 362-4300 or Planning@cityoflagunaniguel.org.

Property taxes: building an ADU does not trigger a complete reassessment of your home. The county tax assessor does a "blended assessment," estimating the ADU's value from its construction cost and adding it to the current assessed value. The primary home is not reassessed.

Source: https://www.cityoflagunaniguel.org/1684/Building-an-ADU

## Pre-Approved ADU Program

Residents can choose from three pre-approved plan sets in different sizes and architectural styles. Plans are free to download, which removes the need to hire design professionals. Because the plans are pre-approved, only the site plan and the final location of the ADU require review.

## ADU Grants and Loans

Property owners apply for these programs directly with the organization, not with the City. Programs listed:
- OC Housing Finance Trust Affordable ADU Loan Program: up to $100,000 construction loan at 0-3% interest, on the condition that the ADU is rented to a very low-income neighbor for ten years (https://ochft.org/affordable-adu-loan-program).
- CalHFA ADU Grant: up to $40,000 toward pre-development and non-reoccurring closing costs.
- CalHome Program (HCD): state funds for local public agencies and nonprofits, including ADU/JADU-related activities.
- CCEDA ADU Program: access to the $40,000 CalHFA ADU grant (https://cceda.com/programs/adu/).
- HPP Cares: administers CalHFA ADU Grants; the submission process for the CalHFA portal is currently paused.
- One Trust Home Loans: up to $40,000 in grants for pre-development costs for owner-occupied, single-family residence borrowers; requires a new construction loan.

## Photography and Video Media Permit — When Required

A permit is required for all commercial (for-profit or sales-related) photography/video activities on City property. It applies if the activity restricts/prevents public access or takes place in a location not ordinarily open to the public; uses a model, constructed set, lighting rig, or large props requiring setup; advertises a business or promotes/sells a product, print, or service; or the City would incur additional administrative costs to monitor it.

A permit is NOT required for personal use, regular news media activities, or activity conducted entirely on private property with no impact on parking or traffic flow. Personal photography or filming at City Parks, the Niguel Botanical Preserve, trails, or open space areas does not require a permit (for example wedding, engagement, family photos, graduations), provided park rules are followed, no space is reserved or blocked, no professional props/sets, actors, models, or large motorized equipment are used, and it takes place during regular park operating hours. Permits are not issued for County, School District, or other public property not owned by the City; contact those agencies directly.
