## Fraud Alert: City Payment Requests

## City Clerk's Office — Contact and Services

City Clerk's Office: 505 S. Vulcan Ave., Encinitas, CA 92024. Phone (760) 633-2601, fax (760) 633-2627, email cityclerk@encinitasca.gov. The City Clerk is Pete Weichers.

The City Clerk prepares agendas and minutes, posts and publishes legal notices, and administers city elections. The Clerk is the local filing officer for Political Reform Act filings, including conflict of interest forms (Form 700) and campaign contributions and expenditures reports. The Clerk also manages the City's records, responds to public records requests, and updates and maintains the Municipal Code.

## Boards and Commissions — Appointments and Eligibility

The City Clerk oversees the recruitment process for City Council appointed Boards and Commissions. Under the Maddy Act (Government Code Section 54970-549742), the Local Appointments List of Boards, Commission, and Committee members is posted annually by December 31. The current list was posted October 20, 2025.

Board Members, Commissioners, and Committee members serve at the pleasure of the City Council. They must be a resident and registered voter in the City of Encinitas. For geographic specific appointments, they must maintain residency and voter registration in the geographic area designated for the position. For current membership, see the corresponding commission page or contact the City Clerk's Office at (760) 633-2601 or cityclerk@encinitasca.gov.

## Council Districts and Elections

The City of Encinitas has four City Council members elected by district voters, and a mayor who is elected at-large. The City transitioned from at-large to district-based Council elections. The Council adopted Resolution 2017-82 on August 30, 2017, and Ordinance 2017-15 on November 15, 2017. The provisions are in Encinitas Municipal Code sections 2.20.010, 2.20.080, and 2.20.090.

The City received a certified letter on July 20, 2017 claiming a violation of the California Voting Rights Act (Elec. Code §§ 14025-14032). The City passed its resolution within the 45-day window that applies to cities that receive a demand letter.

## Redistricting 2021–2022 — Voting District Map

The Council adopted the current Redistricting Map on March 9, 2022 (Resolution 2022-26). The Voting Districts Overview Map can be used to determine what election district an Encinitas address falls within. Redistricting is done every ten years using U.S. census data so that each district is substantially equal in population. The City Council draws the districts. The legal deadline to adopt the plan was April 17, 2022, and the first election using the new lines was November 8, 2022.

District lines follow these criteria, to the extent practicable:
1. Geographically contiguous districts.
2. Respect for the geographic integrity of local neighborhoods or communities, minimizing their division.
3. Easily identifiable boundaries that follow natural or artificial barriers (rivers, streets, highways, rail lines, etc.).
4. Lines drawn to encourage geographic compactness.
