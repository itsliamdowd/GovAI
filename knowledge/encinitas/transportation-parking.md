## Parking Citations: Finding Your Citation

Your citation number is located on the top right corner of your ticket. If your citation was issued within the last 1 to 2 weeks, you may not be able to view or pay it online until it is entered into the parking citation system, which may take up to 2 weeks. If you cannot locate your citation on the website, misplaced your original citation/courtesy notice, need additional information, or have difficulty accessing the website, contact Code at (760) 943-2299.

A DMV registration hold may be placed on your vehicle if the responsible party failed to make full payment on a parking citation(s). It prevents registering or reregistering the vehicle until all fees are paid in full. DMV holds are removed within 72 hours after payment is remitted for all delinquent parking citations. To find out if you have a DMV hold, contact Code at (760) 943-2299.

If the vehicle described on the ticket is not yours, bring the citation along with a copy of your vehicle registration to City of Encinitas Code Enforcement at 505 S Vulcan Avenue, Encinitas, CA 92024.

## Paying a Parking Citation

Failure to pay within 21 days of the original citation or 14 days of the courtesy notice results in double fees and/or a delinquency hold on your account, which may prevent you from registering or re-registering your vehicle with the California DMV. There are 4 payment options:

- Online: available 24 hours/7 days a week, with a $3.50 surcharge. Have the original parking citation or courtesy notice for reference.
- By mail: include the original citation or courtesy notice with payment. Mail to Code Enforcement Division, ATTN: Parking Citations, 505 S. Vulcan Ave, Encinitas, CA 92024. Do not send cash. Make checks or money orders payable to the "City of Encinitas" and put your citation number on them.
- By phone: City of Encinitas Finance Department, Cashier's Station, (760) 633-2654. Have the original parking citation or courtesy notice for reference.
- In person: City of Encinitas Cashier, 505 S. Vulcan Ave, Encinitas, CA 92024. Include the original citation or courtesy notice with payment.

City of Encinitas business hours: Monday - Thursday 8:00AM-5:00PM; alternate Fridays 8:00AM-4:00PM. Payments can be made via cash, check, money order, credit/debit cards. American Express Cards will NOT be accepted. There is a $15.00 service charge for returned checks.

## Indigent Person Payment Plan for Parking Citations

The City offers a Payment Arrangement Program for Indigent Registered Owner(s)/Lessee(s) who owe unpaid parking citations of amounts $500.00 or less. Under Government Code Section 68632, a person is indigent if monthly income is equal to or less than 125% of the current federal poverty line, or if he or she receives assistance from one of these programs: SSI, CalWorks, Tribal TANF, SNAP/California Food Assistance Program (Food Stamps), County Relief/General Relief (GR) or General Assistance (GA), CAPI, IHSS, or Medi-Cal.

Under Government Code Section 40220, citations may be paid in up to $25.00 monthly increments and must be paid off within 24 months of the Payment Plan approval date. The application must be received within 120 calendar days of citation issuance, or within 10 calendar days of an administrative hearing determination, whichever is later. Upon approval, a non-refundable Payment Plan fee of $5.00 is assessed to the total Payment Plan amount and all late fees and penalties are waived. Waived late fees and penalties may be reinstated if the person falls out of compliance with the Payment Plan.

Proof of indigence is either proof of income (most recent pay stub or another proof of earnings, such as a bank statement) or proof of receipt of benefits (a copy of the most recent Verification of Benefits Form or Award Letter for Social Security). If the owner/lessee has no income or receives public assistance, a copy of their annual earnings from the Social Security Department is required.

To request a plan, contact Indigent Payment Plan Request at www.citationprocessingcenter.com or call 1-800-989-2058. Mail the required documentation with a letter that gives the Registered Owner(s)/Lessee(s) full name, address, contact number, contact email, and the parking citation(s) number. Only requests with complete supporting documentation will be accepted. Mail to: AB503 / AB2544 Payment Plan, City of Encinitas, P.O. Box 10479, Newport Beach, CA 92658-0479.

## Contesting a Parking Citation: First Level Appeal (Initial Review)

Minor parking citations are civil offenses, not criminal infractions. To contest one, submit a completed Parking Citation Initial Review Request, a copy of the citation, and any supporting documentation to the Development Services Department - Code Enforcement. The City's Parking Citation Adjudicator reviews the file.

- If the citation is INVALID, a decision letter is mailed and the citation is dismissed.
- If the citation is VALID, you are notified by mail and have 2 options. Option 1: pay the full penalty within 21 calendar days of the initial review decision date. Failure to pay results in double fees and/or a delinquency hold that may prevent DMV registration. Option 2: pay the full penalty AND request an administrative hearing no later than 21 calendar days following the mailing of the initial review results. Failure to respond within 21 calendar days waives the right to further review.

Appeals must be received within 21 calendar days of receiving the original citation or within 14 calendar days of the courtesy notice date. Later appeals will NOT be reviewed.

Submit the appeal form online, in person, or by mail. In person: City of Encinitas Finance Department - Cashier's Station, 505 S. Vulcan Ave, Encinitas, CA 92024. By mail: City of Encinitas, ATTN: Parking Citations, 505 S. Vulcan Ave, Encinitas, CA 92024. Include a copy of the citation, courtesy notice or citation number with the appeal. A citation issued within the last week may not be viewable online, so submit by mail or in person with a copy of the citation or courtesy notice. If you do not receive an Initial Review Decision letter by mail or email within 2 weeks of submission, call (760) 943-2299.

## Contesting an Initial Review Decision: Second Level Appeal (Administrative Hearing)

If you are not satisfied with the Initial Review, you may request an Administrative Hearing with a neutral hearing examiner. The original penalty amount (including any late fees) MUST be paid before the hearing request can be processed. Requests must be received within 21 calendar days of the date of the Initial Review Decision; later requests will NOT be reviewed. Complete the Parking Citation Administrative Hearing Request and mail it with full payment to City of Encinitas, Attn: Parking Citations, 505 S Vulcan Avenue, Encinitas, CA 92024.

Administrative hearings are offered every third Tuesday of the month between 1:00PM-5:30PM at City Hall. Two types are offered:

- Personal (or) Telephone Conference: 30 minutes with a hearing officer. The City schedules it and mails a notification letter with the date, time and location. Failure to appear (or to call in for a phone hearing) waives your right to review and forfeits the right to continue the appeal process.
- Determination By Mail: a hearing examiner reviews the entire appeal file. If INVALID, a disposition letter is mailed, the citation is dismissed and the penalty is refunded. If VALID, a disposition letter is mailed, and you may request a court hearing with the Appellate Division of the Superior Court (North County Regional Center).

If you do not receive an Administrative Hearing Examiner Disposition letter by mail or email within 2 weeks of the hearing, call (760) 943-2299.

Source: https://www.encinitasca.gov/government/departments/development-services/code-enforcement/parking-citations-appeals/second-level-appeal

## Contesting an Administrative Hearing Decision: Third Level Appeal (Superior Court)

The final step is an appeal to the civil division of the San Diego Superior Court, North County Division, within 30 calendar days of the date on the hearing examiner's disposition letter. The appellant pays a $25.00 filing fee to the court. If the appellant prevails, the court refunds the $25.00 and the City refunds the citation penalty. If the violation is upheld, the appellant forfeits the filing fee and all previously posted funds.

The court hearing reviews the facts presented at the administrative hearing. No new evidence can be submitted. To request a court hearing, complete the Notice of Appeal - Administrative Parking Hearing Form and the Proof of Service Form, and file them and make payment directly to the court.

North County Regional Center, 325 South Melrose, Ste 350, Vista, California 92081, (760) 201-8500. Hours: Mon - Thurs 8:30 am – 3:30 pm; Fri 8:30 am – 12:00 pm (noon).

## Disabled Parking Violations and Placard Misuse

A disabled space violation may be dismissed with a $25.00 administrative fee if the registered placard owner provides the City with: a valid handicap placard, valid DMV placard registration/receipt, a valid State driver's license or State identification card, and the original citation or courtesy notice. Placards and/or placard registrations issued after the date of the original citation are not considered valid, and the violation is upheld for the original bail amount.

All documents must be received within 21 calendar days of the original citation date or within 14 calendar days of the courtesy notice. Later documents and/or fees may result in double fees of the original violation amount, and a lien hold may be placed on the DMV registration, preventing registration or reregistration.

Under CVC 4461 and 4463 it is illegal to lend a placard, use someone else's placard, possess or display an altered, counterfeit, forged or falsified placard, alter/counterfeit/forge/falsify a placard identification card, or provide false information to obtain a placard. Misuse is a misdemeanor punishable by a minimum fine of $250 or higher and/or imprisonment. Under CVC 22511.6 a placard may be cancelled or revoked if unlawfully used or possessed. To report unlawful or fraudulent use, contact the California DMV Investigation office in San Diego at (858) 627-3951 or fax (858) 627-3957.

## Proof of Correction for Registration and Plate Violations

The City allows 3 violations to be corrected within 21 calendar days of the original citation date: CVC 4000(a) (current, valid DMV operating registration), CVC 5200 (front and rear plates on California-registered vehicles), and CVC 5204(a) (valid registration tabs, month & year, on the back license plate). Correctable violation penalties can be reduced to $10.00 per violation by either method:

- Submit the proof of correction and fee in person to the City of Encinitas Code Enforcement Division.
- Obtain a Sworn Officer "Sign Off" on the original citation and submit it with the correction fee.

The CVC requires an authorizing agent to inspect the vehicle and sign off on the ticket. Valid registration, plates and tabs must be displayed properly during sign-off. A photocopy of any of these, including online receipts and registration renewal notices, will not be considered sufficient proof. Do not send copies of online receipts, vehicle registrations or renewal notices; these must be submitted in person.

In person (bring citation/notice and proof of correction): Code Enforcement, ATTN: Parking Citations, 505 S. Vulcan Ave, Encinitas, CA 92024. By mail (ONLY for tickets already signed off by a Sheriff's Deputy): same address. Sign-offs at the Sheriff's Department may carry a separate and/or additional fee from the City's fee.

Proof of correction and the total penalty fee must be received within 21 days of citation issuance. Later submissions may result in double fees and eventual lien hold on DMV registration. Vehicles with 5 or more notices may be impounded or immobilized pursuant to California Vehicle Code Sections 22651(1) and 22651.7.

## Non-Liability for Parking Citations (Sold, Leased or Rented Vehicles)

If you believe you received a citation for which you are not responsible, documentation is required along with your citation appeal. If the vehicle was sold before the citation issue date, a completed and signed copy of the Bill of Sale and/or Certified Release of Liability received from the DMV is required. If the vehicle was leased or rented to someone else under a written agreement on the citation date, a copy of the contract is required.

Mail required documents to: City of Encinitas, Citation Processing Center, Po Box 10479, Newport Beach, CA 92658-0479. Do NOT send original documentation, as it will not be returned.
