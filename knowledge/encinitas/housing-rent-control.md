## SB 9 — Two-Unit Development and Urban Lot Splits

SB 9 requires the City to ministerially approve (no public hearing) up to two residences on an existing single-family residential zoned property if the development meets certain requirements. It also requires ministerial approval of splitting an existing single-family lot into two parcels. An Administrative Coastal Development Permit is required within the Coastal Zone. Only properties in the "Coastal Commission Appeal Zone" are appealable to the Coastal Commission.

To apply, submit a Planning Application with the SB 9 Supplement and any other application supplements required for the project. Email planning@encinitasca.gov with questions and to schedule an in-take appointment. The City's SB 9 Project Dashboard shows all SB 9 applications and their status. SB 9 and the local interim ordinance went into effect January 1, 2022. SB 450 took effect January 1, 2025, and City staff is working on bringing the local ordinance into compliance.

## SB 9 — Eligibility, Covenant and Development Rules

Only properties zoned for single-family residential development (RR, RR-1, RR-2, R-3, R-5, R-8, and RS-11) that meet the location requirements of Government Code Section 65913.4(a)(6)(b)-(k) qualify for an SB 9 urban lot split and/or development of up to 4 residential units. The City's e-zoning map (GIS) shows a property's zoning.

SB 9 projects require a recorded covenant with these provisions: non-residential uses on the site are prohibited; short-term rentals (less than 30 days stay) are prohibited; any parcel created from an urban lot split may not be split again; ongoing compliance with all SB 9 requirements and restrictions; access to the public right-of-way must be maintained; all required parking must be maintained.

Urban lot split rules: each parcel must be at least 1,200 square feet; no more than two parcels may be created; new parcels must be approximately equal in size, with one parcel no smaller than 40% of the original lot's size; existing residence(s) must be located fully on a split parcel. The applicant must provide a signed affidavit stating that they intend to occupy one of the housing units as their principal residence for a minimum of three years.

Two-unit development rules: up to two primary dwelling units and two accessory dwelling units may be proposed on a lot. An existing residential unit that has been occupied by a tenant within the past 3 years may not be demolished or altered. Each new primary dwelling unit may be at least 800 square feet. New units may be built as close as four feet from a side or rear property line.

## SB 35/SB 423 — Streamlined Affordable Housing Approval

SB 35/SB 423 (Government Code Section 65913.4) allows eligible multifamily and mixed-use affordable housing projects to apply for streamlined, ministerial (no public hearing) approval if they meet specific objective standards. These projects are not permitted in certain areas of the Coastal Zone, including parcels zoned for single-family, the Coastal Appeal Jurisdiction, areas vulnerable to sea level rise, within 100 feet of a wetland, estuary, or stream, or on prime agricultural land.

Steps: (1) confirm eligibility using the checklist in the SB 35/SB 423 Supplemental Application; (2) submit a Notice of Intent in the form of the Supplemental Application; (3) tribal consultation — within 30 days the City provides formal notice to each Tribe, each Tribe has 30 days to accept, and the City initiates consultation within 30 days of acceptance; (4) submit the Supplemental Application, City Planning Application, State Density Bonus Application Supplemental (if applicable), and architectural plans; (5) ministerial review; (6) submit a building permit.

Timelines: Planning staff determine completeness and conflicts with objective standards within 60 days for projects of 150 or fewer units, and 90 days for more than 150 units. Action on the application, including ministerial design review, must be completed in 90 days for 150 or fewer units and 180 days for more than 150 units, measured from a complete application submittal. An approval expires after three years, and a building permit must be issued before then. A one-time, one-year extension is available if the applicant shows significant progress. A denial may be appealed by the applicant to the City Council. Applications are submitted to the Land Development Division.

## Accessory Dwelling Units (ADUs and JADUs)

An ADU is an attached or detached residential dwelling unit on the same lot as an existing single-family or multi-family dwelling, providing complete independent living facilities. A junior accessory dwelling unit (JADU) is no more than 500 square feet, contained entirely within an existing or proposed single-family residence, and contains only an efficiency kitchen. A JADU is not permitted within multi-family dwelling units.

Fees for plan review and inspections are waived, except for required State fees, postage fees (if a coastal development permit is required), and San Diego County Recorder fees for recording a covenant. If the site is in the Coastal Zone, a Coastal Development Permit is required for ADUs and JADUs that are not completely contained in the existing primary structure, increase habitable area, convert non-habitable space, or are detached.

Apply through the Customer Self-Service Portal (CSS). Review time is approximately 15 working days. Before applying, consult staff:
- Land Development & Building (Planning): planning@encinitasca.gov, 760-633-2710
- Land Development Engineering: developmenteng@encinitasca.gov, 760-633-2609
- Building: building@encinitasca.gov, 760-633-2730
- Fire Prevention: fireprevention@encinitasca.gov, 760-633-2820
- Housing Division: housing@encinitasca.gov, (760) 633-2723

## Housing Element 2021-2029

On April 7, 2021, City Council adopted the Sixth Cycle Housing Element (2021-2029). State law requires the Housing Element to identify and analyze existing and projected housing needs in order to preserve, improve and develop housing for all economic segments of the community. The page offers a subscription to email notifications for Housing Element updates. For assistance in Spanish, call (760) 943-2150.
