## Code Enforcement — Overview and Contact

Code Enforcement ensures compliance with the City's land use codes and regulations. It mainly handles complaints on municipal code regulations ranging from unpermitted structures, abandoned vehicles and hoarding to illegal signs, graffiti and noise. Office: 505 S. Vulcan Ave., Encinitas, CA 92024. Phone: (760) 633-2685.

Code Enforcement Counter Service Hours: Monday-Thursday 10:00am - 12:00pm.

To report a violation, complete the online complaint form. Staff respond according to the standards and procedures in the Code Enforcement Guide. Code Enforcement Manager Bryant Jemison II can be reached at (760) 633-2688.

## Code Enforcement — Filing a Complaint and What It Covers

To file a complaint, complete a Citizen's Complaint Form and give complete contact information in case staff need to reach you. Code Enforcement responds to: barking dogs; business without permits/licenses; construction without permits; fence height regulations; graffiti removal; illegal dwelling units; inoperable vehicles on private property; junk, trash, and debris in public view; noise violations; parking citation appeals; short-term rentals; signs and banners without permits; signs in the public right-of-way; trash cans left out; unpermitted home occupations in residential zones; and use of gas-powered leaf blowers.

For non-emergency law enforcement issues, contact the Sheriff's dispatch line at (858) 868-3200. FOR EMERGENCY SITUATIONS, CALL 911.

## Code Enforcement — Issues It Does Not Handle (Where to Go Instead)

Code Enforcement does NOT respond to the following:
- Air pollution: contact the San Diego County Air Pollution Control District.
- Civil complaints (neighbor-to-neighbor issues) such as property lines, tree trimming and blocked views: the City has a confidential Mediation Program for these.
- Dogs off leash: contact the San Diego Humane Society.
- Mobile home park disputes: contact the California Department of Housing and Community Development.
- Pests such as rats, mice, mosquitoes: contact San Diego County Vector Control.
- Substandard housing and/or mold: contact the San Diego County Department of Environmental Health.
- Tenant/landlord disputes: contact the National Conflict Resolution Center.

## Graffiti Removal Program

The Anti-Graffiti Ordinance (Encinitas Municipal Code Chapter 9.60) provides for FREE removal of graffiti from private property. Graffiti on public or quasi-public property can also be reported for removal. The Graffiti Hotline is (760) 633-2751.

If graffiti is on YOUR property, call the hotline and complete a Graffiti Removal Authorization Form. The form is required and authorizes staff to enter your property and remove the graffiti. Provide your name, day and evening phone number, the location (address/intersection, nearest cross street), what the graffiti is on, and the color and general description (Vulgar? Offensive? Gang style?). Return the form by one of these methods:
- Mail or deliver to 505 S. Vulcan Avenue, Encinitas, CA 92024, Attn: Code Enforcement/Graffiti Abatement.
- Scan/email to code@encinitasca.gov.
- Fax to (760) 633-2818, Attn: Code Enforcement/Graffiti Abatement.

Removal will be arranged as soon as the completed form is received.

If graffiti is on SOMEONE ELSE'S property, call the hotline at (760) 633-2751 and provide the same information. No authorization form is listed for this case. Removal will be arranged as soon as possible. You can also report graffiti through the MyEncinitas App.

## Mediation Program

Mediation is a confidential meeting between people who have a disagreement and a trained, neutral mediator. The final agreement is not legally binding and cannot be used in court. The program is voluntary. The City has retained National Conflict Resolution Center (NCRC) to provide the services.

General Dispute cases cover issues such as blocked views, property line location, overhanging trees and/or root damage, and fencing or other structures typically not regulated by the City. All costs for general dispute cases are paid by the City.

Land Use/Development cases let citizens and project applicants mediate remaining issues after approval of a private development project, before a formal City Council appeal. All costs for NCRC mediation services for these cases are paid by the project applicant, who enters into an agreement directly with NCRC. If mediation is conducted and an appeal is withdrawn, or if a party ultimately prevails on appeal, the appeal fee will be refunded.

To request mediation, contact Code Enforcement, Development Services Department/Planning Division, 505 S. Vulcan Avenue, Encinitas, CA 92024, (760) 633-2685. Separate request forms exist for General Dispute and Land Use/Development mediation.

## Parking Citations — FAQ (Lookup, Delays, DMV Holds, Corrections)

- Citation number: located on the top right corner of the ticket.
- Citation not found online: citation entry may take up to (2) weeks. If you cannot find it, or need more information, contact Code at (760) 943-2299.
- Double fees: apply if payment is not made within twenty-one (21) calendar days of the citation date or fourteen (14) calendar days of the courtesy notice date. Contact Code at (760) 943-2299 to check for delinquent fees.
- DMV registration hold: may be placed if a parking citation is not paid in full. It prevents registering or reregistering the vehicle until all fees are paid. DMV holds are removed within 72 hours after payment is remitted for all delinquent citations. Contact Code at (760) 943-2299 to see if you have a hold.
- Vehicle on the ticket is not yours: bring the citation and a copy of your vehicle registration to Code Enforcement, 505 S Vulcan Avenue, Encinitas, CA 92024.
- Proof of correction: an authorizing agent must inspect the vehicle and sign off on the ticket, confirming a VALID DMV registration, month/year tabs, and/or front/rear plates. Photocopies, smog check VIRs, online receipts and renewal notices/paid renewal fees are NOT sufficient. Proof must be submitted in person at Code Enforcement, 505 S Vulcan Avenue. Sign-offs at the Sheriff's Department may carry a separate and/or additional fee.
- Appeal already submitted but payment notice received: tickets are on hold pending review, so no payment is needed during review. For status, call (760) 943-2299.
- Pay-to-Park lots (San Elijo Campgrounds or Cardiff State Beach): call the San Elijo Campgrounds office at (760) 753-5091. San Diego County Court Notices to Appear and Notices of Parking Violations issued by the California Department of Parks & Recreation are not handled or reviewed by the City of Encinitas.

## Parking Citations — Indigent Payment Plan

The City offers a Payment Arrangement Program for Indigent Registered Owner(s)/Lessee(s) who owe unpaid parking citations of $500.00 or less. Contact the Citation Processing Center at www.citationprocessingcenter.com or 1-800-989-2058.

Under California Government Code Section 68632, a person is indigent if monthly income is equal to or less than 125% of the current federal poverty line, or if they receive assistance from programs such as SSI, CalWorks, Tribal TANF, Food Stamps/CalFresh, County Relief/General Relief/General Assistance, CAPI, IHSS or Medi-Cal.

Under Government Code Section 40220, citations may be paid in up to $25.00 monthly increments, paid off within 24 months of the approval date. Upon approval, a non-refundable Payment Plan fee of $5.00 is assessed to the total plan amount and all late fees and penalties are waived. Waived late fees and penalties may be reinstated if the person falls out of compliance.

## Parking Citations — Disabled Parking Violations

A disabled space violation may be dismissed with a $25.00 administrative fee if the registered placard owner provides: a Valid Handicap Placard; Valid DMV Placard Registration/Receipt; a Valid State Driver's License or State Identification card; and the Original Citation or Courtesy Notice. Placards and/or registrations issued after the date of the original citation are not considered valid, and the violation will be upheld for the original bail amount.

All documents must be received within (21) calendar days of the citation date or (14) calendar days of the courtesy notice date. Later submissions may result in double fees, and a lien hold may be placed on the DMV registration.

To report placard misuse, abuse or fraud, contact the California DMV Investigation office in San Diego at (858) 627-3951 or fax (858) 627-3957. Misuse or abuse of a disabled placard is a misdemeanor punishable by a minimum fine of $250 or higher and/or imprisonment.

## Parking Citations — First Level Appeal (Initial Review)

Submit a completed Parking Citation Initial Review Request, a copy of the citation and any supporting documentation to Code Enforcement. The City's Parking Citation Adjudicator reviews the case. If the citation is INVALID, a decision letter is mailed and the citation is dismissed. If VALID, the appellant is notified by mail and has two options:
- Option 1: Pay the full penalty within (21) calendar days of the initial review decision. Otherwise double fees and/or a delinquency hold may result.
- Option 2: Pay the full penalty AND request an administrative hearing no later than (21) calendar days after the mailing of the initial review results. Failure to respond within (21) calendar days waives the right to further review.

Appeals MUST be received within (21) calendar days of the original citation or (14) calendar days of the courtesy notice date. Later appeals will NOT be reviewed.

Submit online, in person at City of Encinitas Finance Department - Cashier's Station, 505 S. Vulcan Ave, Encinitas, CA 92024, or by mail to City of Encinitas, ATTN: Parking Citations, 505 S. Vulcan Ave, Encinitas, CA 92024. Include a copy of the citation, courtesy notice or citation number. A citation issued within the last week may not be viewable online; in that case submit by mail or in person. If no Initial Review Decision letter arrives after (2) weeks, call (760) 943-2299.

Source: https://www.encinitasca.gov/government/departments/development-services/code-enforcement/parking-citations-appeals/first-level-appeal

## Parking Citations — Second Level Appeal (Administrative Hearing)

If you disagree with the Initial Review, you may request an Administrative Hearing with a neutral hearing examiner. The original penalty (including any late fees) MUST be paid before the request can be processed. Requests MUST be received within (21) calendar days of the Initial Review Decision date. Later requests will NOT be reviewed.

Complete the Parking Citation Administrative Hearing Request and mail it with full payment to City of Encinitas, Attn: Parking Citations, 505 S Vulcan Avenue, Encinitas, CA 92024. Administrative hearings are offered every third Tuesday of the month, 1:00PM-5:30PM, at City Hall.

Hearing types:
- Personal (or) Telephone Conference: 30 minutes with a hearing officer. The City mails a notification letter with date, time and location. Failure to appear or call in waives the right to review.
- Determination By Mail: the examiner reviews the entire appeal file. If INVALID, the citation is dismissed and the penalty refunded. If VALID, the appellant may request a court hearing with the Appellate Division of the Superior Court (North County Regional Center).

If no Hearing Examiner Disposition letter arrives after (2) weeks of the hearing, call (760) 943-2299.

Source: https://www.encinitasca.gov/government/departments/development-services/code-enforcement/parking-citations-appeals/second-level-appeal

## Parking Citations — Third Level Appeal (Superior Court)

The final appeal goes to the civil division of the San Diego Superior Court, North County Division, within (30) calendar days of the date on the hearing examiner's disposition letter. The appellant pays a $25.00 filing fee to the court. If the appellant prevails, the Court refunds the $25.00 and the City refunds the citation penalty. If the violation is upheld, the appellant forfeits the filing fee and all previously posted funds.

The court hearing reviews the facts presented at the administrative hearing. No new evidence can be submitted. To request a court hearing, complete the Notice of Appeal - Administrative Parking Hearing Form and the Proof of Service Form, then file them and pay the court directly.
