## Solid Waste & Recycling — Provider and Sorting Guide

The City of Coronado has an exclusive franchise agreement with EDCO Disposal Corporation for solid waste disposal and recycling services. EDCO is the only company authorized to haul solid waste in the City of Coronado. Its services include collection, transfer and disposal of all solid waste, recyclables and organic waste at residential and commercial properties. Multifamily complexes of less than five units receive Residential Services. Multifamily complexes of five units or more receive Commercial Services.

What goes where. Trash: plastic bags or film, diapers, rubber hoses, snack bags & candy wrappers, broken glassware & dishes, metal hangers, plastic straws, disposable gloves and masks, pet waste & kitty litter. Recycle: aluminum, paperboard, glass containers, clean cardboard, metal, carton containers, Styrofoam, mixed paper. Organics: food waste, coffee grounds, tea bags, bones, green waste and any food soiled paper.

Recycling guides and posters are on EDCO's Resource Center (coronado.edcodisposal.com/resource-center/). The city has an online form for complaints about Recycling Services or Food Recovery Collection programs at businesses and multifamily properties.

## Household Hazardous Waste (HHW) Collection — Schedule and Location

HHW Collection is generally held the second and fourth Saturday of each month, from 9:00 a.m. to 2:00 p.m. It is held at the Public Services Facility, 101 B Avenue. It is available to Coronado residents only. No business or contractor-generated waste is permitted. Have your driver's license handy for residence check, and wait in your vehicle for a collector to assist you.

The City will NOT hold collection events on Saturday, May 23 (Memorial Day), November 28 (Thanksgiving), or December 26 (Christmas). Collection is closed during rainy, stormy weather. The City announces closures through Social Media, the HHW webpage, and its Environmental Services Email List. Please do not illegally dump hazardous waste outside the gate if it is closed.

## HHW Collection — What to Bring, What Not to Bring, and How to Transport

Medical waste and prescription drugs are not accepted at HHW events. Medical waste can be dropped off at the secure drop-off location at the Coronado Police Station, 700 Orange Avenue, Monday through Friday from 8:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m.

Do not bring Bio-Hazardous (red bag) Waste, Controlled Substances, Explosives, Radioactive Materials, Marine and Road Flares, or Business Waste. To ask about disposing of these items, call the San Diego County Hazardous Materials Division. Also do not bring appliances (washers, driers, dishwashers, refrigerators, AC units) or mattresses. For those, call EDCO's customer service line at (619) 287-7555 for bulky waste pickup (fees may apply).

Transport: leave all products in their original containers and do not consolidate them. Make sure containers are properly sealed and not leaking. Put them in the trunk or back of your vehicle, away from passengers. Do not transport more than 15 gallons of any liquid or 125 lbs. of solids at one time. For items not accepted, visit wastefreesd.org or call 1-800-237-2583.

## Bulky Waste Curbside Collection Weeks and Free Mulch

Bulky Waste Curbside Collection Weeks are May 11-15 and October 12-16, 2026. They are held twice a year, in Spring and Fall, on residents' regularly scheduled residential curbside collection days. Place bulky items on the curb along with your regular carts by 6:00 a.m. No hazardous waste is accepted. Debris from contractors, landscapers or commercial businesses is not accepted.

Bulky items typically include broken appliances, water heaters, furniture, old mattresses (wrap them first), yard waste, and other bulky household items. Yard waste must be bagged or bundled. Tree trimmings should be cut into 3-foot lengths and tied into bundles not greater than 18 inches in diameter.

Free self-load/self-haul mulch is available during the Community Cleanup events at the corner of A Avenue and 2nd Street (behind the Little League field) and at Cays Park, 99 Grand Caribe Isle (parking lot). Bring a shovel. Mulch is replenished each morning by 8 am. Bring a tarp if hauling in an open truck or trailer.

## Document Shredding and E-waste Recycling Events

The City and EDCO sponsor a free document shredding and electronic waste event each year for Coronado residents. The 2026 dates are May 16 and October 17. Events are held on a Saturday from 8:00 a.m. to 12:00 p.m. at the City Hall north parking lot, 1825 Strand Way.

EDCO limits documents to two bankers boxes or containers no greater than 10 inches by 12 inches by 15 inches per household. Accepted electronics include TVs, computer monitors, printers, fax machines, VCRs, DVD players and answering machines. Do not bring household hazardous waste, appliances, or trash.

## Used Motor Oil and Filter Recycling

Coronado residents may bring used oil and filters to the Household Hazardous Waste Collection facility for free recycling. Collection is held the second and fourth Saturday of each month from 9:00 a.m. to 2:00 p.m.

You may also use a Certified Used Oil Collection Center (visit wastefreesd.org or call 1-800-237-2583). Centers will not accept oil contaminated with water, antifreeze, gasoline, etc. Drain the oil filter for at least 24 hours and place it in a non-breakable container with a watertight lid.

## Christmas Tree Recycling

Christmas trees, including flocked trees, can be recycled curbside or at drop-off sites. For curbside, place four-foot sections in your yard waste recycling container(s), or bundle sections with twine (maximum 4 feet long and 18 inches in diameter) and place on the curb between December 26, 2025, and January 19, 2026.

The drop-off program runs December 26, 2025, to January 19, 2026, with no size restrictions. Sites are the Glorietta Bay boat launch parking lot and the Cays Park parking lot (look for the large roll-off dumpsters). Remove all ornaments, garlands, lights, nails, tree bags and tree stands. Remove tinsel completely and put it in the trash.

## Animal Services — Animal Care Facility and Who to Call

The City contracts with PAWS of Coronado, a local nonprofit, for animal services. The Coronado Animal Care Facility is open daily from 9 am to 4 pm and is closed on federal holidays. For after-hour assistance, contact the Coronado Police Department at 619-522-7350. The facility cares for lost, stray, and surrendered animals. It offers adoption, microchipping, licensing, and lost-and-found services. When picking up your pet, bring a leash and proof of ownership.

The Coronado Police Department responds to stray or nuisance animals, sick or injured stray animals, aggressive or biting animals, barking dog complaints, animal cruelty or neglect, animals left in hot vehicles, and lost and found pets. Call 619-522-7350.

For adoption questions, call PAWS at 619-435-8247 or visit pawsofcoronado.org.

## Dog Licensing Requirements

Under Coronado Municipal Code Section 32.08.010, all dogs over four months of age that reside in the City for more than 30 days per calendar year must be licensed. Get a license within 30 days of the dog turning four months old, acquiring a new dog, or moving into the City with a dog over four months old. Licenses are only valid while the dog's rabies vaccination is current. Dogs must wear license tags at all times, with limited exceptions.

You can license online through PAWS of Coronado (pawsofcoronado.org/licensing). You need proof of current rabies vaccination, documentation of spay/neuter status (if applicable), and payment. Licenses can also be purchased at the Animal Care Facility, 1395 First Street. Questions: 619-522-7371.

## Aquatics Center — Programs, Registration and Contacts

The Aquatics Center is at 1845 Strand Way, Coronado, CA 92118. Phone: 619-522-2464. Current hours are on the Hours and Fees section of the page, which also has monthly pool schedules and Holiday Hours and Closures. Lap swim lanes are first come, first serve, and swimmers share lanes by splitting or circle swimming when all lanes are full. A minimum of two lap swim lanes are available during all posted lap swim hours.

Family swim is offered in the recreation pool, and participants must wear provided wristbands. Children under age 6 require an adult within arm's reach in the water and on the pool deck. Children under age 14 may not enter the pool deck without an adult present. Children who are not toilet trained must wear a reusable swim diaper (may be purchased at the front desk for $10).

AquaFit classes are held seven days a week at various times. GentleFit is offered Tuesdays and Fridays. Daily drop-ins are first-come, first-served. City-provided swim lessons follow Red Cross Learn-to-Swim guidelines, and private lessons are available. Register online or by phone at the Community Center front desk, 619-522-7342, before the first day of the session. Junior Lifeguards: 619-522-2629.

To get pool closure, chemical imbalance and equipment notifications by text or email, register on the notifications page or call (619) 522-7342.
