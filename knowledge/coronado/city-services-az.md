## Curbside Trash, Recycling and Organics Collection

Each residence is provided curbside collection of trash, recycling, and organic waste. EDCO provides collection throughout the week, and some holidays cause a one-day delay. To start service, call the EDCO Customer Service call center at 619-287-7555 or visit coronado.edcodisposal.com/residential-waste-services/.

No collection is performed on New Year's Day, Memorial Day, Independence Day, Labor Day, Thanksgiving Day, and Christmas Day. That delays collection by one day for that day and the rest of that week. For example, a Thursday holiday delays only Thursday and Friday customers.

Source: https://www.coronado.ca.us/308/Curbside-Residential-Service

## Residential Carts — Gray, Green and Blue

Gray cart: EDCO provides each single-family customer a gray cart for weekly trash collection. Set containers out before 6:00 a.m. on your service day, on the street with the wheels against the curb or the edge of the roadway and the lid arrows pointing toward the street.

Green cart: it takes food waste, food-soiled paper, yard waste, green waste and other organics. Up to two additional green organics carts are available at no extra cost.

Blue cart: it takes paper, cardboard, cans, bottles, all rigid plastics and Styrofoam. Additional blue carts are available at no extra cost, and cart quantity and size do not affect your monthly rate. Recyclables should be empty, dry and loose (not bagged).

Source: https://www.coronado.ca.us/308/Curbside-Residential-Service

## Organics Requirement (SB 1383) and Kitchen Caddies

SB 1383 requires residents to subscribe to and participate in organics curbside collection service and to sort organic waste into the correct containers. Effective January 1, 2024, jurisdictions are required to issue notices of violations and fines for generators that are not compliant.

EDCO offers an optional free kitchen caddie for collecting organics inside the home. Order one on EDCO's Residential Services organic recycling page, coronado.edcodisposal.com/residential-waste-services/curbside-pickup/organic-recycling/.

Source: https://www.coronado.ca.us/308/Curbside-Residential-Service

## Multi-Family Property Waste Requirements

Multi-family owners and managers must subscribe to collection service adequate in size and frequency for all recycling and organics. They must also provide accessible, correctly labeled recycling and organic waste containers for tenants. They must educate tenants annually and give information to new tenants before or within 14 days of occupation of the premises.

Source: https://www.coronado.ca.us/308/Curbside-Residential-Service

## Bulky Trash Curbside Collection Weeks

Bulky Waste Curbside Collection Weeks are May 11-15 and October 12-16, 2026. They are held twice a year, on residents' regularly scheduled curbside collection days. Place bulky items on the curb with your regular carts by 6:00 a.m. No hazardous waste is accepted.

Typical items include broken appliances, water heaters, furniture, old mattresses (wrap them first) and yard waste. Yard waste must be bagged or bundled. Cut tree trimmings into 3-foot lengths and tie them in bundles no more than 18 inches in diameter. Debris from contractors, landscapers or commercial businesses is not accepted.

## Document Shredding and E-waste Recycling Event

The City and EDCO sponsor a free document shredding and electronic waste event for Coronado residents. The dates are May 16 and October 17, 2026. It runs on a Saturday from 8:00 a.m. to 12:00 p.m. at the City Hall north parking lot, 1825 Strand Way.

## Household Hazardous Waste (HHW) Collection

HHW Collection is generally held the second and fourth Saturday of each month, 9:00 a.m. to 2:00 p.m., at the Public Services Facility, 101 B Avenue. It is for Coronado residents only, and no business or contractor-generated waste is permitted. Have your driver's license handy for the residence check, and wait in your vehicle for a collector to assist you.

There are no events on Saturday, May 23, November 28 or December 26, 2026. Collection is closed during rainy, stormy weather. Do not leave hazardous waste outside a closed gate.

Do not transport more than 15 gallons of any liquid or 125 lbs. of solids at one time. Leave products in their original containers.

Source: https://www.coronado.ca.us/288/Household-Hazardous-Waste-Collection

## Medical Waste and Items Not Accepted at HHW

The HHW events do not accept medical waste or prescription drugs. Drop off medical waste at the secure location at the Coronado Police Station, 700 Orange Avenue, Monday through Friday from 8:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m.

Appliances (washers, driers, dishwashers, refrigerators, AC units) and mattresses are also not accepted. Call EDCO customer service at (619) 287-7555 for bulky waste pickup (fees may apply). For other items, visit wastefreesd.org or call 1-800-237-2583.

Source: https://www.coronado.ca.us/288/Household-Hazardous-Waste-Collection

## Used Motor Oil and Filter Recycling

Coronado residents may bring used oil and filters to the HHW facility for free recycling. Collection is held the second and fourth Saturday of each month from 9:00 a.m. to 2:00 p.m.

Certified Used Oil Collection Centers also take used oil. Visit wastefreesd.org or call 1-800-237-2583 for the nearest one. Drain oil filters for at least 24 hours and place them in a non-breakable container with a watertight lid.

Source: https://www.coronado.ca.us/290/Recycling-Events

## Christmas Tree Recycling

Christmas trees, including flocked trees, can be recycled curbside or at drop-off sites from December 26, 2025, to January 19, 2026. For curbside, place four-foot sections in your yard waste container(s). You can also bundle them with twine (maximum 4 feet long and 18 inches in diameter) and set them on the curb.

Drop-off sites are the Glorietta Bay boat launch parking lot and the Cays Park parking lot, with no size restrictions. Remove all ornaments, lights, tinsel, nails, bags and stands first.

## Resident Rates — Proof of Residency and Age

Resident rates require proof of Coronado residency plus a current valid government-issued picture ID. Accepted proof is a lease or rental agreement (at least 6 months) or property tax or mortgage statements. Allow 1-2 business days for staff to update your account after you upload documents.

Proof of age is required before registration for everyone under 18. Accepted documents are a birth certificate, passport, medical or government-issued ID card with a pre-imprinted birth date, or an immunization record with a typed birth date from a doctor's office. You can give documents to staff at the front desk or upload them in CivicRec.
