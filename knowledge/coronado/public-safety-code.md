## Code Enforcement — How to Report a Violation

Under City Council policy, staff will only respond to written complaints. The Code Enforcement Officer issues Notices of Violations to obtain compliance under the Municipal Code. Use the Request for Investigation Form (PDF) to report a suspected violation.

A complaint must include:
- the specific complaint or nuisance;
- your name, address and telephone number, which are kept confidential;
- the location of the problem, using the house number and street name, or the property location if the address is unknown (for example, "northwest corner of Fifth Street and F Avenue");
- a description of the specific complaint, such as the vehicle and license plate number, the location of weeds, or the type of construction debris.

Ways to file:
- **Online:** fill out the form and email it to the Community Development Department.
- **In person:** fill out the form at Coronado City Hall, at the Community Development Department.
- **By mail:** send it to City of Coronado, Department of Community Development, 1825 Strand Way, Coronado, CA 92118.

A "Request for Review of the Issuance of an Administrative Citation (PDF)" form is also available on this page.

Source: https://www.coronado.ca.us/254/Code-Enforcement

## Short-Term Vacation Rental Violations

The City of Coronado prohibits short term vacation rentals in Residential Zones. Property rentals must be at least 26 consecutive days to comply with the Municipal Code. To report a short term rental, call the Short Term Rental Monitoring Hotline at (619) 500-9625. The hotline is only for reporting vacation rentals of 25 days or less. Do not use it for other violations or other code enforcement complaints.

Source: https://www.coronado.ca.us/254/Code-Enforcement

## Emergency Medical Services and CPR Classes

When the Fire Department responds to an emergency, a team of firefighters and paramedics goes out on the fire engine. An ambulance is deployed to the scene of every emergency call. Every fire truck and every ambulance is staffed with at least one paramedic. There are 19 paramedics in the department. They operate out of the main fire station on Sixth Street and the fire station in the Cays.

For questions about ambulance billing or to obtain a copy of a transport report, contact Wittman Enterprises at 800-906-6552. The Fire Department offers CPR classes. For information on paramedics or safety classes, call 619-522-7374. The Emergency Medical Services office phone is 619-522-7375, at 1001 Sixth Street.

## Community Emergency Response Team (CERT)

The Coronado CERT program trains citizens to prepare for and respond safely to disasters. Its goal is to help serve neighborhoods when emergency services are overwhelmed. Coronado CERT was established in 1997, and more than 300 citizens have been trained. For information, call the Coronado Fire Department at 619-522-7374.
