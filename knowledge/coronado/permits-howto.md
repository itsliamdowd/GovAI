## Business Certificate — Cost, Renewal and Who Must Apply

Coronado has no business license tax. 
Beginning January 1, 2019, the City of Coronado will no longer have a Business Licenses Tax.
 A minimal-cost Business Certificate is used instead. The page lists a $25 per year flat fee (plus a $4 State fee). It also says all new Business Certificates require a fee in addition to the $29 annual fee, listed under "Administrative Permits" as "Business Certificate" in the fee schedule. The certificate and fees must be renewed by January 1st of each year. Certificates are issued annually and expire December 31 of the year issued.

Title 13 applies to all commercial activities in Coronado, including professions, trades and occupations. It applies even where the business's official address is outside Coronado. Organizations managed wholly for charitable purposes, or from which no profit is derived, are exempt from the fee. Non-profit organizations are exempt from the City and state fees. That exemption does not extend to other City regulations or ordinances.

## Building Permit — How to Apply

To apply for a building permit, visit City Hall, Monday through Friday from 8:00 a.m. to 4:30 p.m. City Hall is at 1825 Strand Way, Coronado, CA 92118. For Building Services questions, call 619-522-7331.

Over-the-counter submittals are available by emailing the Building Permit Application to the Building Permits email address for these types of work:
- Residential plumbing: water heater change out (same location), tankless water heater change out (same location), and gas line repair.
- Residential mechanical: wall heater change out.
- Residential electrical: panel upgrade.
- Residential re-roof: non-historic properties, like for like.
- Residential furnace replacement: same location, no A/C, single family only.
- Re-pipe of the water piping system: interior only, no right-of-way sewer permits, residential only.

## Building Inspection Request

The Inspection Request Line is 619-522-7361. When calling in a request, provide:
- Permit number
- Project address
- Type of inspection requested
- Day/date requested
- AM (8 to noon) or PM (1 to 4)
- Contact name and phone number
- Any additional remarks, such as job off of alley or card on fence

Incomplete or incorrect information may delay the inspection.

Inspections requested before 4 pm on a regular business day will be scheduled for the next business day, unless the message says otherwise. Inspections left on weekends or City-observed holidays are scheduled for the second business day.

## Solar Photovoltaic (PV) Permits

Expedited "over the counter" permitting is available for roof-mounted residential solar PV projects of 10kW or less in one- and two-family dwellings. Larger systems, or those with storage batteries, must go through standard processing. Planning review is required for Solar PV installations of this size.

Submit the Building Permit Application Form, the Eligibility Checklist for Expedited Permitting, and a complete set of plans. Applications must be submitted in person to the Building Division at 1825 Strand Way, Coronado CA 92118. Contractors may also apply for Solar Permits via SolarAPP+ and the City's online permitting system.

After installation, the system must be inspected before final approval. Schedule inspections with Building Services at 619-522-7361. Requests received before 4 pm are typically scheduled for the next business day. For other questions, call 619-522-7331.

## Accessory Dwelling Units (ADUs) and Junior ADUs

An ADU is a residential unit that can be added to a lot with an existing single-family or multi-family dwelling, and it provides complete independent living facilities. There are three types: detached, attached, and converted existing space. A junior ADU (JADU) is limited to 500 square-feet in size and must be contained within the single-family residence. Development standards are in CMC 86.56.105.

Carriage house ADUs are also allowed. Carriage houses are two story detached accessory structures with an enclosed garage on the first floor and a maximum of 400 sf of habitable space on the second floor. Carriage house ADUs are subject to the same development standards (CMC 86.56.110). They function as complete independent living facilities on the second floor, with a restroom and kitchen facilities.

## Encroachment Permits (Private Improvements in the Public Right-of-Way)

An Encroachment Permit is required to install private permanent/fixed improvements within (or encroaching onto) the public right-of-way. Examples include walls, fences, synthetic turf, and outdoor dining facilities (Coronado Municipal Code 52.08). The City Council may need to approve an encroachment if the City Engineer finds it of sufficient public interest.

To apply:
1. Submit a completed Encroachment Permit Application with a computer-generated diagram showing dimensions and square footage. Synthetic turf applications are exempt from the diagram but must give a detailed description of the location(s) and the square footage.
2. Pay the Encroachment Permit fee, which is in the permit fee schedule.

All Encroachment Permits are recorded at the County Recorder's Office, and applicants pay the recording fees. Once the City Engineer approves the permit, the applicant or their contractor must obtain a Right-of-Way Permit. A Right-of-Way Permit authorizes a contractor to temporarily occupy the public right-of-way for construction. Questions: Engineering Division, 619-522-7383.

Source: https://www.coronado.ca.us/317/Encroachment-Permits

## Special Event Permits — Categories, Fees and Deadlines

A special event is any assembly, procession, march, or other activity using public property as an activity site, including a beach, park, public street, sidewalk, or alley. Events follow Section 20.30 of the City Code. Permits are subject to date availability and community impact. They are not issued for more than one event per weekend, on holidays (except July Fourth and Veterans' Day), or between Memorial Day and Labor Day, with limited exceptions. The City issues no more than 14 major and 15 moderate special events per calendar year.

- Major: letter of intent by August 1 of the prior calendar year; permit application 60 days prior; fee $370; approved by City Council.
- Moderate: letter of intent 6 months prior, preferably by the August 1 deadline; permit application 30 days prior; fee $185; approved by City Council.
- Minor: no letter of intent; permit application 30 days prior; fee $30; approved by City Manager.

Reserving a City facility or hosting a private event in a park or on the beach requires a use permit from Recreation Services.

Source: https://www.coronado.ca.us/228/Special-Events

## Block Party Permits

A minor event permit is required for neighborhood block parties. The City Manager's Office processes applications, which must be submitted no later than thirty (30) days prior to the event. Permits are issued only between 10 a.m. and 10 p.m., including set-up and tear down. The applicant must obtain comprehensive general liability and property damage insurance. Liquor liability coverage is recommended if alcohol will be consumed on private property.

The applicant must notify all impacted residents. The applicant must also submit a site map showing the street(s) closed, barricade placement, tables, chairs and other set-up items. A 20-foot emergency lane and a minimum vertical clearance of 13 feet 6 inches must be maintained throughout the block. Barricades are picked up at the Public Services Department, 101 B Ave., Coronado, CA 92118. Jumpers, slides and moon bounces are not allowed on residential streets or sidewalks. Outdoor music must comply with CMC Section 41.10.010 Property Line Noise Limits.

Source: https://www.coronado.ca.us/228/Special-Events

## Film and Photography Permits — Requirements and Deadlines

Film Permits are required for all motion picture, video, and still photography productions. Applications are due five (5) business days before filming for a basic permit. They are due 10 days before filming for a permit requiring road closures or traffic control. Requests made less than five (5) days prior may be considered, subject to availability of City facilities and staff. Applications must be canceled 24 hours in advance or the applicant will be charged for personnel costs.

Filming follows Section 20.04 of the City Code. Railings, scaffolding, generators, or any additional equipment are not permitted. Alcohol is not allowed on public property in connection with the session. The permittee must clean up trash or debris, may not block pedestrian or vehicular traffic, and must give immediate access to emergency vehicles/personnel. Email the completed application to the City.

Source: https://www.coronado.ca.us/753/Film-and-Photography-Permits
