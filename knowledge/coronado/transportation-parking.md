## Residential Parking Permits

If you live in an area where residential parking decals are required, visit the Police Department Monday through Friday, except holidays, from 8 am to 5 pm to obtain your free parking permit. To get a decal you need proof of residency showing you are a Coronado resident, such as a lease, utility or mortgage bill. You also need the vehicle registration for all vehicles needing permits and a contact phone number. You need the vehicle license plate (and State), make, model, year and color.

Residents may obtain up to three visitor placards in addition to their vehicle decals. The Police Department is at 700 Orange Ave, Coronado, CA 92118. Phone: 619-522-7350.

## Oversized Vehicle Permits

Oversized vehicle permits are available at the Police Department Monday through Friday, except holidays, from 8 am to 5 pm. You will need identification showing you are a Coronado resident and a contact phone number. You will also need the oversized vehicle license plate (and State), make, model, and length of vehicle. If you have a combination of vehicles, bring the attached trailer license plate. You must also say which 48-hour period you want the permit issued for.

Permits are good for 48 hours only. They require that the oversized vehicle is being loaded in preparation for a trip or unloaded after a trip. They cannot be used for commercial purposes. There currently is no fee for an Oversized Vehicle permit. The oversized vehicle must be parked at the residence where the permit was issued.

Source: https://www.coronado.ca.us/305/Parking

## Oversized Vehicle Guest Permit

Residents may obtain up to three Oversized Vehicle Guest Permits per calendar year. The Coronado resident must come to the front counter of the Police Department Monday through Friday, except holidays, between 8 am and 5 pm. Residents must have the same information as for an oversized vehicle permit. They also need the vehicle information about their guest's oversize vehicle that will be parking in front of the residence for more than 3 hours. The Oversized Vehicle Guest Permit shall not exceed 48 hours.

Source: https://www.coronado.ca.us/305/Parking

## Detached Non-Motorized Vehicles (Trailers)

Detached non-motorized vehicles (trailers) are restricted from parking or stopping on public streets for more than three hours. There is no size requirement for this portion of the ordinance, and any detached non-motorized vehicle applies. A Coronado resident may obtain a Detached Non-motorized vehicle permit. It allows the vehicle to be detached and parked in front of the resident's house for up to 48 hours. The permit requires that the vehicle be prepared for loading or unloading for non-commercial purposes. There are no guest non-motorized vehicle permits allowed.

None of the permits mentioned allow for sleeping or lodging in the oversized vehicle or detached non-motorized vehicle while parked on the public street.

Source: https://www.coronado.ca.us/305/Parking

## Free Summer Shuttle (MTS Route 904)

The Free Summer Shuttle provides free rides for residents and visitors. Three San Diego Metropolitan Transit System (MTS) buses run along the existing Route 904 around the downtown Village area. The shuttle is operated by MTS and funded by the City of Coronado. In 2026 it runs June 7 - September 7, seven days a week, 9:00 AM to 8:00 PM in 20-minute intervals.

The route runs from Avenida de Las Arenas through downtown via Orange Avenue and loops around at the Coronado Marriott Resort & Spa. The buses run from the Shores and City Hall to downtown and to the Ferry Landing and Coronado Island Marriott Resort & Spa. Extra service will be provided June 19-21, the weekend of the NASCAR events, and July 3-5 for 4th of July festivities.

## Free Commuter Ferry

The Free Commuter Ferry is city-subsidized ferry service for pedestrians and bicyclists between the Coronado Ferry Landing and the Broadway Pier in San Diego. It operates Monday through Friday during morning commute hours, and morning commuters receive a voucher for a free same-day return trip. Broadway Pier departures are 4:50, 5:30, 6:10, 6:50, 7:30, 8:10 (AM). Coronado departures are 5:10, 5:50, 6:30, 7:10, 7:50, 8:30 (AM). More information is at https://www.flagshipsd.com/commuter-ferry.

## Active Transportation, Safe Routes to School and Bicycle Friendly Status

The City has provided Safe Routes to School education to teach students safety for walking and bicycling to school or for other trips. Currently, about 50% of Coronado students walk or bike to school. The City has adopted an Active Transportation Master Plan covering walking, cycling, and other non-motorized vehicle transportation. Bicycle lane improvements have been made at key bicycle corridors (i.e. Glorietta Boulevard).

In 2023, the League of American Bicyclists recognized the City of Coronado as a Bicycle Friendly City and Bicycle Friendly Business, at the Silver Level. This award cycle runs through 2027.

## Electric Bikes — Definition and Classes

An e-bike is an electric bicycle equipped with fully operable pedals and an electric motor of less than 750 watts. E-bikes are regulated like bicycles, so the same rules of the road apply. They are not subject to the registration, licensing or insurance requirements that apply to motor vehicles.

- Class 1: low speed pedal-assisted. Power maxes out at 20mph. Allowed wherever regular bicycles are allowed.
- Class 2: pedal or throttle assisted. Power maxes out at 20mph. Allowed wherever regular bicycles are allowed.
- Class 3: pedal or throttle assisted. Maxes out at 28mph. Any e-bike that assists the rider over 28mph is not street legal and must stay off of roads, sidewalks and multi-use trails and paths.

Electric bicycles with motors of more than 750 watts, and bicycles with gas motors are technically motorcycles according to the law. They require Class M licenses, registration and insurance.

Source: https://www.coronado.ca.us/835/E-Bike-Safety-Laws

## Electric Bikes — Coronado Rider Requirements and Passengers

Children under 12 years old are prohibited from operating e-bikes in Coronado. For Type 1 and Type 2 e-bikes, no driver's license is required and a helmet is required for 17 and under. For Type 3, the minimum age is 16, no driver's license is required, and a helmet is required for all ages.

Most e-bikes are not equipped to carry passengers. E-bikes can only transport passengers if there is a permanent seat or when using a child safety seat. Passengers are required to wear a helmet if they are under 18. Type 3 e-bike riders must not transport passengers. For e-bike questions, the page lists the Police Department at 619-522-7350.
