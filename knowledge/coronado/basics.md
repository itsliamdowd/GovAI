## City Council Meetings — Attending and Speaking in Person

Regular City Council meetings are held on the first and third Tuesday of each month at 4:00 p.m. in the Council Chamber at City Hall, 1825 Strand Way, Coronado, CA 92118. Meetings are open to the public, and no advance sign-up is required to attend.

Members of the public may address the Council on agenda and non-agenda items. To comment in person, complete a speaker slip and submit it to the City Clerk before the item you wish to address is called. Speaker slips are available in the entrance to the Council Chamber. Speakers are generally allotted up to three (3) minutes, though the Mayor may adjust speaking times. Time limits set by the Mayor apply equally to in-person and virtual speakers.

## City Council Meetings — Written Comments and Presenting Materials

Written comments may be submitted to the City Clerk's Office by email before or during a meeting. Written comments become part of the official public record and are made available online. Comments received sufficiently in advance of a meeting will be provided to the City Council before the meeting.

To present materials to the Council, email them to the City Clerk's Office no later than 24 hours before the meeting. Accepted materials include PowerPoint presentations, images, PDFs, and links to websites. Video and audio files are not accepted. Materials received after the deadline will not be accepted.

## City Council Meetings — Language Access and Accessibility

Closed captioning is available on Coronado TV, the eScribe live stream, and Zoom. To request language interpretation services or other accessibility accommodations, contact the City Clerk's Office at 619-522-7320 at least 72 hours before the meeting.

A Public Agenda Translation Posting Board is located outside City Hall at 1825 Strand Way. Members of the public may post translated versions of City Council agendas on it after the official agenda has been posted. The board is accessible 24 hours a day, 7 days a week. The city does not review, verify, endorse, or certify translated agendas posted by the public. See Council Policy #31 for more information.

If remote access is disrupted during a meeting, the meeting may be recessed for at least one hour while the City makes a good-faith effort to restore service, as provided in City Council Policy #23.

## Agendas and Minutes — Posting and Where to Find Them

Agendas are posted at least 72 hours before a Regular City Council meeting and at least 24 hours before a Special City Council meeting. They are posted at City Hall, the Coronado Public Library, and on the Agendas and Minutes page. Minutes are presented to the Council for approval at the next regular meeting and are posted after they are approved.

City Council agendas, minutes, and packets from January 2020 to present are available through eScribe (pub-coronado.escribemeetings.com). Agendas, minutes, and packets for boards, commissions, and committees are available from January 2023 to present. For records outside these ranges, contact the City Clerk's Office.

## City Clerk's Office — Role and Contact

The City Clerk is the official custodian of Coronado's governmental record, serving as Election Official, Legislative Administrator, and Records Manager. The office administers local elections and processes candidate filings. It also records and preserves City Council proceedings, maintains the City's Records Management Program, coordinates official ceremonies and functions, and supports boards, committees and commissions. The City Clerk is Kelsea Holian, MMC, CPM.

The office is at 1825 Strand Way, Coronado, CA 92118. Phone: 619-522-7320. The office handles public records requests and provides forms for filing an appeal (Appeal Hearing Form and How to File an Appeal). It also posts the Appointments List of Boards, Commissions, and Committees and the 2026 Annual Vacancy List (Maddy Act).

## City Manager's Office — Role and Contact

The City Manager is the administrative head of the municipal government under the direction and control of the City Council. ## Elections — Running for City Council

A packet of candidate information, with nomination petitions and other required forms, is provided to each potential candidate at the City Clerk's Office beginning Monday, July 13, at 7:30 a.m. Appointments are required. Call 619-522-7320 to make one.

To be eligible, a candidate must be 18 years or older, be a resident of Coronado, and be registered to vote in Coronado when Nomination Papers are issued. The nomination period begins 113 days before the election and ends 88 days before election day. To qualify, a candidate needs signatures of at least 20, but no more than 30, registered voters in Coronado. A $25 filing fee is due when the nomination packet is returned to the City Clerk.

Candidates may include a Candidate Statement of Qualifications in the voter information materials produced by the San Diego County Registrar of Voters. The candidate pays for it, and the cost is provided with the nomination packet.

Source: https://www.coronado.ca.us/234/Election-Voter-Information

## Elections — Campaign Contribution Limit and Disclosures

On September 5, 2017, the City Council adopted Ordinance 2066. It reduced the contribution limit for any candidate for elective city office, or a controlled committee, to $200 in any single election. Coronado's city-specific Campaign Finance Reform regulations are in Chapter 1.84 of the Coronado Municipal Code.

Campaign disclosure statements (including Form 460 and related FPPC forms) filed with the City Clerk's Office can be reviewed through the City's NetFile public access portal (public.netfile.com/pub2/?AID=cor). Filed Statements of Economic Interests (Form 700) can be reviewed through the FPPC search portal (form700search.fppc.ca.gov).
