## Affordable Housing — Eligibility and Waiting Lists

The City of Coronado's Affordable Housing Special Revenue Fund is in place to fund new construction, rent subsidies, and the rehabilitation of affordable housing units in Coronado. Affordable housing is only available to those who meet certain income requirements. These requirements are set by the U.S. Department of Housing and Urban Development, per the San Diego County median income. Tenants are required to submit an annual income certification to verify eligibility. The requirements are set by the State and Federal Governments and are the same throughout San Diego County.

Each affordable property is managed individually. The City does not maintain waiting lists and does not directly certify tenants for program eligibility. To find out about vacancies or to be added to a property's waiting list, contact the property managers listed on the List of Affordable Units and Info Flyer. To have a paper copy of the flyer mailed to you, contact Coronado Affordable Housing through the Contact Us section of the page.

Source: https://www.coronado.ca.us/178/Affordable-Housing

## Income-Restricted Rental Units — List of Affordable Units

Several Coronado properties have income-restricted affordable rental units, including some units for seniors only. The City has agreements with these properties that restrict rents to rates affordable to households in the very-low, low, and moderate-income categories. The City of Coronado does not process applications, maintain waiting lists, or directly certify tenants for program eligibility for these properties. Property owners or their property managers manage each property individually.

The "List of Affordable Units and Info Flyer (2026)" is available as a PDF at https://www.coronado.ca.us/DocumentCenter/View/9303. Mailing address for Coronado Affordable Housing: 1825 Strand Way, Coronado, CA 92118. Coronado City Hall phone: 619-522-7300.

## Affordable Housing Development Incentives

The City offers programs, incentives, and technical assistance to encourage affordable housing developments. Affordable housing projects need 1 space per unit for parking, and market-rate projects need 2 spaces per unit. The Inclusionary Housing Fee is no fee for affordable projects that provide 20% affordable units. Market-rate projects pay $25 per square-foot. Affordable projects get expedited/priority processing, and market-rate projects get standard processing.

For more information, contact Coronado Affordable Housing, c/o Community Development Department, 1825 Strand Way, Coronado, CA 92118. Phone: 619-522-7328.

Source: https://www.coronado.ca.us/1141/Affordable-Housing-Development-Incentive

## Density Bonus Program

The California State Density Bonus Law (CA Govt. Code Section 65915) offers development incentives to projects that provide on-site affordable housing. A project receives an increase in residential density. It also receives incentives or concessions to offset the cost of providing affordable housing on-site. The City must waive any local development standard that would preclude construction of the project with the added density or the incentives or concessions. The size of the density bonus and the number of incentives or concessions depend on the amount and level of affordability of the on-site affordable units.

The City's local Density Bonus Ordinance (CMC 86.53) is available for multifamily projects whose zoning allows 5 or more units. Incentives and concessions a developer may request include reduced site development standards (e.g. setbacks or parking spaces). They also include mixed-use development in conjunction with the housing project if commercial, office, industrial, or other land uses will reduce housing costs. Any such waivers or reductions must meet the minimum building standards in the California Building Standards, and they must decrease development costs. Phone: 619-522-7328.

Source: https://www.coronado.ca.us/1146/Density-Bonus-Program

## SB 4 — Affordable Housing on Faith and Educational Land

A state law ("SB 4") that took effect in 2024 allows qualifying housing projects on sites owned by "religious institutions" and "independent institutions of higher education" through a streamlined ministerial review process. The City has identified 12 qualifying properties in Coronado that are eligible for affordable housing development under SB 4.

Qualifying projects get these incentives:
- Waived permit fees
- No special fees or inclusionary housing fees
- Exempt from CEQA
- Ministerial approval process
- Expedited permit timelines
- Increased unit density
- Reduced parking requirements

Source: https://www.coronado.ca.us/1147/SB-4---Affordable-Housing-on-Faith-and-E

## Affordable ADUs

Accessory dwelling units (ADUs) can be deed-restricted to specific income groups and qualify for affordable housing incentives. Affordable ADUs that are deed-restricted to low, very-low, and extremely low-income groups are exempt from planning fees and parking requirements and are eligible for expedited processing.

Source: https://www.coronado.ca.us/1145/Affordable-ADUs

## Pre-Approved ADU Program

The City developed a Pre-Approved ADU Program under AB 1332. It lets residential property owners choose from a selection of pre-approved ADU building plans. The program is anticipated to become available for use by Fall 2026. To use pre-approved plans, the lot must be residentially zoned and contain an existing or proposed residential unit. The plans come in three architectural styles with variable floor plan layouts.

Applicants must provide a site-specific plan showing the location of the pre-approved ADU relative to property lines and other structures. Any modification to the plans requires the applicant to work with an outside plan preparer and obtain updated plans and other required materials for submittal to the City. A modified plan must go through the standard plan check process.

Source: https://www.coronado.ca.us/1144/Pre-Approved-ADU-Program

## Housing Element Update (2021–2029)

For the 2021-2029 planning period, SANDAG allocated 912 planned new housing units to Coronado under the Regional Housing Needs Allocation. On April 9, 2024 the City Council voted to adopt Coronado's Housing Element Update. On August 29, 2024, the California Department of Housing and Community Development (HCD) certified it. On December 12, 2024, the California Coastal Commission approved the City's amendments to the Coronado Municipal Code. On May 6th, 2025 the City Council adopted the amendments, completing the update process for the 2021-2029 cycle.
