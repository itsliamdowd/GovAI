## Building Permits — What They Are and When Required

A building permit grants legal permission to construct or alter a building or structure. It also triggers physical inspection and approval during various phases of construction to assure compliance with the approved plans (where applicable) and all applicable local, state, and federal rules, regulations, and standards.

Most building construction projects or improvements to buildings require a building permit of some kind, with some exceptions. A permit is required for all construction-related activities involving the construction or erection of new buildings and structures, or improvements, alterations, or additions to existing buildings, as regulated by the building codes and ordinances of the City of National City and the State of California or as determined by the Building Official.

Source: https://www.nationalcityca.gov/community/residents/i-want-to/inquire-about-a-building-permit

## Building Permits — Types of Permits

- A Building Permit is required to construct or modify a building or structure, including, but not limited to, the construction of pools, signs, or towers.
- An Electrical Permit is required to install or modify an electrical system.
- A Plumbing Permit is required to install or modify a plumbing system.
- A Mechanical Permit is required to install or modify heating, ventilation, refrigeration, air conditioning and other related systems.
- A Combination Building Permit is required where the construction involves multiple trades. It covers all the building, electrical, plumbing, and mechanical work necessary for the job, and is issued for both residential and commercial projects.
- A Demolition Permit is required to demolish any building, structure, or improvement to such.

## Building Permits — Who May Pull a Permit and Permit Expiration

Building permits are available to properly licensed contractors working on behalf of and as authorized by the tenant or property owner. They are also available to owners of one- and two-family dwellings, provided the work is done by the owner, a member of their immediate family, or individuals hired by them, and the owner resides or intends to reside in the dwelling.

A building permit becomes null and void if the authorized work is not commenced within one year of the issuance date, or if the work is suspended or abandoned at any time after it is commenced for a period of one year. A permit may be extended for an additional period of up to 180 days, or as determined by the Building Official, if the applicant submits a written request and the reasons for it to the Building Division before the original permit expires. Extensions are also regulated by Section 15.24.045 of the National City Municipal Code. The page links an Info Guide (PDF) on how to obtain a Building Permit.

## Schools & Colleges

National School District: 10 public schools offering grades K-6. One location offers preschool. It also offers extended programming and summer camps through its WINGS and Camp WINGS programs.

Sweetwater Union High School District: four campuses in National City, including Sweetwater High, offering instruction primarily in grades 7-12. National City Middle and Granger Junior High offer secondary instruction. National City Adult offers high school equivalency and continuing education.

Southwestern Community College District: one half of the Education Village in Downtown National City is Southwestern College's Higher Education Center. Course offerings include a dental hygienist program.

South County Regional Education Center (San Diego County Office of Education): located in National City and accessible to educators in South Bay school districts. It exists to advance student and teacher training using advanced presentation and communication technologies.

## Library Card Requirements

A library card is issued upon presentation of:

- Current picture identification: Valid California Driver's License, California ID Card, Resident Alien Card, or Military ID.
- Address verification (must include applicant's name): mail postmarked within 30 days, rent receipt, utility bill, check book, or car registration.

Parents or guardians must be present to sign the application and show their ID and proof of address for teens and children under 18 years of age.

Source: https://www.nationalcityca.gov/government/library/about/faqs

## Library — Tax Forms, Wi-Fi, and Reference Help

Tax forms: The library has standard tax forms from January – April 15th. After April 15th, forms can be downloaded at www.irs.gov. California tax forms are available at taxes.ca.gov/Income_Tax/Forms.shtml. The library does not have Renter Assistance Forms; a copy can be downloaded at http://www.ftb.ca.gov/individuals/hra/forms.shtml.

Wireless access: The library's Wi-Fi network is NCPLWireless. No password is required, but there is a waiver you must agree to.

Reference librarians cannot give legal or medical advice. They can direct you to websites or books on legal topics or health concerns. The San Diego County Law Library has legal materials at four locations countywide (hours and locations at http://www.sdcll.org/location.htm). More in-depth medical information can be obtained at UCSD Biomedical & Medical Center.
