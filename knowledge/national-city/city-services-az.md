## Mandatory Commercial Recycling (AB 341)

A business (includes public entities) that generates four cubic yards or more of commercial solid waste per week, or is a multifamily residential dwelling of five units or more, shall arrange for recycling services as set forth by the statewide mandatory commercial recycling program. Commercial recycling programs available through EDCO include Mixed Paper, Beverage Containers, Metals, Old Corrugated Cardboard and Green Waste. For more information, contact EDCO or visit CalRecycle.

## Commercial and Multi-Family Organics Recycling (AB 1826)

California Assembly Bill 1826 requires all businesses to recycle their organic waste beginning April 1, 2016. Organic Waste = food waste, green waste, landscape and pruning waste, non-hazardous wood waste. For implementation dates and thresholds, visit CalRecycle or contact EDCO for collection services.

## Residential Organics (Green Cart) Program

EDCO's Organics Recycling Program collects food scraps and yard waste mixed together in the green cart. Residential collection was scheduled to start on April 1st (2021) and aligns with billing cycles. The service fee for organics, previously approved by council, is 2.48 per mos. starting April 1, 2021. National City residents are provided with up to three commingled green carts. The material is taken to EDCO's Anaerobic Digestion Facility in Escondido, CA to be converted into renewable natural gas and anaerobic compost.

To subscribe to the collection program, visit https://national-city.edcodisposal.com/.

## Organic Waste Reduction (SB 1383) and Edible Food Recovery

SB 1383 requires each resident and business to subscribe to an organic waste collection service that either "source-separates" the waste (e.g. separate bins), or transports all unsegregated waste to a facility that recovers 75 percent of the organic content collected. Those who must comply: residents, commercial businesses, commercial food generators, schools, and government entities.

Per National City Municipal Code Section 9.52.060, edible food generators are required to donate the maximum amount of edible food to food recovery organizations, establish contracts or written agreements with food recovery organizations and services, and track the amount of edible food donated by weight and maintain up-to-date records. Tier 1 generators are required to donate starting in 2022. Tier 2 generators are required to donate starting in 2024. Food recovery organizations must complete the County of San Diego's Food Recovery Capacity Survey within 60 days of receiving it.

Source: https://www.nationalcityca.gov/government/engineering-public-works/public-works-division/organics-recycling

## Sewer Blockages and Wastewater Maintenance

The wastewater division maintains approximately 105 miles of sanitary sewer main (mostly 6 and 8-inch lines) and 2 pump stations. It also maintains 19 miles of storm drain infrastructure. The entire main line system is normally cleaned on average once each year, and storm drain catch basins are cleaned one to two times per year.

For main line blockages during normal business hours, Monday through Thursday, 7:00 a.m. to 6:00 p.m., call the Engineering & Public Works Department at (619) 336-4580. Outside normal business hours, contact the National City Police Department at (619) 336-4411. Email comments on the Sewer System Management Plan to publicworks@nationalcityca.gov.

## Street Sweeping Schedule

Most residential streets are swept once a week between the hours of 7:00 am and 4:00 pm, seven days a week. Downtown streets (e.g. Highland Avenue, National City Boulevard, and all streets in between from the northern to southern city limits) are swept daily between 4 AM and 9 AM. In the event of a holiday, severe weather conditions or equipment breakdown, sweeping shall occur the following week scheduled.

To obtain the route schedule for your area or for any other sweeping related questions, contact (619) 336-4580. Please do not park your car or place any lumber, bricks, foreign objects or portable basketball hoops in the gutter or on the street between the posted hours on scheduled sweeping days.

## Storm Water Program

The Storm Water Division regulates and enforces Clean Water Act standards as prescribed by the National Pollutant Discharge Elimination System (NPDES) permit program. The sanitary sewer system drains to a treatment plant; the storm drain system does not. Everything that goes into storm drains goes out to Paradise Creek, the Sweetwater River, San Diego Bay, and other local waterways without any treatment.

Actions the City takes include street sweeping, storm drain cleaning, and inspecting businesses and construction sites to make sure they are following best practices. Storm water requirement information is provided for Residents, Businesses, Development Projects, Construction, Structural BMPs, and Landscape Irrigation, along with a Storm Water Inspections FAQ, the Storm Water BMP Manual 2024, and the Jurisdictional Runoff Management Program (JRMP).

Source: https://www.nationalcityca.gov/government/engineering-public-works/engineering-division/storm-water-program

## Fire Reports — How to Request

The National City Fire Department processes all fire report requests through the City's secure NextRequest Public Records Portal, where you can submit your request online, communicate with City staff, and track its status. Include as much information as possible: incident date, approximate incident time (if known), incident address or location, your name, your contact information, and any additional information that may assist in locating the report.

Copy fees under the California Public Records Act: $1.00 for the first page and $0.15 for each additional page. If your incident is currently under investigation, the final fire report may not be available until the investigation has been completed.

Source: https://www.nationalcityca.gov/government/fire/fire-administration/get-fire-reports

## Fire Department Community Service Hours

The National City Fire Department offers community service opportunities for individuals who are required to complete verified community service hours for school. Participants assist with non-emergency projects such as cleaning and organizing equipment, facility beautification, administrative support, community events, and public education programs. All participants must receive approval from the Fire Department before beginning any assignment.

To request hours, contact the Fire Department during normal business hours: Phone (619) 336-4550, Email fire@nationalcityca.gov. Be prepared to provide your name and contact information, the reason community service is required, the total number of hours needed, any completion deadlines, and any required paperwork from your school or organization. Placement is not guaranteed.
