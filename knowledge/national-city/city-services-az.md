## Fire Reports — How to Request

The National City Fire Department processes all fire report requests through the City's secure NextRequest Public Records Portal. The portal lets you submit a request online, communicate directly with City staff, and track the status of your request from start to finish.

To help staff locate your report, include as much information as possible: incident date, approximate incident time (if known), incident address or location, your name, your contact information, and any additional information that may assist in locating the report.

If your incident is currently under investigation, the final fire report may not be available until the investigation has been completed.

## Fire Reports — Copy Fees

Under the California Public Records Act, the fees for copies of fire reports are $1.00 for the first page and $0.15 for each additional page. Applicable fees are communicated during the request process.

## Community Services — Refund Policy

Refunds are granted if submitted prior to the third session of any class, activity, or program. A valid doctor's note or U.S. Military Orders that prohibit the participant from continuing the class, activity, or program must be provided. Late refund requests are not accepted. Refunds are issued as a check to the original payee.
