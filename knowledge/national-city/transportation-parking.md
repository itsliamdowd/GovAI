## Parking Citations — Deadlines and Penalties

Parking citations must be contested or paid within 21 calendar days of issuance. Failure to pay the fine within the allotted 21 days will result in additional penalties and/or DMV delay of your vehicle registration until penalties are paid. Unregistered vehicles or vehicles with over five outstanding parking citations may be impounded, per 22651(i)(1) CVC. All fines are the responsibility of the registered vehicle owner.

The City of National City contracts Citation Processing Center for parking enforcement administration within the City.

## Parking Citations — How to Pay

By mail, payments can be sent to either of these locations:
- National City Finance Department, 1243 National City Boulevard, National City, CA 91950
- City of National City, c/o Citation Processing Center, P.O. Box 10479, Newport Beach, CA 92658-0479

Enclose the citation with your check or money order payable to "City of National City". Please do not send cash. A service charge will be assessed for checks not honored by your bank.

In person, payments can be made at the City of National City Finance Department (1243 National City Boulevard). City office hours are Monday - Thursday 7:00 AM - 6:00 PM, closed weekends and holidays. Online payments can be made at Citation Processing Center.

## Parking Citations — Low-Income Payment Plan

CVC 40220 allows for installment payments to qualifying low-income individuals who apply within 120 days from the issuance of a notice of parking violation, or within 10 days after an administrative hearing determination, whichever is later. For more information about the low-income payment plan, call 1(800)989-2058, or visit Citation Processing Center for links to payment plan information and application forms.

## Parking Citations — Contesting a Citation

A vehicle owner/operator who believes that a citation has been issued in error may appeal by requesting an Administrative Review within 21 calendar days from the citation issue date. Appeal online at Citation Processing Center, or submit a written statement (with any supporting documentation and/or photographs) to: City of National City, c/o Citation Processing Center, P.O. Box 10479, Newport Beach, CA 92658-0479.

Original materials submitted will not be returned. You will be notified by mail of the result of the review. For additional information call 1 (800)989-2058.

## Street Sweeping — Schedule and Parking Rules

Most residential streets are swept once a week between the hours of 7:00 am and 4:00 pm, seven days a week. Downtown streets (e.g. Highland Avenue, National City Boulevard, and all streets in between from the northern to southern city limits) are swept daily between 4 AM and 9 AM.

In the event of a holiday, severe weather conditions or equipment breakdown, sweeping shall occur the following week scheduled. To obtain the route schedule for your area or for any other sweeping-related questions, contact (619) 336-4580. A weekly Street Sweeping Schedule (PDF) is posted on the page.

Do not park your car or place any lumber, bricks, foreign objects or portable basketball hoops in the gutter or on the street between the posted hours on scheduled sweeping days.

## Street Light Maintenance — Reporting a Light Out

There are approximately 1,820 street lights throughout National City. Of these, approximately 585 are owned by the City of National City and 1,235 by San Diego Gas and Electric. The City performs a complete inspection of all streetlights twice a year, and residents are encouraged to report any streetlight that is burned out or not working. The City's contract vendor for street light maintenance is Southwest Signal Service, which replaces burned out street light bulbs as they are reported.

To report a street light out, call the daytime line or voice mail at (619) 336-4580. You may also fax the information to Public Works at (619) 336-4594 or email publicworks@nationalcityca.gov. Include your name and daytime phone number and the street light location (street name and address). Indicate if the pole is in front of, across from, or in-between the address given. If there is no specific address, determine how many street lights it is from an intersection and which side of the street. Exact location is very important because repairs are made during the daytime.

## Traffic & Parking — Engineering Division Services

The Engineering Division's Traffic & Parking services include:
- Traffic studies including parking assessments, sight distance evaluations, speed surveys and counter measures, traffic signal / traffic control warrants, analysis of traffic collision data, traffic calming measures and pedestrian connectivity
- Engineering and planning for smart growth redevelopment, including traffic circulation, traffic calming, pedestrian enhancements, streetscaping, connectivity to transit routes / centers, upgrades to bicycle facilities, and improved walkability
- Monitoring, analysis and upgrades for the City's fiber optics and traffic signal system, including traffic signal coordination, video equipment, and high-speed data transfer
- Plan preparation and review of traffic signal plans, corridor improvement plans, site plan circulation and access, and traffic control plans
- Preliminary design and engineering, and preparation of bid document plans and specifications for capital improvement projects related to traffic improvements
- Preparation of grant applications for funding capital improvement projects
- Evaluation of the state-of-the-practice for school zone safety, including in-roadway lighting for crosswalks, flashing beacons, vehicle speed feedback signs, roundabouts, curb extensions, and other traffic calming measures
- Participation and technical advising to the Traffic Safety Committee
- Public outreach and education
