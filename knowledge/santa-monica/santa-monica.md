# City of Santa Monica — Knowledge Base

Facts below were compiled from santamonica.gov in Oct 2026. Fees, dates and hours change; when a question hinges on a current fee, deadline, meeting date or a person currently holding office, confirm with web_search (santamonica.gov) before answering.

## Basics
- Incorporated 1886. Charter city, council–manager government. ~8.4 sq mi, ~93,000 residents. ZIP codes 90401–90405. Los Angeles County.
- City Hall: 1685 Main St, Santa Monica, CA 90401. Main line (310) 458-8411.
- City Hall hours: 7:30 a.m.–5:30 p.m. Mon–Thu and alternating Fridays. Department hours vary (Permit Center 8 a.m.–3 p.m., closed every Friday; Rent Control 8–12:30 & 1:30–4:30 Mon–Thu and every other Friday, (310) 458-8751, rentcontrol@santamonica.gov).
- Website: https://www.santamonica.gov. Open data: https://data.santamonica.gov.

Source: https://www.santamonica.gov/places/city-facilities/santa-monica-city-hall

## Santa Monica 311 (non-emergency services & info)
- Dial 311 inside city limits, (866) 311-7266 from outside. Phone staffed 7:30 a.m.–5:30 p.m. Mon–Thu, 8 a.m.–5 p.m. Fri.
- 24/7: online at santamonica.gov/311, email 311@santamonica.gov, or the "City of Santa Monica" mobile app.
- Use 311 for: potholes, graffiti, streetlights, bulky-item pickup, missed trash, illegal dumping, abandoned vehicles, sidewalk repair, tree issues, noise/code complaints, homelessness outreach requests.
- Emergencies: 9-1-1. Police/Fire non-emergency dispatch: (310) 458-8491.

Source: https://www.santamonica.gov/311

## Permits & Licenses — where things live
- Virtual One-Stop Permits Center: santamonica.gov/topic-explainers/onestop. Public counter: Permit Services Center, City Hall, 8 a.m.–3 p.m. Mon–Thu (closed Fridays).
- Building / electrical / plumbing / mechanical permits: apply online through Citizen Access (e-permits for simple trade permits; plan check for larger work). Plans reviewed by Building & Safety, Planning, Fire, Public Works as applicable. Most residential remodels, additions, ADUs, solar, EV chargers, water heaters, re-roofs need a permit.
- Business license: required for any business operating or based in Santa Monica, including home-based and short-term rental hosts. Apply/renew/pay online via the Business License system. Office: business.license@santamonica.gov, (310) 458-8745. Renewals due annually; tax is gross-receipts based for most categories.
- Block party permits (closing a residential street): separate simpler process via Transportation Planning, online only, 14 days' notice (30 near holidays), neighbor petition (51% R-1 blocks / 25% other), $100.95 fee.
- Community event / special event permits: permits.santamonica.gov/community-events (OpenCounter). Required for gatherings of 150+ people at eligible sites, events obstructing traffic, or temporary structures. Holding an unpermitted event is a misdemeanor. Apply well in advance (large events 60–90+ days). Fees and requirements can change after staff review.
- Film permits: handled since Sept 2025 by Film Santa Monica (filmsantamonica.com). Apply at least 5 business days ahead. Required for commercial, student, educational and non-profit shoots on public property. Still photography on public property also needs a permit.
- Encroachment / use of public property permits: any work or temporary use in the public right-of-way (sidewalk, street, alley): dumpsters, scaffolding, cranes, sidewalk dining, construction staging. Via Public Works / One-Stop.
- Preferential (residential) parking permits: santamonica.gov/process-explainers/how-to-obtain-a-preferential-parking-permit. Rates effective July 1, 2026: 1st residential $41, 2nd $51, 3rd $95, 4th+ $148, visitor $72. Zone terms: Zones 1 & 3 Oct 1–Sep 30; Zone 2 Jul 1–Jun 30; Zone 4 Apr 1–Mar 31; Zone 5 Jan 1–Dec 31; second-half-of-term purchases are prorated. Not enforced on New Year's Day, July 4, Thanksgiving, Christmas. Permits don't exempt you from street sweeping, meters or curb markings.
- Temporary Use Permits (TUP), Conditional Use Permits (CUP), Administrative Approvals, Development Review: Planning Division, via One-Stop.
- Sidewalk vending permits: required under the city's vending ordinance; also need a county health permit for food.
- Short-term rental (home-share) license: only hosted home-shares are allowed; vacation rentals of entire units are prohibited.
- Dog licenses: Santa Monica Animal Shelter / Animal Services (1640 9th St, (310) 458-8594); rabies certificate required; $37.26/yr altered, $74.52 unaltered.
- Alarm permits: required for residential/commercial alarm systems (SMPD).
- Taxi, pedicab, newsrack, banner, tree removal, and sign permits also exist; route to One-Stop / relevant department.

Source: https://www.santamonica.gov/places/city-facilities/permit-services-center

## Trash, Recycling & Street Sweeping
- Resource Recovery & Recycling (RRR) is the city's own hauler; collection runs 7 days a week across the city. Three streams: landfill (black), recycling (blue), organics/food scraps (green). Organics separation is mandatory under SB 1383.
- Holidays: all pickups (trash, recycling, organics, bulky) shift one day later; street sweeping not enforced on holidays.
- Bulky item pickup: up to 5 items per request, Mon–Fri; request via 311 with address, unit, phone, item list and curb/alley; allow 7–10 days. Mattresses, furniture, appliances accepted; no construction debris or hazardous waste.
- Extra trash pickup and container upsizing are fee-based; rates at santamonica.gov/solid-waste-rates (FY 2026-27 rates effective July 1, 2026).
- Street sweeping: obey posted signs; tickets issued for parking during posted sweeping windows.

Source: https://www.santamonica.gov/topic-explainers/residential-collection-services

## Housing & Rent Control
- Rent Control (Charter Amendment 1979) covers most multi-unit buildings built before April 10, 1979. Annual General Adjustment set each year by the Rent Control Board. Tenants/landlords: (310) 458-8751.
- Tenant Harassment Ordinance, Just Cause eviction protections, relocation assistance apply citywide.
- Affordable housing: Below Market Housing (BMH) waitlist via Housing Division; Section 8 administered by the Santa Monica Housing Authority.
- ADUs: permitted on most residential lots; streamlined review.

Source: https://www.santamonica.gov/topic-explainers/rent-control-in-santa-monica

## Water & Utilities
- Water: City of Santa Monica Water Resources (city utility). Billing/questions via Finance utility billing, (310) 458-8224 (verify). Watering restrictions and conservation rules apply.
- Electricity: Southern California Edison (not the city). Gas: SoCalGas. Clean Power Alliance is the default electricity generation provider.

## Public Safety & Codes
- Noise ordinance: construction generally 8 a.m.–6 p.m. Mon–Fri, 9 a.m.–5 p.m. Sat, none Sun/holidays (verify).
- Gas leaf blowers banned; electric ones rated ≤70 dB allowed (SMMC 4.12.180). Smoking banned on the beach, Pier, Promenade and in common areas of multi-unit housing.
- Fireworks prohibited. Anti-camping ordinance applies on public property.
- Code enforcement complaints via 311.

Source: https://www.santamonica.gov/topic-explainers/public-safety-codes

## Taxes & Finance
- Sales tax rate in Santa Monica 10.25% (verify). Transient Occupancy Tax 15% (verify). Measure GS/GSH real estate transfer taxes on high-value sales fund housing and schools.
- Business license tax based on gross receipts; minimum tax applies.
