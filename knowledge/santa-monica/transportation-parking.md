# Transportation & Parking

## Parking Structures — Downtown Rate Changes

As of January 12, 2026, downtown parking structure rates were revised. The first 30 minutes are free at Parking Structures 1, 2, 4–9, and Ken Edwards Center, shifting from a nine-tier to a three-tier rate model. Senior and disabled discounts remain available. Rate details were being finalized for a January 27, 2026 City Council review, so confirm current rates at the individual structure pages on santamonica.gov.

Source: https://www.santamonica.gov/press/2025/12/08/new-streamlined-rates-take-the-guesswork-out-of-public-parking

Beach Lots 2, 4, 5, 6, 9 North similar ranges ($12–$20 summer, $8–$12 winter). Short-term lots: $3/hour summer, $2/hour winter. Rates were set to increase ~$0.25–$2 per hour on average starting January 12, 2026. Confirm current rates on santamonica.gov/places/parking-lots.

Source: https://www.santamonica.gov/places/parking-lots/beach-parking-lot-1-north

## Parking Citations — Payment & Contest

A parking ticket is a parking citation. Parking citations can be paid online through the Finance Department or by mail. To contest a citation, follow the appeal process outlined on your citation or on santamonica.gov. Payment and contest submissions go through the city's online Finance portal. For questions, contact Finance at (310) 458-8220.

Source: https://www.santamonica.gov/process-explainers/how-to-pay-a-parking-citation

## Preferential Parking (Residential) Permits

Residents (tenants or owners living at the address) in preferential parking zones (Zones 1–5) can buy two kinds of permits with the same privileges: Residential permits, tied to one specific vehicle (no maximum), and Visitor permits, usable in any vehicle (maximum 2 per household). Rates effective July 1, 2026: 1st residential permit $41, 2nd $51, 3rd $95, 4th and higher $148; visitor $72 each. Required documents: a valid government-issued photo ID and proof of residency dated within the last 30 days in your name at the Santa Monica address (utility, cable or internet bill, or a bank, credit card or mortgage statement); residential permits also need DMV vehicle registration matching your name and address (if it doesn't match, you can still buy up to 2 visitor permits). Apply online through the parking permit portal or in person at the GoSaMo Center (1444 4th St, 8–11:30 a.m. and 1–4:30 p.m. Mon–Thu and every other Friday); allow 3–5 business days, then pay by emailed link and print a 30-day temporary permit while the permanent one is mailed (up to 2 weeks). Zone terms: Zones 1 & 3 Oct 1–Sep 30; Zone 2 Jul 1–Jun 30; Zone 4 Apr 1–Mar 31; Zone 5 Jan 1–Dec 31; second-half-of-term purchases are prorated. Permits do not exempt you from street sweeping, meters, curb markings or the 2-Block Parking Rule; not enforced on New Year's Day, July 4, Thanksgiving or Christmas. ADA placard holders don't need one. Questions: parking@santamonica.gov.

Source: https://www.santamonica.gov/process-explainers/how-to-obtain-a-preferential-parking-permit

## Big Blue Bus — Fares & Payment

Big Blue Bus (Santa Monica's city-owned transit) charges $1.25 per ride with a TAP card or mobile ticket, or $1.50 cash (May 2026 rates). Discounted fares apply for seniors, disabled riders, and Medicare cardholders (check bigbluebus.com for current discounted amounts). Payment is via TAP card or the Transit, Token Transit, or TAP mobile apps. Transfers are included with fares.

Source: https://www.bigbluebus.com/fares/fare-information.aspx

## Big Blue Bus — Main Routes & Frequencies

Key Big Blue Bus routes include Route 1 (Santa Monica & Main St, every 10–20 min), Route 2 (Wilshire Blvd, every 10–20 min), Route 3 (Lincoln Blvd, every 10–20 min), Route 5 (Olympic Blvd), Route 7 (Pico Blvd, every 10–20 min), Route 8 (Ocean Park Blvd), Route 9 (Pacific Palisades), Route 10 (Downtown LA), Route 43 (San Vicente/26th St), Route 44 (SMC Campus Connector). Metro E Line (Expo Line) stops at Downtown Santa Monica, 17th St/SMC, and 26th St/Bergamot. Check bigbluebus.com/routes-and-schedules for timetables and real-time tracking.

Source: https://www.bigbluebus.com/routes-and-schedules/

## Bike & Scooter Share

Santa Monica operates under a Shared Mobility program with permitted operators. Metro Bike Share is available in the city, as are docked and dockless scooter services. Users must follow operator rules: helmets required, parking in designated zones, and age/safety requirements. Report unsafe or illegally parked vehicles to the city's 311 service.

Source: https://www.santamonica.gov/parks-and-recreation/shared-mobility

## EV Charging Stations

Santa Monica has public EV charging stations throughout the city, including Level 2 chargers (240V, slower charging, often free) and DC fast chargers. Locations are available on the city website. Some are at city facilities, others at commercial locations. Check ChargeHub, PlugShare, or the city's transportation pages for current locations and availability.

Source: https://www.santamonica.gov/sustainability/electric-vehicles

## Street Sweeping — Parking Rules

Street sweeping occurs on posted days and times. Vehicles parked during the sweeping window receive citations. Preferential parking permits do not exempt you from sweeping restrictions. Check street signs for your zone's sweeping schedule, or call 311 for details.

Source: https://www.santamonica.gov/topic-explainers/trash-recycling-street-sweeping

## Accessible Parking & Disabled Permits

California-issued disabled parking placards and license plates are honored throughout Santa Monica. Accessible parking spaces are located throughout the city, including at public facilities, structures, and beach lots. Some lots offer reduced or free parking for disabled permits; confirm at specific lots on santamonica.gov.

Source: https://www.santamonica.gov/places/parking-lots
