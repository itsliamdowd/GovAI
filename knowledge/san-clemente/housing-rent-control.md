## Community Outreach Workers (Homeless Resources)

The City employs two full-time Community Outreach Workers who provide outreach services 7 days a week. They coordinate closely with the Orange County Sheriff's Department, Code Compliance staff, and Park Rangers to provide outreach and case management for homeless individuals. They also provide resources and referrals through their network of outreach partners within South Orange County.

Through intensive case management, the workers offer these services and resources:
- attending all Orange County Housing Authority match meetings on housing vouchers, rapid rehousing programs, and case conferencing
- transportation assistance
- family reunification
- referrals to all homeless and domestic violence shelters
- medical attention and mental health assistance via the Orange County Health Care Agency
- counseling resources
- financial literacy information
- social services assistance
- SSI/SSDI assistance
- homeless prevention via affiliate agencies that provide rental assistance
- drug and alcohol treatment referrals
- storage and disposal of property and/or trash associated with any encampments
