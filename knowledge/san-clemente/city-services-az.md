## Beaches, Parks & Recreation Commission

The Beaches, Parks and Recreation Commission advises the City Council on parks, beaches, recreation, parkways and street trees. It meets on the second Tuesdays of every other month (even numbered months) at City Council Chambers at 6 pm. Agendas and minutes are available on the City's website. You can also call 949-361-8237 or drop by the Beaches, Parks and Recreation Department administration at 100 N Calle Seville, San Clemente, 92672.

To speak at a meeting, complete a form found in the rear of the meeting room and list your name, address, and the topic of interest. You may speak on an agenda topic or under public input. You can also send comments by mail to City staff or to the Commission, by fax, mail drop box, or City email. For general concerns about beaches, parks, or facilities (operations, maintenance, design, etc.), email Recreation.

The Commission has seven members appointed by the City Council to two-year terms. Appointments are staggered, with three Commissioners appointed one year and four the following year. Appointments expire on June 30, and new appointments start July 1. To apply, file an application with the City Clerk before an interview and selection by the City Council. Selections normally occur in June of each year, or when a vacancy occurs.

## Beaches, Parks & Recreation Department

The department has Administration, Recreation, and Golf Divisions. It oversees 324 acres of recreational space, including 23 parks, 25.9 miles of hiking trails and 2 miles of public beaches, plus a 133-acre golf course. The Recreation Division offers an average of 2,500 classes per year, coordinates 45 City and partnered special events, and administers 17,000 rental and program bookings at public facilities, parks, ball fields, and beach locations. Facilities and parks are used through permit processes.

The Golf Division operates the 133-acre San Clemente Municipal Golf Course, which averages 90,000 rounds played per year. Tee times can be booked through the department's Golf pages.

Under the CARE Program (Commemorate. Acknowledge. Remember. Enhance.), a customized amenity can be placed in public spaces. Download the Bench or Picnic Table Application (PDF). For more information, email Recreation or call 949-361-8237.

Contact: Beaches, Parks & Recreation, Community Center, 100 N. Calle Seville, San Clemente, CA 92672. Phone: 949-361-8264.

## Lost & Found Animals

Complete the Lost / Found Animal Form to report a lost or found animal. Animals currently at the San Clemente Dana Point Animal Shelter (dogs, cats, rabbits) can be viewed online.

Dogs and cats with a current license tag attached to their collars are held at the Coastal Animal Services Authority shelter for 10 days. Unlicensed strays are held only 5 days. Owners are notified by telephone. Keep the Animal Services Department informed of any address or telephone number changes after you apply for your pet's license.

Letting a pet run loose is against the law (see San Clemente Municipal Code 6.08.020, Dogs on public property - Animals in proximity to residences).

## Utility Billing — Open or Close Water Service

Notify the City of any service need at least 2 business days in advance. City personnel must have access to your utility meters at all times to change service or take readings.

To open a new account, contact the Utility Billing Division verbally by phone and indicate the day service should begin. A $40 establishment fee is required. A security deposit of $120 for residential service and $200 for commercial service is required for water and sewer service. The deposit may be waived with a letter of reference from a previous utility company showing 12 months of good pay history, or 12 months of good pay history with the City on a previous account.

To close an account, contact the Utility Billing Division with the termination day and your forwarding mailing address. There is no fee to disconnect. A final statement is prepared in the billing cycle following the termination date.

## Utility Billing — Late Payment, Shutoff and Assistance

Accounts unpaid 25 days after the billing date are subject to a 10% penalty. Accounts delinquent for more than 60 days are subject to turn-off, a $25 Final Notice Fee and Reconnection Fee. All charges, penalties, final notice fees and disconnection fees must be paid before service is re-established. Services that remain off for an extended period continue to accrue daily charges for the fixed service charges and the storm drain and clean ocean fees.

Any complaint or request for investigation must be received in writing within seven days of receiving the original bill. It must be mailed to the City of San Clemente Utility Billing Division and received before the final notice expires. Requests for amortization or extension must be received before the final notice expires. Amortization requests extending beyond three months must be in writing and may be subject to manager approval.

Low-income customers may apply for reduced reconnection fees by completing a low-income application. Low-income customers who submit a doctor's certification that termination would result in life-threatening conditions before the expiration date of any amortization plan are eligible for a 60-day extension to an existing amortization plan.

If you have received a written collections notice and cannot pay by your deadline, contact the City to discuss your case. Phone: 949-361-8315 option 4. Office hours: Monday through Thursday and Alternating Fridays, 8 am to 5 pm.

Source: https://www.sanclemente.gov/231/Utility-Bill-Assistance-Programs

The Utilities Division is at 380 Avenida Pico, Bldg. N, open 7 am to 4:30 pm Monday through Thursday and 7 am to 3:30 pm on Fridays.

Under SCMC 13.04.400, a customer may demand in writing that the Water Division test their meter, accompanied by the Water Meter Test fee of $175. If the meter registers more or less than three (3) percent correctly, another meter is substituted, the test fee is refunded, and the current water bill is adjusted. If it is within three (3) percent, the fee is forfeited to the City. A customer or authorized representative may witness the test.
