## Beaches, Parks & Recreation Commission

The Beaches, Parks and Recreation Commission meets on the second Tuesdays of every other month (even numbered months) at City Council Chambers at 6 pm. It advises the City Council on parks, beaches, recreation, parkways and street trees. Members are appointed by the City Council to two-year terms. Appointments expire on June 30, and new appointments begin July 1st.

To speak at a meeting, complete a form found in the rear of the meeting room listing your name, address, and the topic of interest. You may speak on an agenda topic or under public input. To serve on the Commission, file an application with the City Clerk before an interview and selection by the City Council. Selections normally occur in June of each year, or when a vacancy occurs.

Agendas and minutes are available on the City's website. You may also call 949-361-8237 or visit the Beaches, Parks and Recreation Department administration at 100 N Calle Seville, San Clemente, 92672.

## Beaches, Parks & Recreation Department — Overview and Contact

The department has Administration, Recreation, and Golf Divisions. It oversees 324 acres of recreational space, including 23 parks, 25.9 miles of hiking trails and 2 miles of public beaches, plus a 133-acre golf course. The Recreation Division offers an average of 2,500 classes per year, coordinates 45 City and partnered special events, and administers 17,000 rental and program bookings.

Bench or picnic table amenities can be placed in public spaces through the CARE Program. Download the Bench or Picnic Table Application (PDF), email Recreation, or call 949-361-8237.

Contact: Community Center, 100 N. Calle Seville, San Clemente, CA 92672. Phone: 949-361-8264.

## Special Event, Banner, Beach Clean-Up and Film Permits

A Special Event Permit may be needed for organized activities on public property, such as block parties, parades, surf contests, athletic events and carnivals. Construction projects, funerals, and activities that do not at least partially impact the public right-of-way or attract more than 20 people in any 24-hour period do not require one. Event applications must be submitted 60 days in advance of your event date. Call the Beaches, Parks and Recreation Division at 949-361-8264.

For a Special Activity on private non-residential property, apply for a Temporary Use Permit through the Zoning Application webpage or call the Planning Division at 949-361-6197.

A banner across El Camino Real at Avenida Del Mar is open to non-profit groups for public events. Complete a Street Banner Application or call 949-361-8264. For a simple beach clean-up, complete the Beach Clean-Up Application online.

Commercial still and motion photography on public property requires a film permit from the Beaches, Parks and Recreation Department. A minimum of 4 business days is required for processing. Email Recreation or call 949-361-8264.

## Senior Services

Silver Connections (Active Aging) offers free or low-cost programs for participants over the age of 55.

Age Well Senior Services (949-855-8033) provides meal services, transportation, Senior Center activities, case management, and health and wellness services. The Dorothy Visser Senior Center, 117 Avenida Victoria, San Clemente, CA 92672, phone 949-498-3322, is operated by Age Well. It has a fitness room, classes and programs, and daily lunches. For low-cost non-emergency medical transportation, call 949-855-9766.

OC ACCESS (877-628-2232) is a fee-based shared-ride service for qualified applicants whose limitations prevent them from using the regular OC Bus fixed-route service. It operates within ¾-mile of OC Bus service and during the same hours.

San Clemente Village (949-441-1348) is a volunteer-based membership organization for residents age 55 and over. Membership is required.

## Senior Mobility Program

The San Clemente Senior Mobility Program is a free service giving San Clemente residents age 60 and over transportation between their residences and destinations within San Clemente, such as grocery stores, non-emergency medical appointments, the beach/park, and the Dorothy Visser Senior Center (available Monday through Friday, 8 am to 4 pm). Transportation is available 7 days a week from 7 am to 7 pm (excluding holidays).

To sign up, submit an application (PDF), also available at the Dorothy Visser Senior Center. Proof of age and San Clemente residency is required. Questions: Engineering at 949-361-6119.

Source: https://www.sanclemente.gov/331/Senior-Services

## SC Rides with Lyft

Using the code SCRIDES, riders can save up to $5 on rides. In the Lyft app, go to Payment, then "Add Lyft Pass," and enter SCRIDES. Discounts apply only to "Shared Rides." Rides must be taken between 6 am-8 pm, and picked up and dropped off within a 0.25 miles radius of the original 191 and 193 bus routes. For assisted or Wheelchair Accessible Vehicle (WAV) rides, call Butterfli at 855-267-2354, press 3 to book, and mention the SCRIDES code.

## Lost & Found Animals

To report a lost or found animal, complete the Lost / Found Animal Form. Animals currently at the San Clemente Dana Point Animal Shelter (dogs, cats, rabbits) can be viewed on the page.

## Trash & Recycling — Hauler and Service

CR&R is San Clemente's franchise waste hauler. Phone: 877-728-0446. The City's page links to residential, commercial, and multifamily rates, roll off bins, and cub bins (PDFs), and covers starting or stopping trash service, reporting a missed pickup, trash can violations, and illegal dumping.

## Bulky Item Pick-Ups

Schedule a free bulky item pickup on your regular waste collection day using CR and R's Online Scheduling Service. Each San Clemente resident customer can arrange a free bulky item pick-up four times per calendar year, for up to four large items or four bags of material. Additional pick-ups are provided for a small fee. Examples include washing machines, dryers, sofas, and refrigerators.

Source: https://www.sanclemente.gov/275/Trash-Recycling
