## Building Permits: How to Apply

The City of San Clemente offers 3 ways to apply for a building permit:
- **e-Trakit:** You must set up an account with a username and password.
- **In person:** See the Building Division Office Hours. In-person applications do not apply to Solar and/or Energy Storage Systems.
- **Email:** Email Permits and attach PDFs only, including your completed permit application and any supplemental documents.

For questions about permitting, planning, or engineering services, or for help scheduling an in-person appointment, contact the Community Development Liaison at 949-361-6100. For zoning questions, allowable uses for property, and other planning questions, email Planning. For encroachment permits, email Engineering. The Community Development Department is at 910 Calle Negocio, Ste. 100, San Clemente, CA 92673.

## Single-Family Residential Permit Submittals

Residential permit applications may be submitted electronically by emailing the completed application, forms, and electronic plans to Permits.

For paper submittals, email your application to Permits. Once it is received and input, you will receive an invoice for plan check fees and instructions for submittal. Follow the instructions and place the requested items and 3 sets of plans in the drop off shelf marked "Building Drop Off," located inside the front door of City Hall. Drop off submittals are only available during regular business hours: 7:30 am to 5:30 pm Monday through Thursday, and 8 am to 5 pm on Fridays. City Hall is at 910 Calle Negocio, San Clemente, CA 92673.

Forms to be submitted: Building Permit Application, BA-1 HOA Confirmation Form, OCFA Fillable SFD Form, Special Inspections form, and Engineers Structural Observation Inspection form.

## ADU Building Permits

State law allows the construction of ADUs (that meet requirements) on property with single or multiple family residential zoning. To apply for an ADU building permit, email a fully completed ADU submittal packet and ADU fee form to the Planning Division. ADU submittals must be started with Planning, not the Building Division.

If your submittal is found complete, Planning staff will provide an initialed ADU packet to the Building Division and notify you by email. Then a Building permit application may be processed. ADU submittals will not be processed if they are submitted to the Building Division without first receiving approval from the Planning Dept.

Available forms: ADU Application Packet, ADU Fee Form, ADU Standards, and Pre-approved ADU Plans Program. For ADU questions, call 949-361-6197.

Source: https://www.sanclemente.gov/258/Permits

## Legalizing Unpermitted ADUs (AB 2533)

Under State legislation (AB 2533), a streamlined process is available to help legalize an ADU or JADU that was constructed prior to January 1, 2020. The process is explained in two documents on the Permits page: "Legalizing Unpermitted ADUs" and the "Substandard Housing Checklist."

## ADU Types and Information Packet

Planning Services lists four types of ADUs:
- **Attached ADU:** attached to the primary dwelling as an addition. It may be new construction or a conversion of existing space.
- **Detached ADU:** within an accessory building detached from the primary dwelling.
- **Junior ADU:** no more than 500 square feet in size and contained entirely within a single-family residence.
- **ADU Conversions:** an ADU established by converting existing floor space, such as a bedroom, non-living area, or an accessory building.

The City's ADU information packet covers the steps of the review process, development standards, FAQs, a link to the State ADU handbook, and templates for the required address exhibit and deed restriction. For ADU questions, email City planning staff or call 949-361-6197.

Source: https://www.sanclemente.gov/240/Planning-Services

## Owner-Builder Permits

If you will do improvements to your home yourself, you can obtain an "Owner Builder" permit after completing the owner builder verification form. If you are not doing the work yourself, your California Licensed Contractor can obtain the permits.

The City strongly discourages the owner builder option. If you become your own contractor, you assume liability for bad work performed by whoever you hire, which could open the door to lawsuits. The Permits page links to these documents: Owner Builders Beware!, Preventing Mechanic's Liens, 10 Tips On Hiring Contractors, and The Responsibilities of an Owner Builder.

Source: https://www.sanclemente.gov/248/Building-Services

## Scheduling Building Inspections

Schedule an inspection online or by phone at 949-361-3366. Be prepared to provide your Permit Number and your Inspection Code. Request on-site inspections at various stages of construction, and do not cover your work until the City has inspected it. Inspection approvals are recorded by the building inspector on the inspection job card. Have the plans and the inspection job card available for the inspector at the job site.

AM inspections are scheduled for the time slot 8:30 am to noon. PM inspections are scheduled for the time slot 12:30 to 3:30 pm.

Examples of inspection codes: 101: Preconstruction Meeting; 103: Footings/Steel/Hardware; 112: Final Framing; 117: Drywall; 333: Electrical Final; 444: Plumbing Final; 555: Pool/Spa Final; 999: Building Final. The Building Division provides final approval when construction is complete and code compliance is verified.

Source: https://www.sanclemente.gov/259/Inspections

## Planning (Zoning) Permit Applications

The Zoning Code requires a zoning permit to allow certain uses, development, and exceptions. When a project is minor, staff may decide without a public hearing. A public hearing decision is required when there is potential for public concern or land use impacts.

To get started, consult a planner by calling (949) 361-6197 or emailing the planning department. Staff will tell you whether a zoning permit is required and describe the review process, fees, and application. Most zoning applications may be submitted electronically through the eTRAKiT website by signing into your account, or by registering for one.

These applications cannot be submitted online and must be emailed or submitted at the public counter: City Antenna Permit, City Planner zoning interpretations, Landscape plan detailed reviews, Preliminary applications, Reasonable accommodation requests, Short-term lodging zoning permits, Zoning verification letter, and Zoning research and legal reviews.

After you submit, a planner reviews your materials, sets up a project file, and confirms fees and whether the necessary materials are provided. You will then receive a follow-up email or phone call about next steps.

Source: https://www.sanclemente.gov/240/Planning-Services

## When to Contact Planning or Building

Homeowners should contact the Planning or Building Division if they intend to add a room, or build a wall, shed, pool, patio cover, or similar improvement. Setback, height, or construction requirements may affect the design, and most projects require a building permit. Many homeowner improvements can be reviewed and approved at the public counter of the Community Development Department.

Business owners relocating or establishing a new business should contact Planning and Building to determine whether the use is permitted and whether the site or structure is adequate. Developers building a new structure or doing a major renovation should contact the Planning Division early in the design process.

Source: https://www.sanclemente.gov/240/Planning-Services

## Zoning Lookup and Zoning Ordinance

The Zoning Ordinance is "Title 17" of the Municipal Code. It lists the land uses permitted in each district and sets standards for lot size, building height, and required yard and setback provisions. For example, Chapter 17.32, Section 30 (17.32.030) identifies permitted uses in the Residential-Low Density (RL) zoning district, and Section 40 (17.32.040) identifies required setback from property lines.

The City has two web mapping applications. The Zoning Lookup Tool lets you search by property address or click the map to see zoning, overlays, and primary development standards. Community Maps is an interactive zoning map where you turn on layers to see zoning and overlay districts.

Specific Plans cover some areas, mostly east of I-5: Forster Ranch, Marblehead Coastal, Marblehead Inland, Pier Bowl, Rancho San Clemente, Talega, and West Pico Corridor. For a property in a Specific Plan area, refer to the land use map in the Specific Plan to identify its zoning.

Source: https://www.sanclemente.gov/286/Zoning-Information

## Requesting Property Records (Permit History)

If you are buying or selling a home and need information such as permit records or square footage, submit a public records request online.
- Request the "permit history" for the address to get permits, inspection records, square footage notes, name of builder, or names of contractors.
- Request the "engineering history" for soils reports or geological info.
- Request the blueprints for the property, by public records request, to get plans.
- Request the "tract map" and "grading plan" for property boundary lines. The "planning history" can also help.
- Request the "planning history" for development history, Coastal Commission records, zoning info, allowable uses, short-term lodging permits, historic property info, or condo conversions.

Please allow ten days for a determination of your records request. Expediting is not available.

Source: https://www.sanclemente.gov/258/Permits

## Engineering Permits (Grading, Encroachment, Construction, Well)

The Land Development and Permits Section issues permits for encroachments, grading, construction inspection, and well permits for residential and commercial developments. A Grading Permit is required for any grading work specified in the City of San Clemente's Grading Manual and Chapter 15.36 of the Municipal Code. If you are not sure which type of permit you need, email Permits or call 949-218-5343.

Forms and information on the page include Plan Check and Permit Fees, submittal checklists (Single Family Development, Multi-Family Development, Rough Grade Plan, Precise Grading Plan, Slope Repair, Lot Merger, and others), and bond forms for engineering permit surety requirements.

Source: https://www.sanclemente.gov/220/Land-Development-Permits

## Engineering Inspection Requests and Preconstruction Meetings

The preferred way to request Engineering Inspections is online. Requests are accepted until midnight the day before the inspection, and you receive a confirmation email.

By phone, call 949-361-3366, ext. 9002 and have your permit number and inspection code ready. Inspection codes are on the job card issued with the permit or on the yellow flyer at the public counter. The automated system lets you schedule, reschedule, and cancel inspections, view plan check status, and view inspection results.

No work required of any Engineering permit can start without first setting up and completing a Preconstruction Meeting. Use either of the two request methods to set it up.

Source: https://www.sanclemente.gov/220/Land-Development-Permits

## Subdivisions, Parcel Maps and Lot Mergers

Subdividing a single, legal parcel into four or fewer new parcels or units requires a Tentative Parcel Map. Subdividing into five or more parcels or units requires a Tentative Tract Map.

All subdivision and mapping applications are submitted to the Planning Division in the Community Development Department. These include Abandonments, Certificates of Compliance, Easement Deeds, Final Maps (Parcel and Tract), Lot Line Adjustments, Lot Mergers, and Reversions to Acreage. After Planning approval, applicants go to the Engineering Division for final plan check and recordation.

Except for Lot Mergers, plan check application fees for subdivision and mapping are charged on a time and material basis through a deposit account, which is required before plan check begins. Final Maps are plan checked by both the City and the County of Orange. County plan check applications are submitted separately to the County. The Subdivision Ordinance is in Chapter 16 of the Municipal Code.

Source: https://www.sanclemente.gov/220/Land-Development-Permits
