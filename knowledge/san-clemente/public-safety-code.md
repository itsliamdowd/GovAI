## Emergency Planning Program

The Emergency Planning Program provides emergency preparedness and administrative services in support of the City and its residents and visitors. Services include:
- Preparing and maintaining the City's Multi-Hazard Emergency Plan (the Local Hazard Mitigation Plan is posted as a PDF)
- Maintaining the City Emergency Operations Center and communications equipment
- Training City staff who may be called upon to serve in time of disaster
- Conducting emergency exercises with county, state, and federal agencies
- Working with other agencies on integrated plans for an incident at the San Onofre Nuclear Generating Station (SONGS)
- Assisting individuals and community organizations to become prepared for a disaster

Source: https://www.sanclemente.gov/305/Emergency-Planning

## Earthquake Preparedness

The City suggests these steps for a household earthquake plan:
- Build a disaster preparedness kit.
- Place large or heavy objects on lower shelves. Fasten shelves, mirrors, and large picture frames to walls, and brace taller top-heavy objects.
- Store bottled foods, glass, china and other breakables on low shelves or in cabinets that fasten shut.
- Know the safe spots in each room (under sturdy tables or desks) and the danger spots (near windows, mirrors, hanging objects, etc.).
- Install flex fittings on all gas and water connections.
- Practice earthquake drills with family members.
- Maintain an updated out-of-state contact card.

For real-time earthquake information, visit the United States Geological Survey.

Source: https://www.sanclemente.gov/305/Emergency-Planning

## San Onofre Nuclear Generating Station (SONGS) Emergency Planning

On June 7, 2013, Southern California Edison announced it would permanently retire Units 2 and 3 of SONGS. Decommissioning is regulated by the Nuclear Regulatory Commission (NRC). On June 8, 2015, SONGS implemented the Permanently Defueled Emergency Plan (PDEP), which the NRC approved on June 5, 2015.

For the surrounding communities, emergency preparedness remains the same. The SONGS Interjurisdictional Planning Committee (IPC) is responsible for nuclear emergency planning in the region.

Source: https://www.sanclemente.gov/305/Emergency-Planning

## Trauma Intervention Program (TIP)

TIP consists of citizen volunteers who provide support to emotionally traumatized citizens immediately after tragedy strikes. Hospital personnel, police officers, firefighters, and paramedics call for TIP volunteers 24 hours a day, 365 days a year. Volunteers assist family members following natural or unexpected deaths; victims of crime; disoriented or lonely older persons; victims of vehicle accidents; family members after a suicide; and anyone emotionally traumatized by a crisis event.

Source: https://www.sanclemente.gov/304/Fire-Services-OCFA

## Alarm System Permit

Any active residential or commercial alarm system in the City of San Clemente must have an Alarm Permit (Section 8.56.160). Both residential and commercial alarm permits are $40 per year. An Alarm Permit Application (PDF) and an Alarm Permit Cancellation Request are available on the page.

All changes to permit information or cancellation of an Alarm Permit must be submitted in writing within 10 days of such change to: City of San Clemente - Alarm Billing, 910 Calle Negocio, San Clemente, CA 92673.

## "Continuous Sidewalk" Parking Enforcement

It is the policy of Police Services to proactively enforce parking violations and issue citations for vehicles that park on or block any continuous sidewalk. San Clemente Police Services maintains a street survey listing the streets that have and do not have "continuous sidewalks" (list updated April 28, 2009).

Any changes or disputes about whether a street has a continuous sidewalk go to the Police Services Traffic Sergeant, who examines the street and makes a determination. The Police Services Chief reviews and finalizes the decision. An appeal of Police Services' findings is forwarded to the City Manager for review.

Source: https://www.sanclemente.gov/301/Police-Services-OCSD
