## Metered Parking — Rates, Hours and Rules

All parking is $1.50 per hour for those hours designated on the Parking Meters and Pay Station machines. Parking lots and meters at North Beach, Linda Lane, Parque Del Mar/Pier Bowl, and Esplanade/T-Street charge from 9 am to 10 pm. When you display a current City parking permit in your vehicle, you do not have to pay at parking meters or pay stations.

There is no grace period for expired meters. When time is expired, the violation flag goes up. All lots are patrolled at the hours specified, and there are no exceptions.

Residents and visitors may park on residential streets to use the beach. Some areas restrict parking during specific times (Montalvo, Lobeiro and the south end of town). Many streets also restrict parking on specific days of the month for street sweeping. These streets are marked with signs.

## Metrolink Commuter Parking

Monday through Friday, selected spaces near the Metrolink station at North Beach provide commuter parking for $1 per day. You must arrive between 4 am and 9 am, and must park in the designated spaces. After 9 am, the cost reverts back to the $1.50 per hour rate.

## Pay Stations — Locations and How to Use Them

All Pay Stations take credit cards, coins and dollar bills. They are located near the beach at select parking lots with numbered parking spaces: 2 at the Pier Bowl, 2 at Linda Lane, and 3 at North Beach.

To use one, note your parking space number and go to one of the seven automated Pay Station machines. The machines accept bills up to $10. Read the instructions carefully so you pay only for the amount of time you plan to be parked in the space. Time starts from when you pay the machine.

## Mobile Pay — Passport Parking App

Mobile Pay is available in the Pier Bowl Parking Lot and at T-Street via Passport Parking. This is a pilot program. The pay stations in the Pier Bowl remain an option during the pilot.

To use it, download the Passport Parking app from the Apple App Store or Google Play Store. Create an account with your mobile phone number or email address. Park where you see Passport Parking app signs and decals, then pay for your parking session from your phone. You can extend your time remotely.

For technical support with the app, use the "Support" tab in the mobile application. For other questions or comments, email Parking.

## Annual Metered Parking Permit

Metered parking permits are available only online. They cost $50 for San Clemente residents and $100 for non-residents. They are valid for 12 consecutive months.

Permits can be used in any City of San Clemente-operated metered parking space at North Beach, Linda Lane, Parque Del Mar/Pier Bowl, and T-Street. They do not apply to state-operated metered spaces at the Calafia State Beach and Trestles State Beach Lots. The decal must be affixed (no scotch tape) on the lower right-hand (passenger) side of the vehicle's front windshield.

For questions, call the Finance Division at 949-361-8315 or email Parking. Gift Certificates are also available online and may be used toward a Metered Parking Permit or any other Recreation Program.

## Street Sweeping Schedule

The Master Street Sweeping Schedule (PDF) lets you look up the sweeping schedule for your street. Only residential public streets are included. If your property is part of a homeowners association (HOA), contact your HOA for details on street sweeping.

Sweeping groups:
- Group A: 1st & 3rd Mondays
- Group B: 1st & 3rd Tuesdays
- Group C: 1st & 3rd Wednesdays
- Group D: 1st & 3rd Thursdays
- Group E: 2nd & 4th Mondays
- Group F: 2nd & 4th Tuesdays
- Group G: 2nd & 4th Wed & Thurs
- Group H: 2nd & 4th Mon & Tues + Alleys

The City sweeps all residential posted and non-posted streets twice per month. It sweeps Commercial, Industrial, Arterial, Parking Lot and Median areas once to 3 times per week. The schedule map and calendars (with courtesy notification) are reference tools only. The City will not be held liable for any financial obligations or citations resulting from them or from a notification not being received on schedule. It is the resident's responsibility to check and adhere to posted street signs.

For questions, contact Maintenance at 949-361-8317.

## Speed Limits

The California Vehicle Code (CVC) and the California Manual on Uniform Traffic Control Devices (CA MUTCD) govern the criteria for establishing speed limits in local municipalities. For speed limits on non-statutory streets to be valid and enforceable, the CVC requires an Engineering and Traffic Survey (E&TS) every five years (or seven or ten years, depending on specific street criteria).

Per state law, speed limits are established at the nearest 5 mph increment of a surveyed 85th percentile speed (the speed at which 85 percent of surveyed vehicles travel at or below). Based on factors such as accident rate and roadside conditions, speed limits may be slightly reduced from the nearest 85th percentile 5 mph increment. The current Speed Limits E&TS is available as a PDF on the page.

Source: https://www.sanclemente.gov/219/Traffic-Transportation

## Stop Sign Requests

Under the CA MUTCD, the purpose of a stop sign is to control the right-of-way assignment at an intersection. Stop signs are not intended for speed control. By state law, the City of San Clemente must comply with the CA MUTCD requirements when evaluating and recommending installation of new stop signs.

## SC Rides On-Demand Ride Program

In 2016, the City started the pilot on-demand transportation service SC Rides, designed to help mitigate the loss of former local OCTA bus routes 191 and 193. Under its Project V grant program, OCTA awarded funds to the City to provide up to 90% of the cost of the pilot.

Using the code SCRIDES, riders save up to $5 on their rides. Download the Lyft app, go to "Payment" in the main menu, choose "Add Lyft Pass," and type "SCRIDES" in the box. Then book a ride as usual, and the discount is applied after mapping a trip if the ride is eligible.

Eligibility:
- Discounted rides apply only to "Shared Rides."
- Rides must be taken between 6 am-8 pm.
- Rides must be both picked up and dropped off within a 0.25 miles radius of the original 191 and 193 bus routes.
- Look for signs at participating bus stops, or use the purple-text "View Coverage Area" portion of the coupon tile in the Promos section of the Lyft app.

For assisted rides and/or Wheelchair Accessible Vehicle (WAV) rides, call assisted ride vendor Butterfli at 855-267-2354. Press 3 to book a ride, and mention the SCRIDES code.
