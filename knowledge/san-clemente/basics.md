## City Council Meetings — Schedule and Viewing

The City Council meets the first and third Tuesdays of each month in the City Council Chambers. Closed Session items are considered at 4:30 pm, with the public business session commencing at 5:30 pm. Changes to this schedule and additional meetings that may be scheduled are publicly noticed.

Meetings are telecast live on Channel 854 and are rebroadcast the following Saturday at noon and on Thursdays at 2 pm. Video recordings of meetings are available on the City website.

Source: https://www.sanclemente.gov/159/City-Council

## Speaking at a City Council Meeting

Members of the public may address the City Council on matters within the jurisdiction of the City of San Clemente, whether or not the item appears on the meeting agenda. When you arrive at the Council Chambers, complete a "City Council Meeting Speaker Card" (located at the entrance to the Chambers) and submit it to the City Clerk.

When the Mayor calls your name, step to the podium and state your name and city of residence. Speakers are allocated three minutes.

## District Elections for City Council

The San Clemente City Council established four districts for electing City Council representatives to four-year terms. The Mayor will be elected at-large (by the entire city) for a two-year term. The City Council adopted Map 120 as the final district map, adopted with Ordinance 1775 on 5-7-24.

District elections began in November 2024 with the election of members representing Districts 3 and 4. In November 2026, the Mayor will be elected by the entire city, and members representing Districts 1 and 2 will also be elected. Under district elections, each City Council candidate must reside in the district they will represent, and residents vote only for candidates who reside in their district. The candidate receiving the most votes in each district is elected. An interactive map ("Find My District") is available on the City website to locate your district.

Source: https://www.sanclemente.gov/266/District-Elections

## Leadership San Clemente Program

Leadership San Clemente is free to San Clemente residents. The 2026 program takes place every Thursday from 4:00 p.m. to 6:00 p.m., commencing on February 26, 2026. The final session is on April 2, 2026, and includes a tour of the City. Participants attend six two-hour sessions, one night each week.

Sessions cover General Government, Finance and Administrative Services, Community Development, Public Works, Beaches, Parks and Recreation, Marine Safety, Fire Services and Law Enforcement. The class is limited to 18 citizens, and applications are accepted on a first-apply, first-accepted basis. To apply, call 949-361-8200 to request an application, visit the City Clerk's Office at City Hall, 910 Calle Negocio, or email a request to the City Clerk.
