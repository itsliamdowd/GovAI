## Rent Stabilization Ordinance

Santa Ana's Rent Stabilization Ordinance took effect November 19, 2021. Residential rent increases are limited to the lower of 3% per year, or 80% of the percent change in the Consumer Price Index over the most recent 12-month period. If the CPI is negative, no rent increase is permitted. The City publishes the allowable increase no later than June 30 of each year, effective September 1 of that year, at santa-ana.gov/renterprotections.

The rent cap does not apply to residential buildings constructed after February 1, 1995, or to mobile home spaces offered for rent after January 1, 1990. An owner of residential rental property or a mobile home park may petition for relief from the cap. The owner must provide evidence that an increase above the annual allowance is necessary for a fair and reasonable return. The City Manager, or designee, decides on the petition within 60 days of receiving a complete application. Owners must give written notice of the Ordinance and tenant's rights at the start of a lease or as part of any notice to increase rent.

For questions, tenants and owners can email rso@santa-ana.org or call (714) 667-2209.

## Just Cause Eviction Ordinance

The Just Cause Eviction Ordinance took effect November 19, 2021. After 30 days, an owner shall not terminate a tenancy without just cause, which must be stated in a written notice. At-fault just causes include default in payment of rent, breach of a material term of the lease, committing a nuisance, and criminal activity. No-fault just causes include the owner's intent to occupy the property, removal of the property from the rental market, and demolition or substantial remodel. Before issuing a notice to terminate for just cause, the owner must first give notice of the violation, with a time period for the tenant to fix it.

For a no-fault termination, the owner must either provide 3 months of relocation assistance or waive rent for the final 3 months of the tenancy. The ordinance does not apply to certain property types, including housing produced in the last 15 years, deed-restricted affordable housing, hotel and transient occupancy, hospital and care facilities, dormitories, and other shared living quarters. Owners had to notify existing tenants of the protections before December 19, 2021, or at the start or renewal of a lease.

The full text of both ordinances is at santa-ana.gov/renterprotections in English, Spanish and Vietnamese. Questions: rso@santa-ana.org or (714) 667-2209.

## Neighborhood Associations

Santa Ana is home to 64 different neighborhoods. A neighborhood association is a group of homeowners, renters, apartment dwellers, and representatives from neighborhood businesses, churches, and schools. These groups organize activities and events and work to improve the neighborhood. Residents are invited to attend the neighborhood association meeting in their neighborhood.

## Trash and Recycling — Republic Services Agreement

The City Council awarded the exclusive agreement for solid waste collection and handling services to Republic Services. The term runs 10 years, from July 1, 2022 to June 30, 2032, with a mutual option to extend for five years and a City option to extend for up to 36 months. Single-family homes were to pay about the same rate as at the time of approval. Republic will provide bulky items pickup for multifamily properties (apartments and condos) up to 40 times per year.

Republic will collect illegally dumped items within 24 to 48 hours of being reported. Under the Clean City Initiative, Republic will also provide:
- Proactive and on-call litter cleaning and bulky item removal in the public right of way
- 250 trash containers citywide on heavily trafficked sidewalks and other public rights-of-way
- Sidewalk litter and debris cleaning
- Sidewalk power washing
- Washing and sanitizing of all trash bins once per year upon customer request

If a Neighborhood Association requests them, Republic must provide up to 14 public trashcans for each of the City's 64 neighborhoods. These are emptied weekly with residential waste pickup.

## Childcare at Corbin and El Salvador Community Centers

The City Council authorized a facilities use agreement with Training and Research Foundation (TRF) to operate childcare centers at Corbin Community Center and El Salvador Community Center, through Aug. 31, 2026. TRF provides services to children up to age 3 who are Santa Ana residents, from 7:30 a.m. to 5:30 p.m. Monday through Friday. It can serve 65 to 75 infants and toddlers, with class sizes of 6 to 12.

Eligibility is determined through applications reviewed by TRF, which sets the criteria of need for Santa Ana residents. TRF's recruitment process includes a community information session and a waiting list for interested families.

## Park and Park Restroom Projects

A new 1.18-acre park will be built at the northeast corner of Raitt and Myrtle streets. It will feature a skate park, play equipment, exercise area, drought-tolerant landscaping, drinking water fountains, lighting, and a public restroom. The park is expected to be completed in 2023.

New restrooms will be built at Campesino Park, 3311 W. 5th St.; Memorial Park, 2102 S. Flower St.; and Madison Park, 1528 S. Standard. The restrooms are expected to be completed in summer 2022.
