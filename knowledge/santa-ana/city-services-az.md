## Water Service — 60 Days to Pay a Past-Due Bill Before Shutoff

The Santa Ana City Council approved Municipal Code changes on June 4, 2024 to comply with the State of California's Water Shutoff Protection Act (Senate Bill 998). Residential customers now have 60 days instead of 30 days to pay a past-due bill before service is interrupted or discontinued. They also get additional notifications and special provisions for qualified customers with medical and financial hardships. The changes take effect July 4, 2024, and the City is preparing to resume service discontinuations for water customers beginning that date. Water service disconnections will not begin until September 2024, when the first set of past-due accounts become eligible for shutoff.

Under the law, the City must post a written policy on discontinuation of water service for nonpayment in English, Spanish, Vietnamese, Chinese, Korean, and Tagalog. It may not discontinue residential service for nonpayment until a payment has been delinquent for at least 60 days. It must also report the number of annual residential discontinuations for inability to pay on its website and to the State Water Resources Control Board. The City will shut off water as a last resort and will focus first on past-due accounts with higher balances. Payment arrangements may be requested by all water customers, but accommodations are not guaranteed.

The City is not offering payment assistance anymore and has not contracted with any agency or entity to provide assistance. Customers should pay only through the City's approved methods. For questions, call (714) 647-5454, go to santa-ana.gov/municipal-utility-services/, or visit City Hall in person.

## Smart Water Meter Program

The City won the Smart Cities North American Sustainable Infrastructure award for its Advanced Water Metering Infrastructure ("Smart Water Meter") project. The meters will be installed for all Santa Ana customers over the next three years (as of July 16, 2021). They wirelessly send usage information to the utility office, which can then proactively notify customers of leaks, high usage or other potential events. Customers will have around-the-clock water use information on their smartphones and computers.

## Solid Waste Collection — Franchise and Rates

Solid waste collection and disposal in Santa Ana is performed by a private company under an exclusive franchise agreement. The City Council approved the agreement with Republic Services on August 17, 2021. New rates take effect July 1, 2022. Rate adjustments run through June 30, 2027: special rate adjustments in rate years two through four and annual rate adjustments in rate years two through five. Rates cover residential, multifamily, commercial, industrial, and construction and demolition services. They are based on container size and frequency of service, and also cover services such as bin rental.

Any increase requested by Republic Services that exceeds the adopted methodology in any year would require another Proposition 218 noticed public hearing and City Council approval. The Prop 218 public hearing on the rates was scheduled for May 17, 2022.
