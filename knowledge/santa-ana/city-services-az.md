## Business Recycling and Organics Trash Service Requirements

New trash service requirements for businesses require them to transition to using recycle containers and organics containers, instead of only using solid waste containers. Community Development Agency staff and Public Works Agency staff partnered to help Downtown businesses make the transition. As of the August 20, 2020 post, five Downtown Santa Ana businesses had transitioned: Las Cazuelas, El Rincon Mexicano, The Den, Grand Central Arts Center, and Metro PCS. Some businesses reported a decrease in their monthly service bill.

Source: https://santa-ana.gov/santa-ana-businesses-receive-recycling-training/

## Career Online High School (Santa Ana Public Library)

The Santa Ana Public Library offers the Career Online High School program to adult residents living in the City of Santa Ana. The program is 100% virtual, flexible and free. Scholarships for the program are limited. Benefits include a personal academic coach and certified instructors, 24/7 online classroom access, an accredited high school diploma while preparing for a career in high-demand fields, and tools to start or advance a career, including a resume and cover letter. Applications are started through the Library website.

Source: https://santa-ana.gov/career-online-high-school-at-the-santa-ana-public-library/

## General Plan Update ("Golden City Beyond: A Shared Vision")

The Draft General Plan will guide the City's development and conservation for the next 25 years through 2045. It is organized into three Sections: Services and Infrastructure (1), Natural Environment (2), and Built Environment (3). A total of 12 elements will replace 16 existing elements last revised comprehensively in 1982. The Land Use Element identifies five key areas for potential urban and transit-oriented development.

The Draft Program Environmental Impact Report (PEIR) was available for a 45-day public review from Monday, August 3, 2020 through Wednesday, September 16, 2020. The Draft General Plan Elements and Program EIR are found at santa-ana.gov/general-plan. Comments can be emailed to newgeneralplan@santa-ana.org. For more information call (714) 667-2781.

Source: https://santa-ana.gov/draft-santa-ana-general-plan-update-and-environmental-impact-report-available-for-public-review/

## Smart Water Meter Program

The City's Advanced Water Metering Infrastructure ("Smart Water Meter") project will install new meters for all Santa Ana customers over three years, as stated in the July 16, 2021 post. The meters wirelessly send usage information to the utility office, which can proactively notify customers of leaks, high usage or other potential events. Customers will also have access to around-the-clock water use information on their smartphones and computers.

Source: https://santa-ana.gov/water-resources-division-wins-sustainable-infrastructure-award/

## Water Quality Report (Consumer Confidence Report)

The Consumer Confidence Report (CCR) is an annual water quality report that informs Santa Ana residents about where their drinking water comes from and what's in it. The 2020 CCR Water Quality Report states that the City continues to maintain the highest standards, surpassing all state and federal safety requirements. To request a printed copy, contact the Water Resources Division at 714-647-3320.

Source: https://santa-ana.gov/water-quality-report-now-available/
