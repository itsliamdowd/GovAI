## Sidewalk Repairs — How to Report a Problem

The Santa Ana City Council voted to add $1 million for sidewalk repairs as part of the 2021-2022 budget. To report a sidewalk that needs repair, download the mySantaAna app and tap the "Report an Issue" button. These requests go directly to Public Works dispatchers, who assess the problem and send a team out to fix it.

## Bristol Street Protected Bike Lanes

Since February 2021, the City has been constructing protected bike lanes and bus islands on Bristol Street between Edinger Avenue and 1st Street. The original scope is complete, and the project was expanded with additional improvements within the project limit. As of the July 30, 2021 post, completion was scheduled for early fall 2021. Once the project is completed, three lanes in each direction will be reinstated, along with a dedicated, protected bike lane.

## Bus Stop Furniture and Shelter Replacement

The City's Public Works Agency began the latest round of bus stop furniture replacement in May 2021. The new Santa Ana design covers 134 pairs of one bench and one trash container. Installation is prioritized for stops with no furniture or missing furniture components.

The new design will also be used on bus shelter replacements within the Main Street project, which was scheduled to begin in fall 2021. The Orange County Transportation Authority (OCTA) is funding additional bus shelter replacements at 35 locations within the City, anticipated for spring 2022.

Source: https://santa-ana.gov/new-bus-benches-display-santa-ana-design/

## Supplemental Bus Stop and Shelter Cleaning (2020)

Using CARES Act funds, the Public Works Agency – Maintenance Services Division implemented a supplemental bus stop and shelter maintenance program through December 2020. It is over and above the existing weekly services from a bus shelter cleaning contractor. The program began July 24, 2020, and as of the September 3, 2020 post, staff had cleaned nearly 500 bus shelters.

Services include sanitizing bus benches, furniture, and advertisement panels, graffiti removal, and trash can liner replacement. Staff also clean and sanitize adjacent sidewalk areas and crosswalk push buttons.

Source: https://santa-ana.gov/supplemental-bus-stop-cleaning/

## I-5 Central Corridor HOV Project (Main Street Area)

Posted August 11, 2020: construction on the I-5 Central Corridor HOV Project continued on Main Street. Night work on traffic signal timing and electrical activities at the northeast corner of Main Street and Edgewood Road was scheduled for Wednesday, August 12, 2020 from 10 p.m. to Thursday, August 13, 2020 at 6 a.m. During that work, the Northbound Main Street Off-ramp was closed, while the Broadway Off-ramp remained open. All Main Street lanes were closed except one lane in each direction, and Edgewood remained open but could be temporarily controlled by stop signs.

Daytime construction continues on I-5 between SR-55 and SR-57, including permanent signs, electrical work, and the concrete median barrier. When complete, the project will add an additional carpool lane (for a total of two lanes) in each direction on I-5 between SR-55 and SR-57. Construction was expected to be completed in early 2021.

For questions or issues with the construction, call (800) 724-0353 or e-mail central5fwy@octa.net. To sign up for construction alerts, visit www.octa.net/i5Central. Dates and times are subject to change due to inclement weather or unforeseen construction constraints.
