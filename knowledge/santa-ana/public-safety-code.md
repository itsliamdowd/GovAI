## Police Misconduct Complaints

Members of the public who believe they have witnessed police misconduct are encouraged to file a complaint at santa-ana.gov/police-file-a-complaint. For more information, contact the Santa Ana Police Department Internal Affairs Division at (714) 245-8011.

## Police Athletic & Activity League (PAAL)

PAAL is a community program run by the Santa Ana Police Department. It focuses on building a bond between kids and police officers through education, fitness and activities. It offers tutoring, sports teams, field trips and more. The original PAAL center is at 2627 W. McFadden Ave. and was recently renovated, according to the August 18, 2022 post.

As of August 18, 2022, the City Council had directed staff to convert the vacant 1928 fire station at 625 S. Cypress Ave. in the Pacific Park neighborhood into a new PAAL center serving youths and seniors. The planned center will include a large multi-use open space, classrooms, exercise rooms and a police substation. The renovation was expected to take about 18 months. A new PAAL program was also planned at the Roosevelt-Walker Community Center in partnership with the Parks, Recreation and Community Services Agency.
