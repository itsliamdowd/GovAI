## Rent Stabilization Ordinance

The Santa Ana City Council adopted a Rent Stabilization Ordinance on October 19, 2021. It limits residential rent increases to the lower of 3% per year, or 80% of the percent change in the Consumer Price Index over the most recent 12-month period. If the CPI is negative, no rent increase is permitted. The ordinance became effective November 19, 2021.

The City publishes the allowable rent increase at santa-ana.gov/renterprotections no later than June 30 of each year, effective September 1 of that year. The rent cap does not apply to residential buildings constructed after February 1, 1995, or to mobile home spaces offered for rent after January 1, 1990.

An owner of residential rental property or a mobile home park may petition for relief from the cap. The owner must provide evidence that an increase above the annual allowance is necessary to provide a fair and reasonable return. The City Manager, or designee, decides on the petition within 60 days of receiving a complete application. Property owners must give written notice of the ordinance and tenant's rights at the start of a lease or as part of any notice to increase rent. The full ordinance text and FAQs are at santa-ana.gov/renterprotections in English, Spanish and Vietnamese. Questions: rso@santa-ana.org or (714) 667-2209.

## Rent Stabilization — Affordable and Voucher Unit Exclusion

The rent stabilization ordinance limits residential rent increases to no more than 3 percent per year and took effect November 19, 2021. It is not applicable to affordable housing projects or assisted units on the City's Housing Choice Voucher Program.

## Just Cause Eviction Ordinance

Under the Just Cause Eviction Ordinance, after 30 days an owner shall not terminate a tenancy without just cause, which must be stated in a written notice. At-fault just causes include default in payment of rent, breach of a material term of the lease, committing a nuisance, criminal activity, and other actions. No-fault just causes include the owner's intent to occupy the property, removal of the property from the rental market, and demolition or substantial remodel.

Before issuing a notice to terminate for just cause, the owner must first give notice of the violation with a time period for the tenant to fix it. For a no-fault termination, the owner must either provide 3 months of relocation assistance or waive payment of rent for the final 3 months of the tenancy.

The ordinance does not apply to certain residential property, including housing produced in the last 15 years, deed-restricted affordable housing, hotel and transient occupancy, hospital and care facilities, dormitories, and other shared living quarters. Owners had to notify existing tenants of the protections before December 19, 2021, or at the start or renewal of a lease. The City's written notice is at santa-ana.gov/renterprotections. Questions: rso@santa-ana.org or (714) 667-2209.

## Housing Choice Voucher Waiting List (2022 Opening)

The Housing Authority of the City of Santa Ana accepted Housing Choice Voucher Program applications from Monday, May 2, 2022, at 7:30 a.m. until Tuesday, May 31, 2022 at 11:59 p.m. The application was at santa-ana.gov/housing. Applicants must be very low-income and 18 years of age or an emancipated youth by the court. Paper applications are not accepted. There is a local preference for veterans and applicants who live or work in the City of Santa Ana. Applicants were asked not to contact the Housing Authority after submitting. Notifications of selection or non-selection were to be sent by mail and e-mail on or before Sunday, July 31, 2022.

People with a disability who need a reasonable accommodation should call (714) 667-2229 before May 31, 2022. Business hours are Monday through Thursday, 7:30 a.m. to 5:30 p.m., Friday 8 a.m. to 5 p.m., and closed every other Friday.

## Housing Authority — Landlord Rent Increase Requests and Vacancy Listings

Landlords working with the Santa Ana Housing Authority should email all rent increase requests to SAHARentIncrease@santa-ana.org. This account is dedicated only to rent increase requests.

Landlords can list vacant units for free at www.affordablehousing.com, where voucher holders search for units. Listing a unit does not obligate the landlord to rent to a voucher holder. Landlords may also sign up for the regional landlord incentive program, Welcome Home OC: www.unitedtoendhomelessness.org/welcomehome.

## Housing Plus Program

Families assisted through the Housing Choice Voucher (HCV) program can voluntarily join the Housing Plus Program, a HUD program that helps them increase earned income and reduce dependency on welfare assistance and rental subsidies. The Santa Ana Housing Authority works with the Santa Ana WORK Center and a Program Coordinating Committee to provide coaching, guidance and case management to participants.

## Legacy Square Affordable Apartments

Legacy Square is an affordable apartment community at 301 East Santa Ana Boulevard. As of the March 18, 2022 posting it was under construction and expected to be completed in early 2023. It will have 93 apartments: 30 one-bedrooms, 39 two-bedrooms, and 24 three-bedrooms.

To qualify, households must have incomes below 60% of area median income (AMI), approximately $64,560 for a 2-person household. Apartments will be set aside for residents earning under 25%, under 30%, under 50%, and under 60% of AMI. There will be a local preference for Santa Ana residents. For information or to join the mailing list, visit https://nationalcore.org/communities/legacy-square/.

## La Placita Cinco Affordable Apartments

La Placita Cinco is an affordable apartment community at 2239 W. 5th St. It has 20 two-bedroom units, 29 three-bedroom units, and 2 four-bedroom units. Per the September 1, 2020 posting, the first applications were reviewed for completeness. First priority went to people who live in Santa Ana and/or work in Santa Ana at least 32 hours per week for at least the last 6 months. Proof of the preference must be brought to the application appointment, such as a lease or rental agreement, utility bills, or 6 months of paycheck stubs.

Contact Solari Enterprises: (800) 801-8440, extension 7154, or leasing@solari-ent.com.
