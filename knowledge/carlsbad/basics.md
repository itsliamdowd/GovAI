## About Carlsbad: Recreation and Activities

The city's three libraries offer more than 300,000 books, DVDs, CDs and audiobooks. The city has an extensive trail system. It also has a senior center for active adults 50+. Water activities include surfing Carlsbad's beaches, boating or fishing in one of the lagoons, and swimming at Alga Norte Aquatic Center or the Monroe Street Pool. The Crossings at Carlsbad is a golf course. The city also offers a sculpture garden, an art gallery, free concerts in the park, youth and adult sports leagues, and a self-guided tour of historic sites.

Source: https://www.carlsbadca.gov/residents/about-carlsbad

## Transportation Options

Local public transportation, including train, bus and shuttle services, is provided by the North County Transit District (NCTD). Amtrak provides train service from San Diego to points north. The page advises checking for current service interruptions due to track repairs.

McClellan-Palomar Airport is in Carlsbad but is owned and operated by the County of San Diego. The Airport noise office handles complaints about airport noise. San Diego International Airport is operated by the San Diego Regional Airport Authority.

The city encourages carpooling and ride sharing. It offers the Carlsbad Commuter app and website. RideLink offers a range of other transportation options region-wide. The city's Sustainable Mobility Plan outlines its walking and biking projects and programs.

Source: https://www.carlsbadca.gov/residents/about-carlsbad/getting-around

## City Manager

Carlsbad follows the "council-manager" form of government. Elected leaders set the overall direction, and the city manager oversees day to day operations. The city manager manages a staff of over 1,000. The City Manager is Geoff Patnoe, 1200 Carlsbad Village Drive, Carlsbad, CA 92008. Phone: 442-339-2821. Email: manager@carlsbadca.gov. ## City Attorney's Office

Contact: Office of the City Attorney, 1200 Carlsbad Village Drive, Carlsbad, CA 92008. Phone: 442-339-2891. Fax: 760-434-8367. Email: attorney@carlsbadca.gov.

The City Attorney is the chief legal officer of the city. The office prosecutes violations of the Carlsbad Municipal Code but does not prosecute violations of state codes. The District Attorney prosecutes violations of state code in the county. By law, the City Attorney cannot represent or advise private individuals, businesses or groups, including giving a legal interpretation of federal, state or city codes.

## Beaches: Ownership and Dogs

Most Carlsbad beaches are owned and managed by the California State Parks Department, which also provides lifeguard service, restrooms, picnic areas and parking lots. The state owns beaches from South Ponto to Oak Avenue. The city is in charge of the ¾ mile stretch north of Oak Avenue to the Oceanside border. Beaches include South Carlsbad State Beach, Carlsbad State Beach and North Carlsbad beaches.

Dogs are not allowed on the beach in Carlsbad, leashed or unleashed. Leashed dogs are welcome on the city's trail system. To report a dog on the beach, call the Carlsbad Police Department's 24 hour non-emergency line at 442-339-2197. City dispatchers will assist you directly or connect you to State Parks. City police rangers can issue up to a $300 citation for a dog on the beach and/or up to a $600 citation for having a dog off leash. The city has three dog parks, including the all-grass Poinsettia Dog Park.

Source: https://www.carlsbadca.gov/residents/about-carlsbad/beaches

## Lagoons

Carlsbad has three lagoons covering more than 1,000 acres: Agua Hedionda, Batiquitos and Buena Vista. They include nature preserves, hiking trails and water recreation areas. Boats, personal watercraft and paddleboards are allowed on Agua Hedionda Lagoon.

Source: https://www.carlsbadca.gov/residents/about-carlsbad/lagoons

## Trails

Carlsbad has approximately 67 miles of trails and 51 miles of open space trails. The city offers a GoHike Carlsbad app, trail volunteer events and a Happy Trails Newsletter.

Fire prevention rules on trails and open space (CMC 11.32.030): no motorized vehicles, no smoking, no fires, no firearms and no fireworks. Report fires immediately to 9-1-1. For non-emergency issues call the Police Department, 760-931-2197.
