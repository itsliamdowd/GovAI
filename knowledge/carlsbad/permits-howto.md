## Oversized/Recreational Vehicle Parking Permit

The City of Carlsbad restricts overnight parking of recreational and oversized vehicles on city streets between 2:00 a.m. and 5:00 a.m. Every citation is assessed a $50 penalty. An oversized vehicle is defined as either 22 feet in length, or seven feet in height and seven feet in width. Residents and their guests can apply for an oversized vehicle permit. It must be displayed in the window when the vehicle is parked on a city street between 2 and 5 a.m., and the vehicle must be parked within 400 feet of the residence that was granted the permit.

Permit types:
- Yearly Residential Permit: free for City of Carlsbad residents who own an RV. It allows parking on a public street at the residence for up to 72 hours, four times within each month.
- Temporary 72 Hour Guest Permit: a free permit for a resident's guest with an RV, allowing parking at the residence for up to 72 hours, six times per year.
- Commercial Property Permit: hotels and motels can let guests park RVs next to the inn on public streets if the RVs cannot be accommodated in the property's parking lot. The city issues each hotel and motel reusable permits to give to guests.

The free permit can be obtained from the Carlsbad Police Department Monday through Friday from 8:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m. The Police Department is located at 2560 Orion Way, 442-339-2119.

## Business License — Who Needs One and How to Apply

The City of Carlsbad requires all businesses within city limits to have a valid license. This includes businesses based in other cities that enter Carlsbad as part of their work, such as contractors and delivery companies. It also includes businesses located in Carlsbad that may not have customers in Carlsbad. Even companies with a temporary presence require a business license.

You may apply online, by mail or in person. The current processing time is three to four weeks. An invoice for payment is sent once the application has been processed and all necessary department approvals have been received. Do not submit multiple applications or payments. Issuance of the license is not an endorsement, nor certification of compliance with other ordinances or laws.

For questions, call or email the business license office at 442-339-2495 or business.license@carlsbadca.gov.

## Sidewalk Vending and Food Truck Permits

A city ordinance that took effect on March 21, 2019 regulates food trucks and sidewalk vendors. Mobile vendors should review Chapter 8.32 of the Carlsbad Municipal Code. At a minimum, mobile vendors must have:
- City Business License: all mobile vendors are subject to the city's business licensing requirements (CMC sections 5.04 and 5.08).
- City vendor permit application: most mobile vendors will also need this if they use a motorized or non-motorized vehicle, food truck, wagon, stand, bike or other form of conveyance for vending.
- California Seller's Permit: a valid California Department of Tax and Fee Administration seller's permit that designates "City of Carlsbad" as a location or sublocation.
- Insurance: any insurance required by the city's risk manager, such as commercial general liability, auto, or worker's compensation insurance. Submit copies of all existing insurance policies with the business license application.

To obtain a business license or vending permit, or for more information, call 442-339-2495. Special San Diego County rules apply for food product vendors. To make a county health permit complaint, call 858-505-6903.

## Building Permits — Plan Check Process

State and city ordinances require that construction design be approved before a building permit can be issued. This approval process is called "plan check." Submittal requirements vary by type of construction; city bulletins are available for Tenant Improvements and Residential Construction. An application must accompany all submittals.

Plans are routed to various city divisions for review, and the applicant is notified of any corrections needed. Revised plans can be re-submitted for a second review, repeated until all corrections are addressed (CMC 18.04.020). The plan check process extends for one year from the date of submittal. Reviews not completed within one year expire, and renewing after expiration requires a new set of plans with payment for the subsequent plan review (CMC 18.04.035).

Source: https://www.carlsbadca.gov/residents/your-home/building-permits

## Online Building Permits and Exemptions

Homeowners, property owners, and licensed contractors can obtain select simple, no-plan check residential permits online through the Customer Self Service Portal (CSS). Most construction work requires a permit, but some types are exempt. The list of work that does not need a permit is in the municipal code (CMC 18.04.015). The Building Permits page explains how to apply online, by email or in person.

Source: https://www.carlsbadca.gov/residents/your-home/building-permits

## Street Dedication and Public Improvement Thresholds

Under the Dedication and Improvement Ordinance (Chapter 18.40), the Street Dedication Threshold is $38,000 building permit valuation. The threshold for Public Improvements or NIA is $206,000 building permit valuation.
