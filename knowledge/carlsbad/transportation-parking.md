## Traffic Safety & Mobility Commission — Role and Membership

The Traffic Safety & Mobility Commission has seven members, appointed by the mayor and confirmed by the City Council. Members must be citizens of Carlsbad and normally serve four-year terms. The commission's duties (Carlsbad Municipal Code, Chapter 2.28) are to:
- Study all matters referred to it concerning traffic and pedestrian safety.
- Review staff studies and make recommendations to the City Council on traffic and pedestrian safety.
- Provide a public forum to review complaints and requests regarding traffic and pedestrian safety.
- Recommend revisions to city codes and plans on traffic and pedestrian safety, parking, and school safety.
- Make written recommendations to the City Council on measures to promote traffic and pedestrian safety.

Staff contact: Chris Romano, Principal Planner, Transportation, 1635 Faraday Avenue, Carlsbad, CA 92008, 442-339-2156, Chris.Romano@carlsbadca.gov.

Source: https://www.carlsbadca.gov/city-hall/meetings-agendas/boards-commissions/traffic-mobility-commission

## Traffic Safety & Mobility Commission — Public Comment

To comment in person, fill out a speaker request form (located in the foyer) and submit it to the Minutes Clerk before the item begins. The Chair will call your name when it is your turn. Speakers have three minutes unless the presiding officer (usually the Chair) changes that time. You may not give your time to another person. Groups can select a single speaker as long as three other members of the group are present, and group representatives have 10 minutes unless the presiding officer or the Commission changes that time.

To comment in writing, email traffic@carlsbadca.gov and indicate the agenda item number in the subject line. Emails received by 1:00 p.m. will be provided to the Commission before the meeting starts. Emailed comments will not be read out loud during the meeting. Other comments will be included with the meeting record.
